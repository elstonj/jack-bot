## Executive Summary
This addendum revises Phase 4 of the ECS-DoT UAS Integration and Validation contract, splitting it into two payment milestones (Phase 4a and 4b), and offers additional flight support days for controller debugging and evaluation at a rate of $3,000 per day.

## Technical Approach
- Phase 4 restructured to separate engineering support and flight testing from final deliverable completion
- Additional flight support provided on as-needed basis for ECS-DoT controller debugging and evaluation
- Flights conducted using Black Swift's E2 platform with operator and safety pilot support

## Products & Capabilities Described

**E2 (UAS Platform)**
- Available for rental support at $1,000/day as part of flight support services
- Used for controller debugging and evaluation flights
- Supports up to 3 flight hours or 8-hour crew day operations

- Flight pattern debugging and evaluation
