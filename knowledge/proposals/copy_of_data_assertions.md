## Executive Summary
Black Swift Technologies asserts intellectual property restrictions on four categories of technical data and software to be furnished under the proposed SBIR contract with the Air Force, protecting both pre-existing proprietary systems and new contract-developed technology from unrestricted government use, release, or disclosure.
