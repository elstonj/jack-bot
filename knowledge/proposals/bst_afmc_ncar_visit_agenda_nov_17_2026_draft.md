## Executive Summary
Black Swift Technologies hosted an Air Force AFLCMC delegation and NCAR colleagues to demonstrate the S0 air-deployed UAS system and work through technical integration requirements for deploying S0 aircraft from the WC-130J Hurricane Hunter aircraft. The visit built on proven operational experience with NOAA's WP-3D hurricane configuration to establish a practical path toward Air Force integration, testing, and approval.

## Technical Approach
BST proposed to integrate the S0 air-deployed system into the WC-130J platform through:
- **Launch interface definition**: Determining WC-130J tube specifications (internal diameter, length, material) and canister separation/parachute deployment characteristics
- **RF and antenna planning**: Mounting approach, frequency coordination with dropsonde/AVAPS systems to prevent interference, and filtering requirements
- **Ground station integration**: Portable carry-on configuration with aircraft power connection, independent GPS source, and onboard control capability from WC-130J
- **Situational awareness**: Developing methods to display S0 position relative to the aircraft in real-time without NOAA AirOps equivalent system
- **Safety & approvals**: Determining need for safe-separation studies and defining approval pathway for temporary roll-on/roll-off installation

## Products & Capabilities Described

### S0 (Air-Deployed UAS)
- **What it is**: Portable, air-deployed unmanned aircraft system designed for deployment from manned aircraft
- **Operational history**: 
  - First powered-UAS releases from Hurricane Hunter aircraft in 2024
  - 21 operational deployments during 2025 hurricane season with NOAA WP-3D
- **Use context**: Proposed for integration with WC-130J for meteorological data collection

### S0-AD
- **What it is**: Air-deployed variant of S0 system
- **System components**: Aircraft, launch canister, parachute system, ground control station, communication link

### SwiftTab
- **What it is**: Portable ground station operator interface/control console
- **Capabilities**: 
  - Concurrent control of multiple S0 aircraft
  - Operator workflow for mission planning and real-time control

### Portable Ground Station
- **What it is**: Carry-on ground control equipment
- **Specifications/requirements**: 
  - Aircraft power connection capability
  - Independent GPS source
  - Onboard control from WC-130J
  - Power and electrical integration needs

## Use Cases & Applications
- **Hurricane reconnaissance and monitoring**: Direct continuation of proven 2024-2025 NOAA WP-3D operations
- **WC-130J meteorological data collection**: Air Force Weather Reconnaissance Squadron (53rd WRS) atmospheric sampling missions
- **Multi-platform coordination**: Concurrent operation of S0 with dropsonde systems and other aircraft sensors
