## Executive Summary
BST provided a Phase I Option progress update on the development of an expendable sonobuoy-launched UAV for Anti-Submarine Warfare (ASW) cued search, detection, tracking, and classification. The meeting focused on magnetometer testing progress, airframe modifications for ground launch systems, and commercialization discussions with the UK Royal Navy.

## Technical Approach
**Magnetometer Systems Integration:**
- Testing two magnetometer platforms: Bartington UAS-MAG and QuSpin magnetometer
- Custom ADC (Analog-to-Digital Converter) interface board received for Bartington to achieve higher resolution data recording
- QuSpin magnetometer refurbished with firmware upgrade to reduce previously observed noise
- Exploring native Bartington ADC settings to achieve necessary output resolution

**Airframe Modifications:**
- Launch rail assembly completed, awaiting dynamic testing
- Initial testing planned with dummy weighted airframe to establish safe launch parameters

## Products & Capabilities Described

### S0 (Magnetometer Variant)
- ASW detection platform with integrated magnetometer
- Planned for helicopter deployment
- Commercialization target: Q1/Q2 2027 for UK Royal Navy helicopter drop operations

### Bartington UAS-MAG
- High-resolution magnetometer sensor platform
- Custom ADC interface enables improved data resolution
- Investigating native ADC optimization options

### QuSpin Magnetometer
- Recently refurbished and upgraded with new firmware
- Previous testing showed elevated noise levels; firmware/settings adjustments expected to resolve this

## Use Cases & Applications
- Anti-Submarine Warfare (ASW) operations
- Cued search, detection, tracking, and classification missions
- UK Royal Navy helicopter-launched operations (commercialization interest)
- Sonobuoy-integrated UAV deployment
