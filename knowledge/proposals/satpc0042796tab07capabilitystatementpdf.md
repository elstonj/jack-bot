## Executive Summary
NASA/NSSC issued a sole source procurement notice for the BlackSwift S0 UAS Flight System, determining that Black Swift Technologies, LLC is the sole provider capable of delivering this system. The notice was issued under FAR 12.102(a) authority for commercial items, with performance to be located at NASA Langley Research Center.
