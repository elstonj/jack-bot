## Executive Summary
This proposal seeks $1.85M in FY2026 one-year Operations, Research, and Facilities (ORF) funds to scale the proven Black Swift S0 air-deployed UAS into an operational-tempo observation program for the 2027 Atlantic hurricane season. The effort converts existing research capability into a full-season expendable platform deployment launched from NOAA's WP-3D Orion Hurricane Hunter to improve hurricane intensity forecasting through enhanced boundary-layer observations.

## Technical Approach
- **Platform:** S0 air-deployed UAS (3 lbs, launched from WP-3D Orion)
- **Operational envelope:** Profiles storm boundary layer from 50 ft to 15,000 ft; sustained flight at 10 m above sea surface for 25 minutes; mission duration exceeding 70 minutes; communications to 125 nm
- **Sensor suite:** Measures pressure, temperature, humidity, 3-D winds, and sea-surface temperature in real time
- **Data pathway:** Real-time link hardening, BUFR formatting for National Hurricane Center and EMC assimilation
- **Integration:** P-3 launch-tube integration, environmental qualification, clear-air verification flights
- **Field support:** Two embedded BST field engineers at AOC Lakeland for 2027 season; training for NOAA flight directors and scientists

## Products & Capabilities Described

### Black Swift S0 Air-Deployed UAS
- **What it is:** Lightweight (under 3 lbs), expendable unmanned aerial system designed specifically for tropical cyclone sampling
- **Demonstrated performance:**
  - Successful deployment in Tropical Storm Tammy (2023)
  - Mission duration exceeding 70 minutes
  - Communications range to 125 nautical miles
  - Capable of sustained flight at 10 m above sea surface for 25 minutes
  - Profiles from 50 ft to 15,000 ft altitude
- **Sensor measurements:** Pressure, temperature, humidity, 3-D winds, sea-surface temperature (real-time)
- **Manufacturing:** Entirely U.S.-designed and manufactured, compliant with statutory foreign-entity restrictions on UAS procurement
- **Proposed operational tempo:** Target deployment on every operational P-3 tasking

## Use Cases & Applications

**Hurricane Intensity Forecasting:**
- Boundary-layer observations from dangerously low altitudes (50 ft and below) inaccessible to crewed aircraft
- Data assimilation into operational Hurricane Analysis and Forecast System (HAFS)
- Associated with approximately 10% improvement in hurricane intensity prediction when boundary-layer observations are assimilated
- 2027 Atlantic hurricane season operational deployment
