## Executive Summary
This white paper proposes federally grant-funded rapid-response UAS kits to equip state emergency management agencies in hurricane-belt states with pre-landfall atmospheric sensing and post-landfall damage assessment capabilities. The program leverages newly eligible federal UAS funding under FY26 NDAA provisions and capitalizes on covered-entity restrictions favoring domestic platforms. Each state program ($750K) delivers two kits pairing the S0-VTOL (atmospheric profiling) with the S3 (EO reconnaissance), operator training, COP integration, and 2027-season support.

## Technical Approach
- **Pre-landfall capability**: S0-VTOL autonomous profiles (wind, temperature, humidity to 15,000 ft) launched from any parking lot or staging area; operates in conditions grounding crewed aviation; derived from NOAA's tropical cyclone observation platform.
- **Post-landfall capability**: S3 with 5 lb modular EO payload for damage reconnaissance, infrastructure assessment, search-area survey; packable into commercial air-transport cases.
- **Data integration**: Feeds into state common operating picture (WebEOC-class tools) with pre-landfall wind-hazard decision product tailored to state timelines.
- **Operations**: Launch-and-forget autonomy enables non-specialist emergency managers to generate data on day one.

## Products & Capabilities Described

### S0-VTOL
- Vertical takeoff/landing from any parking lot or staging area
- Autonomous atmospheric profiling: wind, temperature, humidity to 15,000 ft altitude
- Flyable in conditions that ground crewed aviation
- Airframe family matches NOAA tropical cyclone platform
- Two units per state program

### S3
- 5 lb modular EO (electro-optical) payload
- Post-landfall damage reconnaissance and infrastructure assessment
- Search-area survey capability
- Packs into commercial air-transportable cases
- Two units per state program
- Note: S3 production line noted as "oversubscribed several times over" with 6 S3-Military units planned for 2026

## Use Cases & Applications
- **Pre-landfall**: 72-hour window around hurricane landfall; atmospheric sensing over state territory when surface stations fail and crewed aviation is grounded; wind and hazard structure mapping for pre-positioning, evacuation, and SAR decision support
- **Post-landfall**: First-light damage picture generation for resource allocation; infrastructure assessment; search-and-rescue area survey
- **Target states**: Florida, Texas, Louisiana, North Carolina (hurricane belt priority); template scalable across four states

## Program Structure & Deliverables (12-month period)
- Two rapid-response kits per state (2 S0-VTOL + 2 S3 + payloads + ground stations + cases)
- Kit staging at state logistics centers or regional staging areas designated by state EMA
- Operator training and certification for state EM and USAR personnel (two cohorts)
- Data feed integration into state COP with pre-landfall wind-hazard product
- One state-level hurricane exercise participation
- On-call surge support for 2027 hurricane season

- **Eligible federal sources**: DHS Homeland Security Grant Program (HSGP), Emergency Management Performance Grant (EMPG), DOJ Byrne JAG
- **State matching funds**: Required

## Notable Details & Issues

### Regulatory/Policy Drivers
- FY2026 NDAA (Pub. L. 119-60, 18 December 2025) amended Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act to make UAS and counter-UAS eligible for federal grant funding and authorize federal money for public safety UAS purchase and operation
- Sections 8602–8604 add drone protection for critical infrastructure under Homeland Security Act
- **Precedent**: FEMA awarded $250 million to eleven states in December 2025 ahead of FIFA World Cup
- **Sunset risk**: FCC Covered List UAS exemption scheduled to sunset 1 January 2027 absent extension

### Competitive Context
- Foreign-manufactured platforms in state/local fleets becoming non-compliant under covered-entity restrictions
- Convergent requirement: fleet replacement + new capabilities (pre-landfall atmospheric sensing) not in commercial fleet
- Federal grant dollars positioned as funding instrument for both
