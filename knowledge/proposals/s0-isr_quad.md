# S0-ISR Quad Experimentation Proposal

## Document Metadata
- Type: Experimentation proposal / capability demonstration brief
- Client/Agency: USSOCOM (U.S. Special Operations Command)
- Program/Solicitation: USSOCOM 2027 Experimentation Call
- Date: August 8, 2026
- BST Products/Systems Referenced: S0-ISR micro-UAS (air-deployed and VTOL variants)
- Key Personnel: Beck Cotter (POC), Daniel Prendergast (last editor)

## Executive Summary
Black Swift Technologies proposes to conduct an operationally relevant test of two attritable variants of the S0-ISR micro-UAS in a collaborative ISR mission. The objective is to validate cooperative mission accomplishment between two micro-UAS variants and gather user feedback for CONOPS refinement, with availability targeting April 2027.

## Technical Approach
- **System**: Two variants of the S0-ISR micro-UAS—one air-deployed and one VTOL configuration
- **Capability**: Collaborative/swarm ISR operations between attritable platforms
- **Payload**: EO/IR (electro-optical/infrared) sensor package
- **Communications**: Standard ISM bands (2.4GHz and 900MHz) for RF control and data link
- **Certification**: Self-certified to fly under FAA Part 107 rules
- **Deployment Model**: BST civilian personnel deploy with host-unit operators; aircraft can be assembled, launched, and landed in any clear outdoor location with 30-foot clearance
- **TRL Status**: Base S0 system at TRL 9; swarm and EO/IR payload upgrades in development, completion targeted for February 2027

## Products & Capabilities Described

### S0-ISR Micro-UAS
- **Description**: Attritable micro-UAS platform capable of Group 1 classification
- **Variants**: Air-deployed and VTOL (Vertical Take-Off and Landing) configurations
- **Payload**: EO/IR sensor for ISR
- **Unique Capability**: Collaborative multi-platform operation; designed for cooperative mission execution
- **Portability**: Fits in hard case with ground station, batteries, charger, and payload; deployable by two civilian personnel

## Use Cases & Applications
- Collaborative ISR missions for special operations units
- Operator feedback and system usability validation in operationally relevant environment
- CONOPS (Concept of Operations) refinement for multi-platform micro-UAS employment
- Cold/severe weather operational testing (preferred exercise condition)

## Objectives
1. Validate cooperative accomplishment of mission tasks between two attritable micro-UAS variants
2. Seek user feedback from host-unit operators
3. Refine operational concepts for deployment

## Logistics & Support Requirements
- **Personnel**: 2 BST civilian personnel to deploy and conduct experiment
- **Host Unit Support Requested**: 
  - Any unit capable of operating Group 1 UAS
  - Operator feedback team
  - Access to aviation planning and control organizations for flight deconfliction
- **Lodging & Transportation**: BST prepared to coordinate and self-fund with company resources; requests host-unit support but can support independently
- **Equipment**: Portable hard case system (aircraft, payload, ground station, batteries/charger)

## Program Risks & Mitigation
- **Risk**: Swarm and EO/IR payload upgrades currently in development
- **Mitigation**: Targeted completion February 2027; base S0 system already at TRL 9 provides fallback capability
- **Risk Mitigation Strategy**: Self-funded experiment reduces external dependency
