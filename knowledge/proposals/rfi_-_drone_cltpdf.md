# RFI - Drone CLT

## Document Metadata
- Type: Sources Sought / RFI (Request for Information)
- Client/Agency: United States Special Operations Command (USSOCOM)
- Program/Solicitation: RFI-Drone CLT (Notice ID H92408 26RFID RONECLT)
- Date: Published July 17, 2026; Response Deadline August 17, 2026
- BST Products/Systems Referenced: None explicitly mentioned in this RFI document
- Key Personnel: Amy Chauvin, HQ USSOCOM SOF AT&L KO (Government POC)

## Executive Summary
USSOCOM is conducting market research to understand industry capability to design, manufacture, and employ Group 1 and Group 2 Unmanned Aerial Systems (UAS) that can be launched from the Common Launch Tube (CLT) integrated across SOF fixed-wing air platforms. This is a pre-RFP sources-sought notice seeking white paper submissions describing technical and programmatic approaches to develop an air-launch loitering munition meeting specified performance and form-factor requirements.

## Technical Approach & Requirements
USSOCOM seeks industry responses on capability to design and manufacture an air-launch vehicle with the following specifications:

**Form Factor & Physical Constraints:**
- Employment: Release from SOF fixed-wing platforms utilizing Common Launch Tube (CLT)
- Munition weight: Not-to-exceed 55 lbs
- System form factor: 5.9-inch diameter, 42-inch maximum overall length
- Warhead/payload: Maximize kinetic warhead within NTE weight
- Payload options: Electro-optical (EO) cameras, infrared (IR) sensors, warheads, or communication equipment

**Performance Requirements:**
- Airspeed release window: 120-300 KTAS
- Altitude release window: 5,000 feet AGL to 35,000 feet MSL
- Range: Minimum 75 nautical miles from launch point to loitering point
- Loitering time: Minimum 40 minutes at altitudes between 500-3,000 feet AGL
- Cruise/loitering speed: 50-100 knots; minimum 100+ knots dash speed on attack profile
- Temperature operating range: -40°F to 135°F
- Vibration: MIL-STD-810H, Category 8, 6-Bladed C-130
- Shock: Survives 2-foot drop test
- Minimum Technology Readiness Level (TRL): 6

**Seeker & Autonomy:**
- Seeker Assembly: Passive system (limited RF emissions) with integrated Automatic Target Recognition (ATR) preferred
- Mission Profile: Pre-planned routing with altitude changes and/or direct-to loiter point
- C2 Integration: Support government-owned FANTOM Core collaborative mission autonomy via machine-to-machine (M2M) API and/or C2 integration via Battle Management System (BMS)
- BMS compatibility: Must identify compatibility with BMS Generic Canister
- Alternative Position, Navigation & Timing (AltPNT) technologies: Must describe any currently integrated, tested, and planned

**Environment:**
- Contested and non-contested operational environments with intra-platform communications
- No icing environment specified

## Requested White Paper Content
Respondents must submit unclassified white papers (maximum 10 pages) addressing:

1. Company identification (name, address, CAGE code, DUNS, POC, contact information)
2. Experience and capability with Agile practices in spiral, rapid development, engineering services, and production of SOF-peculiar munitions
3. Technical and programmatic processes for engineering design and manufacturing, with associated risk identification
4. Systems analysis & design, requirements management, peer reviews, build, installation, integration, delivery, quality, test, evaluation, and configuration management processes
5. Current Modular Open Systems Approach (MOSA) and Weapon Open Systems Architecture (WOSA) compatibility with government assessment substantiation
6. Notional timeline from past experience to deliver clean sheet design or build upon in-house capability
7. Manufacturing assessment (maturity level) and monthly manufacturing capability at startup and once production line established
8. Notional manpower requirements for design and manufacturing phases
9. Available contract vehicles (GSA Schedule, GWACs, USSOCOM contracts)

## Rough Order of Magnitude (ROM) Cost Estimate Required
Respondents must provide ROM estimate for integrating an air-launch munition onto a SOF platform for capability demonstration, addressing:
- Cost
- Schedule
- Assumptions/constraints
- Risks
- Government-furnished equipment (GFE) required
- Scope: Single demonstration evaluation on AC-130J Ghostrider or another SOF platform utilizing CLTs

## Notable Details
- **Market Research Phase**: Explicitly stated as FAR 15.101 market research—NOT a request for proposal. Responses are not offers and cannot form binding contracts. Government will not pay for information or administrative costs; information will not be returned.
- **Competition**: This RFI seeks multiple industry responses to understand competitive landscape and inform acquisition strategy.
- **Emphasis on Innovation**: Government explicitly states it seeks "true innovative, out-of-the-box thinking, conceptual approaches, and ideas on how industry can expediently design, manufacture and deliver stated capabilities."
- **Spiral Development Focus**: Emphasis on Agile practices and spiral rapid development methodology indicates the government's interest in iterative development approach rather than traditional waterfall.
- **Submission Window**: 30 days from RFI posting (through approximately August 16, 2026)
