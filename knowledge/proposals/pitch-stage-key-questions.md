## Document Purpose
This is not a proposal document itself, but rather a template or guidance document outlining the key evaluation criteria and question framework for the pitch stage of a DOE SBIR Genesis Mission solicitation. It provides the four primary evaluation categories that applicants must address.

## Evaluation Framework

### 1. Summary, Topic, and Mission Alignment (100 words max)
Required to provide:
- Layperson-friendly overview of the project
- Alignment with chosen topic area
- Alignment with DOE mission and priorities

### 2. Technical Promise (200 words max)
Must address three components:
- **Significance**: Importance and urgency of the challenge
- **Innovation**: Novelty of the proposed solution and viability as product/service
- **Feasibility**: Ability to prove concept within proposed timeline and budget
- *Note: Instructions specify avoiding deep technical jargon for non-technical evaluators*

### 3. Commercialization Potential (200 words max)
Must address three components:
- **Value Proposition**: Technology's ability to meet identified customer needs
- **Competitive Advantage**: Realistic assessment of competitive landscape
- **Go-to-Market Strategy**: Pathway to market including supply chain planning, target customers, and sales channels

### 4. Team Qualifications (200 words max)
Must address three components:
- **Company**: Relevant experience of the team
- **Partnerships**: Strength and relevance of external partners (existing or planned)
- **Financials**: Credible plan for securing follow-on funding
