## Executive Summary
Black Swift Technologies proposes a 2-year hurricane monitoring program using small fixed-wing unmanned aircraft (S0-VTOL and optionally S3) to sample atmospheric boundary layer conditions during a 14-day tropical storm scenario. Three tiered funding options ($1.4M–$2.1M) define scope: from proof-of-concept coastal operations to fully comprehensive multi-phase sampling including air-deployed inner-core profiling and offshore coverage.

## Technical Approach

**Operating Scenario:** A single hypothetical easterly African wave that becomes a landfalling U.S. hurricane, spanning 14 days:
- **Days 1–3:** No operations; clock starts
- **Days 4–6:** Barbados deployment/demobilization; pre-storm atmospheric boundary layer (ABL) sampling at 20–3,000 m altitude (T−24–48 h), in-storm ABL (20–1,500 m) at storm time (T=0); real-time data relay to NOAA centers
- **Days 7–14:** Traditional Hurricane Hunter operations (Caribbean, W. Atlantic, Gulf, landfall):
  - S0-VTOL coastal/landfall sampling within ~12 miles of coast, 12–24 hours advance warning (remote operations; unsafe inside T=12 h)
  - Air-deployed S0 from G-IV/G-III at ~40,000 ft, profiling 40k–0 ft (subject to NHOP altitude/WRA constraints)

**Platform Selection:**
- Primary: S0-VTOL (fixed-wing deep-stall with VTOL recovery)
- Long-range option: S3 (VTOL fixed-wing) for extended offshore in High tier
- Both EAR99, U.S.-built, no ITAR restrictions

## Products & Capabilities Described

### S0 / S0-VTOL
- **Type:** Fixed-wing, deep-stall vertical takeoff/landing recovery
- **Endurance:** 90 minutes
- **Ceiling:** 15,000 ft AGL
- **Cruise/wind resistance:** 17 m/s (38 mph) cruise; 30 kt wind resistance
- **Range:** ~170 nm (air-deployed glide profile)
- **Payload/weight:** 3.5 lb AUW
- **Integrated sensors:** 3-D wind, temperature, RH/dewpoint, pressure, sea surface temperature (SST)
- **Unit cost:** $18,000
- **Proposed use:** Primary platform for all operational phases; affordable attrition-tolerant profiling

### S3 (Long-range option)
- **Type:** VTOL fixed-wing
- **Endurance:** 110 min (90 min nominal)
- **Ceiling:** 20,000 ft MSL
- **Cruise/wind resistance:** 15 m/s, 30 kt wind resist
- **Range:** 110 km (60 nm)
- **Payload:** 2.7 kg (6 lb); field-swappable payloads
- **Unit cost:** $65,000
- **Proposed use:** Extended offshore ABL coverage in High tier only

## Use Cases & Applications

**Hurricane/Tropical Storm Monitoring:**
- Pre-storm atmospheric boundary layer characterization (20–3,000 m): offshore SST, relative humidity, low-level wind structure at T−24–48 h
- In-storm ABL sampling (20–1,500 m): low-level wind maxima, surface circulation center at T=0
- Coastal/landfall remote sampling (within 12 mi of coast), 12–24 h advance condition assessment
- Inner-core profiling (40,000–0 ft) from crewed G-IV/G-III platforms around circulation center (High tier only)
- Real-time data delivery to NOAA forecast centers

## Key Results / Capability Constraints by Tier

### High Tier — Fully Comprehensive ($2,100,000 / 2-year)
- **Y1 cost:** $1,260,000; **Y2 cost:** $840,000
- **Scope:** Both operational phases + air-deployed inner-core sampling from G-IV/G-III + S3 long-range offshore
- **Storm response rate:** ~3 per season
- **Profiles generated:** ~133 over 2 years
- **Cost per profile:** $9,000 (profiling sortie)
- **Deployments:** 3 per year ($50k mob/demob each)
- **NRE:** $550k Y1, $50k sustaining Y2
- **Can achieve:** Everything in Medium plus air-deployed inner-core profiling (40k–0 ft), multi-aircraft coordinated operations, S3 long-range coverage
- **Caveats:** Inner-core air-deployed ops carry highest airworthiness/integration risk and airframe attrition; subject to NHOP altitude/WRA deconfliction

### Medium Tier — Partially Comprehensive ($1,750,000 / 2-year)
- **Y1 cost:** $1,000,000; **Y2 cost:** $750,000
- **Scope:** Both operational phases (Barbados D/TS pre-storm + in-storm ABL sampling AND coastal/landfall S0-VTOL ops)
- **Storm response rate:** ~2 per season
- **Profiles generated:** ~130 over 2 years
- **Cost per profile:** $9,000
- **Deployments:** 2 per year ($45k mob/demob each)
- **NRE:** $370k Y1, $30k sustaining Y2
- **Can achieve:** Barbados forward-deployed pre-storm and in-storm ABL sampling; coastal/landfall S0-VTOL ops
- **Cannot achieve:** Air-deployed inner-core ops from G-IV/G-III; S3 long-range coverage

### Low Tier — Proof of Concept ($1,400,000 / 2-year)
- **Y1 cost:** $785,000; **Y2 cost:** $615,000
- **Scope:** One operational phase (recommend coastal/landfall S0-VTOL within 12 mi of coast, remote ops)
- **Storm response rate:** ~1 per season
- **Profiles generated:** ~119 over 2 years
- **Cost per profile:** $9,000
- **Deployments:** 1 per year ($40k mob/demob)
- **NRE:** $250k Y1, $0 Y2
- **Can achieve:** Integration foundation and one operational phase demonstration; real-time delivery to NOAA centers
- **Cannot achieve:** Barbados D/TS forward operations; air-deployed inner-core (G-IV/G-III) sampling; S3 long-range; realistically limited to ~1 storm per season

## Notable Details

**Funding Structure Notes:**
- All three tiers are 2-year, not-to-exceed totals; NOAA stated both caps and objectives remain "somewhat flexible"
- Cost-per-profile ($9,000) held constant across all tiers; differentiation via number of deployments, storm responses, NRE allocation, and operational phases
- Year 1 is NRE-heavy; Year 2 emphasizes data collection and sustaining operations
- Data collection as % of total budget: Low tier 76%, Medium 67%, High 57% (inverse relationship with integration complexity)

**Regulatory/Operational Constraints:**
- S0-VTOL coastal/landfall operations are remote (unmanned) due to unsafe conditions inside T=12 h
- Air-deployed inner-core profiling from G-IV/G-III subject to NHOP (National Hurricane Operations Program) altitude constraints and WRA (Weather Reconnaissance Area) deconfliction
- Airframe attrition higher in high-risk inner-core operations; cost model reflects per-profile airframe allocation variance

**Data Delivery:**
- Real-time ABL data stream to NOAA forecast centers across all tiers
- Sensor suite: 3-D wind, T, RH/dewpoint, pressure, SST across S0 fleet
