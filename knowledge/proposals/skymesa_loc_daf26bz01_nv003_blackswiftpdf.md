# SkyMesa Systems Letter of Commitment for S3-EW SBIR Phase I

## Document Metadata
- Type: Letter of Commitment / Subcontractor Agreement
- Client/Agency: U.S. Department of the Air Force (DAF)
- Program/Solicitation: DoW Phase I SBIR, "S3-EW: A Modular Payload Vehicle for Agile Electronic Warfare Swarms with VTOL Ground Launch from Confined Terrain"
- Date: June 2, 2026
- BST Products/Systems Referenced: S3-EW, SwiftCore, modular payload interface
- Key Personnel: Dr. Jack Elston (BST Principal Investigator and CEO), Arthur Shune (SkyMesa Founder & CEO)

## Executive Summary
SkyMesa Systems commits to serve as a subcontractor to Black Swift Technologies on a Phase I SBIR proposal for the S3-EW platform, a modular, VTOL ground-launched electronic warfare swarm vehicle. SkyMesa will contribute its Locus RF/SIGINT mission layer software to provide passive RF cueing and geolocation capabilities for the system.

## Technical Approach
SkyMesa will define and develop the RF/SIGINT mission layer for S3-EW across Phase I tasks, including:
- Passive RF cueing and bounded geolocation CONOPS (concept of operations)
- RF observation and data contracts
- Antenna placement and data-quality requirements
- Edge-compute assumptions
- TAK/CoT and JSON output concepts
- Replay/OQE (Operational Quality Evaluation) artifacts
- Phase II RF/SIGINT demonstration metrics

The Locus platform is described as software-first and hardware-agnostic, ingesting RF observations, platform pose, timing, and calibration metadata to produce confidence-scored RF cues, bounded geolocation estimates, track state, uncertainty, and machine-readable outputs.

## Products & Capabilities Described

**SkyMesa Locus (RF/SIGINT Mission Layer)**
- Software-first, hardware-agnostic RF/SIGINT processing system
- Ingests: RF observations, platform pose, timing, calibration metadata
- Outputs: confidence-scored RF cues, bounded geolocation estimates, track state, uncertainty data, machine-readable outputs for mission systems and evaluation
- Provides: passive RF cueing and geolocation when geometry supports
- Integrates with: TAK/CoT and JSON data formats

**S3-EW (Black Swift Technologies)**
- Modular payload vehicle for agile EW swarms
- VTOL ground launch capability from confined terrain
- Low-cost platform
- Modular payload interface
- Integrates with SwiftCore mission system

## Use Cases & Applications
- Electronic Warfare (EW) swarm operations
- Passive RF/SIGINT collection
- DAF (Department of Air Force) missions requiring ground-launched, modular EW platforms
- Operations from confined terrain with VTOL launch capability

## Subcontract Terms

- Phase I participation
- Support for Tasks 1, 3, 4, 5, and 7
- Contingent on proposal selection and execution of mutually acceptable subcontract
