## Technical Specifications & Capabilities

### Flight Operations
- **Hand-launching**: Not recommended
- **Net-catch landing**: Not an option for emergency ship motion scenarios
- **Flight endurance**: ~60 minutes per charge (nominal)
- **Operational cycles**: Defined as any flight with VTOL operations, regardless of duration
- **Battery life**: ~200 operational cycles before significant degradation
- **Total battery lifetime**: ~200 flight hours over usable life
- **Charge time**: ~4 hours (full charge from minimum reserved power)
- **Charging capacity**: One aircraft at a time

### Environmental Capability
- **Weather rating**: All-weather platform designed for severe environments
- **Operational conditions**: Rated for landfalling hurricanes, volcanic environments, arctic conditions
- **Marine durability**: Sea spray and marine exposure accelerate wear/corrosion; requires appropriate maintenance and handling
- **Water resistance**: Capable of sustained operations in harsh marine environments (specific IP rating not stated)

### Datalink & Communications
- **Data transmission**: Real-time telemetry streaming via SwiftCore flight management system
- **Standard range (900MHz)**: Up to 10km communication and data link range
- **Custom options**: Configurable based on frequency, antenna configuration, and operational profile
- **Multi-drone capability**: SwiftTab UI capable of multiple simultaneous drone control; onboard computer and communication suite designed for coordinating multiple aircraft, though this has not been a primary platform focus
