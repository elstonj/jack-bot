## Executive Summary
Black Swift Technologies proposes mechanical modifications to enable the S0-AD airframe to integrate with SOCOM's Pneumatic Common Launch Tube (PCLT) for launch from host aircraft. The effort includes design modifications, component fabrication/procurement, ground testing with a dummy system, and live flight testing of the test article, with an estimated total cost of $64,435.

## Technical Approach
- **Mechanical Interface Design**: Modify S0-AD airframe to enable loading and launch from PCLT, which features a fiberglass outer tube
- **Integration Scope**: Purely mechanical interface without electrical or data connection
- **Power Management**: No remote power-on capability included; assume S0 powered on prior to loading into PCLT (either in-flight or pre-takeoff)
- **Design & Testing Tasks**:
  1. Mechanical design modifications to integrate with PCLT
  2. Fabrication/procurement of components
  3. Build test article
  4. Ground test with dummy system in PCLT
  5. Live flight test of test article

## Products & Capabilities Described

**S0-AD Airframe**
- Small unmanned aerial system compatible with tube-launch mechanisms
- Proposed for modification to integrate with SOCOM's pneumatic launch system
- Expected to survive PCLT ejection intact (no structural reinforcement included in this effort)

## Use Cases & Applications
- SOCOM special operations missions utilizing pneumatic tube launch from host aircraft
- Rapid deployment scenarios requiring tube-launched UAS capability
