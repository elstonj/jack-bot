## Executive Summary
Black Swift Technologies is developing a Sonobuoy-Launched Unmanned Aerial System (SL UAS) equipped with advanced Magnetic Anomaly Detection (MAD) and acoustic sensing capabilities to address the Navy's need for persistent, multi-modal Anti-Submarine Warfare (ASW) search and detection. Phase II efforts focus on transitioning Phase I's laboratory-demonstrated magnetic noise floor performance (<20 pT/√Hz) into a production-ready, mission-validated system capable of autonomous water-landing-to-sensing transitions and coordinated multi-vehicle operations.

## Technical Approach

**Phase I Achievements:**
- Demonstrated magnetic noise floor below 20 pT/√Hz threshold in laboratory and static ground environments
- Preliminary component selection for sensor integration

**Phase II Objectives:**

**Objective 5 - Sensor-Responsive Autonomy and Coordinated Multi-Vehicle Operations:**
- Implement avionics-level interfaces and control abstractions enabling sensor-responsive autonomy
- Enable coordination between magnetometer-equipped (SØ-MAD) and acoustic-equipped (SØ-Acoustic) SL UAS platforms
- Allow acoustic-sensor detections to cue adaptive flight behaviors and trigger detailed search patterns from magnetometer-equipped platforms
- Support coordinated operations without requiring onboard Navy-specific data fusion or classification algorithms

**Objective 6 - Transition-Ready Architecture and Interfaces:**
- Define and document system interfaces, data products, and control abstractions for Navy UAS Control Segment (UCS) integration
- Develop interface control documentation (ICD)
- Prepare transition-ready technical data package consistent with Navy acquisition practices
- Enable Phase III transition activities

**Critical Technical Advancement:**
Phase II moves beyond Phase I by validating high-sensitivity MAD performance under dynamic flight maneuvers and propulsion-driven environments (addressing real-world operational conditions).

## Products & Capabilities Described

**SØ-MAD (Sonobuoy-Launched Magnetometer)**
- High-sensitivity magnetometer with noise floor <20 pT/√Hz
- Designed to fit within LAU-126A launch container constraints
- Capable of autonomous water-landing-to-sensing transitions
- Phase II: Hardened, production-representative airframe capable of launch shocks
- Enables distributed, expendable sensing for ASW applications

**SØ-Acoustic (Sonobuoy-Launched Acoustic variant)**
- Acoustic sensing payload variant of the SL UAS platform
- Provides detection cueing for magnetometer-equipped platforms
- Enables distributed sensing approach

**SL UAS (Sonobuoy-Launched Unmanned Aerial System)**
- Expendable, distributed sensing platform
- Capable of autonomous operations and coordinated multi-vehicle coordination
- Reduces detection latency and operator workload vs. traditional episodic sonobuoy sampling
- Constrained by LAU-126A launch container envelope and shock requirements

## Use Cases & Applications

**Primary Application: Anti-Submarine Warfare (ASW)**
