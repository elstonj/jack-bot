## Executive Summary
This document identifies and asserts restrictions on the U.S. Government's rights to use, release, or disclose BST technical data and computer software submitted as part of a DoD SBIR proposal on Runtime Assured Autonomy. BST claims limited/restricted rights to several proprietary systems developed at private expense, and SBIR data rights to new work developed under the proposed contract.

## Products & Capabilities Described

### SwiftCore Flight Management System
- Source code and node firmware
- Status: Proprietary, developed exclusively at private expense over the preceding decade
- Rights Claimed: Limited Rights (technical data) and Restricted Rights (computer software)

### BST WASM Static Validator and Signing Chain
- Purpose: WebAssembly validation and cryptographic signing infrastructure
- Status: Developed at private expense by BST prior to and outside this contract
- Rights Claimed: Limited Rights / Restricted Rights

### BST NixOS Base Flake and Reproducibility Manifest
- Purpose: Operating system configuration and reproducible build system
- Status: Developed at private expense by BST
- Rights Claimed: Limited Rights / Restricted Rights

### RTAA (Runtime Assured Autonomy) Architecture and Software
- Status: To be developed under the proposed SBIR contract
- Rights Claimed: SBIR Data Rights per DFARS 252.227-7018

- This is a supporting document for Volume 5 of a DoD/DAF SBIR proposal
- The document establishes that multiple BST technologies have significant prior development history (SwiftCore over a decade of private investment)
