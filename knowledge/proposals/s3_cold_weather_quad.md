# S3 Cold Weather Quad

- Type: Proposal/Experimentation Application

- Program/Solicitation: DoD USSOCOM 2027 Experimentation Call
- Date: August 7, 2026

- Key Personnel: Daniel Prendergast (last editor), Beck Cotter (POC)

## Executive Summary
Black Swift Technologies proposes to test and demonstrate the S3 VTOL fixed-wing UAS in cold weather conditions as part of USSOCOM's 2027 Experimentation Call. The objective is to validate the aircraft's performance in extreme cold temperatures (-40°C), high winds (30 knots), heavy precipitation, and prove rapid cold-weather assembly and launch capabilities with an integrated EO/IR payload.

## Technical Approach
- **System**: S3 VTOL fixed-wing UAS (currently TRL 8 at baseline)
- **Cold-weather modifications**: In development, completion targeted for February 2027
- **Payload**: EO/IR (Electro-Optical/Infrared) sensor in modular payload bay
- **Communications**: Standard 2.4GHz and 900MHz ISM bands
- **Regulatory compliance**: Self-certified to fly under Part 107 rules
- **Deployment model**: BST civilian personnel deploy with host-unit operators; aircraft assembles/launches/lands in any obstruction-free 30-foot radius outdoor location

## Products & Capabilities Described

### S3 (VTOL Fixed-Wing UAS)
- **Classification**: Group 2 UAS, VTOL (vertical takeoff and landing) fixed-wing hybrid
- **Design purpose**: Harsh environment operations
- **Baseline maturity**: TRL 8
- **Operating environment specifications**:
  - Temperature range: Down to -40°C (being validated)
  - Wind capability: Reliable launch/land in 30-knot winds
  - Precipitation: Demonstrated operation in heavy precipitation
  - Assembly time**: Rapid cold-weather assembly capability
- **Modularity**: Modular payload bay for mission customization
- **Transportability**: Fits in two hard cases (50" x 30" x 18" each) for air/ground transport

### EO/IR Payload
- Integrated into S3's modular payload bay
- Purpose: ISR (Intelligence, Surveillance, Reconnaissance) missions

## Use Cases & Applications
- **Primary use case**: Cold-weather ISR operations
- **Target operational environment**: Arctic/high-latitude harsh weather conditions
- **Operational requirements**: 
  - Rapid deployment and assembly in field conditions
  - Reliable performance in extreme cold, precipitation, and wind
  - Group 2 UAS-level tactical reconnaissance capability

## Objectives & Success Metrics
1. Validate S3 operation in temperatures down to -40°C
2. Demonstrate reliable launch and landing in 30-knot winds with heavy precipitation
3. Prove rapid assembly and launch capability in cold weather
