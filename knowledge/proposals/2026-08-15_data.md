## Executive Summary
BST conducted isolated motor testing for the S0 motor at various distances (90–200 cm) and throttle settings to assess magnetic noise compliance with a 20 pT/√Hz threshold for magnetometer applications in ASW (anti-submarine warfare) operations. Testing revealed that motor noise scales as r^-3 (inverse cube law) and passes the noise requirement at most configurations, with one notable failure at 90 cm during high cruise.

## Technical Approach
- **Test Setup:** Isolated S0 motor tested at four nose-to-sensor distances: 90 cm, 110 cm, 130 cm, and 200 cm
- **Throttle States:** Motor off, low cruise, high cruise, and throttle pump (200 cm only)
- **Test Duration:** 1 minute per configuration
- **Data Logging:** 500 Hz sampling with vector correction disabled (static testing only)
- **Measurement Basis:** Magnetic field noise in pT/√Hz (picoTesla per root Hertz)
- **Compliance Threshold:** 20 pT/√Hz

## Products & Capabilities Described

### S0 Motor
- **Function:** Propulsion motor for expendable sonobuoy-launched UAV
- **Magnetic Noise Characteristic:** Motor noise follows inverse-cube distance relationship (∝ r^-3)
- **Performance Notes:** 
  - Noise scales predictably with distance, enabling distance-based mitigation strategies
  - Low-frequency noise (below 1 Hz) dominated by geomagnetic background; requires separate evaluation approach

## Use Cases & Applications
- **Primary Mission:** Anti-submarine warfare (ASW) cued search, detection, tracking, and classification
- **Deployment Method:** Expendable launch from sonobuoys
- **Magnetometer Integration:** Motor must operate within magnetic noise budgets for onboard magnetometer sensors (20 pT/√Hz constraint)

### Key Findings
- **Single Failure Point:** 90 cm at high cruise (mean 49.2 pT/√Hz, 2.46× threshold)
- **Distance Mitigation:** At 110 cm and beyond, high-cruise noise drops to acceptable levels (10 pT/√Hz or below), demonstrating effective distance-based noise reduction
- **Motor Noise Law:** Noise follows inverse-cube distance relationship, enabling predictive modeling for sensor placement
- **Low-Frequency Limitation:** Below 1 Hz, geomagnetic background noise dominates; static testing approach may not capture dynamic compensation strategies
- **Throttle Pump Performance:** Acceptable at 200 cm (9.1 pT/√Hz)

## Notable Details
- **Testing Limitations:** Only static testing conducted (vector correction disabled); dynamic flight conditions and vector compensation not yet evaluated
- **Design Implication:** 90 cm minimum nose-to-motor distance appears insufficient at high cruise; 110+ cm separation required for full throttle operation
- **Low-Frequency Challenge:** Current 500 Hz logging and static protocol insufficient to address sub-1 Hz geomagnetic interference; future testing strategy may need to incorporate dynamic filtering or alternative assessment methods
- **Next Steps Implied:** Full system integration testing with magnetometer in flight configuration needed to validate noise compliance under operational conditions
