- Program/Solicitation: NOAA UMS (Unmanned Aircraft Systems) RFP
- Date: June 15, 2026
- BST Products/Systems Referenced: S0 (S0-VTOL), S3
- Key Personnel: Jack Elston (BST), Annette Hollingshead (NOAA contact)

## Executive Summary
BST provides revised 2-year cost estimates for NOAA hurricane observation missions across three funding tiers ($1.4M–$2.1M), using S0-VTOL and S3 unmanned aircraft to profile atmospheric boundary layer conditions before, during, and after landfalling U.S. hurricanes. The estimates map capability scope to budget caps, with the Low tier as proof-of-concept (coastal operations only), Medium tier adding offshore pre-storm and in-storm sampling, and High tier including air-deployed inner-core profiling and extended S3 coverage.

## Technical Approach

**Operational Scenario:** Single 14-day hurricane lifecycle (easterly African wave to U.S. landfall)

**Days 1–3:** No effort; storm genesis
**Days 4–6:** Deployed tropical disturbance/TS operations out of Barbados (S0-VTOL forward-deployed)
- Pre-storm ABL sampling: 20–3000 m altitude, offshore SST, RH, low-level wind structure at T−24–48 h
- In-storm ABL sampling: 20–1500 m, low-level wind maxima, surface circulation center at T=0
- Real-time data to NOAA centers

**Days 7–14:** Traditional Hurricane Hunter ops (Caribbean, W. Atlantic, Gulf, landfall)
- **(a) Coastal/landfall phase:** S0-VTOL remote operations within ~12 mi of coast, conditions forecast 12–24 h out; unsafe operations after T=12 h
- **(b) Inner-core phase:** Air-deployed S0 from G-IV/G-III manned aircraft at ~40,000 ft, profiling 40k–0 ft around circulation center (subject to NHOP altitude and WRA deconfliction constraints)

**Platform basis:**
- **Primary:** S0-VTOL (fixed-wing VTOL with deep-stall recovery capability)
- **Secondary:** S3 (long-range VTOL fixed-wing, High tier only)
- Both EAR99-classified, U.S.-built, no ITAR

## Products & Capabilities Described

### S0 / S0-VTOL
- **Type:** Fixed-wing with deep-stall / VTOL recovery
- **Endurance:** 90 minutes
- **Ceiling:** 15,000 ft AGL
- **Cruise speed:** 17 m/s (38 mph), 30 kt wind resistance
- **Range:** ~170 nm air-deployed glide profile
- **Payload/Weight:** 3.5 lb AUW with integrated meteorological sensor
- **Sensors:** 3-D wind, temperature, RH/dewpoint, pressure, SST
- **Unit cost:** $18,000
- **Use case:** Pre-storm ABL profiling (offshore), in-storm ABL sampling, coastal/landfall remote operations

### S3 (VTOL Fixed-Wing)
- **Type:** VTOL fixed-wing
- **Endurance:** 110 min (90 min nominal)
- **Ceiling:** 20,000 ft MSL
- **Cruise:** 15 m/s; resistant to 30 kt wind
- **Range:** 110 km (60 nm)
- **Payload:** 2.7 kg (6 lb), field-swappable
- **Unit cost:** $65,000
- **Use case:** Extended offshore coverage (High tier only); longer-range pre-storm monitoring

## Use Cases & Applications

**Hurricane observation and forecasting support:**
- **Pre-storm ABL characterization** (Barbados forward deployment, Days 4–6): Offshore SST, boundary layer humidity, low-level wind structure 24–48 h before storm landfall
- **In-storm ABL profiling** (Barbados forward deployment): Low-level wind maxima and surface circulation center structure at storm time
- **Coastal/landfall operations** (Days 7–14): Remote S0-VTOL operations within 12 mi of coast to sample conditions 12–24 h ahead of landfall
- **Inner-core profiling** (High tier only): Air-deployed S0 from G-IV/G-III at 40,000 ft for vertical wind and thermodynamic structure through circulation center
- **Extended offshore coverage** (High tier only): S3 for sustained offshore pre-storm monitoring and post-landfall tracking

**Mission tempo:** 1–3 storm responses per season, depending on tier

## Tier Breakdown & Capabilities

### Low Tier – Proof of Concept ($1,400,000 / 2-year NTE)
- **Scope:** Single operational phase; recommend coastal/landfall S0-VTOL operations within 12 mi of coast (remote ops only)
- **Mission tempo:** ~1 storm response/season
- **Data sets:** ~119 profiles over 2 years (@$9k per profile)
- **Deployments:** 2 total (1 per year) @$40k/deployment
- **NRE:** $250k (Year 1 only); integration foundation
- **Cost breakdown:** ~76% data acquisition, 24% deployment/integration
- **Limitations:** No Barbados D/TS forward operations; no air-deployed inner-core sampling; no S3 long-range

### Medium Tier – Partially Comprehensive ($1,750,000 / 2-year NTE)
- **Scope:** Both operational phases — Barbados D/TS pre-storm and in-storm ABL sampling PLUS coastal/landfall S0-VTOL ops
- **Mission tempo:** ~2 storm responses/season
- **Data sets:** ~130 profiles over 2 years (@$9k per profile)
- **Deployments:** 4 total (2 per year) @$45k/deployment
- **NRE:** $370k Year 1, $30k sustaining Year 2
- **Cost breakdown:** ~67% data acquisition, 33% deployment/integration
- **Limitations:** No air-deployed inner-core operations from G-IV/G-III; no S3 long-range offshore coverage

### High Tier – Fully Comprehensive ($2,100,000 / 2-year NTE)
- **Scope:** Medium tier capabilities PLUS air-deployed S0 from G-IV/G-III around circulation center (40k–0 ft vertical profiling) and S3 long-range offshore coverage
- **Mission tempo:** ~3 storm responses/season
- **Data sets:** ~133 profiles over 2 years (@$9k per profile)
- **Deployments:** 6 total (3 per year) @$50k/deployment
- **NRE:** $550k Year 1, $50k sustaining Year 2
- **Cost breakdown:** ~57% data acquisition, 43% deployment/integration
- **Aircraft:** Multi-aircraft coordinated ops (S0-VTOL, S3, G-IV/G-III)
- **Caveats:** Air-deployed inner-core ops carry highest airworthiness/integration risk and airframe attrition; 40k-ft G-IV/G-III deployment subject to NHOP altitude and WRA deconfliction constraints; may require elevated per-profile airframe cost for inner-core subset

## Notable Details

- **Budget structure:** All three tiers are 2-year not-to-exceed totals; NOAA noted both caps and objectives remain "somewhat flexible"
- **Cost-per-profile:** Consistent at $9,000 across all tiers, suggesting economies of scale in data collection
- **NRE philosophy:** High tier front-loads integration costs (Y1: $550k) with reduced sustaining costs (Y2: $50k); Low tier minimizes initial integration ($250k, Y1 only)
- **Deployment model:** Mob/Demob ranges from $40–50k per deployment depending on tier; increases with operational complexity
- **Airworthiness/risk:** Inner-core air-deployed operations (High tier) flagged as highest-risk integration area, requiring airframe attrition budgeting and subject to operational constraints (NHOP, WRA deconfliction)
- **Real-time data delivery:** All tiers include real-time data transmission to NOAA forecast centers
- **Regulatory compliance:** Both S0 and S3 are EAR99 (no ITAR), U.S.-built; operates within NHOP and WRA frameworks
- **Scalability:** Profile count scales modestly (119→133) across tiers despite 50% budget increase, indicating deployment frequency and mission complexity (not sensor payload) as primary cost drivers
