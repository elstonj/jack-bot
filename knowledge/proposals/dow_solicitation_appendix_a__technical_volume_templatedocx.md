## Executive Summary
This is a template and instruction document for completing Phase I Technical Volume proposals in response to DoW SBIR/STTR solicitations. It provides formatting requirements, section structure guidance, and compliance instructions for small business contractors submitting proposals to the Department of War.

## Document Purpose
This is a **procedural/administrative document**, not a BST proposal or capability brief. It serves as the official template that small businesses (including BST if submitting to this solicitation) must follow when preparing Phase I technical proposals.

## Key Required Sections for Proposals Using This Template

1. **Identification and Significance of the Problem or Opportunity** - Define technical problem/opportunity and importance
2. **Phase I Technical Objectives** - Enumerate specific objectives and research questions
3. **Phase I Statement of Work** - Detailed approach, tasks, schedule, deliverables
4. **Related Work** - Prior/similar activities with client references and completion dates
5. **Relationship with Future Research** - Anticipated Phase II foundation and required clearances
6. **Commercialization Strategy** - Market need, market size, quantitative results schedule (~1 page)
7. **Key Personnel** - Principal investigator and team qualifications, education, publications, patents
8. **Foreign Citizens** - Disclosure of foreign nationals or dual citizens involved
9. **Facilities/Equipment** - Instrumentation, physical facilities, environmental compliance statement
10. **Subcontractors/Consultants** - SBIR/STTR compliance with cost allocation percentages
11. **Prior/Current/Pending Support** - Disclosure of similar proposals elsewhere
12. **Restrictions on Government Use** - Technical data and IP assertions per DFARS

## Format Requirements
- Single column, single-spaced
- 8.5" x 11" paper, 1" margins
- Minimum 10-point font (smaller allowed in headers, footers, tables, figures)
- No encrypted files or embedded active media (videos, animations)
- Page limits vary by Component (must verify topic-specific instructions)
- Header format: Company name, topic number, DSIP-assigned proposal number

## Disclosure & IP Protection
- Proposers may mark data as "not for public disclosure" for evaluation purposes only
- First page legend required for restricted data with page numbers identified
- Subsequent pages must be marked with restriction legend
- Upon contract award, Government retains royalty-free license for Government purposes for 20 years post-completion
- After 20 years, Government gains unlimited rights to SBIR data
- DFARS 252.227-7018 (JAN 2025) governs technical data rights

## Compliance Notes
- SBIR: Minimum 2/3 of Phase I work (by cost) must be performed by proposing small business
  - Subcontracts with universities and FFRDCs permitted without waiver
  - Federal lab subcontracts permitted but must be certified
- STTR: Minimum 40% by small business, 30% by single Research Institution (by cost)
  - FFRDCs permitted without waiver
  - Federal labs only within remaining 30%, must be certified
- Human/animal subjects or recombinant DNA research: Must identify applicable protocols; funds cannot be released until approvals obtained
- Universities cannot publicly release Export Control/ITAR-restricted information
