# Ship-Deployable S3 VTOL Operations: Seeding the Autonomous Fleet Initiative

## Document Metadata
- Type: White paper / proposal (seed funding request)
- Client/Agency: NOAA OMAO / Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC)
- Program/Solicitation: UxSOC semi-annual request-for-projects cycle; positioned for FY2027 autonomous fleet initiative
- Date: 6 August 2026 (created/modified 2026-08-07)
- BST Products/Systems Referenced: S3 VTOL UAS, S2 science airframe, SwiftCore FMS
- Key Personnel: Jack Elston (last editor), Daniel Pendergast (point of contact)

## Executive Summary
Black Swift proposes a $950,000 FY2026 year-end seed project to demonstrate shipboard deployment of the S3 VTOL UAS across two NOAA research cruises, positioning the aircraft as the organic aerial survey asset for NOAA's planned autonomous fleet initiative funded in FY2027. The demonstration would include ship-integration engineering, airworthiness documentation, and reusable deck procedures applicable across NOAA's vessel fleet.

## Technical Approach
- Deploy two S3 VTOL systems with deck-handling kits and marinized ground stations aboard NOAA research vessels
- Three field-swappable payload configurations: atmospheric profiling, electro-optical (EO) survey, and sea-surface temperature (SST)/radiometric sensors
- SwiftCore FMS provides sensor-driven autonomous flight, reducing operator workload to launch-and-monitor
- Two at-sea demonstration cruises: one supporting fisheries/protected-species survey, one supporting hydrographic/coastal mapping
- Develop ship-integration engineering package with deck procedures and airworthiness/deconfliction documentation for fleet-wide reuse
- Generate concept-of-employment report informing OMAO's FY2027 autonomous-fleet execution

## Products & Capabilities Described

### S3 VTOL UAS
- **Description**: Vertical takeoff and landing variant of proven S2 science airframe
- **Key Specs**: 20,000 ft ceiling, 110-minute endurance, 5 lb enclosed modular payload capacity
- **Advantage Over Fixed-Wing**: Eliminates need for launchers and recovery nets (traditional constraints for ship operations)
- **Portability**: Packs into small cases transportable on commercial aviation; enables rapid embarkation on ships of opportunity
- **Payload Flexibility**: Field-swappable nose-cone payloads support multiple mission areas (atmospheric, EO, SST, hydrographic)
- **Proposed Cost (this project)**: $560,000 for two systems with three modular payload sets

### SwiftCore FMS
- **Description**: Flight Management System enabling sensor-driven autonomous flight
- **Capability**: Aircraft adjusts its own flight path based on data collection needs, reducing shipboard operator workload

## Use Cases & Applications
1. **Fisheries and Protected-Species Survey Augmentation**: One demonstration cruise providing aerial reconnaissance for NOAA Fisheries (NMFS) missions
2. **Hydrographic and Coastal Mapping Augmentation**: One demonstration cruise supporting National Ocean Service (NOS) hydrographic missions
3. **Atmospheric Profiling**: Payload configuration for OAR (Office of Atmospheric Research) missions
4. **Sea-Surface Temperature and Radiometric Sensing**: SST payload for oceanographic measurements
5. **Multi-Mission Support**: Single airframe adaptable across NOS, NMFS, and OAR mission areas

### Editor's Redline Notes (Critical Caveats)
The document includes extensive editor notes (Jack Elston) flagging significant technical and capability concerns:

**Cold Weather Qualification**:
- S2 Arctic/Greenland heritage exists and Cold-weather JIATF (CJFC) funded work substantiates cold-weather claims
- CJFC qualification effort has grown to ~2,050 billable hours just to reach -40°C
- Two shipboard S3 systems in 12-month PoP would compete for same engineering resources

**Shipboard Operations Risk** (flagged as critical):
- Precision recovery to pitching/rolling/heaving deck inside ship's air wake is "a distinct engineering problem we have not demonstrated"
- Deck-motion compensation, touchdown accuracy, tie-down, and handling "are nowhere in the $160K integration line"
- Datasheet 30-knot wind rating applies to steady wind, not actual deck operations
- Aircraft is IP42 rated (drip protection only); salt fog, green water, and stack exhaust require IP54+ qualification (future work)

**Mission Capability Gaps**:
- **Bathymetry**: "Out of our expertise"—survey-grade bathymetry from Group 2 airframe requires lidar or photogrammetry; 6 lb payload and 110-minute endurance insufficient
- **Marine/Fisheries Survey**: BST has no NOS or NMFS heritage; editorial note recommends cutting both applications
- **Ground Station Marinization**: Ground station "is the easy half"; the aircraft itself lacks marinization for ship deck environment

**Alternative Recommendation**:
- Editor notes existing S0-VTOL concept from R/V Ron Brown ($100K, awaiting detail) already in pipeline—raised by OMAO on 6 May call
- Recommends re-cutting proposal around proven S0-VTOL concept and "holding the S3 back until CJFC qualification produces something we can point at"
