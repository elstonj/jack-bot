# Krateo Sky Capital Commitment

## Document Metadata
- Type: Letter of Strategic Support
- Client/Agency: NASA SBIR/STTR Program
- Program/Solicitation: NASA SBIR Phase II
- Date: 13 May 2026
- BST Products/Systems Referenced: SwiftCore
- Key Personnel: Jack Elston (BST), Scott Wine (KrateoSky CEO), Robert Smith (KrateoSky President of Defense, CAPT US Navy retired)

## Executive Summary
KrateoSky LLC provides a letter of strategic support for Black Swift Technologies' NASA SBIR Phase II proposal on "Adaptive and Secure Autonomy for UAS: A Modular Approach to Flight Control, Machine Learning Integration, and Multi-Vehicle Coordination." KrateoSky confirms an active strategic partnership with BST and commits to supporting commercialization of BST's UAS autonomy technologies through investment and resources.

## Technical Approach
The letter references BST's proposed innovation under SwiftCore for adaptive and secure autonomy in UAS, encompassing:
- Flight control systems
- Machine learning integration
- Multi-vehicle coordination capabilities

No specific technical details are provided in this support letter.
