## Executive Summary
This is a standardized questionnaire form (CJFC-CW-001) issued by Canadian Joint Forces Command to collect vendor responses regarding cold weather and Arctic ISR platform capabilities. It is designed to replace standalone whitepapers and capture comprehensive data on platform specifications, Arctic operational performance, ISR suite capabilities, logistics, supply chain risk, and engineering effort estimates for Arctic deployment.

## Document Structure & Key Evaluation Areas

### Section 1: Vendor Identification
Basic company and point-of-contact information.

### Section 2: Platform Identification
- Platform name, model, designation
- Weight (kg), MTOW (kg)
- Configuration details
- Technology Readiness Level (TRL 1-9) with justification

### Section 3: Cold Weather Specifications
- Operational temperature floor and ceiling (°C)
- Max sustained wind capability (knots)
- Cold weather testing documentation (max 100 words): test environment type, duration, conditioning protocol, verifying authority
- List of integrated cold-weather features (max 100 words): heaters, insulation, anti-icing, battery management, fluids, kits

### Section 4: ISR Capability
- Platform ISR suite description (sensors, payloads, datalinks, processing)
- Platform cost estimate without ISR suite (CAD)
- Platform cost estimate with ISR suite (CAD)

### Section 5: Endurance & Range Under Arctic Conditions
- Endurance (hours)
- Range (km)
- Operating temperature at which tested (°C)
- Payload-dependent variants and endurance/range trade-offs

### Section 6: Navigation & Comms in GPS-Degraded Environments
- Alternative navigation methods
- Beyond Line-of-Sight (BLOS) communications capability
- Jamming/spoofing resilience (max 100 words)

### Section 7: Logistics & Sustainment Burden
- Deployment footprint description
- Crew size requirements
- Ground Support Equipment (GSE)
- Consumables (max 100 words)

### Section 8: Interoperability & Open Architecture (MOSA)
- Standards compliance (STANAG, FACE, MOSA, open data formats)

### Section 9: Supply Chain & Sovereign Risk
- Country of origin for critical components
- ITAR/CGP exposure assessment
- Sanctions risk posture (max 100 words)

### Section 10: Manufacturing Capacity & Delivery Timelines
- Current production rate (units/year)
- Standard lead time (weeks)
- Production scalability assessment

### Section 11: Operational Concept Fit
- Alignment with CJFC Arctic ISR CONOPS (max 150 words)

### Section 12: Engineering Effort Estimate
- Vendor assessment of LOE for current state to Arctic-capable (max 250 words)
- Engineering modifications, components, integrations, labor, materials, testing, timeline

### Section 13: Red Flags / Program Risk Declaration
- Vendor self-disclosure of known risks, limitations, or program issues (max 100 words)

### Section 14: Certification
- Authorized signer certification statement; signature and date required
