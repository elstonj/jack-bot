- **Sponsor:** Department of the Navy
- **Period:** 01/2027 – 06/2030
- **Total Amount:** $1,397,610
- **Person Months:** 0.15 (2027), 0.15 (2028), 0.17 (2029), 0.17 (2030)
- **Objective:** Phase II transitions technical feasibility from Phase I into production-ready, mission-validated Sonobuoy-Launched Unmanned Aerial System (SL UAS). Coordinates SØ-MAD and SØ-Acoustic variants to reduce detection latency and operator workload versus traditional episodic sonobuoy sampling.

### 6. Expendable Air-Sea Profiling Observations in Hazardous Weather Conditions via Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (Phase II)
- **Sponsor:** Department of the Navy
- **Period:** 01/2027 – 12/2030
- **Total Amount:** $2,000,000
- **Person Months:** 0.17 (2027), 0.15 (2028), 0.13 (2029), 0.13 (2030)
- **Objective:** Transition S0 from proven NOAA research platform to fully integrated, turnkey DOW operational capability. Seven key objectives:
  1. **Calibration and Validation:** Over-ocean flight campaigns to validate base sensing accuracy and responsiveness against buoy and tower data
  2. **Turnkey DOW Operation:** Web-based, low-cognitive-load user interface and automated data quality control
  3. **Real-Time Onboard Processing:** Upgrade S0 compute architecture with heterogeneous dual-core System-on-Chip (STM32MP1) to run turbulence and wave-state algorithms natively with real-time data transmission
  4. **Forecast Improvements:** Use S0 data to improve turbulent mixing parameterizations and enhance track/intensity forecasts; assimilate into models to evaluate boundary-layer structures
  5. **Extreme Environment Hardening:** Mitigate extreme cold (-65°F) and icing using heaters on pressure ports and advanced chemical coatings
  6. **Autonomous Adaptive Sampling:** Implement closed-loop AI using Temporal Multimodal Multivariate Learner and IT-RRT planner for real-time flight path optimization to maximize information gain in high-uncertainty storm regions
  7. **DOW Integration:** Adapt S0 for compatibility with Navy Common Launch Tubes (CLT) and A-size sonobuoy canisters for P-8 and MH-60 deployment
- **Products Referenced:** S0 UAS, STM32MP1 System-on-Chip
- **Overlap Statement:** None

## Products & Capabilities Described

### S0 UAS (Small Unmanned Aircraft System)
- **Description:** Proven atmospheric research and operational platform capable of expendable deployment from Navy platforms
- **Applications:** Air-sea profiling, hurricane observation, atmospheric refractivity measurement, weather data collection
- **Key Capabilities:**
  - Atmospheric data collection (higher-resolution, more accurate than radiosondes)
  - Real-time onboard processing with STM32MP1 System-on-Chip
  - Turbulence and wave-state algorithm execution
  - Extreme cold (-65°F) and icing resilience
  - Compatible with Navy Common Launch Tubes and A-size sonobuoy canisters
  - Autonomous adaptive sampling with AI path optimization

### SØ-MAD (S0 Magnetic Anomaly Detection)
- **Description:** S0 variant configured for magnetic anomaly detection
- **Application:** Anti-Submarine Warfare (ASW) search and detection
- **Key Specification:** Magnetic noise floor <20 pT/√Hz
- **Launch System:** LAU-126A launch container with extreme volumetric and magnetic constraints
- **Capability:** Persistent multi-modal ASW sensing with reduced detection latency

### SØ-Acoustic (S0 Acoustic Sensing)
- **Description:** S0 variant with passive acoustic sensing capability
- **Application:** Anti-Submarine Warfare (ASW) detection
- **Capability:** Distributed sensing reducing operator workload vs. episodic sonobuoy sampling
- **Water Landing:** Autonomous water-landing-to-sensing transitions

### SwiftCore Flight Management System
- **Description:** BST's flight-proven autopilot and autonomy architecture
- **Phase II Objective:** Advance from benchtop-validated to flight-validated, customer-deployable research platform
- **Key Innovation:** Safe Sandbox supervisor—deterministic runtime-assurance arbiter with isolation-enforcing module host
- **Ecosystem:** Hardware-in-the-loop, software-in-the-loop simulation, faster-than-real-time Monte Carlo capability
- **Capability:** Enables third-party algorithm development from concept
