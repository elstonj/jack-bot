# Aureum Phase II LOI - Collaborative Distributed Swarm Radar

## Document Metadata
- Type: Letter of Intent (LOI)
- Client/Agency: Aureum LLC
- Program/Solicitation: DoW SBIR topic OSW26BZ05-DV019
- Date: 2026-09-17
- BST Products/Systems Referenced: S3 platform
- Key Personnel: Jack Elston (CEO, Black Swift Technologies); Michael Shields (CEO and Co-Founder, Aureum); Beck Cotter (last editor)

## Executive Summary
Black Swift Technologies expresses interest in supporting Aureum's Phase II SBIR effort on a collaborative distributed swarm radar system. BST would provide UAS platforms, payload integration, flight operations, and field testing support using its S3 aircraft for a testbed of three or more radar-equipped aircraft.

## Technical Approach
BST proposes to leverage its expertise in small UAS platforms and payload integration to support Aureum's swarm radar testbed. The primary approach would involve:
- Utilizing BST's S3 platform as the UAS carrier
- Integrating small radar modules onto multiple aircraft
- Providing flight operations and field testing in challenging environments
- Testbed scale: three or more aircraft minimum

## Products & Capabilities Described

**S3 Platform**
- Small uncrewed aircraft system
- Capable of carrying radar payload modules
- Suitable for distributed swarm operations
- Can operate in challenging operational environments

## Use Cases & Applications
- Collaborative distributed swarm radar testbed development
- Multi-platform radar operation
- Ocean/offshore applications (implied by DoW/OSW topic designation)

## Notable Details
- This is a preliminary expression of interest; the LOI explicitly states it "does not constitute a commitment of resources or a binding agreement"
- Scope, schedule, and price for Phase II support to be negotiated
- BST's role is as a subcontractor/partner providing UAS and integration services to Aureum's prime effort
- Focus on practical field testing and operational integration rather than radar algorithm development
