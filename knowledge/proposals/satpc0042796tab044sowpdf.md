## Executive Summary
NASA Langley Research Center is procuring two (2) Black Swift S0 UAS Flight Systems with associated ground control equipment and flight operations support to conduct atmospheric thermodynamic measurements including wind, pressure, temperature, and humidity data collection.

## Technical Approach
The procurement includes fully assembled S0 UAS systems configured for atmospheric measurement missions. Operations will be managed through a Ground Control Station with SwiftTab UI interface running on a Samsung Galaxy Tab Active2 tablet. BST will provide flight operations support including qualified operator and observer/technician for full-day operations.

## Products & Capabilities Described

### S0 UAS Flight System
- **What it is:** Fully assembled unmanned aerial system designed for atmospheric thermodynamic measurements
- **Measurement capabilities:** Wind, pressure, temperature, and humidity
- **Configuration:** Includes charger; two units procured
- **Deployment context:** Atmospheric research and flight operations at NASA Langley

### Ground Control Station (S0 GCS)
- **What it is:** Ground control station for S0 UAS operations
- **Interface:** SwiftTab UI running on Samsung Galaxy Tab Active2 tablet
- **Quantity:** One unit
- **Includes:** Tablet setup and SwiftTab UI installation

### Flight Operations Support
- **Scope:** Full-day flight operations support
- **Staffing:** Operator and observer/technician
- **Duration:** As needed for experiments
