## Executive Summary
Black Swift Technologies expresses interest in supporting Aureum LLC's Phase II SBIR effort for a collaborative distributed swarm radar system. BST would provide UAS expertise, platform support (S3), payload integration, and flight operations for a testbed of three or more aircraft carrying small radar modules.

## Technical Approach
- Utilize BST's S3 UAS platform as the primary airborne vehicle
- Integrate small radar modules onto multiple aircraft (testbed of 3+ platforms)
- Provide full flight operations support
- Provide payload integration engineering and support
- Conduct field testing in challenging operational environments
- Specific scope, schedule, and pricing to be negotiated in Phase II contract
