## Executive Summary
This proposal seeks $850,000 in FY26 one-year funds from FEMA/DHS S&T to develop a rapid-response atmospheric sensing capability for hurricane landfall and disaster operations. Black Swift proposes to stage three integrated rapid-response kits (S0-VTOL profilers + S3 EO survey systems) at FEMA logistics centers in hurricane-prone regions, integrate data feeds into FEMA's common operating picture, and provide training and on-call support for the 2027 hurricane season.

## Technical Approach

**Problem Statement:**
- In the 72 hours around hurricane landfall, responders operate with degraded situational awareness: surface stations fail, crewed aviation is grounded, and low-altitude wind/hazard data becomes unavailable precisely when pre-positioning and SAR decisions are made.
- NOAA has demonstrated rapid-response uncrewed surface vehicles but no equivalent rapidly deployable atmospheric aerial layer exists on the FEMA side.

**Proposed Solution:**
- Deploy S0-VTOL for vertical launch wind profiling to 15,000 ft from parking lots/staging areas in conditions that ground crewed aviation
- Deploy S3 VTOL with 5 lb modular EO payload for post-landfall damage reconnaissance, infrastructure assessment, and search-area survey
- Leverage launch-and-forget autonomy enabling emergency-management operators (not UAS specialists) to generate data on day one
- Utilize U.S.-manufactured systems compliant with federal covered-entity UAS restrictions

## Products & Capabilities Described

### S0-VTOL
- **What it is:** Vertical-takeoff VTOL platform
- **Proposed use:** Wind profiling to 15,000 ft altitude
- **Key capabilities:** 
  - Launches from parking lots or staging areas
  - Flyable in conditions that ground crewed aviation
  - Proven operational history inside tropical cyclones with NOAA
  - Autonomous operation requiring no specialized UAS pilot

### S3 (S3 VTOL)
- **What it is:** Vertical-takeoff VTOL platform with modular EO payload
- **Proposed use:** Post-landfall damage reconnaissance, infrastructure assessment, search-area survey
- **Specifications:**
  - 5 lb modular EO payload capacity
  - Commercial-air-transportable cases for rapid deployment

## Use Cases & Applications

1. **Pre-Landfall Wind Profiling:** S0-VTOL generates atmospheric wind data during the critical 72-hour pre-landfall window when crewed aviation is grounded
2. **Post-Landfall Damage Reconnaissance:** S3 conducts EO survey for damage assessment and infrastructure evaluation
3. **Search and Rescue:** S3 supports search-area surveying for post-disaster SAR operations
4. **Pre-Positioning Decision Support:** Data feeds into FEMA common operating picture to support critical resource pre-positioning decisions
5. **Airspace Integration:** Supports National Exercise participation and real-world 2027 hurricane season activations

## Proposed Scope & Deliverables

1. **Three rapid-response kits** staged at FEMA logistics centers in hurricane-prone regions (each kit includes S0-VTOL profiling system + S3 EO survey system, payloads, ground stations, and transportable cases)
2. **COP data integration** of atmospheric feeds into FEMA common operating picture tools with wind-hazard products tailored to pre-landfall decision timelines
3. **Exercise participation** in one national-level exercise plus on-call activation support for 2027 hurricane season
4. **Training** for designated FEMA and urban search-and-rescue operators

## Notable Details & Issues

**Strengths:**
- Bounded, non-severable requirement suited to expiring one-year FY26 funds
- First customer relationship with FEMA (new agency expansion in BST portfolio)
- Addresses demonstrated capability gap (NOAA has surface vehicles; no atmospheric equivalent for FEMA)
- U.S.-manufactured, compliant with federal covered-entity UAS restrictions (advantage over commercial drone fleet)
- Proven operational history: S0-VTOL and S3 already demonstrated inside tropical cyclones with NOAA

**Internal Critique (per redline notes from editor):**
- **Airspace Access Risk:** Proposed language claiming aircraft "flyable in conditions that ground crewed aviation" is misleading. Post-landfall airspace is TFR'd (Temporary Flight Restriction), saturated with crewed SAR, and controlled by incident air ops branch. The conditions that ground crewed aviation are precisely when a 15,000 ft UAS profile will NOT be approved. Proposal should be reframed around pre-landfall and post-event windows where clearance is obtainable.
- **Sustainment Staffing Issue:** Cannot provide standing duty across FEMA regions for a full season with 12-14 FTE in Boulder. Either price a real sustainment model or scope to scheduled exercise support only.

**Funding Timeline:**
- Period of Performance: 12 months (kit build, exercise integration, 2027-season on-call capability)
- Obligation path: FY26 one-year funds expiring 30 September 2026
