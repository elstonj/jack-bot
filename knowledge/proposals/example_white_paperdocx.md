## Executive Summary
This white paper proposes a demonstration of novel lightweight water purification products—the Waterstick™ (personal device) and Waterbox™ (small-unit system)—utilizing Seldon Technologies' patented Nanomesh™ filtration media. Both systems are designed to provide USEPA-compliant drinking water from fresh water sources in austere military conditions without chemicals, heat, UV light, or extensive training.

## Technical Approach
**Core Technology: Nanomesh™ Media**
- Patented filtration technology combining paper technology and functionalized carbon nanotubes (CNT)
- Scalable reel-to-reel manufacturing process
- Properties: extreme surface area increase, enhanced strength, high electrical conductivity, rapid heat transport
- Removes bacteria, virus, Cryptosporidium, and Giardia to USEPA Drinking Water Standards
- No contact time, high pressure, chemicals, heat, or UV light required

## Products & Capabilities Described

### Waterstick™ (Personal Water Filter)
- **Capacity:** 70 gallons total; ~6 oz per minute flow rate
- **Design:** Compact two-stage design
- **Features:** Bite valve or hand pump for point-of-use filtration
- **Weight:** ~1.1 lbs (wet)
- **Applications:** Stand-alone use, integration with hydration pack, gravity-fed filtration
- **Performance:** Dependent on input water quality

### Waterbox™ (Mobile Point-of-Use System)
- **Capacity:** Up to 1,200 gallons total; ~0.5 gallons per minute
- **Design:** Two-filter, self-contained, mobile configuration
- **Power Options:** 12V DC, 24V DC, 115V AC, 230V AC, or manual pump
- **Weight:** 54 lbs per unit
- **Dimensions:** ~4 cubic feet per unit
- **Setup:** Quick setup, simple operation, no waiting time for water production
- **Performance:** Dependent on input water quality

### Watertap™ (Fixed Systems)
- Mentioned but not proposed for Talisman Sabre '11 demonstration
- Fixed point-of-use systems for residential/commercial applications

## Use Cases & Applications
- **Primary Mission:** Austere military operations requiring remote drinking water
- **Exercise:** Talisman Sabre '11 (international military exercise)
- **Deployment Context:** Support for ground personnel in field conditions
- **Scenarios:** Squad-level hydration support (Waterbox™), individual soldier hydration (Waterstick™)

## Logistics & Deployment Details
- **Field Team:** 1-2 scientists for demonstration
- **Waterbox™ Requirements:** 
  - Priming height ≤14 feet
  - Fresh (not salt/brackish) water source
  - Reservoir for product water storage
  - Electrical power (multiple voltage options available)
- **Transportation:** Minimal shipping burden; equipment returned post-exercise
- **Scalability:** Up to four Waterbox™ units can be deployed (216 lbs, ~16 cubic feet total)
