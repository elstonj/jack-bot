## Executive Summary
Black Swift Technologies proposes to provide pilot/operator support and two S0 VTOL aircraft for a 45-day field deployment as part of the TEPEX Field Campaign, operating between Honolulu and American Samoa under NOAA's UxS Operations Center program.

## Technical Approach
BST will deploy 2 S0 VTOL platforms for atmospheric/oceanographic data collection during the TEPEX field campaign. The deployment involves:
- One BST pilot/operator for the full 45-day duration
- Aircraft operation at $250/day per aircraft
- Support equipment, aircraft consumables, and platform shipping logistics

## Products & Capabilities Described

### S0 VTOL
- Vertical takeoff and landing unmanned aircraft system
- Proposed quantity: 2 platforms
- Cost basis: $250/day per aircraft for 45-day deployment = $22,500
- Capability: Atmospheric/oceanographic measurement platform for tropical ocean research

- **Scientific focus**: Apparent involvement in NOAA atmospheric and oceanographic research
