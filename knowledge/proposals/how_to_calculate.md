## Domestic Content Calculation Methodology

**65% Domestic Threshold Formula:**
- **Numerator:** Total cost of U.S. manufactured components
- **Denominator:** Total cost of all components
- Result must equal or exceed 65% to meet standard

**For U.S.-Manufactured Components, include:**
- All manufacturing costs
- Transportation costs to incorporation point
- Allocable overhead costs (facility expenses, machinery depreciation, indirect labor, quality control, manufacturing supplies, general production overhead)

**For Purchased Components, include:**
- Acquisition cost
- Transportation costs to incorporation point
- Applicable duties

**Explicitly Excluded:**
- Entity profit margin on manufactured components
- Costs associated with manufacturing the end product itself

## Key Compliance Details
- No waivers or exemptions normally applicable to Buy American Standard may be used to meet the 65% threshold
- All components must be evaluated for cost, regardless of whether they are designated as "critical"
- Calculation applies to end product as a whole
