## Executive Summary
This is a standard template and instruction document issued by the Department of the Navy for completing Phase I Final Reports under the SBIR/STTR program. It prescribes the format, content requirements, submission procedures, and page limits for all Navy SBIR/STTR Phase I final reports across all Systems Commands (SYSCOMs).

## Document Purpose & Scope
This document does not describe a specific Black Swift Technologies project, proposal, or capability. Rather, it is a **government-issued template** that BST (or any SBIR/STTR Phase I contractor) must use when submitting final reports to the Navy. The document found in the BST file system indicates that this template was being used to guide preparation of a Phase I final report for CLIN 0007 (Line Item) under a Navy STTR contract related to Hazardous Weather.

## Key Requirements for Phase I Final Reports

### Format & Structure
- **Total length:** Not to exceed 35 pages (excluding unlimited Appendices A and B)
- **Paper:** 8.5" x 11", 1-inch margins, Times New Roman 12-point font
- **File format:** Single Adobe PDF
- **Header requirement:** Company name, report date, on every page
- **Footer requirement:** Topic number, contract number, page number on every page

### Required Sections (within 35-page limit)
1. **Title Page** (1 page max)
2. **Table of Contents** (1 page max)
3. **Executive Summary** (1 page max) – findings, problem, results, conclusions, recommended actions
4. **Report Body** (32 pages max) containing:
   - Project Objective (2-3 sentences: technology and intended Navy use)
   - Project Approach (physical/analytical models, basic science, validation experiments)
   - Analysis (how objectives achieved, Navy applicability, models used)
   - Results (organized data, tables, diagrams, system overviews)
   - Conclusions (substantiated findings, implications, independent interpretation)
   - Recommendations (numbered/bulleted course of action)
   - Proposed Additional SBIR/STTR Funded Research (Phase II description if applicable)
   - Transition and Acquisition Planning (transition plan, Programs of Record interest)
   - References

### Optional Appendices (unlimited pages)
- **Appendix A:** Key terms and acronyms
- **Appendix B:** Raw data, detailed computations, supporting documentation

## Submission Requirements
- Submit through Navy SBIR/STTR Firm Portal: https://www.navysbirprogram.com/navydeliverables
- Upload as "Contract Deliverable" with Deliverable Type: "Final Report – Distribution B"
- Must comply with Phase I contract timelines/deadlines
- Standard Form 298 (SF298) required for DTIC submission (but NOT required for Navy portal submission)

## Data Rights & Classification
- **SBIR/STTR Data Rights Period:** 20 years from contract award date
- **Government Rights:** Royalty-free license for Government purposes during 20-year period; unlimited rights thereafter
- **Marking Required:** DFARS 252.227-7018 Class Deviation 2020-O0007 restrictive legend
- **Distribution:** Distribution Statement B – U.S. Government agencies only (unless contractor permits otherwise)
- **Export Control:** May apply (ITAR/EAR restrictions noted if applicable)
