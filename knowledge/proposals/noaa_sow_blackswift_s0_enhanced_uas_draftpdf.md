## Executive Summary
NOAA is acquiring six (6) enhanced Black Swift Technologies S0 uncrewed aircraft systems to enable beyond-line-of-sight operations in tropical cyclones. The enhancement adds satellite communication and/or land-based command-and-control capability plus video imaging, allowing the S0 to operate independently of the parent P-3 aircraft while collecting atmospheric data in extreme weather environments.

## Technical Approach
BST will design, develop, and integrate satellite communication and/or land-based C2 systems onto six baseline S0 platforms, addressing the critical technical challenge of maintaining reliable communications in the high-attenuation environment of tropical cyclones (high winds, heavy precipitation, RF interference). The approach includes:
- Research, development, engineering, and integration effort
- Signal-attenuation mitigation strategies specific to hurricane conditions
- Video imaging integration compatible with the communication architecture
- Acceptance testing representative of operational environment

## Products & Capabilities Described

### S0 Uncrewed Aircraft System
- **What it is:** Air-deployed, precision atmospheric measurement UAS; lightest platform successfully used to sample tropical cyclones (~1.2 kg / 2.6 lbs)
- **Current capabilities:**
  - Maximum flight endurance: ≥120 minutes under standard conditions
  - Integrated sensor suite: air temperature, wind speed/direction, dewpoint, atmospheric pressure
  - Pivotable-wing design compatible with P-3 drop-tube deployment
  - Deep-stall recovery capability
  - Autonomous flight with automated sampling patterns
  - Ability to operate in hurricane-force winds with heavy precipitation
  - U.S. designed and manufactured
- **Baseline unit price:** $18,000 per platform
- **Current limitation:** Dependence on line-of-sight (LOS) radio links from parent P-3 aircraft, limiting operational flexibility and endurance

### Enhanced S0 Configuration (New)
- **Satellite Communication and/or Land-Based Command/Control System**
  - Purpose: Enable beyond-line-of-sight command, control, and telemetry in tropical cyclone environment
  - Must function reliably with high winds, heavy precipitation, and RF attenuation challenges
  - Non-recurring engineering/R&D/integration cost: ~$30,000 for entire effort (6 systems)

- **Video Imaging Capability**
  - Purpose: Real-time or near-real-time situational awareness during atmospheric sampling
  - Must integrate with communication architecture
  - No specific resolution or performance specs stated in SOW

## Use Cases & Applications
- **Primary mission:** Tropical cyclone (hurricane) atmospheric sampling
- **Deployment method:** Air-deployed from NOAA P-3 crewed aircraft via drop tube
- **Data collection:** High-resolution atmospheric thermodynamic data in tropical cyclones
- **Operational benefit:** Ability to continue data collection after P-3 departure from area; reduced dependence on continuous line-of-sight support
