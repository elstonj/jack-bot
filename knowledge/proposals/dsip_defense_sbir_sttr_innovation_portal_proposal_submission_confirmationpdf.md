## Executive Summary
Black Swift Technologies submitted a SBIR Phase I proposal to develop the S3-EW, a modular payload vehicle designed for electronic warfare applications. The system is intended to operate in agile swarms and be launched via VTOL (vertical takeoff and landing) from confined terrain environments.

## Technical Approach
The proposal describes a modular payload vehicle (S3-EW variant) optimized for:
- Electronic warfare (EW) mission capabilities
- Swarm operations with multiple vehicles
- VTOL ground launch capability
- Operation from confined terrain (enabling launch from challenging environments)

## Products & Capabilities Described

**S3-EW**
- A modular variant of BST's S3 platform adapted for electronic warfare
- Supports swarm operations (multiple coordinated vehicles)
- VTOL ground launch from confined terrain
- Modular payload architecture (allowing different EW payloads/configurations)

## Use Cases & Applications
- Electronic warfare swarm operations
- Missions requiring deployment from confined or austere locations
- Agile, coordinated multi-vehicle EW missions
