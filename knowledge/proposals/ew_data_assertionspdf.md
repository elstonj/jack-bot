## Executive Summary
This document formally identifies and asserts restrictions on the U.S. Government's rights to use, release, or disclose technical data and computer software developed by Black Swift Technologies under an Air Force SBIR contract (Topic DAF26BZ01-NV003). BST and its partner SkyMesa Systems claim SBIR Data Rights protection for multiple technology components.

## Products & Capabilities Described

**S3-EW Airframe**
- Airframe geometry and composite layup design
- Includes wing-embedded antenna integration
- Status: BST background IP; pre-existing investment under S3 product program
- Protection: SBIR Data Rights

**SwiftCore™ FMS (Flight Management System)**
- Includes SwiftPilot, SwiftTab, and SwiftStation software components
- Source code asserted as restricted
- Status: BST background IP; pre-existing investment
- Protection: SBIR Data Rights

**SwiftCore Cooperative-Control / Swarm Orchestration Algorithms**
- Algorithms for multi-vehicle coordination
- Status: BST background IP with lineage from VORTEX2 and CRATER programs
- Protection: SBIR Data Rights

**DS-GPS Signal-of-Opportunity Navigation Pipeline**
- Developed under NOAA SBIR award
- Status: Data rights established under that NOAA award, now transitioned
- Protection: NOAA SBIR Data Rights (transitioned)

**Modular Payload Bay ICD (Interface Control Document)**
- Interfaces include: power, data, time, RF feed, mount
- Status: Developed under this Phase I SBIR award
- Protection: Phase I SBIR Data Rights

**SkyMesa Systems Geolocation Pipeline**
- Developed by SkyMesa Systems (partner)
- Status: SkyMesa background IP
- Protection: Asserted by SkyMesa; flow-down per teaming agreement

## Notable Details
- **Multi-source IP**: The proposal leverages IP from multiple sources (BST proprietary, NOAA SBIR-developed, SkyMesa partner IP)
- **Teaming Agreement**: SkyMesa IP is flow-through under a formal teaming agreement with appropriate IP protections
