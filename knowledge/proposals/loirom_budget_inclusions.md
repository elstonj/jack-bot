## Executive Summary
Internal guidance document preparing Black Swift Technologies to submit a Letter of Interest to NOAA's UxS Operations Center for a data buy contract to operate an S0 VTOL unmanned aircraft during the TEPEX field campaign aboard a research vessel. BST can bid as primary contractor or subcontractor if partnered with an NOAA IDIQ-listed vendor.

## Operational Requirements
**Deployment Location & Duration:**
- Port locations: Honolulu and American Samoa
- Total cruise: 45 days (port to port)
- Operating window: 30 observation days on ship
- Flight operations: 2 flights per day average
- Flight duration: Maximum range/time permitted by weather and sea conditions

**Logistics:**
- Travel to/from ports required
- Meals and berths on research vessel provided at no cost
- Equipment/parts and salaries to be budgeted

## Products & Capabilities Described
**S0 VTOL**
- Vertical takeoff and landing unmanned aircraft system
- Proposed for deployment aboard research vessel in Pacific Ocean
- Capable of extended operations in marine environment with weather-dependent range constraints

## Contracting Structure
- This is a data buys solicitation (operational services contract)
- Open bidding process for vendor selection
- **Eligibility requirement:** Only vendors on NOAA IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity) list can bid as primary contractors
- **Alternative path:** Non-listed vendors can participate as subcontractors to IDIQ-listed prime contractors

## Budget Components Needed
For LOI submission, ballpark estimate required covering:
- Travel costs (Honolulu and American Samoa)
- Personnel/salaries (flight operations)
- Equipment and parts
- Contingency for 30 days × 2 flights/day operations
