## Executive Summary
Black Swift Technologies notified U.S. Customs and Border Protection regarding the temporary export and return of their S3 UAS to Mexico for a scientific data collection mission on behalf of the U.S. Geological Survey. The deployment involved sampling volcanic emissions at Popocatépetl volcano and claimed exemption under the "tools of trade" export license exception.

## Technical Approach
- Deployed S3 UAS system equipped with gas collection sensors
- System maintained under positive custody throughout deployment
- S3 secured in locked container during storage and transport
- System returned to the United States within required six-month regulatory period
- Compliance maintained with 15 C.F.R. Part 740.9(a)(1) temporary export requirements

## Products & Capabilities Described

**S3 Unmanned Aircraft System (UAS)**
- Unmanned aerial platform equipped with gas collection sensors
- Designed to sample volcanic emissions in vicinity of active volcanoes
- Portable enough for international temporary deployment
- Can be secured in locked containers for transport
