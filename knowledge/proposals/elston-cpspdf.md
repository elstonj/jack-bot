## Pending Projects (Future Support)

### 3. Adaptive and Secure Autonomy for UAS: A Modular Approach to Flight Control, Machine Learning Integration, and Multi-Vehicle Coordination (NASA Phase II)
- **Award Number:** A2.02-1017
- **Status:** Pending (01/2027 – 12/2028)
- **Source:** NASA
- **Total Amount:** $1,167,689
- **Elston Commitment:** 2.81 PM/year (both years)
- **Objective:** Advance SwiftCore Flight Management System from benchtop to flight-validated, customer-deployable research platform. Key innovation is Safe Sandbox supervisor—a deterministic runtime-assurance arbiter enabling third-party algorithm developers to move code from concept to certified flight in days rather than months. Includes isolation-enforcing module host, HIL/SIL simulation, and faster-than-real-time Monte Carlo capability.
- **Systems/Products:** SwiftCore Flight Management System

### 4. Development of a SL UAS with Advanced MAD and Acoustic Sensing Capability (Phase II)
- **Status:** Pending (01/2027 – 06/2030)
- **Source:** Department of the Navy
- **Total Amount:** $1,400,000
- **Elston Commitment:** 0.11 PM (2027–2028), 0.14 PM (2029–2030)
- **Objective:** Transition Phase I feasibility into production-ready, mission-validated SL UAS for persistent, multi-modal Anti-Submarine Warfare (ASW) search and detection within LAU-126A launch container constraints. Validates high-sensitivity performance (magnetic noise floor <20 pT/√Hz) under dynamic flight maneuvers and propulsion environments. Integrates hardened, production-representative airframe capable of launch shocks and autonomous water-landing-to-sensing transitions. Coordinates S0-MAD and S0-Acoustic variants to reduce detection latency and operator workload versus traditional sonobuoy sampling.
- **Systems/Products:** S0-MAD, S0-Acoustic (sonobuoy-launched variants)

### 5. Expendable Air-Sea Profiling Observations in Hazardous Weather Conditions via Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (Phase II)
- **Status:** Pending (01/2027 – 12/2030)
- **Source:** Department of the Navy
- **Total Amount:** $2,400,000
- **Elston Commitment:** 0.14 PM (2027–2028), 0.12 PM (2029–2030)
- **Objective:** Transition S0 from proven NOAA research platform to fully integrated, turnkey DoN operational capability. Seven key objectives:
  - Over-ocean flight campaigns for sensing validation against buoy/tower data
  - Web-based, low-cognitive-load user interface and automated data quality control
  - Real-time onboard processing with heterogeneous dual-core System-on-Chip (STM32MP1) running turbulence and wave-state algorithms
  - Forecast improvement via S0 data assimilation into models for boundary-layer structures
  - Extreme environment hardening (−65°F, icing mitigation with heaters and advanced coatings)
  - Autonomous adaptive sampling using Temporal Multimodal Multivariate Learner and IT-RRT planner for real-time flight path optimization
  - Adaptation for Navy Common Launch Tubes (CLT) and A-size sonobuoy canisters for P-8 and MH-60 deployment
- **Systems/Products:** S0 UAS

### 6. S0-MET / S0-ARO Atmospheric Refractivity Survey
