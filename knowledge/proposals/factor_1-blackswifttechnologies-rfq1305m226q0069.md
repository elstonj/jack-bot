### Project Description
Black Swift Technologies procured and delivered 26 Black Swift S0 UAS platforms to NOAA's Aircraft Operations Center (AOC) for transition to routine hurricane operations during the 2025 Atlantic hurricane season. The contract included:
- Procurement of 26 S0 UAS platforms
- Development and delivery of operator, observer, and instructor training
- Operational support for hurricane research and testing

**Mission Profile:**
- Air-launch platforms designed for deployment from NOAA's WP-3D (P-3 "hurricane hunter" aircraft)
- Collection of in-situ atmospheric and oceanographic measurements including:
  - Pressure, temperature, humidity
  - Three-dimensional winds
  - Surface temperature
  - Wave height
  - Measurements taken in hurricane conditions near the air-sea boundary
- Real-time data transmission capability from UAS to P-3 aircraft
- Long-range communications integration
- Personnel deployed on short notice from Lakeland, Florida and forward locations (Bermuda, San Juan, Puerto Rico)

**Final Deliverable:** Operating manuals (cited as explanation for any delays)
