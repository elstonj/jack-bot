# JCP Certification - Black Swift Technologies LLC

## Document Metadata
- Type: DD Form 2345 - Militarily Critical Technical Data Agreement (Joint Certification Program application)
- Client/Agency: Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA)
- Program/Solicitation: Joint Certification Program (JCP)
- Date: Submitted 20250605 (June 5, 2025); Document created/modified 20260526 (May 26, 2026)
- BST Products/Systems Referenced: High-end UAS (unmanned aircraft systems) equipment
- Key Personnel: Jack S. Elston (CEO, Data Custodian, Authorized Entity Representative)

## Executive Summary
Black Swift Technologies LLC submitted an initial Joint Certification Program application to become a certified contractor eligible to receive militarily critical technical data. BST seeks certification to bid on DoD solicitations with export-controlled technical information packages related to their UAS equipment for environmental research applications.

## Business Activity Description
BST is a prime contractor (not a sub-contractor) that manufactures high-end unmanned aircraft systems (UAS) equipment for environmental research. The business activity is classified under NAICS 541715 (Research and Development in the Physical, Engineering, and Life Sciences).

## Export Control & Compliance Status
- **Manufacturing Status**: Does not manufacture munitions
- **Controlled Lists**: Equipment is NOT related to the U.S. Munitions List (USML) or Commerce Control List (CCL)
- **DDTC Registration**: Not registered with the Directorate of Defense Trade Controls
- **Export Control License**: Attached to application; company is subject to export control regulations but equipment falls outside USML/CCL categories
- **System Access**: Does not require access to external systems (SAM.gov, DIBBS, etc.)

## Certifications & Representations
BST certifies:
- Acknowledgment of responsibilities under U.S. export control laws and regulations
- Data custodian (Jack S. Elston) is a U.S. citizen/permanent resident
- Will not disseminate militarily critical technical data in violation of export control laws
- Will restrict access to employees and authorized representatives only
- No debarment, suspension, or ineligibility for U.S./Canadian Government contracts
- No violations of export control laws
- Certification valid upon approval through expiration date 20300605 (June 5, 2030)
