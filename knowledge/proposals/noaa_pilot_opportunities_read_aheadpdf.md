## Executive Summary
Black Swift Technologies, now part of the KrateoSky platform, proposes six independently fundable UAS pilot programs to advance NOAA's operational and research missions while demonstrating capabilities relevant to broader government priorities. The pilots range from distributed weather reconnaissance to autonomous maritime recovery, with a total base portfolio value of $7.85M and maximum potential value of $21.95M with options.

## Technical Approach
Black Swift proposes a portfolio of modular, mission-specific UAS deployments:
- Multi-UAS coordination for distributed sensing and dynamic retasking
- Autonomous launch, recovery, and recharge systems ("DIB-style autonomous deployment")
- Air-deployed UAS from fixed-wing carrier aircraft
- Autonomous recovery systems for moving maritime vessels
- Airborne communications relay with rotating aircraft for persistent coverage
- Design and development of an uncrewed carrier aircraft for multi-vehicle deployment in severe weather

## Products & Capabilities Described

### Black Swift UAS (Core Platform)
- **What it is**: U.S.-designed and U.S.-manufactured small UAS for demanding scientific and environmental missions
- **Proven operational history**: 48 operational sorties across 11 named storms over four consecutive hurricane seasons, launched from NOAA's WP-3D Hurricane Hunters
- **Performance**: Capable of profiling atmosphere from 10,000 feet to 10 meters above water; operational in eyewall winds exceeding 70 m/s
- **Current integration**: Black Swift observations now entering NOAA's operational hurricane forecast system (as of this document date)

### Proposed Derivative Systems
- Air-deployed UAS capability (proven with NOAA; proposed for adaptation to additional government aircraft)
- Autonomous recovery systems for moving vessels

## Use Cases & Applications

### PILOT 1: Distributed Extreme Weather Reconnaissance and Sensing
- **Base ROM**: $1.0M | **With Options**: $2.1M
- Deploy five UAS to survey incident areas and collect georeferenced imagery
- Dynamic redirection of coverage as conditions change
- **NOAA value**: Faster, broader situational awareness during severe weather and disasters when crewed observation is dangerous, delayed, or incomplete
- **DoW relevance**: Coordinated multi-UAS reconnaissance, distributed sensing, dynamic retasking

### PILOT 2: Reusable Lower-Atmosphere Observations
- **Base ROM**: $2.85M | **With Options**: $4.90M
- Deploy two reusable UAS with automated launch, recovery, recharge, and data transfer
- **NOAA value**: More frequent, targeted atmospheric observations without proportional increase in staffing, expendables, or logistics
- **DoW relevance**: Reusable, remotely operated environmental sensing from distributed locations

### PILOT 3: Airborne Communications Extension
- **Base ROM**: $0.9M | **With Options**: $1.55M
- Deploy two UAS with communications payload; rotate aircraft to maintain coverage and backup
- **NOAA value**: Restore or extend connectivity when infrastructure, congestion, or terrain isolates NOAA teams and responders
- **DoW relevance**: Airborne communications where terrestrial networks unavailable or disrupted

### PILOT 4: Air-Deployed Sensing from Additional Government Aircraft
- **Base ROM**: $0.7M | **With Options**: $1.2M
- Adapt Black Swift's proven air-deployed capability to another government-selected fixed-wing platform
- **NOAA value**: Expand aircraft able to deploy UAS into severe-weather and difficult-to-access environments
- **DoW relevance**: Portable air-launch capability across multiple government aircraft

### PILOT 5: Autonomous Recovery on Moving Ships
- **Base ROM**: $0.9M | **With Options**: $2.3M
- Develop and demonstrate autonomous UAS recovery to a moving government vessel; progress to at-sea demonstration
- **NOAA value**: Enable persistent maritime observation without requiring vessel to stop for recovery
- **DoW relevance**: Autonomous recovery for distributed maritime operations

### PILOT 6: Uncrewed Carrier Aircraft for Hurricane Reconnaissance
- **Base ROM**: $1.5M | **With Options**: $9.9M
- Design an uncrewed aircraft that carries, releases, and relays multiple air-deployed sensing UAS inside tropical cyclones
- Assess growth path to hosting next-generation storm radar
- Base effort is design and requirements; options cover build, fly, and demonstration
- **NOAA value**: Increases boundary-layer sampling density independent of crewed sortie availability; returns Hurricane Hunter capacity to radar and dropsonde collection
- **DoW relevance**: Autonomous multi-vehicle deployment and relay from fixed-wing aircraft in severe weather
