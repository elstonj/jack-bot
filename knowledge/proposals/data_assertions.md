## Executive Summary
Black Swift Technologies asserts intellectual property restrictions on multiple technical data and software components being submitted to the U.S. Air Force under Topic DAF26BZ01-NV008 (Agile EW). The company claims pre-existing proprietary rights to core flight management and software infrastructure developed at private expense, and seeks to protect those assets under Limited Rights (technical data) and Restricted Rights (software) classifications.

## Technical Components Under IP Protection

### SwiftCore Flight Management System
- **What it is:** Flight management system source code and node firmware
- **Development history:** Developed exclusively at private expense over the preceding decade by BST
- **Rights claimed:** Limited Rights (technical data) and Restricted Rights (computer software)
- **Significance:** Core BST platform component; long development history suggests foundational technology

### BST WASM Static Validator and Signing Chain
- **What it is:** WebAssembly (WASM) static validator and associated cryptographic signing chain
- **Development history:** Developed at private expense by BST prior to and outside this contract
- **Rights claimed:** Limited Rights / Restricted Rights
- **Significance:** Software validation and security infrastructure; predates current contract

### BST NixOS Base Flake and Reproducibility Manifest
- **What it is:** NixOS-based configuration and reproducibility manifest for system builds
- **Development history:** Developed at private expense by BST
- **Rights claimed:** Limited Rights / Restricted Rights
- **Significance:** Build/deployment infrastructure emphasizing reproducibility and configuration management

### RTAA Architecture and Software
- **What it is:** Real-time architecture and associated software (presumed acronym: Real-Time Autonomous Architecture or similar)
- **Development context:** Developed under the proposed SBIR contract
- **Rights claimed:** SBIR Data Rights per DFARS 252.227-7018
- **Significance:** New development under this specific contract; subject to standard SBIR data rights

## Strategic IP Approach
BST distinguishes between:
1. **Pre-existing proprietary components** (SwiftCore, WASM validator, NixOS flake) - claimed under Limited/Restricted Rights based on private development history
2. **New contract-specific development** (RTAA) - protected under standard SBIR data rights provisions
