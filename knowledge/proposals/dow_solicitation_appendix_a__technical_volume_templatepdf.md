## Document Summary
This is an **instruction document and template** for preparing Phase I Technical Volume proposals in response to DoW SBIR/STTR solicitations. It is not a BST proposal itself, but rather the standardized format that BST or other small business contractors would use when submitting Phase I proposals to DoW.

## Purpose
Provides guidelines and formatting requirements for small business contractors preparing Technical Volume (Volume 2) submissions for Department of War SBIR/STTR Phase I topics.

## Required Technical Volume Sections

The template requires the following sections in Phase I Technical Proposals:

1. **Identification and Significance of the Problem or Opportunity** - Define the technical problem/opportunity and importance
2. **Phase I Technical Objectives** - Enumerate specific objectives and feasibility questions
3. **Phase I Statement of Work** - Detailed description of approach, tasks, schedule, deliverables, and methods
4. **Related Work** - Describe prior/related activities, state-of-the-art awareness, references with POCs
5. **Relationship with Future Research** - Anticipated results, Phase II foundation, required clearances/approvals
6. **Commercialization Strategy** - Market need, market size, commercialization timeline and quantitative results
7. **Key Personnel** - Principal Investigator and key personnel resumes with education, relevant experience, awards, patents, publications
8. **Foreign Citizens** - Disclosure of foreign nationals/dual citizens with visa/work permit status
9. **Facilities/Equipment** - Available instrumentation, physical facilities, environmental compliance
10. **Subcontractors/Consultants** - Details per SBIR or STTR requirements and cost-sharing ratios
11. **Prior, Current or Pending Support** - Disclosure of similar proposals with other Federal agencies
12. **Restrictions on Government Use of Technical Data** - Table asserting any data/software restrictions with basis and asserted rights categories

## Format Requirements
- Single column, single-spaced
- Standard 8.5" x 11" paper
- 1-inch margins on all sides
- Minimum 10-point font
- Header with company name, topic number, DSIP proposal number
- Page limits per Component-specific instructions
- Unencrypted, unlocked files
- No embedded active media (videos, animations)

## Key Compliance Requirements

**SBIR Minimum Work Share:** 2/3 of Phase I research/analytical work (direct + indirect costs) must be performed by the proposing small business firm

**STTR Minimum Work Share:** 
- Small business firm: minimum 40% of work
- Single Research Institution: minimum 30% of work

**Disclosure Obligations:**
- Proprietary data must be marked with specific restrictive legends
- Foreign citizens involvement must be fully disclosed
- Similar proposals to other Federal agencies must be reported
- Prior/pending awards must be documented

**Data Rights:** 
- Technical data developed under resulting contract generally remains with contractor
- Government obtains 20-year royalty-free license for Government purposes
- SBIR data marked per DFARS 252.227-7018

## Notable Details
- Separate human/animal subject and recombinant DNA tasks recommended to avoid contract award delays
- Universities cannot publicly release export-controlled/ITAR-restricted information
- Federal Laboratories and FFRDCs can be used in SBIR without waiver (certification required)
- Evaluators must be persuaded of proposer's state-of-the-art awareness
- Phase I should determine technical feasibility of proposed concept
