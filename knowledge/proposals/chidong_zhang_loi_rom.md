## Executive Summary
Black Swift Technologies is proposed as a subcontractor to NOAA for the TEPEX Field Campaign, providing pilot/operator services and two S0 VTOL aircraft for approximately 45 days of operations from Honolulu to American Samoa. The total ROM cost proposal is $167,957.

## Technical Approach
- Deployment of 2 S0 VTOL (Vertical Takeoff and Landing) platforms for sustained field operations
- BST pilot/operator on-site for the full 45-day deployment
- Aircraft support including consumables and logistical shipping to/from deployment location
