## Executive Summary
This document outlines a hypothetical 14-day operational scenario for Black Swift Technologies to support NOAA hurricane monitoring and research, spanning from African tropical wave emergence through U.S. landfall. It proposes deploying S0 VTOL platforms for pre-storm and in-storm atmospheric sampling in the Caribbean/Atlantic, followed by traditional hurricane hunter operations including landfall and high-altitude deployments from a G3 aircraft. This is an early-stage planning document intended to establish operational scope and develop budget estimates.

## Technical Approach

### Phase 1: Pre-Storm and In-Storm Operations (Days 4-6, Barbados-based)
- Deploy S0 VTOL sUAS platforms to sample offshore atmospheric conditions
- Pre-storm sampling (T=-24 to -48 hours): Characterize Atmospheric Boundary Layer (ABL) from 20-3000m altitude
- In-storm sampling (T=0h): Sample ABL 20-1500m, with focus on low-level wind maxima and surface circulation centers
- Real-time data transmission to NOAA operational centers
- Number of platforms required: TBD

### Phase 2: Hurricane Hunter Operations (Days 7-14, Caribbean through Gulf/landfall)
- **Landfall Operations (S0 VTOL)**: Limited to within 12 nautical miles of coastline; restricted to capturing storm conditions 12-24 hours from storm center due to range limitations; unsafe for operations within T=12 hours of storm center; remote operation after deployment
- **High-Altitude Deployments (S0 from G3)**: Deploy multiple S0 platforms around main circulation center at 40,000 feet altitude; conduct X missions collecting data from 40K to 0K altitude; real-time data transmission
- **Regulatory Note**: NHOP (National Hurricane Operations Program) concerns regarding altitude operations and WRA (Waiver/airspace) requirements flagged

## Products & Capabilities Described

### S0 VTOL (Small Unmanned Aircraft System)
- Vertical takeoff and landing capability
- Atmospheric sampling platform
- Operating altitude range: 0-40,000 feet (capable of deployments across full range)
- Range limitations: suitable for pre-deployment up to 12 nautical miles from coast
- Capable of real-time data transmission
- Can be deployed independently or from G3 aircraft
- Suitable for both pre-storm and in-storm sampling missions

### G3 (Aircraft Platform)
- Crewed or support aircraft platform
- Capable of carrying/deploying multiple S0 VTOL platforms
- Operating altitude: 40,000 feet demonstrated
- Can support hurricane operations across extended geographic area

## Use Cases & Applications

### Hurricane/Tropical Cyclone Research and Operations
1. **Pre-storm atmospheric characterization**: 
   - Sea surface temperature (SST) mapping
   - Atmospheric boundary layer relative humidity (RH)
   - Low-level wind field structure analysis
   - 24-48 hour lead time sampling

2. **In-storm environmental measurement**:
   - Low-level wind maxima detection
   - Surface circulation center identification
   - Real-time operational data for NOAA hurricane forecasting

3. **Landfall operations**:
   - Coastal storm monitoring
   - 12-24 hour pre-landfall data collection

4. **High-altitude hurricane structure sampling**:
   - Multi-platform deployment around storm center
   - Vertical profiling from flight level to surface
   - Upper-level outflow characterization
