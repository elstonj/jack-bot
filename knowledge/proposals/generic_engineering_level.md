## Notable Details
- Document provides minimum qualifications for four DoD engineering labor categories:
  - **Engineer I**: 0–2 years experience, Bachelor's degree required
  - **Engineer II**: 3–5 years experience, Bachelor's degree required
  - **Engineer III/Senior Engineer**: 6–10 years experience, Bachelor's degree required
  - **Engineer IV/Lead Engineer**: 10–15+ years experience, Bachelor's degree required, team leadership experience required

- Outlines common DoD education substitutions:
  - Associate's degree + 4 years experience replaces Bachelor's degree
  - Master's degree substitutes for 2 years of experience
  - PhD substitutes for 4 years of experience

- Maps traditional job titles to modern level-based system (Junior Engineer → Engineer I through Principal Engineer → Engineer V)

- Intended for use in structuring labor categories for DoD proposals
