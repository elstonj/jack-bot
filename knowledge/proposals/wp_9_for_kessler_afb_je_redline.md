# Air-Deployed S0 Integration on the WC-130J: Extending Boundary-Layer Sensing to Air Force Hurricane Reconnaissance

## Document Metadata
- **Type:** White paper / capability proposal
- **Client/Agency:** 53rd Weather Reconnaissance Squadron, 403rd Wing, Air Force Reserve Command (Keesler AFB); Air Force weather enterprise; AFLCMC
- **Program/Solicitation:** SBIR Phase III sole-source
- **Date:** 6 August 2026
- **BST Products/Systems Referenced:** S0 air-deployed UAS
- **Key Personnel:** Daniel Pendergast (point of contact); Jack Elston (editor)

## Executive Summary
Black Swift Technologies proposes integrating the S0 air-deployed UAS onto the Air Force's WC-130J hurricane reconnaissance aircraft to extend boundary-layer sensing capability to DoD hurricane operations. This 14-month effort would develop launch integration, obtain airworthiness certification, conduct clear-air trials, and demonstrate operational capability during the 2027 hurricane season—closing a capability gap with NOAA's WP-3D fleet while leveraging proven airframe and data assimilation pathways.

## Technical Approach

**Integration Strategy:**
- Launch via existing AVAPS dropsonde dispenser infrastructure (same class installed on WC-130J), substantially reducing integration risk versus novel carriage/release mechanisms
- Development of WC-130J launch integration engineering: dispenser interface, electrical/data provisions, crew procedures
- Airworthiness and modification approval package (AF Form 1067 pathway) prepared for certification authorities in coordination with AFLCMC and 403rd Wing
- Real-time data formatting to National Hurricane Center (NHC) and Environmental Modeling Center (EMC) assimilation pathways (already built/exercised under NOAA program)

**Demonstration Schedule:**
- Clear-air verification flights at Keesler AFB
- Operational demonstration during 2027-season tasked missions coordinated with NHC/CARCAH and interdepartmental hurricane coordination process

## Products & Capabilities Described

**S0 Air-Deployed UAS:**
- **What it is:** Lightweight air-deployable unmanned aircraft system designed for tropical cyclone sampling
- **Specifications:**
  - Weight: <3 lbs (lightest UAS to successfully sample tropical cyclone)
  - Endurance: 100+ minute missions
  - Communications range: 125 nm
  - Operating altitude: Sustained flight at 10 m above sea surface
  - Already proven in tropical cyclone operations (Tropical Storm Tammy, 2023)
- **Proposed use on WC-130J:** Deploy from aircraft to profile boundary layer beneath reconnaissance tracks, capturing sustained low-altitude measurements that dropsondes only transect momentarily
- **Data pathway:** Real-time profiles formatted for NHC and EMC operational hurricane forecast model assimilation
- **Manufacturing:** Entirely U.S.-designed and U.S.-manufactured supply chain

**Prior Air Force Employment:**
- S0 air-deployed already employed as tactical weather drone supporting cargo-drop accuracy and localized forecasts (airframe not new to Air Force, only to weather reconnaissance mission)

## Use Cases & Applications

**Primary Mission:** Hurricane reconnaissance and intensity prediction
- Boundary-layer profile measurement beneath WC-130J reconnaissance tracks during tropical cyclone operations
- Data assimilation into National Hurricane Center operational forecast models (to begin 2026 season on NOAA side)
- Extended duration low-altitude sampling of the layer that drives hurricane intensity change
- Real-time data support for interdepartmental hurricane coordination processes (CARCAH, National Hurricane Operations Plan)

**Operational Context:**
- 53rd Weather Reconnaissance Squadron operates ten WC-130J aircraft conducting majority of DoD hurricane reconnaissance tasking
- Closes asymmetry with NOAA WP-3D Orion capability (now operationalizing S0 in 2026 season)
- Supports C-130J fleet modernization priorities in Congressional appropriations cycles
