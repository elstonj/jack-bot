1. **Timeline Discrepancy:**
   - Original due date (01/31/25) vs. actual delivery (10/13/25) is a ~9-month slip.
   - Asana does not document the reason (scope change, supply chain delay, client request, etc.).
   - **Recommendation:** Confirm with Project Manager Josh Fromm whether this was a known schedule adjustment or an unplanned delay.

2. **Training/Travel Logistics:**
   - Asana notes "Training/travel required: 2 people, 4 days to Tampa (scheduling needed)."
   - No evidence in the invoicing or QBO data that training costs were invoiced separately.
   - **Possible explanations:**
     - Travel cost absorbed in the $38K S0 platform price.
     - Training deferred or not yet invoiced (unlikely given 10/21/25 project completion).
     - Training handled as internal cost (employee time, not invoiced to client).
   - **Recommendation:** Clarify whether training/travel costs were included in the $38K or invoiced separately.

3. **Missing Invoice Detail:**
   - QuickBooks entry shows only "ND Air ($38,000)" with no line-item detail.
   - No evidence of separate invoices for equipment, labor, materials, or travel.
   - **Recommendation:** Cross-check with Accounting to confirm all costs were captured and properly allocated.

4. **Cost Accuracy:**
   - Invoice 1643 amount and QBO amount both $38,000, suggesting data alignment.
   - However, no supporting cost breakdown in budget docs means we cannot independently verify the $38K is accurate or reasonably allocated.
