### Burn Rate & Remaining Period of Performance
- **Invoicing Rate:** $22,200 invoiced over ~2 years (Nov 2024 to Nov 2026 snapshot date) = ~$11,100/year
- **Remaining Period:** Unclear. Original due date was Oct 1, 2024 (project is past due). Current tasks reference July 2026 delivery and training, but no formal end date is documented.
- **Assessment:** The project is essentially at budget ceiling ($136.67 remaining) while still having 4 open deliverables with no formal due dates. This is a **critical concern.**

### Cost Overrun / Budget Status by Category
- **No category breakdown available** — cannot assess which cost categories are over or under budget.
- **However:** The project has invoiced 99.4% of approved budget ($22,200/$22,337) while key deliverables remain open and unscheduled. This suggests either:
  1. Final payment/invoice is still pending for $136.67, or
  2. Budget was consumed early with delivery/completion still outstanding.

### Key Concerns

1. **Schedule Overrun:** Project due October 1, 2024; currently 2+ years overdue. No revised end date formally documented.

2. **Budget Nearly Exhausted:** $136.67 remaining (0.6% of budget) with 4 open tasks, 3 of which lack due dates and 1 which is unassigned.

3. **Missing Delivery Milestones:** 
   - "Deliver S0 VTOL" task has no due date.
   - Training dates are contradictory (Sabu available May 5, 2025 week vs. training scheduled July 1–4, 2026).
   - NetCDF capability task is unscheduled and has no completion target.

4. **Invoicing vs. Delivery Mismatch:** $22,200 has been invoiced (and paid by client), but it is unclear what deliverables have actually been provided. The Asana task list shows zero completions, suggesting invoiced amounts may exceed completed work.

5. **Vendor/Partner Uncertainty:** Invoices are to Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University, not to Barbados Meteorological Services directly. No clarity on whether Embry-Riddle is building/delivering the system or if Embry-Riddle is a pass-through.

6. **Regulatory Risk:** Asana notes flag potential RF interference concerns from the Barbados Prime Minister's Office regarding the Microhard P900 radio (902–928 MHz ISM band). This could impact system approvals/delivery.

7. **Process/Transition Gaps:** Per team feedback (Daniel Prendergast, Oct 2026), there is no well-defined process for transitioning proposed S0 builds into production. This project may be stalled in transition planning.

### Recommendation
- **Immediate Actions:**
  1. Obtain final invoice/payment status from client and confirm what work has been delivered against the $22,200 paid to date.
  2. Assign formal due dates to all 4 open Asana tasks and align with client on revised schedule (original Oct 2024 date is no longer realistic).
  3. Clarify Embry-Riddle's role (supplier, subcontractor, integrator, or pass-through).
  4. Resolve training date conflict (May 2025 vs. July 2026) with client and assign training task to a team member.
  5. Obtain regulatory clearance status on RF interference concerns before final delivery.
  6. Establish a change order or contract amendment if additional work/budget is required to complete deliverables.
