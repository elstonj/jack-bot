# Project 026-06 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Code:** 026-06 (Murphy's Pond CH4 Monitoring)

**Full Project Name:** Murphy's Pond CH4 Monitoring

**Client/Agency:** Murray State University (Dr. Bassil El Masri, belmasri@murraystate.edu, 270-809-3110)

**Contract Type:** Research Services / Direct Client Engagement (non-federal; competitive bidding required due to >$10k threshold)

**Period of Performance:** Funding expires 2026-12-31

**Brief Description:** Remote sensing project to monitor methane (CH4) emissions from wetlands and bottomland hardwood forests at Murphy's Pond (Fancy Farm, KY). The project uses Black Swift Technologies' Sasquatch S0 and S2 uncrewed aircraft systems (UAS) to conduct multiple survey flights with methane payload, FLIR thermal imaging, and optical camera systems to upscale site measurements to larger geographic areas. Four planned flights; Flights #1 and #2 completed November and December 2025; Flight #3 scheduled May 27, 2026; Flight #4 pending funding confirmation (June 29, 2026).
