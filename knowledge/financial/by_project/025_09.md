## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposals & Reports documents**

### Navy SBIR Phase I (N251-016)
- **Upcoming Milestone:** October 14, 2025 (sensor requirements and development milestone due)
- **Technical Milestones:**
  - Magnetometer integration and noise mitigation (shielding of electric motor, heading error calibration)
  - Acoustic hydrophone (omnidirectional) deployment system validation
  - Sensor-to-autopilot direct integration (replacing Sensor Control Board)
  - Time-sync solution for real-time processing
- **Financial Implications:** Navy SBIR Phase I milestone completion likely tied to next invoice tranche; Invoice 1703 ($131,954.73, dated 2025-09-29) may represent Phase I final payment pending milestone acceptance.

### ByLight Long-Range Fixed-Wing Evaluation (BYL-LRFE)
- **Duration:** 8-week engagement (commenced late September 2025)
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Airframe intake QA and build verification
  - Ground and launcher testing (thrust mapping, speed validation)
  - Flight testing and raw data collection
  - Aerodynamic modeling and performance analysis
  - ArduPilot/Cube autopilot parameter development and tuning
  - Design change prioritization matrix and technical memorandum
  - Optional hybrid propulsion demonstration
  - Airframe return condition report
- **Target Performance:** 400–500 km range at ~35 lb GTOW
- **Financial Implications:** Invoice 1697 ($3,473.96, dated 2025-09-02) is labeled "By Light Prototype Build"; likely first tranche of subcontract. Additional invoices expected as milestones complete.

### 4th Infantry Division Autonomous Systems Experiment (Proposal Stage)
- **Status:** Conceptual proposals submitted; not yet contracted
- **Proposal 1 Deliverables:** UAS-mounted EMS analysis system (wideband RF receiver, post-processing algorithms, operational resilience to GPS jamming)
- **Proposal 2 Deliverables:** Multi-UAS collaborative electronic patrol system (signal detection, classification, source localization, network integration)
- **Financial Implications:** No invoices linked to 4ID proposals in current invoice history; contract negotiations likely ongoing.
