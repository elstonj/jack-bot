## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposals/Reports**

### Navy SBIR Magnetometer Phase I (N251-016)
- **Client:** U.S. Navy
- **Deliverable:** Sonobuoy-launched UAV (S0) with integrated magnetometer and acoustic hydrophone sensors for anti-submarine warfare cued search, detection, tracking, classification
- **Milestone Due:** October 14, 2025 (sensor requirements & development milestones)
- **Technical Challenges Identified:**
  - Magnetometer time jitter requiring time-sync solutions
  - Heading error 120–150 nanoTesla; platform noise 250–600 pT/√Hz (10–30× requirement)
  - Magnetic shielding of motor components needed
  - Acoustic hydrophone freq. range objective: 0.001 Hz–25 kHz
- **Financial Implication:** Phase I budget likely fixed; Sept 2025 Navy SBIR invoicing (~$70K) suggests burn rate consistent with phase completion by Oct 2025. Phase II continuation unknown.
