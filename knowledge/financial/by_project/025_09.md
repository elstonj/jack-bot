## Key Deliverables & Milestones

### 1. **Navy SBIR Phase I — Magnetometer/Sonobuoy-Launched UAV** (N251-016)
**Source:** 2025-09-04 Navy SBIR Magnetometer Phase I Meeting (meeting slides/status report)

- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Navy
- **Key Milestone:** **Magnetometer & Acoustic Sensor Requirements Due: October 14, 2025**
- **Deliverables in Progress:**
  - Integrated magnetometer on S0 air-deployed platform (noise/heading error issues identified; mitigation plan under development)
  - Omnidirectional hydrophone sensor for acoustic detection (operating frequency range ~1 Hz to 25 kHz)
  - Sonobuoy launch capability modifications to S0
  - Sensor-to-autopilot integration (replacing current Sensor Control Board integration)
  
- **Technical Challenges & Resolutions:**
  - Magnetometer platform noise (250–600 pT/√Hz vs. <30 pT/√Hz requirement) — *Solution:* Magnetic shielding of motor components
  - Heading error (120–150 nanoTesla) — *Solution:* Calibration and removal of magnetic materials
  - System time jitter — *Solution:* Time-sync integration for real-time processing

- **Financial Implication:** Invoices 1677 ($70,000, 2025-06-17) and 1703 ($131,954.73, 2025-09-29) reference "[301-3] S0 Hurricane Phase II 2025" and appear Navy SBIR-related. The unclassified.md entry references "$112,492.50 Navy SBIR" (truncated) as of Sept 2025.
