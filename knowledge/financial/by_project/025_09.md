## Key Programs & Deliverables

### 1. Navy SBIR N251-016: Expendable Sonobuoy-Launched UAS for ASW
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Navy  
- **Program Phase:** Phase I  
- **Period:** Ongoing  
- **Status:** In development (meeting held 2025-09-04)  
- **Key Milestones:** October 14, 2025 (sensor integration deliverables)  
- **Technical Scope:** Magnetometer and acoustic hydrophone integration on S0 platform; sonobuoy launch capability; GPS-denied operations  
- **Invoiced to Date:** ~$119K (Navy SBIR line items from 2025-09 and 2025-10)  
- **Key Challenges:** Magnetometer heading error (120–150 nT), platform noise (250–600 pT/√Hz, 10–30x requirement), time-jitter in sensor integration  
- **Next Steps:** Magnetic shielding of motor components, direct sensor-to-autopilot integration

**Source:** Navy SBIR Magnetometer Phase I Meeting (2025-09-04)
