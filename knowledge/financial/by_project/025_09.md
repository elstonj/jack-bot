### 2. **Navy SBIR Magnetometer Phase I** (Active)
- **Client:** U.S. Navy  
- **Program:** Navy SBIR N251-016 "Expendable Sonobuoy-Launched Unmanned Aerial Vehicle for ASW"
- **Platform:** S0 (air-deployed)
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Magnetometer integration with S0 platform
  - Acoustic hydrophone sensor integration
  - Sonobuoy launch capability modification
  - **Milestone Due:** October 14, 2025 (for sensor requirements and development)
- **Technical Challenges:**
  - Magnetometer heading error (120–150 nanoTesla) — requires calibration and magnetic shielding
  - Platform noise (250–600 pT/√Hz vs. 10–30x requirement) — motor magnetic shielding needed
  - Time jitter and data formatting issues
- **Key Personnel:** Alex Lomis (presentation lead)
- **Financial Status:** Invoice [301-3] (S0 Hurricane) shows $201,954.73 total invoiced. Navy SBIR contribution noted in Sept 2025 actuals ($70,000 unclassified.md). **Unclear if project is over/under budget; no proposed budget provided.**
