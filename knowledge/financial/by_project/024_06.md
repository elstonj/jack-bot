# Project 024-06 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Code:** 024-06

**Primary Program:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II – Soil Moisture Mapping (SMM Phase II, Project X22.4D)  
**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF)  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II  
**Period of Performance:** Data available 2024-06 through 2025-10 (from QuickBooks actuals)

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS. BST is integrating L-band radiometer, thermal, and NDVI sensors onto the E2 UAS platform for soil moisture and runway integrity assessment. Work includes sensor procurement, payload design, integration, field validation, and algorithm development for soil integrity calculation. Milestone 3 (sensor integration and testing) was completed as of the June 2024 report; work progressing toward Milestone 4 algorithm development.

**Key Products/Systems:** S2 UAS, E2 UAS platforms with integrated multi-sensor payload
