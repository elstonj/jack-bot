# Project 024-06 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**PROJECT CODE:** 024-06

**Project Name:** Soil Moisture Mapping (SMM) Phase II – Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF)

**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II (Program: Project X22.4, also referenced as "22.4D")

**Period of Performance:** Data available from 2024-06 through 2025-09 (ongoing as of last data point)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
BST is developing and integrating L-band radiometer with auxiliary sensors (thermal and NDVI) on the E2 UAS platform for soil integrity validation. Work includes sensor design, procurement, integration, field testing, and algorithm development for soil integrity calculation. Milestone 3 (sensor integration and testing) was completed as of June 2024; work progresses toward Milestone 4 algorithm development.
