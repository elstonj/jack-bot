## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**From Proposals & Reports:**

### SMM Phase II 22.4D (AFWERX SBIR Phase II – Soil Moisture Mapping)
**Source:** Milestone #3 Report, submitted 2024-06-11

- **Milestone 3 (Completed as of June 2024):** Design, procurement, integration, and field testing of thermal and NDVI sensors integrated into self-contained bolt-on payload module for BST's E2 UAS platform.
  - Key accomplishment: Validation of sensor performance against commercial reference instruments.
  - Deliverables: Modified E2 UAS with integrated L-band radiometer, thermal sensor, and NDVI sensor.
  
- **Milestone 4 (In Progress):** Algorithm development for soil integrity calculation from radiometer, NDVI, and thermal data.
  
- **Technical System:** Integration of new auxiliary sensors (thermal, NDVI) with L-band radiometer on E2 UAS; simplified data logging architecture (single logging point via FTDI interface).

- **Financial Implication:** SMM Phase II 22.4D represents a significant ongoing cost (~$850K over 18 months), suggesting a multi-quarter funded program with recurring development, testing, and personnel costs.

**Source:** Statement of Joint Development and Demonstration (SOW), modified 2024-06-06

- **Task 1 (Target completion: End of January 2023):** Iterative performance enhancements to QM3000-VA2 methane sensor (System #1, University of Colorado) based on Alaska field test results; lab validation; integration and field testing support.

- **Task 2 (Target completion: February/March 2023):** Design and fabrication of QM3000-VA2 System #2 for BST field testing with prospective customer PDC; component procurement, fabrication, testing.

- **Constraint:** FPGA processing unit procurement delays due to chip shortage; loaner unit available only through end of March 2023.
