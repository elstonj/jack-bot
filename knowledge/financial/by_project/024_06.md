## Project Overview

**Project Code:** 024-06

**Primary Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SMM Phase II)

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (AFWERX SBIR Phase II – Soil Moisture Mapping, Project X22.4)

**Period of Performance:** Data available 2024-06 through 2025-11 (ongoing as of last entry)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
This is a multi-year Air Force SBIR Phase II project focused on developing runway integrity validation through soil moisture measurements using BST's small UAS platforms (S2 and E2). The project involves integration of L-band radiometer with thermal and NDVI sensors into a modular payload, algorithm development for soil integrity calculation, and field validation. This is one of several active BST projects being tracked under the 024-06 project code.
