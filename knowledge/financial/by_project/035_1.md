**Brief Description:**
ADONIS is a commercial engineering services project focused on autonomous swarming unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS). Work encompasses three core areas: (1) autonomous sUAS integration and design iteration for multiple-aircraft swarming capability; (2) large aircraft integration for mothership-deployed swarm release mechanism; and (3) transition and commercialization support including licensing and regulatory planning. All major milestones including hardware freezes, flight trials, swarming tests, and final reporting were completed by May 2026.
