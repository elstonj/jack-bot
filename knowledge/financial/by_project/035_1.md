**Detailed Budget Analysis:**

1. **Labor:** Proposed original labor budget of $93,000 (372 hours @ $250/hr) vs. actual spend of $98,047.20 (413:26 hours @ $250/hr). The 41:26-hour overage represents an 11% increase in scheduled hours. However, this overage was absorbed within the original $93,000 budget envelope via invoice 1646 ($31,500 for 126 hours in Oct 2024–Feb 2025 period). The remaining labor hours (236+ hours, invoiced as $59,000 in Inv 1713 and $62,500 in Inv 1756) were covered under additional funding allocations (Nov 2025 verbal agreement for $62,500 engineering work).

2. **Aircraft (S0 VTOLs):** Only 1x S0 VTOL ($15,000) was formally invoiced and paid. Verbal agreement indicated $90,000 for 6 units, but only $15,000 was documented and invoiced. Remaining $75,000 allocation and 5 additional aircraft remain undrawn/uninvoiced as of project close (04/10/2026).

3. **Equipment:** Non-aircraft equipment spend of $3,971.33 is below the allocated budget, indicating underspend in this category.

4. **Materials:** Minor spend ($444.68); no proposed budget was identified for this category in Drive documents.

5. **Additional Engineering (Nov 2025 Addendum):** $62,500 allocated under verbal modification (11/13/2025 email) for additional engineering work. This was invoiced in full via Inv 1756 (04/10/2026, $62,500).

**Summary:** Original contract labor was 5.4% overspent in terms of actual hours worked (41:26 hours over 372 budgeted), but this was resolved through the Nov 2025 verbal modification which allocated an additional $62,500 for engineering work. Total invoiced to client ($168,000 across three invoices) tracks closely to approved budget of $175,000 (original $93K + $15K VTOL + $62.5K addendum), resulting in 96% budget utilization. The $90,000 verbal allocation for additional VTOLs (6 units) remains largely undrawn.
