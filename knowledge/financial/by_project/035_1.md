### Budget Variance Reconciliation

**Key Discrepancy:** Drive Budget documents show:
- Total Budget: $160,000 (as of 03/18/2026)
- Total BST Spent: $27,708.02
- Remaining Budget: $83,500.00

However, QuickBooks shows:
- Total Invoiced (through project closure): $105,500.00 (pre-closure) + $62,500.00 (final, 04/10/2026) = $168,000.00
- Total Paid: $76,500.00 (through March) + $62,500.00 (final invoice, assumed paid) = $139,000.00

**Analysis:**
- Drive Budget figure of $27,708.02 "spent" appears to reflect only expenses recorded through mid-March 2026, excluding invoiced labor hours not yet paid.
- Invoiced amounts ($105,500 pre-closure) represent billable labor and aircraft, but only $76,500 was paid by March 18, 2026; $29,000 remained outstanding on Invoice 1713.
- Final invoice ($62,500, posted 04/10/2026 after project closure) represents final engineering work and was apparently paid in full by April closure.
- Actual QBO spend aligns with accrual of invoiced amounts ($168,000 total) rather than cash basis.

**Conclusion:** Project invoiced at $168,000 total ($105,500 + $62,500), exceeding the Drive Budget "Total Budget" figure of $160,000 by $8,000. This suggests either: (a) budget was informally expanded post-March 18, 2026 to cover final invoice, or (b) final invoice was billed outside budget envelope due to scope extensions. **No formal budget modification document identified in Drive Budget files.**
