#### 1. **Labor Budget Status: SLIGHT OVERAGE (Manageable)**
- **Budgeted Hours:** 372 hours @ $250/hr = $93,000
- **Actual Hours Worked:** 413:26:00 hours (41 hours 26 minutes over budget)
- **Overage Percentage:** 11.1% over scheduled hours
- **Cost Impact:** Despite the hour overage, total labor invoiced ($90,500 through original invoices + $62,500 final engineering invoice) remained within the expanded contracted budget ($93,000 + $62,500 additional engineering = $155,500 for labor + engineering)

**Assessment:** The 11% hour overage is typical for hardware/integration-heavy projects with unanticipated field testing iterations. The client accepted this through verbal agreement (Nov 13, 2025 email) to allocate an additional $62,500 for continued engineering work, suggesting satisfaction with progress despite schedule compression.
