5. **Data Gaps:**

   - **Labor Costs:** No direct labor or engineering costs documented in budget or QuickBooks for this project (invoice appears to be equipment-only pass-through).

### Concerns:
- **Training costs potentially unaccounted for:** If BST incurred labor or travel expenses for the Tampa training, these are not reflected in the budget or invoices to the client. This could represent an uncompensated cost if training was performed.
- **No expense detail in QuickBooks:** The $38,000 entry in QuickBooks (unclassified.md) is a single line item with no breakdown of actual direct costs, suggesting invoicing may have preceded or been decoupled from internal cost tracking.

### Recommendation:
- **Project is financially closed.** Verify that any training-related labor or travel costs were either (a) absorbed as indirect/overhead, (b) invoiced separately, or (c) completed at customer site without BST cost.
