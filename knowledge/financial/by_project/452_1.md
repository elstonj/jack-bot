## Scope Summary
Single-unit E2 UAS Flight System procurement and delivery to CU Boulder. This was an inventory-clearing deal with significant service discount applied (46.6% off list price). Scope included:
- Complete E2 airframe with SwiftCore™ Flight Management System
- SwiftTab™ Android-based control tablet
- Futaba 14SG handset
- SwiftStation™ RTK-enabled ground control unit
- Toolkit, tripod, battery, charger, hard case
- 12-month manufacturer defect warranty
- 2-year Flight Management System software warranty with free upgrades
- Training and delivery/installation (team: Nate Straus, Maciej Stachura, Meredith O'Hara Needham, Beck Cotter)
