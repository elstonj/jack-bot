## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 550-2  
**Full Project Name:** Navy STTR: Hazardous Weather / N25A-T025 Expendable Air-sea Profiling Observations in Hazardous Weather Conditions  
**Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (Office of Naval Research); TPOC: Joshua Cosseth (joshua.h.cossuth.civ@us.navy.mil, 407-696-0703)  
**Contract Number:** N6833525C0270  
**Contract Type:** Navy Small Business Technology Transfer (STTR) Phase I with Option Period  
**Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies, LLC  
**Contract Award Date:** July 22, 2025  

**Period of Performance:**
- **Base Period:** July 22, 2025 – January 7, 2026 ✓ **COMPLETED**
- **Option Period:** January 7, 2026 – July 6, 2026 **(In progress)**
- **Final Deliverable Due:** September 1, 2026

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Research and development of expendable air-sea profiling observation systems for Navy operations in hazardous weather conditions. Project includes integration of magnetometer technology, validation flights (EMASS), and ice icing condition testing via NASA Glenn Ice Research Tunnel. Subcontracted work through Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University (ERAU), Old Dominion University, and University of Miami.
