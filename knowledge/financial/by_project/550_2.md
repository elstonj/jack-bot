# Project 550-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** N25A-T025 Expendable Air-sea Profiling Observations in Hazardous Weather Conditions

**Client/Agency:** United States Department of the Navy, Office of Naval Research

**Contract Number:** N6833525C0270

**Contract Type:** Navy STTR (Small Business Technology Transfer) Phase I with Option Period

**Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies, LLC

**Period of Performance:**
- Base Period: July 7, 2025 – January 6, 2026 *(Completed)*
- Option Period: January 7, 2026 – July 6, 2026 *(Completed)*
- Project Due Date: September 1, 2026 *(Completed on schedule)*

**Contract Award Date:** July 22, 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**

Project 550-2 focuses on the development and demonstration of expendable unmanned aircraft systems (UAS) for collecting atmospheric and oceanographic data in hazardous weather conditions on behalf of the U.S. Navy. The project involves refinement of the S0 platform with integrated sensors (gimballed EO/IR camera, magnetometer, GPS heading) for operational Navy utility in weather profiling. Work encompasses sensor integration, calibration/validation (Cal/Val), technical reports, and field demonstrations. Subcontractors include Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University (ERAU), Old Dominion University, and University of Miami (funded through ERAU).

**Project Status:** ACTIVE — Base Period and Option Period both completed on schedule as of September 1, 2026. All deliverables and final invoices submitted. Phase II cost proposal has been submitted to Navy.
