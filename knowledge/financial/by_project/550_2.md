# Project 550-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy STTR: Hazardous Weather (N25A-T025 — Expendable Air-sea Profiling Observations in Hazardous Weather Conditions)
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy, Office of Naval Research (TPOC: Joshua Cosseth)
- **Contract Type:** Navy STTR (Small Business Technology Transfer) Phase I with Option Period
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0270
- **Contract Award Date:** July 22, 2025
- **Period of Performance:** 
  - Base Period: July 7, 2025 – January 6, 2026 ✓ **COMPLETED**
  - Option Period: January 7, 2026 – July 6, 2026 ✓ **COMPLETED**
  - Final deliverables due: September 1, 2026
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies, LLC
- **Project Status:** **Active — Option Period work completed; Final Report (CLIN 0007) and Final Invoice (CLIN 1007) pending submission by September 1, 2026. Pioneer Array Flight test due July 28, 2026.**

**Brief Scope Description:**
Research and development of expendable air-sea profiling observation systems for use in hazardous weather conditions. Key technical work includes magnetometer integration, Pioneer Array Flight testing, and validation of weather profiling capabilities. Project involves subcontracting with Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University (ERAU), Old Dominion University (ODU), and University of Miami.
