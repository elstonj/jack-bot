# Project 550-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 550-2  
**Full Project Name:** Navy STTR: Hazardous Weather (N25A-T025 — Expendable Air-sea Profiling Observations in Hazardous Weather Conditions)  
**Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy, Office of Naval Research (ONR)  
**Contract Type:** Navy STTR (Small Business Technology Transfer) Phase I with Option Period  
**Contract Number:** N6833525C0270  
**Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies, LLC  
**Contract Award Date:** July 22, 2025  

**Period of Performance:**
- **Base Period:** July 22, 2025 – January 7, 2026 ✓ **COMPLETED**
- **Option Period:** January 7, 2026 – July 6, 2026 ✓ **COMPLETED**
- **Final Deliverables Due:** September 1, 2026

**Project Status:** Active — Option Period work completed. Final Report (CLIN 0007) and Final Invoice (CLIN 1007) pending submission by September 1, 2026. One critical open task: Pioneer Array Flight (due July 28, 2026; assigned to Alex Lomis).

**Brief Scope Description:**  
Research and development to refine the S0 platform (expendable small UAS) and demonstrate its utility for operational Navy use in hazardous weather conditions. Work includes systems integration, calibration/validation testing, stakeholder engagement, and technical documentation. Subcontractors include Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University (ERAU), Old Dominion University (ODU), and University of Miami (sub to ERAU).
