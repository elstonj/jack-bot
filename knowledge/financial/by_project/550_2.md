- **Final Deliverables Due:** September 1, 2026

**Scope Description:**  
Navy STTR Phase I research on expendable air-sea profiling observations and data collection systems for operations in hazardous weather conditions. Work includes magnetometer integration, calibration/validation planning, field testing, and technical reporting. BST serves as prime contractor with subcontractors at Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University (ERAU), Old Dominion University (ODU), and University of Miami (hosted through ERAU).
