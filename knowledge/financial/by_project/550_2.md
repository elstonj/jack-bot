- **Period of Performance:**
  - Base Period: July 7, 2025 – January 6, 2026 ✓ **COMPLETED**

- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development of expendable small unmanned aircraft systems (S0 VTOL variant) for air-sea profiling observations in hazardous weather conditions. Subcontractors: Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University (ERAU), Old Dominion University (ODU), and University of Miami.
