# Project 550-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy STTR: Hazardous Weather (N25A-T025 Expendable Air-sea Profiling Observations in Hazardous Weather Conditions)
- **Client/Agency:** United States Department of the Navy, Office of Naval Research (ONR); TPOC: Joshua Cosseth (joshua.h.cossuth.civ@us.navy.mil, 407-696-0703)
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0270
- **Contract Type:** Navy STTR (Small Business Technology Transfer) Phase I with Option Period
- **Period of Performance:**
  - Base Period: July 7, 2025 – January 6, 2026 ✓ **COMPLETED**
  - Option Period: January 7, 2026 – July 6, 2026 ✓ **COMPLETED**
  - Final deliverables due: **September 1, 2026**
- **Contract Award Date:** July 22, 2025
- **Project Status:** **Active — Option Period work completed; Final Report & Invoice pending**

**Brief Description of Scope:** Development and refinement of the S0 platform (expendable unmanned aircraft system) for operational Navy use, demonstrating utility for air-sea profiling observations in hazardous weather conditions. Work includes technology development, calibration/validation planning, stakeholder engagement, and testing (including Pioneer Array Flight validation). Prime contractor: Black Swift Technologies, LLC. Key subcontractors: Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University (ERAU), University of Miami (via ERAU), and Old Dominion University.
