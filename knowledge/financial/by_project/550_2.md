## Project Overview

**Project Name:** Navy STTR: Expendable Air-sea Profiling Observations in Hazardous Weather Conditions

**Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of the Navy, Office of Naval Research

**Contract Number:** N6833525C0270

**Contract Type:** Navy STTR (Small Business Technology Transfer) Phase I with Option Period

**Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies, LLC

**Period of Performance:**
- Base Period: July 7, 2025 – January 6, 2026 ✓ Completed
- Option Period: January 7, 2026 – July 6, 2026 ✓ Completed
- Project Due: September 1, 2026 ✓ On time

**Brief Scope Description:**
Development and demonstration of expendable S0 and S0-AD unmanned aircraft platforms for profiling hazardous weather conditions in maritime environments. Work includes platform refinement, calibration/validation (Cal/Val) of magnetometer and GPS heading sensors, integration of EO/IR camera systems, and operational demonstrations for Navy utility assessment. Subcontractor support from Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University (ERAU), University of Miami, and Old Dominion University.

**Project Status:** ACTIVE – All base and option period deliverables completed on schedule as of September 1, 2026. Phase II cost proposal submitted (awaiting award decision).
