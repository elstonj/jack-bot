- **Full Project Name:** N25A-T025 Expendable Air-sea Profiling Observations in Hazardous Weather Conditions
- **Client/Agency:** United States Department of the Navy, Office of Naval Research
- **Contract Type:** Navy STTR (Small Business Technology Transfer) Phase I with Option Period
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies, LLC
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0270
- **Period of Performance:**
  - **Base Period:** July 22, 2025 – January 7, 2026 ✓ **COMPLETED**
  - **Option Period:** January 7, 2026 – July 6, 2026 (in progress)
  - **Final Deliverable Due:** September 1, 2026
- **Status:** Active (Option Period in progress)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and testing of expendable air-sea profiling observation systems for use in hazardous weather conditions. The project includes integration with existing BST uncrewed aircraft systems (UAS), environmental testing (NASA Glenn Ice Research Tunnel), and comprehensive reporting. Key subcontractors include Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University (ERAU), University of Miami, and Old Dominion University for specialized research and technical support.
