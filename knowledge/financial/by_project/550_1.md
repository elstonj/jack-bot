# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of the Navy (NAVAIR) — Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division (NAWCAD)
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option proposal; currently in Phase I Option Period transitioning to Phase II
- **Technical Points of Contact:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes (Physicist, NAWCAD Advanced Technology Development) and Anthony Brescia (Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead)
- **Period of Performance:** 
  - Phase I Base: (dates not specified in available documents)
  - Phase I Option Period: April 14, 2026 – September 28, 2026 (6 months)
  - **Phase II Proposed Base:** January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
  - **Phase II Proposed Option I:** July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)
- **Brief Description:** Development and demonstration of a high-performance magnetometer sensor payload integrated onto Black Swift Technologies' S0 UAS platform. Phase I included design, analysis, and field testing of both magnetometer and acoustic sensor configurations. Phase I completed with successful Camp Pendleton demonstration flights (September 2026). Project transitioning to production planning and export compliance phase.
