  - Proposed Phase II Base: January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
  - Proposed Phase II Option I: July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Design, build, and ground-test magnetometer-equipped small unmanned aircraft systems (S0-MAD and S0-AD variants) for Navy atmospheric survey applications. Phase I Option focuses on compressed 6.5-month development (April–September 2026) culminating in ground testing and hand-launched flight demonstrations at Camp Pendleton (September 14–25, 2026). Deliverables include three Navy-required reports (Kick-Off & FWA Certification, Progress Report, Final Report) plus technical integration of Bartington and QuSpin magnetometer sensors onto BST S0 UAS platforms.
