- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of the Navy, Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division (NAWCAD)
- **Technical Point of Contact:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes (NAWCAD Physics Advanced Technology Development Department)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies, LLC (Prime), Ultra Maritime (Subcontractor)
- **Period of Performance:**
  - **Phase I Option Period:** April 14, 2026 – September 28, 2026 (completed)
  - **Phase II Base Period (Proposed):** January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
  - **Phase II Option I (Proposed):** July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)
- **Brief Description:** Demonstrate technical feasibility of integrating magnetic anomaly detection (MAD) and passive acoustic sensing into SL UAS platform (S0-MAD variant). Phase I work encompassed sensor integration, platform modification, system testing, and lab/field validation at BST facilities with partners Cetacean Research Technology (CRT) and QuSpin. Phase I successfully completed with final report submitted September 29, 2026; Phase II negotiation in progress (due January 1, 2027).
