# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD), Patuxent River, MD
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base, completed January 2026) + Option Period
- **Period of Performance:** 
  - Phase I Base: January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months, per proposal)
  - Phase I Option Period (Active): April 14 – September 28, 2026 (6.5 months)
- **Brief Scope:** Development and demonstration of a magnetometer payload for small unmanned aircraft systems (S0-AD UAS) to support naval operations. Includes sensor integration (Bartington, QuSpin magnetometers), ground launcher development, field testing, and Camp Pendleton demonstration flights (scheduled September 14–25, 2026).
