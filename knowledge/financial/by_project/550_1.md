- **Period of Performance (Current Phase I Option):** April 14 – September 28, 2026 (6.5 months)  
- **Future Period (Phase II Proposed Base):** January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)  

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Design, build, and flight-test a magnetometer-equipped Black Swift Technologies S0-AD unmanned aircraft system (UAS) for the U.S. Navy to conduct atmospheric magnetic field mapping and sensor validation. Phase I Option includes onboard magnetometer integration, ground testing, hand-launched flights, and a demonstration at Camp Pendleton (September 14–25, 2026). Three Navy-required reports (Kick-Off/FWA Certification, Progress Report, and Final Report) must be delivered across the option period. Compressed 6.5-month timeline with significant technical backlog as of June 8, 2026.
