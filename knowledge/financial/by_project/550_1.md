# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR Phase I Option Period: Magnetometer  
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD — Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division)  
  - **Primary TPOC:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department  
  - **Secondary Contact:** Anthony Brescia, Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead  
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I with Option Period; Phase II awarded (separate contract N6833525C0492)  
- **Contract Number (Phase II):** N6833525C0492  
- **Period of Performance:** Phase I Option Period: April 14 – September 28, 2026 (6.5 months)  
- **Brief Description:** Design, build, ground test, and demonstrate a compact magnetometer payload integration on Black Swift S0/S0-AD UAS platforms for Navy maritime and airborne magnetic field mapping applications. Technical scope included onboard sensor logging, Python-based data analysis tools, ground-launched and hand-launched flight testing, and a live demonstration at Camp Pendleton (Sep 14–25, 2026).
