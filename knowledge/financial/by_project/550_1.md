- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD Advanced Technology Development)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option proposal; current contract N6833525C0492
- **Period of Performance:**
