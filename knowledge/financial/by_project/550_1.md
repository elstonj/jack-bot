  - Secondary Contact: Anthony Brescia, Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead

- **Period of Performance:** 
  - Phase I: Completed (January 2026)
  - **Option Period (Current):** April 14, 2026 – September 28, 2026
  - Proposed Phase II Base: January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
  - Proposed Phase II Option I: July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)
