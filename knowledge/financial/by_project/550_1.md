# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer (UAS-based high-performance magnetic field sensing for naval applications)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of the Navy (NAVAIR) — Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division (NAWCAD), Advanced Technology Development
- **Technical Points of Contact:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes (Physicist, NAWCAD) and Anthony Brescia (Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead)
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option and Phase II Proposal
- **Period of Performance:**
  - Phase I Base: January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months, proposed)
  - Phase I Option: July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months, proposed)
  - *Actual Phase I Option Period (completed):* April 14, 2026 – September 28, 2026
- **Brief Description of Scope:**
  Develop and demonstrate a high-performance magnetometer system integrated into Black Swift Technologies' S0 unmanned aircraft system (UAS) for naval applications. Phase I option period included design, analysis, testing, and field demonstration (Camp Pendleton flights, Sept 14–25, 2026) of magnetometer and acoustic sensor payloads on hand-launched S0-MAD platform. Project transitioning to production planning and export compliance (ECCN classifications).
