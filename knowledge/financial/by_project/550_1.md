- **Period of Performance:**
  - Phase I Option Period: April 14, 2026 – September 28, 2026 ✅ **Completed**
  - Phase II Base Period (proposed): January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
  - Phase II Option I (proposed): July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)
- **Team Lead:** Alex Lomis
- **Project Status:** **Active but transitioning** — Phase I deliverables completed and closed out; Option Period in progress; project moving into Phase II production planning and export compliance phase

### Brief Scope Description
Design, build, test, and deliver a magnetometer payload system integrated with Black Swift Technologies' S0-AD unmanned aircraft system (UAS) for U.S. Navy applications. Phase I encompassed sensor design/analysis, acoustic sensor testing (via Cetacean Research Technology partnership), ground launcher development, and field demonstration flights at Camp Pendleton (Sept 14–25, 2026). Phase II (proposed) focuses on production build-out, technical assistance, and deployment support over 42 months (Base + Option).
