# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer (Magnetic Anomaly Detection & Passive Acoustic Sensing Integration)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of the Navy (SBIR Program)
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option proposal
- **Contract Value:** $1,397,610 (Base + Option I, proposed)
- **Period of Performance:** 
  - Phase I Option Period: April 14, 2026 – September 29, 2026 (completed)
  - Phase II Base Period: January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months, proposed)
  - Phase II Option I: July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months, proposed)
- **Brief Description:** Integration of magnetometer sensors (QuSpin, Bartington) and passive acoustic sensors (Cetacean Research Technology) into small unmanned aerial systems (S0-MAD platform). Phase I focused on sensor onboarding, ground testing, and field validation at Camp Pendleton (Sept 2026). Phase II will expand to helicopter-launched systems (S0-AD) in coordination with Lockheed Martin UK / Royal Navy "Project RAVEN."
- **Project Lead:** Alex Lomis (Phase I technical lead); Beck Cotter (Phase II negotiations)
- **Team:** Beck Cotter, Maciej Stachura, Jack Elston, Meredith O'hara Needham
