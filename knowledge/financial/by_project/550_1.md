## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD)
  - **TPOC:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department
  - **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option proposal
- **Period of Performance:** 
  - Phase I Base: January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
  - Phase I Option: July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)
  - Current Execution: April 14 – September 28, 2026 (Option Period, Phase I)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development of a high-performance magnetometer payload for unmanned aircraft systems (UAS) for Navy applications. Includes design, build, ground testing, and flight demonstration of magnetometer-equipped S0 UAS platforms with integrated Bartington and QuSpin sensors. Compressed 6.5-month timeline with Camp Pendleton demonstration (Sep 14–25, 2026) and three Navy-mandated reports.
