## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD)
  - **Technical Point of Contact:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department
  - **Secondary Contact:** Anthony Brescia, Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies, LLC (Prime); Ultra Maritime (Subcontractor)
- **Period of Performance:**
  - Base Period: January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
  - Option I Period: July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)
  - **Completed Option Period (Phase I):** April 14 – September 28, 2026

**Brief Scope:** Design, build, test, and demonstrate a magnetometer payload for Navy unmanned aircraft systems (UAS). The system integrates high-performance magnetic sensors (Bartington Compass, QuSpin sensors) with Black Swift Technologies' S0-AD UAS platform for atmospheric measurements and Camp Pendleton demonstration flights.
