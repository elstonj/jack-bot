# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR Phase I (Option Period): Magnetometer Development for Naval Aviation
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy, Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division (NAWCAD), Patuxent River, MD
  - **Technical Point of Contact:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department
  - **Secondary Contact:** Anthony Brescia, Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I with Phase II Option
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Period of Performance (Current Phase):** April 14 – September 28, 2026 (Option Period, 6.5 months compressed timeline)
- **Subsequent Period:** Phase II Base (January 2027 – June 2029, 30 months) + Option I (July 2029 – June 2030, 12 months) — **proposed, not yet awarded**
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Design, build, and flight-test a magnetometer payload integrated onto Black Swift Technologies' S0 UAS platform. Deliverables include ground testing, hand-launched flights, and field demonstration at Camp Pendleton (Sep 14–25, 2026). Technical scope includes onboard logging of dual magnetic sensors (Bartington and QuSpin), Python-based analysis tools, and three Navy administrative reports (Kick-Off, Progress, Final).
