## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD)
  - **TPOC:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department
  - **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option; Phase I Option currently executing
- **Period of Performance:** 
  - Phase I Base: January 2026 (completed)
  - Phase I Option Period: April 14 – September 28, 2026 (ACTIVE)
  - Phase II Base Period: CY 2027–2029 (30 months, proposed)
  - Phase II Option I: CY 2029–2030 (12 months, proposed)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development of a compact magnetometer payload integrated with Black Swift S0 small UAS for airborne magnetic field mapping and sensing in naval aviation applications. Phase I Option period (Apr–Sep 2026) focused on compressed design, build, ground test, and flight demonstration at Camp Pendleton (Sep 14–25, 2026), with three Navy-mandated administrative deliverables (CLINs 0005–0007).
