# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of the Navy (NAVAIR), Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division (NAWCAD)
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option; Phase I Option period completed, Phase II negotiations underway
- **Period of Performance:** 
  - Phase I Option: April 14, 2026 – September 28, 2026 (completed)
  - Phase II (proposed): January 2027 – June 2029 (Base, 30 months) + July 2029 – June 2030 (Option I, 12 months)
- **Brief Description:** Demonstrate technical feasibility of integrating magnetic anomaly detection (MAD) and passive acoustic sensing technologies into a small-lift UAS (SL UAS) platform. Scope includes sensor integration, platform modification, system testing, laboratory and field testing (Camp Pendleton demo completed September 2026). Collaborators: Cetacean Research Technology (CRT), QuSpin. Team lead: Alex Lomis.
