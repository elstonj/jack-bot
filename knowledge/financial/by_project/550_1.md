# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer (UAS-Mag Integration for Magnetic Anomaly Detection)
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD)
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option; Phase II negotiation currently in progress
- **Period of Performance:**
  - Phase I Base: January 2026 – March 2026
  - Phase I Option: April 14, 2026 – September 28, 2026 ✅ **COMPLETED**
  - Phase II (Proposed): January 2027 – June 2029 (Base) + July 2029 – June 2030 (Option I) — *Pending negotiation*
- **Brief Description:** Feasibility demonstration of integrating magnetic anomaly detection (MAD) and passive acoustic sensing into a small, lightweight unmanned aerial system (SL UAS) platform. Phase I included sensor integration, platform modification, system testing, lab validation, and field demonstration at Camp Pendleton (Sept 14–25, 2026). Phase II will expand testing, pursue Arctic Edge field operations, and conduct operational validation.
