# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer (Magnetic Anomaly Detection & Passive Acoustic Sensing Integration)
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR) — Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division (NAWCAD), Patuxent River, MD
- **Technical Points of Contact:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes (Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department); Anthony Brescia (Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead)
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option; currently transitioning from Phase I to Phase II negotiation
- **Period of Performance:** 
  - **Phase I Option Period:** April 14, 2026 – September 28, 2026 (6 months) — **COMPLETED**
  - **Phase II Base Period (Proposed):** January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
  - **Phase II Option I (Proposed):** July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Integration of magnetic anomaly detection (MAD) and passive acoustic sensing into BST's S0 Super Light UAS platform. Deliverables include hand-launched S0-MAD with dual sensor logging, validated field testing (completed at Camp Pendleton, Sept 14–25, 2026), Python analysis tools for magnetometer data, and proposed Arctic Edge demonstration flights (pending flight permissions).
