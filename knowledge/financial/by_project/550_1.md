# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer (Magnetic Anomaly Detection and Passive Acoustic Sensing Integration)
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR); Technical POCs: Angel Ruiz-Reyes & Anthony Brescia (NAWCAD)
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option; Base period now transitioning to Phase II negotiations
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies, LLC
- **Subcontractors:** Ultra Maritime (Phase II proposal), Cetacean Research Technology (Phase I), QuSpin (sensors)
- **Period of Performance:**
  - Phase I: Completed September 2026
  - Phase I Option Period: April 14–September 29, 2026
  - Phase II (Proposed): January 2027–June 2029 (30 months Base) + July 2029–June 2030 (12 months Option I)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Phase I demonstrated technical feasibility of integrating magnetic anomaly detection (MAD) and passive acoustic sensing into a small, hand-launched UAS platform (S0-MAD variant). Work included sensor integration, platform modification, system testing, ground launcher development, and field validation at Camp Pendleton. Phase II proposes continued development, Arctic Edge flight testing, and expanded sensor payload integration.
