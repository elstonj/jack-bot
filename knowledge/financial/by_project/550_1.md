# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer  
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD)  
  - **Primary TPOC:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department  
  - **Secondary Contact:** Anthony Brescia, Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead  
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) + Phase II with Option Period  
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492  
- **Period of Performance:**  
  - **Phase I Base:** January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)  
  - **Phase II Option I:** July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)  
  - **Current Activity (Option Period):** April 14 – September 28, 2026 (6.5 months)  
- **Brief Scope:** Development of magnetometer payload integration on Black Swift S0-AD and S0-MAD unmanned aircraft systems (UAS) for Navy airfield detection and environmental survey applications. Includes sensor integration (Bartington Compass, QuSpin sensors), ground testing, hand-launched and launcher-deployed flight testing, and demonstration at Camp Pendleton.
