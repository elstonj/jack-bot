# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer (UAS-integrated magnetic anomaly detection and passive acoustic sensing)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD)
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base + Option Period) with Phase II proposal under negotiation
- **Period of Performance:**
  - **Phase I Option Period:** April 14, 2026 – September 28, 2026 (completed)
  - **Phase II Base (proposed):** January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
  - **Phase II Option I (proposed):** July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Integration of magnetic anomaly detection (MAD) and passive acoustic sensing into a small-launch (SL) UAS platform (Black Swift S0-MAD); includes sensor integration, platform modification, system testing, validation, and demonstration flights at Camp Pendleton and Camp LeJeune.
