# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer (Mapping Coherent Atmospheric Structures with Small Unmanned Aircraft Systems)
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD, Patuxent River, MD)
  - **TPOC:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department
  - **Secondary Contact:** Anthony Brescia, Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base, completed Jan 2026) with Phase II Option Period
- **Period of Performance:** 
  - Phase I Option Period: April 14 – September 28, 2026 (6.5 months)
  - Phase II Base Period (Proposed): January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
  - Phase II Option I (Proposed): July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)
- **Brief Description:** Development and demonstration of a magnetometer payload integrated with Black Swift Technologies' S0 UAS platform for naval applications. Phase I option focuses on compressed 6.5-month timeline including design, ground testing, field demonstration at Camp Pendleton (Sep 14–25, 2026), and three Navy-mandated progress/final reports (CLINs 0005–0007).
