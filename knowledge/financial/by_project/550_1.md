## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division – NAWCAD, Patuxent River, MD)
- **Technical Points of Contact:**
  - Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department
  - Anthony Brescia, Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option Period
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Period of Performance:**
  - Base Phase I: January 2026 – (completion date from proposal suggests Phase II starts CY 2027)
  - **Current Phase I Option Period: April 14 – September 28, 2026**
  - Proposed Phase II Base: January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
  - Proposed Phase II Option I: July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)

- **Brief Scope Description:** Development and field demonstration of a magnetometer payload system for small unmanned aircraft systems (S0-MAD and S0-AD variants) to support Navy aeronautical research. Phase I Option deliverables include kick-off/FWA certification, progress reporting, and final demonstration at Camp Pendleton (Sep 14–25, 2026). Technical scope includes onboard sensor integration, data logging, ground testing, hand-launched and launcher-based flight operations, and three Navy-required reports.
