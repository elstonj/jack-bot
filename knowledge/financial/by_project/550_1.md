# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer (Magnetic Anomaly Detection & Passive Acoustic Sensing Integration)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of the Navy (NAVAIR), NAWCAD (Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division)
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option proposal
- **Period of Performance:** 
  - Phase I Option Period: April 14, 2026 – September 28, 2026 (completed)
  - Phase II Base Period (if exercised): January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
  - Phase II Option I (if exercised): July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)
- **Project Status:** **COMPLETED** — Final Report (CLIN 0007) submitted September 29, 2026
- **Brief Description:** Phase I effort demonstrated feasibility of integrating magnetic anomaly detection (MAD) and passive acoustic sensing into a Black Swift Technologies S0 UAS platform (S0-MAD variant). Work included dual sensor integration (magnetometer + acoustic), S0-AD ground launcher design and testing, platform modifications, onboard logging systems, laboratory validation, and field demonstration flights at Camp Pendleton (September 14–25, 2026).
