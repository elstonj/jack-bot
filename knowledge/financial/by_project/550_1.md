## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD)
  - **TPOC:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department
  - **Secondary Contact:** Anthony Brescia, Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option proposal
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Period of Performance:** 
  - Phase I (completed): January 2026
  - **Option Period (Active):** April 14 – September 28, 2026
  - Phase II (proposed): Base January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months); Option I July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)
- **Brief Description:** Development of a magnetometer payload system integrated onto Black Swift Technologies' S0 UAS platform for Navy airborne magnetic field measurement and analysis. Phase I Option period focused on compressed 6.5-month timeline including design, build, ground testing, hand-launched flights, and Camp Pendleton demonstration (Sep 14–25, 2026).
