# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Code:** 550-1  
**Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer  
**Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (Government)  
**Contract Number:** N6833525C0492  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option; currently in Phase I Option Period with Phase II negotiation underway  
**Period of Performance:**
- Phase I Option Period: April 14, 2026 – September 28, 2026 (completed)
- Phase II Base (proposed): January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
- Phase II Option I (proposed): July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Integration of magnetic anomaly detection (MAD) and passive acoustic sensing into a small-launch (SL) UAS platform (S0-AD/S0-MAD variants) for Department of the Navy submarine detection and oceanographic research applications. Phase I demonstrated feasibility through sensor integration, platform modification, ground and flight testing at Camp Pendleton. Phase II will conduct comprehensive testing at Camp LeJeune and Camp Pendleton with full S0 platform validation.

**Key Personnel (from Asana):** Alex Lomis (owner), Beck Cotter, Maciej Stachura, Jack Elston, Meredith O'hara Needham  
**Collaborators:** Cetacean Research Technology (CRT), Ultra Maritime (subcontractor), QuSpin
