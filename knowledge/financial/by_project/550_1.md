# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of the Navy (Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division – NAWCAD)
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option
- **Technical Points of Contact:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes (Physicist, NAWCAD Advanced Technology Development) and Anthony Brescia (Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead)
- **Project Team Lead:** Alex Lomis
- **Period of Performance:**
  - **Phase I Base:** January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
  - **Phase I Option Period (Executed):** April 14, 2026 – September 28, 2026 (6 months)
  - **Option I Period (Proposed):** July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)
- **Project Status:** Phase I completed; Option Period work underway; transitioning to production and export compliance planning

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development of a high-performance magnetometer sensor integrated onto Black Swift Technologies' S0 unmanned aircraft system (UAS) for U.S. Navy applications. Phase I deliverables included design, analysis, and testing of both magnetometer and acoustic sensor payloads, with demonstration flights completed at Camp Pendleton (September 14–25, 2026). Project is moving into production planning, export classification, and integration with advanced avionics systems.
