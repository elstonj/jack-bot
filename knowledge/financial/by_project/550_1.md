- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division – NAWCAD, NAVAIR)
  - **Primary TPOC:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department
  - **Secondary Contact:** Anthony Brescia, Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead

- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option proposal
- **Period of Performance:** 
  - Phase I Option Period (Current): April 14 – September 28, 2026 (6.5 months)
  - Phase II Base Period (Proposed): January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
  - Phase II Option I (Proposed): July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)
- **Brief Description:** Design, build, and flight-test an air-deployable magnetometer payload integrated with Black Swift Technologies S0-AD small unmanned aircraft systems (UAS) for Navy airborne magnetic field survey applications. Compressed 6.5-month timeline includes ground testing, hand-launched flights, Camp Pendleton demonstration (Sep 14–25, 2026), and three Navy technical/administrative reports.
