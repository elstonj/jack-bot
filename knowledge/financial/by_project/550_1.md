# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer (Department of Navy Advanced Technology Development)
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD — Patuxent River, MD)
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option proposal; currently executing **Phase I Option Period**
- **Period of Performance:**
  - Phase I Base: January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months) — *per budget proposal*
  - **Option Period (Asana/Active):** April 14 – September 28, 2026
- **Brief Scope:** Development and demonstration of advanced magnetometer payload integration on Black Swift S0 UAS platforms for Navy maritime/avionics applications. Includes sensor integration (Bartington & QuSpin magnetometers), onboard logging software (Python-based), ground testing, hand-launched flights, and Camp Pendleton operational demonstration flights.
