## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD)
  - **Technical Point of Contact:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department
  - **Secondary Contact:** Anthony Brescia, Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead
  - **Location:** NAWCAD, 22347 Cedar Point Road, Bldg. 2185, Patuxent River, MD 20670
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option; current snapshot covers **Phase I Option Period**
- **Period of Performance:** 
  - **Phase I Option Period (Current):** April 14 – September 28, 2026 (6.5 months)
  - **Phase II Base Period (Proposed):** January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
  - **Phase II Option I Period (Proposed):** July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)
- **Brief Description:** Development and demonstration of an integrated magnetometer system using Black Swift Technologies S0-MAD (Magnetometer Airborne Demonstrator) small unmanned aircraft for naval applications. Compressed 6.5-month timeline includes design, build, ground testing, hand-launched flights, and operational demonstration at Camp Pendleton (Sep 14–25, 2026). Deliverables consist of three Navy-mandated administrative reports (CLINs 0005, 0006, 0007) plus technical milestones.
