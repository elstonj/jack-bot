# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer (Magnetic Anomaly Detection & Passive Acoustic Integration for Small UAS)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of the Navy (SBIR Program)
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies, LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Ultra Maritime (CPFF)
- **Key Contact:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes (Navy); Project Owner: Alex Lomis (BST)
- **Period of Performance:**
  - **Phase I Option Period:** April 14, 2026 – September 28, 2026 (6 months) ✅ **COMPLETE**
  - **Phase II Base Period:** January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months) 🟡 **NEGOTIATION UNDERWAY**
  - **Phase II Option I:** July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months) 🟡 **PENDING**

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Phase I successfully demonstrated technical feasibility of integrating magnetic anomaly detection (MAD) and passive acoustic sensing into a small hand-launched UAS platform (S0-MAD). Phase I work included design, build-up, ground testing, local flight testing, and a field demonstration at Camp Pendleton (Sept 14–25, 2026). Phase II aims to advance the system toward operational integration with Navy platforms (VXS-1 and other systems) with expanded testing, documentation, and multi-platform validation.
