## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of the Navy (SBIR Program)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (completed) with Phase II option under negotiation
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Period of Performance (Proposed Phase II):** January 2027 – June 2030 (Base: 30 months; Option I: 12 months)
- **Key Contact:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes (Navy); Alex Lomis (BST Project Owner)
