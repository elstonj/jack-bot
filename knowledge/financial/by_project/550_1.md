# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer (Mapping Coherent Atmospheric Structures with Small Unmanned Aircraft Systems)
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD), Patuxent River, MD
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option Period
- **Primary TPOC:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department (angel.r.ruiz-reyes.civ@us.navy.mil, (240) 587-9542)
- **Secondary TPOC:** Anthony Brescia, Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead (anthony.d.brescia.civ@us.navy.mil, (240) 538-5265)

**Current Period of Performance:** April 14 – September 28, 2026 (Phase I Option Period, 6.5 months)
**Proposed Future Period:** January 2027 – June 2030 (Phase II Base 30 months + Option I 12 months)

**Brief Scope:** Development of a small unmanned aircraft system (UAS) equipped with magnetometer sensors to map atmospheric structures and magnetic field variations. Phase I Option includes system design, build, ground testing, hand-launched flight testing, and a Camp Pendleton demonstration (Sep 14–25, 2026) with three Navy deliverable reports. Phase II proposal (pending) extends to full system validation and operational deployment.
