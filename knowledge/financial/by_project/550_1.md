# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer (Contract N6833525C0492)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of the Navy (Small Business Innovation Research Program)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option
- **Program Manager:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes
- **Period of Performance:**
  - **Phase I Option Period:** April 14, 2026 – September 28, 2026 (6 months, completed)
  - **Phase II Base Period:** January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months, proposed/negotiating)
  - **Phase II Option I:** July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months, proposed)
- **Brief Description of Scope:**
  
  Phase I successfully demonstrated technical feasibility of integrating magnetic anomaly detection (MAD) and passive acoustic sensing into a small-lift unmanned aircraft system (SL UAS) platform. Key deliverables included design and build-up of hand-launched S0-MAD with integrated sensors, ground testing, local flight testing, and a Camp Pendleton field demonstration (September 14–25, 2026). Phase II (under negotiation) proposes expanded testing with the VXS-1 platform, formalized test event planning, and integration of QuSpin and Bartington magnetometer sensors across multiple flight systems. Prime contractor: Black Swift Technologies, LLC; subcontractor: Ultra Maritime.
