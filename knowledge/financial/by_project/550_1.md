## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD)
  - **TPOC:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department
  - **Secondary Contact:** Anthony Brescia, Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option
- **Period of Performance:**
  - **Phase I Base:** January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
  - **Phase II Option I:** July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)
  - **Phase I Option Period (Historical):** April 14 – September 28, 2026 (6.5 months)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development of a magnetometer payload system integrated on Black Swift Technologies' S0-AD UAS platform for Navy applications, including design, build, ground testing, hand-launched flights, and Camp Pendleton demonstration. Phase II expands to full technical maturation with multiple flight tests at Navy test ranges (Camp LeJeune, Camp Pendleton).
