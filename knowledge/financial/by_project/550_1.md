# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer (Mapping Magnetometer Data with Small UAS)
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD — Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division, Patuxent River, MD)
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option proposal
- **Technical Point of Contact:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department (angel.r.ruiz-reyes.civ@us.navy.mil)
- **Period of Performance:** 
  - Phase I Option Period (Completed): April 14 – September 28, 2026
  - Phase II Base Period (Proposed): January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
  - Phase II Option I Period (Proposed): July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)
- **Project Status:** ✅ **PHASE I OPTION PERIOD COMPLETE** (all CLINs submitted; final report & invoice delivered Sep 29, 2026)
- **Brief Description:** Development of a compact magnetometer sensor package integrated onto Black Swift Technologies' S0-MAD (Magnetometer-Anomaly Detection) unmanned aircraft system for Navy airborne geophysical survey and anomaly detection missions. Phase I option period included design, build, ground testing, hand-launched flight testing, and demonstration at Camp Pendleton (Sep 14–25, 2026).
