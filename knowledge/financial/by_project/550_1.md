# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of the Navy (NAVAIR) — Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division (NAWCAD), Advanced Technology Development
- **Technical POCs:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes (Physicist, NAWCAD) and Anthony Brescia (Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead)
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option selected; currently in Option Period transitioning to Phase II production planning
- **Period of Performance:** 
  - Phase I Option Period: April 14, 2026 – September 28, 2026 (completed)
  - Phase II Base: January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months, proposed)
  - Phase II Option I: July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months, proposed)
- **Project Status:** Active; Phase I Option completed; Phase II proposed and in transition/planning phase
- **Team Lead:** Alex Lomis (Lead Engineer)
- **Priority:** High

**Scope Summary:** Design, develop, and demonstrate a magnetometer sensor payload integrated onto Black Swift Technologies' S0-MAD (small unmanned aircraft system) for naval applications. Phase I included sensor design, analysis, testing, acoustic sensor partnering (Cetacean Research Technology), and Camp Pendleton demonstration flights (Sept 14–25, 2026). Phase II proposes full production, integration, testing, and delivery of multiple S0-AD systems equipped with magnetometer sensors for U.S. Navy and allied (UK/Canada) operations.
