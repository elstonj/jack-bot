- **Period of Performance:**

  - **Phase I Option Period (Active):** April 14 – September 28, 2026 (6.5 months)  
  - **Phase II Proposed Base:** January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)

- **Brief Description of Scope:**  
Develop and demonstrate an integrated magnetometer payload for small unmanned aircraft systems (UAS) to support Navy airborne reconnaissance and science missions. Phase I Option Period focuses on design, ground testing, launcher integration, and preparation for Camp Pendleton demonstration. Phase II (proposed, not yet funded) extends development with expanded testing, demonstrations at multiple Navy facilities, and integration with Navy platforms.
