# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer (Mapping Coherent Atmospheric Structures with Small Unmanned Aircraft Systems)
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD)
  - **TPOC:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department
  - **Secondary Contact:** Anthony Brescia, Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option
- **Period of Performance:** 
  - Base Phase I: January 2026 – June 2026 (6 months)
  - Option Period: April 14 – September 28, 2026 (5.5 months)
  - Phase II (Proposed): Base Period January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months); Option I July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)
- **Brief Description:** Development of a magnetometer payload system for small unmanned aircraft systems (UAS) to map magnetic field anomalies and coherent atmospheric structures. Project involves ground testing, flight operations, integration of Bartington and QuSpin sensors, and demonstrations at Camp Pendleton (Sep 14–25, 2026).
