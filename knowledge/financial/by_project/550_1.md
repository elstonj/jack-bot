Design, build, and demonstrate a magnetometer payload integrated onto Black Swift Technologies S0 UAS platforms for Navy maritime detection and reconnaissance applications. ## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Project Status: **YELLOW FLAG (Compressed Timeline, Budget Ambiguity)**

#### **1. Phase I Option Period: Invoicing Complete, Actual Spending Exceeds Invoicing**

- **Invoiced:** $99,459 (100% of contracted Option Period CLINs)
- **Actual Spend (QBO [550-1]):** $213,000 
- **Delta:** ($113,541) overage, or **114% actual-to-invoiced ratio**

**Assessment:**
- ✓ All three Phase I Option CLINs were invoiced on schedule (Apr 14, Jun 29, Sep 28, 2026).
- ✗ QuickBooks shows actual project spending of $213,000 for [550-1] as of May 2026 update, which significantly exceeds invoiced amounts.
- **Likely Explanation:** The $213,000 figure in QBO likely includes:
  - Equipment procurement (S0 UAS systems, magnetometer sensors: ~$108K per equipment quotes).
  - Internal labor allocations and overhead not yet billed to the Navy (Phase I Option was a cost-plus-fixed-fee contract based on CLIN structure, not a labor-hour reimbursable).
  - Phase I base period carryover costs.
- **Risk:** If Phase I Option Period was intended as a capped $99,459 deliverable-based contract and internal costs are being absorbed, there is a margin squeeze. Alternatively, if equipment costs are being capitalized (not expensed to project), the expense/invoice mismatch is non-critical.
