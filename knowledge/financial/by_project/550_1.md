# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer  
**Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD — Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division)  
**TPOC:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department  
**Contract Number:** N6833525C0492  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) + Phase II Option  
**Period of Performance:**
- Phase I Base: January 2026 – June 2026 (completed)
- **Option Period (Active):** April 14, 2026 – September 28, 2026
- Phase II Proposed Base: January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
- Phase II Proposed Option I: July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)

**Brief Scope:** Development of an integrated magnetometer payload system for small unmanned aircraft (S0-AD platform) to detect and map magnetic field anomalies. Phase I completed January 2026. Current work (Option Period) focuses on design validation, ground testing, launcher development, and preparation for Camp Pendleton demonstration by August 17, 2026.
