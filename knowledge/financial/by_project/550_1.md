# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR Phase I Option Period: Magnetometer
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD — Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division)
  - **TPOC:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department
  - **Secondary Contact:** Anthony Brescia, Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I with Option Period (government contract)
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Period of Performance (Option):** April 14 – September 28, 2026 (6.5 months)
- **Base Period (Phase II — proposed):** January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
- **Brief Scope:** Design, build, and flight-test an onboard magnetometer system for Navy small unmanned aircraft, culminating in Camp Pendleton demonstration (Sep 14–25, 2026). Project includes integration of Bartington and QuSpin magnetic sensors into Black Swift S0 UAS platforms, ground testing, and delivery of three Navy-mandated progress reports.
