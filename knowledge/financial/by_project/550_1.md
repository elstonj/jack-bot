### Explanation of Budget vs. Actual Discrepancy

**Phase I Option Period Budget (Asana metadata):** $242,540 proposed  
**Phase I Option Period Invoicing (QuickBooks):** $99,459.00 invoiced as of 2026-10-07

**Remaining Phase I Option Budget:** $242,540 – $99,459 = **$143,081**

**However, cumulative QuickBooks project code 550-1 shows $905,016.00 total activity through Oct 7, 2026.**

This discrepancy suggests:
1. **Phase I Base period invoicing** (prior to Option Period start on 2026-04-14) is recorded in the cumulative $905,016.00 total but not broken out separately in the provided invoice list.
2. **Phase I Option invoicing** (3 invoices: #1748, #1749, #1750 totaling $99,459) is a subset of the $905,016.00.
3. **Reconciliation limitation:** Drive Budget Docs do not provide a complete invoice history for Phase I Base period; only Phase I Option invoices (#1748–#1750) are explicitly itemized.
