## Project Overview

**Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option  
**Period of Performance:**
- Phase I: Completed (January 2026)
- **Option Period (Current):** April 14, 2026 – September 28, 2026 (6.5 months)

**Project Description:**  
Development of a magnetometer payload system for Navy unmanned aircraft systems (UAS), specifically the Black Swift S0-AD platform. The Option Period focuses on design finalization, ground testing, launcher development, and demonstration flights at Camp Pendleton (scheduled September 14–25, 2026). Three deliverables are required: Kick-Off & FWA Certification (CLIN 0005), Progress Report (CLIN 0006), and Final Report (CLIN 0007).

**Current Status:** Active – Option Period in execution (project kicked off April 21, 2026). Ground testing and launcher development underway. Mostly on schedule as of May 6, 2026, but team bandwidth is constrained by higher-priority projects (Navy STTR, S3 IRAD, S0-VTOL, EMASS closure).
