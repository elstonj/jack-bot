# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer (Magnetic Anomaly Detection & Passive Acoustic Sensing Integration)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of the Navy, Naval Air Systems Command (NAVAIR)
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option proposal
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies, LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Ultra Maritime (CPFF)
- **Period of Performance:** 
  - Phase I Option Period: April 14, 2026 – September 28, 2026 (completed)
  - Phase II Base Period (proposed): January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
  - Phase II Option I Period (proposed): July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)
- **Project Owner:** Alex Lomis
- **Technical Points of Contact:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes (NAVAIR Physicist), Anthony Brescia (Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Demonstrate technical feasibility of integrating magnetic anomaly detection (MAD) and passive acoustic sensing into a small launch (SL) UAS platform. Work includes sensor integration (Bartington fluxgate magnetometer, QuSpin sensors), platform modification (S0-AD airframe), system testing, laboratory validation, and field demonstration flights. Phase I Option Period focused on platform build, sensor integration, onboard logging design, ground launcher design, and Camp Pendleton field testing (Sept 14–25, 2026). Phase II will expand to operational validation and platform delivery.

1. **Subcontractor Costs Dominate:** Ultra Maritime subcontract represents ~30% of Phase II Base and Option I, making it the single largest cost category. Justification for sole-source award provided in documents.

2. **Labor Intensive Project:** Direct labor + fringe + overhead = ~48% of base period cost. Three core team members (Elston, Stachura, Lomis) carry the project across 30-month base and 12-month option periods.

4. **Travel Costs Modest:** ~$17.3K base + $8.6K option = 1.7–2.2% of total, reflecting focused field testing at two DoN locations (Camp LeJeune, Camp Pendleton).

5. **Indirect Cost Rates:** G&A at 18.32% + Fixed Fee at 7% are standard DoN SBIR rates. Fringe (29.28%) and overhead (46.67%) reflect BST's provisional billing rates for internal cost allocation.
