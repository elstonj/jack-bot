# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer (Contract N6833525C0492)
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD — Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division, Patuxent River, MD)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option proposal
- **Period of Performance:** 
  - Phase I Base: January 2026 – June 2026
  - Phase I Option: April 14 – September 28, 2026
  - Phase II Proposed Base: January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
  - Phase II Proposed Option I: July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)
- **Brief Description:** Development of an advanced magnetometer system integrated with Black Swift S0 UAS platform for Naval applications. Compressed 6.5-month Phase I Option period involved design, ground testing, component procurement (Bartington compass, QuSpin sensors), build of hand-launched S0-MAD variant, local test flights, and Navy-required documentation (kick-off briefing, progress report, final report). Demonstration planned for Camp Pendleton, CA (September 14–25, 2026).
