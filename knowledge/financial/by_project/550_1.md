# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Code:** 550-1  
**Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer  
**Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD)  
**Contract Number:** N6833525C0492  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option  
**Period of Performance (Current):** April 14 – September 28, 2026 (Option Period: 6.5 months)  
**Full Program:** January 2027 – June 2030 (Base 30 months + Option 12 months proposed)

**Brief Description:**  
Development of an integrated magnetometer payload system for Navy unmanned aircraft systems (UAS), specifically the BST S0-AD platform. The project involves design, build, ground testing, and field demonstration of dual magnetometer integration (Bartington UAS-Mag DroneCAn compass + QuSpin vector magnetometer sensors) for atmospheric and magnetic anomaly detection. Current phase is an abbreviated 6.5-month option focused on ground testing, hand-launched flights, and Camp Pendleton demonstration (Sept 14–25, 2026) with three Navy-required reports due at milestones.
