# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer (Magnetic Anomaly Detection for Small UAS)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of the Navy (SBIR Program)
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies, LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Ultra Maritime (CPFF)
- **Government POC:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes
- **Project Manager:** Alex Lomis (Asana); Beck Cotter (Phase II lead)

**Period of Performance:**
- **Phase I Option Period:** April 14, 2026 – September 28, 2026 (6 months) ✅ **COMPLETE**
- **Phase II Base Period:** January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months) 🟡 **NEGOTIATING**
- **Phase II Option I:** July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months) 🟡 **PROPOSED**

**Brief Description:**
Develop and demonstrate magnetic anomaly detection (MAD) and passive acoustic sensing capability for small UAS platforms. Phase I successfully integrated MAD/acoustic sensors into hand-launched S0-MAD platform, completed ground testing, and conducted field demonstration at Camp Pendleton (Sept 14–25, 2026). Phase II will expand to larger VXS-1 platform and conduct expanded operational testing at Navy facilities.
