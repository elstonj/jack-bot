# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer  
**Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (Naval Air Systems Command – NAVAIR; Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division – NAWCAD)  
**Contract Number:** N6833525C0492  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base, completed January 2026) with Phase II Option Period  
**Period of Performance:** 
- Phase I Option Period: April 14 – September 28, 2026 (6.5 months)
- Phase II Base: CY 2027–2029 (30 months, proposed)
- Phase II Option I: CY 2029–2030 (12 months, proposed)

**Scope:** Development of a compact, aerial magnetometer payload for integration on Black Swift Technologies S0 unmanned aircraft systems (UAS). The system combines Bartington Instruments fluxgate sensors and QuSpin magnetometer sensors to provide high-precision magnetic field mapping for Navy applications, including demonstrations at Camp Pendleton and Camp LeJeune.

**Key TPOC:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, NAWCAD; (240) 587-9542
