### **Actual Spend vs. Approved Invoicing: ⚠️ COST OVERRUN ALERT**

**Critical Finding:**
- **Phase I Option Period Approved Invoicing:** $99,459
- **Phase I Option Period Actual Expenses (QuickBooks):** $213,000
- **Variance:** **+$113,541 (114% overspend)**

This represents a **significant cost overrun.** The actual expenses ($213,000) are **2.14× the invoiced amount** ($99,459). 

**Possible Explanations:**
1. **Uncompensated internal costs:** BST may have absorbed ~$113,541 in labor, equipment, or overhead costs not billed to the Navy (possible for SBIR Phase I cost-share requirements or internal R&D allocation).
2. **Equipment procurement:** The equipment quote (EQ003) shows $108,000 in S0 UAS systems ordered (Invoice #1744, March 25, 2026, due Apr 24, 2026). If this was expensed to [550-1] but not invoiced separately, it would account for much of the variance.
3. **Accrual vs. cash-basis reporting:** QuickBooks may be recording accrual-basis expenses while Navy invoicing follows milestone/CLIN completion.

**Data Quality Note:** QuickBooks government.md does not itemize cost categories or provide accrual detail for Phase I Option. Cannot determine whether the $213,000 represents paid expenses, accrued costs, or equipment capitalization.
