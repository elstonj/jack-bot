# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer  
**Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD)  
**TPOC:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department  
**Contract Number:** N6833525C0492  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base completed January 2026) with Phase II Option Period  
**Period of Performance:**
- **Phase I Option Period (Active):** April 14, 2026 – September 28, 2026 (6.5 months)
- **Phase II Proposed Base Period:** January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
- **Phase II Proposed Option I:** July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Development of an integrated magnetometer payload system for small unmanned aircraft systems (UAS), including sensor integration (Bartington DroneCAn compass, QuSpin magnetometers), onboard logging and data processing, ground testing, hand-launched flight testing, and demonstration at U.S. Marine Corps Camp Pendleton. Contractor includes Black Swift Technologies (Prime) and Ultra Maritime (Subcontractor). Technical leads: Jack Elston (PI, onboard logging & reporting), Maciej Stachura (Python tools), Alex Lomis (PM, builds & flights), Beck Cotter (Camp Pendleton coordination, UK Navy EOI).
