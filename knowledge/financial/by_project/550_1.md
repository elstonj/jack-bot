## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD)
  - **TPOC:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department
  - **Secondary Contact:** Anthony Brescia, Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option
- **Period of Performance:** 
  - **Phase I Option Period:** April 14 – September 28, 2026 (6.5 months) ✓ **COMPLETED**
  - **Phase II Base Period:** January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months) — *Proposed*
  - **Phase II Option I Period:** July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months) — *Proposed*
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Integration of magnetic anomaly detection (MAD) and passive acoustic sensing into Black Swift's S0 UAS platform. Phase I Option focused on sensor configuration, ground testing, hand-launched flight testing, and Camp Pendleton demonstration. Phase II proposes design, build, test, and operational demonstrations with Navy field deployments (Camp LeJeune, Camp Pendleton).
