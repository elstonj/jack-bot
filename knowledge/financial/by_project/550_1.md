## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division — NAWCAD, NAVAIR)
  - TPOC: Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department
  - Secondary: Anthony Brescia, Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option; Contract # N6833525C0492
- **Period of Performance:** 
  - Phase I Option Period (currently active): April 14 – September 28, 2026
  - Phase II Base (proposed): January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
  - Phase II Option I (proposed): July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)
- **Brief Description:** Development and flight demonstration of small unmanned aircraft systems (S0-AD, S0-MAD) equipped with high-performance magnetometer sensors (Bartington UAS-Mag, QuSpin) for naval applications. Phase I Option includes design, ground testing, hand-launched flights, and Camp Pendleton demonstration flights (Sep 2026).
