# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD — Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division, Patuxent River, MD)
  - **Primary TPOC:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department
  - **Secondary Contact:** Anthony Brescia, Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Base 30 months) with Option I (12 months)
- **Period of Performance (Proposed):** January 2027 – June 2030 (Base) + July 2029 – June 2030 (Option I)
- **Current Active Period:** April 14 – September 28, 2026 (Phase I Option Period)
- **Brief Description:** Design, build, and flight-test a magnetometer payload for small unmanned aircraft systems (UAS) to support Navy atmospheric and magnetic field mapping operations. Compressed 6.5-month timeline includes ground testing, hand-launched flights, and Camp Pendleton demonstration (Sep 14–25, 2026).
