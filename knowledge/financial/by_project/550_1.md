# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD — Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division)
  - Technical Point of Contact: Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department
  - Secondary Contact: Anthony Brescia, Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base, completed January 2026) with Phase II Option period (currently active)
- **Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies, LLC (Prime); Ultra Maritime (Subcontractor)
- **Period of Performance:**
  - Base Period: January 2026 – June 2029 (30 months proposed for Phase II)
  - **Current Active Period (Phase I Option):** April 14, 2026 – September 28, 2026 (6.5 months)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development and demonstration of an integrated magnetometer payload system for small unmanned aircraft systems (S0-AD UAS) for Navy applications. Phase I completed; current option period focuses on system integration, ground testing, launcher development, and field demonstration at Camp Pendleton (Sep 14–25, 2026).
