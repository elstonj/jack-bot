# Project 550-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD)
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option; currently in Phase II proposal stage
- **Period of Performance:** 
  - Phase I Option Period (completed): April 14 – September 28, 2026
  - Phase II Base (proposed): January 2027 – June 2029 (30 months)
  - Phase II Option I (proposed): July 2029 – June 2030 (12 months)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development of a magnetometer payload integrated with Black Swift Technologies S0 unmanned aircraft systems (UAS) for Navy applications. Phase I Option focused on design, ground testing, hand-launched flights, and demonstration at Camp Pendleton (Sep 14–25, 2026). Phase II proposes expanded development with multiple sensor integration, extended field trials at Navy bases (Camp LeJeune, NC and Camp Pendleton, CA), and technical reporting. Prime contractor: Black Swift Technologies, LLC; Subcontractor: Ultra Maritime.
