- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD)
  - Primary Technical Point of Contact: Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department
  - Secondary Contact: Anthony Brescia, Avionics Engineering S&T Domain Lead
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I with Phase II Option proposal
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Period of Performance (Current):** April 14 – September 28, 2026 (6.5 months, Option Period)
- **Status:** 🟡 **ACTIVE — IN EXECUTION** (Final technical milestone execution and administrative closure)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** 
  Compressed 6.5-month development and demonstration of a magnetometer payload system for Navy small unmanned aircraft systems (UAS). Technical scope includes design of ground testing apparatus (S0-MAD), dual magnetometer integration, onboard data logging, Python analysis tools, and hand-launched flight testing culminating in a Camp Pendleton demonstration (Sep 14–25, 2026). Deliverables consist of three Navy-mandated CLINs: Kick-Off & FWA Certification, Progress Report, and Final Report.
