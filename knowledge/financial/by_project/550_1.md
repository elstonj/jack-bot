  - Phase I Base: January 2026 (completed)
  - **Phase I Option Period (Active):** April 14 – September 28, 2026
  - Phase II Proposed: January 2027 – June 2030 (Base 30 months + Option 12 months)

Deliverables include onboard logging of magnetic sensors, Python analysis tools, and final report to Navy.
