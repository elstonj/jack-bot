- **Period of Performance:**
  - **Phase I Base:** January 2026 – completion (implied completed January 2026)
  - **Phase I Option Period (Active):** April 14, 2026 – September 28, 2026 (6.5 months)
  - **Phase II Proposed Base:** CY 2027 – 2029 (30 months)
  - **Phase II Option I (Proposed):** CY 2029 – 2030 (12 months)
- **Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies, LLC (Prime); Ultra Maritime (Subcontractor)
- **Brief Description of Scope:**

Deliverables include three Navy reports with technical and operational data.
