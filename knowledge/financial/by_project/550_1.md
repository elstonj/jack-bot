- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR: Magnetometer
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (NAVAIR, NAWCAD — Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division, Patuxent River, MD)
- **Technical Point of Contact:** Angel Ruiz-Reyes, Physicist, Advanced Technology Development Department
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Base) with Phase II Option proposal
- **Contract Number:** N6833525C0492
- **Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies, LLC (Prime); Ultra Maritime (Subcontractor)
- **Period of Performance (Option I Executed):** April 14 – September 28, 2026 (6.5 months)
