**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and demonstration of an advanced airborne magnetometer system integrated onto Black Swift Technologies' S0 UAS platform for Navy applications. Compressed 6.5-month timeline includes design, build, ground testing, hand-launched flights, and field demonstration at Camp Pendleton (Sep 14–25, 2026). Phase I focuses on proof-of-concept and integration of Bartington and QuSpin magnetic sensors with onboard logging, Python analysis tools, and operational readiness for Navy testing.
