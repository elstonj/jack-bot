#### 2. **Project Schedule Risk** (High Priority)
- **Issue:** All 21 Asana tasks are unassigned and lack due dates; no scheduled timeline exists in project management system.
- **Impact:** 
  - Cannot verify whether deliverables are on track for completion.

  - Scope 2 depends on receipt of fuselages from By Light—no tracking of this dependency in Asana.
  - No resource allocation or critical path visibility.

- **Issue:** Contract is firm fixed price ($126,629.00 total) with no cost escalation or contingency clause.

  - No leverage to adjust scope or budget without formal contract modification.
- **Status:** No cost overruns documented to date, but actual labor and material costs are not tracked in provided data.
- **Mitigation:** 
  - Establish internal timekeeping and material tracking to monitor actual spend vs. $126,629.00 contract cap.
