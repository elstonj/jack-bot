## Financial Health Assessment

### ⚠️ **CRITICAL ISSUES**

#### 1. **Invoice Amount Exceeds Contract Value by $99,629 (79% overage)**

| Metric | Amount | Note |
|--------|--------|------|
| Contract Value | $126,629.00 | Firm Fixed Price PO J20265067 |
| Total Invoiced | $226,258.00 | Sum of all invoices in QuickBooks |
| **Overbilled Amount** | **($99,629.00)** | **78.6% over contract** |

**Analysis:**
- Contract provides for 3 payment milestones: Initial ($15k), Scope 1 ($47,894), and Scope 2 ($63,735) = **$126,629 total**.
- QuickBooks shows **5 invoices totaling $226,258**, including apparent duplicates:
  - Invoice #1756 & #1754 both claim Scope 1 ($47,894 each) = **$95,788 total for $47,894 scope**
  - Invoice #1757 & #1755 both claim Scope 2 ($63,735 each) = **$127,470 total for $63,735 scope**
  - Initial invoice #1753 = $15,000 (appears valid)

**Hypothesis:** Either:
- a) **Duplicate invoices** were submitted by error (invoices #1754 and #1755 may be corrections or duplicates of #1756 and #1757)
- b) **Invoice numbering mismatch** between QuickBooks and actual submissions to By Light
- c) **Scope changes or undocumented amendments** increasing contract value

**Impact:** 
- If duplicates were actually submitted to By Light, **BST may face clawback or invoice rejection**.
- Firm fixed price means **BST absorbs any cost overruns**; overbilling does not increase revenue.
- **Payment risk:** By Light may refuse payment on duplicate invoices or reduce Scope 2 payment by 50%.
