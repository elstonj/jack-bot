- **Contract Type:** Firm Fixed Price Purchase Order (PO J20265067 / J20255067)

  - **Scope 2:** Within one month of receipt of fuselages (manufacturing/build completion)

### Brief Description of Scope
This is a continuation of the Mustang platform work (Phase 2). The project involves design, engineering, and manufacturing of two (2) flight-ready M2 platforms (Halo airframe) for By Light, including:
- **Scope 1:** Airframe design validation, flight testing, CAD deliverables (~8 weeks)
- **Scope 2:** Complete build-out of two (2) M2 platforms with integrated avionics (BST mounting), laser altimeter (LW20/C), pouch cell battery design, car launch compatibility, Chilli wing/tail sets, actuator boards, and ADS-B transponders (Ping200x)

Key integration areas: avionics, gimbal integration, car launch mounting, sensor integration, and design iteration based on flight analysis.
