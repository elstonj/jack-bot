- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Navy (Department of Defense)

- **Subrecipient:** Old Dominion University Research Foundation
- **Contract Type:** STTR Phase I Subcontract
- **Submission Date:** January 29, 2025
- **Proposal Evaluation Debrief:** April 23, 2025
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development and validation of the S0 expendable atmospheric profiling system for Navy operations in hazardous weather.
