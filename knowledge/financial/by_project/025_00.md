## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** N25A-T025-0009 — Expendable Air-Sea Profiling Observations in Hazardous Weather Conditions via Small Uncrewed Aircraft System
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Navy (Department of Defense)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies
- **Subrecipient:** Old Dominion University Research Foundation
- **Contract Type:** STTR Phase I Subcontract
- **Solicitation:** DOD STTR 25.A for Navy, Topic N25A-T025-0009
- **Proposal Submission Date:** January 29, 2025
- **Period of Performance:** Not explicitly stated in provided documents
- **Brief Description of Scope:**
  
  Develop and validate the S0 expendable atmospheric profiling system for Navy operations in hazardous weather. Work includes engineering enhancements to integrate S0 with Navy platforms (sonobuoy launchers, P-8A Poseidon external pod mounts, CH-53E helicopter mounts), calibration and validation activities against reference systems (instrumented towers, NCAR dropsondes), error analysis of wind and turbulence measurements, algorithm refinement for vertical velocity estimation, and onboard data processing enhancement with quality control capabilities for remote oceanic operations.
