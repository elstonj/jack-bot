- **Contract Type:** STTR Phase I Subcontract (Old Dominion University Research Foundation as subrecipient; Black Swift Technologies as prime contractor)

- **BST Product Focus:** S0 Expendable Profiling System
- **Key Personnel:** Dr. John Park (PI, Old Dominion University); Beck Cotter (Black Swift Technologies contact)

**Scope Summary** *(from Proposal)*:
Engineering enhancements and integration of the S0 expendable atmospheric profiling system with Navy platforms (sonobuoy launch systems, P-8A Poseidon aircraft, CH-53E helicopters). Phase I activities include error analysis, calibration/validation testing against reference systems (instrumented towers, NCAR dropsondes), algorithm refinement for vertical velocity and turbulence estimation, and onboard data processing development for remote oceanic operations in hazardous weather conditions.
