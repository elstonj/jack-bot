## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Expendable Air-Sea Profiling Observations in Hazardous Weather Conditions via Small Uncrewed Aircraft System
- **Proposal Number:** N25A-T025-0009
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Navy (Department of Defense)
- **Contract Type:** STTR Phase I Subcontract
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies
- **Subrecipient:** Old Dominion University Research Foundation
- **Key Personnel:** Dr. John Park (Principal Investigator, ODU); Beck Cotter (Black Swift Technologies contact)
- **Period of Performance:** January 29, 2025 (proposal submission) – Phase I duration not explicitly specified in available documents
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development and validation of the S0 expendable atmospheric profiling system for Navy operations in hazardous weather conditions. Work includes engineering enhancements for integration with Navy launch platforms (sonobuoy systems, P-8A Poseidon external pod mounts, CH-53E helicopter mounts), calibration and validation activities (wind tunnel tests, field deployments, comparative studies with instrumented towers and NCAR dropsondes), error analysis of wind/turbulence measurements, and onboard data processing development with quality control capabilities for remote oceanic environments.
