- **Full Project Name:** Adaptive and Secure Autonomy for UAS: A Modular Approach to Flight Control, Machine Learning Integration, and Multi-Vehicle Coordination
- **Client/Agency:** NASA (Shared Services Center, Stennis Space Center, Mississippi)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25C0155
- **Principal Investigator:** Dr. Jack Elston
- **Period of Performance:** September 29, 2025 – March 27, 2026 (6 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — All work completed successfully; final administrative closure April 16, 2026

**Scope Summary:** Development of modular, layered architecture for SwiftCore avionics system enabling aircraft autonomy with multi-vehicle coordination, machine learning-based sensing/control, and safe sandbox framework for experimental modules. Deliverables included 5-layer functional architecture, terrain-following ML module with automated PID tuning, supervisory controller, flight/bench testing, and JSBSim integration.
