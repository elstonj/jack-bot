- **Project Name:** Autonomy (SwiftCore Modular Architecture for Flight Control, Machine Learning Integration, and Multi-Vehicle Coordination)
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Shared Services Center (Stennis Space Center, Mississippi)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25C0155
- **Principal Investigator:** Dr. Jack Elston, Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Period of Performance:** September 29, 2025 – March 27, 2026 (6 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — All work completed; final payment received April 16, 2026

**Scope Summary:** Develop modular, layered architecture for SwiftCore avionics system enabling aircraft autonomy with multi-vehicle coordination, machine learning-based sensing and control, safe sandbox framework for experimental modules, and supervisory controller with safety protocols. Deliverables include 5-layer functional architecture, ML terrain-following module with automated PID tuning, plugin architecture integration, JSBSim simulation environment, flight and bench tests, and comprehensive technical reporting.
