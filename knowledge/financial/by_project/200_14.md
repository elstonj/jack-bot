# Project 200-14 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Adaptive and Secure Autonomy for UAS: A Modular Approach to Flight Control, Machine Learning Integration, and Multi-Vehicle Coordination
- **Client/Agency:** NASA (Shared Services Center, Stennis Space Center, Mississippi)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25C0155
- **Period of Performance:** September 29, 2025 – March 27, 2026 (6 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — All work completed and final payment received; project closed April 16, 2026
- **Principal Investigator:** Dr. Jack Elston, Black Swift Technologies LLC

**Brief Description:** Development of a modular, layered architecture for the SwiftCore avionics system enabling aircraft autonomy with multi-vehicle coordination and machine learning-based sensing and control. Work included architecture definition, messaging protocol specification, safe sandbox framework, ML-enabled control loop tuning, and supervisory controller development. All four major milestones and five CLINs completed on schedule.
