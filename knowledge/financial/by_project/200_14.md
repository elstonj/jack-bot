# Project 200-14 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** [200-14] Autonomy  
**Full Title:** Adaptive and Secure Autonomy for UAS: A Modular Approach to Flight Control, Machine Learning Integration, and Multi-Vehicle Coordination

**Client/Agency:** NASA (Shared Services Center, Stennis Space Center, MS)  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I  
**Contract Number:** 80NSSC25C0155  
**Principal Investigator:** Dr. Jack Elston, Black Swift Technologies LLC

**Period of Performance:** September 29, 2025 – March 27, 2026 (6 months)

**Project Status:** ✅ **ARCHIVED & COMPLETE**
- Final payment received: April 16, 2026
- Project closed: April 16, 2026
- All deliverables completed and closed out

**Brief Scope Description:**  
Phase I R&D effort to develop and validate a modular, layered architecture for SwiftCore avionics enabling aircraft autonomy. Work included: (1) definition of a five-layer architecture with pub/sub messaging protocol; (2) SwiftCore firmware/software updates (4.0 SBC and RTOS Autopilot board); (3) machine learning–based automatic PID controller tuning proof-of-concept; (4) safe sandbox framework for integrating experimental control modules; and (5) benchtop, simulation, and flight test validation. All technical deliverables completed on schedule.
