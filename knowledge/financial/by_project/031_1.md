### **Category Analysis Notes:**

1. **UAS Flight Systems:** Proposed budget splits between S3 ($65K per PO 142750) and S2 ($44.6K per Invoice 1652). **Critical mismatch:** contract specifies S3; invoicing shows S2 platform. Likely both platforms procured, or platform type misspecified. Actual spend approximately matches proposed for each platform type independently.

2. **Batteries & Ancillary Equipment:** Proposed and actual spend align; S3 batteries specified in PO 142750 but S2 batteries invoiced in 1652/1653, confirming platform discrepancy.

3. **Launcher & Payload:** S2-specific equipment (launcher $19.6K, photogrammetry payload $23.5K) proposed and invoiced in full (03/05/2025). No equivalent S3 launcher/payload in PO 142750, further confirming platform spec mismatch.

4. **Training & Maintenance:** Two separate training line items ($3K each) in PO 142750 and Invoice 1652 suggest potential duplicate billing or bundled services. Maintenance package ($7.5K for 3 years) extends beyond contract expiration (05/31/2026), creating obligation ambiguity.

5. **Shipping:** Both S2 and S3 (inferred) shipping costs included; total ~$5K across both proposed budgets.
