## Discrepancy Analysis

### ⚠️ CRITICAL: Budget Overrun & PO Mismatch

**Finding 1: Invoiced Amount Exceeds Approved PO #142750**
- **Approved (PO #142750):** $81,306.00
- **Invoiced (PO #142750 + PO #132664):** $102,181.00
- **Overage:** $20,875.00 (125.7% of approved budget)

**Finding 2: PO #132664 ($100,213) Not Documented in Budget Approval**
- Invoice #1652 (03-05-2025, $100,213) references **PO #132664**, which is **NOT listed in the Drive budget documents** provided.
- Contents of PO #132664 invoice: S2 UAS system, launcher, photogrammetry payload, training, 3-year maintenance package.
- **These are S2 systems, not S3 systems as specified in PO #142750.**
- Both invoices are recorded in QuickBooks against project code **(031-1)**, suggesting they may be related sub-orders or prior commitments for the same project.

**Finding 3: Specification Mismatch**
- **Contract/PO #142750 Line Items:** Lists "S3 VTOL UAS Flight System" (primary deliverable)
- **Drive Budget Breakdown:** Reflects S3 system, S3 batteries, spare S2 batteries, training, shipping
- **Asana Project Notes:** State "Owe them 2 S3 but sending 2 S2s or S0"; notes indicate **product substitution not yet approved by customer**
- **Invoice #1652 (PO #132664):** Explicitly includes S2 UAS system (not S3), plus S2 Pneumatic Launcher, S2 Photogrammetry Payload
- **Question:** Was PO #132664 (S2 equipment) pre-authorized as part of the overall UMES engagement, or is this an unapproved substitution/scope creep?

**Finding 4: Asana Risk Flag – Product Spec Unresolved**
- Asana notes: "Contract P.O. #142750 specifies Qty 1 'S3 VTOL'; project notes state 'Owe them 2 S3 but sending 2 S2s or S0'; contract Products/Services field lists only 'S0, S2, Training Materials and Supplies' (no S3). **No customer approval documented for substitution.**"
- This suggests the actual product being delivered may **not match the contract commitment**, creating legal/financial risk.
