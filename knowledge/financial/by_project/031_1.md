**Financial Implication:** BST has invoiced $100,213 for S2 equipment and services that may not match the UMES contract requirement for S3. **At minimum, $95,229 of Invoice #1652 (all items except the spare batteries and training already on PO 142750) appears unauthorized or represents scope creep not approved by UMES.**
