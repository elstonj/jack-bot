**Analysis:**

- **Contracted Budget (PO #142750):** $81,306.00 (S3 VTOL + batteries + training + shipping)
- **Actual Invoiced:** $102,181.00 (S2 system + launcher + payload + maintenance + training + shipping)

**Key Findings:**

1. **Product Mismatch:** Contract specifies S3 VTOL ($65K); invoiced equipment includes S2 system ($44,619) instead. No S3 VTOL invoiced.
2. **Scope Creep:** Invoice #1652 includes equipment not in original PO #142750:
   - S2 Pneumatic Launcher ($19,575) — not contracted
   - S2 Photogrammetry Payload ($23,535) — not contracted
   - Advanced Maintenance Package 3-year subscription ($7,500) — not contracted
3. **Batteries:** Original PO included 2x S3 batteries ($7,338) + 2x S2 spare batteries ($1,968); actual invoiced only 1x S2 spare ($984) + 1x S2 spare via Invoice #1652 ($984) = $1,968 total (matches contracted amount for S2 spares; S3 batteries not invoiced).
4. **Training & Shipping:** Training invoiced as contracted ($3,000); shipping invoiced below budget (S2 shipping $1,000 vs. $4,000 contracted).
5. **Duplicate Entries:** Spare Battery S2 appears in both PO #142750 ($1,968) and Invoice #1652 ($984), suggesting possible double-billing or line item consolidation.
