## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Project Status: ⚠️ **CRITICAL CONCERNS — MULTIPLE UNRESOLVED ISSUES**

#### 1. **Budget Overage**
- **Contract Amount (PO #142750):** $81,306.00
- **Total Invoiced:** $102,181.00
- **Overage:** **$20,875.00 (25.7% over contract)**
- **Root Causes:**
  - Undocumented PO #132664 added $100,213.00 (S2 system + launcher + payload + maintenance) with no formal change order or budget modification documented
  - Duplicate or unclear operator training invoicing ($3,000 × 2 = $6,000 vs. $3,000 budgeted)
  - Advanced Flight Maintenance Package ($7,500/3 years) not in original PO #142750
  - Additional shipping costs ($5,000 actual vs. $4,000 budgeted)

**Assessment:** Project is significantly over-budget. Lack of documented budget modifications or change orders suggests inadequate contract control or scope creep.

---

#### 2. **Critical Product Specification Mismatch**
- **Contract Specifies:** S3 VTOL UAS (PO #142750 line item: "$65,000 S3 VTOL UAS Flight System")
- **Invoiced Deliverable:** S2 UAS System (Invoice 1652: "$44,619 S2 UAS Flight System + $19,575 S2 Pneumatic Launcher + $23,535 S2 Photogrammetry Dual Payload")
- **Asana Notes:** "Owe them 2 S3s but sending S2s or S0. No written customer confirmation of actual product shipped."
- **Invoice 1765 (05/18/2026):** Appears to invoice S3 VTOL balance ($65,000), but:
  - Amount matches original S3 line item (invoice narrative not provided)
  - Issued 13 days before contract expiration
  - **Unclear if S3 was actually delivered or if invoice is corrective/retroactive**

**Assessment:** 
- **CRITICAL RISK:** Contract-customer mismatch creates potential legal/compliance exposure. UMES paid for S3 VTOL; unclear what platform was actually delivered.
- **Required Immediate Action:** Obtain written confirmation from UMES (Chris Hartman) documenting what platform(s) were actually received and accepted.
- **Training Implications:** Scheduled S3 training (07/23–25) should align with delivered platform — if S2 was shipped, training curriculum/content may be incorrect.
