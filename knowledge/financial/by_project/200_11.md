## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Persistence Demo (NASA) — Demonstrating persistent IR measurements over wildfires using small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III, Fixed-Price Research & Development (R&D)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED & CLOSED** — Contract completed and officially closed November 24, 2025. All deliverables accepted; 100% of contract value invoiced and paid.

**Scope Summary:** Demonstrate 24-hour persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using Black Swift's S2 small uncrewed aircraft platform (equipped with NightFOX IR payload and Remote ID detection systems). Establish operational CONOPS for scalable wildfire monitoring. Live demonstration executed August 27–28, 2025 at Pawnee National Grassland with attendees from USFS, NASA, and USDA.
