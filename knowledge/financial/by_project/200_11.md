## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Persistence Demo (NASA) — Demonstrating persistent IR measurements over wildfires using small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III, Fixed-Price Research & Development (R&D) Demonstration
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED** — Contract completed and closed November 24, 2025
- **Primary NASA Contact:** Matthew Fladeland, NASA Ames Technology Transfer Office, 650.604.3325, matthew.fladeland@nasa.gov

**Scope Summary:** Demonstrate persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using Black Swift Technologies' S2 platform (small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft) with integrated NightFOX IR payload and Remote ID detection system. Establish scalable, operational CONOPS for 24-hour wildfire monitoring. Key deliverable: live 24-hour persistence demonstration at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025) with USFS, NASA, and USDA participants.
