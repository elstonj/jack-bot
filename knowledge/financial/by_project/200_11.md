# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Fixed Price)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED** — Contract completed and closed November 24, 2025. All deliverables accepted; 100% of payments received.

**Brief Scope:** Demonstrate persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the S2 small uncrewed aircraft platform with NightFOX IR payload. Project included development of Remote ID detection systems, payload data ingestion app, and operational CONOPS demonstration with USFS, NASA, and USDA stakeholders. Key event: 24-hour persistence demonstration August 27–28, 2025 at Pawnee National Grassland, Colorado.
