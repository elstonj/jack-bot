# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Research & Development Demonstration)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED** — Contract completed and officially closed November 24, 2025

**Brief Scope Description:** Demonstrate persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using Black Swift Technologies' S2 small fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft platform, integrated with NightFOX IR payload and Remote ID detection systems. Project culminated in a successful 24-hour persistence demonstration at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025) with attendees from USFS, NASA, and USDA. Deliverables included IT Security Management Plan, two quarterly/final demonstration reports, and technical certifications.
