# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Project Name:** Persistence Demo (NASA)
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III, Fixed-Price Demonstration Contract
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Primary Contact:** Matthew Fladeland, NASA Ames Technology Transfer Office, 650.604.3325, matthew.fladeland@nasa.gov

**Scope:** Demonstrate persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft (S2 platform) and establish a scalable, operational concept of operations (CONOPS) for 24-hour wildfire monitoring. Project included development of Remote ID detection system, payload data ingestion application, and live demonstration event at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025).
