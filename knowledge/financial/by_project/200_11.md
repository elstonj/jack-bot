# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Fixed Price)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED** — Contract completed and officially closed November 24, 2025. All deliverables accepted and payments received.
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Demonstrate persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the S2 small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft platform and establish a scalable, operational concept of operations (CONOPS) for 24-hour wildfire monitoring. Project included technical development of Remote ID detection systems, payload integration, live demonstration event (August 27–28, 2025 at Pawnee National Grassland), and comprehensive reporting.
