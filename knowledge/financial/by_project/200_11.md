# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Persistence Demo (NASA) — Demonstrating persistent IR measurements over wildfires using small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III, Fixed-Price
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Status:** ARCHIVED (completed and officially closed November 24, 2025)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Demonstrate 24-hour persistent infrared (IR) wildfire monitoring capability using BST's S2 fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft platform (NightFOX payload), establish operational CONOPS, and conduct live demonstration at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025).
