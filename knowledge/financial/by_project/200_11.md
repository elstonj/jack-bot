# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Research & Development Demonstration)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Code:** 200-11
- **Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED & CLOSED** — All deliverables accepted; contract fully paid and officially closed November 24, 2025

**Project Scope:** Demonstrate persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the BST S2 small uncrewed aircraft platform and establish an operational concept of operations (CONOPS) for 24-hour wildfire monitoring capability. Key technical deliverables included integration of NightFOX IR payload, Remote ID detection system, payload data ingestion app, and live 24-hour persistence demonstration at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025) with USFS, NASA, and USDA attendees.
