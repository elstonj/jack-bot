# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III, Fixed-Price Research & Development Demonstration
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED — Contract completed and officially closed November 24, 2025
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Demonstrate persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the S2 small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft platform with NightFOX IR payload. Conduct live 24-hour persistence demonstration at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025) and establish a scalable operational CONOPS for continuous wildfire monitoring with integrated Remote ID detection system and TAK data downlink capability.
