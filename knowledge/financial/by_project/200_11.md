# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III, Fixed-Price Research & Development (R&D)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED** — Contract completed and officially closed November 24, 2025
- **Brief Description:** Demonstration of persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the S2 small uncrewed aircraft platform with integrated NightFOX payload and Remote ID detection system. Project included a 24-hour live demonstration at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025) with USFS, NASA, and USDA participation. All deliverables accepted and all payments received.
