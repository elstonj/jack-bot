   - **The difference ($49,241.82) represents the embedded overhead, G&A, and fee components** ($16,270 + $10,841 + $4,411 = $31,522) plus additional markup/bundling within the fixed deliverable prices.
   - ✅ This is **normal for fixed-price contracts** and represents appropriate margin capture by BST.
