# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Persistence Demo — Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Fixed Price)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED & CLOSED** (November 24, 2025)
- **Brief Description:** Demonstration of 24-hour persistent infrared (IR) monitoring of wildfires using BST's S2 uncrewed aircraft platform with integrated NightFOX IR payload and Remote ID detection system. Live demonstration conducted August 27–28, 2025 at Pawnee National Grassland with participation from USFS, NASA, and USDA.
