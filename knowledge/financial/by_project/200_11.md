# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Fixed Price)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED** — Contract completed and closed November 24, 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Demonstrate persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the Black Swift S2 platform (small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft) and establish a scalable, operational CONOPS for 24-hour wildfire monitoring. Key achievements include integration of Remote ID detection systems, development of payload data ingestion application, and successful live 24-hour persistence demonstration at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025) with attendees from USFS, NASA, and USDA.
