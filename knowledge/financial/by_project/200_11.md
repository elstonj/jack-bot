# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Research & Development Demonstration)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED — Contract completed and closed November 24, 2025**

**Scope Summary:** Demonstrate persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using Black Swift Technologies' S2 small uncrewed aircraft platform. Project included integration of Remote ID detection systems, payload data ingestion app development, and a live 24-hour persistence demonstration at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025). All deliverables accepted by NASA; 100% of contract value invoiced and paid.
