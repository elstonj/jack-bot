# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Project Code:** 200-11
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III, Fixed-Price Research & Development (R&D) Demonstration
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED & CLOSED** (November 24, 2025)

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Demonstrate persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using Black Swift Technologies' S2 small uncrewed aircraft platform with NightFOX IR payload. Establish scalable, operational concepts of operations (CONOPS) for 24-hour wildfire monitoring. Key event: successful 24-hour persistence demonstration at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025) with participation from USFS, NASA, and USDA.
