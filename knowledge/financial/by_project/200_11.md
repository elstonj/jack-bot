# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating persistent IR measurements over wildfires using small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Research & Development demonstration)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **COMPLETED & CLOSED** (November 24, 2025)
- **Brief Description:** Demonstration of persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the S2 small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft platform with integrated NightFOX IR payload and Remote ID detection system. Project included development of operational CONOPS for 24-hour wildfire monitoring and a live demonstration event at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025) with USFS, NASA, and USDA attendees.
