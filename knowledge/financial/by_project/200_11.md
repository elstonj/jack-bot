# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Persistence Demo (NASA) – Demonstrating persistent IR measurements over wildfires using small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III, Fixed-Price Research & Development (R&D)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Primary Contact:** Matthew Fladeland, NASA Ames Technology Transfer Office, 650.604.3325, matthew.fladeland@nasa.gov
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED** — Contract completed and closed November 24, 2025. All deliverables accepted, all payments received.

**Scope Summary:** Demonstrate persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the BST S2 uncrewed aircraft platform with NightFOX payload. Project included integration of Remote ID detection systems, development of payload data ingestion applications, and execution of a 24-hour persistence demonstration event at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025) with participation from USFS, NASA, and USDA.
