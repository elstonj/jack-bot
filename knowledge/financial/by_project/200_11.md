# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Research & Development Demonstration)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED — Contract completed and closed November 24, 2025. All deliverables accepted; all payments received.
- **Brief Description:** Demonstrated persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the S2 small uncrewed aircraft platform and established a scalable operational concept of operations (CONOPS) for 24-hour wildfire monitoring. Key deliverable was a live 24-hour persistence demonstration conducted August 27–28, 2025 at Pawnee National Grassland with attendees from USFS, NASA, and USDA.
