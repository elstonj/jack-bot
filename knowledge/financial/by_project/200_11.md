# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Persistence Demo (NASA) — Demonstrating persistent IR measurements over wildfires using small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III, Fixed-Price R&D Demonstration
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED** — Contract completed and closed November 24, 2025. All deliverables accepted and payments received.
- **Brief Description:** Demonstration of persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the Black Swift S2 uncrewed aircraft platform. Project culminated in a live 24-hour persistence demonstration at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025) with attendees from USFS, NASA, and USDA. Key achievements include integration of NightFOX IR payload, Remote ID detection system, and operational CONOPS for wildfire monitoring.
