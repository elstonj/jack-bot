# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Fixed Price R&D Demonstration)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Primary Contact:** Matthew Fladeland, NASA Ames Technology Transfer Office, 650.604.3325

**Project Scope:** Demonstrate persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the S2 platform (small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft) with integrated NightFOX IR payload and Remote ID detection systems. Establish operational CONOPS for 24-hour wildfire monitoring capability. Live demonstration conducted August 27–28, 2025 at Pawnee National Grassland with attendees from USFS, NASA, and USDA.

**Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED & CLOSED** — Contract completed and officially closed November 24, 2025. All deliverables accepted. 100% of contract value invoiced and paid.
