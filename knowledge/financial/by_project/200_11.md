# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Research & Development)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED** — Contract completed and closed November 24, 2025. All deliverables accepted; all payments received.
- **Primary Contact:** Matthew Fladeland, NASA Ames Technology Transfer Office, 650-604-3325, matthew.fladeland@nasa.gov

**Scope:** Demonstrate persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the BST S2 small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft platform and establish a scalable operational concept of operations (CONOPS) for 24-hour wildfire monitoring. Key deliverable was a live 24-hour persistence demonstration conducted August 27–28, 2025 at Pawnee National Grassland, CO.
