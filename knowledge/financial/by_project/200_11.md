## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Persistence Demo (NASA) — Demonstrating persistent IR measurements over wildfires using small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III, Fixed Price
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED** — Contract completed and closed November 24, 2025. All deliverables accepted and all payments received.
- **Brief Description:** Demonstration of persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using a Black Swift S2 platform (fixed-wing small uncrewed aircraft) with integrated NightFOX payload and Remote ID detection capabilities. Project culminated in a successful 24-hour live demonstration at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025) with attendees from USFS, NASA, and USDA.
