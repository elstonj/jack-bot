# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Project Name:** Persistence Demo (NASA)
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Fixed Price)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED & CLOSED** (November 24, 2025)

**Scope Description:** Demonstrate persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft (S2 platform) and establish a scalable, operational CONOPS for 24-hour wildfire monitoring. Project included development of Remote ID detection systems, payload integration, and a live 24-hour demonstration event at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025) with USFS, NASA, and USDA participation.
