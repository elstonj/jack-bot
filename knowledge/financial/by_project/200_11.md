# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Research & Development)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED — Completed and closed November 24, 2025**

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Demonstration of persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the S2 small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft platform with integrated NightFOX IR payload and Remote ID detection system. Project included payload development, 24-hour persistence flight demonstration (August 27–28, 2025 at Pawnee National Grassland), integration with operational CONOPS, and coordination with USFS, NASA, and USDA partners.
