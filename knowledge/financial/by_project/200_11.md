# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating persistent IR measurements over wildfires using small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III, Fixed-Price Research & Development
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Primary Contact:** Matthew Fladeland, NASA Ames Technology Transfer Office, 650.604.3325
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED (Contract completed and officially closed November 24, 2025)
- **Brief Scope:** Demonstrate persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the S2 small uncrewed aircraft platform with NightFOX IR payload; establish operational CONOPS for 24-hour wildfire monitoring; conduct live 24-hour persistence demonstration at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025).
