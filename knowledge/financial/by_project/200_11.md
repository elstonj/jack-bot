# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Fixed Price)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED & CLOSED** (November 24, 2025)
- **Brief Description:** Demonstrated 24-hour persistent infrared (IR) monitoring of wildfires using Black Swift Technologies' S2 small uncrewed aircraft platform integrated with NightFOX thermal payload. Established operational concept of operations (CONOPS) for scalable wildfire monitoring with coordination from USFS, NASA, and USDA. Live demonstration conducted August 27–28, 2025 at Pawnee National Grassland.

### Category-by-Category Analysis

**1. Direct Labor (Proposed $34,906 | Actual Tracked $25,186.18 | 72.2% used)**
- **Status:** Under budget by $9,719.82 on tracked labor.
- **Detail:** 
  - Estimated 280 hours budgeted (PI 60 hrs, Engineer 60 hrs, Technician 80 hrs, Pilot 60 hrs @ specified rates + fringe).
  - Actual logged hours: ~234 hours (approximately 46 hours under estimate).
  - Peak activity in August: PI/Elston/Stachura logged 105:31:13 hours (well over estimated 60 hrs for the entire project).
  - Other personnel logged within or under estimated hours.
- **Concern:** Labor budget was tight, with PI logging 105+ hours in August alone during demo preparation. However, fixed-price contract absorbed overages; no cost impact to NASA.

**2. Equipment & Materials (Proposed $6,000 | Actual $3,440.13 | 57.3% used)**
- **Status:** Under budget by $2,559.87.
- **Detail:** 
  - S2 daily rental budgeted at 2 days × $3,000/day = $6,000.00 not explicitly invoiced; instead, SkyGuard Technologies equipment invoice #13260 for $3,120.00 (radio spectrum analyzer, screen, signal generator).
  - Small materials purchases through July–August (~ $320.13 in other supplies).
- **Assessment:** Equipment procurement was efficient; lower-than-budgeted material spend suggests either shared resources or efficient procurement.

**3. Indirect Costs & Fee (Proposed $27,111 + $4,411 = $31,522)**
- **Status:** Data not available from QuickBooks; embedded in fixed contract invoices.
- **Detail:** Overhead (46.61% of base labor) and G&A (18.32% of total direct costs) + 6.30% fee were calculated during proposal phase but not separately tracked in monthly actuals. These costs are recovered through the $74,428.00 fixed contract price delivered via three milestone invoices.
