# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Persistence Demo (NASA) — Demonstrating persistent IR measurements over wildfires using small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III, Fixed Price R&D Demonstration
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED & CLOSED** (November 24, 2025)
- **Primary Contact:** Matthew Fladeland, NASA Ames Technology Transfer Office, 650.604.3325, matthew.fladeland@nasa.gov

**Brief Scope:** Demonstrate 24-hour persistent infrared (IR) wildfire monitoring capability using Black Swift Technologies' S2 small uncrewed aircraft platform with integrated NightFOX IR payload and Remote ID detection systems. Establish scalable operational concept of operations (CONOPS) for wildfire surveillance in coordination with USFS, NASA, and USDA.
