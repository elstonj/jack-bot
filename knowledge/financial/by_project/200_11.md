# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Research & Development Demonstration)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED & CLOSED** — Contract completed and officially closed November 24, 2025. All deliverables accepted; all payments received.
- **Brief Description:** Demonstrated persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the S2 fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft platform. Key achievement: 24-hour continuous flight demonstration at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025) with integrated Remote ID detection system and real-time TAK data downlink, coordinated with USFS, NASA, and USDA partners.
