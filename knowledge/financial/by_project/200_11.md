# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III, Fixed-Price Research & Development
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED & CLOSED** (November 24, 2025)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Demonstrate persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using Black Swift's S2 small uncrewed aircraft platform equipped with NightFOX IR payload and Remote ID detection systems. Establish operational CONOPS for 24-hour wildfire monitoring with coordination from USFS, NASA, and USDA. Project culminated in a successful 24-hour persistence demonstration at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025).
