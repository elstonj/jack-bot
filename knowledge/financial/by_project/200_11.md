# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Research & Development)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED & CLOSED** (November 24, 2025)
- **Project Code:** 200-11

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Demonstrate persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the Black Swift Technologies S2 small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft platform. Technical scope includes integration of NightFOX IR payload, Remote ID detection system (ground and airborne variants), payload data ingestion application, and operational concept of operations (CONOPS) coordination with USFS, NASA, and USDA. Key deliverable: 24-hour live persistence demonstration conducted August 27–28, 2025 at Pawnee National Grassland.
