# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Research & Development)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED & CLOSED** (November 24, 2025)

**Scope:** Demonstrate 24-hour persistent infrared (IR) monitoring of wildfires using the S2 fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft platform with integrated NightFOX payload and Remote ID detection systems. Establish a scalable operational CONOPS for wildfire monitoring coordination with USFS, NASA, and USDA partners. Live demonstration conducted August 27–28, 2025 at Pawnee National Grassland.
