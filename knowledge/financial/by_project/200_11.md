## Budget Reconciliation: Contract Value vs. Tracked Spend

**Key Finding:** There is a significant gap between the $74,428 contract value (fully invoiced) and the $27,368.98 tracked direct costs.

**Explanation (from Drive Budget Docs):**
- The contract is structured as **three fixed-price milestone deliverables:** $35,000 (Interim Report, 03/28/25) + $35,000 (Final Report, 09/15/25) + $4,428 (Flight Report, 09/15/25) = $74,428.
- These invoice amounts are **not itemized labor/materials breakdowns;** they are lump-sum deliverable payments.
- The detailed budget breakdown ($34,906 labor base + fringe + $6,000 materials + $27,111 indirect + $4,411 fee) totals $72,428 before fee, or $74,428 with fee.
- However, only labor and materials were tracked in the Monthly Budget Tracking spreadsheet ($25,186.18 as of Sept 2025, including $3,440.13 materials and ~$21,746 labor).
- **Indirect costs and fee** ($27,111 + $4,411 = $31,522) are **not separately tracked** in the provided QuickBooks data or project tracking sheets.

**Reconciliation:**
- **Invoiced:** $74,428.00 (100% of contract)
- **Tracked Direct Costs:** $27,368.98 (labor + materials)
- **Unaccounted for (in tracking data):** ~$47,059 (corresponds approximately to indirect costs $27,111 + fee $4,411 + potential cost pool allocations or accruals)
