# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Persistence Demo (NASA) — Demonstrating persistent IR measurements over wildfires using small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III, Fixed-Price Research & Development (R&D) Demonstration
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED** — Contract completed and officially closed November 24, 2025. All deliverables accepted and all payments received.
- **Brief Description:** Demonstrated persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the S2 small unmanned aircraft system (UAS) platform with NightFOX payload. Project included integration of a Remote ID detection system, payload data ingestion app development, and a live 24-hour persistence demonstration event at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025) with USFS, NASA, and USDA participation.

### Budget vs. Contract Value Reconciliation

**Question:** Why is direct cost tracking ($25,186.18) so much lower than contract value ($74,428.00)?

**Answer:** This is a **fixed-price deliverable contract**, not a time-and-materials engagement. The $74,428 award is divided into:
- **Deliverable payments (100% of contract):** $35,000 (Demo Report #1) + $35,000 (Final Report) + $4,428 (Flight Report) = **$74,428**
- **Embedded in each payment:** Direct labor, materials, indirect costs (OH + G&A), and fee are all bundled

The "Direct Costs Tracked" figure of $25,186.18 represents only the costs that BST's finance team itemized by account category (labor wages, material invoices, travel, equipment). The remaining ~$49,242 consists of:
- Untracked or overhead-allocated costs (e.g., facilities, general administrative salaries)
- Fee/profit margin (budgeted at $4,411 = 6.30% of cost base)
- Contingency or cost absorption

This is normal and healthy for a fixed-price federal R&D contract.
