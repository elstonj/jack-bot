# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Persistence Demo (NASA) – Demonstrating persistent IR measurements over wildfires using small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III, Fixed Price Research & Development
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED (contract completed and officially closed November 24, 2025)
- **Brief Description:** Demonstrate 24-hour persistent infrared (IR) monitoring capability over wildfires using Black Swift's S2 small uncrewed aircraft platform with NightFOX IR payload, integrated Remote ID detection system, and TAK data downlink. Culminated in live 24-hour persistence demonstration at Pawnee National Grassland, August 27–28, 2025.
