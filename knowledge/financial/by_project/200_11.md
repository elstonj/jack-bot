# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Research & Development)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED / Completed and closed November 24, 2025
- **Primary Contact:** Matthew Fladeland, NASA Ames Technology Transfer Office, 650.604.3325

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Demonstrate persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the S2 small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft platform equipped with the NightFOX IR payload. Establish a scalable, operational concept of operations (CONOPS) for 24-hour wildfire monitoring. Key deliverable: live 24-hour persistence demonstration conducted August 27–28, 2025 at Pawnee National Grassland with attendees from USFS, NASA, and USDA.
