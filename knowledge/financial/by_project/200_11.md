#### 3. **Budget Model Mismatch — Key Finding**
The significant gap between the $74,428 contract value and the $25,186.18 tracked direct costs reveals that **this is a milestone-based/fixed-price contract, not a cost-reimbursable contract:**
- The three invoices ($35,000 + $35,000 + $4,428) represent deliverable payments, not itemized cost billing.
- The $74,428 includes embedded overhead (OH), G&A, and profit, which are **not separately tracked in the QuickBooks actuals provided.**
- Estimated indirect costs and fee total $31,242.00 ($27,111.00 + $4,411.00), leaving only $39,428 for direct costs—and BST has spent $25,186.18 of that.
- **This structure means BST's actual profit margin depends on how efficiently they execute, not on the invoice amounts.**
