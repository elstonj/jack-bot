## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Project Status: **ON BUDGET (Contractually Complete)**

#### ✅ Strengths
1. **Contract Fully Invoiced:** All $74,428.00 in authorized funding has been invoiced as of 09/15/2025, 15 days before period end (09/30/2025). This demonstrates on-time project completion.
2. **Direct Cost Tracking Under Control:** Tracked direct costs ($25,186.18) represent only 63.9% of $39,428.00 budgeted for direct items, leaving $14,241.82 unspent.
3. **Labor Within Estimate:** Actual labor hours logged (~234 hours) are under the 280-hour estimate by ~46 hours (~16% under).
4. **Materials Under Budget:** Materials & Supplies spent $3,440.13 of $6,000.00 budgeted (57.3% utilization), leaving $2,559.87 unspent.

#### ⚠️ Observations & Clarifications
1. **Fixed-Price Deliverable Model:** The project operates on **milestone-based fixed pricing** ($35K + $35K + $4.428K = $74,428K), not hourly cost reimbursement. This means:
   - The $74,428 contract value is **not directly comparable** to the $25,186.18 in tracked direct costs.
   - Much of the overhead (OH: $16,270), G&A ($10,841), and fee ($4,411) is embedded in the three milestone invoices and not separately tracked in the Monthly Budget Tracking spreadsheet.
   - Only direct labor hours and materials are itemized in the tracking data; indirect costs and profit are not separately expensed.

2. **Labor Utilization Variance by Role:**
   - **PI (Elston/Stachura):** Budgeted $11,635.00 and actual spend exactly matches this; hours logged show -48:30:13 discrepancy, suggesting administrative/accounting adjustment.
   - **Engineer & Technician/Pilot:** Appear to be under budget, but allocation is unclear from the provided tracking data.
   - **Peak Activity:** August 2025 saw the highest spending ($21,516.66), with PI logging 105:31:13 hours in a single month—consistent with flight operations phase.

3. **Payment Status Unknown:** 
   - Invoices #1660 (issued 03/28/2025, due 04/27/2025), #1699, and #1700 (issued 09/15/2025, due 10/15/2025) are marked **"Issued"** in Drive Budget Documents.
   - **Data not available from QuickBooks** regarding whether payments have been received from NASA.
   - If Invoice #1660 payment (due 04/27/2025) has not been received, this represents a 5+ month payment delay as of end of project (09/30/2025).
