# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Persistence Demo (NASA)
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III, Fixed-Price Research & Development
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED** — Contract completed and closed November 24, 2025

**Project Scope:** Demonstrate persistent infrared measurements over wildfires using small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft (S2 platform) and establish a scalable, operational concept of operations for 24-hour wildfire monitoring. Key deliverables included IT Security Management Plan, Interim Demonstration Report #1, Final Report, and live 24-hour persistence demonstration at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025).
