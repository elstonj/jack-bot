## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Persistence Demo (NASA) — Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Research & Development demonstration)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED** — Contract completed and closed November 24, 2025. All deliverables accepted; all payments received.

**Brief Scope:** Demonstrate persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the S2 uncrewed aircraft platform with NightFOX payload and establish scalable operational CONOPS for 24-hour wildfire monitoring. Key achievement: successful 24-hour persistence demonstration at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025) with USFS, NASA, and USDA attendees.
