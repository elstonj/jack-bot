# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Project Code:** 200-11
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Research & Development Demonstration)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED & CLOSED** (November 24, 2025)
- **Primary Contact:** Matthew Fladeland, NASA Ames Technology Transfer Office, 650.604.3325, matthew.fladeland@nasa.gov

**Scope Summary:** Demonstrated persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the Black Swift Technologies S2 fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft platform with integrated NightFOX IR payload and Remote ID detection systems. Established operational concept of operations (CONOPS) for 24-hour wildfire monitoring with participation from USFS, NASA, and USDA. Key demonstration event conducted August 27–28, 2025 at Pawnee National Grassland, CO.
