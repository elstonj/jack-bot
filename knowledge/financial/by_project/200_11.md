## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Persistence Demo (NASA) — Demonstrating persistent IR measurements over wildfires using small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Research & Development demonstration)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED (contract completed and officially closed November 24, 2025)
- **Primary Contact:** Matthew Fladeland, NASA Ames Technology Transfer Office, 650.604.3325

**Scope Summary:** Demonstrate persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the S2 small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft platform and establish a scalable operational concept of operations (CONOPS) for 24-hour wildfire monitoring. Key deliverable: live 24-hour persistence demonstration at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025) with integration of Remote ID detection system and NightFOX IR payload.
