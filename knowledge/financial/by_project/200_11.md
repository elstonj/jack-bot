# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Persistence Demo (NASA) — Demonstrating persistent IR measurements over wildfires using small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III, Fixed-Price Research & Development
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED & CLOSED** — Contract completed and officially closed November 24, 2025. All deliverables accepted; all payments received.
- **Primary Contact:** Matthew Fladeland, NASA Ames Technology Transfer Office, 650.604.3325

**Scope Summary:** Demonstrated persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the S2 uncrewed aircraft platform with NightFOX thermal payload. Conducted live 24-hour persistence demonstration August 27–28, 2025 at Pawnee National Grassland with USFS, NASA, and USDA coordination. Integrated Remote ID detection systems (airborne and ground-based) and developed operational concepts of operations (CONOPS) for scalable wildfire monitoring.
