# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III, Fixed-Price Research & Development
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Primary Contact:** Matthew Fladeland, NASA Ames Technology Transfer Office, 650-604-3325, matthew.fladeland@nasa.gov
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED — Completed and closed November 24, 2025**
- **Brief Scope:** Demonstrate persistent infrared measurements over wildfires using the S2 small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft platform. Establish scalable, operational CONOPS for 24-hour wildfire monitoring. Integrate Remote ID detection systems and develop real-time payload data ingestion application. Key deliverable: live 24-hour persistence demonstration at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025) with USFS, NASA, and USDA coordination.
