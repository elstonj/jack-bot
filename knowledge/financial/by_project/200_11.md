# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft (S2 Platform)
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Research & Development Demonstration)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED & CLOSED** (November 24, 2025)

**Scope:** Demonstrate persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the Black Swift S2 fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft platform and establish a scalable, operational concept of operations (CONOPS) for 24-hour wildfire monitoring. Key deliverable: live 24-hour persistence demonstration at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025) with USFS, NASA, and USDA participation.
