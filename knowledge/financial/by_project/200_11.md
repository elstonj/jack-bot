# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Research & Development Demonstration)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED** — Contract completed and closed November 24, 2025. All deliverables accepted and payments received.
- **Brief Description:** Demonstrated persistent IR measurements over wildfires using the S2 small, fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft platform with NightFOX IR payload. Project included development of a Remote ID detection system, payload data ingestion application, and operational CONOPS validation through a live 24-hour persistence demonstration at Pawnee National Grassland (August 27–28, 2025).

- Indirect overhead (46.61% of labor base = $16,270)
- General & Administrative (18.32% of costs = $10,841)
- Fee/profit (6.30% = $4,411)

This is **normal and expected** for government contracts. The $25,186.18 in direct costs tracks actual labor effort and materials; the indirect and fee components are built into the milestone payment amounts.
