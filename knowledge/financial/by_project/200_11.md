## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Research & Development)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED & CLOSED** — Contract completed and officially closed November 24, 2025. All deliverables accepted and all payments received.
- **Brief Description:** Demonstrated persistent infrared measurement capabilities for wildfire monitoring using the Black Swift S2 small uncrewed aircraft platform with NightFOX IR payload. Successfully integrated Remote ID detection systems and developed real-time data ingestion application.
