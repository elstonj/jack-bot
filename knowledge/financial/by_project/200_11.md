# Project 200-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Demonstrating Persistent IR Measurements Over Wildfires Using Small, Fixed-Wing Uncrewed Aircraft
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III, Fixed-Price R&D Demonstration
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED & CLOSED** (November 24, 2025)

**Scope Summary:** Demonstrate persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the Black Swift S2 small uncrewed aircraft platform and establish a scalable, operational concept of operations (CONOPS) for 24-hour wildfire monitoring. Key deliverable: live 24-hour persistence demonstration conducted August 27–28, 2025 at Pawnee National Grassland with USFS, NASA, and USDA attendees. All technical deliverables accepted; contract fully invoiced and paid.
