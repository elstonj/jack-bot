- **Client/Agency:** NASA Ames Research Center
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase III (Fixed Price)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25CA043
- **Period of Performance:** January 2025 – September 2025 (9 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED** — Contract completed and closed November 24, 2025. All deliverables accepted; all payments received.
- **Brief Description:** Demonstrated persistent infrared (IR) measurements over wildfires using the S2 small uncrewed aircraft platform with NightFOX payload. Conducted 24-hour persistence demonstration August 27–28, 2025 at Pawnee National Grassland with USFS, NASA, and USDA coordination. Integrated Remote ID detection systems and developed real-time payload data ingestion capabilities.
