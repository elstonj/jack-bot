## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Project Status: **CAUTION — Multiple Financial Risks**

#### 1. **Budget Overage (Equipment/Invoicing)**
- **Invoiced amount ($102,181) exceeds documented S3 VTOL component budget ($75,338) by $26,843 (135% of budget).**
- This overage likely reflects the April 2025 invoicing for the earlier S2/S0 delivery, but the split between S2/S0 and S3 components is not documented.
- **Risk:** If the entire $102,181 invoicing relates only to the S3 component, the project is significantly over budget. If it includes both S2/S0 and S3, allocation needs clarification.

#### 2. **Contract Scope Mismatch — CRITICAL**
- **Asana notes clearly state: "Owe them 2 S3s but sending S2s or S0."**
- This indicates a fundamental contract/delivery discrepancy. Customer expected 2× S3 units; BST appears to be delivering lower-cost S2 or S0 variants instead.
- **Financial Impact:** Unquantified. If customer disputes the delivery, BST may be obligated to supply additional S3 units at no additional charge, or may face payment withholding.
- **Recommendation:** Urgent clarification needed with UMES (Chris Hartman) on contractual obligations vs. actual scope.

#### 3. **Unbudgeted Warranty Costs**
- **S3 VTOL has a power issue requiring return and repair.** Return shipping label sent 2026-09-06 (6 days late), return due 2026-10-12.
- **Repair task is unassigned** (potential bottleneck) and cost is unknown.
- **Impact:** Warranty repair and logistics are unbudgeted expenses that will reduce project profitability or require adjustment.

#### 4. **Post-Delivery Support Costs (Training & Logistics)**
- **Training scheduled July 23–25, 2026** requires Dan Prendergast travel and support. No travel dates confirmed; no cost budget provided.
- **Return logistics and repair coordination** are ongoing and may incur additional labor and shipping costs.
- **Impact:** Pending invoicing; current budget does not appear to account for these post-delivery service costs.

#### 5. **Cash Flow Status: Positive (Short-term)**
- **Payments received ($102,181) match invoices issued**, indicating on-time payment from UMES. No payment delays or disputes noted in QuickBooks data.
- **Risk:** No additional S3 invoices recorded in QuickBooks as of October 2026, suggesting either billing is delayed or the contract may be treated as closed despite ongoing support obligations.

#### 6. **Burn Rate & Period of Performance**
- **S3 VTOL budget expires 2026-05-31**, but training and repair activities extend beyond that date (training July 2026, repair due ~2026-10-12).
- **Costs incurred after budget expiration** may not be recoverable unless additional change orders or support contracts are in place.
- **Recommendation:** Confirm with UMES whether post-delivery training and warranty repair are included in original contract or require separate billing.
