- **Client:** NOAA / National Hurricane Center (NHC)
- **Program:** 2024 Hurricane Field Program (HFP), Mature Stage Science
- **BST Role:** Deployment of Black Swift S0 uncrewed aircraft systems from NOAA P-3 aircraft
