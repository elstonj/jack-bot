## Summary Recommendations

1. **Immediately clarify contract scope with UMES:** Confirm whether 2 S3 units or 1 S3 unit are contractually obligated. If 2 are owed, quantify unfunded liability and develop mitigation plan.

2. **Reconcile invoiced amount ($102,181) against contract value ($81,306):** Determine if additional services were billed, if a contract amendment exists, or if billing error occurred. Provide customer with corrected statement if needed.

3. **Assign ownership and establish timeline for S3 power issue repair:** Designate technical owner, define repair scope, estimate cost, and clarify whether warranty or customer-paid repair.

4. **Capture training delivery costs:** Track Dan Prendergast's time and travel for July 23–25 training; determine if customer should be invoiced separately or if absorbed by original contract.

5. **Close out project formally:** Once repair is complete, unit returned, and training delivered, finalize all costs and reconcile against approved budget. Document lessons learned on contract mismatch and post-delivery cost management.
