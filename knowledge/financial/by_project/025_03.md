# Project 025-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 025-03

**Full Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Phase II (Air Force D2P2 SBIR)

**Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of the Air Force)

**Contract Type:** Direct-to-Phase II (D2P2) SBIR (Small Business Innovation Research)

**Solicitation:** AFX255-DPCSO1

**Proposal Number:** F2D-16162

**Period of Performance:** 24 months (proposed; specific start/end dates not provided in source documents)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Adaptation of Black Swift Technologies' proven S0 UAS platform for integration with U.S. Air Force C-130 aircraft to provide high-resolution atmospheric boundary layer measurements for enhanced hurricane forecasting. Includes aircraft certification, redesigned deployment mechanisms, cybersecurity compliance, and demonstration of at least 10 sorties with operational C-130 platforms. Deliverables include 20 prototype units and comprehensive test/evaluation reports.
