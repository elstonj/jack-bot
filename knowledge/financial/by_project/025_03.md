## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 025-03  
**Project Name:** S0 Hurricane (Air Force Direct-to-Phase II SBIR)  
**Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of the Air Force)  
**Contract Type:** Direct-to-Phase-II (D2P2) SBIR  
**Solicitation:** AFX255-DPCSO1  
**Proposal Number:** F2D-16162  
**Period of Performance:** 24 months (project duration per proposal technical volume)  
**Date Submitted:** FY25 (Modified 2025-03-06)

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Black Swift Technologies is adapting its proven S0 UAS platform for integration with U.S. Air Force C-130 aircraft to provide high-resolution atmospheric boundary layer measurements (3D wind, temperature, humidity, pressure) for hurricane forecasting and operational situational awareness. The project includes aircraft certification, redesigned deployment mechanisms, cybersecurity compliance, and operational demonstration with at least 10 sorties using operational C-130 platforms. Deliverables include 20 prototype units and comprehensive test/evaluation reports.

- Dr. Jun A. Zhang (Scientist, University of Miami)
