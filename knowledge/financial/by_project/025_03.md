## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source:** RICO SUAVE proposal documentation (NOAA/National Hurricane Center Program)

### S0 Hurricane Platform Deployments (Primary Activity)
The S0 Hurricane project (consuming ~$589K of project actuals) is associated with **RICO SUAVE** — a NOAA-led experiment deploying small uncrewed aircraft systems (sUAS) from P-3 aircraft into hurricane environments.

**Technical Deliverables:**
- Boundary-layer thermodynamic, kinematic, and turbulence measurements in mature hurricane eyewalls, inflow layers
- Real-time situational awareness data for National Hurricane Center (NHC) hurricane track and intensity forecasting
- High-resolution atmospheric and oceanographic observations in high-risk hurricane regions

**Operational Milestones (from RICO SUAVE plan):**
- Six coordinated flight patterns including eyewall circumnavigation, inflow sampling, turbulence characterization
- P-3 and sUAS concurrent operations with expendable instrument deployments (dropsondes, streamSondes, AXBT, SFMR)
- Data transmission and validation protocols for real-time NHC support

**Financial Implication:** S0 Hurricane is the largest active sub-project ($589,203 YTD) with consistent quarterly invoicing (2025-03 through 2026-09), suggesting ongoing operational support and platform sustainability costs.
