# Project 025-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 025-03  
**Project Name:** S0 Hurricane (Air Force D2P2 SBIR)  
**Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of the Air Force)  
**Contract Type:** Direct-to-Phase II (D2P2) SBIR  
**Solicitation:** AFX255-DPCSO1  
**Proposal Number:** F2D-16162  
**Period of Performance:** 24 months (proposal submitted FY25, document modified 2025-03-06)  

**Brief Scope:**  
Black Swift Technologies is adapting its proven S0 UAS platform for integration with U.S. Air Force C-130 aircraft to provide high-resolution atmospheric boundary layer measurements for hurricane forecasting. Deliverables include aircraft certification, redesigned deployment mechanisms, cybersecurity compliance, 20 prototype units, and demonstration of at least 10 operational sorties with C-130 platforms over 24 months.

**Key Personnel:**
- Dr. Jack Elston (CEO, Principal Investigator)
- Dr. Joshua Wadler (Co-PI, Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University)
- Major Ian Park (End-User Point of Contact, 53rd Weather Reconnaissance Squadron)
