# Project 025-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 025-03  
**Project Name:** S0 Hurricane (Air Force Direct-to-Phase II SBIR)  
**Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of the Air Force)  
**Contract Type:** Direct-to-Phase-II (D2P2) SBIR  
**Solicitation:** AFX255-DPCSO1  
**Proposal Number:** F2D-16162  
**Period of Performance:** 24 months (FY25 — estimated completion FY27)  
**Last Document Update:** 2025-03-06 (proposal technical volume)

**Scope Description:**  
Adaptation of Black Swift Technologies' proven S0 UAS platform for integration with U.S. Air Force C-130 aircraft to provide high-resolution atmospheric boundary layer measurements (3D wind, temperature, humidity, pressure) for hurricane forecasting and operational situational awareness. Deliverables include aircraft certification, redesigned deployment mechanisms, cybersecurity compliance, and demonstration of at least 10 sorties with operational C-130 platforms. Total planned deliverables: 20 prototype units and comprehensive test/evaluation reports over 24 months.
