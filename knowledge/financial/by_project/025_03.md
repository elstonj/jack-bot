- **Projected 24-month spend at current rate:** ~$1,219,606
- **Range observed:** Low month ($36K, May) to high month ($140.4K, June)

### Key Observations

1. **Budget Data Gap:** Without the executed contract budget ceiling, cannot determine if project is on-budget, overspent, or underspent. This is a **critical missing data point** for financial health assessment.

2. **Expense Volatility:** Monthly spending ranges from $36K to $140K, suggesting variable invoicing patterns or milestone-based payments rather than steady burn. June 2025 showed the highest spend ($140.4K); May showed the lowest ($36K). This volatility is typical for hardware development and prototype manufacturing projects.

3. **Payment Status:** All QuickBooks entries for S0 Hurricane Phase II are marked "Paid." No outstanding or overdue invoices noted.

4. **Project Staffing & Scope:**  
   - D2P2 proposal indicates involvement of:
     - Dr. Jack Elston (BST Founder, CEO, PI)
     - Dr. Joshua Wadler (Co-PI, Embry-Riddle)
     - Dr. Jun A. Zhang (Scientist, University of Miami)
     - Air Force end-users (53rd Weather Reconnaissance Squadron)
   - Suggests likely subcontractor costs for Embry-Riddle and University of Miami (no separate invoicing visible for these in provided data).

5. **Risk Factors:**
   - **No budget oversight data:** Cannot verify spend is within approved limits.
   - **No milestone completion status:** Asana data not provided; cannot confirm technical progress aligns with financial spend.
   - **Aircraft certification dependency:** C-130 certification is a regulatory gate with potential for delays and cost overruns if FAA/DoD issues arise.
   - **Prototype production phase ahead:** If burn rate increases during months 12–18 (production ramp-up for 20 units), monthly costs could exceed recent averages.

6. **Recommendations for Finance Team:**
   - **Obtain executed contract:** Retrieve contract document with approved budget, CLINs, and budget ceiling to enable variance analysis.
   - **Reconcile subcontractor costs:** Embry-Riddle and University of Miami invoices should be tracked separately or cross-referenced to confirm co-PI/subcontractor expenses are captured.
   - **Establish milestone burn rates:** Align upcoming deliverables (certification, prototype production, flight trials) with projected spending to flag cost overrun risks early.
   - **Monitor Asana for schedule:** Track task completion vs. invoice dates to ensure spending matches progress.
