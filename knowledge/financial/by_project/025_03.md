## Documented Deliverables & Milestones

### RICO SUAVE (NOAA/NHC Hurricane Deployment)
**Source:** RICO SUAVE research plan document (2025-03-05)

- **Deliverable:** Black Swift S0 uncrewed aircraft deployment into hurricane environments
- **Objective:** High-resolution atmospheric/oceanographic boundary-layer observations to improve hurricane track and intensity forecasts
- **Technical Components:**
  - Six modular flight patterns (eyewall circumnavigation, inflow layer sampling, inflow-layer turbulence, etc.)
  - Real-time data transmission and situational awareness to National Hurricane Center
  - Coordinated sampling with NOAA P-3 aircraft (collocated dropsondes, streamSondes, AXBT, SFMR)
  - Target environment: Mature hurricane eyewall and inflow regions
- **Status:** Document dated 2025-03-05; consistent high invoicing to "S0 Hurricane" through 2025-09 suggests active/ongoing operations
