# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS), on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, October 2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Scope Summary:** Development, integration, and deployment of uncrewed small aircraft systems (S0 UAS platforms) with meteorological payloads (Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR sensors, laser altimeter, video/imaging) for real-time hurricane research data collection. Includes non-recurring engineering for payload integration, flight automation, NOAA data compliance (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), and in-field operational support for hurricane deployments (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton in 2024).
