# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); Government research contract
- **Contract/PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (issued 02/02/2022)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via multiple addenda; originally 10/31/2023)
- **Brief Scope Description:** Development, integration, deployment, and field support of Black Swift S0 sUAS platforms equipped with NOAA METOC payload (Vaisala PTH sensors, IR imaging, laser altimetry, video) for real-time atmospheric data collection in tropical cyclones. Includes NRE for payload integration, automation, real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), in-storm hurricane deployments (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton), and calibration flights.
