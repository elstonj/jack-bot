# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, dated 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Scope:** Development and deployment of small Unmanned Aircraft Systems (sUAS) platforms with integrated METOC (Meteorological & Oceanographic) payload for in-storm hurricane research, including hardware (16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with Vaisala sensors, IR cameras, laser altimeters), non-recurring engineering (NRE) for payload integration and real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), in-field support, and hurricane deployment operations (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton 2024).
