# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/Atmospheric Oceanography and Meteorology Lab [AOML], Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via multiple addenda)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Design, development, integration, and deployment of small unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS) platforms with integrated meteorological (METOC) payloads for real-time tropical cyclone research, including Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking infrared sensors, laser altimeters, and video/image capture. In-field hurricane deployment support and data processing.
