# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via addendum)
- **Brief Description:** Development, integration, and deployment of sUAS (small Unmanned Aircraft Systems) platforms with integrated meteorological and oceanographic (METOC) payloads for real-time data collection in tropical cyclone research. Scope includes hardware (S0 UAS platforms with Vaisala sensors, IR camera, laser altimeter, video/image capability), non-recurring engineering (NRE) for payload integration and NOAA real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM generation), field support, and travel for hurricane deployments.
