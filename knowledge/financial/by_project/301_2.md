# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/Atlantic Oceanographic and Meteorological Laboratory [AOML] Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **Period of Performance:** October 31, 2021 – June 30, 2025 (extended via Addendum 4, October 24, 2024)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated February 2, 2022)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Brief Description:** Development, integration, and deployment of sUAS (small Unmanned Aircraft Systems) platforms equipped with METOC (Meteorological/Oceanographic) payloads for real-time tropical cyclone data collection. Scope includes hardware procurement (16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with Vaisala RD41 sensors, IR imaging, laser altimeter, video capability), non-recurring engineering for payload integration and automation, NOAA real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), and field deployment support for hurricane missions.
