# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **Contract/PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, dated 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Brief Description:** Development and deployment of small unmanned aircraft systems (S0 UAS platforms) with integrated METOC (meteorological and oceanographic) payloads for real-time hurricane research data collection. Scope includes hardware procurement (16–20 fully assembled S0 units with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR sensors, laser altimeters), non-recurring engineering for payload integration and automation, field deployment support for active hurricane operations (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton in 2024), and data processing/algorithm development.
