# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II / NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Scope:** Development, integration, and deployment of small uncrewed aircraft systems (S0 VTOL platforms) equipped with integrated meteorological payloads (Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR sensors, laser altimeter) for real-time tropical cyclone observation and data collection. Includes non-recurring engineering (NRE) for payload integration, automation, NOAA data format compliance (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), in-field support, and hurricane deployment operations (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton in 2024).
