# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Development, integration, and deployment of Small Unmanned Aircraft Systems (sUAS) platforms equipped with meteorological payloads (Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR, laser altimeter, video/image capture) for in-situ hurricane research. Includes 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units, Area-I MHTP integration for Altius 600 platform, non-recurring engineering, real-time data processing (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM formatting), field deployments, and travel support for hurricane operations.
