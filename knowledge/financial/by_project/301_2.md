# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, dated 10/24/2024)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development, integration, and deployment of multiple small uncrewed aerial systems (sUAS) platforms with integrated meteorological (METOC) and oceanographic payload sensors (Vaisala RD41, IR sensors, laser altimeter, video/imaging). Integration of Area-I MHTP (Multi-Hole Turbulence Probe) with Altius 600 platform. Non-recurring engineering for real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM generation), full automation, and field deployment in support of hurricane research operations. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units, engineering documentation, and in-field operational support during active hurricane season deployments (2024–2025).
