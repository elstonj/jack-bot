# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Funding Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Brief Scope:** Development, integration, and field deployment of multiple small Unmanned Aircraft Systems (sUAS) platforms (S0 variants) equipped with integrated METOC (Meteorological & Oceanographic) payloads including Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, infrared sensors, and laser altimeters. Includes Non-Recurring Engineering for payload integration, full automation, real-time NOAA data format compliance (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), and in-storm hurricane deployment support across multiple 2024 tropical cyclones (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton).
