# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, dated 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development, integration, and deployment of small uncrewed aerial systems (sUAS) with meteorological payloads (Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR sensors, laser altimeter, video/image capture) for tropical cyclone research. Includes hardware procurement (16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS platforms with integrated METOC payload and Area-I MHTP integration), non-recurring engineering, real-time data processing, WMO HDOBS formatting, and in-storm hurricane deployment support (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton 2024).
