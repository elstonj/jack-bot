# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (issued 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, dated 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Scope Summary:** Development and deployment of sUAS (small Unmanned Aircraft System) platforms with integrated meteorological and oceanographic (METOC) payload for hurricane research. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensor, laser altimeter, and video/image capture; integration support for Area-I MHTP probe; non-recurring engineering for payload integration and automation; real-time data processing (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS formatting); and field deployment support including in-storm hurricane flights (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton in 2024).
