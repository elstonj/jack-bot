# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) / NOAA Atlantic Oceanographic and Meteorological Laboratory (AOML) Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); SBIR Phase II equivalent structure
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via addendum)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Brief Description:** Development, integration, and deployment of small unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS) equipped with NOAA-compatible meteorological payloads (Vaisala PTH sensors, IR, laser altimeter) for real-time tropical cyclone data collection. Includes platform hardening, full automation, WMO/AirOPS data formatting, and in-storm hurricane deployments (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton).
