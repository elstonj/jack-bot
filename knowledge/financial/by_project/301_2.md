**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Design, development, integration, and field deployment of sUAS (small unmanned aircraft systems) platforms equipped with METOC (Meteorological & Oceanographic) payloads for in-situ hurricane research. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with integrated Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR sensors, laser altimeters, and video/image capture; Area-I MHTP integration support; non-recurring engineering for payload integration and real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM); field support and deployment during hurricane events (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton); and comprehensive project documentation.
