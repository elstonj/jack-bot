# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, October 2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Brief Scope:** Development, integration, and deployment of sUAS (small Unmanned Aircraft Systems) platforms with METOC (meteorological and oceanographic) payload sensors for NOAA hurricane research. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with integrated Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensor, laser altimeter, and video/image capture capability. Work includes non-recurring engineering for payload integration, real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), automation enhancement, and in-field hurricane deployment support.
