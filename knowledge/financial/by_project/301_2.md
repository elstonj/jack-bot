# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/Atmospheric Oceanography Research and Development Lab, Hurricane Research Division)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via multiple addenda)
- **Brief Description:** Development, integration, and deployment of small unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS) S0 platforms with integrated METOC (meteorological and oceanographic) payloads for hurricane reconnaissance and data collection. Work includes hardware fabrication, non-recurring engineering (NRE), payload integration, in-storm deployment support, and real-time data processing for NOAA standards (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS formatting, VDM generation).
