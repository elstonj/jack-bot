# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency**: University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type**: Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **Contract/PO Number**: PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance**: 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts**: Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Brief Description of Scope**: Development, integration, and deployment of autonomous small Unmanned Aircraft Systems (sUAS) platforms with integrated meteorological and oceanographic (METOC) payloads for real-time tropical cyclone research. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensors, laser altimeters, and video/image capture; integration support for Area-I MHTP (Multi-Hole Turbulence Probe) on Altius 600 platforms; non-recurring engineering for payload integration, flight automation, real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM generation); project documentation; and in-field support including deployment to active hurricanes (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton, 2024).

   - Direct labor costs (billed hourly or monthly as part of invoices 1581–1641)
   - Overhead/indirect costs (embedded in invoice rates)
   - Professional services (NRE, engineering, program support)

3. **Variance Analysis**:
   - The large positive variance (+$817,537.42 remaining) does **not** indicate the project is underspent. Rather, it reflects that the majority of project costs (labor, NRE, overhead) are captured in invoiced amounts ($422,640.61 through Invoice 1641), not in itemized vendor expenses.
   - True project spending is approximately **$422,640.61 invoiced + $168,462.58 in itemized expenses = ~$591,103.19 total**, representing **59.9% of the $986,000 contract value**.

4. **Hardware & Equipment**: QBO shows only $25,645.04 in vendor charges for electronics (Digi-Key, Vaisala, Proto Labs, 3D printing, supplies). Drive Budget Doc allocates $370,000 for S0 UAS platforms and MHTP integration. The discrepancy suggests:
   - Bulk hardware procurements (fully assembled S0 units, integration labor) are invoiced as complete line items (e.g., Invoice 1601: $151,400 for "fully assembled S0 UAS"; Invoice 1649: $126,000 for additional platforms).
   - Component and consumable purchases are tracked in QBO; capital equipment may be invoiced separately.

5. **Travel**: QBO shows minimal itemized travel ($64.99–$537.78 for specific items like trackers and tablets). However, Drive Budget Docs detail $4,506.15 in documented hurricane deployment travel (Ernesto $2,512.32, Francine $1,993.83) plus $18,726.78 additional travel allocated in final invoice (1641). This suggests travel is either embedded in larger invoices or processed through a different accounting method.
