# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); Federal research contract
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, dated 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Brief Scope:** Design, development, and deployment of small uncrewed aircraft systems (sUAS) platforms equipped with integrated meteorological payloads for hurricane research. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS platforms with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR imaging, laser altimeter, and video capture. Additional scope includes Non-Recurring Engineering (NRE) for payload integration, NOAA real-time data requirements (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS formatting, VDM generation), in-field support, and hurricane deployment operations.
