# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, dated 10/24/2024)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development, integration, and deployment of S0 sUAS (small unmanned aerial systems) platforms equipped with integrated METOC (meteorology and oceanography) payloads for hurricane and tropical cyclone research. Scope includes 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensors, laser altimeters, and video/image capture capability; non-recurring engineering (NRE) for payload integration and NOAA real-time data compliance (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS formatting, VDM generation); Area-I MHTP (Multi-Hole Turbulence Probe) integration for Altius 600 platform; in-field hurricane deployment support; and project documentation.
