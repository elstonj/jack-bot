# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via addendum)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Brief Scope:** Design, development, and deployment of sUAS (small Unmanned Aircraft Systems) platforms integrated with NOAA-standard meteorological payload (Vaisala PTH sensors, IR sensors, laser altimeter) for real-time hurricane research data collection. Includes hardware development, non-recurring engineering, payload integration, field deployment support, and data processing to NOAA standards (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM formatting).
