# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)

**Scope Description:**  
Development, integration, and deployment of small Unmanned Aircraft Systems (sUAS) platforms with integrated meteorological (METOC) payload for real-time tropical cyclone research. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensors, laser altimeters, and video/image capture capability. Engineering support includes payload integration, NOAA real-time data requirements (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS formatting, VDM generation), and in-field hurricane deployment support.
