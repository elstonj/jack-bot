# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); Government research contract
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (issued 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via addendum)
- **Brief Description:** Development and deployment of small unmanned aerial systems (sUAS) platforms (S0 UAS) equipped with integrated METOC payload for tropical cyclone reconnaissance and data collection. Scope includes 16–20 fully assembled aircraft units, sensor integration (Vaisala RD41 PTH, downward IR, laser altimeter), non-recurring engineering, real-time data processing (AirOPS/WMO HDOBS/VDM formatting), field support, and in-storm hurricane deployment operations.
