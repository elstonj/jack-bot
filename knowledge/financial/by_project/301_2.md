# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS), on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via addendum)
- **Brief Description:** Development, integration, and deployment of sUAS (small Unmanned Aircraft Systems) platforms with integrated METOC (meteorological and oceanographic) payloads for real-time hurricane research data collection. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with Vaisala RD41 sensors, IR imaging, laser altimetry, and video capture; integration of Area-I MHTP probe; non-recurring engineering for payload automation and NOAA data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM); and in-field deployment support for hurricane missions (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton in 2024).
