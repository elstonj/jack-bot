# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development, integration, and deployment of small unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS) platforms with integrated METOC (Meteorological & Oceanographic) payloads for hurricane research. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with Vaisala RD41 sensors, IR imaging, laser altimetry, and video capability; Area-I MHTP (Multi-Hole Turbulence Probe) integration support; non-recurring engineering for payload automation and NOAA real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM); in-field hurricane deployment support; and project documentation.
