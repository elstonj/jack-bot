# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (issued 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, dated 10/24/2024)

### Scope Summary
Design, integration, testing, and field deployment of small unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS) platforms with integrated meteorological payloads (METOC) for in-situ hurricane data collection. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units equipped with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensors, laser altimeters, and video/image capture capability. Project includes non-recurring engineering (NRE) for payload integration, full automation, NOAA real-time data requirements (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS formatting, VDM generation), and in-field hurricane deployment support.
