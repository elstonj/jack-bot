# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Scope Summary:** Development and delivery of 16–20 fully assembled sUAS (small Unmanned Aircraft Systems) platforms with integrated METOC payload (Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensor, laser altimeter, video/image capture) for real-time hurricane research data collection. Includes non-recurring engineering (NRE) for payload integration, NOAA data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), automation enhancements, field deployment support, and in-storm hurricane missions (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton in 2024).
