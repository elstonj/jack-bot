# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via addendum)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Brief Scope:** Design, development, integration, and deployment of sUAS (small Unmanned Aerial Systems) platforms equipped with meteorological and oceanographic (METOC) payloads for in-storm hurricane data collection. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS platforms with integrated Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR sensors, laser altimeters, and video/image capture. Work includes payload integration engineering, real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), field deployment support, and hurricane operational flights.
