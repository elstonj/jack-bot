# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, October 2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Brief Description:** Development, integration, and deployment of S0 sUAS platforms with integrated METOC payload (Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensor, laser altimeter, video/image capability) for real-time tropical cyclone research. Includes non-recurring engineering for payload integration, automation, NOAA real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), field support for hurricane deployments (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton in 2024), and Area-I MHTP integration work.
