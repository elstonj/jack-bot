# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)

**Client/Funding Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division

**Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)

**Contract/PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)

**Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, dated 10/24/2024)

**Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Project Scope:** Design, development, integration, and deployment of small uncrewed aerial systems (sUAS) with integrated meteorological payloads (METOC) for in-storm hurricane research. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS platforms with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR sensors, laser altimeter, and video/image capture. Includes non-recurring engineering (payload integration, real-time data formatting per NOAA AirOPS/WMO HDOBS standards), field deployment support for hurricane intercepts, and documentation.
