# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); SBIR-related research contract
- **Principal Contact:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)

**Brief Description:** Development, integration, and deployment of small unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS) platforms with integrated METOC (Meteorological and Oceanographic) payloads for in-storm hurricane research. Includes 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, infrared imaging, laser altimetry, and video capture. Work encompasses hardware procurement, non-recurring engineering (NRE) for payload integration and automation, field support for hurricane deployments (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton in 2024), and data processing/algorithm development.
