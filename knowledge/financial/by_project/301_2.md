# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via addendum)
- **Brief Description:** Development, integration, and deployment of sUAS (small Unmanned Aircraft Systems) platforms with METOC (Meteorological & Oceanographic) payloads for NOAA hurricane research. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with integrated Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR imaging, laser altimeter, and video/image capture; payload integration for Area-I MHTP; engineering support for real-time data processing (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS formatting, VDM generation); in-field hurricane deployment support; and technical documentation.
