# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); federally funded research contract
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (issued 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, dated 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Brief Scope:** Development, integration, and deployment of small unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS) platforms (S0 designation) equipped with METOC (meteorological and oceanographic) sensor payloads for in-flight hurricane research. Includes hardware procurement (16–20 fully assembled units with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR downward-looking sensors, laser altimeter, video/image capture), non-recurring engineering (NRE) for payload integration and NOAA real-time data requirements (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS formatting, VDM generation), Area-I MHTP integration support, field deployment support, and travel for hurricane storm chasing operations.
