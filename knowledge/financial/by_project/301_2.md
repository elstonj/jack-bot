# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); SBIR Phase II equivalent
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (issued 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Brief Description:** Development and deployment of small unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS) platforms with integrated meteorological (METOC) payload for in-storm hurricane research. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with integrated Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR imaging, and laser altimetry; integration of Area-I MHTP (Multi-Hole Turbulence Probe) onto Altius 600 platform; engineering support for automation and real-time NOAA data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM); and field deployments for Ernesto, Francine, Helene, and Milton hurricanes (Aug–Oct 2024).
