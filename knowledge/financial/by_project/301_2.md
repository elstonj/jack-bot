# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **Contract/PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, dated 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Scope:** Development, integration, and deployment of small unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS) platforms with integrated meteorological (METOC) payloads for hurricane research. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR sensors, laser altimeters, and video/image capability; integration of Area-I MHTP (Multi-Hole Turbulence Probe) with Altius 600 platform; non-recurring engineering for payload integration, flight performance enhancement, automation, and NOAA data requirements (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS formatting, VDM generation); in-field deployment support for multiple hurricane seasons; and comprehensive project documentation.
