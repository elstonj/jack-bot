# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Principal Contact:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and deployment of small unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS) platforms (S0 with integrated meteorological payload including Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR imaging, laser altimeter, and video capture) for real-time tropical cyclone research and data collection. Includes platform hardware procurement, non-recurring engineering for NOAA data standards compliance (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), and operational hurricane deployments (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton 2024).
