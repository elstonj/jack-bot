# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Scope Description:** Development and deployment of Black Swift S0 UAS (unmanned aerial systems) platforms integrated with meteorological payload (Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR sensors, laser altimeter, video/image capture) for real-time hurricane research data collection. Includes non-recurring engineering for NOAA real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), flight automation, payload integration, Area-I MHTP probe support, and in-field hurricane deployment support (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton, 2024).
