# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (University of Miami/NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS), on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contact:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Design, manufacture, and deploy small unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS) — specifically the S0 platform with integrated METOC payload (Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR, laser altimeter, video/imaging) for in-situ hurricane research data collection. Includes non-recurring engineering for payload integration, real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), full automation support, Area-I MHTP integration, and field deployment support during hurricane operations.
