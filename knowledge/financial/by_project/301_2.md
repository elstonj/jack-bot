# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **Contract/PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Brief Scope:** Development and deployment of small unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS, S0 platform) with integrated METOC (Meteorological & Oceanographic) payload for hurricane research. Includes 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR imaging, laser altimeter, and video capability. Scope encompasses hardware procurement, non-recurring engineering, payload integration, real-time data delivery (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), automation enhancements, and in-field hurricane deployment support.
