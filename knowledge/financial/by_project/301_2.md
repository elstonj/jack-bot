# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Funding Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (issued 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via addendum)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development, integration, and deployment of Black Swift S0 sUAS platforms with integrated METOC payload (Vaisala PTH sensors, IR imagery, laser altimeter) for real-time hurricane research data collection. Includes hardware procurement, non-recurring engineering (payload integration, automation, NOAA data formatting compliance), field support, and in-storm deployment operations.
