# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, dated 10/24/2024)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Brief Description:** Development, integration, and deployment of small Unmanned Aerial Systems (sUAS) platforms with integrated meteorological and oceanographic (METOC) payload for real-time tropical cyclone research. Includes 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with Vaisala sensors, IR imaging, laser altimeter, and video capture; integration of Area-I MHTP; non-recurring engineering support; and in-field hurricane deployment operations.
