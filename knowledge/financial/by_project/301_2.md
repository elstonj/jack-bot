# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via addendum)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Scope:** Development and deployment of sUAS (small Unmanned Aircraft Systems) platforms with integrated METOC (Meteorology & Oceanography) payloads for hurricane research, including Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR sensors, laser altimeter, and video/image capture; deployment support for multiple 2024 hurricane events (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton); engineering support for payload integration, real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), and full automation.
