# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS), on behalf of NOAA Atlantic Oceanographic and Meteorological Laboratory (AOML) Hurricane Research Division
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); SBIR/Government Research
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, dated 10/24/2024)
- **Brief Scope:** Development, integration, and field deployment of Small Unmanned Aircraft Systems (sUAS) platforms with integrated METOC (Meteorological and Oceanographic) payload for hurricane research, including Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR imaging, laser altimetry, and video capture. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units, Area-I MHTP integration support, real-time data processing (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS formatting), and in-storm hurricane deployment support.
