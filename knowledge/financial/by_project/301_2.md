# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II / NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development, integration, and field deployment of small uncrewed aerial systems (sUAS) platforms with integrated METOC (meteorological and oceanographic) payloads for in-situ hurricane research. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with Vaisala RD41 sensors, IR sensors, and data integration for real-time NOAA systems (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS formatting). Includes hurricane field deployments (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton in 2024) and calibration flights.
