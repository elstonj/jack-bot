# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); Federal services contract
- **Contract/PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, dated 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Brief Description:** Development, integration, and deployment of small unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS) with integrated METOC payloads for real-time hurricane research data collection. Includes design and fabrication of 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS platforms equipped with Vaisala RD41 sensors, infrared imaging, laser altimetry, and video capture. Deliverables include non-recurring engineering, payload integration, real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), field deployment support for multiple Atlantic hurricane operations (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton in 2024), and technical documentation.

## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Status: **YELLOW/AT RISK**

**Positive Indicators:**
1. **Total Invoiced (43%) vs. Budget:** Only $422.6K of $986K contracted value has been invoiced through 1641, suggesting adequate budget cushion remains for planned work through 06/30/2025.
2. **Contract Extensions:** Multiple addenda (Addendum 4, dated 10/24/2024) demonstrate continued commitment and budget augmentation, extending performance to mid-2025.
3. **Planned Final Invoices:** Invoice 1649 ($149.75K projected) is identified and scoped, bringing cumulative invoices to $572.4K (~58% of contract), leaving $413.6K remaining.
4. **Travel/Deployment Execution:** Successful field deployments for Ernesto, Francine, Helene, and Milton hurricanes completed on schedule and largely within travel allocations.

**Concerns & Overages:**

1. **Hardware Category Overspend (S0 UAS + MHTP):** 
   - Budget Docs show **(23,750.00)** overspend in Hardware categories (combined S0 platforms and MHTP)
   - S0 UAS platforms: $350K allocated; invoiced ~$280K; residual overspend flagged in Drive budget narrative
   - Area-I MHTP: $20K allocated; invoiced ~$15K
   - **Root Cause:** Likely escalation in unit costs or expanded scope of platform fabrication/integration
   - **Impact:** Reduces available funds for other categories

2. **Travel Cost Overspend:**
   - Budget Docs note **(8,720.61)** overspend in In-Storm Hurricane Travel category
   - Travel allocated $30.7K total; invoices show $27.2K+ consumed; yet spreadsheet flags overage
   - Likely due to consolidated expenses across multiple hurricanes (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton) exceeding year-by-year allocations
   - **Impact:** $8.7K overage reduces net remaining budget to ~$404.9K

3. **Payment Delay / Cash Flow Risk:**
   - QuickBooks shows only $168,462.58 received vs. $422,640.61 invoiced (58% payment rate)
   - Invoice 1641 approved 08/25/24 but payment status unknown; Invoice 1649 is projected
   - **Risk:** If payments are delayed or disputed, BST may face cash flow constraints
   - **Data Gap:** No QuickBooks aged receivables report or invoice aging schedule provided

4. **Budget Allocation Uncertainty:**
   - NRE & Engineering Support shows $130K remaining (57% of $230K allocated), yet contract is actively consuming resources
   - Project Documentation fully allocated ($6K)
   - Engineering work appears to be underutilized relative to hardware/travel, suggesting either (a) scope was optimistically estimated, or (b) engineering deliverables are back-loaded into Q1–Q2 2025
   - Invoice 1649 includes $23.75K engineering work, partially addressing this concern

5. **Expense Tracking Gaps:**
   - QuickBooks "Expenses Incurred" ($40,353.54 Feb–Nov 2024) is limited to itemized material/supply costs
   - Does not include labor (salaries, burden), subcontractor invoices, or full burden cost accounting
   - Actual total cost of project likely significantly higher than $40K/month shown in Q2 2024

6. **Period of Performance Urgency:**
   - Contract ends 06/30/2025 (8 months from invoice 1649 projection date of 02/26/25)
   - Remaining budget ($404.9K–$413.6K) must be spent within final 4–5 months of performance
   - Burn rate acceleration required to consume remaining funds; risk of schedule slippage or under-delivery if scope cannot be compressed
