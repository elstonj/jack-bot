# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS), on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Scope Summary:**  
Development, integration, and deployment of 16–20 fully assembled sUAS (small Unmanned Aircraft Systems) platforms equipped with integrated METOC (meteorological/oceanographic) payload for real-time hurricane research data collection. Includes Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, infrared sensors, laser altimeter, and video/image capture. Deliverables include hardware, Non-Recurring Engineering (NRE) support for automation and NOAA real-time data formatting, field deployment support, and technical documentation.
