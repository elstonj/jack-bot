# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Funding Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS), on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, dated 10/24/2024)
- **Brief Scope:** Design, development, integration, and field deployment of small Uncrewed Aerial Systems (S0 UAS platforms) with integrated METOC (Meteorological/Oceanographic) payload for real-time hurricane data collection. Includes 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR imaging, laser altimeter, video capture, and NOAA real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM). Active field deployments during 2024 hurricane season (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton).
