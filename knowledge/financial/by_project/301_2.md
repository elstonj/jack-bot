# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Brief Description:** Development, integration, and field deployment of small unmanned aircraft systems (S0 UAS platforms) with integrated meteorological payload (Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensor, laser altimeter, video/image capture) for hurricane research. Includes non-recurring engineering for payload integration, real-time data systems (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS formatting, VDM generation), and in-field support for multiple hurricane deployments (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton in 2024).
