# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, dated 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Scope:** Development and deployment of sUAS (small Unmanned Aircraft Systems) platforms with integrated METOC (Meteorology and Oceanography) payload for tropical cyclone research; includes 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensor, laser altimeter, and video/image capture; integration of Area-I MHTP (Multi-Hole Turbulence Probe) for Altius 600 platform; non-recurring engineering for payload integration, flight performance enhancement, full automation, and NOAA real-time data formatting; in-field support and hurricane deployment travel.
