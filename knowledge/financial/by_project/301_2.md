# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **Period of Performance:** October 31, 2021 – June 30, 2025 (extended via Addendum 4, October 24, 2024)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated February 2, 2022)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development, integration, and deployment of small uncrewed aircraft systems (sUAS) with integrated meteorological (METOC) payloads for hurricane research. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS platforms equipped with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensors, laser altimeters, and video/image capture. Includes non-recurring engineering (NRE), payload integration, real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), in-field support, and hurricane deployment travel.

However, this reflects the schedule-of-payments billing model typical of government contracts, where hardware and engineering are invoiced upon delivery/completion of milestones, not on an expense-accrual basis.
