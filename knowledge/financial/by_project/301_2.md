# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via addendum)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Scope Summary:** Design, development, integration, and deployment of advanced small Unmanned Aircraft Systems (sUAS) platforms (S0 variant) with integrated meteorological and oceanographic (METOC) payload for real-time hurricane observation and data collection. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with integrated sensors (Vaisala RD41 PTH, IR, laser altimeter, video), non-recurring engineering (NRE) for payload integration and automation, NOAA data format compliance (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), field support, and hurricane deployment operations.
