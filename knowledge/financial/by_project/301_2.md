# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); SBIR Phase II equivalent
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD); Jack Elston (Black Swift Technologies)
- **Brief Description of Scope:**
  - Design, integration, and deployment of 16–20 fully assembled S0 sUAS platforms with integrated METOC (Meteorological & Oceanographic) payload
  - Payload integration: Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensor, laser altimeter, video/image capture
  - Area-I MHTP (Multi-Hole Turbulence Probe) integration for Altius 600 platform
  - Non-recurring engineering for payload automation, NOAA real-time data requirements (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS formatting, VDM generation)
  - In-field support and hurricane deployment operations (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton, 2024)
  - Data processing, algorithm development, and documentation
