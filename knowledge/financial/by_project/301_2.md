# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **Contract/PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Brief Scope:** Development and deployment of small unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS) platforms with integrated meteorological and oceanographic (METOC) payload for in-storm hurricane research. Includes 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS platforms equipped with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR imaging, laser altimeter, and video capture. Deliverables include non-recurring engineering, payload integration, real-time data systems (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS), and in-field hurricane deployment support.
