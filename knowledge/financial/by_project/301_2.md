# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Brief Description:** Development and deployment of small Unmanned Aerial System (sUAS) platforms (S0 series with 16–20 fully assembled units) integrated with METOC (Meteorological and Oceanographic) payload sensors (Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR cameras, laser altimeters, video/image capture) for real-time hurricane research data collection. Includes non-recurring engineering for payload integration, automation, NOAA real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM generation), field deployment support, and hurricane storm chase operations.
