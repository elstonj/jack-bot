# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); Government contract
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (issued 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Scope Description:**  
Development, integration, and deployment of sUAS (small unmanned aircraft systems) platforms for NOAA hurricane research, including Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensors, laser altimeters, and video/image capture. Includes 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with integrated METOC payload, Area-I MHTP turbulence probe integration, non-recurring engineering, real-time data processing (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM formatting), and in-field hurricane deployment support (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton).
