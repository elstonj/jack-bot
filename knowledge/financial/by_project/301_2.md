# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Design, development, integration, and deployment of S0 unmanned aerial systems (UAS) platforms with integrated METOC payload (Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR sensors, laser altimeter, video/imaging) for real-time hurricane research data collection. Includes 16–20 fully assembled units, non-recurring engineering for payload integration and NOAA data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), Area-I MHTP integration support, in-field hurricane deployment support, and documentation.

**Sources:** Budget docs (UMiami Addendum_Blackswift Jack Signed 10.24.2024.pdf, Hurricane Working Budget spreadsheet)
