# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); government R&D contract
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)

**Scope Summary (from Drive Budget Docs & Proposals):**
Black Swift Technologies (BST) is providing sUAS (small Unmanned Aerial System) platforms with integrated METOC (meteorological and oceanographic) payload for NOAA hurricane research. The project includes delivery of 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units equipped with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensors, laser altimeters, and video/image capture capability. Additional scope includes non-recurring engineering (NRE) for payload integration, real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), full flight automation, hurricane field deployment support, and documentation. Deployments have included hurricanes Ernesto (Aug 2024), Francine (Sept 2024), Helene (Sept 2024), and Milton (Oct 2024).
