# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, dated 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Brief Scope Description:**  
Development, integration, and field deployment of S0 sUAS (small Unmanned Aerial System) platforms with integrated NOAA meteorological payloads (Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR sensors, laser altimeters, video/imaging) for real-time tropical cyclone research. Includes hardware procurement (16–20 fully assembled units), non-recurring engineering for payload integration and automation, NOAA real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), and in-field hurricane deployment support.
