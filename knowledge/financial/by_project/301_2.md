# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS), on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, dated 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Scope Summary:** Development and deployment of small unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS) platforms integrated with meteorological and oceanographic (METOC) payloads for hurricane research. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS platforms with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensors, laser altimeters, and video/image capture capability. Work includes non-recurring engineering (NRE) for payload integration, real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), automation enhancements, and in-field deployment support for live hurricane operations (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton in 2024).
