# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Funding Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); Government research contract
- **Contract/PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (issued 02/02/2022)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development, integration, and deployment of S0 Unmanned Aerial Systems (UAS) platforms equipped with METOC (meteorological and oceanographic) payloads for hurricane research. Includes hardware procurement (16–20 fully assembled sUAS units with Vaisala RD41 sensors, IR sensors, laser altimeters), non-recurring engineering (NRE) for payload integration and NOAA real-time data requirements, and in-field hurricane deployment support.

### Notes on Budget vs. Actual Analysis

**Data Sources:**
1. **Actual Spend (Feb–Nov 2024):** From Hurricane 2024 Spending spreadsheet (Drive Budget Docs). This reflects itemized vendor invoices only; does not include labor charges or invoiced project costs.
2. **Cumulative Invoiced:** From Hurricane Working Budget (invoices 1581–1641). These invoices bundle hardware, NRE, labor, and travel into lump-sum line items; granular cost category breakdown not provided in Budget Doc.
3. **Proposed Budget:** From Budget Breakdown section of Budget Doc.

**Key Observations:**

- **Hardware Category:** Actual Feb–Nov 2024 component spending ($35,653.54) is well below the $370k hardware budget, but cumulative invoiced hardware (embedded in invoices 1601, 1628, 1649) suggests ~$350k allocated. **Variance shows ~$20k underspend relative to proposed, but category is tracking toward 95% utilization.**

- **NRE & Engineering:** Proposed $236k; cumulative invoiced ~$140k through 1641. This category appears **under-budget by ~$96k**, with remaining engineering work projected in Invoice 1649 and beyond.

- **Travel & In-Field Support:** Proposed $36,726.78 (Y1–Y2 budgets) + Addendum 2024–2025 allocation. Cumulative invoiced ~$14,506.15. However, budget doc notes indicate **overspend of $(8,720.61)** for in-storm hurricane deployments (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton), suggesting actual travel costs exceeded Y1–Y2 allocated budget but are covered by 2024–2025 addendum.

- **% of Budget Used:** Approximately **58% cumulative** (invoiced $572.4k of $986k approved). Remaining budget of ~$413.6k is allocated for Invoice 1649 completion and final project closeout (through 06/30/2025 PoP).
