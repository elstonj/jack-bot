# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency**: University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type**: Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **PO Number**: PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance**: 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts**: Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Brief Description of Scope**: Development, integration, and deployment of advanced small Unmanned Aerial Systems (sUAS) platforms equipped with meteorological and oceanographic (METOC) payloads for tropical cyclone reconnaissance and research. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS platforms with integrated Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensors, laser altimeters, and video/image capture; Area-I MHTP (Multi-Hole Turbulence Probe) integration support; non-recurring engineering for payload integration and NOAA real-time data compliance (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS formatting); in-field hurricane deployment support; and project documentation.
