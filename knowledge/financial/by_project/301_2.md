# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Development and deployment of small unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS) platforms integrated with METOC (meteorological and oceanographic) payloads for hurricane research. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensors, laser altimeters, video/image capture capability, and integration of Area-I MHTP (Multi-Hole Turbulence Probe) with Altius 600 platform. Non-recurring engineering includes payload integration, flight performance enhancement, full automation, NOAA real-time data requirements (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS formatting, VDM generation), video/image integration, and project documentation. Field support includes Clear Air S0 calibration flights and in-storm hurricane deployments (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton in 2024).
