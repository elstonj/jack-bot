# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Scope Description:**
Development, integration, and deployment of small unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS) for NOAA hurricane research. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS platforms with integrated METOC payload (Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR sensors, laser altimeter, video/image capture), Area-I MHTP integration support, non-recurring engineering for payload integration and real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), in-field hurricane deployment support, and technical documentation.
