# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via addendum)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Scope Summary:** Development, integration, deployment, and support of 16–20 fully assembled S0 sUAS platforms with integrated METOC payload (Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR imaging, laser altimeter, video/image capture) for real-time hurricane and tropical cyclone research. Includes non-recurring engineering for payload integration, automation, NOAA data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), Area-I MHTP integration, field deployment support, and hurricane mission travel.
