# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/Atmospheric Oceanography and Meteorology Lab [AOML] / Hurricane Research Division [HRD])
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, dated 10/24/2024)
- **Brief Scope:** Development, integration, and deployment of small unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS) platforms with meteorological (METOC) payloads for real-time tropical cyclone data collection. Includes 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units equipped with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensors, laser altimeters, and video/image capture. Platforms integrated with Area-I MHTP (Multi-Hole Turbulence Probe) for Altius 600. Deployments to hurricanes Ernesto, Francine, Helene, and Milton (2024).
