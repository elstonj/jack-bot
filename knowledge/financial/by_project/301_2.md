# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Scope Description:**
Development, integration, and deployment of 16–20 fully assembled S0 sUAS (small Unmanned Aircraft Systems) platforms with integrated METOC (Meteorological & Oceanographic) payload for hurricane research. Payloads include Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensor, laser altimeter, video/image capture, and Area-I MHTP (Multi-Hole Turbulence Probe) integration. Deliverables include NRE/engineering support for payload integration, NOAA real-time data requirements (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS formatting, VDM generation), in-field hurricane deployment support, and project documentation.
