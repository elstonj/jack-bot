# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via addendum)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development, integration, and deployment of small unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS) platforms with integrated METOC (meteorological and oceanographic) payloads for NOAA hurricane research. Includes Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensors, laser altimeter, video/image capture, and real-time data delivery (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM formatting). Multiple in-storm hurricane deployments conducted (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton in 2024).
