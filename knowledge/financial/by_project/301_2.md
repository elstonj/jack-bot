# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **Contract/PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Brief Scope:** Development, integration, and operational deployment of Black Swift S0 UAS platforms with integrated METOC payload (Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR sensors, laser altimeter, video/image capability) for real-time hurricane research data collection. Includes 16–20 fully assembled units, payload integration, flight automation, NOAA data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), Area-I MHTP integration support, and in-field hurricane deployments (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton, 2024).
