# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (issued 02/02/2022)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Brief Description:** Development and deployment of sUAS platforms (S0 UAS with integrated METOC payload including Vaisala RD41 sensors, IR imaging, laser altimeter, and video capability) for in-storm hurricane data collection. Includes non-recurring engineering for payload integration, real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), flight automation, and field support for hurricane deployments (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton in 2024).
