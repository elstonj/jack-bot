# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Brief Description:** Development, integration, and deployment of S0 sUAS (small Unmanned Aerial Systems) platforms equipped with METOC (Meteorological and Oceanographic) payloads for hurricane research. Work includes hardware procurement (16–20 fully assembled units with Vaisala RD41 sensors, IR sensors, laser altimeter), non-recurring engineering for payload integration and automation, real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), field deployments for in-storm hurricane data collection, and technical documentation.
