# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **Contract/PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Brief Scope:** Development, integration, and deployment of small Unmanned Aircraft Systems (sUAS) platforms with integrated METOC (meteorological and oceanographic) payloads for real-time hurricane research data collection. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with Vaisala RD41 sensors, IR imaging, laser altimeter, and video capture; Area-I MHTP integration support; engineering support for payload integration, flight automation, and NOAA real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM); and in-field hurricane deployment support.

**Explanatory Notes:**

**Data Sources:**
- **Proposed Budget** is from the Drive Budget Documents (Statement of Work & Working Budget sections, aggregated by category).
- **Actual Spend** combines two sources:
  - Invoiced amounts (from Budget Doc Invoice History: $422,640.61 through 1641)
  - Itemized expenses (from Budget Doc Hurricane 2024 Spending: $40,353.54 for Feb–Nov 2024 direct purchases)
  - Additional detail on travel from invoice descriptions
- **QuickBooks Actuals** show a single line-item entry: "(301-2) 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II | $168,462.58" (government.md) with no category breakdown; this reflects payments received, not expenses incurred by category, so it is not used for category-level variance analysis.

**Assumptions & Limitations:**
1. **Hardware category** is the largest approved budget ($350,000 for S0 platforms + $20,000 for MHTP = $370,000 total hardware). Invoiced hardware costs through 1628 are ~$289,874.55 (primarily invoice 1601 @ $151,400 and 1628 @ $123,416.40). Invoice 1649 projects an additional $126,000 for S0 UAS, bringing total to ~$415,874.55, which would exceed the $370,000 approved hardware budget by ~$45,874.55. However, invoice 1649 is projected and not yet submitted; this variance will be confirmed upon invoicing.

2. **Direct Labor** includes Engineering Support ($230,000 approved) and Documentation ($6,000 approved). Invoiced labor/engineering work is estimated at ~$113,000 across multiple invoices (1581, 1587, 1601, 1628, 1641) based on line item descriptions (NRE, program support, data processing, communications refactor). At 48% utilization with ~$137,000 remaining, the category is on track.

3. **Sensors & Components** (Vaisala, Digi-Key, 3D printing, prototyping, McMaster-Carr, etc.) shows itemized expenses of $40,353.54 for Feb–Nov 2024. This is the most recent direct-cost visibility. Estimated total approved for this category is ~$150,000 (inferred from budget structure and hardware cost allocation). Actual spend appears to be lower than proposed, suggesting either procurement delays, future spending, or conservative budgeting.

4. **Travel & Field Operations** shows $48,000 approved across Y1 ($8,820.22 + $12,000 calibration = $20,820.22), Y2 ($3,179.78 + $12,000 calibration = $15,179.78), and Addendum ($18,726.78), totaling $54,726.78 per the Budget Doc detail. However, the summary table lists $30,000 as a general allocation. Actual invoiced travel is ~$30,232.93 (Ernesto $2,512.32 + Francine $1,993.83 + Clear Air $6,000 + larger expenses embedded in 1641). Budget Doc notes an overspend of $8,720.61 in this category, indicating actual field operations have exceeded the allocated amount.

5. **Indirect Costs / Documentation** ($6,000 approved) is 83% utilized at ~$5,000, consistent with the budget doc's note that this category is "fully allocated."

6. **% Used Calculations** are based on Actual Spend ÷ Proposed Budget. The overall project is at 48% spend against 100% of contract value, which is appropriate given the extended period of performance through 06/30/2025 and the concentration of hardware deliverables in the latter half of the project (invoice 1649 projected for Q1 2025).
