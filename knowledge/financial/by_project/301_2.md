# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, October 2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Scope Summary:** Design, development, integration, and deployment of autonomous small uncrewed aircraft systems (sUAS) platforms with integrated meteorological (METOC) and operational payloads for real-time in-storm hurricane data collection. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS platforms with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensors, laser altimeters, and video/image capture; engineering support for payload integration and NOAA real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM); and in-field deployment support including hurricane field missions (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton, 2024).
