# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via addendum)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Principal Contact:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development, integration, and deployment of 16–20 small uncrewed aerial systems (sUAS) platforms (designated "S0") with integrated meteorological and oceanographic (METOC) payload for hurricane research. Work includes hardware development (Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR sensor, laser altimeter, video/image capability), non-recurring engineering (payload integration, flight automation, real-time data formatting for NOAA AirOPS and WMO HDOBS), and field deployment support for in-storm hurricane operations.
