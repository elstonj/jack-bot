# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **Contract/PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and deployment of small unmanned aircraft systems (sUAS) with integrated meteorological (METOC) payloads for real-time tropical cyclone data collection. Deliverables include 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS platforms with Vaisala RD41 sensors, IR imaging, laser altimeter, video/image capture; Area-I MHTP integration; non-recurring engineering for payload integration, automation, and NOAA data compliance (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM formatting); field support and in-storm hurricane deployments (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton); and project documentation.
