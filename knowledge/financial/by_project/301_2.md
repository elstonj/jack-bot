# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency**: University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type**: Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **Period of Performance**: 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via addendum)
- **PO Number**: PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Principal Contacts**: Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Scope**: Development and deployment of S0 UAS (unmanned aerial systems) platforms with integrated METOC (meteorological/oceanographic) payload for in-storm hurricane reconnaissance. Work includes 16–20 fully assembled S0 units with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensors, laser altimeter, and video/image capture; Area-I MHTP integration; NRE/engineering support; real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM); and field deployment support for multiple hurricane missions (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton in 2024).
