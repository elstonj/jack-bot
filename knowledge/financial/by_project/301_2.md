# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS), on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, dated 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Black Swift Technologies is developing and delivering 16–20 fully assembled S0 sUAS (small Unmanned Aircraft Systems) platforms integrated with METOC (meteorological and oceanographic) payloads for real-time hurricane research. Deliverables include Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensors, laser altimeters, video/image capture, Area-I MHTP (Multi-Hole Turbulence Probe) integration support, non-recurring engineering for full automation and NOAA data requirements (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM generation), and in-field deployment support for hurricane operations (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton in 2024).
