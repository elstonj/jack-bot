# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (CIMAS) on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, October 2024)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Design, development, and deployment of S0 uncrewed aerial systems (UAS) platforms with integrated METOC (Meteorological & Oceanographic) payload for hurricane research. Includes Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensors, laser altimeters, video/image capture, and real-time data transmission in NOAA-compliant formats (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM). Platforms deployed operationally during 2024 Atlantic hurricane season (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton).
