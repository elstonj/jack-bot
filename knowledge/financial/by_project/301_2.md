# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II (NOAA Tropical Cyclone Research Advancement)
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development)
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via Addendum 4, 10/24/2024)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Brief Description:** Development, integration, and deployment of S0 unmanned aerial systems (UAS) platforms with integrated METOC (Meteorological/Oceanographic) payload for hurricane research. Scope includes hardware acquisition (16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS units with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, IR sensors, laser altimeters, and imaging capability), non-recurring engineering for payload integration and real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), in-field support for hurricane deployments (Ernesto, Francine, Helene, Milton in 2024), and Area-I MHTP (Multi-Hole Turbulence Probe) integration for Altius 600 platform.
