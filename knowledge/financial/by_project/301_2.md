# Project 301-2 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** University of Miami (on behalf of NOAA/AOML Hurricane Research Division)
- **Contract Type:** Services Agreement (Research & Development); NOAA tropical cyclone research advancement
- **PO Number:** PO-0000470189 (dated 02/02/2022)
- **Principal Contacts:** Jun Zhang (University of Miami/CIMAS); Dr. Joseph Cione (NOAA/AOML/HRD)
- **Period of Performance:** 10/31/2021 – 06/30/2025 (extended via addendum)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development and deployment of Black Swift S0 unmanned aerial systems (UAS) with integrated METOC (meteorology/oceanography) payload for real-time hurricane research and data collection. Includes 16–20 fully assembled S0 UAS platforms with Vaisala RD41 PTH sensors, downward-looking IR sensors, laser altimeters, video/image capture, and integration of Area-I MHTP (Multi-Hole Turbulence Probe) on Altius 600 platform. Deliverables include non-recurring engineering (NRE) for payload integration, real-time data formatting (AirOPS, WMO HDOBS, VDM), in-field hurricane deployment support, and project documentation.
