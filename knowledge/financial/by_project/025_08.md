**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Black Swift Technologies is proposing development of an all-weather UAS platform for long-term tactical deployments, building on the S3 UAS architecture. The system features VTOL-to-fixed-wing capability, solar-based autonomous recharge, vision-based GPS-denied navigation, and advanced autonomy for persistent operations in extreme environments. A prior related project (Soil Moisture Mapping Phase II under contract FA864924P0003) has just completed with final report submission in August 2025.
