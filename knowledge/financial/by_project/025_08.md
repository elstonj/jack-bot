**Brief Description of Scope:**
Black Swift Technologies is executing a DoD AFWERX SBIR Phase II contract for Soil Moisture Mapping (Topic 22.4), with final report delivered in August 2025. In parallel, BST has submitted a proposal for an All Weather UAS for Long Term Tactical Deployments (AF Open Topic 25.5E) for a 90-day development effort. The S3 UAS platform is designed for autonomous, all-weather persistent ISR operations in extreme/contested environments using solar recharging, vision-based GPS-denied navigation, and advanced autonomy.
