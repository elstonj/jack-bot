# Project 025-08 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 025-08  
**Full Project Name:** AF Open Topic 25.5E - All Weather UAS for Long Term Tactical Deployments (S3 UAS)  
**Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (AFWERX)  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (reference to Soil Moisture Mapping Phase II also noted)  
**Contract Number:** AFX255-PCSO1 / FX255-PCS01-1104 (primary); FA864924P0003 (Soil Moisture Mapping Phase II)  
**Period of Performance:** 1/2/2026 – 3/29/2026 (90 days proposed)  
**Key Personnel:** Dr. Jack Elston (Principal Investigator), Dr. Maciej Stachura (Lead Technical Advisor)

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Development of an all-weather VTOL-to-fixed-wing UAS platform (S3 UAS) for autonomous, long-duration, persistent operations in extreme environments. The system integrates solar-based autonomous recharge, vision-based GPS-denied navigation, and advanced autonomy for military ISR missions in CBRN zones, contested areas, and severe weather conditions (up to 50-knot gusts, precipitation, icing). Designed for unattended operations lasting days to months from unprepared sites.
