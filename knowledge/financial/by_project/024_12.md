# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a small UAS (Soil Moisture Mapping Phase II)

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - 2022.4D (SMM Phase II 22.4D)

**Period of Performance:** Data available 2024-12 through 2025-10

**Brief Description of Scope:** Development and validation of a small UAS platform equipped with L-band radiometer sensor suite to measure soil moisture and validate runway integrity. Work includes RF noise resolution, flight test approvals, and field testing at Sunny Slope Sod Farm in Longmont, CO to collect soil moisture and soil integrity ground truth data.
