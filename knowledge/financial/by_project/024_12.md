# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a small UAS (Soil Moisture Mapping Phase II)

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)

**Period of Performance:** Data available 2024-12 through 2025-09 (9 months); full PoP not specified in source documents.

**Brief Description of Scope:** 
Develop and validate a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite to measure soil moisture and infer runway integrity. Technical work includes RF noise interference resolution, flight test approvals, field testing at Sunny Slope Sod Farm, and integration of onboard algorithms to compute soil integrity from L-band soil moisture data and soil type classification.
