# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Code:** 024-12

**Primary Project:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a small UAS (SMM Phase II)

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)

**Period of Performance:** Data available 2024-12 through 2025-11 (from QuickBooks actuals)

**Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is developing a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite to validate runway integrity through soil moisture measurements. The project addresses RF noise interference challenges and includes flight testing at Sunny Slope Sod Farm to collect soil moisture and soil integrity ground truth data. Technical approach involves real-time soil moisture calculation and onboard algorithms to compute soil integrity (cone index) from L-band measurements and soil type classification.
