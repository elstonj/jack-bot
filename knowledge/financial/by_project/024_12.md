# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Code:** 024-12

**Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)

**Period of Performance:** Data available from 2024-12 through 2025-10 (12 months shown)

**Brief Description of Scope:** Black Swift Technologies is developing an L-band radiometer sensor suite integrated with a small UAS platform to validate runway integrity through soil moisture measurements. The project addresses RF noise interference challenges and includes real-time soil moisture calculation and soil integrity validation capabilities.
