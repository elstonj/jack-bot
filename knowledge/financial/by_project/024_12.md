# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a small UAS  
**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II  
**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)  
**Period of Performance:** Data not available from proposals/reports  
**Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is developing a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite to validate runway integrity through soil moisture measurements. The system includes auxiliary sensors for surface temperature and NDVI, with real-time soil moisture calculation and onboard/post-processing algorithms to compute soil integrity (cone index) from soil moisture data and soil type classification. Key technical accomplishment to date: resolution of RF noise interference through shielding and absorber layer modifications, achieving 5 dB noise reduction.
