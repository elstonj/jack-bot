# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-12

**Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SMM Phase II)

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)

**Period of Performance:** Data not available from Proposals/Reports or Drive Budget Docs

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite to measure soil moisture and validate runway integrity. Key technical achievements include resolution of RF noise interference issues through shielding and RF absorption solutions, completion of flight test approval processes, and positioning for field testing at Sunny Slope Sod Farm in Longmont, CO to collect soil moisture and soil integrity ground truth data.
