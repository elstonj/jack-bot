# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a small UAS (SMM Phase II 22.4D)

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)

**Period of Performance:** Data available from 2024-12 through 2025-10 (invoicing period)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a small UAS platform equipped with L-band radiometer sensor suite to measure soil moisture for runway integrity assessment. The project includes RF noise mitigation, flight test approvals, field testing at Sunny Slope Sod Farm in Longmont, CO, and algorithms to compute soil integrity (cone index) from L-band soil moisture data and soil type classification.
