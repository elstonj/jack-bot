# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-12  
**Primary Project:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a small UAS (SMM Phase II 22.4D)  
**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)  
**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II — Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II  
**Period of Performance:** Data available 2024-12 through 2025-10 (from QuickBooks actuals)  
**Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is developing a small UAS platform with L-band radiometer sensor suite to validate runway integrity through soil moisture measurements. The system includes real-time soil moisture calculation and post-processing algorithms to compute soil integrity (cone index) from L-band soil moisture data and soil type classification. Key technical achievements include resolution of RF noise interference through shielding and absorber layers.

**Note:** Project 024-12 appears as "SMM Phase II 22.4D" in QuickBooks actuals. Additional DARPA Albatross-related work (WAANDER proposal, technical data disclosures) is referenced in proposals but not clearly mapped to project 024-12 accounting.
