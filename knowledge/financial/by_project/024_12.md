# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Code:** 024-12

**Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SMM Phase II)

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)

**Period of Performance:** Data available from 2024-12 through 2026-09; specific contract PoP end date not provided in source documents.

**Brief Description of Scope:** Development and validation of a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite to measure soil moisture and validate runway integrity. The project addresses RF noise interference challenges, completes flight test approvals, and conducts field testing to collect soil moisture and soil integrity ground truth data at locations including Sunny Slope Sod Farm in Longmont, CO.
