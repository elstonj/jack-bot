# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a small UAS (SMM Phase II 22.4D)

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping 2022.4)

**Period of Performance:** Data available 2024-12 through 2025-09 (partial year tracking)

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Validation of runway integrity through soil moisture measurements using a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite. Technical scope includes RF noise interference resolution, flight test approval, field testing at Sunny Slope Sod Farm, and real-time soil moisture calculation with algorithms to compute soil integrity (cone index) from L-band soil moisture data and soil type classification.
