# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-12  
**Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SMM Phase II / 2022.4D)  
**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II  
**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)  
**Period of Performance:** Data available 2024-12 through 2025-10 (from QuickBooks); full PoP not specified in provided documents  
**Brief Description of Scope:** Development and validation of runway integrity assessment capability using L-band radiometer sensor suite mounted on a small UAS platform to measure soil moisture and derive soil integrity (cone index) metrics. Technical focus includes RF noise mitigation, flight test approvals, and field validation at Sunny Slope Sod Farm in Longmont, CO.
