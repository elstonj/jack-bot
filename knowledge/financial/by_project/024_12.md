# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-12  
**Primary Project:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SMM Phase II)  
**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II  
**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)  
**Period of Performance:** Not explicitly stated in provided documents  
**Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is developing a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite to validate runway integrity through soil moisture measurements. The system includes auxiliary sensors for surface temperature and NDVI, with real-time soil moisture calculation and onboard algorithms to compute soil integrity (cone index) from L-band soil moisture data and soil type classification. Key technical achievements include resolution of RF noise interference issues and completion of flight test approval processes.
