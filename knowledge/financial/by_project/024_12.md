# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Code:** 024-12

**Primary Project:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SMM Phase II)

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Program:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II — Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)

**Period of Performance:** Data available from 2024-12 through 2025-11 (12 months shown)

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Black Swift Technologies is developing a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite to validate runway integrity through soil moisture measurements. The project addresses RF noise interference challenges, completes flight test approvals, and conducts field testing to collect soil moisture and soil integrity ground truth data.
