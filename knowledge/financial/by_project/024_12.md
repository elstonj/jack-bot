## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-12  
**Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SMM Phase II / Soil Moisture Mapping 2022.4D)  
**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)
