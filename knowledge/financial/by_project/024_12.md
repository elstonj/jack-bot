# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-12

**Primary Project Identity:** Soil Moisture Mapping (SMM) Phase II 22.4D

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (AFWERX solicitation 2022.4)

**Period of Performance:** Not explicitly stated in provided documents (Milestone #5 report dated 2024-12-11 indicates ongoing Phase II work)

**Brief Description of Scope:** 
Validate runway integrity through soil moisture measurements from a small UAS platform. The project develops an L-band radiometer sensor suite integrated with Black Swift Technologies' small UAS platform to measure soil moisture and compute soil integrity (cone index) in real-time. Key technical challenge resolved: RF noise interference from radiometer digital back end and drone E2 systems through shielding and RF absorber materials. Project positioned for field testing at Sunny Slope Sod Farm in Longmont, CO.
