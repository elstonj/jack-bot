# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** Soil Moisture Mapping (SMM) Phase II - Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - 2022.4D

**Period of Performance:** [Data not available from provided sources]

**Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is developing and validating a small UAS equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite to measure soil moisture and derive runway integrity (cone index) predictions. The project addresses RF noise interference challenges in sensor systems and includes field testing at Sunny Slope Sod Farm in Longmont, CO to collect ground truth data for soil moisture and integrity measurements. Milestone #5 report dated 2024-12-11 indicates successful resolution of RF shielding issues and readiness for local field testing phase.
