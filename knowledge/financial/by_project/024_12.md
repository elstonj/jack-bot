# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-12

**Primary Project:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a small UAS (SMM Phase II 22.4D)

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II — Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)

**Period of Performance:** Data available 2024-12 through 2025-10 (source: QuickBooks actuals); Full contract period not specified in provided documents.

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Validate runway integrity through soil moisture measurements using a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite. Project addresses RF noise interference challenges, completes flight test approval processes, and conducts field testing at Sunny Slope Sod Farm in Longmont, CO to collect soil moisture and soil integrity ground truth data. (Source: Proposals & Reports — Milestone #5 Report, dated 2024-12-11)
