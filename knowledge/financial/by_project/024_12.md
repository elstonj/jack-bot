# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-12  
**Primary Project:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a small UAS (SMM Phase II)  
**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II  
**Program:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)  
**Period of Performance:** 2024–2025 (estimated, based on milestone reporting dates)  

**Brief Description:**  
Black Swift Technologies is developing and validating a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite to measure soil moisture and derive runway integrity metrics. The project addresses RF noise interference challenges through hardware shielding and filtering, with field testing planned at Sunny Slope Sod Farm in Longmont, CO. Milestone #5 (dated 2024-12-11) confirms completion of RF noise resolution and flight test approval processes.

**Note:** Project 024-12 aggregates multiple related programs reflected in QuickBooks actuals (SMM Phase II 22.4D, S0 Hurricane, and related initiatives). The financial summary below consolidates all transactions attributed to this project code for the period 2024-12 through 2025-08.
