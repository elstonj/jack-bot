# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a small UAS (Soil Moisture Mapping Phase II)

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - 2022.4D

**Period of Performance:** Data not available from proposals/reports. QuickBooks actuals span 2024-12 through 2025-10.

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Validate runway integrity through soil moisture measurements using a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite. Technical work includes resolving RF noise interference, developing real-time soil moisture calculation capability, and conducting field testing to collect soil moisture and soil integrity ground truth data. Project successfully advanced from Phase I through RF noise mitigation and flight test approvals.
