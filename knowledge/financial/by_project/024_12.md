# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-12  
**Primary Project:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SMM Phase II / SBIR Phase II Milestone #5)

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II  
**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)  
**Report Period Covered:** 2024-12 through 2025-11 (per QuickBooks actuals)

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Black Swift Technologies is developing and validating a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite to measure soil moisture and derive runway integrity estimates. The project addresses RF noise interference challenges, has completed flight test approvals, and is positioned for field testing at Sunny Slope Sod Farm in Longmont, CO. The milestone report (dated 2024-12-11) documents progress on Milestone #5, with successful resolution of RF interference through shielding and absorber techniques.

**Note:** Project 024-12 appears as "SMM Phase II 22.4D" in the QuickBooks data, confirming alignment with the Soil Moisture Mapping Phase II effort.
