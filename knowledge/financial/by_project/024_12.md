# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-12

**Primary Project:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SMM Phase II)

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)

**Period of Performance:** Data available from 2024-12 through 2025-11 (12 months of actuals shown)

**Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is developing and validating a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite to measure soil moisture and validate runway integrity. Key achievements include RF noise interference resolution, completion of flight test approvals, and preparation for field testing at Sunny Slope Sod Farm in Longmont, CO. The project involves real-time soil moisture calculation and post-processing algorithms to compute soil integrity metrics.
