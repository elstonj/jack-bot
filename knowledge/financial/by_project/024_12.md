# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Code:** 024-12  
**Primary Project:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SMM Phase II)  
**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II  
**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)  
**Period of Performance:** Ongoing through at least 2026  
**Scope:** Development and validation of L-band radiometer sensor suite on small UAS platform for runway integrity assessment via soil moisture measurements; includes RF noise resolution, flight test approval, and field data collection at Sunny Slope Sod Farm, Longmont, CO.

**Note:** Project 024-12 serves as a consolidation code for multiple concurrent BST projects and activities. The primary SBIR Phase II contract referenced in proposals is **SMM Phase II 22.4D**, with associated work on **S0 Hurricane** (platform development) and several secondary projects tracked under this umbrella.
