# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SMM Phase II)

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)

**Period of Performance:** Data available from 2024-12 through 2025-11 (12 months shown)

**Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is developing and validating a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite to measure soil moisture and derive runway integrity assessments. Key technical accomplishments include resolving RF noise interference issues and completing flight test approval processes. The project is positioned for field testing at Sunny Slope Sod Farm in Longmont, CO.
