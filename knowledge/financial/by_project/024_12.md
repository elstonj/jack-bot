# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-12  
**Project Name:** Soil Moisture Mapping (SMM) Phase II – Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS  
**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II  
**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - 2022.4D  
**Period of Performance:** Not explicitly stated in provided documents; Milestone #5 report dated 2024-12-11 indicates active execution as of late 2024.  
**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Develop and validate a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite to measure soil moisture and derive runway integrity (cone index) predictions. Technical work includes RF noise mitigation, real-time soil moisture calculation, integrity algorithm development, and field testing at Sunny Slope Sod Farm in Longmont, CO.
