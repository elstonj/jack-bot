# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a small UAS (SMM Phase II 22.4D)

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)

**Period of Performance:** Not explicitly stated in provided documents

**Scope:** Validation of runway integrity through soil moisture measurements using a small UAS platform equipped with L-band radiometer sensor suite. Project includes RF noise interference resolution, flight test approvals, and field testing at Sunny Slope Sod Farm in Longmont, CO to collect soil moisture and soil integrity ground truth data.
