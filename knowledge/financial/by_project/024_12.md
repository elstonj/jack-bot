# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Designation:** 024-12

**Primary Project Identified:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a small UAS (SMM Phase II)

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)

**Period of Performance:** Not explicitly stated in provided documents; Milestone #5 Report dated 2024-12-11

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Black Swift Technologies is developing and validating a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite to measure soil moisture and assess runway integrity. The project addresses RF noise interference challenges, completed flight test approvals, and is progressing toward field testing at Sunny Slope Sod Farm in Longmont, CO to collect soil moisture and soil integrity ground truth data.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source:** Proposals/Reports — Milestone #5 Report (2024-12-11)

### Completed Milestones
- **RF Noise Interference Resolution:** Successfully addressed wideband radiation noise from radiometer digital back end and narrowband noise from drone E2 systems through conductive spray coating, RF absorber layers, and circuit modifications. Achieved 5 dB noise level reduction.
- **Flight Test Approvals:** Completed airspace approval processes, positioned to begin field testing.

### Planned/In-Progress Milestones
- **Field Testing:** Local soil moisture and soil integrity ground truth data collection at Sunny Slope Sod Farm, Longmont, CO
- **Algorithm Development:** Real-time soil moisture calculation and post-processing algorithms to compute soil integrity (cone index) from L-band soil moisture data and soil type classification
- **Data Validation:** Validation of runway integrity through correlated soil moisture and structural integrity measurements

**Financial Implications:**
- Milestone #5 report (as of 2024-12-11) does not specify remaining work scope, budget allocation to subsequent milestones, or projected final costs
- Field testing phase anticipated to incur additional expenses (travel, ground truth data collection equipment, personnel deployment)
