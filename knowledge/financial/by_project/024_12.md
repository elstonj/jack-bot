### **AFRL SBIR Phase II: Soil Moisture Mapping (SMM 2022.4)**

**From:** Milestone #5 Report, dated 2024-12-11 (Proposal/Reports source)

- **Scope:** Validate runway integrity through soil moisture measurements using small UAS with L-band radiometer sensor suite
- **Key Accomplishment (M5):** Resolved RF noise interference; ready for field testing at Sunny Slope Sod Farm, Longmont, CO
- **Technical Deliverables:** Real-time soil moisture calculation, cone index (soil integrity) computation algorithms, onboard and post-processing capabilities
- **YTD Spend (from QBO):** ~$449,725 (invoiced under "SMM Phase II 22.4D" in Mar, May, Aug)
- **Status:** Active field testing phase

### **DARPA Albatross: WAANDER (Autonomous Soaring)**

**From:** Oral Presentation Proposal Draft, dated 2024-11-27 to 2024-12-18 (Proposal/Reports source)

- **Scope:** Develop autonomous soaring capabilities for small UAS to extend endurance/range through wind energy harvesting
- **BST Role:** Provide S0 UAS platform, SwiftCore™ FMS avionics, SwiftTab™ operator interface
- **Partners:** BAE Systems (prime), NCAR (weather forecasting), UCSB, Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution
- **Technical Components:**
  - Preflight Planning Perk (PF2P): Weather/energy forecasting using NCAR FastEddy™
  - Sensor and Harnessing Control System (SHCS): Onboard soaring optimization
  - Integration: Real-time in-flight replanning
- **YTD Spend (estimated):** Not explicitly segregated; may be included in "S0 Hurricane" platform costs (~$1.2M) or internal R&D
- **Status:** Proposal phase (draft presentation materials); not yet funded
