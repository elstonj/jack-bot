# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-12

**Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SMM Phase II 22.4D)

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)

**Period of Performance:** Data available 2024-12 through 2025-09; Milestone #5 report dated 2024-12-11

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Validation of runway integrity through soil moisture measurements using a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite. Key technical work includes RF noise interference resolution, flight test approvals, and field testing at Sunny Slope Sod Farm in Longmont, CO to collect soil moisture and soil integrity ground truth data. System computes soil integrity (cone index) from L-band soil moisture data and soil type classification.
