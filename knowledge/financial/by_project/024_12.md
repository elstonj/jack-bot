# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-12  
**Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SMM Phase II)  
**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II  
**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)  
**Period of Performance:** Data available 2024-12 through 2025-10 (minimum; full POP not specified in source documents)  
**Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is validating runway integrity through soil moisture measurements using a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite. The project addresses RF noise interference challenges and field testing at Sunny Slope Sod Farm in Longmont, CO to collect soil moisture and soil integrity ground truth data.
