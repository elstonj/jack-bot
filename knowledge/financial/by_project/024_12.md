# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a small UAS (SMM Phase II 22.4D)

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)

**Period of Performance:** Data available 2024-12 through 2025-10

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Validation of runway integrity through soil moisture measurements using a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite. Technical approach includes resolving RF noise interference, conducting flight test approvals, and collecting soil moisture and soil integrity ground truth data. The system computes soil integrity (cone index) from L-band soil moisture data and soil type classification.
