# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-12  
**Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a small UAS (SMM Phase II)  
**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II  
**Program:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)  
**Period of Performance:** Data available 2024-12 through 2025-10 (from QuickBooks actuals)  

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Phase II SBIR project to validate runway integrity through soil moisture measurements using a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite. Key technical milestones include resolution of RF noise interference, flight test approval, and field validation at Sunny Slope Sod Farm in Longmont, CO. The project involves development of real-time soil moisture calculation and onboard algorithms to compute soil integrity (cone index) from L-band measurements.
