# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-12  
**Primary Project:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a small UAS (Soil Moisture Mapping / SMM Phase II 22.4D)  
**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II  
**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II (2022.4)  
**Milestone Report Date:** 2024-12-11  
**Key Personnel:** Jack Elston (CEO/Project Lead)  

**Scope:** Development and validation of a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite to measure soil moisture and validate runway integrity. The project addresses RF noise interference challenges, has completed flight test approvals, and is positioned to begin field testing at Sunny Slope Sod Farm in Longmont, CO.
