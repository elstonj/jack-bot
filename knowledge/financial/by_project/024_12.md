# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a small UAS (Soil Moisture Mapping Phase II)

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)

**Period of Performance:** Data available 2024-12 through 2025-09 (9 months of actuals documented)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Validation of runway integrity through soil moisture measurements using a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite. Technical work includes RF noise interference resolution, flight test approvals, field testing at Sunny Slope Sod Farm (Longmont, CO), and development of onboard/post-processing algorithms to compute soil integrity (cone index) from L-band soil moisture data and soil type classification.
