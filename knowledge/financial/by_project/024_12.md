# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Code:** 024-12  
**Primary Program:** Soil Moisture Mapping (SMM) Phase II SBIR 2022.4D  
**Secondary/Related Programs:** S0 Hurricane Phase II, Navy SBIR/STTR, DARPA Albatross (WAANDER), and other initiatives  
**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL); Navy; DARPA; and others  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II, SBIR Phase I, STTR, IDIQ, subcontract (BAE Systems prime)  
**Period of Performance:** 2024-12 through 2025-10 (data provided); Soil Moisture Mapping Phase II initiated 2022  
**Brief Description:**  
Project 024-12 encompasses multiple concurrent government-funded research and development initiatives, with the primary focus on validating runway integrity through soil moisture measurements from small UAS platforms. The portfolio includes SBIR Phase II work on L-band radiometer soil moisture sensing, Phase I efforts with Navy on methane emissions and autonomous control, DARPA's WAANDER program for autonomous soaring flight, and Hurricane Phase II platform development. The project demonstrates Black Swift Technologies' core capabilities in small UAS design, advanced sensor integration, and autonomous flight systems.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposals/Reports**

### Soil Moisture Mapping (SMM) Phase II 22.4D — AFRL SBIR
*(Primary project accounting for ~$549.7K of spend to date)*

**Status:** Milestone #5 Report (Runway Integrity Validation) — completed 2024-12-11

**Key Completed Milestones (per Milestone #5 Report):**
- Resolution of RF noise interference in L-band radiometer system (5 dB reduction achieved via shielding and absorber design)
- Flight test approval processes completed
- Site approval obtained for Sunny Slope Sod Farm, Longmont, CO
- System integration of auxiliary sensors (surface temperature, NDVI)
- Real-time and post-processing algorithm development for soil moisture and soil integrity calculation

**Upcoming/In-Progress Work:**
- Local field testing at Sunny Slope Sod Farm to collect soil moisture and soil integrity ground truth data
- Development of cone index calculation algorithms from L-band soil moisture and soil type classification
- Platform endurance and sensor performance validation
- Final runway integrity validation testing (anticipated in later phases)

**Financial Implication:** SBIR Phase II contract; funding likely tied to milestone completion. Continued draw-down expected as field testing advances.

### DARPA Albatross / WAANDER (Wind-powered Autonomous Albatross-like Navigation Developed for Endurance and Range)
*(Proposal in development; not yet funded in the invoice data provided)*

**Status:** Oral presentation proposal drafted (2024-11-27 to 2025-12-18); BAE Systems prime contractor with BST as subcontractor (technical data disclosure filed 2024-12)

**BST Role & Deliverables:**

- SwiftTab™ UI for operator interface

**Partners:** BAE Systems (prime), NCAR (weather forecasting), UC Santa Barbara (consultant), Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution (consultant)

**Financial Implication:** Currently a proposal; if awarded, will generate future contract obligations and revenue. Not yet reflected in 2024-12 through 2025-10 invoice data.
