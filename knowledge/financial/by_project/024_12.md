# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Code:** 024-12

**Primary Project:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a small UAS (SMM Phase II / SBIR Phase II 22.4D)

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)

**Period of Performance:** Data available 2024-12 through 2025-11 (12 months shown)

**Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is validating runway integrity through soil moisture measurements using a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite. The project involves resolving RF noise interference, conducting flight test approvals, and performing field testing at Sunny Slope Sod Farm in Longmont, CO to collect soil moisture and soil integrity ground truth data.
