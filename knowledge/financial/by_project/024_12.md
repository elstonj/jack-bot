## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-12  
**Project Name:** Soil Moisture Mapping (SMM) Phase II – Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS  
**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II  
**Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - 2022.4D  
**Period of Performance:** Data available 2024-12 through 2025-10  
**Brief Description:** Development and validation of a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite to measure soil moisture and validate runway integrity.
