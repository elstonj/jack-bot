# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-12

**Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SMM Phase II 22.4D)

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Period of Performance:** Data available from 2024-12 through 2025-11 (12 months shown in actuals)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
This Phase II SBIR project validates runway integrity through soil moisture measurements using a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite. The project addresses RF noise interference challenges, completes flight test approvals, and conducts field testing to collect soil moisture and soil integrity ground truth data. The system includes auxiliary sensors for surface temperature and NDVI, with onboard and post-processing algorithms to compute soil integrity (cone index) from L-band soil moisture data and soil type classification.
