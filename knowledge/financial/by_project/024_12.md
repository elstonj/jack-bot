# Project 024-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SMM Phase II 22.4D)

**Client/Agency:** Department of Air Force (DAF), Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)

**Period of Performance:** Not explicitly stated in provided documents; Milestone #5 report dated 2024-12-11 indicates ongoing Phase II work.

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a small UAS platform equipped with an L-band radiometer sensor suite to measure soil moisture and validate runway integrity. Key accomplishment: resolution of RF noise interference through hardware shielding and filtering techniques. Project positioned to begin field testing at Sunny Slope Sod Farm in Longmont, CO to collect soil moisture and soil integrity ground truth data.
