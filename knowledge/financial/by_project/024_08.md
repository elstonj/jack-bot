## Financial Health Assessment

### **Critical Issues**

1. **Unclassified Project Code:**  
   Project code 024-08 does not represent a single project. It is a catch-all account combining 10+ distinct funded research/development initiatives. **Financial health cannot be assessed without splitting into individual project codes.**

2. **No Budget Baseline:**  
   No proposed, approved, or contracted budget figures are available. All numbers are **actuals only**; no variance analysis is possible.

3. **No Payment/Collections Data:**  
   QuickBooks source shows expenses but does not indicate:
   - Which projects have received payments from clients/agencies
   - Accounts receivable or invoice status
   - Revenue recognition vs. cost incurrence timing
   - Profitability or cost recovery per program

4. **Sparse Invoicing Data:**  
   Only 4 commercial invoices (totaling $38,430) are documented, yet $2.3M in costs are recorded. This suggests:
   - Most costs are internal (labor, subcontractor invoices, cloud services) not yet billed to clients
   - Or invoicing occurs outside the commercial.md subset provided
   - Or cost tracking and billing are significantly out of sync

5. **Burn Rate Concern:**  
   Monthly spend averages ~$174K (excluding the recent 2026-11 anomaly). Without knowing contract values or remaining PoP dates, it is **impossible to assess whether the organization is on-pace or at risk of budget overrun**.

## Recommendations

**Immediate Actions:**

1. **Segregate project codes.** Assign each funded program (SMM Phase II, Navy SBIR, NSF CH4, Volcano CCRPP, etc.) its own cost center. Do not comingle budgets and actuals.

2. **Align invoicing to cost tracking.** Ensure QuickBooks invoices (commercial.md) match costs (unclassified.md) by project. Currently, only $38K of $2.3M is documented as invoiced.

3. **Establish budget baselines.** Import or create approved budget records from:
   - Contract documents (CLINs, funded amounts)
   - Proposal budgets (Drive docs)
   - Internal budget approvals for internal R&D projects (e.g., S3 development, refurbished S2)

4. **Document period of performance.** For each program, record start date, end date, and any milestone-based funding phases. This enables burn-rate and time-to-exhaustion analysis.

5. **Classify all expenses.** Tag each transaction with:
   - Cost category (direct labor, subcontractors, equipment, materials, travel, indirect, other)
   - Funding source (contract, internal R&D, facilities, etc.)
   - Project/program code
   - Cost object (personnel, vendor, etc.)
