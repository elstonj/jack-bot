### 2. **Cost Category Breakdown**

- **Proposed budget:** No cost category detail provided — appears to be a single-line fixed-price contract ($20,805.39).

- **Risk:** Five open Asana tasks (shipment, training, payment verification, project closeout) lack due dates and clear ownership. If May 2025 delivery is still the target, these tasks are now critically overdue.

### 4. **Payment & Receivables Risk**
- **Payment status:** No customer invoice issued; no payment received.
- **Open Asana task:** "Payment verification" is unassigned with no due date.
- **Cash exposure:** BST has fronted >$21K in production costs with no billing or payment received to offset.
- **Recommendation:** Issue invoice immediately to recover costs and establish payment terms with ERAU.

- **Status of initial units unclear:** With 5 unassigned tasks and no due dates, delivery readiness for May 2025 is uncertain, which could jeopardize the follow-on.

- ERAU may exemplify this gap — project definition has been ongoing for months, contributing to schedule slippage and cost overruns.
