**Brief Description of Scope:**
Project 024-08 aggregates multiple UAS platform development and operational efforts:
1. **Multi-UAS Operations:** Development of simultaneous command & control for 2+ S0 aircraft from single ground station (NOAA/WPO)
2. **S2 Persistence:** Coordinated multi-aircraft infrared imaging for wildfire detection and monitoring (USFS/NASA partnership)
3. **Methane Emissions:** S2-based CH4 detection and flux mapping for emissions surveying on rural/tribal lands (NSF funding)
