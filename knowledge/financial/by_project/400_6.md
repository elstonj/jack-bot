### Key Financial Observations

#### 1. **Labor Underutilization**
- **Issue:** Only 5 hours of labor charged across 9 months (January–September 2025), totaling $969.59.
- **Budgeted:** 73 hours for Dr. Elston over Phase I (3-month PoP, Feb–May 2025).
- **Analysis:** Actual hours represent only **6.8% of budgeted hours**. This suggests either:
  - Work was concentrated in periods not tracked in the available data.
  - Backend loading: minimal effort until Phase I final report (due 04/16/2025), after which work ceased.
  - Reporting lag: time entries may not yet be recorded or reconciled.

#### 2. **Invoicing vs. Cash Flow**
- **Timing Gap:** Invoice issued 04/14/2025 (for $24,556); interim payment of $12,500 received in **September 2025** (~5 months later).
- **Implication:** 
  - Significant cash flow timing mismatch between invoicing and collection.
  - May indicate milestone-based or approval-dependent payment structure from AFWERX through Curable Composites.
  - Outstanding balance of $12,055.41 remains unpaid as of last tracking date (October 2025).

#### 3. **Budget Allocation & Indirect Costs**
- **Proposed Structure:** 57.6% direct labor ($14,156) + 42.4% indirect costs ($10,400).
- **High Indirect Rate:** Combined OH (46.61%) + G&A (18.32%) represents substantial overhead allocation typical for Phase I SBIR work under government contracts.
- **Concern:** With only $969.59 in actual labor charged, the allocation mechanism for $10,400 in indirect costs is unclear. No QuickBooks data provided to verify how/when these were billed or absorbed.

#### 4. **Early Project Completion**
- **Deliverable Delivered Early:** Final Report submitted 04/16/2025, **4 days before Phase I PoP end date** (05/02/2025).
- **Implication:** Front-loaded invoicing (04/14) prior to formal deliverable completion suggests confidence in work product and payment terms.
- **Phase II Opportunity:** Report notes Phase II advancement in progress (customer memos pending); funding to be determined.
