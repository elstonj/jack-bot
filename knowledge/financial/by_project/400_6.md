## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Status: **UNDERSPENT (On-Track, with Caveats)**

**Positive Indicators:**
1. **Project well under budget** — Only $969.59 actual labor expense incurred against $14,156 budgeted labor ($24,556 total contract) as of August 2025 = **96.1% remaining**.
2. **Deliverable completed on schedule** — CLIN0002 Final Report submitted 04/16/2025, within PoP window (PoP ended 05/02/2025).
3. **Invoice issued and partially paid** — Invoice #1664 ($24,556) issued 04/14/2025; $12,500 (50.9%) received in September 2025, demonstrating payment flow.
4. **No cost overruns identified** — No equipment, travel, or materials costs incurred; no change orders or contract modifications noted.
5. **No indirect cost overruns tracked** — Indirect costs applied at budgeted rates (46.61% OH, 18.32% G&A); no actual over-allocation documented.

**Concerns & Risk Factors:**

1. **Labor Hours Severely Underutilized:**
   - **Budgeted:** 73 hours over 3-month PoP (Feb–May 2025)
   - **Actual charged:** 5 hours total (Jan–Aug 2025) = **93.2% shortfall**
   - Implies either:
     - Schedule delay or milestone-driven work concentration (suggested by April spike: 4 hours)
     - Underreporting/billing lag in first half of project
     - Fewer PI hours required than budgeted
   - **Impact:** Large upfront budget allocation not fully consumed, but deliverable delivered on time suggests work scoping may have been conservative.

2. **Payment Timing & Collection Risk:**
   - Invoice issued **04/14/2025** but only **$12,500 received by 09/2025** (5-month delay)
   - **Outstanding balance: ~$12,056** (49.1% of invoice) remains unpaid as of October 2025
   - **Terms:** Net 30 from invoice date (due ~05/14/2025); **significantly overdue**
   - **Risk:** Non-payment or delayed payment of remainder; cash flow impact to BST
   - **Mitigation:** None documented; no follow-up actions noted

3. **Backend-Loaded Work Pattern:**
   - No labor hours charged Jan–March 2025 despite Feb 3 PoP start
   - Spike in April (4 hours) followed by zero hours May–August
   - Large payment in September suggests **milestone-based rather than time-and-materials billing**
   - **Risk:** If final payment is contingent on additional deliverables or approvals, further delay possible

4. **Indirect Cost Allocation Uncertainty:**
   - Indirect costs totaling **$10,400 (42.4% of contract)** are budgeted but **actual OH/G&A charges are not itemized** in Monthly Tracking spreadsheet
   - If indirect costs are applied to full labor ($14,156), but actual labor is only $969.59, **indirect cost rate applied against actual labor may be significantly lower** than planned
   - **Implication:** Potential profit/margin compression; true fully-loaded cost per hour much lower than proposed
   - **Data Gap:** QuickBooks GL breakdown by account (direct labor, overhead, G&A) not provided

5. **Phase II Funding Uncertain:**
   - Final Report notes Phase II opportunity in development (Marine Corps & SOCOM memos pending)
   - **No Phase II budget, award notice, or contract extension documented** in Drive Budget Documents
   - **Risk:** If Phase II does not materialize, project winds down; no follow-on revenue
