## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Performance
- **Project Status:** **SIGNIFICANTLY UNDER BUDGET**
  - Approved Budget: $24,556.00
  - Actual Spend (labor only, through August 2025): $969.59
  - Utilization Rate: **3.9%**
  - Underspend: $23,586.41 (96.1%)

### Burn Rate Analysis
- **Budgeted for 73 labor hours over 3-month PoP** (Feb–May 2025) at $150/hr = ~24 hours/month
- **Actual labor hours through August 2025:** Only 5 hours total (1 hour in January + 4 hours in April)

### Key Concerns & Observations

1. **Outstanding Payment Risk:** Half of the invoice ($12,056) remains unpaid 6+ months after invoice issuance. This represents a cash flow gap and potential collection risk.

2. **Labor Underutilization:** Budgeted 73 hours; only 5 hours charged through August 2025 (6.9% utilization). Either:
   - Work was compressed into final weeks of PoP (late April–May) and not yet logged/invoiced, or
   - Actual labor effort was significantly less than planned, or
   - Time tracking was delayed and not reflected in monthly summaries through August.

3. **Milestone-Based Billing:** The $12,500 payment in September (after CLIN0002 Final Report completion in April) suggests invoice processing and payment were contingent on deliverable acceptance rather than monthly billings.

4. **PoP Elapsed:** Original PoP ended May 2, 2025. As of October 2025 (5 months post-PoP), project is administratively closed but final payment reconciliation may be pending.

5. **Phase II Opportunity:** Curable Composites pursuing Phase II with customer memos; however, no Phase II funding or timeline documented in provided materials. Potential for scope expansion pending customer approval.

### Overall Financial Health: **CAUTION — Payment Risk**

**Strengths:**
- Project completed on time (CLIN0002 Final Report delivered 04/16/2025, ahead of 05/02/2025 PoP end).
- Proof-of-concept successfully demonstrated and documented.
- Labor spending well below budget.

**Weaknesses:**
- 50% of invoiced amount unpaid 6+ months post-invoice.
- Minimal labor tracking during active PoP; potential cost accounting or time reporting issues.
- No evidence of final invoice reconciliation or contract close-out documentation.

**Recommendation:**
Verify outstanding payment status with Curable Composites and AFWERX. Confirm final labor hours and indirect cost allocations for invoice reconciliation. If Phase II is awarded, ensure labor forecasting and billing procedures are aligned with actual project execution timeline.
