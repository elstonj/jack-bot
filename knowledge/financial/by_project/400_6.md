## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Status: **GOOD / UNDERSPENT (with caveats)**

**Positive Indicators:**
1. **Invoice issued and partial payment received:** $24,556 invoiced (04/14/2025); $12,500 paid (09/2025); remaining $12,055.41 outstanding but within payment terms (Net 30, due 05/14/2025). Payment delay into September may reflect prime contractor cash flow or AFWERX processing, not BST delivery failure.
2. **Deliverable completed on schedule:** CLIN0002 Final Report submitted 04/16/2025, meeting contract deadline and enabling final invoice.
3. **Minimal labor costs incurred:** Only $969.59 in labor charges (5 hours) through August 2025 vs. $14,156 budgeted, indicating either (a) scope completion more efficient than estimated, or (b) work concentrated post-PoP window (backend-loaded schedule).
4. **No scope creep, modifications, or de-obligations:** Contract remains stable at $24,556; no documented budget adjustments.
5. **Cost structure managed:** Low material/travel/equipment burden (zero budgeted); project labor-heavy but controlled.

**Concerns & Risk Factors:**

1. **Significant labor underutilization (Jan–Aug 2025):**
   - Budgeted: 73 hours over ~3 months ≈ ~24 hours/month
   - Actual: 5 hours total (1 hr Jan, 4 hrs Apr) ≈ 0.5% of plan
   - **Interpretation:** Either the project experienced significant schedule slip (minimal work Jan–Mar; work pushed post-May 2), or labor hours were not logged during PoP and backfilled later, or the final deliverable was produced with substantially fewer hours than estimated.
   - **Risk:** If work was deferred past the May 2 PoP end date, there may be cost overrun or schedule variance; if hours are substantially underestimated, labor cost controls may be weak.

2. **Outstanding payment (40.9% of contract) as of 10/03/2025:**
   - $12,055.41 remains unpaid > 4 months after invoice issuance (due date was 05/14/2025)
   - **Likely explanation:** Prime contractor (Curable Composites) billing cycle or AFWERX payment processing; BST has invoiced and delivered, so cash flow risk is on Curable/AFWERX, not BST.
   - **Recommendation:** Confirm payment schedule with Curable and follow up on outstanding balance if payment not received by end of October 2025.

3. **Reporting lag / data currency:**
   - Monthly burn rate tracking ends August 2025; data modified 10/03/2025 but no actuals after September payment recorded.
   - **No QuickBooks data provided** for this project; all actuals from Drive budget documents/tracking spreadsheets.
   - **Risk:** May not reflect true project-to-date spend if labor hours or expenses were incurred post-08/2025 and not yet logged in tracking spreadsheet.

4. **Indirect cost allocation uncertain:**
   - Budget includes $6,598 overhead (46.61% rate) + $3,802 G&A (18.32% rate) = $10,400 (42.4% of total)
