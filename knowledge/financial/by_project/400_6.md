## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Status: **ON TRACK** (with caveats)

**Summary:**
- Project is **nominally under budget** in terms of labor hours charged (5 hours vs. 73 budgeted = 6.8% utilization through August 2025)
- However, **full contract value ($24,556.00) has been invoiced**, suggesting this is a fixed-price or milestone-based deliverable contract, not a time-and-materials engagement
- **Payment collection is behind schedule:** $12,055.41 (49%) remains outstanding as of October 2025, with Net 30 terms due May 14, 2025

### Category-Level Assessment:

**Direct Labor:**
- **Status:** Significantly UNDERSPENT to date
- **Details:** Only 5 hours charged (Jan 2025: 1 hr; April 2025: 4 hrs) totaling $969.59 against a budgeted $14,156.00
- **Analysis:** This dramatic underutilization (6.8%) suggests either:
  1. Work was backend-loaded (most effort occurred late in PoP or post-May 2025)
  2. Dr. Jack Elston's time was applied to final deliverable documentation in April 2025, with remaining budgeted hours potentially not required or not yet invoiced separately
  3. Labor costs are embedded in the fixed milestone payment rather than tracked separately
- **Risk:** If remaining 68 budgeted hours are not utilized, the project may not have consumed planned resources, raising questions about scope delivery or effort allocation

**Indirect Costs (Overhead + G&A):**
- **Status:** NOT SEPARATELY TRACKED in actuals
- **Details:** Budgeted at $10,400.00 (42.4% of total contract) but no corresponding actual expense entries in monthly tracking
- **Analysis:** Indirect costs are likely applied as a burden rate or fixed overhead allocation embedded in the $24,556.00 invoice, rather than being charged separately by labor hour
- **Recommendation:** Confirm with finance/accounting whether indirect costs are rolled into the milestone invoice or tracked separately

**Equipment, Travel, Materials:**
- **Status:** NOT BUDGETED; $0.00 spent
- **Assessment:** Phase I SBIR scope appropriately scoped to labor and overhead only; no deviations

### Key Financial Concerns:

1. **Payment Delay:**
   - Invoice issued April 14, 2025; due May 14, 2025 (Net 30)
   - Payment of only $12,500 received by October 2025 (4.5 months late)
   - **Outstanding: $12,055.41 remains unpaid**
   - **Risk Level:** MEDIUM — Partial payment received suggests AFWERX/Curable is processing the invoice, but nearly 50% is still outstanding after 6 months

2. **Labor Hour Tracking Discrepancy:**
   - Budgeted 73 hours; only 5 hours charged through August 2025
   - Only 4 hours charged during the actual PoP (Feb–May); 1 hour in January (pre-PoP)
   - **Question:** Where are the remaining 68 budgeted hours? Are they:
     - To be charged after May 2025 (post-PoP)?
     - Not required for the deliverable?
     - Absorbed into the fixed milestone price?
   - **Risk Level:** LOW-MEDIUM — Requires clarification with PM and Curable Composites

3. **Fixed-Price vs. Time-and-Materials Ambiguity:**
   - Contract appears to be a **fixed-price deliverable** (SBIR Phase I) rather than time-and-materials
   - Full $24,556.00 invoiced upon CLIN0002 completion, not based on hours worked
   - Suggests budgeted hours are an internal planning tool; actual cost to customer is the fixed $24,556.00
   - **Financial Impact:** If designed this way, margin is protected regardless of actual hours; BST could profit if hours < 73

4. **Post-PoP Work:**
   - Period of Performance officially ends May 2, 2025
   - Invoice issued April 14, 2025; final report submitted April 16, 2025 (within PoP)
   - However, most tracking data and payment activity occurs post-PoP (April–October 2025)
   - **Risk:** Confirm no out-of-scope work is being performed without additional authorization/funding

5. **Phase II Planning:**
   - Final Report indicates Phase II opportunity is being pursued (customer memos from Marine Corps and SOCOM in progress)
   - No Phase II contract or budget provided in current documents
   - **Recommendation:** Plan Phase II separately; no financial impact to Project 400-6 unless Phase II formally awarded
