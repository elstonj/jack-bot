## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Status: **ON TRACK / WELL CONTROLLED**

**Strengths:**
1. **Significant Budget Cushion:** Project has spent only $969.59 in direct labor through August 2025, against a $14,156.00 budgeted labor amount (3.9% burn rate). Remaining budget is $23,586.41.
2. **Milestone-Based Billing:** Invoice structure ($24,556.00 invoiced upon CLIN0002 report submission in April 2025) indicates performance-based delivery rather than cost-plus billing, reducing cost overrun risk.
3. **No Cost Overruns by Category:** Equipment, travel, and materials all at $0.00 with no budgeted amount, so no variance.
4. **Conservative Overhead Allocation:** Indirect costs are built into the fixed contract value; no additional overhead risk.

**Areas of Note:**
1. **Labor Underutilization:** Only 5 hours charged (Jan–Apr 2025) against 73 budgeted hours for a 3-month PoP (Feb–May). This suggests either:
   - Minimal actual work performed by Dr. Elston during the PoP, or
   - Backend-loaded effort with most work occurring after May 2025 and labor tracked retrospectively, or
   - Reporting lag in the monthly tracking spreadsheet.
   
2. **Schedule Risk:** Period of Performance was Feb 3 – May 2, 2025, but significant labor hours appear not to have been logged until after the PoP ended. The April log (4 hours, $775.67) is the only charged activity during the official performance window.

3. **Payment Timing / Cash Flow:** 
   - Invoice issued 04/14/2025 (during PoP)
   - Full invoice ($24,556.00) submitted 04/16/2025
   - Only $12,500.00 received by September 2025 (~5 months later)
   - Outstanding balance of $12,055.41 remains as of October 2025
   - **Cash flow risk:** BST may have funded work upfront and is awaiting final payment from Curable Composites/AFWERX.

4. **Indirect Costs:** 42.4% of the total contract ($10,400.00) is allocated to overhead (46.61%) and G&A (18.32%). These are fixed within the contract but not individually tracked in the monthly actuals — actual indirect cost allocation unknown.
