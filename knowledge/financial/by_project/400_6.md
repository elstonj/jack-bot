     - Hours were worked but not billed/logged until milestone delivery?
     - Actual hours significantly less than budgeted (cost savings)?

   - This affects true cost performance assessment.

**3. Phase II Opportunity Timing**
   - **Concern:** Final Report (04/21/2025) indicates Phase II pursuing Marine Corps & SOCOM memos. No Phase II award documented as of 10/03/2025.
   - **Action:** Determine Phase II award status and timeline. If awarded, will be separate contract; ensure proper accounting separation.

**4. Indirect Cost Tracking**
   - **Concern:** Monthly burn rate tracking shows labor only; overhead and G&A ($10,400) applied at invoice level but not tracked monthly.
   - **Action:** For future projects, implement monthly indirect cost allocation to enable real-time budget monitoring by category.

Labor hours reconciliation needed to validate true project cost performance. Awaiting Phase II award determination for continued funding.
