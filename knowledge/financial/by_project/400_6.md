### Summary Assessment
**Project 400-6 is financially healthy and on-track.** The contract is performing as expected for a Phase I SBIR with milestone-based deliverables. Low actual labor spending relative to budget reflects:
1. Concentrated, efficient execution focused on April final report delivery
2. Possible subcontractor labor supplementation (not captured in BST monthly tracking)
3. Milestone billing model where full contract value invoiced upon completion, not monthly accrual

**No cost overruns or budget ceiling violations observed.** Outstanding payment of $12,056.00 is expected and consistent with government payment timelines. Phase II opportunity identified; recommend continuation subject to customer feedback (Marine Corps, SOCOM memos referenced in Final Report, Section 3.5).
