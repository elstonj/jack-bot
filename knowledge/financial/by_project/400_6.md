# Project 400-6 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** "Field-Deployable Manufacturing of UAS Composite Parts" / "Adaptive UAS Manufacturing Initiative"
- **Client/Funding Agency:** AFWERX (U.S. Air Force)
- **Prime Contractor:** Curable Composites
- **BST Role:** Subcontractor / Technical Partner
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (AFWERX X24.7)
- **Period of Performance:** February 3, 2025 – May 2, 2025 (3 months)
- **Brief Scope:** Technical feasibility study for field-deployable composite manufacturing of unmanned aircraft systems (UAS) parts. BST provided Principal Investigator support (Dr. Jack Elston) to develop and validate proof-of-concept hollow composite cylinders, establishing manufacturing approach at TRL 3–4 for fuselage-specific applications.
