### By Category Analysis:

**Direct Labor (Budgeted $14,156.00)**
- **Status:** SEVERELY UNDERSPENT
- **Actual Spend:** $969.59 (6.8% of budgeted $14,156)
- **Details:** Only 5 billable hours logged across Jan–Aug 2025:
  - 1 hour in January ($193.92)
  - 4 hours in April ($775.67)
  - 0 hours Feb, March, May–August
- **Variance:** +$13,186.41 unspent
- **Assessment:** Dr. Elston was budgeted for 73 hours over 3-month PoP (Feb–May) but logged minimal hours. This suggests either:
  - Work occurred but was not billable/tracked
  - Scope was reduced or shifted
  - Hours were reported under a different cost center

**Indirect Costs (Budgeted $10,400.00 | 46.61% OH + 18.32% G&A)**

However, the $12,500 interim payment (50.9% of contract value) in September suggests milestone-based billing rather than time-and-materials cost-plus reimbursement.

**Equipment, Travel, Materials & Supplies**

### Key Financial Concerns:

1. **Schedule Compliance Risk - MODERATE**
   - Period of Performance ended May 2, 2025, but minimal labor hours recorded during that window.
   - 5 total hours (1 Jan + 4 Apr) suggests work may have been concentrated outside documented timeline or not billed to this subcontract.
   - However, CLIN0002 Final Report was completed and submitted on time (04/16/2025), indicating deliverables met schedule.

2. **Labor Hour Discrepancy - MODERATE**
   - Budgeted 73 hours; only 5 hours logged through August.
   - Variance of +68 hours (~93%) suggests either:
     - Underestimation of effort in budget vs. actual efficiency
     - Time reporting lag or allocation to different cost centers
     - Scope change not reflected in budget documents
   - **Mitigation:** Final Report delivered on schedule, indicating scope completion despite hour variance.

3. **Payment Reconciliation - MINOR**
   - Invoice issued 04/22/2025 for full $24,556.00
   - Only $12,500.00 received as of October 2025
   - Outstanding balance: $12,056.00 (49.1% of contract value)
   - **Assessment:** Standard for milestone-based SBIR Phase I; likely awaiting additional deliverables, final acceptance, or contract closeout documentation from AFWERX.

4. **Cost Control - POSITIVE**
   - Project is well under budget across all tracked categories.
   - No cost overruns or budget ceiling concerns.
   - Conservative spending aligns with Phase I lean innovation model.

### Phase II Opportunity:

- Phase II would represent expansion of contract scope and revenue; financial impact TBD.

### Conclusion:
**Project 400-6 is financially healthy with no cost overrun concerns.** The extreme underspend of actual labor ($969.59 vs. $14,156.00 budgeted) is offset by successful on-time delivery of CLIN0002 Final Report and partial payment receipt ($12,500.00). The discrepancy between budgeted and actual labor hours warrants verification of time tracking methodology and cost allocation, but the business outcome (deliverable completion + interim payment) confirms project viability. Recommend confirming final payment status and Phase II prospects with Curable Composites.
