In typical cost-reimbursable contracts, indirect rates are applied to actual direct labor; therefore, if only $969.59 in direct labor has been charged, indirect costs billed would be proportionally lower (~$452 in OH + $177 in G&A, totaling ~$629 in indirect allocation rather than the full budgeted $10,400).
