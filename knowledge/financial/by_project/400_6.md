## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Status: **UNDERSPENT / ON TRACK (with caveats)**

**Strengths:**
1. **Invoice issued on schedule:** CLIN 0002 final report and invoice ($24,556.00) submitted as planned on 04/16/2025, indicating deliverable completion.
2. **Well under labor budget (through Aug 2025):** Only $969.59 in labor charges against $14,156.00 budgeted (6.8% utilization). This suggests either conservative scheduling, backend-loaded work, or a lag in timekeeping reporting.
3. **No cost overruns in line items:** Equipment, travel, materials, and supplies all at zero spend as budgeted (Phase I lean scope).
4. **Partial payment received:** $12,500.00 interim payment in September 2025 demonstrates customer acknowledgment and cash flow.

**Concerns & Risks:**
1. **Severe schedule underutilization:** Budgeted 73 hours over 3-month PoP (Feb–May 2025), but only **5 hours logged** through August 2025 (1 hour Jan + 4 hours Apr). This is a **93.2% variance** from expected labor consumption.
   - *Possible explanations:* Milestone-based or lump-sum billing rather than time-and-materials; report writing deferred to April/May; timekeeping lag in Curable system; or actual work performed outside tracked hours.
   
2. **Payment delay / Outstanding balance:** Full invoice ($24,556.00) issued 04/14/2025 with Net 30 terms (due 05/14/2025), but as of 10/03/2025 (5+ months later), only $12,500.00 has been received. **$12,055.41 remains outstanding** (49.1% of invoice unpaid).
   - *Risk level: MEDIUM.* Typical government payment cycles can extend 45–90 days, but 6-month delay warrants follow-up with Curable Composites and AFWERX.
   
3. **Indirect cost bridge unclear:** Budget allocates $6,598.00 (OH) + $3,802.00 (G&A) = $10,400.00 in indirects. These are **percentage-based allocations**, not direct expense data. QuickBooks actual indirect spending is **not provided** in documentation.
   - *Data gap:* Unable to verify whether actuals for overhead and G&A match rates or require reconciliation.

4. **Phase II funding uncertain:** Final report (04/21/2025) notes Curable is pursuing Phase II with customer validation memos but no Phase II contract or follow-on budget is documented. Continuation of work depends on external approval.
