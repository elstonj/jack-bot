**Summary:**
Project 400-6 presents a complex financial picture. On paper, the full contract value ($24,556.00) was invoiced in April 2025 for CLIN0002 Final Report completion. However, actual labor expenses tracked through August 2025 are minimal ($969.59 for only 5 hours), and only a partial payment ($12,500.00) has been received as of October 2025. ### Key Findings by Category:

#### **Direct Labor**
- **Status:** SIGNIFICANTLY UNDERSPENT (only 6.8% of $14,156 budget used)
- **Details:**
  - Budgeted: 73 hours at $150/hr + 29.28% fringe = $14,156.00
  - Actual (tracked Jan–Aug 2025): Only 5 hours = $969.59
  - **Variance:** $(13,186.41) favorable (to date)
- **Concern:** Severe discrepancy between budgeted 73 hours over 3-month PoP (≈24 hrs/month) and actual 5 hours logged. Possible causes:
  1. Work was heavily backend-loaded into April with minimal tracking (4 of 5 hours logged in April)
  2. Hours may not be fully recorded in provided monthly tracking (reporting lag)
  3. Dr. Elston's effort may have been front-loaded before January or shifted post-May (outside PoP)
  4. Work conducted off-hours or bundled into invoice as fixed-price deliverable rather than T&M
