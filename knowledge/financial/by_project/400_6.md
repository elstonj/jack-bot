# Project 400-6 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Project Title:** "Field-Deployable Manufacturing of UAS Composite Parts" / "Adaptive UAS Manufacturing Initiative"
- **Client/Funding Agency:** AFWERX (U.S. Air Force); subcontract from Curable Composites
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (AFWERX X24.7)
- **Prime Contractor:** Curable Composites
- **BST Role:** Subcontractor/Technical Partner
- **Period of Performance:** February 3, 2025 – May 2, 2025 (3 months)

  - **Reporting lag:** Hours tracked after invoice submission not yet recorded in Drive tracking documents

- **Invoice submitted:** April 14, 2025 (full $24,556.00) for CLIN0002 Final Report submitted April 16, 2025

- **Payment pattern:** Interim $12,500.00 payment (50%) received September 2025 (~5 months after invoice); suggests AFWERX/Curable Composites processing delay or holdback pending Phase II decision
