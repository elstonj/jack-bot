## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Status: **ON BUDGET (Significantly Underspent)**

**Key Findings:**

1. **Labor Spending vs. Budget:**
   - **Budgeted:** $14,156.00 (73 hours @ $150/hr + 29.28% fringe)
   - **Actual (through Aug 2025):** $969.59 (approximately 5 hours only)
   - **Variance:** $(13,186.41) favorable; only 6.9% of budgeted labor consumed
   - **Assessment:** Severe underspend. Only 5 hours logged (1 in January, 4 in April) despite 3-month PoP. This suggests either:
     - Work schedule was backend-heavy with deliverable completed in April
     - Labor hours are being tracked/invoiced through indirect cost allocation rather than direct charge
     - Significant schedule lag or reporting delay

2. **Overall Spend vs. Budget (Approved Amount):**
   - **Total Budget:** $24,556.00
   - **Total Invoiced:** $24,556.00 (100% of contract value)
   - **Total Paid to Date:** $12,500.00 (50.9% of contract value)
   - **Outstanding:** $12,056.00 (49.1% pending)
   - **Assessment:** Full invoice amount claimed (suggesting full scope completion and indirect cost allocation), but only 50% payment received as of October 2025. No cost overrun.

3. **Cost Category Performance:**
   - **Labor:** 6.9% actual vs. 57.6% budgeted → Strong underspend; likely deferred to Phase II or allocated to indirect costs
   - **Overhead (46.61% of labor):** Billed in Invoice #1664; actual allocations not itemized

4. **Burn Rate & Schedule Performance:**

   - **Assessment:** Massive disparity between tracked labor hours and invoiced amount suggests invoice is for **deliverable-based milestone billing** rather than time-and-materials. Final Report delivered 04/16/2025 triggered Invoice #1664 for full contract value.

5. **Payment Status Concerns:**

## Financial Health Risk Assessment

### **HIGH PRIORITY CONCERNS:**

1. **Payment Recovery Risk — CRITICAL**
   - **Issue:** 49% of invoice ($12,056.00) remains unpaid 6+ months after invoice issuance and 5 months after PoP end date.
   - **Cash Flow Impact:** BST funded the full deliverable but has not recovered nearly half the contract value.
   - **Recommendation:** Follow up with Curable Composites on payment schedule. Verify if Phase II award is contingent on Phase I final payment.

2. **Labor Hour Reconciliation — MODERATE**
   - **Issue:** Budgeted 73 hours; only 5 hours charged through August 2025. Invoiced full contract value including full labor and indirect costs.
   - **Explanation:** Likely that final invoice was milestone-based (upon Final Report submission 04/16/2025) rather than time-based. Indirect costs appear to be fully billed upfront.
   - **Risk:** If final 68 hours were not actually worked, there may be cost control or labor tracking issues. If hours were deferred to Phase II, clarify Phase II budget and funding source.

3. **Schedule vs. Budget Timing**
   - **Issue:** PoP ended May 2, 2025, but major work appeared to occur in April (4 hours charged). Very light activity in January, February, March.
   - **Assessment:** Schedule is extremely compressed or heavily backend-loaded. No impact on budget ceiling, but raises questions about resource allocation and rework risk.
