### Key Concerns & Mitigation Notes

1. **Significant Hour Variance (5 actual vs. 73 budgeted)**
   - **Risk:** Potential compliance or cost accounting issue if hours should have been charged but were not
   - **Mitigation Needed:** Reconcile labor tracking with actual PI effort; confirm all billable hours were captured; review whether indirect costs were appropriately allocated to delivered invoice

2. **Outstanding Invoice Balance ($12,055.41 > 120 days overdue)**
   - **Risk:** Cash flow impact; potential need for follow-up with Curable Composites/AFWERX on payment status
   - **Mitigation:** Contact Curable Composites for payment status; verify second invoice (if applicable) was submitted for remaining balance; escalate if payment is not received by end of Q4 2025

3. **Indirect Cost Recovery**
   - **Risk:** $10,400 in budgeted indirect costs may not be fully recovered if labor underbilling persists
   - **Mitigation:** Confirm indirect costs were included in Invoice 1664 ($24,556 total); if labor charges were reduced, clarify whether indirect cost allocation was proportionally adjusted; reconcile against negotiated indirect cost rates

4. **Phase II Continuity**
   - **Opportunity:** Phase II prospects are strong; customer engagement (Marine Corps, SOCOM) suggests Phase I was successful
   - **Action:** Prepare for Phase II proposal if customers provide formal interest; budget implications will depend on Phase II scope and award structure

### Recommendation
**Project is financially healthy from a budget consumption standpoint but requires clarification on labor accounting and immediate follow-up on outstanding invoice payment.** The 3.9% spend rate and early deliverable completion are positive indicators, but the $12,055 outstanding balance and significant hour discrepancy warrant attention from finance and program management.
