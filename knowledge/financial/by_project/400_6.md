- **Budgeted:** 73 hours × $150/hr = $10,950.00 (plus $3,206 fringe = $14,156 total labor)
- **Actual Charged (Jan–Aug 2025):** 5 hours = $969.59
- **Variance:** −$13,186.41 (6.85% utilization) — **Major underrun**

**Assessment:** The project is **significantly under-budget on labor**, but this is a red flag rather than good news because:
- Budgeted labor represents only ~0.53 calendar months of effort over a 3-month PoP (already lean)
- Only 5 hours charged through August, yet CLIN0002 Final Report (a substantial deliverable) was completed and submitted April 16
- **Possible explanations:**
  - Hours may be under-reported or back-loaded into final invoicing (reconciliation with full Phase I billing)
