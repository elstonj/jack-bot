## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Status: **SIGNIFICANTLY UNDERSPENT BUT FULLY INVOICED**

#### Key Findings:

1. **Invoicing vs. Actual Spend Disconnect:**
   - **Full contract amount ($24,556.00) invoiced on 04/14/2025** per CLIN0002 completion
   - **Only $969.59 in actual labor charges recorded through August 2025** (5 hours logged)
   - **Variance:** $23,586.41 (96.1% of contract) between invoiced amount and tracked labor expenses
   - **Status:** This is typical for **milestone-based SBIR contracts** where invoicing is tied to deliverable completion, not contemporaneous labor tracking. The invoice represents the full value of the CLIN0002 Final Report deliverable, not time-and-materials billing.

2. **Payment Status:**
   - **Invoice 1664 (04/14/2025):** $24,556.00 issued; Net 30 terms (due 05/14/2025)
   - **Payment Received (09/2025):** $12,500.00 (50.9% of invoice)
   - **Outstanding Balance:** $12,056.00 as of October 2025
   - **Risk:** Invoice due date was 05/14/2025; payment not received until 09/2025 = **4-month payment delay**
   - **Assessment:** Significant accounts receivable aging; remainder still unpaid as of data cutoff

3. **Labor Category Analysis:**
   - **Budgeted Direct Labor:** $14,156.00 (73 hours @ $150/hr + 29.28% fringe)
   - **Actual Labor Charged (Jan–Aug 2025):** $969.59 (5 hours)
   - **Remaining Labor Budget:** $13,186.41 (93.2% unspent)
   - **Likely Explanation:** 
     - Work concentrated in Q2 (April: 4 hours charged; minimal Jan, Feb, Mar activity)
     - Labor hours may not be fully reported or backdated through August 2025
