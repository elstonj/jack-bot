## Conclusion

**Project 400-6 (Adaptive UAS Manufacturing Initiative)** is a lean, time-limited SBIR Phase I subcontract to Curable Composites valued at **$24,556.00** for 3 months (Feb–May 2025). The project delivered its primary CLIN0002 Final Report on schedule (04/16/2025), demonstrating proof-of-concept composite manufacturing and establishing TRL 3–4 feasibility.

**Financial Position:** The project remains **significantly under budget** with only $970 in labor charges through August 2025 (3.9% burn), consistent with a Phase I deliverable-focused scope. An interim payment of $12,500 (51% of contract) was received in September 2025; $12,056 remains outstanding as of October 2025.

**Risks:** Primary concerns are (1) outstanding payment balance post-5-month invoice lag, and (2) minimal labor tracking relative to budgeted hours and stated PoP — recommend verification of actual effort and payment schedule with Curable Composites and AFWERX to ensure cash flow and schedule alignment.

**Next Phase:** Phase II opportunity is in process (Marine Corps/SOCOM customer engagement); Phase II funding will likely fall under a separate project code upon award.
