# Project 018-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Murphy's Pond CH4 Monitoring
- **Client/Agency:** Murray State University (Dr. Bassil El Masri, Principal Investigator)
- **Contract Type:** Commercial service contract for drone-based greenhouse gas measurement collection
- **Period of Performance:** Field campaigns in 2026 (May, July, August); funding expires December 31, 2026
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Collection of CH4, CO2, and H2O measurements over a 5 km × 5 km area above Murphy's Pond, Hickman County, Kentucky using S2 VTOL fixed-wing drone. Research focus: methane emissions from wetlands and bottomland hardwood forests; using remote sensing to upscale site measurements to larger geographic areas. Three flights completed or planned (Flights #1 & #2 completed Nov–Dec 2025; Flight #3 due June 30, 2026); potential Flight #4 funding ($31k) awaiting confirmation (overdue as of May 20, 2026).
