### Key Findings

**1. On-Budget Overall:**
The project is tracking well within the approved contract value. Invoiced amount ($62,283.66) includes two flights (#1 and #2) and represents ~70% of the $88,862.94 contract. The remaining ~$26.6K should cover Flight #3 and associated logistics (assuming similar costs per flight). **However, contingency for Flight #4 (+$31K) will depend on funding confirmation due 2026-06-29.**

**2. Underspend in Specific Categories:**
- **Travel:** Only $1,751.90 spent vs. $8,213 proposed = 21% of budget used. **Risk:** Travel for Flight #3 training/rental operations not yet scheduled per Asana (Jack Elston). Additional travel costs are likely pending, which could narrow this variance.
- **Freight:** $2,966.24 incurred, but BST Internal Budget proposed $0 for freight/shipping (marked "TBD"). These costs are unbudgeted but appear reasonable given equipment shipments (S2 UAS, batteries, launcher) to Kentucky and Mexico. **Risk:** Battery shipment destination (Mexico, permanent import) may incur additional customs or return-shipping costs if scope clarification with Dr. Masri reveals a mismatch.

**3. Direct Labor & Indirect Costs:**
The BST Internal Budget proposed $37,232 in direct labor (Jack Elston, Maciej Stachura) plus $29,632 in indirect costs (OH & G&A). **Detailed actuals by labor category are not available from QuickBooks**, making it difficult to assess whether labor hour allocations are on track. The invoiced line items ($37,704.37 + $24,579.29) represent service deliverables (S2 rental + flight planning) rather than labor cost breakdowns. **Recommendation:** Cross-reference Asana time tracking or internal timesheets to verify labor burden against proposed budget.

**4. Payment Status Risk:**
- Invoice #1698 (09/09/2025, $37,704.37): **Paid** ✓
- Invoice #1728 (12/11/2025, $24,579.29): **Outstanding** as of due date 01/10/2026. No indication in provided data whether this has since been paid. **Action:** Verify payment status with Murray State accounting.

**5. Cash Flow & Schedule Concentration:**
Two invoices totaling $62,283.66 were submitted for flights #1 & #2 (Nov–Dec 2025). Flight #3 (May 27, 2026) and potential Flight #4 (if confirmed by June 29, 2026) will likely trigger additional invoices in May–August 2026. **Timing risk:** All remaining work must complete by Dec 31, 2026 when funding expires. Competing project scheduling (INSTAAR x2, Ottawa demo) may delay Flight #3 execution, compressing the timeline for Flights #3 & #4.

**6. Unbudgeted/At-Risk Costs:**
- **Battery Shipment to Mexico:** Permanent import fees paid; scope/destination alignment with Dr. Masri uncertain. Could trigger additional customs, return-shipping, or re-import costs to U.S. if clarification reveals a mismatch. **Status per Asana:** "Clarification with Dr. Masri may be needed."
- **Travel for Flight #3/4:** $8,213 proposed but only $1,751.90 spent to date. Jack Elston has open task to schedule S2 rental training/travel (due 2026-06-01). Expect ~$2,000–$3,000 in travel costs to materialize in May–June 2026.
- **Payload Parts & Modifications:** Joshua Fromm reported parts arriving end of week (2026-06-02/03); DB9 rewiring ongoing. Cost source not specified in Asana; may be charged against materials budget or included in payload engineering labor (Fromm is not listed in proposed labor budget, suggesting potential unbudgeted allocation).

**7. Contract Variance vs. Proposal:**
- BST Proposed Budget: $84,847
- Murray State Contracted Amount: $88,862.94
- **Variance:** +$4,015.94 (4.7% above internal proposal)

The contract was signed at a higher value than the internal budget estimate. This provides a ~$4K buffer, but it is being consumed by unbudgeted freight costs ($2,966.24) and will be further pressured by upcoming travel and possible payload/parts costs.
