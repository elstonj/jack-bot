# Project 018-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Murphy's Pond CH4 Monitoring
- **Client/Agency:** Murray State University, Department of Earth and Environment Sciences (Dr. Bassil El Masri, belmasri@murraystate.edu, 270-809-3110)
- **Contract Type:** Commercial service contract for drone-based greenhouse gas measurement collection
- **Period of Performance:** Funding expires December 31, 2026; three field campaigns planned for May, July, and August 2026
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Collection of CH₄, CO₂, and H₂O measurements via S2 VTOL fixed-wing UAS over a 5 km × 5 km area above Murphy's Pond, Hickman County, Kentucky. Research objective: methane emissions from wetlands and bottomland hardwood forests; remote sensing data to upscale site measurements to larger geographic areas.
