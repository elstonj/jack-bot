# Project 018-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Murphy's Pond CH4 Monitoring
- **Client/Agency:** Murray State University (Dr. Bassil El Masri, Department of Geosciences)
- **Contract Type:** Commercial service contract for drone-based greenhouse gas measurement collection
- **Period of Performance:** Funding effective through December 31, 2026; field campaigns scheduled May–August 2026 (3 flights minimum; potential 4th flight pending funding confirmation)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Collection of CH₄, CO₂, and H₂O measurements over 5 km × 5 km area above Murphy's Pond, Hickman County, KY using Black Swift S2 VTOL aircraft. Includes flight operations, data processing, flux mapping via Atmofacts, and payload configuration (FLIR + A5100 cameras, methane sensor).

**Note:** Flights #1 and #2 completed November and December 2025. Flight #3 confirmed (task closed 2026-05-13); due 2026-06-30. Optional Flight #4 has $31k available funding but confirmation is **OVERDUE** (due 2026-05-20 per Beck Cotter task).
