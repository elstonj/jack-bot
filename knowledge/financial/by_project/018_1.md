# Project 018-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Murphy's Pond CH4 Monitoring
- **Client/Agency:** Murray State University (Dr. Bassil El Masri, Principal Investigator)
- **Contract Type:** Service contract for drone-based greenhouse gas measurement collection
- **Period of Performance:** 3 field campaigns in 2026 (May, July, August); funding expires 2026-12-31
- **Scope:** Collection of CH4, CO2, and H2O measurements via S2 fixed-wing UAS over a 5 km × 5 km area above Murphy's Pond, Hickman County, KY. Three confirmed flights; fourth flight ($31k) pending funding confirmation by 2026-06-29.
- **Brief Description:** Drone-based atmospheric sampling to measure methane, carbon dioxide, and water vapor concentrations for environmental monitoring. Includes data processing, flux mapping, and deliverables via Atmofacts.
