1. **Proposed vs. Contracted Mismatch:** BST's internal budget (12/16/2024) totaled $84,847, but Murray State was quoted $88,862.94 (per Quote MUR-228S-25). The difference ($4,015.94) is not explained in available documents.

   - The proposed budget ($84,847) does not list "Leased Equipment" as a category, yet all invoiced services ($62,084.68 base + $2,966.24 freight) are for S2 UAS rental and support.

3. **QBO Total Expense vs. Invoice Total:** 
   - QBO unclassified.md lists [018-1] as $62,084.68 (total expense).
   - Invoiced amounts (Invoices #1698, #1728, #1779) total $86,862.94.
   - Drive Budget Docs freight ($2,966.24) is NOT included in QBO $62,084.68 figure, suggesting freight was paid by Murray State or invoiced separately.
   - **Data reconciliation needed:** Is $62,084.68 QBO figure only labor/services, with freight handled outside? Or is it outdated?

4. **Flight #4 Funding:**
   - Asana notes Flight #4 (~$24.5K) was added via SOW modification (July 2026).
   - No Flight #4 invoice appears in QBO commercial.md or Drive Budget Docs yet.
   - If Flight #4 invoiced, total would be ~$111,362.94, exceeding approved contract ($88,862.94) by ~$22,500.

5. **Actuals vs. Proposed—Cost Category Breakdown:**
   - **No granular breakdown by cost category available from QBO.** Invoices consolidate all services into single line items.
   - Proposed budget assumes internal labor + consultants + travel; actual invoicing consolidates into "UAS rental + data processing" flat fees.
   - **Recommendation:** Request detailed invoice cost allocation from BST or Murray State to determine if labor, consultants, travel, and indirect costs are embedded in the rental fees or invoiced separately.
