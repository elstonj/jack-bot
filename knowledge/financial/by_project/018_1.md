# Project 018-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Murphy's Pond CH4 Monitoring
- **Client/Agency:** Murray State University (Dr. Bassil El Masri, Principal Investigator)
- **Contract Type:** Service contract for drone-based greenhouse gas measurement collection
- **Period of Performance:** May 2026 – December 31, 2026 (funding expires year-end 2026)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Collection of CH₄, CO₂, and H₂O measurements via fixed-wing UAS (S2 aircraft) over a 5 km × 5 km area above Murphy's Pond, Hickman County, Kentucky. Project comprises three primary field campaigns (Flights #1, #2, #3) with optional Flight #4 contingent on funding confirmation. Deliverables include raw sensor data, processed flux maps (via Atmofacts), and scientific analysis to upscale site measurements to larger geographic areas for wetland and bottomland hardwood forest emissions research.
