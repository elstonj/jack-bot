# Project 018-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Murphy's Pond CH4 Monitoring
- **Client/Agency:** Murray State University (Dr. Bassil El Masri, Department of Geosciences)
- **Contract Type:** Commercial service contract for drone-based greenhouse gas measurement collection
- **Period of Performance:** May 2025 – December 31, 2026 (funding expires year-end 2026)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Collection of CH₄, CO₂, and H₂O measurements using S2 VTOL aircraft over a 5 km × 5 km area above Murphy's Pond, Hickman County, Kentucky. Project includes 3–4 field campaigns (Flights #1–#4), data processing, flux mapping, and analysis. Research objective: methane emissions from wetlands and bottomland hardwood forests using remote sensing to upscale site measurements to larger geographic areas.
