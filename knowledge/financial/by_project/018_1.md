- **Full Project Name:** Murphy's Pond CH4 Monitoring
- **Client/Agency:** Murray State University, Department of Earth and Environment Sciences (Dr. Bassil El Masri, Principal Investigator)

- **Period of Performance:** August 2025 – December 31, 2026
