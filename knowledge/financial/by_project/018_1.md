# Project 018-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Murphy's Pond CH4 Monitoring
- **Client/Agency:** Murray State University (Dr. Bassil El Masri, Principal Investigator)
- **Contract Type:** Service contract for drone-based greenhouse gas measurement collection
- **Period of Performance:** Funding expires 2026-12-31; field campaigns planned for May, July, August 2026 (per Drive Budget Docs); Flights #1 & #2 completed November–December 2025; Flight #3 target Spring 2026 (2026-03-02), **confirmation deadline 2026-04-10 OVERDUE as of 2026-04-20**
- **Brief Scope:** Collection of CH₄, CO₂, and H₂O measurements over 5 km × 5 km area above Murphy's Pond, Hickman County, KY using Black Swift S2 UAS; research focus on methane emissions from wetlands and bottomland hardwood forests; remote sensing to upscale site measurements to larger geographic areas
