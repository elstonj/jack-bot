# Project 018-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Murphy's Pond CH4 Monitoring
- **Client/Agency:** Murray State University (Principal Investigator: Dr. Bassil El Masri)
- **Contract Type:** Service contract for drone-based greenhouse gas measurement collection
- **Period of Performance:** Funding expires 2026-12-31; three field campaigns planned in 2026 (May, July, August per budget docs; actual dates uncertain per Asana risk signals)
- **Brief Description:** Collection of CH₄, CO₂, and H₂O measurements via drone over 5 km × 5 km area above Murphy's Pond, Hickman County, KY. Research focus: methane emissions from wetlands and bottomland hardwood forests; using remote sensing to upscale site measurements to larger geographic areas.
