# Project 018-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Murphy's Pond CH4 Monitoring
- **Client/Agency:** Murray State University (Dr. Bassil El Masri, Department of Earth and Environment Sciences)
- **Contract Type:** Service contract for drone-based greenhouse gas measurement collection
- **Period of Performance:** 3 field campaigns in 2026 (May, July, August); funding expires 2026-12-31
- **Brief Description:** Collection of CH4, CO2, and H2O measurements over a 5 km × 5 km area above Murphy's Pond, Hickman County, KY using S2 VTOL aircraft. Research focus: methane emissions from wetlands and bottomland hardwood forests; using remote sensing to upscale site measurements to larger geographic areas. Field location: 7192 State Route 307 North, Fancy Farm, KY.
