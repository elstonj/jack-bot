- **Full Project Name:** Murphy's Pond CH4 Monitoring
- **Client/Agency:** Murray State University (Dr. Bassil El Masri, Procurement Services)

### Reconciliation & Notes:

1. **Data Limitations:** QuickBooks expense line [018-1] ($62,084.68) appears to be a **total project cost** rolled up from multiple sub-categories (labor, subcontractors, equipment, travel, materials). The breakdown by category is **not available** in the provided QuickBooks extract.

2. **Travel Variance:** Budget estimated $8,213 for 6 person-trips (2 persons × 3 campaigns × $1,609.62/trip). Murray State Expense Tracking shows $3,956.24 for the first campaign (Aug–Sept 2025). Remaining campaigns' travel costs are not yet incurred or booked. Flight #3 confirmation is **overdue and unconfirmed**; S2 rental travel (2026-06-01) is **not yet scheduled** per Asana. This category is **significantly under budget** to date but remains incomplete.

3. **Freight:** BST internal budget listed Materials/Supplies/Shipping as "TBD ($0.00)." Actual freight costs total $2,966.24 (two shipments via third-party carriers). One invoice ($1,269.24 from Aeronet) is still **outstanding** as of the summary date.

4. **Invoiced vs. Actual Spend:**
   - **Invoiced to Murray State:** $62,283.66 (Invoice #1698 $37,704.37 paid; #1728 $24,579.29 outstanding)
   - **QBO Actual Spend + Freight:** $65,050.92
   - **Difference:** ~$2,767 (likely includes Aeronet freight invoice #103029915 $1,269.24 not yet included in QBO total, plus other timing/accrual adjustments)

5. **Budget Status:** Project is **under the proposed BST budget of $84,847** on a cost basis ($65,050.92 actual + freight). However, against the **contracted amount of $88,862.94**, actual spend plus invoiced amounts total $65,050.92, leaving an **estimated remaining budget of ~$23,811.02**. However, significant deliverables remain: Flight #3 (unconfirmed, estimated $24,579.29), S2 aircraft rental (cost TBD), and optional Flight #4 ($31k available per Dr. Masri).
