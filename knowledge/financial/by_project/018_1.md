# Project 018-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Murphy's Pond CH4 Monitoring
- **Client/Agency:** Murray State University (Dr. Bassil El Masri, Principal Investigator)
- **Contract Type:** Service contract for drone-based greenhouse gas measurement collection
- **Period of Performance:** Field campaigns in 2025–2026; funding expires **2026-12-31**
- **Scope:** Collection of CH₄, CO₂, and H₂O measurements over 5 km × 5 km area above Murphy's Pond, Hickman County, KY using Black Swift S2 UAS platform

**Brief Description:**
Black Swift Technologies provides drone-based atmospheric sampling services to measure greenhouse gas concentrations and flux estimates. Project includes three completed field flights (Flights #1–#3 in Nov 2025, Dec 2025, Jul 2026) and one proposed Flight #4 now **indefinitely postponed as of 2026-07-22** per Daniel Prendergast (Asana). Remaining open tasks include payload checklists, CO₂ payload data rate investigation, and travel finalization—all status uncertain pending Flight #4 rescheduling decision. **Critical risk: Only ~4.5 months remain in funding window; Flight #4 scope/timeline alignment with 2026-12-31 deadline is unresolved.**
