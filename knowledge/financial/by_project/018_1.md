# Project 018-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Project Name:** Murphy's Pond CH4 Monitoring
- **Client/Agency:** Murray State University (Dr. Bassil El Masri, Department of Earth and Environment Sciences)
- **Contract Type:** Service contract for drone-based greenhouse gas measurement collection
- **Period of Performance:** Funding expires 2026-12-31; three field campaigns planned (May, July, August 2026)
- **Brief Description:** Collection of CH₄, CO₂, and H₂O measurements via S2 UAS over a 5 km × 5 km area above Murphy's Pond, Hickman County, KY. Research focuses on methane emissions from wetlands and bottomland hardwood forests; using remote sensing to upscale site measurements to larger geographic areas.
