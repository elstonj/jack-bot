# Project 018-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Murphy's Pond CH4 Monitoring
- **Client/Agency:** Murray State University / Dr. Bassil El Masri
- **Contract Type:** Service contract for drone-based greenhouse gas measurement collection
- **Period of Performance:** May 2026 – December 31, 2026 (funding expires 2026-12-31)
- **Brief Description:** Collection of CH₄, CO₂, and H₂O measurements over 5 km × 5 km area above Murphy's Pond, Hickman County, KY using Black Swift S2 fixed-wing UAS. Project includes 3–4 field campaigns with flux mapping and data processing. Research focus: methane emissions from wetlands and bottomland hardwood forests; upscaling site measurements to larger geographic areas.
