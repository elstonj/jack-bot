## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Status: **OVER BUDGET**

**Key Findings:**

1. **Total Project Cost Overrun: -$7,102 (109% of approved budget)**
   - Approved contract value: $80,975.00
   - Projected total cost: $88,077.00 (as of May 2025)
   - **Overage: $7,102.00**
   - Two invoices issued ($78,619.00) represent 97% of contract value, but actual expenses already exceed this, suggesting final billing may also be over-budget or a financial adjustment is needed.

2. **Direct Labor Overrun: -$7,553 (137% of budget)**
   - **Primary Driver:** This is the single largest variance and root cause of overall budget overrun.
   - J. Elston (PI) exceeded estimate by 43.63 hours (143.63 actual vs. 100 budgeted). At $127.42/hr loaded, this is $5,553 overrun.
   - D. Prendergast exceeded estimate by 59.5 hours (159.5 actual vs. 100 budgeted). At $56.59/hr loaded, this is $3,369 overrun.
   - **Root Cause:** In-field deployment complexity—emergency repairs (avionics damage 5/13, flap servo damage 5/19) required immediate on-site troubleshooting. Additionally, training of team members (Tyler Willhite, Will Wade) on SwiftCore and S2 systems was conducted during the deployment, driving extended hours.
   - **Mitigation:** Engineering AL came in 70% under budget (-24.25 hours), but insufficient to offset other labor overages.
   - **Concern Level:** HIGH. Labor overruns consumed the project's entire contingency/profit margin.

3. **Travel Category: Slightly Under Budget (+$949)**
   - Airfare exceeded budget by $1,333 (higher-than-expected ticket costs, possible additional travelers or last-minute bookings).
   - Car rental exceeded budget by $306.
   - Meals, incidentals, and lodging came in under budget (combined savings of $2,594).
   - **Overall Status:** Travel is performing well despite airfare surprises; cost controls on meals and accommodations are effective.
   - **Concern Level:** LOW.

4. **In-Country Consulting: On Budget ($25,000)**
   - Universidad de Costa Rica Foundation subcontract spent exactly as budgeted.
   - **Concern Level:** NONE.

5. **Other Direct Costs: Unbudgeted but Minimal ($727)**
   - Computer Services ($499) and Shipping/Insurance ($228.05) were not budgeted but are necessary project expenses.
   - Represents only 0.9% of total contract value.
   - **Concern Level:** LOW.

6. **Indirect Costs & Fee: On Target (100%)**
   - Overhead, G&A, and fee applied as planned. No variance.
   - **Concern Level:** NONE.
