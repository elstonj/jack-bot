### Key Concerns & Risks:

1. **Labor Cost Escalation (Primary Concern)**
   - Field deployment in May consumed 43.6 additional PI hours and 59.5 additional engineer hours beyond estimates.
   - Reason: Unplanned repairs (avionics 5/13, flap servo 5/19) and real-time problem-solving.
   - If similar issues arise in June/July field work, labor costs will continue to overrun.
   - **Recommendation:** Monitor labor hours weekly; consider capping PI/engineer field time or front-loading non-field work (analysis, reporting) to June/July.

2. **Contract Value Ceiling Breach (Cash Flow Concern)**
   - Total invoiced ($81,022) already exceeds approved contract ($80,972) by $50.
   - May actuals ($49,494) plus invoiced reports ($78,619) suggest total project costs will be $90,000+ when June/July actuals are finalized.
   - **Recommendation:** Secure contract modification (amendment) to increase ceiling, or negotiate cost reduction with NASA.

3. **Delayed Payment Risk (Operational Concern)**
   - NASA invoices are Net 30–60 but payment status not confirmed.
   - If payments are delayed beyond Net 30, BST may face cash flow strain.
   - **Recommendation:** Follow up with NASA on payment status of Invoices 1702 (due 06/18) and 1669 (due 08/20); confirm receipt and processing.

4. **Unbudgeted ODCs (Scope Creep)**
   - $727 spent on computer services and shipping with $0 budget allocation.
   - Suggests field operations required unanticipated support (software updates, emergency supplies, etc.).
   - **Recommendation:** Establish contingency budget (suggest 5–10%) for future field deployments.

### Summary Assessment:

**Project 200-13 is financially unhealthy and at risk of significant cost overrun.** The May 2025 actuals ($49,494) represent only ~61% of the project timeline (1.5 months of 6-month PoP) yet consume a disproportionate share of the budget. Direct labor is the primary driver of overrun (136.8% through May), caused by unplanned field repairs and real-time problem-solving. 

**If labor and travel costs continue at May rates through August, the total project cost will exceed the approved $80,972 contract value by an estimated $8,000–$15,000.** Invoiced amounts already surpass the contract ceiling. 

**Immediate action required:** 
- Monitor labor hours and approve contract modification if overrun is confirmed.
- Confirm payment receipt from NASA on invoices 1702 and 1659.
- Prioritize cost control in June/July remaining field work.
