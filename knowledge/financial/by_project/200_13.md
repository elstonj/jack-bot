## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Status: **AT RISK / OVER BUDGET**

#### Summary
As of May 2025 (mid-project), Project 200-13 shows **total actual costs of $88,077.00 against an approved budget of $80,972.00**, resulting in a **$7,102.00 budget overrun (9% over)**.

#### Cost Category Analysis

**✗ OVERRUN — Direct Labor (+$7,553 / +37%)**
- **Root Cause:** Field deployment required significantly more labor hours than estimated:
  - J. Elston (PI): 143.63 actual vs. 100 budgeted (+43.63 hrs; 44% over)
  - D. Prendergast (Travel Engineer): 159.5 actual vs. 100 budgeted (+59.5 hrs; 60% over)
- **Reason:** Real-time problem-solving during Costa Rica field operations, including avionics repairs (5/13/2025), flap servo damage (5/19/2025), and comprehensive personnel training
- **Impact:** Most significant budget driver; difficult to recover given project is in execution phase
- **Mitigation Status:** Pre/Post Engineering (AL) came in $1,368.75 under budget, providing only partial offset

**✓ FAVORABLE — Travel (+$949 / -10%)**
- **Drivers:** Lower meal/incidental spending (+$2,005 savings) and lodging savings (+$589) offset by airfare overruns (-$1,333)
- **Status:** This category is providing positive variance; however, insufficient to offset labor overrun

**✓ FAVORABLE — In-Country Consulting (On Budget)**
- Universidad de Costa Rica Foundation: $25,000.00 budgeted and spent exactly
- Critical to project success; no variance

**✗ UNBUDGETED — Other Direct Costs (-$727.05)**
- Computer Services ($499) and Shipping/Insurance ($228.05) were not originally budgeted
- Minor impact but indicates scope creep or planning gaps

**✓ ON BUDGET — Indirect Costs**
- Overhead and G&A calculated at budgeted rates; no variance
- Fixed percentage allocations tracking to plan
