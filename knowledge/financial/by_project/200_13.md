## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Status
**Project is OVER BUDGET by ~$7,331 through May 2025 (109% of proposed budget spent).**

**Key Concerns:**

1. **Labor Overrun — Critical Issue**
   - Actual labor spend ($27,935) vs. budgeted ($20,382) = **-$7,553 (37% over budget)**
   - Root causes (per Drive Budget Docs):
     - J. Elston (PI): 43.63 hours over estimate (143.63 vs. 100 budgeted)
     - D. Prendergast (Travel Engineer): 59.5 hours over estimate (159.5 vs. 100 budgeted)
     - Field deployment required extensive real-time training and problem-solving (e.g., avionics repair on 5/13, flap servo damage on 5/19)
   - At May spend rate, labor will significantly exceed full-contract budget by project end (08/20/2025)

2. **Travel Slightly Underspent (Positive)**

   - Airfare overages (+$1,333) offset by lower meal/incidental and lodging spending
   - This partial offset partially mitigates overall labor overrun

3. **Unanticipated Other Direct Costs**
   - Computer Services ($499) and Shipping/Insurance ($228.05) = $727.05 not pre-budgeted
   - These are modest but contribute to overall variance

4. **Indirect Costs — On Budget**
   - Overhead (46.61%) and G&A (18.32%) fully allocated as planned; no variance
   - These are fixed costs and will not vary

5. **Invoice Discrepancy**
   - Total invoiced ($81,972) exceeds contract value ($80,972) by $1,000
   - Potential contract modification or billing error; not resolved in provided documents
