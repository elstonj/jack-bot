## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Status: **OVER BUDGET**
- **Actual spend through May 2025:** $49,493.59
- **Budgeted project cost:** $80,975.00
- **Overrun to date:** ~$7,331 (9.0% over budgeted costs through May)
- **Remaining budget (May baseline):** ~$31,478.41 for June–August activities

### Category-Level Analysis:

**🔴 CRITICAL OVERRUN — Direct Labor (+$7,553 / +137%)**
- **Root Cause:** Field deployment required intensive real-time training, troubleshooting, and system repairs not fully anticipated in pre-deployment estimates
  - PI (J. Elston) exceeded estimate by 43.63 hours (44% overrun on 100-hr estimate)
  - Travel Engineer (D. Prendergast) exceeded estimate by 59.5 hours (60% overrun on 100-hr estimate)
  - Flight management system issues and avionics repairs drove unexpected labor intensity
- **Concern:** Labor-heavy project; if additional field work occurs in June–August, labor costs could continue to exceed budget
- **Recommendation:** Close monitoring of remaining labor allocation; consider whether final report writing and data processing will consume additional hours

**✅ ACCEPTABLE — In-Country Consulting (On Budget, $25,000)**
- Universidad de Costa Rica Foundation services tracking as budgeted
- No variance to date

**✅ ACCEPTABLE — Travel (Under Budget by $949)**
- Airfare overrun (+$1,333) offset by meal/incidental savings (+$2,005) and lodging savings (+$589)
- Net result: 89.5% of budget consumed, leaving ~$949 cushion
- **Note:** May travel data only; June–August field operations may require additional travel expenses

**⚠️ MONITOR — Other Direct Costs ($727.05 incurred, not originally budgeted)**
- Computer services ($499) and shipping/insurance ($228.05) necessary for field operations
- These are unbudgeted but proportionally small; no major concern

**✅ ACCEPTABLE — Indirect Costs (On Budget, $21,218)**
- Overhead and G&A calculated and accrued as planned
- No variance

### Burn Rate & Projections:

**May 2025 Monthly Spend:**
- Total direct + indirect costs: ~$49,493.59 (from 5/23/2025 snapshot)
- Extrapolating 6 months: ~$59,392/month × 6 = ~$356,352 (if burn continues at May rate)
- **Actual budgeted total:** $80,975

**Reality Check:**
- May was the **peak deployment month** with field operations, travel, and training
- June–August likely to include reduced travel, post-field analysis, and report writing
- Projected burn rate of $49,493/month is artificially high for non-field months
- **More realistic projection:** June–August activities estimated at $31,478 remaining budget, which is feasible if labor hours taper post-deployment

### Key Financial Risks:

1. **Labor Cost Overruns (Primary Risk):** 
   - May labor actuals ($27,935) already 37% above budget for a single month
   - Project budget total ($20,382) assumed only ~330 labor hours at fully-loaded rates
   - Actual through May: ~313 hours (J. Elston 143.63 + D. Prendergast 159.5), all in one month
   - **If June–August require significant additional labor**, project will exceed total budget
   - **Mitigation:** Ensure final report writing and data processing are resourced efficiently

2. **Invoice-to-Payment Lag:**
   - Invoice #1702 (05/19/2025) due 06/18/2025; payment status unknown
   - Invoice #1669 (07/21/2025) due 08/20/2025; payment status unknown
   - Total invoiced: $81,022; QuickBooks shows only $80,620.37 recorded (possible payment/accrual timing difference)
   - **Risk:** If NASA delays payment, cash flow impact on BST operations
   - **Recommendation:** Follow up with NASA Ames on Invoice #1702 payment status

3. **Budget Ceiling Risk:**
   - Contract value ($80,972) is firm; NASA will not authorize overages
   - Current overspend trajectory suggests final project costs could exceed award by $7,331–$10,000+
   - **This must be absorbed by BST** unless contract modification is negotiated
   - **Recommendation:** Obtain written approval from NASA if any additional scope/labor is required

4. **Timing Risk:**
   - Period of performance ends 08/20/2025
   - Invoices #1702 and #1669 both on Net 30 terms, meaning final payment may not be received until September
   - Any project closeout or final reconciliation must occur before 08/20/2025 contract expiration

### Financial Health Summary: ⚠️ **AT RISK (Moderate Concern)**
- **Current status (May 2025):** Project is running 9% over budget due to unanticipated labor intensity
- **Remaining runway:** ~$31,478 budgeted for June–August; should be sufficient if labor hours stabilize post-deployment
- **Critical success factor:** Controlling labor hours on final report writing and data analysis to stay within remaining budget
- **Positive indicator:** Travel and consulting costs on track or under budget; mainly labor is the issue
- **Recommendation:** Implement weekly labor tracking for remaining months; escalate to project manager if June labor hours exceed 50 hrs/person/week
