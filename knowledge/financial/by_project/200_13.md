## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Status: **OVER BUDGET** ⚠️

**Critical Finding:** Project has exceeded the $80,972 awarded contract value by approximately **$1,808** as of May 2025 actuals, with two months of operations (June–August) still remaining.

### Analysis by Category:

**1. Direct Labor — SIGNIFICANT OVERAGE (-$7,553 or +37%)**
- **Root Cause:** Field deployment required extensive unbudgeted engineering support due to:
  - Avionics repair (5/13/2025)
  - Flap servo damage (5/19/2025)
  - Real-time problem-solving with minimal contingency hours in original budget
- **Specific Overruns:**
  - J. Elston (PI): 43.63 hours over budget (143.63 actual vs. 100 estimated @ $127.42/hr loaded = **-$5,560 overage**)
  - D. Prendergast (Travel Engineer): 59.5 hours over budget (159.5 actual vs. 100 estimated @ $56.59/hr loaded = **-$3,368 overage**)
  - Engineering AL (Pre/Post): 24.25 hours *under* budget (10.75 actual vs. 35 estimated = **+$1,375 savings**)
- **Impact:** This is the primary driver of overall project budget overrun. Labor contingency was insufficient for field operations complexity.

**2. Travel Costs — MODESTLY OVER BUDGET (-$176 net or +2%)**
- **Airfare:** +$1,333 over budget (higher than estimated $866/person)
  - This reflects actual Denver/San Jose to Costa Rica roundtrip costs being higher than budgeted
- **Car Rental:** +$306 over budget (in-country vehicle costs)
- **Offsetting Savings:**
  - Meals & Incidentals: -$2,005 under budget (40% of budget consumed; possible in-country meals provided by host or lower-cost options)
  - Lodging: -$589 under budget (11 nights at lower rate than budgeted average)
  - Parking: -$6 over budget (negligible)
- **Overall Travel Assessment:** Travel planning was reasonably accurate; international airfare volatility was the main variance driver. Meal/incidental savings suggest good cost management in-country.

**3. In-Country Consulting — ON BUDGET (0% variance)**

- No overage or underage
