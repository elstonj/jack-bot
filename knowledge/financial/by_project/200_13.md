### Key Concerns

1. **Labor Cost Overrun (HIGH RISK):**
   - Direct labor exceeded budget by $7,553.00 in May alone (43.63 PI hours + 59.5 engineer hours over estimate).
   - Root causes cited: Field troubleshooting (avionics repair 5/13, flap servo damage 5/19), personnel training, real-time problem-solving.
   - No evidence that labor hours have been constrained in June-August; if overages continue at same rate, total labor overrun could exceed $10,000–$12,000 by project end.

2. **Contract Ceiling Exceeded (COMPLIANCE RISK):**
   - Total invoices issued ($81,972.00) already exceed contract award ($80,972.00) by $1,000.00.
   - Federal cost-plus contracts (typical for NASA Phase III R&D) have strict cost ceiling compliance requirements.
   - **Invoicing above the approved contract value without a formal modification may create a billing dispute or require cost recovery.**

3. **Unplanned Direct Costs ($727.05):**
   - Computer services and shipping/insurance were not originally budgeted.
   - Indicate unplanned field expenses; potential flag for scope creep or unforeseen technical challenges.

4. **Travel Overages on Airfare:**
   - Airfare costs $1,333 over budget (actual $3,205 vs. budgeted $1,872), representing a 71% overrun in this subcategory.
   - Possible factors: additional personnel travel, last-minute bookings, route changes. Not fully explained in provided docs.

5. **Payment Receipt Uncertainty:**
   - While three invoices totaling $81,972.00 have been issued, no evidence of payment receipt is documented in QuickBooks or other sources.
   - The contract requires net 30 payment terms; invoices #1702 (05/19) and #1669 (07/21) should have been paid by 06/18 and 08/20 respectively.
   - Delayed payments could impact BST cash flow during active project operations.

### Positive Indicators

- **Travel savings:** Meals, lodging, and incidentals came in $2,594.00 under budget, partially offsetting labor overages.
- **On-schedule delivery:** All major deliverables (kick-off, demonstrations, reports) completed on or before target dates.
- **Personnel training completed:** SwiftCore and S2 training delivered successfully (certificates issued 05/21), supporting project capability goals.
- **In-country subcontract on budget:** Universidad de Costa Rica Foundation coordination costs fully allocated and controlled at $25,000.00.

3. **Labor Tracking:** Implement daily labor hour tracking for remaining project period (June-August) to flag additional overages in real-time and allow mid-course corrections.
4. **Cost Control:** Prioritize completion of remaining milestones with pre-approved headcount; avoid scope creep or unbudgeted consulting/services.
5. **Project Closure:** Reconcile final actuals against contract by 08/20/2025; prepare cost summary and closeout invoice (if applicable).
