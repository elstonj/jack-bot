### Key Concerns

1. **Critical Labor Overrun:**
   - J. Elston (PI): 43.63 hours over budget (143.63 actual vs. 100 estimated)
   - D. Prendergast (Travel Engineer): 59.5 hours over budget (159.5 actual vs. 100 estimated)
   - **Root Cause:** Field deployment complications (avionics repair 5/13, flap servo damage 5/19) requiring extensive troubleshooting and real-time problem-solving
   - **Impact:** Overrun of $7,553 in May alone; project already exceeded total contract budget by mid-performance period

2. **Insufficient Remaining Budget for Final 3 Months:**
   - Remaining budget estimate: ~$31,478 (if starting from $80,972 contract value minus $49,493.59 May spend)
   - If labor continues at May burn rate (~$27,935/month), final 3 months would require ~$83,805, vastly exceeding remaining allocation
   - **Risk:** Significant cost overrun unless labor hours are dramatically reduced in June–August

3. **Unbudgeted Expenses:**
   - Computer services ($499) and shipping/insurance ($228) were not forecasted, further eroding available budget

4. **Invoice vs. Actual Timing Mismatch:**
   - Two large invoices issued ($39,310 + $39,309 = $78,619) but cumulative QBO spend is $82,780–$88,306
   - Suggests invoices may not reflect full period-to-date costs, or payment/accrual timing differences exist
   - **Clarification Needed:** Was cost recovery fully captured in invoicing?

5. **Payment Receipt Uncertainty:**
   - Invoice payment status not explicitly documented in provided materials
   - Invoices issued with Net 30 terms; some may not yet be collected

### Positive Indicators
- ✅ Travel spending controlled (under budget by $949 despite higher airfare)
- ✅ In-country consulting on track ($25,000 subcontract on budget)
- ✅ Indirect costs and fee applied correctly with no variance
- ✅ Major deliverables (two demonstration reports) delivered on schedule and invoiced

### Recommendations
1. **Immediate:** Conduct labor hour forecast for June–August. At current burn rate, project will significantly exceed contract value.
2. **Escalation:** Review with NASA Ames whether field complications (avionics/servo repairs) qualify for contract modification or cost relief.
3. **Cash Flow:** Prioritize collection of outstanding invoices (1702 due 06/18, 1669 due 08/20) to offset spending.
4. **Future Projects:** Implement stricter hour tracking and contingency buffers for hardware-intensive field deployments.
