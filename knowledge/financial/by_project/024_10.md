- **Downstream Consequences:**
  - June 23, 2026 handoff meeting at risk

  - Invoice 1667 ($21,181.81) contingent on successful ISARRA campaign delivery (August/September 2026); delay in primary S0 VTOL unit may cascade to ISARRA units currently in QC
- **Financial Implication:** Reputational damage, potential client dissatisfaction, possible renegotiation of payment terms or refund liability if training cannot be delivered as promised.

#### 🔴 **Risk #2: Bench Test Work Overdue (Blocking Delivery)**
- **Status:** Visual Observation Bench Test overdue as of April 30, 2026 (per Maciej Stachura); Instrumented Bench Test due ~May 1, 2026 and in progress.
- **Impact:** Both must complete before May 22 delivery deadline.
- **Financial Implication:** Each day of delay increases sunk labor costs without revenue realization; extends project timeline and may incur additional Barbados travel/logistics costs.

#### 🔴 **Risk #3: Operator Training Unassigned & Without Formal Due Date**
- **Status:** Training scheduled July 1–4, 2026 (6 trainees, Barbados); **ownership reassigned to Jack Elston per June 1, 2026, but no formal Asana due date assigned** as of July 6, 2026.
- **Impact:** 
  - Training is implicit deliverable under invoice 1634 ($22,337 includes "Training").
  - Lack of formal assignment and due date suggests inadequate planning for imminent (within days of July 6 status report) travel commitment.
  - Depends on successful S0 VTOL delivery and June 23 handoff.
- **Financial Implication:** Risk of training cancellation/delay = potential breach of invoice 1634 scope; could trigger client dispute or refund demand.

#### 🔴 **Risk #4: 902–928 MHz ISM Band Radio Interference (Microhard P900) — Critical Blocker**
- **Status:** Barbados Prime Minister's Office flagged interference risk; **no verification plan, assignee, or due date assigned**.
- **Impact:** Directly affects **operational readiness** of both invoiced deliverables (1634 & 1667). If interference is confirmed and cannot be mitigated, the S0 VTOL platforms may be non-functional for their intended purpose (meteorological data collection, ISARRA campaign).
- **Financial Implication:** Reputational and legal liability; potential contract dispute or client demand for refund/replacement if platforms are operationally deficient.
