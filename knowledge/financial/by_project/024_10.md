   - **Status:** May 22, 2026 delivery deadline substantially missed due to S0 VTOL crash/tracking bug under active investigation (Beck Cotter, March 17, 2026). Flight test restart in progress as of late April/early May 2026.

     - **Risk:** Customer dissatisfaction, potential refund claims, reputational damage affecting future commercial business.
   - **Revised Due Date:** Per Maciej Stachura (May 4, 2026), due dates require realistic update post-ByLight meeting. **No revised delivery date has been formally communicated or documented in available sources.**

#### 2. **Operator Training Unassigned & Imminent (July 1–4, 2026)**
   - **Status:** Training dates are fixed (July 1–4, 2026 in Barbados); six (6) trainees confirmed; **no trainer assigned and no training due date formally documented in Asana.**
   - **Financial Exposure:**
     - **Travel costs** (airfare, per diem, ground transport for trainer/staff) are unbudgeted or untracked in available data.
     - **Facility rental** (training venue in Barbados) not documented.
     - **Risk:** If trainer is not assigned and training materials (reassigned to Jack Elston per June 1, 2026) are incomplete, BST may not be able to deliver training on schedule, forcing reschedule (additional travel costs) or customer penalty.
     - **Timeline pressure:** Only ~6 weeks from May 4, 2026 (when delays were noted) to July 1, 2026 training start.

#### 3. **ISARRA Campaign ($21,181.81) in QC — Delivery Contingent on Bug Fixes**
   - **Status:** Two additional S0 VTOL units + ground station + tablet invoiced May 21, 2026; QC ongoing as of July 3, 2026. August delivery target confirmed by Kevin Adkins (July 6, 2026).
   - **Financial Risk:**
     - Invoice already issued and paid ($21,181.81 received in May 2026).
     - If S0 VTOL tracking bug affects campaign units (same platform as original delivery unit), QC failure or rework delays may push delivery beyond August 2026.
     - Customer may demand schedule recovery or cost recovery if campaign is delayed beyond August.
   - **Mitigation:** QC in progress; Kevin Adkins monitoring logistics approval.

#### 4. **Bench Test Work Overdue**
