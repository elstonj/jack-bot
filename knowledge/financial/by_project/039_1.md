## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Refurbished S2 Oklahoma State
- **Client/Agency:** Oklahoma State University (Board of Regents for Oklahoma State University and the A&M Colleges); OK Aerospace Institute (OAIRE), Tulsa, OK
- **Primary Contact:** Jamey Jacob (jdjacob@okstate.edu, 405-744-3208)

- **Period of Performance:** December 11, 2024 (PO received) – August 19, 2025 (training completed); Project archived October 21, 2025
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Design, complete overhaul, and delivery of a refurbished S2 UAS flight system with pneumatic launcher and multi-hole probe for meteorological research.
