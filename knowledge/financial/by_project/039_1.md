## Scope Summary
Complete refurbishment of S2 UAS flight system including:
- Motor replacement and avionics upgrades (ADS-B, Remote ID, RTK)
- Battery cycling and replacement (packs 61, 62, 67, 68)
- Launcher system overhaul (rail inspection, battery replacement, control box and connector upgrades)
- Component replacement (hinges, wipers, wing buttons, fuselage inspection, tail connectors, case foam, GCS/handset/tablet batteries)
- Air spring swap with S20009 units
- Freight delivery via special double-long pallet with power lift gate
- Operator training at OSU (July 30 – August 19, 2025)
