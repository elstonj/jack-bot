## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: Refurbished S2 Oklahoma State
- **Client/Agency**: Oklahoma State University, Board of Regents for Oklahoma State University and the A&M Colleges
- **Primary Contact**: Jamey Jacob (jdjacob@okstate.edu, 405-744-3208)
- **Contract Type**: Purchase Order for Equipment and Systems (Research/Educational Procurement)
- **Period of Performance**: December 11, 2024 – August 19, 2025
  - PO Received: December 11, 2024
  - Original Delivery Due: March 3, 2025
  - Actual Delivery/Freight Pickup: March 14, 2025 (11-day delay due to customer spring weather observation timeline)
  - Operator Training Completed: August 19, 2025

**Brief Description of Scope**:
Complete overhaul and refurbishment of one S2 UAS Flight System with pneumatic launcher and multi-hole probe for meteorological observations. Work included motor replacement, avionics upgrades (ADS-B, Remote ID, RTK integration), battery cycling and replacement, launcher system overhaul, component replacement (hinges, connectors, fuselage inspection, etc.), and operator training delivery at OSU. Aircraft delivered via specialized freight (double-long pallet with power lift gate due to non-standard launcher rail length). Project archived/completed with all 26 tasks finished successfully.

## Notes & Context

- **Contract Type**: Standard purchase order for refurbished equipment system with training and support services.
- **Discount Context**: $10,000 discount applied to refurbished S2 system service (likely reflecting refurbished vs. new pricing); discount was incorporated into the final contract value of $57,814.
- **Shipping Logistics**: Non-standard freight arrangement required due to launcher rail length (double-long pallet, power lift gate, and pallet jack operator; BST has no loading dock). Shipped via FPA01215407 bill of lading.
- **Air Spring Condition**: Rear air spring rods on the Oklahoma unit showed wear patterns; Black Swift proactively swapped with S20009's units. No cost impact or change order noted.
- **Scope**: Part of a larger potential multi-aircraft opportunity (OSU initially requested estimate for VTOL, S2, and E2 systems) but ordered separately as individual POs.
- **Training**: Operator training delivered on-site at Oklahoma State University (July 30 – August 19, 2025) by Dan Prendergast; included in contract scope.
- **Warranty**: 12-month manufacturer defect warranty on S2 UAS; 2-year software warranty included.
