- **Quote #1337** (dated 11/21/2024 by Meredith Needham) is the basis for PO P1454424 (issued 12/11/2024)
- **Special Shipping Logistics:** Aircraft launcher rail required double-long pallet, power lift gate, and pallet jack operator (BST has no loading dock). Freight carrier was responsible for coordination.
- **Battery Sourcing:** GCS battery with JST balance lead 16AWG (no connector) sourced from MaxAmps per customer specification.
- **Air Spring Condition:** Rear air spring rods on Oklahoma unit showed wear; swapped with S20009 unit (damage patterns noted for future reference).
- **Training Certification:** Operator training conducted by Dan Prendergast at OSU on July 30, 2025; completion certification provided August 19, 2025.
- **Warranty:** 12-month manufacturer defect warranty and 2-year software warranty included with refurbished S2 system (per budget docs).
