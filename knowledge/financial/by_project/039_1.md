# Project 039-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Refurbished S2 Oklahoma State
- **Client/Agency:** Oklahoma State University (Board of Regents for Oklahoma State University and the A&M Colleges); OK Aerospace Institute (OAIRE)
- **Primary Contact:** Jamey Jacob (jdjacob@okstate.edu, 405-744-3208)
- **Contract Type:** Purchase Order for equipment and systems (educational/research procurement)
- **Period of Performance:** December 11, 2024 – August 19, 2025
  - PO Received: December 11, 2024
  - Original Delivery Due: March 3, 2025
  - Actual Delivery: March 14, 2025 (11-day delay due to customer spring weather observation timeline)
  - Training Completed: August 19, 2025

**Brief Scope Description:**
Complete refurbishment and delivery of a Black Swift S2 UAS flight system with pneumatic launcher and multi-hole probe for atmospheric research. Scope included motor replacement, avionics upgrades (ADS-B, Remote ID, RTK), battery cycling and replacement, launcher system overhaul, component replacements, and on-site operator training at Oklahoma State University.
