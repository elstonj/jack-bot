- **Full Project Name**: Refurbished S2 Oklahoma State
- **Client/Agency**: Oklahoma State University (Board of Regents for Oklahoma State University and the A&M Colleges); OK Aerospace Institute for Research and Education (OAIRE)
- **Primary Contact**: Jamey Jacob, OK Aerospace Institute (jdjacob@okstate.edu, 405-744-3208)
