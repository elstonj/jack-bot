# Project 039-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Refurbished S2 Oklahoma State
- **Client/Agency:** Oklahoma State University (Board of Regents for Oklahoma State University and the A&M Colleges); OK Aerospace Institute (OAIRE)
- **Primary Contact:** Jamey Jacob, OK Aerospace Institute, Tulsa, OK (jdjacob@okstate.edu, 405-744-3208)
- **Contract Type:** Purchase Order for Equipment and Systems (Research/Educational Procurement)
- **Period of Performance:** December 11, 2024 (PO received) – August 19, 2025 (training completed)
- **Brief Description:** Complete overhaul and refurbishment of an S2 UAS (unmanned aircraft system) including motor replacement, avionics upgrades (ADS-B, Remote ID, RTK), battery cycling and replacement, launcher system overhaul, and component refurbishment. Includes pneumatic launcher and multi-hole probe for meteorological observations. Delivery via freight on March 14, 2025; operator training completed July 30 – August 19, 2025.
