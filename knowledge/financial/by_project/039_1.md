### Scope Summary
Complete refurbishment of S2 UAS Flight System with pneumatic launcher and multi-hole probe for meteorological observation. Work included motor replacement, avionics upgrades (ADS-B, Remote ID, RTK), battery cycling and replacement, launcher system overhaul, component replacement, and operator training delivery at OSU.
