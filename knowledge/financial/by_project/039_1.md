# Project 039-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Refurbished S2 Oklahoma State
- **Client/Agency:** Oklahoma State University (Board of Regents for Oklahoma State University and the A&M Colleges); OK Aerospace Institute for Research and Education (OAIRE)
- **Primary Contact:** Jamey Jacob, OK Aerospace Institute, Tulsa, OK (jdjacob@okstate.edu, 405-744-3208)
- **Contract Type:** Purchase Order for equipment and systems (educational/research procurement)
- **Period of Performance:** December 11, 2024 (PO received) – August 19, 2025 (training completed); Project archived October 21, 2025
- **Project Status:** **COMPLETED AND ARCHIVED**

**Scope Description:**
Complete overhaul and refurbishment of S2 UAS Flight System with pneumatic launcher and multi-hole probe. Work included motor replacement, avionics upgrades (ADS-B, Remote ID, RTK), battery cycling and replacement, launcher system overhaul, component replacements (hinge/wiper tape, wing QR buttons, tail connectors, case foam, GCS/handset/tablet batteries), air spring swap, delivery via freight with special logistics, and operator training at OSU.
