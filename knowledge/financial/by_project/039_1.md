# Project 039-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Project Name:** Refurbished S2 Oklahoma State
- **Client/Agency:** Oklahoma State University (Board of Regents for Oklahoma State University and the A&M Colleges)
- **Contact:** Jamey Jacob, OK Aerospace Institute, Tulsa, OK (jdjacob@okstate.edu, 405-744-3208)
- **Contract Type:** Purchase Order for equipment and systems (educational/research procurement)
- **Period of Performance:** December 11, 2024 (PO received) – August 19, 2025 (Training completed)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED/COMPLETED — All deliverables completed successfully

**Brief Description:**
Complete refurbishment of an S2 UAS (unmanned aerial system) flight system including aircraft, pneumatic launcher, and multi-hole probe for meteorological research. Scope included motor replacement, avionics upgrades (ADS-B, Remote ID, RTK), battery cycling and replacement, launcher system overhaul, component replacement, and operator training. Aircraft delivered March 14, 2025; training completed August 19, 2025.
