## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Refurbished S2 Oklahoma State (S2 UAS Flight System with Pneumatic Launcher and Multi-Hole Probe)
- **Client/Agency:** Oklahoma State University, Board of Regents for Oklahoma State University and the A&M Colleges
- **Contact:** Jamey Jacob (jdjacob@okstate.edu, 405-744-3208) / Teresa Blakley (teresa.blakley@okstate.edu)
- **Contract Type:** Purchase Order (Educational/Research Equipment Procurement)
- **Period of Performance:** 
  - PO Issued: December 11, 2024
  - Equipment Delivery/Freight Pickup: March 14, 2025
  - Operator Training Completed: August 19, 2025
  - Project Status: **COMPLETED & ARCHIVED** (October 21, 2025)
- **Brief Description:** Complete refurbishment and delivery of a Black Swift S2 fixed-wing UAS flight system including pneumatic launcher and multi-hole probe for atmospheric research at Oklahoma State University. Project included motor replacement, avionics upgrades (ADS-B, Remote ID, RTK), battery cycling, launcher system overhaul, component replacements, and operator training.
