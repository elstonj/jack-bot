# Project 039-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Refurbished S2 Oklahoma State
- **Client/Agency:** Oklahoma State University (Board of Regents for Oklahoma State University and the A&M Colleges), Oklahoma Aerospace Institute for Research and Education (OAIRE)
- **Primary Contact:** Jamey Jacob (jdjacob@okstate.edu, 405-744-3208)
- **Contract Type:** Purchase Order (Equipment/Systems Sale)
- **Period of Performance:** December 11, 2024 – August 19, 2025
- **Brief Description:** Complete overhaul and refurbishment of S2 UAS Flight System with pneumatic launcher and multi-hole probe for meteorological research. Scope includes motor replacement, avionics upgrades (ADS-B, Remote ID, RTK), battery cycling and replacement, launcher system overhaul, component replacements, delivery via freight, and operator training at customer site.
