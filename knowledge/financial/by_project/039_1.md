# Project 039-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Refurbished S2 Oklahoma State
- **Client/Agency:** Oklahoma State University, Board of Regents for Oklahoma State University and the A&M Colleges
- **Contact:** Jamey Jacob (jdjacob@okstate.edu, 405-744-3208); Buyer: Teresa Blakley (teresa.blakley@okstate.edu)
- **Contract Type:** Purchase Order for equipment and systems (research/educational procurement)
- **Period of Performance:** December 11, 2024 (PO received) – August 19, 2025 (training completed)
- **Status:** **COMPLETED & ARCHIVED** (Oct 21, 2025)

**Description of Scope:**
Complete refurbishment and delivery of a Black Swift S2 UAS flight system with pneumatic launcher and multi-hole probe to Oklahoma State University's Oklahoma Aerospace Institute for Research and Education (OAIRE). Work included motor replacement, avionics upgrades (ADS-B, Remote ID, RTK), battery cycling and replacement, launcher system overhaul, component replacement, and operator training. Aircraft delivered March 14, 2025; operator training completed August 19, 2025.
