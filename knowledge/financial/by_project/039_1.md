- **Client/Agency:** Oklahoma State University (Board of Regents for Oklahoma State University and the A&M Colleges); OK Aerospace Institute (OAIRE)
- **Primary Contact:** Jamey Jacob, OK Aerospace Institute (jdjacob@okstate.edu, 405-744-3208)

- **Period of Performance:** December 11, 2024 – October 21, 2025
  - PO Received: December 11, 2024
  - Original Due Date: March 3, 2025
  - Delivery/Freight Pickup: March 14, 2025 (11-day slip due to customer spring weather observation timeline requirements)
  - Operator Training Completed: August 19, 2025
  - Project Archived: October 21, 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Refurbishment and delivery of a Black Swift S2 UAS Flight System with pneumatic launcher and multi-hole probe for meteorological research. Work included motor replacement, avionics upgrades (ADS-B, Remote ID, RTK integration), battery cycling and replacement, launcher system overhaul, component replacements, air spring swap, special logistics/shipping, and operator training at OSU. Project completed successfully with all 26 tasks closed and 100% completion rate.
