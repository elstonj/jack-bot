# Project 039-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Refurbished S2 Oklahoma State
- **Client/Agency:** Oklahoma State University, Board of Regents for Oklahoma State University and the A&M Colleges
- **Contact:** Jamey Jacob (jdjacob@okstate.edu, 405-744-3208)
- **Contract Type:** Purchase Order for equipment and systems (educational/research procurement)
- **Period of Performance:** December 11, 2024 (PO received) – August 19, 2025 (training completed); Project archived October 21, 2025
- **Brief Description:** Complete refurbishment and delivery of S2 UAS flight system with pneumatic launcher and multi-hole probe, including operator training. Project involved motor replacement, avionics upgrades (ADS-B, Remote ID, RTK), battery cycling, launcher system overhaul, and component replacement. Aircraft delivered March 14, 2025; operator training completed at OSU July 30 – August 19, 2025.
