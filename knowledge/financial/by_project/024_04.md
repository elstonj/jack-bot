# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DAF — Department of Air Force)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II — Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-11-05 (31 months)
- **Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Work includes upgrading the radiometer with improved RFI mitigation and calibration capabilities, integrating thermal and NDVI sensors, and developing algorithms to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics.

**Milestone 2 (Completed as of report date 2024-04-11):**
- **Radiometer Modifications:**
  - Designed new revision of L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) with real-time in-flight calibration
  - Enhanced RFI (radio frequency interference) mitigation
  - Digital correlator for improved RFI detection
  - Lumped element 0°/180° hybrid at L-band with gain imbalance <0.15 dB, phase imbalance <0.5°
  - 2×2 microstrip patch antenna array (vs. previous MiCo antenna) with ~40° beamwidth and ~35 MHz bandwidth

- **UAS Modifications:**
  - Integrated thermal and NDVI sensors to E2 platform
  - Developed algorithms to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics
