# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Soil Moisture Mapping (SMM) Phase II – Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force, AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II – Project 400-5, X22.4D
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-06-05 (26 months, 1 day)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development and validation of L-band soil moisture sensing system integrated with Black Swift Technologies' E2 small UAS platform. Scope includes radiometer modifications (RFI mitigation, calibration), integration of thermal and NDVI sensors, algorithm development to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics (cone index), and operational runway integrity validation for U.S. Air Force bases. Milestone 2 (radiometer modifications) completed as of 2024-04-11.
