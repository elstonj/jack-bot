# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Soil Moisture Mapping (SMM) Phase II – Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DAF – Department of Air Force), via AFWERX SBIR
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II, Project 400-5, X22.4 (also referenced as "SMM Phase II 22.4D" in QuickBooks)
- **Period of Performance:** Data in QuickBooks spans 2024-04-05 to 2026-06-05 (26 months, 1 day); Milestone 2 Report dated 2024-04-11; last modified 2025-07-08
- **Brief Description of Scope:**  
  Development of modifications to Black Swift Technologies' L-band soil moisture sensing system (LDCR radiometer) and E2 small UAS platform to enable runway integrity validation for U.S. Air Force bases. Key deliverables include upgraded radiometer with improved RFI mitigation and calibration, integration of auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors, and algorithms to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics for operational deployment.
