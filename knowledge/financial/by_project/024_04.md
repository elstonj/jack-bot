# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II – Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04 to 2026-05 (24 months, 48 days) *(Source: QuickBooks)*
- **Scope:** Development of modifications to L-band soil moisture sensing system (LDCR radiometer) and E2 small UAS platform to enable runway integrity validation for U.S. Air Force bases through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Includes radiometer redesign with RFI mitigation, auxiliary sensor integration (thermal, NDVI), and algorithm development. *(Source: Milestone 2 Report)*
