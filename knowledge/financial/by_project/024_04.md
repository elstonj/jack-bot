# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SMM Phase II)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force, DAF)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-05-22 (24 months, 48 days) *(based on QuickBooks date range)*
- **Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Scope includes upgrading the radiometer with improved RFI mitigation and calibration capabilities, integrating auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors, and developing algorithms to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

*Source: Proposals & Reports (Milestone 2 Report, dated 2024-04-11 / last modified 2025-07-08)*

### Completed Milestones:
- **Milestone 2 — Radiometer Modifications (Completed as of 2024-04-02):**
  - Designed new revision of L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) with:
    - Real-time in-flight calibration using interior references
    - Enhanced RFI (radio frequency interference) mitigation
    - Digital correlator for improved RFI detection
    - Lumped element 0°/180° hybrid at L-band
    - 2x2 microstrip patch antenna array (~40° beamwidth, ~35 MHz bandwidth)
    - Three-stage amplifier chain optimally driving down converter/digital back end

- **Milestone 2 — UAS Modifications (In Progress / Partial Completion):**
  - Integration of auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors to E2 platform
  - Development of algorithms to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics (cone index)

### Upcoming/Ongoing Deliverables:
- Runway integrity validation algorithms and operational deployment guidance for Air Force bases
- Software integration and field validation testing
- Final Phase II report (documentation not provided)
