# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Name:** Soil Moisture Mapping (SMM) Phase II – Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS

**Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force, DAF)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II (Project 400-5, X22.4D)

**Period of Performance:** 2024-04 to 2026-07 (approximately 28 months)

**Scope:** Development and validation of L-band soil moisture sensing system integrated with Black Swift Technologies' E2 small UAS platform to enable runway integrity assessment for Air Force bases. Technical work includes radiometer modifications (L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer with RFI mitigation), integration of auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors, algorithm development, and field validation at Air Force installations.
