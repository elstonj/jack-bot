## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source:** Proposal/Milestone Report (SBIR Phase II Milestone 2 Report)

### Completed Milestones

**Milestone 2 — Radiometer Modifications & UAS Integration (Status: Completed as of 2024-04-11)**

**Radiometer Development (LDCR — L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer):**
- New revision designed with:
  - Real-time in-flight calibration using interior references (adjustable, wide temperature range)
  - Enhanced RFI (radio frequency interference) mitigation
  - Digital correlator for improved RFI detection
  - Improved immunity to gain fluctuation and thermal drift
- RF chain design includes integrated 90° hybrid coupler
- Three amplifier stages optimally driving down converter/digital back end
- Lumped element 0°/180° hybrid at L-band: gain imbalance <0.15 dB, phase imbalance <0.5°
- 2×2 microstrip patch antenna array (~40° beamwidth, ~0.8× flight height antenna footprint, ~35 MHz bandwidth)

**UAS Modifications (E2 Platform):**
- Integration of radiometer, thermal sensor, and NDVI sensor on E2 small UAS
- Development of sensor interfaces and data acquisition system
- Flight testing and validation (in progress per document timestamp 2025-07-08)
