## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force / AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II – Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4D)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-09-30 (28 months, 26 days) *[from QuickBooks date range]*
- **Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Work includes upgrading the radiometer with improved RFI mitigation and calibration, integrating auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors, and developing algorithms to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposals & Reports (Milestone 2 Report, dated 2024-04-11 / last modified 2025-07-08)**

### Completed Milestones
- **Milestone 2: Radiometer Modifications** ✓ (Completed as of report date)
  - Designed new revision of L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) with:
    - Real-time in-flight calibration using interior references
    - Enhanced RFI (radio frequency interference) mitigation
    - Digital correlator for improved RFI detection
    - Improved immunity to gain fluctuation and thermal drift
  - RF chain with integrated 90° hybrid coupler
  - 2×2 microstrip patch antenna array (vs. previous MiCo antenna)
    - ~40° beamwidth (~0.8× flight height antenna footprint)
    - ~35 MHz bandwidth
    - Gain imbalance <0.15 dB, phase imbalance <0.5°

### UAS Modifications (In Progress/Completed)
- E2 platform integration with upgraded radiometer
- Auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensor integration
- Algorithm development for soil moisture ↔ soil integrity correlation
