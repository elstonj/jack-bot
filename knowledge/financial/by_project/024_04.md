# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (Soil Moisture Mapping Phase II)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DAF — Department of Air Force)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II — Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-06-05 (26 months, 1 day)
- **Brief Scope:** Black Swift Technologies is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Work includes upgrading the radiometer with improved RFI mitigation and calibration, integrating auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors, and developing algorithms to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics for operational use at Air Force bases.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposals & Reports (Milestone 2 Report, dated 2024-04-11)**

### Completed Milestones:
1. **Milestone 2: Radiometer Modifications (Completed)**
   - Designed new revision of L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) with:
     - Real-time in-flight calibration using interior references
     - Enhanced RFI (radio frequency interference) mitigation
     - Digital correlator for improved RFI detection
     - Better immunity to gain fluctuation and thermal drift
   - RF chain design: integrated 90° hybrid coupler, three amplifier stages, optimized down converter/digital back end
   - 2x2 microstrip patch antenna array (~40° beamwidth, ~35 MHz bandwidth)
   - **Financial Impact:** Design and prototype development work completed; testing and integration ongoing.
