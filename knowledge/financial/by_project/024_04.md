# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force, AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program Code:** Project 400-5, X22.4 (Soil Moisture Mapping / SMM Phase II 22.4D)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-05-06 (24 months, 31 days; estimated)
- **Brief Description:** Development of modifications to BST's L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for U.S. Air Force bases. Includes radiometer upgrades with improved RFI mitigation and calibration, integration of thermal and NDVI sensors, and algorithm development to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics for operational use.
- **Key Personnel:** Daniel Prendergast (Project Lead)

### Completed Milestones

**Milestone 2 — Radiometer Design & Integration (Completed)**
- New L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) revision designed with:
  - Real-time in-flight calibration using interior references
  - Enhanced RFI (radio frequency interference) mitigation
  - Digital correlator for RFI detection
  - Improved thermal stability (gain imbalance <0.15 dB, phase imbalance <0.5°)
  - 2x2 microstrip patch antenna array (~40° beamwidth, ~35 MHz bandwidth)
