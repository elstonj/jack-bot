# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Soil Moisture Mapping (SMM) Phase II – Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force – DAF)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II, Project 400-5, X22.4D
- **Period of Performance:** Approximately 24 months (2024-04 through 2026-05, per QuickBooks data range)
- **Brief Scope:** Development and integration of L-band soil moisture sensing system (LDCR radiometer with RFI mitigation) and auxiliary thermal/NDVI sensors onto BST E2 small UAS platform to enable real-time runway integrity validation for Air Force bases. Project includes radiometer redesign, UAS modifications, sensor integration, algorithm development, and validation testing.
