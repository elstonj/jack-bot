# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Soil Moisture Mapping (SMM) Phase II – Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DAF – Department of Air Force)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II – Project 400-5, X22.4
- **Period of Performance:** Ongoing (Milestone 2 completed 2024-04-11; data extends through 2025-11)
- **Brief Description:** Development of modifications to Black Swift Technologies' L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Work includes radiometer upgrades with improved RFI mitigation, integration of thermal and NDVI sensors, and algorithm development for soil integrity correlation.
