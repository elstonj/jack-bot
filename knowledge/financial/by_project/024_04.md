# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DAF — Department of Air Force)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II — Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-06-05 (26 months, 1 day)
- **Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. The project involves upgrading the radiometer with improved RFI mitigation and calibration capabilities, integrating auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors, and developing algorithms to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source:** Proposals & Reports — Milestone 2 Report (SBIR Phase II Milestone Report)

### Milestone 2: Radiometer Modifications (Status: Completed as of 2024-04-11)
- **Deliverable:** New revision of L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) with:
  - Real-time in-flight calibration using interior references with adjustable, wide temperature range
  - Enhanced RFI (radio frequency interference) mitigation
  - Digital correlator for improved RFI detection
  - Better immunity to gain fluctuation and thermal drift
  - Lumped element 0°/180° hybrid at L-band: gain imbalance <0.15 dB, phase imbalance <0.5°
  - 2×2 microstrip patch antenna array (~40° beamwidth, ~35 MHz bandwidth)

### UAS Modifications (In Progress)
- Modifications to E2 small UAS platform for integration with upgraded radiometer and auxiliary sensors (thermal, NDVI)

### Sensor Integration & Algorithm Development (In Progress)
- Integration of thermal and NDVI sensors
- Development of algorithms to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics (cone index) for operational use at Air Force bases
