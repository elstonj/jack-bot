# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Name:** Soil Moisture Mapping (SMM) Phase II – Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS

**Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force – DAF)

**Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II (Project 400-5, X22.4D)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II

**Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-07-31 (27 months, 26 days)

**Scope:** Development of modifications to Black Swift Technologies' L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) and E2 small UAS platform to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Work includes radiometer upgrades (RFI mitigation, calibration improvements), auxiliary sensor integration (thermal, NDVI), and algorithm development for correlation between soil moisture and soil integrity metrics.
