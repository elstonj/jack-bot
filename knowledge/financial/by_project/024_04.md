# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force / AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II – Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4D)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-06-05 (26 months, 1 day) *(from QuickBooks date range)*
- **Brief Scope:** Development of L-band soil moisture sensing system modifications and E2 small UAS integration to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Key work includes radiometer design upgrades with RFI mitigation, integration of thermal and NDVI sensors, and algorithm development for soil integrity correlation. Milestone 2 (radiometer modifications) completed as of report date 2024-04-11.
