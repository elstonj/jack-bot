# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force, AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4, also referenced as "SMM Phase II 22.4D")
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04 to 2026-09 (approximately 29 months, based on QuickBooks data range and project report dates)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development and validation of modifications to Black Swift Technologies' L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for U.S. Air Force operations. Includes radiometer redesign with improved RFI mitigation and calibration, integration of thermal and NDVI sensors, and algorithm development to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics.
