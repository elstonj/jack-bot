# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force, DAF)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II — Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-09-30 (28 months, 26 days) *(per QuickBooks date range)*
- **Brief Description:** BST is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system (LDCR radiometer) and E2 small UAS platform to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. The project includes radiometer upgrades (RFI mitigation, calibration), auxiliary sensor integration (thermal, NDVI), algorithm development, and field validation at Air Force bases.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

*From Proposal/Milestone 2 Report (dated 2024-04-11, last modified 2025-07-08):*

### Completed Milestones
- **Milestone 2: Radiometer Modifications (Completed)**
  - Designed new revision of L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) with:
    - Real-time in-flight calibration with adjustable temperature range
    - Enhanced RFI (radio frequency interference) mitigation
    - Digital correlator for improved RFI detection
    - Lumped element 0°/180° hybrid at L-band (~40° beamwidth)
    - 2×2 microstrip patch antenna array (~35 MHz bandwidth)
  - Integration of auxiliary sensors (thermal, NDVI)
  - Algorithm development for soil moisture–soil integrity correlation

- Real-time algorithm deployment and refinement
- System integration with E2 UAS platform
