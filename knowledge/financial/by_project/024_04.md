# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a small UAS (Soil Moisture Mapping – SMM Phase II)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DAF – Department of Air Force)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II – Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4D)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-11-05 (31 months, 31 days) *[based on QuickBooks date range]*
- **Brief Description:** Development of modifications to Black Swift Technologies' L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Work includes radiometer upgrades with improved RFI mitigation, integration of thermal and NDVI sensors, and algorithm development for correlating soil moisture with soil integrity metrics.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source:** Proposals & Reports – Milestone 2 Report (created 2024-04-02, last modified 2025-07-08)

### Completed Milestones:

**Milestone 2 — Radiometer Modifications & Integration (Completed)**
- Designed new revision of L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) with:
  - Real-time in-flight calibration using interior references
  - Enhanced RFI (radio frequency interference) mitigation
  - Digital correlator for improved RFI detection
  - Immunity to gain fluctuation and thermal drift improvements
- RF chain design with 90° hybrid coupler, three amplifier stages, optimized down converter/digital backend
- 2×2 microstrip patch antenna array (vs. previous MiCo antenna):
  - ~40° beamwidth (~0.8× flight height antenna footprint)
  - ~35 MHz bandwidth
  - Lumped element 0°/180° hybrid: gain imbalance <0.15 dB, phase imbalance <0.5°

**Status:** Milestone 2 is marked as completed in the report (last modified 2025-07-08).
