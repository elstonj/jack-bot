# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DAF — Department of Air Force)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II — Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-06-05 (26 months, 1 day) per QuickBooks data; documents dated through 2025-07-08
- **Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Work includes upgrades to the radiometer with RFI mitigation, integration of auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors, and algorithm development for correlating soil moisture with soil integrity metrics.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposal/Milestone 2 Report (dated 2024-04-11 creation, last modified 2025-07-08)**

**Completed:**
- **Milestone 2 — Radiometer Modifications (Completed)**
  - Designed new revision of L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) with:
    - Real-time in-flight calibration using interior references
    - Enhanced RFI (radio frequency interference) mitigation
    - Digital correlator for improved RFI detection
    - Lumped element 0°/180° hybrid at L-band with <0.15 dB gain imbalance, <0.5° phase imbalance
    - 2×2 microstrip patch antenna array (~40° beamwidth, ~35 MHz bandwidth)

**Upcoming/In Progress:**
- **UAS Modifications** — Integration of thermal and NDVI sensors, firmware updates for E2 platform
- **Algorithm Development** — Correlating soil moisture with soil integrity/cone index metrics
- **Operational Validation** — Testing at Air Force bases
