# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force / AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program Reference:** Soil Moisture Mapping (SMM) Phase II, Project 400-5, X22.4D
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-07 to 2026-09-28 (approximately 29 months)
- **Brief Description:** BST is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Deliverables include upgraded radiometer with RFI mitigation, auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors, algorithms correlating soil moisture to soil integrity metrics, and operational capability for Air Force bases.
