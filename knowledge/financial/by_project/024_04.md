# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (Soil Moisture Mapping Phase II)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force, AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program Code:** 400-5, X22.4D
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-10-05 (29 months, 1 day) *(from QuickBooks date range)*
- **Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is developing L-band soil moisture sensing system modifications and E2 small UAS enhancements to enable runway integrity validation for USAF through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Work includes radiometer upgrades with improved RFI mitigation, auxiliary sensor integration (thermal, NDVI), and algorithm development to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics.
