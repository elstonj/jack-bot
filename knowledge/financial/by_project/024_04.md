# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Project Name:** Soil Moisture Mapping (SMM) Phase II - Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II (Project 400-5, X22.4)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-05-22 (24 months, 48 days)
- **Brief Description:** Development and validation of L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS platform to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Scope includes radiometer modifications with improved RFI mitigation, integration of auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors, and algorithm development for soil integrity correlation.
