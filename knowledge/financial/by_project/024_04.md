# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II – Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4)
- **Period of Performance:** Approximately 2024–2026 (24+ months)
- **Brief Description of Scope:**
  Black Swift Technologies is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force. The project involves upgrading the radiometer with improved RFI mitigation and calibration, integrating auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors, and developing algorithms to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics for operational Air Force base use. Milestone 2 (Radiometer Modifications) was completed as of the Milestone 2 Report dated 2024-04-11.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**From Proposal/Report (Milestone 2 Report, dated 2024-04-11):**

- **Milestone 2: Radiometer Modifications — COMPLETED**
  - Designed new revision of L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) with:
    - Real-time in-flight calibration using interior references with adjustable, wide temperature range
    - Enhanced RFI (radio frequency interference) mitigation
    - Digital correlator for improved RFI detection
    - Improved immunity to gain fluctuation and thermal drift
  - RF chain design includes integrated 90° hybrid coupler, optimized amplifier stages, lumped element 0°/180° hybrid at L-band
  - 2×2 microstrip patch antenna array (replacing previous MiCo antenna) with ~40° beamwidth and ~35 MHz bandwidth
