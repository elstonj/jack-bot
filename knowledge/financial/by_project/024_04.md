# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Soil Moisture Mapping (SMM) Phase II – Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force), AFWERX SBIR Program
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program Reference:** Project 400-5, X22.4D
- **Period of Performance:** Data available 2024-04-05 to 2026-06-05 (26 months documented)
- **Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Work includes radiometer upgrades with improved RFI mitigation, integration of auxiliary sensors (thermal, NDVI), and algorithm development.
