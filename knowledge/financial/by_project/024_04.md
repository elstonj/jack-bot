# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (Soil Moisture Mapping)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force / AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II – Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4)
- **Period of Performance:** Not explicitly stated in provided documents; Milestone 2 Report dated 2024-04-11; testing activities April 12–26, 2024
- **Brief Description of Scope:**  
  Development and integration of L-band soil moisture sensing system (LDCR radiometer with RFI mitigation) and E2 small UAS platform to enable runway integrity validation for U.S. Air Force bases through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Work includes radiometer redesign, UAS modifications, auxiliary sensor integration (thermal, NDVI), and algorithm development.
