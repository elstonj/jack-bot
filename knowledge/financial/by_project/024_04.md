# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (Soil Moisture Mapping Phase II)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force — DAF)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II — Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4D)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-11-05 (31 months)
- **Brief Description:** BST is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for USAF through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Work includes radiometer upgrades with improved RFI mitigation, integration of thermal and NDVI sensors, and algorithm development to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics for operational use at Air Force bases.
