# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II – Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-05-06 (24 months, 31 days) *(from QuickBooks actuals date range)*
- **Brief Description:** Development of modifications to Black Swift Technologies' L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Project includes radiometer upgrades with improved RFI mitigation and calibration, integration of thermal and NDVI sensors, and algorithm development.
