# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Soil Moisture Mapping (SMM) Phase II – Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force – DAF)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II (Project 400-5, X22.4D)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-06-05 (26 months, 1 day)
- **Brief Description:** BST is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. The project includes upgrading the radiometer with improved RFI mitigation and calibration capabilities, integrating auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors, and developing algorithms to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics for operational use at Air Force bases.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**From Proposal/Report (SBIR Phase II Milestone 2 Report, dated 2024-04-11):**

- **Milestone 2 (Completed):** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements
  - **Radiometer Modifications:** Designed new revision of L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) with:
    - Real-time in-flight calibration using interior references with adjustable, wide temperature range
    - Enhanced RFI (radio frequency interference) mitigation
    - Digital correlator for improved RFI detection
    - Better immunity to gain fluctuation and thermal drift
    - 2×2 microstrip patch antenna array (vs. previous MiCo antenna) providing ~40° beamwidth and ~35 MHz bandwidth
  - **UAS Modifications:** Upgraded E2 small UAS platform integration with thermal and NDVI sensors
  - **Algorithm Development:** Correlation algorithms developed to link soil moisture with soil integrity metrics
