# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force / AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II – Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-06-05 (26 months, 1 day) *(per QuickBooks date range)*
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development of modifications to Black Swift Technologies' L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS platform to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force. Includes upgrades to the L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) with improved RFI mitigation and calibration, integration of thermal and NDVI sensors, and development of algorithms correlating soil moisture to soil integrity metrics for Air Force base operations. Milestone 2 report indicates completion of radiometer design modifications and UAS integration work.
