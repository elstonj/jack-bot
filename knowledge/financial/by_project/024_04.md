# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force, AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program:** Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4) — also referenced as "SMM Phase II 22.4D"
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-05-06 (24 months) [Source: QuickBooks]
- **Key Personnel:** Daniel Prendergast
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development of modifications to Black Swift Technologies' L-band soil moisture sensing system (LDCR radiometer) and E2 small UAS platform to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Includes radiometer upgrades with RFI mitigation, integration of thermal and NDVI sensors, and algorithm development to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics for operational use at Air Force bases.
