# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DAF — Department of Air Force)
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II — Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-07-09 (27 months, 5 days) *(from QuickBooks date range)*
- **Brief Description:** BST is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system (LDCR radiometer) and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for U.S. Air Force bases through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Project includes radiometer design improvements (RFI mitigation, calibration), integration of thermal and NDVI sensors, algorithm development, and field validation. Milestone 2 (Radiometer Modifications) was completed as of the 2024-04-11 report date.
