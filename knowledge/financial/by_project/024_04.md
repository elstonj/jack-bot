# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (Soil Moisture Mapping Phase II)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force, AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Project 400-5, X22.4D
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-07-18 (27 months, 13 days)
- **Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Work includes upgrading the radiometer with improved RFI mitigation and calibration capabilities, integrating auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors, and developing algorithms to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics for operational use at Air Force bases.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source:** Proposals & Reports (Milestone 2 Report, dated 2024-04-11, last modified 2025-07-08)

### Completed Milestones:

**Milestone 2 – Radiometer Modifications (Completed):**
- Designed new revision of L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) with:
  - Real-time in-flight calibration using interior references with adjustable, wide temperature range
  - Enhanced RFI mitigation and digital correlator for improved RFI detection
  - Optimized RF chain design with integrated 90° hybrid coupler
  - Lumped element 0°/180° hybrid at L-band: gain imbalance <0.15 dB, phase imbalance <0.5°
  - 2x2 microstrip patch antenna array (~40° beamwidth, ~0.8× flight height footprint, ~35 MHz bandwidth)

**UAS Modifications (In Progress/Ongoing):**
- Integration of LDCR with E2 small UAS platform
- Auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors integration
- Algorithm development for soil moisture to soil integrity correlation
