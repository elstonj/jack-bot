# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force, AFWERX)
- **Program/Solicitation:** SBIR Phase II – Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-07-31 (27 months, 26 days)  
  *Source: QuickBooks actuals date range*
- **Brief Description of Scope:**  
Black Swift Technologies is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. The project includes upgrading the radiometer with improved RFI mitigation and calibration capabilities, integrating auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors, and developing algorithms to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics for operational use at Air Force bases.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposals & Reports (Milestone 2 Report dated 2024-04-11, created 2024-04-02, last modified 2025-07-08)**

### Completed
- **Milestone 2 – Radiometer Modifications (Completed):**
  - Designed new revision of L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) with:
    - Real-time in-flight calibration with adjustable, wide temperature range
    - Enhanced RFI (radio frequency interference) mitigation
    - Digital correlator for improved RFI detection
    - Lumped element 0°/180° hybrid at L-band with <0.15 dB gain imbalance, <0.5° phase imbalance
    - 2x2 microstrip patch antenna array providing ~40° beamwidth and ~35 MHz bandwidth

### In Progress / Upcoming
- **UAS Modifications** – Integration of upgraded radiometer and auxiliary sensors (thermal, NDVI) into E2 platform
- **Algorithm Development** – Correlation of soil moisture measurements with cone index and soil integrity metrics
- **Operational Validation** – Field testing and deployment readiness for Air Force bases
