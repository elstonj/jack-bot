## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Soil Moisture Mapping (SMM) Phase II - Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DAF - Department of Air Force)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II, Project 400-5 (X22.4D)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-06-05 (26 months, 1 day)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Black Swift Technologies is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system (LDCR radiometer) and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force. The project includes radiometer upgrades with improved RFI mitigation and calibration, integration of thermal and NDVI sensors, algorithm development, and field validation at Air Force bases.
