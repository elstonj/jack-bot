# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force / AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program:** Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-10-05 (29 months, 1 day) — based on QuickBooks transaction range
- **Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Key deliverables include an upgraded L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) with improved RFI mitigation, integrated thermal and NDVI sensors, and soil integrity correlation algorithms.
