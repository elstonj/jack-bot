# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force, DAF)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping)
- **Program/Solicitation:** Project 400-5, X22.4 (referred to as "SMM Phase II 22.4D" in QuickBooks)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-05-06 (24 months, 31 days) *(Data range from QuickBooks)*
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development and validation of modifications to Black Swift Technologies' L-band soil moisture sensing system (LDCR radiometer) and E2 small UAS platform to enable runway integrity validation for Air Force bases through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Work includes radiometer design upgrades (RFI mitigation, calibration), integration of auxiliary sensors (thermal, NDVI), algorithm development, and field validation.
