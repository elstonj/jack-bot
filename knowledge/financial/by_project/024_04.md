# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force, AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II – Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4D)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-07 to 2026-09-28 (approximately 29 months) *(from government.md date range)*
- **Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for U.S. Air Force bases through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. The project includes upgrading the L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) with improved RFI mitigation, integrating thermal and NDVI sensors, and developing algorithms to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics.

### Completed Milestones:
- **Milestone 2: Radiometer Modifications** *(Completed as of report date 2024-04-11)*
  - Designed new L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) revision with:
    - Real-time in-flight calibration with interior references
    - Enhanced RFI (radio frequency interference) mitigation
    - Digital correlator for RFI detection
    - Lumped element 0°/180° hybrid at L-band (gain imbalance <0.15 dB, phase imbalance <0.5°)
    - 2×2 microstrip patch antenna array (~40° beamwidth, ~35 MHz bandwidth)
    - Three amplifier stages with optimized down converter/digital back end
  - **Financial Impact:** Included within SMM Phase II 22.4D allocations (QBO shows $200K in 2024-06, $200K in 2024-07, $200K in 2024-10, $150K in 2025-05)
