# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force, AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II – Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4D)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-07-09 (27 months, 5 days)
- **Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. The project involves upgrading the radiometer with improved RFI mitigation and calibration capabilities, integrating auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors, and developing algorithms to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics for operational Air Force base use.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

Source: **Proposals/Reports (Milestone 2 Report, dated 2024-04-11, last modified 2025-07-08)**

### Completed:
- **Milestone 2: Radiometer Modifications (Completed)**
  - Designed new revision of L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) with:
    - Real-time in-flight calibration using interior references
    - Enhanced RFI (radio frequency interference) mitigation
    - Digital correlator for improved RFI detection
    - Better immunity to gain fluctuation and thermal drift
  - RF chain design includes integrated 90° hybrid coupler
  - 2×2 microstrip patch antenna array (vs. previous MiCo antenna) with ~40° beamwidth and ~35 MHz bandwidth
  - Lumped element 0°/180° hybrid at L-band: gain imbalance <0.15 dB, phase imbalance <0.5°

### In Progress / Upcoming:
- **UAS Platform Modifications:** Integration of thermal and NDVI sensors onto E2 UAS platform
- **Algorithm Development:** Correlating soil moisture with soil integrity (cone index) metrics for Air Force operational use
- **Field Validation:** Testing at Air Force runway sites
