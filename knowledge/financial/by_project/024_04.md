# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force, AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program:** Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-10-05 (29 months, 1 day)
- **Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. The project involves upgrading the radiometer with improved RFI mitigation and calibration capabilities, integrating auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors, and developing algorithms to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics for operational use at Air Force bases.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones
**Source: Proposals & Reports (Milestone 2 Report, dated 2024-04-11, last modified 2025-07-08)**

**Milestone 2: Radiometer Modifications (Completed)**
- Designed new revision of L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) with:
  - Real-time in-flight calibration using interior references with adjustable, wide temperature range
  - Enhanced RFI (radio frequency interference) mitigation
  - Digital correlator for improved RFI detection
  - Better immunity to gain fluctuation and thermal drift
- RF chain design with integrated 90° hybrid coupler
- Three amplifier stages optimally driving down converter/digital back end
- Lumped element 0°/180° hybrid at L-band: gain imbalance <0.15 dB, phase imbalance <0.5°
- 2x2 microstrip patch antenna array (~40° beamwidth, ~0.8x flight height antenna footprint, ~35 MHz bandwidth)

**UAS Modifications:**
- Integration of radiometer with E2 small UAS platform
- Integration of auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors
- Algorithm development to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics
