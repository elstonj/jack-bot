# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II – Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-10-05 (29 months, 1 day)
- **Brief Scope:** Development and validation of modifications to BST's L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) and E2 small UAS platform to enable real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements for runway integrity validation at U.S. Air Force bases. Includes radiometer upgrades with RFI mitigation, integration of thermal and NDVI sensors, algorithm development, and field testing.
