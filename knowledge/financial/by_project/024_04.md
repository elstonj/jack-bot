# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force — DAF)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II — Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-07-31 (27 months, 26 days per QuickBooks data)
- **Brief Scope:** Development of modifications to Black Swift Technologies' L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Includes radiometer upgrades with improved RFI mitigation, integration of auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors, and algorithm development to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposals & Reports (Milestone 2 Report, last modified 2025-07-08)**

### Completed Milestones:

**Milestone 2 — Radiometer Modifications (Completed as of 2024-04-11 Report Date):**
- Designed new revision of L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) with:
  - Real-time in-flight calibration using interior references
  - Enhanced RFI (radio frequency interference) mitigation
  - Digital correlator for improved RFI detection
  - 2×2 microstrip patch antenna array (~40° beamwidth, ~35 MHz bandwidth)
- RF chain design with integrated 90° hybrid coupler
- Three amplifier stages with optimized thermal performance

### Integration & Testing Phase:
- Integration of L-band radiometer with E2 UAS platform
- Auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors
- Algorithm development for soil moisture–to–soil integrity correlation
- Field validation at Air Force operational sites

### Financial Implications:
- Radiometer redesign and fabrication costs (embedded in SMM Phase II 22.4D allocation: ~$849,725)
- UAS integration and testing labor and materials
