# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Soil Moisture Mapping (SMM) Phase II – Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force, AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II (Project 400-5, X22.4)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-06-05 (26 months, 1 day) *(based on QuickBooks date range)*
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development of modifications to Black Swift Technologies' L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS platform to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force. Scope includes radiometer upgrades with improved RFI mitigation and calibration, integration of auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors, algorithm development to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics, and field testing at Air Force bases.
