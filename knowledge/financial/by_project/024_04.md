# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SMM Phase II)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force, AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program Code:** 400-5, X22.4D
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-10-05 (29 months, 1 day)
- **Brief Description:** Development of modifications to Black Swift Technologies' L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Project includes radiometer upgrades with improved RFI mitigation, integration of thermal and NDVI sensors, and development of correlation algorithms for operational use at Air Force bases.
