# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DAF — Department of Air Force)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II — Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-06-05 (26 months, 1 day) *(from QuickBooks date range)*
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development of modifications to Black Swift Technologies' L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Scope includes upgrades to the radiometer with RFI mitigation and calibration, integration of auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors, and algorithm development for correlating soil moisture with soil integrity metrics.

- **Milestone 2: Radiometer Modifications (Completed by 2024-04-11 Report Date)**
  - Designed new revision of L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) with:
    - Real-time in-flight calibration using interior references with wide temperature range
    - Enhanced RFI (radio frequency interference) mitigation
    - Digital correlator for improved RFI detection
    - Immunity to gain fluctuation and thermal drift
  - RF chain design: 90° hybrid coupler, three amplifier stages, optimized down converter/digital back end

  - Technical specifications met: gain imbalance <0.15 dB, phase imbalance <0.5°
