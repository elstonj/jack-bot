# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (Soil Moisture Mapping Phase II)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force / AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II – Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-11-05 (31 months, 31 days) — *Note: Last transaction 2026-11-04/05*
- **Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force. Project includes upgrades to the L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) with improved RFI mitigation and calibration, integration of thermal and NDVI sensors, algorithm development for soil moisture-to-integrity correlation, and operational deployment at Air Force bases.
