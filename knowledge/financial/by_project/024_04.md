## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source:** Proposals & Reports (Milestone 2 Report, dated 2024-04-11; created 2024-04-02, last modified 2025-07-08)

### Completed Milestones

**Milestone 2: Radiometer Modifications & Design (COMPLETED)**
- Designed new revision of L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) with:
  - Real-time in-flight calibration using interior references with wide temperature range adjustability
  - Enhanced RFI (radio frequency interference) mitigation
  - Digital correlator for improved RFI detection
  - Better immunity to gain fluctuation and thermal drift
- RF chain design includes integrated 90° hybrid coupler
- Three-stage amplifier chain optimally driving down converter/digital back end
- Lumped element 0°/180° hybrid at L-band: gain imbalance <0.15 dB, phase imbalance <0.5°
- 2×2 microstrip patch antenna array (~40° beamwidth, ~0.8× flight height antenna footprint, ~35 MHz bandwidth)

**UAS Integration & Modifications (IN PROGRESS / COMPLETED AS OF REPORT DATE)**
- Integration of L-band radiometer, thermal sensor, and NDVI sensor into E2 UAS platform
- Development of soil moisture retrieval algorithms
- Field validation methodology for runway integrity correlation
