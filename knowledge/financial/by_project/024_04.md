# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force, AFWERX)
- **Program/Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program ID:** Project 400-5, X22.4 (also referenced as "SMM Phase II 22.4D" in accounting)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-10-05 (29 months, 1 day) *(from QuickBooks date range; full contract PoP may extend to 2026-11-16)*
- **Brief Description:** Development and validation of L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS platform to enable real-time runway integrity assessment for U.S. Air Force bases. Work includes radiometer modifications (RFI mitigation, in-flight calibration), auxiliary sensor integration (thermal, NDVI), and algorithm development to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics.
