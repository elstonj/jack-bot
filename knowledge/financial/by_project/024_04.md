# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Soil Moisture Mapping (SMM) Phase II – Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II – Project 400-5 (X22.4D)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-11-05 (31 months)
- **Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. The project includes upgrades to the radiometer with improved RFI mitigation and calibration, integration of thermal and NDVI sensors, and algorithm development to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics for operational use at Air Force bases.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**From Proposal/Milestone 2 Report (Asana data not provided):**

- **Milestone 2 (Completed as of 2024-04-02):** Radiometer Modifications
  - Designed new L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) with real-time in-flight calibration, enhanced RFI mitigation, and digital correlator
  - Developed RF chain design with 90° hybrid coupler
  - Lumped element 0°/180° hybrid at L-band with <0.15 dB gain imbalance and <0.5° phase imbalance
  - 2x2 microstrip patch antenna array (~40° beamwidth, ~35 MHz bandwidth)
  - **Financial Implication:** Completed milestone; associated spend visible in 2024-06 ($200K SMM Phase II 22.4D allocation)
