# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (Soil Moisture Mapping – SMM Phase II)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force – DAF)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II – Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4D)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-05-06 (24 months, 31 days)
- **Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. The project includes upgrading the radiometer with improved RFI mitigation and calibration, integrating thermal and NDVI sensors, and developing algorithms to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics for operational use at Air Force bases.

### Completed (Milestone 2 Report, dated 2024-04-11):

1. **Radiometer Modifications (L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer – LDCR)**
   - Designed new revision with real-time in-flight calibration, enhanced RFI mitigation, digital correlator
   - RF chain with integrated 90° hybrid coupler, three amplifier stages
   - Lumped element 0°/180° hybrid at L-band (gain imbalance <0.15 dB, phase imbalance <0.5°)

2. **UAS Modifications (E2 Platform)**
   - Integration of auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors
   - Algorithm development for soil moisture ↔ soil integrity correlation

3. **Operational Integration**
   - Real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements
