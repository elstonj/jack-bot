# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DAF — Department of Air Force)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II — Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-07-31 (27 months, 26 days)
- **Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is developing modifications to its L-band soil moisture sensing system and E2 small UAS to enable runway integrity validation for the U.S. Air Force through real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements. Work includes upgrading the radiometer with improved RFI mitigation and calibration, integrating thermal and NDVI sensors, and developing algorithms to correlate soil moisture with soil integrity metrics for operational use at Air Force bases.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source:** Proposals & Reports (Milestone 2 Report — Runway Integrity Validation)

### Completed Milestones
- **Milestone 2 — Radiometer Modifications (Completed as of report date 2024-04-11):**
  - New L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) revision designed with:
    - Real-time in-flight calibration using interior references
    - Enhanced RFI mitigation and digital correlator
    - Improved immunity to gain fluctuation and thermal drift
    - 2×2 microstrip patch antenna array (~40° beamwidth, ~35 MHz bandwidth)

- **UAS Modifications (In Progress):**
  - Integration of thermal and NDVI sensors onto E2 platform
  - Signal processing and software updates for multi-sensor fusion

### Upcoming Deliverables (from Milestone 2 Report, last modified 2025-07-08)
- Algorithm development for correlating soil moisture with soil integrity metrics (cone index)
- Operational integration and testing at Air Force bases
- Final demonstration and validation of runway integrity assessment capability
