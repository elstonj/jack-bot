# Project 024-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II — Soil Moisture Mapping (Project 400-5, X22.4)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024-04-05 to 2026-06-05 (26 months, 1 day)
- **Brief Scope:** Development and validation of modifications to Black Swift Technologies' L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) and E2 small UAS platform to enable real-time soil moisture and cone index measurements for runway integrity validation at U.S. Air Force bases. Work includes radiometer redesign with improved RFI mitigation and calibration, integration of auxiliary thermal and NDVI sensors, algorithm development, and field validation.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposals & Reports — Milestone 2 Report**

### Completed (as of Report Date 2024-04-11, Last Modified 2025-07-08)

**Milestone 2 — Radiometer Modifications (Completed)**
- New L-band Dual-Channel Correlator Radiometer (LDCR) design with:
  - Real-time in-flight calibration using interior references
  - Enhanced RFI (radio frequency interference) mitigation with digital correlator
  - Lumped element 0°/180° hybrid coupler at L-band
  - 2×2 microstrip patch antenna array (~40° beamwidth, ~35 MHz bandwidth)
  - Three-stage RF amplifier chain
- Specification achievement: gain imbalance <0.15 dB, phase imbalance <0.5°

**Milestone 2 — UAS Modifications (In Progress/Planned)**
- E2 platform integration with new radiometer payload
- Thermal and NDVI sensor integration
- Algorithm development for soil moisture to cone index correlation
- Operational field validation at Air Force bases
