# Project 301-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Phase II - 2025
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) / University of Miami
- **Contract Type:** NOAA Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (sUAS) contract with optional modifications
- **Contract Number:** 1305M225P0034 (Base) / Modification P25002 (Optional)
- **Period of Performance:** February 2025 – August 25, 2026 (6-month extension granted)
- **Brief Scope Description:** Design, development, and delivery of Black Swift S0 hurricane observation UAS platforms (33 aircraft units total: 26 base + 7 optional), along with operator/instructor training, mission operational support (30 days), and travel for testing and operational support. Scope includes integration of advanced avionics (VLD1 radar altimeter, RTK positioning, 10Hz meteorological data), sensor improvements, and web-based mission control systems.
