# Project 301-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Phase II - 2025
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) / University of Miami
- **Primary Contacts:** Joe Cione (NOAA), Jun Zhang (jun.zhang@noaa.gov)
- **Contract Number:** 1305M225P0034
- **Contract Type:** NOAA Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (sUAS) contract with optional modifications
- **Period of Performance:** February 2025 – August 25, 2026 (6-month extension granted)
- **Brief Description:** Development, construction, and delivery of Black Swift S0 uncrewed aircraft systems for hurricane observation and meteorological data collection, including 33 total aircraft platforms (26 base + 7 optional), operator/instructor training courses, mission support operations, and critical technical enhancements (10Hz Met Data, Increased Endurance, Dual GPS, GUI improvements, Airops updates).
