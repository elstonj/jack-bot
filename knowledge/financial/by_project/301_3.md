## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Phase II - 2025
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) / University of Miami
- **Primary Contacts:** Joe Cione (NOAA), Jun Zhang (jun.zhang@noaa.gov)
- **Contract Type:** NOAA Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (sUAS) contract with optional modification
- **Contract Number:** 1305M225P0034 (Base) / Modification P25002 (Optional)
- **Period of Performance:** February 2025 – August 25, 2026 (6-month extension granted)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Delivery of 33 Black Swift S0 UAS platforms (26 base + 7 optional) to NOAA for hurricane observation operations, plus operator/instructor training course development and delivery, mission day operational support, and travel for testing/operational support. Includes critical technical enhancements (10Hz Met Data, Increased Endurance, Dual GPS, GUI improvements, Airops updates).
