# Project 301-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Phase II – 2025
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration); University of Miami (subcontractor)
- **Primary Contacts:** Joe Cione (NOAA), Jun Zhang (jun.zhang@noaa.gov)
- **Contract Number:** 1305M225P0034 (with modification P25002 for optional items)
- **Contract Type:** NOAA Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (sUAS) contract with base and optional line items
- **Period of Performance:** February 2025 – August 25, 2026 (6-month extension granted)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development, construction, and delivery of Black Swift S0 hurricane observation UAS platforms, operator and instructor training course development and delivery, mission operational support, and travel. Contract includes 26 base aircraft platforms, 7 optional additional platforms, and four training CLINs (course development, operator training, instructor training).
