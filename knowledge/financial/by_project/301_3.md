# Project 301-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Phase II - 2025
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) / University of Miami
- **Primary Contacts:** Joe Cione (NOAA), Jun Zhang (jun.zhang@noaa.gov)
- **Contract Number:** 1305M225P0034 (with Modification P25002 for optional items)
- **Contract Type:** NOAA Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (sUAS) contract with base and optional CLINs
- **Period of Performance:** February 2025 – August 25, 2026 (6-month extension granted)
- **BST Project Owner:** Josh Fromm

**Scope Summary:**  
Development, construction, and delivery of Black Swift S0 UAS platforms for hurricane observation and research in partnership with NOAA and University of Miami. Deliverables include 33 total S0 aircraft systems (26 base + 7 optional), operator/observer training courses and sessions, instructor training, mission operational support (30 days), and technical enhancements (web-based controller, 10Hz meteorological data, increased endurance, dual GPS, GUI improvements, airops updates).
