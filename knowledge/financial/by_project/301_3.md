# Project 301-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Phase II - 2025
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) / University of Miami
- **Primary Contacts:** Joe Cione (NOAA), Jun Zhang (jun.zhang@noaa.gov)
- **Contract Number:** 1305M225P0034 (Base + Optional Modification P25002)
- **Contract Type:** NOAA Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (sUAS) contract with optional items
- **Period of Performance:** February 2025 – August 25, 2026 (6-month extension granted)
- **Brief Description:** Development, construction, and delivery of Black Swift S0 hurricane observation platforms with associated training, operational support, and mission days. Project includes 26 aircraft units in base contract and 7 additional units under optional modification, plus operator/instructor training courses and 30 days of operational support.
