- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) / University of Miami
- **Primary Contacts:** Joe Cione (NOAA), Jun Zhang (jun.zhang@noaa.gov)

- **Contract Type:** NOAA Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (sUAS) development and operational support contract
- **Period of Performance:** February 2025 – August 25, 2026 (6-month extension granted)
- **Brief Description of Scope:**
  - Development, construction, and delivery of Black Swift S0 hurricane observation UAS platforms
  - Hardware/software freeze completed November 2025; 34 systems delivered to AOC in December 2025

  - Multiple critical technical deliverables (10Hz Met Data, Increased Endurance, Dual GPS, GUI improvements, Airops updates) required by August 25, 2026 final deadline
