# Project 301-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Phase II - 2025
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) / University of Miami
- **Primary Contacts:** Joe Cione (NOAA), Jun Zhang (jun.zhang@noaa.gov)
- **Contract Number:** 1305M225P0034 (with Modification P25002 for optional items)
- **Contract Type:** NOAA Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (sUAS) contract with base and optional CLINs
- **Period of Performance:** February 2025 – August 25, 2026 (6-month extension granted from original contract)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Design, manufacture, and delivery of Black Swift S0 UAS platforms for hurricane observation research, including operator/instructor training development and operational support. Contract includes 26 base units (CLIN 0001) plus 7 additional optional units (CLIN 1007), training course development and delivery (CLINs 1002–1006, 1008), 30 days of mission operational support (CLIN 1009), and travel for testing/operations support (CLINs 1006, 1010).
