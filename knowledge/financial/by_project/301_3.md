# Project 301-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Phase II - 2025
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) / University of Miami
- **Primary Contacts:** Joe Cione (NOAA), Jun Zhang (jun.zhang@noaa.gov)
- **Contract Number:** 1305M225P0034 (with modification P25002 for optional items)
- **Contract Type:** NOAA Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (sUAS) contract with optional line items
- **Period of Performance:** February 2025 – August 25, 2026 (6-month extension granted)
- **Brief Description:** Delivery of 33 Black Swift S0 aircraft platforms (26 base + 7 optional units) to NOAA for hurricane observation operations, including operator/instructor training, mission support, and technical enhancements (meteorological data improvements, endurance optimization, dual GPS integration, GUI updates, airops modifications).
