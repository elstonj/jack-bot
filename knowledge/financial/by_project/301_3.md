## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Phase II - 2025
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) / University of Miami
- **Primary Contacts:** Joe Cione (NOAA), Jun Zhang (jun.zhang@noaa.gov)
- **Contract Number:** 1305M225P0034 (with Modification P25002 for optional items)
- **Contract Type:** NOAA Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (sUAS) contract with optional items
- **Period of Performance:** February 2025 – August 25, 2026 (extended 6 months from original contract)
- **Brief Description:** Design, manufacture, and deliver Black Swift S0 aircraft platforms for hurricane observation operations, including operator/instructor training courses and operational support missions.
