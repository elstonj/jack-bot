# Project 301-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Phase II - 2025
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) / University of Miami
- **Primary Contacts:** Joe Cione (NOAA), Jun Zhang (jun.zhang@noaa.gov)
- **Contract Type:** NOAA Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (sUAS) contract with optional modifications
- **Contract Number:** 1305M225P0034 (with Modification P25002 for optional items)
- **Period of Performance:** February 2025 – August 25, 2026 (6-month extension granted)
- **Project Code:** 301-3
- **Brief Description:** Design, manufacture, and delivery of 33 Black Swift S0 UAS platforms (26 base + 7 optional) for hurricane observation missions with NOAA, including operator/instructor training, operational support, and mission days. Scope includes critical technical enhancements (10Hz meteorological data, increased endurance, dual GPS, GUI improvements, airops updates) and web-based controller development.
