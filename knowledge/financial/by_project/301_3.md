# Project 301-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Phase II - 2025
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) / University of Miami
- **Primary Contacts:** Joe Cione (NOAA), Jun Zhang (jun.zhang@noaa.gov)
- **Contract:** 1305M225P0034 (Base + Optional Modification P25002)
- **Contract Type:** NOAA Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (sUAS) contract with optional items
- **Period of Performance:** February 2025 to August 25, 2026 (6-month extension granted)
- **Brief Description:** Design, manufacture, and delivery of Black Swift S0 UAS platforms for hurricane observation operations, including operator/instructor training and operational support. Base contract covers 26 S0 platforms and training CLINs; optional modification adds 7 additional platforms, operator training, mission days operational support (30 days @ $2,850/day), and travel support (6 trips × 5 days).
