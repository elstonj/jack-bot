# Project 301-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Phase II - 2025
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) / University of Miami
- **Contract Number:** 1305M225P0034 (with Modification P25002 for optional items)
- **Contract Type:** NOAA Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (sUAS) contract with base and optional CLINs
- **Period of Performance:** February 2025 – August 25, 2026 (6-month extension granted)
- **Primary Contacts:** Joe Cione (NOAA), Jun Zhang (jun.zhang@noaa.gov)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Black Swift Technologies is delivering S0 hurricane observation UAS platforms, operator/instructor training courses, and operational support to NOAA for hurricane research operations. The contract includes delivery of 26 base S0 platforms plus 7 optional additional units (13 total additional in modified scope), web-based flight controller, operator and instructor training CLINs, 30 days of mission operational support, and travel for testing and operations. Work includes hardware assembly, avionics integration, software development (RTK positioning, radar altimetry, flight control), sensor testing, and technical training delivery through August 2026.
