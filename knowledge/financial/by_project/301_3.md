## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Phase II - 2025
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) / University of Miami
- **Primary Contacts:** Joe Cione (NOAA), Jun Zhang (jun.zhang@noaa.gov)
- **Contract Number:** 1305M225P0034
- **Contract Type:** NOAA Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (sUAS) procurement with optional items and training
- **Period of Performance:** February 2025 – August 25, 2026 (6-month extension granted)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Design, manufacture, and delivery of Black Swift S0 UAS platforms for hurricane observation operations, including operator/instructor training courses, mission support, and technical enhancements (10Hz meteorological data, increased endurance, dual GPS, GUI improvements, airops updates).
