# Project 301-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Phase II - 2025
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) / University of Miami
- **Primary Contacts:** Joe Cione (NOAA), Jun Zhang (jun.zhang@noaa.gov)
- **Contract Number:** 1305M225P0034 (with Modification P25002 for optional items)
- **Contract Type:** NOAA Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (sUAS) supply and training contract
- **Period of Performance:** February 2025 – August 25, 2026 (6-month extension granted)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** 
  - Supply and delivery of Black Swift S0 UAS platforms (26 units base + 7 optional = 33 total units)
  - Development and delivery of operator/observer and instructor training courses
  - 30 days of mission-day operational support
  - Travel support for training and operational testing
  - Technical milestones: 10Hz Met Data, Increased Endurance, Dual GPS, GUI/usability improvements, Airops updates
  - Optional enhancements: Skyfora Integration, MultiUAS
