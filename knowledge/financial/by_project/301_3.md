# Project 301-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Phase II - 2025
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) / University of Miami
- **Primary Contacts:** Joe Cione (NOAA), Jun Zhang (jun.zhang@noaa.gov)
- **Contract Number:** 1305M225P0034 (with Modification P25002 for optional items)
- **Contract Type:** NOAA Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (sUAS) contract with base and optional line items
- **Period of Performance:** February 2025 – August 25, 2026 (6-month extension granted)
- **BST Project Owner:** Josh Fromm

**Scope Description:**
Development, construction, and delivery of Black Swift S0 UAS platforms for hurricane observation, including operator and instructor training, mission support, and operational field deployment with NOAA. Contract includes 26 base units plus 7 optional additional units, with web-based controller development, training CLINs, and operational mission support.
