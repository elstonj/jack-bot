   - QuickBooks unclassified.md summary shows $935,473.80 for [301-3] as of June 2026 (latest data)

     - 34 systems delivered to AOC (December 2025, post-Feb report)
     - Additional 13 S0 systems manufacturing ramp-up (due July 1, 2026)
     - Operational mission support delivery (Jan-Jun 2026)
     - University of Miami subcontract drawdown (estimated $90.3K additional invoicing pending)

2. **Budget Category Breakdown (Feb 2026 Detail):**
   - Labor ($241.2K) — appears appropriately allocated based on Toggl reports (416 hrs in Apr-May alone, extensive avionics/assembly work)
   - Equipment ($252K) — consistent with S0 platform manufacturing and avionics integration
   - Travel ($15.2K) — lower than CLIN 1010 allocation ($36K); suggests additional travel costs may be deferred or invoiced separately
   - Overhead ($158.1K) — applied on top of direct costs; represents ~23% of direct spend

3. **Variance Analysis (June 2026 Latest):**
   - **Total project budget:** $969,833 (NOAA $747.5K + UMiami $222.3K)
   - **Actual spend (QBO):** $935,473.80
   - **Net variance:** $34,359.20 UNDER budget (3.5% remaining)
   - **Status:** Project is operating near budget ceiling; minimal margin for overruns in final 2.5 months (through August 25, 2026)
