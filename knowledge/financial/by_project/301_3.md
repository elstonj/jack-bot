# Project 301-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Phase II - 2025
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) / University of Miami
- **Primary Contacts:** Joe Cione (NOAA), Jun Zhang (jun.zhang@noaa.gov)
- **Contract Number:** 1305M225P0034 (Base) / 1305M225P0034 Mod P25002 (Optional)
- **Contract Type:** NOAA Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (sUAS) contract with optional items
- **Period of Performance:** February 2025 – August 25, 2026 (6-month extension granted)
- **Brief Description:** Design, manufacture, and deliver Black Swift S0 uncrewed aircraft systems (UAS) for hurricane observation operations. Includes 26 base systems, 7 optional additional systems, operator training, instructor training, mission operational support, and travel. Additional funding via University of Miami subcontract for S0 construction labor and materials.

### Budget Category Breakdown Notes:

1. **Direct Labor:** QBO shows $241,197.49 vs. estimated $197,600 proposal (labor component of CLINs 0001, 1002–1006, 1007–1010). **+$43,597 overspend.** Toggl reports (Feb–May 2025) document extensive hands-on construction, testing, and integration requiring skilled technicians (Sam Hild, Jack Elston, Ethan Domagala, etc.). Extended Phase II and optional systems likely drove higher labor demand.

2. **Equipment:** QBO $252,029.21 vs. estimated $484,500 (primarily CLIN 0001 aircraft platforms $468,000 + CLIN 1007 additional platforms $126,000 = $594,000 budgeted). **–$232,470 underspend.** Possible reasons:
   - Equipment costs may be capitalized separately (not expensed in P&L).
   - Invoicing lag: CLIN 0001 invoices ($468k) paid; CLIN 1007 invoices ($175,650 invoiced, $54k pending approval) may not yet fully expense.
   - Materials and subcontractor supplies (U of Miami $132k) recorded as separate line items.

3. **Travel:** QBO $15,207.70 vs. budgeted $37,400 (CLIN 1006 $1,400 + CLIN 1010 $36,000). **–$22,192 underspent.** Training and mission support trips may not yet have been incurred or accrued; contract extends to August 25, 2026 (future travel anticipated).

4. **Other Direct Costs:** $17,419.03 (matches proposal). Materials, supplies, shipping, miscellaneous.

5. **Overhead/Indirect:** $158,143.46 (matches proposal). Applied as overhead to labor and direct costs.

6. **Subcontractor:** U of Miami $222,333 approved. $132,000 invoiced and paid (Invoice 1721, 11/06/2025); $90,333 extended PO pending invoice.
