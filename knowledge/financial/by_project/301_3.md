# Project 301-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Phase II - 2025
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) / University of Miami
- **Primary Contacts:** Joe Cione (NOAA), Jun Zhang (jun.zhang@noaa.gov)
- **Contract Type:** NOAA Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (sUAS) contract with optional items
- **Contract Number:** 1305M225P0034 (Base) / Modification P25002 (Optional)
- **Period of Performance:** February 2025 to August 25, 2026 (6-month extension granted)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Design, construction, and delivery of Black Swift S0 hurricane observation platforms to NOAA for use in hurricane research operations led by Joe Cione. Includes 26 base aircraft units, 7 optional additional units, operator/instructor training development and delivery, mission support operations, and critical technical enhancements (10Hz meteorological data, increased endurance, dual GPS, GUI improvements, airops updates).
