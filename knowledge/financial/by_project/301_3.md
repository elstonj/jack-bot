### Budget Category Analysis

**Important:** QuickBooks does not provide granular cost category breakdown in the data provided. The table above shows:
- **Proposed Budget:** From Drive Budget Docs (consolidated NOAA + University of Miami budgets)
- **Actual Spend:** From QuickBooks project total ($935,473.80), without category-level detail
- **Analysis:** Project has spent 96.5% of approved budget with 3.5 months remaining in contract (through August 25, 2026)

**Key Observations:**
1. **Labor:** Drive Budget Docs show 3,501.84 hours logged through February 2026 at average ~$69/hr, totaling $241,197.49. Toggl reports (Apr–May 2025) show active field work (hardware assembly, avionics testing, RTK development) at ~416 hours/month during peak activity. Actual labor costs likely higher than proposed given extended contract period.

2. **Equipment:** $252,029.21 proposed includes S0 platform costs (major expense), plus avionics, test equipment. NOAA contract CLINs 0001 & 1007 total $594,000 for 33 S0 platforms (26 base + 7 optional). Actual platform manufacturing/procurement costs not separately itemized in QBO data.

3. **Subcontractors:** University of Miami extended from $132,000 to $222,333; $90,333 of addendum not yet invoiced. This is critical expense remaining on budget.

4. **Travel:** $15,207.70 proposed for training delivery and field ops. Additional optional contract includes $36,000 for "Travel for Operational & Testing Support" (CLIN 1010, NOAA optional). Drive Budget Docs show actual travel spend ~$15,207.70 through Feb 2026; additional travel costs post-February likely substantial given mission operations and training delivery schedule.

5. **Overhead/Indirect:** $158,143.46 proposed. Typical for government contracts; allocated monthly based on labor rates.
