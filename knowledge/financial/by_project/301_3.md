# Project 301-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Phase II - 2025
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) / University of Miami
- **Contract Number:** 1305M225P0034 (with Modification P25002 for optional items)
- **Contract Type:** NOAA Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (sUAS) contract with optional items and subcontract
- **Primary Contacts:** Joe Cione (NOAA), Jun Zhang (jun.zhang@noaa.gov)
- **Period of Performance:** February 2025 – August 25, 2026 (6-month extension granted)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** 
  - Design, manufacture, and delivery of Black Swift S0 UAS platforms (26 base + 7 optional = 33 total units planned)
  - Development and delivery of operator/observer training courses and instructor training
  - Operational support and mission days (30 days @ $2,850/day)
  - Travel for training and operational support
  - Critical technical enhancements: 10Hz meteorological data, increased endurance, dual GPS, GUI/usability improvements, and airops updates
