# Project 301-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Phase II - 2025
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) / University of Miami
- **Primary Contacts:** Joe Cione (NOAA), Jun Zhang (jun.zhang@noaa.gov)
- **Contract Number:** 1305M225P0034 (with Modification P25002 for optional items)
- **Contract Type:** Government (NOAA Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems sUAS contract) with base and optional CLINs
- **Period of Performance:** February 2025 – August 25, 2026 (6-month extension granted)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Delivery of 33 Black Swift S0 aircraft platforms (26 base + 7 optional) to NOAA for hurricane observation operations, operator and observer training course development and delivery, instructor training, and 30 days of operational support with mission-critical payload integration (radar altimeter, RTK positioning, meteorological sensors). Includes university subcontract for S0 construction labor and materials.
