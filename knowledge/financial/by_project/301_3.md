# Project 301-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Phase II - 2025

**Client/Agency:** National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) / University of Miami

**Contract Number:** 1305M225P0034 (with modifications; note: some invoices incorrectly reference 1350M225P0034)

**Contract Type:** Government—NOAA Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (sUAS) contract with optional modifications

**Primary Contacts:** Joe Cione (NOAA), Jun Zhang (jun.zhang@noaa.gov)

**Period of Performance:** February 2025 – August 25, 2026 (6-month extension granted; originally scheduled for earlier completion)

**Brief Scope:** Development, construction, and delivery of Black Swift S0 uncrewed aircraft systems platforms for NOAA hurricane observation operations, including operator and instructor training, operational support, and system enhancements (10Hz meteorological data, increased endurance, dual GPS, GUI improvements, airops updates).
