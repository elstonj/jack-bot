# Project 301-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Phase II - 2025
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration)
- **Contract Type:** NOAA Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (sUAS) contract with optional items
- **Primary Contract:** 1305M225P0034 (Base + Optional Modification P25002)
- **Subcontract:** University of Miami (PO-0000867443)
- **Period of Performance:** February 2025 – August 25, 2026 (18 months; includes 6-month extension)
- **Brief Description:** Development and delivery of 33 Black Swift S0 uncrewed aircraft systems for hurricane observation operations, including operator/observer training, instructor course development, and mission day operational support (30 days @ $2,850/day). S0 platforms feature hurricane controller algorithms, Moving Base RTK GPS, radar altimeter integration (VLD1), and deployment systems for atmospheric data collection.
