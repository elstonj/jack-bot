### Key Observations:

1. **QuickBooks Total ($935,473.80) exceeds Approved Budget ($879,500 + Univ. of Miami extension $90,333 = $969,833) by ~-$34,359**, i.e., *marginally within* the extended authorization if Univ. of Miami addendum is counted.

2. **Invoiced amounts to date ($786,200) are well below QBO actuals ($935,473.80)**, indicating:
   - Significant labor and materials expensed but not yet invoiced
   - Potential for additional invoicing in remaining 6 months of contract
   - Work may be in progress awaiting deliverable completion before invoicing

3. **Equipment category appears under-spent relative to approved budget ($658,500 approved vs. $252,029 QBO spend)**, but:
   - NOAA invoices show $468,000 (26 base units) fully invoiced and paid
   - 7 optional units ($175,650 invoiced, exceeding $126,000 CLIN 1007 by $49,650)
   - Univ. of Miami materials ($64,500) invoiced and paid
   - **Discrepancy:** QBO equipment spend ($252,029) is much lower than invoiced amounts ($658,500+), suggesting labor and overhead are captured in other cost categories in QBO

4. **Direct Labor (25.8% of QBO spend) exceeds Univ. of Miami budget ($67,500) by $173,697**, indicating:
   - Significant BST internal labor charged to project (not captured in budget docs)
   - Monthly labor tracking shows peak spending June 2025 ($49,462), declining in recent months

5. **Overhead ($158,143.46 = 16.9% burden) not separately budgeted**, included in QBO actuals
