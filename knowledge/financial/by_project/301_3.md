# Project 301-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Phase II - 2025

**Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) / University of Miami

**Primary Contacts:** Joe Cione (NOAA), Jun Zhang (jun.zhang@noaa.gov)

**Contract Number:** 1305M225P0034

**Contract Type:** NOAA Small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (sUAS) contract with base and optional components

**Period of Performance:** February 2025 – August 25, 2026 (includes 6-month extension)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Black Swift Technologies is delivering 33 S0 UAS platforms (26 base + 7 optional units) to NOAA for hurricane observation missions, along with comprehensive operator and instructor training programs, mission operations support (30 days), and travel. The project involves hardware construction, avionics integration, sensor testing, and software development to support hurricane research operations led by NOAA scientist Joe Cione.
