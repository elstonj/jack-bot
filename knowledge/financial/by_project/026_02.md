# Project 026-02 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 026-02

**Full Project Name:** Expendable Air-Sea Profiling Observations in Hazardous Weather Conditions via Small Uncrewed Aircraft System (S0 UAS)

**Client/Agency:** Office of Naval Research (ONR)

**Contract Type:** STTR Phase II (Small Business Technology Transfer)

**Program/Solicitation:** Navy STTR N2-9533; Topic N25A-T025

**Period of Performance:** Data not available from proposals/reports

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Transition the S0 expendable UAS from NOAA-operational research into a Navy-adoptable atmospheric profiling capability for hazardous weather operations. Phase II focuses on sensor calibration/validation, real-time turbulence and wave-state algorithm development, autonomous adaptive sampling, and demonstration of forecast improvements in Navy operational models. The S0 provides significant improvements over traditional dropsondes at ~10x lower cost per effective vertical profile.
