## Project Overview
**Full Project Name:** Expendable Air-sea Profiling Observations in Hazardous Weather Conditions via Small Uncrewed Aircraft System

**Client/Agency:** Office of Naval Research (ONR)

**Contract Type:** STTR Phase II (Small Business Technology Transfer)

**Program/Solicitation:** Navy STTR N2-9533; Topic N25A-T025

**Proposal Submission Date:** 2026-02-25 (Source: Proposals & Reports)

**Period of Performance:** Data not available from provided sources

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Black Swift Technologies proposes to transition the S0 expendable UAS from a NOAA-operational research system into a Navy-adoptable atmospheric profiling capability for hazardous weather. Phase II focuses on sensor calibration/validation, real-time turbulence and wave-state algorithms, autonomous adaptive sampling, and forecast improvements in Navy operational models. The S0 provides orders-of-magnitude improvements over traditional dropsondes in spatiotemporal data density at ~10x lower cost per effective vertical profile. System is air-deployable from Navy platforms (P-8 preferred) or NOAA/USAF aircraft with 2+ hours endurance and ~$15,000 per-unit cost.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposals & Reports**

The proposal document outlines the following Phase II technical objectives (specific milestone dates not provided):

1. **Sensor Calibration & Validation**
   - Complete pressure, temperature, humidity, and salinity sensor calibration in controlled environments
   - Validate sensor performance in representative marine boundary layer conditions

2. **Real-Time Algorithm Development**
   - Develop turbulence intensity and vertical velocity algorithms for real-time onboard processing
   - Develop wave-state and sea-surface temperature retrieval algorithms

3. **Autonomous Adaptive Sampling**
   - Enable autonomous altitude adjustment based on real-time thermal structure detection
   - Implement adaptive sampling to optimize data collection in dynamic weather scenarios

4. **Forecast Model Integration & Validation**
   - Integrate S0 profiles into Navy operational atmospheric and ocean models (COAMPS, etc.)
   - Demonstrate forecast skill improvement through case studies in realistic typhoon/hurricane scenarios

5. **Extended Environment Qualification**
   - Extend S0 operating envelope to Arctic conditions (-65°F, icing environments)
   - Validate performance in extreme weather beyond current TRL 9 Category 5 hurricane qualification

6. **Transition Documentation**
   - Navy operational procedures and pilot training materials
   - Cost-benefit analysis vs. traditional dropsonde systems
