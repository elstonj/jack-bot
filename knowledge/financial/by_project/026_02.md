## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Expendable Air-sea Profiling Observations in Hazardous Weather Conditions via Small Uncrewed Aircraft System
- **Client/Agency:** Office of Naval Research (ONR)
- **Contract Type:** STTR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** Navy STTR N2-9533; Topic N25A-T025
- **Proposal Submission Date:** 2026-02-25
- **Period of Performance:** Data not available from Proposals/Drive Budget Docs
- **Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies proposes to transition the S0 expendable UAS from NOAA-operational research into a Navy-adoptable atmospheric profiling capability for hazardous weather. Phase II focuses on sensor calibration/validation, real-time turbulence and wave-state algorithms, autonomous adaptive sampling, and demonstrating forecast improvements. The S0 provides orders-of-magnitude improvements over traditional dropsondes at ~10x lower cost per effective vertical profile.
