## Technical Scope Summary
*(From ONR STTR Phase II Proposal)*

**System:** S0 expendable UAS
- **Endurance:** 2+ hours (standard S0); extended pathway to 12+ hours (S0-LR)
- **Deployment:** Air-launched from P-8 or NOAA/USAF aircraft (P-3, C-130J)
- **Cost per Unit:** ~$15,000 (~10× lower cost per effective profile vs. dropsondes)
- **Qualification:** Currently TRL 9 in Category 5 hurricanes; Phase II extends to Arctic (-65°F) and icing environments
