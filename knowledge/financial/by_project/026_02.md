### Brief Description of Scope
Black Swift Technologies proposes to transition the S0 expendable UAS (uncrewed aircraft system) into a Navy-adoptable atmospheric profiling capability for hazardous weather operations. Phase II deliverables include:
- Sensor calibration and validation
- Real-time turbulence and wave-state algorithms
- Autonomous adaptive sampling capability
- Forecast improvement demonstration in Navy operational models
- Extension to Arctic (-65°F) and icing environments (beyond current TRL 9 in Category 5 hurricanes)

Key system: **S0 UAS** — air-deployable, expendable platform with 2+ hours endurance, deployable from P-8/P-3/C-130J aircraft, ~$15,000 per unit cost, 10x cost reduction vs. traditional dropsondes.
