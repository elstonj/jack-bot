## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Status
- **Project Status:** Early stage funding received; limited burn rate to assess.
- **% of Budget Spent:** 24.9% of the $333,503 approved budget has been invoiced/paid as of 2026-02-26.
- **Burn Rate:** Approximately $83,212.50 invoiced across ~2 months (Jan–Feb 2026), suggesting a monthly burn of ~$41,606. At this rate, the project would exhaust the full approved budget in approximately 8 months, well before the contracted 24-month base period (01/01/2027–12/31/2028).

### Key Concerns
1. **Timeline Misalignment:**
   - Invoices are dated Jan–Feb 2026, but the approved Period of Performance does not begin until **01/01/2027**.
   - This suggests either:
     - Pre-award spending (proposal development, preliminary experiments, facilities booking) is underway.
     - Project code or invoice assignment may be incorrect.
   - **Action:** Verify that pre-award costs are being charged to a pre-project account and will be reclassed to 026-02 upon official award.

2. **Unpaid Invoices:**
   - Two invoices totaling $80,000.00 (1733, 1734) to Black Swift Technologies are flagged as unpaid as of 2026-02-26.
   - Need clarification on payment status and expected collection date.

3. **Lack of Granular Cost Tracking:**
   - QuickBooks invoices do not show GL codes or cost categories (labor, materials, travel, indirect).
   - Cannot verify that spending aligns with proposed budget structure (Direct Labor $186K, F&A $103K, etc.).
   - Recommendation: Implement detailed project accounting codes and tie invoices to labor timesheets and expense reports.

4. **Subcontractor Management:**
   - ERAU (Embry-Riddle) invoices total only $2,462.50 to date, suggesting partner spending is minimal or not yet invoiced.
   - Budget does not clearly separate BST prime costs from ERAU subcontractor costs; verify cost allocation.
