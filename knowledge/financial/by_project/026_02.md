# Project 026-02 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Expendable Air-sea Profiling Observations in Hazardous Weather Conditions via Small Uncrewed Aircraft System
- **Client/Agency:** Office of Naval Research (ONR)
- **Contract Type:** STTR Phase II
- **Program/Solicitation:** Navy STTR N2-9533; Topic N25A-T025
- **Proposal Submission Date:** 2026-02-25
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Transition the S0 expendable UAS from NOAA-operational research system into a Navy-adoptable atmospheric profiling capability for hazardous weather. Phase II focuses on sensor calibration/validation, real-time turbulence and wave-state algorithms, autonomous adaptive sampling, and demonstration of forecast improvements in Navy operational models.

The proposal identifies the following technical objectives for Phase II:

1. **Sensor Calibration & Validation** – Complete laboratory and field validation of pressure, temperature, humidity, and GPS-derived wind measurements against reference instruments
2. **Real-Time Algorithm Development** – Develop autonomous algorithms for detecting turbulence intensity and wave-state conditions
3. **Autonomous Adaptive Sampling** – Enable the S0 to adjust flight profile and sampling strategy in response to detected atmospheric conditions
4. **Forecast Model Integration & Validation** – Demonstrate improved forecast skill in Navy operational atmospheric models through assimilation of S0 profile data
5. **Arctic & Icing Environment Qualification** – Extend S0 operational envelope from Category 5 hurricanes to Arctic conditions (-65°F) and icing environments
6. **Transition Planning** – Develop logistics, supply chain, training, and operational integration pathways for Navy adoption
