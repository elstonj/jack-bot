## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Performance
- **Status**: Project fully executed and closed on schedule.
- **Total Variance**: **$17.37 underspent** (99.99% of approved budget utilized).
- **Assessment**: Excellent budget control. Project executed almost exactly to approved budget across both year-long periods.

### By Cost Category

**Direct Labor** (102.5% of approved; overspent by $2,172.00)
- **Approved**: $88,116.00 (Project Management + Flight Crew Operations + Data Processing)
- **Actual**: $90,288.00
- **Analysis**: Minor labor overage (~2.5%), likely due to:
  - Additional data processing hours beyond initial estimates
  - Additional project management overhead during Year Two
  - Possible scope creep in deployment operations
- **Status**: *Slightly concerning but manageable;* overages offset by travel savings.

**Materials/Equipment Rental** (111.4% of approved; overspent by $4,000.00)

  - Possible equipment maintenance or upgrade costs bundled into rental category
- **Status**: *Moderate concern;* represents 11.4% overage in this category, but was absorbed within overall budget.
