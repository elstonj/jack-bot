## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Performance
✅ **Project on Budget**: The project closed with **$17.37 remaining** against a $148,729 contract value. Total variance is **–$0.01%**, indicating exceptional budget alignment. This is a **CLOSED project** (final invoice 11/28/2023) with all funds expended.

### Category-Level Analysis

**Overruns**:
- **Materials/Equipment Rental**: **+$4,000 over budget (111.4% spent)**
  - Year One: +$1,000 over ($20k budgeted vs. $21k actual)  
  - Year Two: +$3,000 over ($15k budgeted vs. $18k actual)  
  - **Concern**: Aircraft rental costs increased across both years, possibly due to operational changes, additional flight hours, or rate increases. No offsetting explanation in budget docs.

- **Direct Labor**: **+$2,172 over budget (102.5% spent)**
  - Year One: +$1,508 over ($50,352 budgeted vs. $51,860 actual)  
  - Year Two: +$664 over ($37,764 budgeted vs. $38,428 actual)  
  - **Concern**: Labor scaling consistent with rental overruns. May indicate additional work scope (e.g., extra flight days, processing hours) not originally budgeted.

**Underspends**:
- **Travel**: **–$6,189 under budget (75.8% spent)**
  - Year One: –$3,167 under ($14,636 budgeted vs. $11,469 actual)  
  - Year Two: –$3,022 under ($10,977 budgeted vs. $7,955 actual)  
  - **Note**: Travel savings absorbed Equipment and Labor overruns, resulting in net project balance.
