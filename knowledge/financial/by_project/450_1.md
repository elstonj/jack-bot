### Performance by Cost Category

#### ✅ **Travel: WELL CONTROLLED (75.8% of budget used)**
- **Budgeted**: $25,613.00
- **Actual**: $19,423.63
- **Variance**: $6,189.37 **under budget** (24.2% savings)
- **Assessment**: Travel costs came in significantly below budget. This was likely due to:
  - Lower-than-expected lodging and meal expenses
  - Potentially shorter or more efficient field deployment logistics
  - No significant travel cost overruns or changes to deployment locations
- **Status**: ✅ Well-managed; no concerns.

#### ⚠️ **Materials/Equipment Rental: OVERSPENT (111.4% of budget used)**
- **Budgeted**: $35,000.00
- **Actual**: $39,000.00
- **Variance**: –$4,000.00 **over budget** (11.4% overage)
- **Details**:
  - Year One: $20,000 budgeted / $21,000 actual (–$1,000 over; 1 additional aircraft flight day)
  - Year Two: $15,000 budgeted / $18,000 actual (–$3,000 over; 3 additional aircraft flight days)
- **Assessment**: 
  - The overage was driven by additional S2 aircraft rental days beyond original deployment plans.
  - Year Two incurred significantly higher rental costs ($18,000 vs. $15,000 budgeted), suggesting either:
    - Additional/extended deployments to meet project scope
    - Weather delays or scheduling adjustments requiring extra flight days
    - Potential scope creep or additional data collection requirements
  - Total overage of $4,000 was offset by Travel underspend ($6,189.37), keeping overall project within budget.
- **Status**: ⚠️ **Overspent but mitigated by other savings**; not a critical concern given overall project balance.

#### ⚠️ **Direct Labor: SLIGHTLY OVERSPENT (102.5% of budget used)**
- **Budgeted**: $88,116.00
- **Actual**: $90,288.00
- **Variance**: –$2,172.00 **over budget** (2.5% overage)
- **Details**:
  - Year One: $50,352 budgeted / $51,860 actual (–$1,508 over; 3.0% overage)
  - Year Two: $37,764 budgeted / $38,428 actual (–$664 over; 1.8% overage)
- **Assessment**:
  - Labor costs exceeded budget in both years, suggesting:
    - Extended crew operations hours beyond planned deployment schedules
    - Additional data processing required (both NRE and per-flight-day)
    - Possible additional project management overhead
  - However, the 2.5% overage is modest and typical for field operations where variables (weather delays, data complexity) can extend timelines.
- **Status**: ⚠️ **Slightly overrun but within acceptable variance**; managed as part of overall project balance.
