   - **Recommendation**: For future projects, build in 5–10% contingency for aircraft rental or negotiate fixed-rate agreements with vendors.

2. **Incomplete Invoice Documentation**:

   - **Note**: These savings were critical to offsetting equipment overages and keeping the project on overall budget.
