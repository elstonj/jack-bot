## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Status: **PROJECT CLOSED — FULLY EXPENDED**

**Key Findings**:

1. **On-Budget Status**: 
   - **Overall Project**: Essentially **on budget**. Total invoiced ($148,711.63) is $17.37 under the contracted amount of $148,729.00 (99.99% utilization). Final invoice (1595) for $652.50 on 11/28/2023 closed out remaining balance.
   - **Year One**: **Slightly under budget** by $659.36 (99.2% utilization). Invoiced $84,328.64 against $84,988.00 budget.
   - **Year Two**: **Slightly over budget** by $641.99 (100.1% utilization). Invoiced $64,382.99 against $63,741.00 budget.

2. **Budget by Category**:

   | Category | Status | Notes |
   |----------|--------|-------|
   | **Aircraft Rental** | **Over budget by $4,000 (111.4% spent)** | Year One: +$1,000; Year Two: +$3,000. Suggests either additional flight days, higher daily rates, or scope adjustments not captured in budget docs. |
   | **Direct Labor** | **Over budget by $2,172 (102.5% spent)** | Year One: +$1,508; Year Two: +$664. Likely due to extended operations, additional crew hours, or rate adjustments. |
   | **Travel** | **Under budget by $6,189 (75.8% spent)** | Year One: +$3,167 savings; Year Two: +$3,022 savings. Cost savings in lodging, meals, vehicle rental, or logistics planning. |

3. **Burn Rate & Spending Pattern**:
   - **Year One** (11 months, Aug 2021 – Jun 2022): Invoiced $80,545 by mid-October 2022 (opening 11 months), then $3,784 more through end of Year One. Monthly average: ~$7,300/month.
   - **Year Two** (Jan–Nov 2023): Invoiced $64,382.99 over ~11 months. Monthly average: ~$5,853/month. Final invoice ($652.50) in November 2023 was minimal (close-out costs).
   - **Overall burn rate**: Steady, predictable invoicing aligned with deployments (5 days per deployment at $1,000/day + crew + processing + travel).

4. **Concerns & Observations**:

   - **Materials (Aircraft Rental) Overrun**: $4,000 over budget (11.4% variance). This is the largest budget variance and suggests either:
     - Additional flight days beyond the planned 5 days per deployment
     - Rate escalation from $1,000/day
     - Unbudgeted equipment or support costs rolled into "rental"
     - *Recommendation*: Clarify with UCSub or BST whether this was approved as a scope or cost adjustment.

   - **Labor Overrun**: $2,172 over budget (2.5% variance). Modest but cumulative, suggesting:
     - Longer operations or more crew hours per deployment
     - Rate increases for key personnel (e.g., pilot/crew daily rate)
     - *Recommendation*: Verify against timesheets or task allocations if available.

   - **Travel Savings**: $6,189 under budget (24.2% savings). This is a positive variance, but notable. Possible causes:
     - Fewer overnight stays required than budgeted (e.g., day trips instead of multi-day lodging)
     - Lower per diem or shared accommodations
     - Reduced vehicle rental needs
     - Effective logistics planning reducing overhead
     - *Note*: This offset the materials and labor overruns, keeping the project near-net-zero variance.

5. **Late Payments / Invoice Status**:
   - Invoice 1514 (earliest): Signed/approved 07/01/2022 (1 day after invoice date 06/30/2022).
   - Invoices 1521 & 1530: Status listed as "Submitted" (no approval date provided); typical processing time likely 2–4 weeks.
   - Invoices 1535, 1566, 1580, 1586: Referenced in Budget Tracking but no detailed invoice documents or approval dates provided.
   - **Data not available from QuickBooks**: No payment dates or payment status (paid vs. pending) available. Cannot assess cash flow timing or payment delays.
   - *Recommendation*: Request from QBO: invoice payment dates, payment method, and any late/disputed invoices to assess cash flow health.

6. **Contract Scope Evolution**:
   - **Original Proposal**: 6-week budget proposal at $164,093.50 (with 15% overhead).
   - **Awarded Contract**: Scaled back to Year One (4 deployments, $84,988) + Year Two (3 deployments, $63,741) = $148,729 total.
   - **Scope Reduction**: Deployed 7 total deployments over 27 months instead of original plan, suggesting cost control or client budget constraints.
   - **Funding Modifications**: No formal contract modifications documented; scope adjustments appear to have been incorporated into the annual budgets.

7. **Project Closure**:
   - **Status**: **CLOSED** as of 11/29/2023 (Final Invoice 1595).
   - **Final Activities**: Close-out invoice ($652.50) covered meetings, final data processing, and final report submission.
