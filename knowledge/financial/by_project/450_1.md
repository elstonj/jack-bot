   | **Travel** | ✅ Under Budget | $6,189 savings (75.8% of budget). Per diem and lodging managed efficiently; less travel than anticipated. |

   - **Year One** (11 months): $84,328.64 = ~$7,666/month average
   - **Year Two** (12 months): $64,382.99 = ~$5,365/month average
   - **Final Month**: $652.50 (minimal close-out costs)
   - **Spending Pattern**: Deployment-driven; higher burn during operational periods, lower during planning/post-processing phases

5. **Payment & Collections Status**:
   - **Data not available from QuickBooks** regarding actual payment dates, cash received, or accounts receivable aging
   - Based on Drive Budget Docs invoice statuses: Invoices 1514 signed promptly (07/01/2022); later invoices marked "Submitted" — final payment status unknown

   - **Materials cost overrun**: Aircraft rental exceeded budget by ~11%, suggesting either increased flight days, higher daily rates, or scope creep. Worth reviewing actual flight logs vs. budgeted 5 days/deployment.
   - **Labor overages**: Modest but consistent across both years. Crew and data processing hours may have underestimated complexity or post-flight processing requirements.
   - **Travel underutilization**: Significant underspend ($6,189) may indicate economies in logistics or reduced team size vs. plan. Verify staffing against original proposal.
