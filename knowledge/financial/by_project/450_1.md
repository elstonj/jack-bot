## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Performance
✅ **Project is essentially on budget overall**: Total budgeted $148,729.00 vs. total invoiced $148,711.63 = $17.37 remaining (0.01% variance). Project status: **CLOSED** as of 11/29/2023.

### Category-by-Category Analysis

**Materials/Equipment Rental: OVERSPENT**
- Budget: $35,000.00 | Actual: $39,000.00 | Variance: –$4,000.00 (111.4% of budget)
- **Issue**: Aircraft rental exceeded budgeted cost. Possible causes: additional flight days beyond the planned 5 days/deployment, higher daily rental rates, or equipment delays requiring extended rental periods.
- **Impact**: Offset by savings in Travel category.

**Direct Labor: OVERSPENT (MINOR)**
- Budget: $88,116.00 | Actual: $90,288.00 | Variance: –$2,172.00 (102.5% of budget)
- **Issue**: Crew operations and data processing labor slightly exceeded budget. Likely due to additional weather delays, rework, or extended data processing requirements across the 7 deployments.
- **Impact**: Manageable overrun; indicates labor efficiency was close to plan.

**Travel: UNDERSPENT (STRONG CONTROL)**
- Budget: $25,613.00 | Actual: $19,423.63 | Variance: $6,189.37 (75.8% of budget)
- **Performance**: Excellent cost control in per diem, lodging, and vehicle rental. Savings of ~$6,189 offset the overages in materials and labor.
- **Insight**: Team may have used shared accommodations, optimized meal costs, or reduced vehicle rental days through better logistics planning.
