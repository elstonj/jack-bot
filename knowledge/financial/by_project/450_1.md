### Budget Performance by Category

#### ✅ **Travel (Under Budget)**
- **Proposed Budget**: $25,613.00
- **Actual Spend**: $19,423.63
- **Variance**: +$6,189.37 (Under budget by 24.2%)
- **Assessment**: **HEALTHY** – Travel costs came in significantly below budget, particularly lodging and per diem in both years. Year One travel saved $3,167.36; Year Two saved $3,022.01. Likely due to efficient logistics planning and potentially fewer/shorter deployment trips than anticipated.

#### ⚠️ **Materials & Equipment Rental (Over Budget)**
- **Proposed Budget**: $35,000.00
- **Actual Spend**: $39,000.00
- **Variance**: –$4,000.00 (Over budget by 11.4%)
- **Assessment**: **CAUTION** – Aircraft rental exceeded budget by approximately $4,000.00 across both years:
  - Year One: $1,000.00 over (budgeted $20,000; spent $21,000)
  - Year Two: $3,000.00 over (budgeted $15,000; spent $18,000)
  - Possible causes: Additional flight days, higher daily rental rates, or unbudgeted equipment costs. Year Two overage is particularly notable (20% over budget for that year's aircraft rental).

#### ⚠️ **Direct Labor (Over Budget)**
- **Proposed Budget**: $88,116.00
- **Actual Spend**: $90,288.00
- **Variance**: –$2,172.00 (Over budget by 2.5%)
- **Assessment**: **ACCEPTABLE** – Labor costs exceeded budget by $2,172.00, representing a modest 2.5% overrun:
  - Year One: $1,508.00 over
  - Year Two: $664.00 over
  - Overrun likely attributable to additional project management hours (Jack Elston), extended data processing work (Ben Busby), or higher crew operations utilization. However, the overrun is small relative to total labor budget and was offset by travel savings.

### Financial Concerns & Observations

#### ✅ **No Significant Cost Overruns**
The project remained essentially on budget overall, with total spend ($148,711.63) within $0.01% of approved amount ($148,729.00). This indicates strong cost control by BST and effective budget management by CU Boulder.

#### ⚠️ **Year Two Materials Overage**
The $3,000.00 overage in Year Two equipment/materials is the largest single-category variance. While modest in absolute terms, it represents a 20% overrun on Year Two aircraft rental budget. This could indicate:
- Unexpected additional flight days or longer deployment periods
- Aircraft rental rate increases
- Unbudgeted equipment or consumables
- **Recommendation**: Clarify with BST whether overage was due to scope increase or budgeting underestimate for future similar projects.

#### ✅ **Travel Cost Discipline**
Travel savings of $6,189.37 (24.2% under budget) suggest efficient field operations and effective logistics planning. This partially offset labor and materials overruns.

#### ✅ **No Funding Modifications or Disputes**
No formal contract modifications, cost overrun requests, or funding disputes are documented. Project proceeded as planned with minor variances managed within the overall budget envelope.

#### ✅ **Timely Project Closure**
Final invoice (1595) submitted on schedule (11/28/2023) for project period ending 11/29/2023, indicating organized close-out and no scope creep into subsequent periods.
