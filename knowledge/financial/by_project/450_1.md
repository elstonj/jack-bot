### Key Findings

1. **Equipment Rental Overruns**: The largest variance driver ($4,000 over) was S2 Aircraft rental costs. This suggests either:
   - Additional flight days beyond the 5 days per deployment originally budgeted
   - Higher rental rates negotiated or charged
   - Contingency flights or weather makeup flights
   
2. **Labor Overages**: Cumulative labor overruns of $2,172 indicate:
   - Extended project management oversight
   - Additional crew hours during deployments
   - More extensive data processing time than anticipated

3. **Travel Savings**: Travel came in 24.2% under budget ($6,189.37 savings), partially offsetting equipment and labor overages. This suggests:
   - Efficient logistics and trip consolidation
   - Lower-than-expected meal and lodging costs
   - Fewer crew members deployed per flight than budgeted (or shared accommodations)

4. **Net Project Result**: Despite $6,172 in identified overages across equipment and labor, the project closed essentially at budget due to $6,189.37 in travel savings, resulting in a **$17.37 favorable variance overall**.
