✅ **Project is essentially on budget overall**: Total approved $148,729.00 vs. total invoiced $148,711.63 (variance: $17.37 under, <0.01%).

### By Category Analysis

| Category | Status | Notes |

Year Two overage of $3,000 (possible scope adjustment or additional deployment hours). Combined $4,000 overage offset by travel savings. |
| **Direct Labor** | 🔴 **OVERSPENT** | Budgeted $88,116, actual $90,288 (+2.2%). Year One overage of $1,508; Year Two overage of $664. Likely due to extended crew operations or additional data processing hours beyond original estimates. |

Combined $6,189.37 under budget — likely due to reduced meal/lodging costs, shorter deployment windows, or optimized logistics. This category offset labor and rental overages. |

### Burn Rate & Timeline Alignment
- **Year One Burn Rate**: ~$7,320/month over 11 months (Aug 2021 – Jun 2022), then 3 additional months of invoicing (Jul–Sep 2022) = ~$27,325/month during active deployment season.
- **Year Two Burn Rate**: ~$21,461/month (Year Two total $64,382.99 over ~3 months of invoicing activity in 2023), indicating concentrated deployment activity.
- **Project Duration**: 27 months (Aug 2021 – Nov 2023). Actual invoicing occurred in concentrated periods aligned with 7 deployments.
- **Monthly Average (Full Duration)**: $148,729 / 27 = $5,508/month (steady-state), but actual spend is deployment-dependent, not monthly uniform.

### Concerns & Observations
1. ⚠️ **Rental Overage**: $4,000 over budget (11%) suggests scope creep or cost escalation in aircraft operations. No formal change order documented in provided materials.
2. ⚠️ **Labor Overage**: $2,172 over budget (2.2%) indicates labor hours exceeded estimates. Possible causes: extended data processing, additional crew standby time, or rework.
3. ✅ **Travel Savings Mitigated Overages**: $6,189 underspend in travel absorbed both rental and labor overages, enabling project to close with only $17.37 remaining (negligible variance).
4. ✅ **No Funding Modifications Documented**: Despite budgetary pressures, no formal contract modifications are referenced in Drive docs, suggesting BST absorbed overages internally or reallocated travel savings.
5. ✅ **On-Time Completion**: Final invoice submitted 11/28/2023, one day before PoP end (11/29/2023). Project closed cleanly without extension.
6. ⚠️ **Payment Status Unknown**: Drive Budget Docs do not include QuickBooks payment records or confirmation of cash received from University of Colorado. Invoice status shows "Signed" for 1514 and "Submitted" for others, but no payment receipts are provided.
