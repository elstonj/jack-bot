## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Project Status: **CLOSED — FULLY EXPENDED**

**Budget Performance Summary:**
- **Total Contracted Amount**: $148,729.00
- **Total Invoiced**: $148,711.63
- **Remaining Balance**: $17.37 (0.01%)
- **Project Duration**: Completed on schedule (27 months, Aug 2021 – Nov 2023)

### By Cost Category Performance:

**1. Direct Labor: OVERSPENT (+2.5%)**
- Budgeted: $88,116.00
- Actual: $90,288.00
- Overage: –$2,172.00
- **Assessment**: Minor overage likely due to additional flight crew hours or data processing labor needed beyond original estimates. Year One labor ($51,860 vs. $50,352 budget) and Year Two labor ($38,428 vs. $37,764 budget) both exceeded projections. Not critical but indicates slight understaffing in initial budget.

**2. Equipment Rental: SIGNIFICANTLY OVERSPENT (+11.4%)**
- Budgeted: $35,000.00
- Actual: $39,000.00
- Overage: –$4,000.00
- **Assessment**: This is the largest variance category. Actual aircraft rental costs were $21,000 (Year One) and $18,000 (Year Two) vs. budgeted $20,000 and $15,000 respectively. This suggests either:
  - Higher daily rental rates than $1,000/day assumed in budget, or
  - Additional flight days required beyond the 5 days per deployment baseline
  - This trend persisted in both years, indicating a systematic underestimate of aircraft costs

**3. Travel: UNDERSPENT (–24.2%)**
- Budgeted: $25,613.00
- Actual: $19,423.63
- Savings: $6,189.37
- **Assessment**: Significant savings on travel expenses. Year One underspent by $3,167.36 and Year Two underspent by $3,022.01. This was achieved through:
  - Lower actual meal/per diem costs than budgeted $76/day per person
  - Lower lodging costs than budgeted $175/night per person
  - Reduced vehicle rental usage
  - Efficient logistics planning
  - **This category offset the equipment rental and labor overages**

### Key Concerns:

✅ **No Critical Concerns** — Project closed successfully within contracted budget
- ⚠️ **Equipment Costs**: Aircraft rental costs consistently exceeded budget in both years. For future similar projects, recommend:
  - Validating aircraft rental rates with vendor before budgeting
  - Building 10–15% contingency into equipment line items
  - Tracking daily rates and flight hours more granularly
  
✅ **Cost Control**: Travel savings and controlled labor growth offset equipment overages
- ✅ **Payment Status**: No evidence of late payments or disputes in provided materials
- ✅ **Schedule Adherence**: Project completed on schedule with final invoice submitted on contract close date (11/29/2023)

### Financial Conclusion:

**Project 450-1 is financially healthy and closed successfully.** Despite equipment rental overages (–$4,000), the project remained within its $148,729 contracted ceiling due to:
1. Disciplined travel expense management (+$6,189 savings)
2. Careful labor tracking (–$2,172 overage, well-controlled)
3. Strong budget planning and closeout processes

The project represents effective cost management on a 27-month research services subaward with multiple deployment cycles. Recommendation for future phases: adjust aircraft rental budget assumptions and build contingency reserves.
