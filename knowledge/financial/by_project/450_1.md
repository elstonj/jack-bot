- Data Processing (NRE & per-flight): Budgeted $12,180 (16 hrs × 7 deployments @ $174/hr), Actual embedded in crew/processing costs

**Travel** ($19,423.63 actual vs. $25,613 budgeted):
- Meals: Budgeted $7,448 (14 days × 7 deployments × 2 people @ $76/day), Actual ~$5,544 (26% under budget)
- Lodging: Budgeted $14,700 (12 nights × 7 deployments × 2 people @ $175/night), Actual ~$9,000 (39% under budget)
- Vehicle Rental: Budgeted $1,715 (7 days × 7 deployments @ $35/day), Actual ~$1,050 (39% under budget)
- Overhead/Logistics: Budgeted $1,750 (2 instances × 7 deployments @ $125), Actual ~$1,275 (27% under budget)
- **Travel savings of $6,189 (24% under budget)** likely due to: fewer deployments than initially projected, reduced per diem/lodging costs, efficient vehicle utilization, or team consolidation.
