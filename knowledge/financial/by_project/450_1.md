### Key Observations

1. **No Cost Overruns at Project Level**: Despite Year One labor/rental and Year Two equipment overages, the project stayed within the $148,729 contract ceiling with $17.37 remaining. Travel cost management offset operational overages.

2. **Equipment Costs Higher Than Budgeted**: S2 aircraft rental exceeded budget by $4,000 cumulatively. This suggests:
   - Actual flight days exceeded planned 5 days per deployment, OR
   - Daily rental rate increased during the contract period, OR
   - Additional backup/contingency flights were required

3. **Labor Tracking Tight**: Direct labor exceeded budget by only 2.5%, indicating good project management despite field operational complexity.

4. **Travel Management Strong**: Actual travel spending was 24.2% below budget ($6,189 savings), suggesting efficient logistics and field planning.

5. **No Payment/Invoice Issues Noted**: All invoices marked as "Signed," "Submitted," or completed. No evidence of rejected invoices, payment holds, or disputes in provided documents. **However, actual payment status from CU Boulder is not documented in provided sources.**

6. **Project Fully Closed**: Final invoice dated 11/28/2023, one day before end of Period of Performance (11/29/2023). Project delivered on schedule with no contract extensions or modifications documented.
