## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Project Status
✅ **PROJECT CLOSED** — All contractual obligations fulfilled; final invoice submitted 11/29/2023.

- **Total Budget**: $148,729.00 (Approved/Contracted)
- **Total Invoiced**: $148,711.63
- **Final Remaining Balance**: $17.37 (essentially fully expended)
- **Budget Performance**: **WITHIN 0.01% of approved budget** — Exceptional cost control

### Budget Performance by Category

**Strengths:**
1. **Travel Costs Well-Managed**: 
   - Year One: 21.6% under budget ($3,167.36 savings)
   - Year Two: 27.5% under budget ($3,022.01 savings)
   - **Total Travel Savings**: $6,189.37 across both years
   - Meals, lodging, vehicle rental, and logistics consistently came in under estimate

2. **Overall Cost Control**:
   - Year One: 0.8% under budget ($659.36 savings)
   - Year Two: 0.9% over budget ($641.99 overage)
   - **Net Project Variance**: +$17.37 (negligible; within 0.01%)

**Concerns / Areas of Overspend:**

1. **Equipment Rental (Aircraft)**:
   - Year One: **$1,000.00 over** ($20K budgeted vs. $21K actual) — 5% overage
   - Year Two: **$3,000.00 over** ($15K budgeted vs. $18K actual) — 20% overage
   - **Combined Overage**: $4,000.00 (11.4% above budgeted equipment costs)
   - **Possible Causes**: 
     - Extended flight days or additional deployment days beyond planned 5 days per deployment
     - Higher aircraft rental rates than anticipated
     - Additional ferrying or positioning flights
     - Maintenance or contingency flight hours

2. **Direct Labor (Overall)**:
   - Year One: **$1,508.00 over** ($50,352 budgeted vs. $51,860 actual) — 3% overage
   - Year Two: **$664.00 over** ($37,764 budgeted vs. $38,428 actual) — 1.8% overage
   - **Combined Overage**: $2,172.00 (2.5% above budgeted labor)
   - **Likely Drivers**:
     - Additional project management hours (Jack Elston, CEO)
     - Extended flight crew operations beyond 7 days per deployment
     - Additional data processing (Ben Busby, software engineer) for post-processing or quality assurance

### Burn Rate Analysis

**Year One (11 months: Aug 2021 – Jun 2022, then Jul 2022 – Sep 2022 invoicing):**
- Invoices 1514–1535: $80,545.00 cumulative
- Average monthly spend: **~$7,320/month**
- Burn rate aligned with budget; slight overage absorbed through travel savings

**Year Two (Invoices 1566–1595: Oct 2022 – Nov 2023):**
- Invoices 1566–1586: $63,730.49 (operational spend)
- Invoice 1595: $652.50 (final closeout)
- Average monthly spend: **~$5,300/month** (lower than Year One due to fewer deployments: 3 vs. 4)
- Final invoice dated 11/28/2023 confirms project completion on schedule

### Key Findings

✅ **No Cost Overruns on Total Contract**: Despite equipment rental overspends in both years, travel savings offset these costs, keeping the project within $17.37 of the approved $148,729 budget.

⚠️ **Equipment Costs Higher Than Expected**: Aircraft rental exceeded budget by $4,000 (11.4%). This warrants investigation into:
- Whether actual deployment scope matched original plan (5 days × 4 deployments vs. actual days flown)
- If aircraft rental rates increased mid-contract
- Whether contingency or repositioning flights were necessary

✅ **Excellent Travel Budget Management**: Both years came in significantly under travel budget (21.6% and 27.5% savings), suggesting efficient logistics and potential opportunities for more accurate travel estimation in future similar projects.

✅ **Project On Schedule**: Final invoice 1595 submitted 11/29/2023, matching the scheduled Period of Performance end date (11/29/2023).

✅ **Cash Flow Healthy**: Regular quarterly/monthly invoicing (1514 in Jun 2022, 1521 in Aug 2022, 1530 in Sep 2022, etc.) indicates steady billings and likely timely payments, though specific payment receipt dates are not available from QuickBooks data.
