   - **Year One** (11 months): ~$7,660/month average invoicing

   - Spending tapered significantly in Year Two despite deployment continuance, indicating improved operational efficiency or reduced scope intensity in final deployments.

   - ✅ **No payment delays**: All invoices processed and project completed on schedule (11/29/2023 = end of PoP).
   - ✅ **No budget ceiling breach**: Total spend matches contract value exactly (within rounding).

6. **Financial Risk Assessment**:
   - **Risk Level**: 🟢 **LOW**
   - Project completed on time, on budget, with controlled cost categories.
   - Equipment rental overspend was offset by travel savings.
   - Labor variance minimal and typical for field research operations.
