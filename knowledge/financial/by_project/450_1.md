### Budget Category Performance

#### ✅ **Direct Labor – SLIGHTLY OVERSPENT**
- **Approved**: $88,116.00
- **Invoiced**: $90,288.00
- **Variance**: –$2,172.00 (overspent by 2.5%)
- **Assessment**: Labor costs ran 2.5% over budget. Breakdown:
  - Project Management (Jack Elston): Within range
  - Flight Crew Operations: Primary cost driver; 7-day deployment cycles @ $1,500/day × 7 deployments = $73,500 core cost
  - Data Processing (NRE + per-flight-day): Slight overages likely due to additional post-processing or extended analysis
  - **Action**: Minor overage acceptable given field operations complexity; no corrective action required post-close.

#### ⚠️ **Materials/Equipment Rental (S2 Aircraft) – OVERSPENT**
- **Approved**: $35,000.00 (Year One $20K + Year Two $15K)
- **Invoiced**: $39,000.00
- **Variance**: –$4,000.00 (overspent by 11.4%)
- **Assessment**: Aircraft rental exceeded budgeted rate ($1,000/day).
  - Year One: Budgeted $20,000 (5 days × 4 deployments), invoiced $21,000 (+$1,000 / 5%)
  - Year Two: Budgeted $15,000 (5 days × 3 deployments), invoiced $18,000 (+$3,000 / 20%)
  - **Possible causes**: 
    - Rate increases mid-contract (no modification documented)
    - Extended flight days beyond budgeted 5-day per deployment
    - Aircraft availability constraints requiring premium pricing
  - **Impact**: $4,000 overrun partially offset by travel underspend; overall project remained within contract ceiling.

#### ✅ **Travel – UNDERSPENT**
- **Approved**: $25,613.00 (Year One $14,636 + Year Two $10,977)
- **Invoiced**: $19,423.63
- **Variance**: $6,189.37 (underspent by 24.2%)
- **Assessment**: Travel costs were significantly lower than budgeted.
  - **Breakdown by subcategory** (from proposed budget):
    - Overhead (logistics, planning): Budgeted $1,750, likely underspent
    - Meals (per diem): Budgeted $7,448, likely efficient group dining or fewer personnel deployed
    - Lodging: Budgeted $14,700, possible shorter stays or shared accommodations
    - Vehicle Rental: Budgeted $1,715, possible efficient local transport
  - **Impact**: $6,189 savings offset equipment overruns and supported overall project cost control.
  - **Note**: No detailed travel breakdown in actual invoices provided; amounts inferred from cumulative actuals.
