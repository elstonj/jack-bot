**Key Observations**:
- **Labor overspend** of $2,172.00 (2.5%) suggests either longer crew deployments, higher hourly rates, or unbudgeted tasks.
- **Equipment overspend** of $4,000.00 (11.4%) indicates additional S2 rental days or aircraft rate increases (possibly market-driven or scope adjustments).
- **Travel savings** of $6,189.37 (24.2%) offset the labor and equipment overages, likely due to:
  - Lower-than-budgeted lodging rates
  - Reduced per diem or meal costs
  - Fewer vehicle rental days than planned
  - Consolidated travel logistics

**Net Result**: Category overages in labor and equipment were fully absorbed by travel cost savings, keeping the overall project on budget.
