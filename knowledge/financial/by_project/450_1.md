## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Performance
✅ **EXCELLENT** – Project completed within 0.01% of approved budget ($17.37 remaining on $148,729.00 contract)

### Category-Level Analysis

**1. Materials/Equipment Rental: OVERSPENT**
- Budgeted: $35,000.00
- Actual: $39,000.00
- Overage: $4,000.00 (11.4% over budget)
- **Analysis**: S2 aircraft rental costs exceeded approved amounts by approximately $4,000 across both years. This likely reflects either:
  - Additional flight days required beyond the 5 days/deployment originally budgeted
  - Increased hourly or daily rental rates
  - Contingency usage for weather delays or repeat missions
- **Assessment**: Moderate concern, but absorbed by other categories

**2. Direct Labor: SLIGHTLY OVERSPENT**
- Budgeted: $88,116.00
- Actual: $90,288.00
- Overage: $2,172.00 (2.5% over budget)
- **Breakdown** (from Drive Budget):
  - Project Management (Jack Elston): Budgeted ~$2,436.00 | Likely at budget
  - Flight Crew Operations: Budgeted ~$73,500.00 | Likely overspent (~$1,500/day × deployments + contingency)
  - Data Processing (NRE + Per-Flight): Budgeted ~$12,180.00 | Likely at or slightly under budget
- **Analysis**: Minor overages in flight crew operations, likely due to additional flight hours, shift extensions, or additional crew deployments
- **Assessment**: Acceptable – 2.5% variance is within typical project tolerance

**3. Travel: SIGNIFICANTLY UNDERSPENT**
- Budgeted: $25,613.00
- Actual: $19,423.63
- Savings: $6,189.37 (24.2% under budget)
- **Analysis**: Travel costs (meals, lodging, vehicle rental, overhead) were managed well below budget. Possible reasons:
  - Fewer team members deployed per mission than budgeted
  - Shorter deployment durations than planned
  - Negotiated lower per diem or lodging rates
  - Consolidated logistics or shared transportation
- **Assessment**: Positive – demonstrates cost-effective project execution and strong field operations planning
