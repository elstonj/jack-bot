1. **On-Budget Overall**: Total actual spend ($148,711.63) versus approved budget ($148,729.00) is essentially perfect alignment.
2. **Travel Cost Control**: Travel category underspent by $6,189 (24.2% below budget), indicating efficient logistics planning and possible consolidation of per diem usage.
3. **Timely Invoicing**: Seven invoices issued over 27-month period; final invoice submitted at project end date (11/29/2023).
4. **Predictable Deployment Costs**: Budget structure aligned deployments to fiscal years; invoicing pattern followed deployment schedule.

#### Areas of Concern:

1. **Equipment Rental Overrun**: 
   - **Overspent by $4,000 (+11.4%)**

   - Year One: +$1,508 (103% of $50,352 budget)
   - Year Two: +$664 (101.8% of $37,764 budget)
   - **Implication**: Labor costs slightly exceeded budgeted hours/rates, likely reflecting additional crew hours for operational support or data processing during deployments.

3. **Incomplete Invoice Documentation**:
   - **Data not available from Drive Budget Docs**: Four invoices (1535, 1566, 1580, 1586) lack full documentation (dates, period covered). Amounts inferred from cumulative Budget Tracking spreadsheet.
   - **Recommendation**: Reconcile against original invoice files for audit compliance.
