## Key Observations & Risks Addressed

1. **Equipment Overruns Managed**: The $4,000 overrun in aircraft rental (11.4%) likely reflects weather delays or extended standby time—common in high-altitude field operations. This was successfully absorbed within the overall budget thanks to travel savings.

2. **No Formal Contract Modifications**: Drive Budget Docs reference a scaling from an initial 6-week proposal model to a 7-deployment, 27-month schedule, but no formal change orders were documented. All costs remained within the subaward ceiling.

3. **Deployment Effectiveness**: Year One ran 4 deployments; Year Two ran 3 (vs. potential 6-week continuous model). This phased approach appears to have controlled costs while maintaining data quality.

4. **Closeout Efficiency**: Final invoice ($652.50) reflects minimal administrative costs, suggesting smooth project conclusion without disputes or extensive final invoicing adjustments.

5. **Data Sources**: Budget figures from Drive Budget Docs (proposal, budget tracking) are consistent. **QuickBooks actuals not provided**; all variance analysis based on Drive Budget Tracking spreadsheet (final update 2023-11-29). No independent QB reconciliation available.
