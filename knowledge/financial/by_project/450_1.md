### Overall Budget Performance: **EXCELLENT**

- **Remaining Balance**: $17.37 (essentially zero overspend or underspend)

### Performance by Category

**Materials/Equipment Rental — OVERSPENT**
- Overrun: $4,000.00 (111.4% of budget)
- **Issue**: Aircraft rental costs exceeded approved budget in both years:
  - Year One: $1,000 over ($21,000 actual vs. $20,000 budgeted)
  - Year Two: $3,000 over ($18,000 actual vs. $15,000 budgeted)
- **Assessment**: Year Two overspend was larger, suggesting either additional flights, higher daily rates, or extended rental periods not initially planned
- **Impact**: Offset by travel underspending

**Labor — SLIGHTLY OVERSPENT**
- Overrun: $2,172.00 (102.5% of budget)
- **Issue**: Flight crew operations and data processing labor exceeded estimates in both years
  - Year One: $1,508 over
  - Year Two: $664 over
- **Assessment**: Incremental overspend suggests additional hours or higher hourly rates than planned. Not a significant concern given 2.5% variance.
- **Key Personnel**: Jack Elston (PM), Maciej Stachura, Joshua Fromm, Daniel Troke (flight crew), Ben Busby (data processing)

**Travel — SIGNIFICANTLY UNDERSPENT**
- Savings: $6,189.37 (75.8% of budget, 24.2% underutilization)
- **Issue**: Meals, lodging, vehicle rental, and logistics overhead came in well under budget in both years
  - Year One: $3,167.36 under
  - Year Two: $3,022.01 under
- **Assessment**: Consistent underspending across both years suggests conservative initial budgeting, efficient operations planning, or fewer deployment days than anticipated. Likely reflects reduced scope in Year Two (3 deployments vs. initial planning).
