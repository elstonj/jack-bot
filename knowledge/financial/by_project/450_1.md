## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Project Status: **CLOSED & FULLY EXPENDED**

**Key Findings**:

1. **Budget Performance**:
   - **Total Budget**: $148,729.00 (contracted value)
   - **Total Invoiced**: $148,711.63
   - **Remaining**: $17.37 (0.01% unused)
   - **Status**: Project closed on 11/29/2023 with essentially full budget consumption.

2. **Performance by Cost Category**:

   - **Direct Labor** — **OVERSPENT**
     - Budgeted: $88,116.00 | Actual: $90,288.00 | Variance: $(2,172.00) | 102.5% used
     - **Concern**: 2.5% overage. Driven by higher-than-budgeted flight crew and data processing hours (likely due to additional scope, extended processing, or higher hourly rates for certain personnel).

   - **Equipment Rental (S2 Aircraft)** — **OVERSPENT**
     - Budgeted: $35,000.00 | Actual: $39,000.00 | Variance: $(4,000.00) | 111.4% used
     - **Concern**: 11.4% overage ($4,000 over budget). Likely due to additional flight days beyond the planned 5 days per deployment (e.g., weather delays requiring extra days, or additional deployments/missions).

   - **Travel** — **UNDERSPENT**
     - Budgeted: $25,613.00 | Actual: $19,423.63 | Variance: $6,189.37 | 75.8% used
     - **Favorable**: 24.2% underage. Travel costs (meals, lodging, vehicle rental) came in under budget, likely due to fewer personnel on-site, shorter deployments, or cost-saving logistics measures.

3. **Burn Rate & Timeline**:
   - **Year One** (Aug 2021 – Jul 2022, ~11 months): $84,328.64 invoiced
     - Average monthly burn: ~$7,666/month
     - Slightly **underspent** by $659.36 against $84,988.00 budget
   - **Year Two** (Aug 2022 – Nov 2023, ~15 months): $64,382.99 invoiced
     - Average monthly burn: ~$4,292/month
     - Slightly **overspent** by $(641.99) against $63,741.00 budget
   - **Overall**: Steady, predictable burn pattern aligned with 7 deployment cycles across 27-month period.

4. **Risk Assessment**:
   - **No Outstanding Risk**: Project is closed. All invoices have been submitted (final invoice 1595 on 11/29/2023).
   - **Cost Overruns Addressed**: Labor and equipment overages were absorbed within total contract value ($17.37 remaining is negligible).
   - **Payment Status**: Data not available from QuickBooks; however, Budget Doc indicates invoices 1514 (signed 07/01/2022), 1521–1530 (submitted), and 1595 (submitted 11/29/2023) are on record. No payment status details provided.

5. **Key Concerns** (Historical/Closed Project):
   - **Equipment costs exceeded budget by 11.4%** — suggest future similar projects may need higher aircraft rental contingency or stricter flight-day controls.
   - **Labor exceeded budget by 2.5%** — indicates data processing and/or crew staffing was more resource-intensive than estimated.
   - **Travel savings (24.2% under)** partially offset overages in labor and equipment, keeping project near budget.

6. **Financial Conclusion**:
   - Project remained within **total contract value** ($148,729.00).
   - Individual cost categories show overspend in labor (2.5%) and equipment (11.4%), but were offset by travel underspend (24.2%).
   - No indicators of late payments, cost disputes, or billing anomalies in provided documentation.
   - **Project successfully closed with minimal unused balance ($17.37)**.
