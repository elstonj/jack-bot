## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Performance
✅ **Project is essentially on budget overall**.
- **Total Budgeted**: $148,729.00
- **Total Invoiced**: $148,711.63
- **Final Variance**: **$17.37 under budget (0.01%)**
- **Project Status**: **CLOSED** as of 11/28/2023

The project remained under budget despite overruns in labor and equipment rental, which were offset by underspending in travel costs.

### Category-Level Performance

**🔴 Labor — 2.5% Over Budget ($2,172 overage)**
- Budgeted: $88,116.00 | Actual: $90,288.00
- **Cause**: Likely additional labor hours for flight operations (crew) or project management beyond planned scope, or billing rate adjustments.
- **Assessment**: Modest overrun absorbed by travel savings.

**🔴 Materials/Equipment Rental — 11.4% Over Budget ($4,000 overage)**
- Budgeted: $35,000.00 | Actual: $39,000.00
- **Breakdown**:
  - Year One: $1,000 over ($20,000 budgeted vs. $21,000 actual)
  - Year Two: $3,000 over ($15,000 budgeted vs. $18,000 actual)
- **Cause**: Additional aircraft rental days beyond 5 days/deployment plan, or rate increases over the 27-month period.
- **Assessment**: Year Two showed larger variance, suggesting scope creep or unplanned flights.

**🟢 Travel — 24.2% Under Budget ($6,189.37 savings)**
- Budgeted: $25,613.00 | Actual: $19,423.63
- **Year One**: $3,167.36 under ($14,636 budgeted vs. $11,468.64 actual)
- **Year Two**: $3,022.01 under ($10,977 budgeted vs. $7,954.99 actual)
- **Cause**: Efficient logistical planning; possibly reduced per diem usage, lower lodging costs, or fewer vehicle rental days than budgeted.
- **Assessment**: Strong cost control in travel category.
