## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Status: ✅ **COMPLETED & ON BUDGET**

**Key Findings**:

1. **Budget Adherence**: 
   - Total contract budget: $148,729.00
   - Total invoiced: $148,711.63
   - **Final variance: +$17.37 (underspent 0.01%)**
   - **Project fully expended and closed** as of 11/29/2023
   
2. **Category Performance**:
   - ✅ **Materials/Equipment Rental**: 111% utilized ($39,000 of $35,000 budgeted)
     - **Concern**: Year Two overran by $3,000 (120% of Year Two rental budget)
     - Likely cause: additional flight days or rate adjustments; within overall project tolerance
   - ✅ **Labor**: 102% utilized ($90,288 of $88,116 budgeted)
     - Minimal overage; consistent across both years (Year 1: 103%, Year 2: 102%)
     - Possible root cause: additional crew hours for data processing or extended deployment operations
   - ✅ **Travel**: 76% utilized ($19,424 of $25,613 budgeted)
     - **Significant underspend**: $6,189 below budget (24% savings)
     - Drove overall project to come in under contract value
     - Likely caused by lower-than-estimated lodging, meals, and vehicle rental costs

3. **Year-by-Year Performance**:
   - **Year One** (4 deployments): Underspent by $659.36 (99% of budget)
     - Balanced overage in rental ($1,000) and labor ($1,508) offset by travel savings ($3,167)
   - **Year Two** (3 deployments): Overspent by $641.99 (101% of budget)
     - Driven by materials overage ($3,000); partially mitigated by travel underspend ($3,022)
   - **Net effect**: Projects offset each other, landing project within $17.37 of total budget

4. **Burn Rate & Cash Flow**:
   - **Year One**: ~$7,320/month average over 11 months of invoicing (first invoice lumpy at $15,558 for initial 11 months)
   - **Year Two**: ~$21,461/month average (3 invoices + final close-out)
   - Year Two burn higher due to concentrated 3-deployment cycle vs. Year One's staggered 4-deployment schedule
   - **No cash flow concerns identified**: All invoices submitted; payment status not confirmed but no arrears noted

5. **Spend Pattern**:
   - Invoices follow deployment cycles closely (expected for a services contract)
   - Final invoice ($652.50) is minimal, indicating clean project closure
   - No evidence of retainage or holdback amounts in budget documents

6. **Risk Assessment**:
   - ⚠️ **Minor concern – Year Two Materials Overrun**: The $3,000 overage in aircraft rental (120% of Year Two budget) suggests potential scope creep or rate changes. However, this was managed within the overall contract value.
   - ✅ **No overrun concerns**: Project completed on budget; no contract modifications or amendments documented
   - ✅ **No payment delays**: Invoices show "Submitted" or "Signed" status; no past-due amounts noted
   - ✅ **Clean closure**: Final invoice submitted on contract end date; project marked CLOSED
