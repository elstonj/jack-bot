## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Performance
✓ **Project is essentially on budget**: Total invoiced ($148,711.63) vs. approved budget ($148,729.00) = **$17.37 remaining (0.01% variance)**. Project closed on 11/29/2023 with final invoice 1595.

### Performance by Cost Category

**✓ Direct Labor: +2% Overage (Manageable)**
- Budgeted: $88,116.00 | Actual: $90,288.00 | Overage: –$2,172.00
- Reason: Likely additional crew hours or higher billing rates for flight operations and data processing than budgeted. Year Two labor saw $664 overages; Year One labor $1,508 overages. Cumulative 2% overage is within typical contingency tolerance.
- **Assessment**: Minor overage; not a concern.

**✗ Equipment Rental: +11% Overage (Significant)**
- Budgeted: $35,000.00 | Actual: $39,000.00 | Overage: –$4,000.00
- Reason: Budget assumed $1,000/day S2 aircraft rental for 5 days per deployment (7 total deployments = $35,000). Actual invoiced $39,000, suggesting either:
  - Additional flight days beyond the 5 planned per deployment, OR
  - Rate increases during Year Two deployments.
- **Assessment**: Largest category variance. However, offset by Travel underage, so overall project impact minimal. Worth reviewing deployment logs to confirm whether scope creep or rate adjustment drove this overage.

**✓ Travel: –24% Underage (Favorable)**
- Budgeted: $25,613.00 | Actual: $19,423.63 | Savings: $6,189.37
- Reason: Per-diem meal rates, lodging costs, and vehicle rental came in below budgeted daily rates. Logistics overhead also lower than estimated.
- **Assessment**: Favorable variance. Crew likely found lower-cost accommodation, negotiated vehicle rates, or per-diem was conservatively budgeted.

### Year-Over-Year Spend Variance
- **Year One**: **Underspent by $659.36** (Budgeted $84,988.00 | Spent $84,328.64 | 99.2% utilization)
- **Year Two**: **Overspent by $641.99** (Budgeted $63,741.00 | Spent $64,382.99 | 100.1% utilization)
- **Net**: Year Two overage offset by Year One underage, resulting in project-level near-perfect alignment.

### Burn Rate vs. Period of Performance
- **Contract Duration**: 27 months (Aug 2021 – Nov 2023)
- **Average Monthly Burn**: $148,729 ÷ 27 = **$5,508.11/month**
- **Actual Spend Pattern**:
  - **Year One (12 months)**: $84,328.64 = $7,027.39/month average
  - **Year Two (15 months, through Nov 2023)**: $64,382.99 = $4,292.20/month average
- **Interpretation**: Year One had higher monthly burn due to 4 deployments in 12 months. Year Two spread 3 deployments over 15 months, reducing monthly burn. Spending tracked closely to deployment schedule, indicating no significant cost overruns or underutilization.
