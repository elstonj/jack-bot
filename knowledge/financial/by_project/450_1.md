## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Performance: **HEALTHY (Project On Budget)**

**Summary**:
- Project expended **$148,711.63 of $148,729.00** approved budget (99.99% utilization).
- Only **$17.37 remaining** — effectively zero balance.
- All 8 invoices submitted and project closed on schedule (11/29/2023).
- No cost overruns at the project level, despite category-level variances.

### Category-Level Analysis

#### ✅ **Direct Labor** — Overspent by $2,172.00 (–2.5%)
- **Budgeted**: $88,116.00
- **Actual**: $90,288.00
- **Impact**: Minor overage. Likely due to:
  - Additional data processing hours (NRE and per-flight-day processing) than estimated.
  - Possible increases to hourly rates or additional project management time required for Year Two deployments.
- **Status**: Within acceptable variance (~2.5%); not a major concern on a $148.7K budget.

#### ⚠️ **Materials/Equipment Rental** — Overspent by $4,000.00 (–11.4%)
- **Budgeted**: $35,000.00
- **Actual**: $39,000.00
- **Impact**: Most significant overage. 
- **Root Cause** (from budget document notes):
  - S2 aircraft rental exceeded budgeted $1,000/day rate.
  - Year Two Materials budget overrun of $3,000 suggests aircraft rental rates or deployment duration varied.
  - Budget assumed 5 flight days × 7 deployments (Y1+Y2) = $35,000; actual flew more days or at higher daily rates.
- **Status**: This is the largest variance category. However, the project absorbed this within the overall approved budget by underspending travel.

#### ✅ **Travel** — Underspent by $6,189.37 (–24.2%)
- **Budgeted**: $25,613.00
- **Actual**: $19,423.63
- **Impact**: Strong cost control. 
- **Reasons**:
  - Actual meals per diem came in below $76/day budgeted estimate.
  - Lodging rates were lower than $175/night assumption (possibly negotiated rates or shorter stays).
  - Vehicle rental costs were minimized.
- **Status**: This travel savings offset the equipment rental overages, keeping the project whole.
