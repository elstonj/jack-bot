### Category-by-Category Analysis

#### 1. **Materials/Equipment Rental** — **OVERSPENT**
- **Budgeted**: $35,000.00
- **Actual**: $39,000.00
- **Variance**: –$4,000.00 (overspent by 11.4%)
- **Root Cause** (from Budget Docs): 
  - Year One: Aircraft rental actual $21,000 vs budgeted $20,000 (–$1,000 over, possibly additional flight day or higher daily rate)
  - Year Two: Aircraft rental actual $18,000 vs budgeted $15,000 (–$3,000 over, possibly additional deployments or days beyond 3×5-day plan)
- **Assessment**: **Moderate concern.** Equipment costs exceeded budget in both years. Possible causes: unplanned additional flights, rate increases, or scope creep in deployment days. However, overspend was absorbed within overall project by Travel category underspend.

#### 2. **Labor** — **OVERSPENT**
- **Budgeted**: $88,116.00
- **Actual**: $90,288.00
- **Variance**: –$2,172.00 (overspent by 2.5%)
- **Root Cause** (from Budget Docs):
  - Year One: Labor actual $51,860 vs budgeted $50,352 (–$1,508 over; 3% overspend)
  - Year Two: Labor actual $38,428 vs budgeted $37,764 (–$664 over; 1.8% overspend)
  - Likely causes: additional hours for data processing (Ben Busby, Software Engineer), extended project management overhead (Jack Elston), or extended crew operations
- **Assessment**: **Minor concern.** Labor overspend is modest (2.5% overall). Reflects possible scope extension or unplanned data processing work post-flight. Within acceptable variance for research/services contracts.

#### 3. **Travel** — **UNDERSPENT**
- **Budgeted**: $25,613.00
- **Actual**: $19,423.63
- **Variance**: $6,189.37 (underspent by 24.2%)
- **Breakdown** (from Budget Docs):
  - Year One: Travel actual $11,468.64 vs budgeted $14,636 (under by $3,167.36; 23.6% under)
  - Year Two: Travel actual $7,954.99 vs budgeted $10,977 (under by $3,022.01; 27.5% under)
  - Categories: Overhead (logistics), meals (per diem), lodging, vehicle rental
- **Assessment**: **Positive.** Significant travel underspend across both years. Likely due to:
  - Fewer deployment days than budgeted (e.g., weather delays, operational efficiencies)
  - Reduced lodging/meal costs (shorter trips, different per diem rates, or local deployments requiring less overnight stays)
  - Vehicle rental optimization
  - This underspend offset the Equipment and Labor overruns.
