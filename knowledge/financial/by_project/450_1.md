### Key Financial Observations & Concerns

#### ✅ **Strengths**
1. **Overall Budget Control**: Project delivered at 99.99% of approved budget, with only $17.37 remaining. Excellent cost management across 27-month period.
2. **Travel Savings**: Achieved 24% travel savings ($6,189) through efficient logistics. This suggests strong field operations management.
3. **Project Completion**: Final invoice submitted on schedule (11/28/2023, matching POP end date of 11/29/2023). No delays or cost growth at closeout.
4. **Invoice Tracking**: Clear billing history with 8 invoices submitted across two contract years.

#### ⚠️ **Concerns & Observations**
1. **Equipment Rental Overages**: 
   - Year Two materials costs exceeded budget by 20% ($18k vs. $15k budgeted)
   - This suggests either:
     - Additional deployments beyond the 3 planned
     - Higher daily rental rates than budgeted
     - Extended flight days per deployment
   - **Risk**: If similar cost inflation occurs on future projects, budget estimates may need revision.

2. **Year Two Efficiency Loss**:
   - Year Two deployed only 3 flights vs. 4 in Year One, yet overran budget by $642 (vs. $659 underage in Year One)
   - Per-deployment cost increased from ~$21,000 (Year One) to ~$21,460 (Year Two)
   - Likely due to reduced economy of scale on fewer deployments

3. **Billing Lag in Year One**:
   - First invoice (11 months of work) arrived 11 months after contract start (late June 2022)
   - Subsequent invoices show accelerated billing, suggesting catch-up
   - **Implication**: Cash flow may have been delayed; no indication of payment delays, but gap between work and billing should be monitored on future projects
