## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Performance: **HEALTHY / ON BUDGET**

**Key Findings**:

1. **Total Project Execution**: 99.99% of approved budget expended ($148,711.63 of $148,729.00).
   - Remaining balance: **$17.37** (negligible rounding difference)
   - **Source**: Drive Budget Documents (Budget Tracking, dated 2023-11-29)

2. **Year One Performance**: **Slight Underspend** (–$659.36 or –0.8%)
   - Budgeted: $84,988.00
   - Actual: $84,328.64
   - Primarily driven by travel cost savings ($3,167.36 under) despite overruns in Materials ($1,000 over) and Labor ($1,508 over)
   - **Source**: Budget Tracking, Drive Budget Documents

3. **Year Two Performance**: **Slight Overspend** (+$641.99 or +1.0%)
   - Budgeted: $63,741.00
   - Actual: $64,382.99
   - Materials/Equipment overran by $3,000 (likely additional aircraft rental days or rate increases)
   - Travel again came in under budget ($3,022.01 under), offsetting equipment and labor overruns
   - **Source**: Budget Tracking, Drive Budget Documents

### Cost Category Analysis

#### ✅ **Materials/Equipment Rental** — OVER BUDGET
- **Year One**: $1,000 over ($20,000 budgeted vs. $21,000 actual; +5%)
- **Year Two**: $3,000 over ($15,000 budgeted vs. $18,000 actual; +20%)
- **Total**: $4,000 over budgeted $35,000 (111.4% of budget used)
- **Analysis**: Aircraft rental costs exceeded proposal estimates. This could reflect:
  - Additional flight days beyond the 5 days per deployment baseline
  - Rate increases during contract performance
  - Weather delays requiring rescheduled missions
  - **Recommendation**: Review actual rental invoices and flight logs to validate overages. Year Two overrun was more severe (+$3,000), suggesting possible rate escalations or scope creep.

#### ✅ **Direct Labor** — OVER BUDGET (Minor)
- **Year One**: $1,508 over ($50,352 budgeted vs. $51,860 actual; +3%)
- **Year Two**: $664 over ($37,764 budgeted vs. $38,428 actual; +1.8%)
- **Total**: $2,172 over budgeted $88,116 (102.5% of budget used)
- **Analysis**: Labor costs slightly exceeded forecast, likely due to:
  - Extended flight operations or data processing hours (per deployment)
  - Higher hourly rates or different staffing mix than anticipated
  - **Recommendation**: Year One overrun was more substantial; Year Two was well-controlled. Overall labor performance was acceptable.

#### ✅ **Travel** — UNDER BUDGET (Consistent Savings)
- **Year One**: $3,167.36 under ($14,636 budgeted vs. $11,468.64 actual; 78.4% of budget used)
- **Year Two**: $3,022.01 under ($10,977 budgeted vs. $7,954.99 actual; 72.5% of budget used)
- **Total**: $6,189.37 under budgeted $25,613 (75.8% of budget used)
- **Analysis**: Significant and consistent travel savings across both years indicate:
  - Lower-than-expected meal, lodging, or vehicle rental costs
  - Possible reduction in personnel travel (e.g., consolidated teams, local personnel)
  - Efficient logistics planning and scheduling
  - **Recommendation**: Travel savings offset material and labor overruns, resulting in net project underspend in Year One. This cost control was beneficial to overall budget management.
