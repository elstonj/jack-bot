### Key Financial Observations

1. **Equipment Rental Overages Offset by Travel Savings**: The $4,000 overage in aircraft rental was partially offset by $6,189.37 in travel savings, resulting in a net favorable variance of ~$2,189 for the overall project (before labor overage).

2. **Labor Creep**: The 2.5% labor overage ($2,172) suggests that crew operations required more hours than budgeted, possibly due to:
   - Extended flight days or additional crew time
   - Data processing taking longer than anticipated (NRE and per-flight-day allocations)
   - Potential scope adjustments mid-project

3. **Year Two Tighter Margins**: Year Two showed tighter budget control overall (only –$641.99 overage vs. Year One's $659.36 favorable variance), despite higher equipment costs. This suggests BST adjusted operations to manage costs more closely in the second year.

4. **Burn Rate Steady**: Monthly average spend was approximately:
   - Year One: ~$7,320/month (11-month period)
   - Year Two: ~$8,065/month (8-month period, ending 11/29/2023)
   - Consistent with planned 4 + 3 deployment structure

5. **Final Close-out Efficient**: The final invoice (1595, $652.50) on 11/28/2023 captured minimal residual costs (meetings, final data processing, reporting), indicating clean project closure.
