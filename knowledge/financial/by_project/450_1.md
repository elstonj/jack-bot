### Budget Performance by Category

#### ✓ **Materials/Equipment Rental** — OVERSPENT ($4,000 / 11.4% over budget)
- **Budgeted**: $35,000.00 (S2 aircraft rental: $1,000/day × 5 days × 7 deployments)
- **Actual**: $39,000.00
- **Analysis**: Aircraft rental exceeded expectations, likely due to:
  - Additional flight hours beyond budgeted 5 days per deployment
  - Extended operational requirements (weather delays, additional survey passes)
  - Rising market rates for aircraft rental
- **Concern**: Moderate cost overrun managed within overall project budget due to travel savings.

#### ✓ **Labor** — OVERSPENT ($2,172 / 2.5% over budget)
- **Budgeted**: $88,116.00
- **Actual**: $90,288.00
- **Breakdown**:
  - Project Management (Jack Elston, CEO): modest overage
  - Flight Crew Operations: primary cost driver (~$73,500 across project at $1,500/day × 7 days × 7 deployments); slight overage likely due to extended operations
  - Data Processing (NRE & per-flight): marginal overspend
- **Analysis**: Labor cost growth of 2.5% suggests operational efficiency challenges or extended processing timelines, but well-controlled.

#### ✓ **Travel** — UNDERSPENT ($6,189.37 / 24.2% under budget)
- **Budgeted**: $25,613.00
- **Actual**: $19,423.63
- **Breakdown**:
  - Lodging, meals, vehicle rental, and logistics overhead came in significantly under budget.
  - Year One savings: $3,167.36
  - Year Two savings: $3,022.01
- **Analysis**: Strong cost control in field logistics; possible reasons include:
  - Efficient team planning and local sourcing
  - Fewer field personnel than budgeted in some deployments
  - Reduced meal/lodging costs through negotiated rates or shorter stay periods
- **Benefit**: Travel savings fully offset equipment and labor overspends.
