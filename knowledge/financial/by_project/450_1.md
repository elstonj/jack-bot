2. **Performance by Category**

   | Category | Budgeted | Actual | Variance | Health |
   |----------|----------|--------|----------|--------|
   | **Direct Labor** | $88,116.00 | $90,288.00 | –$2,172.00 (2.5% over) | ⚠ **Slightly Overrun** |
   | **Materials/Equipment Rental** | $35,000.00 | $39,000.00 | –$4,000.00 (11.4% over) | ⚠ **Overrun** |

   **Analysis**:
   - **Labor Overage ($2,172)**: Modest overrun likely due to additional flight operations, crew time, or data processing hours beyond initial estimate. ~2.5% overage is reasonable for field operations with weather delays or scope expansion.
   - **Equipment Rental Overage ($4,000)**: Significant 11.4% overage suggests additional aircraft rental days or rental rate increases. This is the largest cost category variance and warrants investigation (e.g., were additional deployment days required? Did rates increase mid-contract?).
   - **Travel Underspend ($6,189.37)**: Substantial savings achieved through efficient logistics, reduced meal/lodging costs, or fewer actual field days than budgeted. This underspend offset the labor and equipment overruns.
