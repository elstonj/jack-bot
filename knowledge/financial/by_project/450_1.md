## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Project Status: **CLOSED — FULLY EXPENDED**

**Key Findings:**

1. **Budget Execution**: Project invoiced **$148,711.63 against approved $148,729.00**, leaving only **$17.37 remaining** (99.99% expended). Final invoice submitted 11/28/2023 closes out contract.

2. **Cost Control — Mixed Performance**:
   - ✅ **Travel**: Underspent by **$6,189.37 (24.2% under budget)** across both years. Year One savings of $3,167.36 and Year Two savings of $3,022.01 suggest efficient logistics/meal management.
   - ❌ **Equipment Rental (S2 Aircraft)**: Overspent by **$4,000.00 (11.4% over budget)**. Year One overage of $1,000 and Year Two overage of $3,000 indicate either additional rental days, rate increases, or scope creep in deployment requirements.
   - ❌ **Direct Labor**: Overspent by **$2,172.00 (2.5% over budget)**. Year One overage of $1,508 and Year Two overage of $664 suggest additional hours for crew operations or data processing beyond initial estimates.

3. **Category Performance Summary**:
   - Labor and Equipment combined overage: **$6,172.00**
   - Travel underspend: **$6,189.37**
   - **Net result**: Travel savings nearly offset labor/equipment overruns, leaving project at 99.99% of approved budget.

4. **Burn Rate Analysis** (Source: Drive Budget Documents):
   - **Year One**: $84,328.64 invoiced over 11 months (approx. $7,666/month average)
   - **Year Two**: $64,382.99 invoiced over 15 months (approx. $4,292/month average)
   - **Year Two burn rate lower** due to reduced deployment frequency (3 vs. 4 deployments) and summer 2023 project wind-down
   - Final invoice ($652.50) represents close-out activities only

5. **Period of Performance Compliance**: 
   - Approved PoP: 08/01/2021 – 11/29/2023 (27 months)
   - Final invoice dated 11/28/2023 → **On-time project close-out**
   - No evidence of contract extensions or amendments documented

6. **Financial Concerns — Addressed or Mitigated**:
   - ⚠️ **Equipment overspend**: Year Two aircraft rental 20% over budget ($3,000 overage on $15,000). **Root cause not documented** in provided materials; suggests possible additional deployment days or rate increase not reflected in original budget. However, offset by travel savings.
   - ⚠️ **Labor overspend**: Modest 2.5% overage suggests minor scope creep (additional hours) or rate adjustments. Not materially problematic given overall project discipline.
   - ✅ **Budget discipline**: Despite line item overages, project completed within **0.01% of approved total**, indicating strong financial controls and cost-offset management.

7. **Invoice Payment Status**:
   - **Data not available from QuickBooks** — Cannot confirm actual payment receipt or timing. Budget documents show invoices "Submitted" but lack payment confirmation.
   - **Critical gap**: No evidence provided of CU Boulder payment receipt. Recommend reconciliation with QuickBooks aging report and university accounts payable system.

8. **Recommendations / Observations**:
   - **For Finance Review**: Investigate Year Two aircraft overspend ($3K) to understand scope vs. contractual commitment.
   - **For Future Projects**: Use deployment-based rate cards and stricter aircraft rental caps to prevent recurrence.
