- All 7 planned deployments were completed within the 27-month period of performance.
- **Project Status**: CLOSED as of November 29, 2023.

### Category-Level Performance:

#### ✅ **Materials/Equipment Rental: OVERSPENT by $4,000 (–11.4%)**
- **Budget**: $35,000.00 | **Actual**: $39,000.00
- **Root Cause**: Additional S2 aircraft rental days beyond the original deployment plan.
  - Year One: 1 extra day ($21,000 vs. $20,000)
  - Year Two: 2 extra days ($18,000 vs. $15,000)
- **Assessment**: Overage was necessary to complete mission objectives; absorbed through labor and travel savings.

#### ✅ **Direct Labor: OVERSPENT by $2,172 (–2.5%)**
- **Budget**: $88,116.00 | **Actual**: $90,288.00
- **Root Cause**: Crew labor for the additional 3 aircraft rental days (correlates with equipment overage).
- **Assessment**: Minimal overage; reasonable for supporting extended flight operations.

#### ✅ **Travel: UNDERSPENT by $6,189 (–24.2%)**
- **Budget**: $25,613.00 | **Actual**: $19,423.63
- **Root Cause**: Meals, lodging, and logistics costs were lower than anticipated.
  - Possible reasons: fewer crew members deployed per cycle, shorter stays, or more efficient site logistics.
- **Assessment**: Significant savings offset equipment and labor overages.
