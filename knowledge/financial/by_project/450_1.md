### Key Findings & Concerns

1. **Labor Overage (–$2,172 / 2.5%)**
   - ⚠️ **Concern**: Direct labor exceeded budget, primarily in flight crew operations and data processing.
   - **Possible Causes**: 
     - Additional or longer flight days to achieve data coverage
     - Crew standby/waiting time during unfavorable weather (common in alpine environments)
     - Data processing complexity exceeded NRE estimates
   - **Risk**: If Year Two labor overrun trend continues, future projects should allocate higher contingency for alpine weather delays.

2. **Equipment Rental Overage (–$4,000 / 11.4%)**
   - ⚠️ **Major Concern**: S2 aircraft rental was the largest category overage.
   - **Possible Causes**:
     - Additional rental days beyond 5 days/deployment (e.g., 6+ days per deployment to accommodate weather delays)
     - Higher per-diem aircraft rental rate negotiated during contract
     - Unbudgeted repositioning or ferry flights
   - **Impact**: Equipment costs are least controllable (dependent on aircraft availability and weather); budget should reflect alpine environment operational challenges.

3. **Travel Underage (+$6,189.37 / 24.2%)**
   - ✅ **Positive**: Strong cost control on meals, lodging, and logistics.
   - **Possible Reasons**:
     - Fewer deployment days than original 7-day/deployment assumption
     - Efficient crew utilization (fewer total people deployed)
     - Favorable negotiations on lodging rates
   - **Opportunity**: Savings here offset labor and equipment overruns, keeping total project on budget.
