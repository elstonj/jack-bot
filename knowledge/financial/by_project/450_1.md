## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Project Status: **ON BUDGET (Closed)**

**Summary**: Project 450-1 closed on 11/29/2023 with expenditures of $148,711.63 against an approved budget of $148,729.00, resulting in a $17.37 remaining balance (0.01% variance). The project executed without formal budget modifications and maintained financial discipline despite Category overruns offset by Category underruns.

---

### Category Performance Analysis

#### **Materials/Equipment Rental: OVERSPENT**
- **Budgeted**: $35,000.00
- **Actual**: $39,000.00
- **Overage**: $4,000.00 (111% of budget)
- **Assessment**: Year Two particularly overran by $3,000 (120% of $15,000 Year Two budget), suggesting:
  - Additional aircraft rental days beyond the planned 5-day deployments
  - Possible rate increases mid-contract
  - Extended contingency operations or weather delays requiring extra rental hours
- **Risk**: This category would have resulted in project cost overrun if not offset by travel underruns

#### **Direct Labor: OVERSPENT**
- **Budgeted**: $88,116.00
- **Actual**: $90,288.00
- **Overage**: $2,172.00 (102% of budget)
- **Breakdown**:
  - Year One: +$1,508 (103% of $50,352)
  - Year Two: +$664 (102% of $37,764)
- **Assessment**: Consistent 2–3% overruns in both years indicate:
  - Actual crew labor rates or hours exceeded planned rates
  - Scope creep in data processing (NRE or per-flight-day hours)
  - Project management overhead absorption
- **Impact**: Modest overrun suggests efficient execution with minor labor adjustments

#### **Travel: UNDERSPENT**
- **Budgeted**: $25,613.00
- **Actual**: $19,423.63
- **Savings**: $6,189.37 (76% of budget, or 24% under budget)
- **Breakdown**:
  - Year One: –$3,167.36 under budget (78% of $14,636)
  - Year Two: –$3,022.01 under budget (72% of $10,977)
- **Assessment**: Significant travel savings across both years suggest:
  - Consolidated logistics or local crew base reducing travel frequency
  - Lower meal per-diem utilization than anticipated
  - Fewer lodging nights required (deployments may have been shorter or used reduced crew)
  - Reduced vehicle rental (possibly shared transportation or local resources)
- **Benefit**: Travel underruns exactly offset labor and equipment overruns, maintaining total budget alignment
