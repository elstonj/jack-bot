## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Status: **PROJECT CLOSED – FULLY EXPENDED**

#### On-Budget Performance
- **Net Position**: Project spent $148,711.63 of $148,729.00 approved budget, leaving $17.37 remaining (0.012% margin).
- **Overall Assessment**: **ON BUDGET** — The project concluded essentially at planned cost despite categorical variances.

#### Performance by Category

**Overbudget Categories:**
1. **Direct Labor**: +$2,172.00 (2.5% over)
   - Year One: $1,508.00 over
   - Year Two: $664.00 over
   - **Concern**: Modest but consistent labor overruns. May reflect additional hours for project management, flight crew operations, or data processing than initially estimated.

2. **Materials/Equipment Rental**: +$4,000.00 (11.4% over)
   - Year One: $1,000.00 over
   - Year Two: $3,000.00 over
   - **Concern**: Most significant categorical variance. Possible causes: additional aircraft rental days, higher daily rates for S2 UAS, or extended logistics/setup time not fully captured in budget assumptions.

**Underbudget Categories:**
- **Travel**: –$6,189.37 (24.2% under)
  - Year One: $3,167.36 under
  - Year Two: $3,022.01 under
  - **Positive**: Significant savings on meals, lodging, vehicle rental, and overhead. Indicates efficient cost management in field operations logistics.

#### Burn Rate & Timeline Adherence
- **Year One Burn Rate**: ~$7,320/month average over 11-month opening invoice period (08/01/21–06/30/22); accelerated in Q3–Q4 2022 with deployment-based invoicing ($15,558 → $24,508 → $21,359).
- **Year Two Burn Rate**: ~$21,461/deployment (consistent 3-deployment cycle); front-loaded in first two invoices ($22,516 + $27,993.71), with minimal close-out costs ($652.50 final invoice).
- **Timeline**: Project concluded on schedule (11/29/2023) with final invoice submitted same day. No evidence of timeline delays or cost acceleration due to schedule slippage.

#### Key Concerns & Observations
1. **Equipment Rental Cost Escalation**: The 11.4% overage in aircraft rental warrants review to understand if:
   - Daily rates increased mid-project
   - Additional unplanned flight days were required
   - Logistics or standby costs were higher than budgeted

2. **Labor Consistency**: Modest labor overruns in both years suggest either:
   - Underestimated staffing rates in original proposal
   - Scope creep in data processing or project management
   - Longer operational days than anticipated

3. **Travel Savings**: Strong travel performance (+24.2% under) may mask underlying issues (e.g., reduced crew size, shortened field campaigns) or simply reflect efficient execution. Recommend review of whether reduced travel affected data quality or scope delivery.

4. **No Cost Escalation Over 27-Month Period**: Despite spanning inflation period (2021–2023), project maintained stable cost structure. Suggests either fixed-rate subcontract or active cost management.

#### Budget Ceiling & Overrun Risk
- **Overrun Status**: **NONE** — Project concluded with minimal remaining balance ($17.37). Year Two overage ($641.99) was completely absorbed by Year One underage ($659.36).
- **No Contract Modifications Documented**: No formal Change Orders or Amendments noted in provided materials, indicating scope and budget remained stable throughout performance period.
