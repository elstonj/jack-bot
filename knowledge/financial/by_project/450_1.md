## Financial Health Assessment

### 1. **Overall Budget Performance: EXCELLENT**
- **Total Project Spend**: $148,711.63 (actual) vs. $148,729.00 (approved)
- **Net Variance**: **+$17.37 under budget** (–0.012% variance)
- **Status**: Project is **CLOSED** as of 11/29/2023. Fully expended with negligible underage.

### 2. **Category-Level Performance**

**Materials/Equipment Rental: OVERSPENT** ⚠️
- Year One: $21,000 actual vs. $20,000 budgeted (–$1,000 / –5.0%)
- Year Two: $18,000 actual vs. $15,000 budgeted (–$3,000 / –20.0%)
- **Project Total**: $39,000 actual vs. $35,000 budgeted (–$4,000 / –11.4% overage)
- **Assessment**: The largest cost driver overage. Likely reflects:
  - Additional aircraft rental days beyond 5 days per deployment
  - Potential higher daily rental rates ($1,000/day may have increased)
  - Weather delays requiring additional flight days
  - **Recommendation**: Review Year Two flight operations for scope creep; verify actual aircraft utilization against planned 5-day cycles.

**Direct Labor: MODESTLY OVERSPENT** ⚠️
- Year One: $51,860 actual vs. $50,352 budgeted (–$1,508 / –3.0%)
- Year Two: $38,428 actual vs. $37,764 budgeted (–$664 / –1.8%)
- **Project Total**: $90,288 actual vs. $88,116 budgeted (–$2,172 / –2.5% overage)
- **Assessment**: Modest overage, likely due to:
  - Additional flight crew hours for weather delays or extended operations
  - Potential labor rate increases (pilots, observers, engineers)
  - Data processing work extending beyond initial 8-hour estimates
  - **Recommendation**: Within acceptable tolerance for field operations; no corrective action needed.

**Travel: UNDERSPENT** ✓
- Year One: $11,468.64 actual vs. $14,636.00 budgeted (+$3,167.36 / +21.6% savings)
- Year Two: $7,954.99 actual vs. $10,977.00 budgeted (+$3,022.01 / +27.5% savings)
- **Project Total**: $19,423.63 actual vs. $25,613.00 budgeted (+$6,189.37 / +24.2% savings)
- **Assessment**: Significant cost savings across logistics:
  - Fewer meal days than projected (per diem savings)
  - Potentially shorter lodging stays or more efficient accommodation
  - Lower vehicle rental costs or shorter rental periods
  - Project overhead/planning less costly than budgeted
  - **Result**: These savings offset equipment and labor overages, allowing overall project to stay on budget.
