## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Project Status: **CLOSED & FULLY EXPENDED**

**Key Findings**:

1. **Budget Adherence - Overall**:
   - **Total Approved**: $148,729.00
   - **Total Invoiced/Spent**: $148,711.63
   - **Final Remaining**: $17.37
   - **Status**: Project is **essentially fully expended** at contract close-out (11/29/2023). Within $0.01% of budget.
   - **Data Source**: Drive Budget Documents (Budget Tracking, dated 2023-11-29)

2. **Category-Level Performance**:

   | Category | Performance | Variance |
   |----------|-------------|----------|
   | **Direct Labor** | **OVERSPENT** | +$2,172.00 (102.5% of budget) |
   | **Equipment/Aircraft Rental** | **OVERSPENT** | +$4,000.00 (111.4% of budget) |
   | **Travel** | **UNDERSPENT** | –$6,189.37 (75.8% of budget) |

   **Analysis**:
   - **Labor overruns** (+$2,172): Likely due to additional hours for data processing, crew coordination, or project management beyond initial estimates.
   - **Equipment overruns** (+$4,000): Aircraft rental costs exceeded budget, possibly due to extra flight days, higher daily rates, or additional deployments not originally planned.
   - **Travel savings** (–$6,189.37): Meal, lodging, and vehicle costs came in under budget—possibly fewer personnel deployed, shorter stays, or more efficient logistics.
   - **Net result**: Labor and equipment overruns were partially offset by travel savings, resulting in a net project surplus of only $17.37.
   - **Data Source**: Drive Budget Documents (Budget Tracking, Year One and Year Two variance tables)

3. **Spending by Year**:

   | Year | Approved | Actual | Variance | Status |
   |------|----------|--------|----------|--------|
   | **Year One** | $84,988.00 | $84,328.64 | +$659.36 (under) | **Within Budget** |
   | **Year Two** | $63,741.00 | $64,382.99 | –$641.99 (over) | **Over Budget** |
   | **TOTAL** | $148,729.00 | $148,711.63 | +$17.37 (under) | **On Budget** |

   - **Year One** had a modest surplus due to travel savings exceeding labor/equipment overruns.
   - **Year Two** exceeded budget by ~$642, suggesting higher equipment/labor costs in the second year (3 deployments vs. 4, but potentially at higher daily rates or with additional processing work).
   - **Data Source**: Drive Budget Documents (Budget Tracking, dated 2023-11-29)

4. **Burn Rate Analysis**:
   - **Year One Average Monthly Burn**: ~$7,320/month over 11 months (through 06/30/2022)
   - **Year Two Estimated Average**: ~$21,460/month (Year Two total $64,382.99 ÷ 3 months per deployment, or ~$10,730/month over 6-month period)
   - **Final Month (11/23)**: Minimal spend ($652.50 for project close-out)
   - **Data Source**: Drive Budget Documents (Invoice history and Budget Tracking)

5. **Key Concerns**:
   - ⚠️ **Equipment Costs Overran Significantly** (+$4,000 or 11.4%): Aircraft rental was a major cost driver. This may indicate either:
     - Underestimated daily rates in original budget
     - Additional flight days required beyond original scope
     - Fuel/maintenance surcharges not initially budgeted
     - **Recommendation**: Review aircraft rental invoices (not provided) to identify root cause for Year Two overruns.

   - ⚠️ **Year Two Overspent by $642**: The second year ran over budget despite fewer deployments (3 vs. 4). This suggests:
     - Per-deployment costs increased year-over-year
     - Longer processing timelines or additional QA/deliverable work
     - **Recommendation**: Compare Year One and Year Two deployment costs per unit to identify specific drivers.

   - ✓ **Travel Management Effective**: Despite potentially higher per-diem rates or additional personnel, travel costs came in 24.2% under budget. This indicates:
     - Efficient logistics planning
     - Reduced personnel deployment or fewer overnight stays
     - Negotiated lodging/meal rates
     - **Status**: No concern; this category performed well.

   - ✓ **Overall Project Delivery**: Project closed on time (11/29/2023) with final invoice submitted and project essentially fully expended within $17.37 of approved budget.
