## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Status: **PROJECT CLOSED — ESSENTIALLY ON BUDGET**

#### Budget Performance by Category

**1. Direct Labor — **OVERRUN: –$2,172 (102.5% of budget)**
   - Approved: $88,116.00 (Flight crew, project management, data processing)
   - Actual: $90,288.00
   - **Analysis**: Crew operations and data processing required slightly more hours than budgeted, possibly due to additional per-flight-day processing tasks or extended field operations. Overrun is modest (~2.5%) and manageable.

**2. Materials/Equipment Rental — **OVERRUN: –$4,000 (111.4% of budget)**
   - Approved: $35,000.00 (S2 Aircraft rental @ $1,000/day × 5 days × 7 deployments)
   - Actual: $39,000.00
   - **Analysis**: Represents ~$571 additional rental cost per deployment (likely additional flight days or rate increases across Year Two). This is the largest variance driver but remains within typical project contingency tolerance.

**3. Travel — **UNDERSPEND: $6,189.37 (75.8% of budget)**
   - Approved: $25,613.00 (Meals, lodging, vehicle, overhead)
   - Actual: $19,423.63
