## Key Concerns & Observations

### 1. **Equipment Rental Overrun in Year Two** (Priority: Medium)
- Year Two materials/equipment costs exceeded budget by 20% ($18,000 actual vs. $15,000 budgeted).
- While only 3 deployments were planned in Year Two (vs. 4 in Year One), the per-deployment cost appears to have increased significantly.
- **Possible causes**: Higher aircraft rental rates, extended field time per deployment, or unplanned equipment needs.
- **Mitigation**: Future projects should negotiate fixed rental rates and establish clear field day limits per deployment.

### 2. **Labor Cost Overruns** (Priority: Medium)
- Labor exceeded budget by 2.5% overall ($90,288 actual vs. $88,116 budgeted).
- This is a smaller overrun but consistent across both years, suggesting systemic underestimation of labor hours or rates.
- **Possible causes**: Extended flight operations, additional data processing complexity, crew overtime, or revised billing rates.
