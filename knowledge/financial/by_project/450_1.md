## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Project Status: ✓ **ON BUDGET & CLOSED**

The project has been **successfully completed and fully expended** as of November 29, 2023, with only $17.37 remaining from the $148,729.00 contract value. The final invoice (1595) was submitted 11/28/2023.

### By-Category Analysis:

**✓ Materials/Equipment Rental — OVERSPENT by $4,000 (111.4% of budget)**
- **Approved**: $35,000.00
- **Actual**: $39,000.00
- **Variance**: –$4,000.00 (4% overrun)
- **Assessment**: The S2 aircraft rental exceeded budget expectations, likely due to additional flight hours, extended rental periods, or operational contingencies (weather delays, repeat flights for data quality). This overrun was partially offset by underspending in Travel.

**✗ Labor — OVERSPENT by $2,172 (102.5% of budget)**
- **Approved**: $88,116.00
- **Actual**: $90,288.00
- **Variance**: –$2,172.00 (2.5% overrun)
- **Assessment**: Flight crew operations and data processing labor ran slightly over budget. This is typical for field operations where actual site conditions, team size, or processing complexity may differ from estimates. The overrun is modest and within normal project variance tolerance (~2.5%).

**✓ Travel — UNDERSPENT by $6,189 (75.8% of budget)**
- **Approved**: $25,613.00
- **Actual**: $19,423.63
- **Variance**: $6,189.37 (24.2% savings)
- **Assessment**: Significant travel savings achieved. This likely reflects:
  - Optimized logistics planning (fewer trips, consolidated transport)
  - Lower actual meal and lodging costs (negotiated rates, shorter stays)
  - Fewer or shorter vehicle rental periods than budgeted
  - Efficient crew scheduling reducing per diem exposure
- These savings directly offset the overruns in Materials/Equipment and Labor, bringing the project to near-exact budget closure.
