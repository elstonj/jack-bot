### Key Financial Observations

1. **Equipment Costs Drove Overrun**: The $4,000 equipment rental overspend (11.4% above budget) is the primary cost variance, particularly in Year Two. This suggests either:
   - Additional flight days beyond planned 5 days per deployment
   - Rate increases from aircraft provider
   - Extended or unplanned operations

2. **Labor Efficiency Slightly Negative**: Combined labor overspend of $2,172 (2.5% above budget) indicates actual crew and processing time exceeded estimates. However, the overspend is modest relative to the $88K labor budget.

3. **Travel Savings Partially Offset Overruns**: The $6,189 underspend in travel (24.2% below budget) was significant enough to nearly offset equipment and labor overruns at the project level, resulting in a net near-zero variance.

4. **Tight Final Budget Management**: The final balance of $17.37 remaining suggests the project team managed costs closely in the final quarter (Invoice 1595 at $652.50 for closeout activities) to stay within the awarded $148,729.00.

5. **Year Two Challenges**: The second year of the project saw proportionally larger variances (equipment +20% over budget, while Year One was only +5% over). This could indicate:
   - Scope changes or extended deployments in Year Two
   - Inflation in aircraft rental costs
   - Reduced scope efficiencies
