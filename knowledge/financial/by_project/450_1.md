## Key Concerns & Observations

### 1. **Equipment Rental Overrun**
The $4,000 overage in materials/equipment rental (111.4% of budget) is the primary cost deviation. Investigation should confirm:
- Were additional deployment days required beyond the budgeted 5 days per mission?
- Did aircraft rental rates increase mid-contract?
- Were there aircraft maintenance or positioning costs not initially anticipated?

### 2. **Incomplete Invoice Documentation**
Four invoices (1535, 1566, 1580, 1586) lack full invoice documents; amounts are inferred from budget tracking summaries. For audit and reconciliation purposes:
- Obtain complete invoice files with itemized charges
- Confirm invoice submission and approval dates
- Verify payment receipt and clearing

### 3. **Missing QuickBooks Data**
No QuickBooks export was provided, limiting ability to confirm:
- Actual payment dates and cash receipt timing
- Internal expense coding and allocation
- Reconciliation of invoiced amounts to company accounting records
- Any write-downs, adjustments, or disputes

### 4. **Travel Cost Underspend**
While the $6,189 travel savings is favorable, the 24.2% underspend suggests either:
- Deployments required less logistical support than budgeted, or
- Actual daily meal/lodging rates were lower than estimated
- This information should be retained for future project estimating.

### 5. **Labor Rate Verification**
Labor costs are stated at fixed rates ($174/hr for management/processing, $1,500/day for crew operations). Confirm these rates remained constant throughout the contract and that actual hours/days charged matched performance.
