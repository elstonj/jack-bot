## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Project Status: ✅ **CLOSED & ON BUDGET**

**Key Findings**:

1. **Budget Adherence — Overall**
   - Total budgeted: **$148,729.00**
   - Total spent/invoiced: **$148,711.63**
   - **Variance: $17.37 underspent (0.01%)**
   - **Assessment**: Exceptionally tight cost control. Project is essentially fully expended and closed.

2. **Year One Performance**
   - Budgeted: $84,988.00
   - Spent: $84,328.64
   - **Variance: $659.36 underspent (0.77% favorable)**
   - **Status**: Slight favorable variance driven by lower-than-budgeted travel costs, offset by minor overruns in equipment rental and labor.

3. **Year Two Performance**
   - Budgeted: $63,741.00
   - Spent: $64,382.99
   - **Variance: $641.99 overspent (1.01% unfavorable)**
   - **Status**: Minor overspend. Favorable travel savings did not fully offset equipment rental and labor overruns.

4. **Category-Level Analysis**

   **Materials/Equipment Rental** — **OVERSPENT**
   - Year One: Budgeted $20,000 → Spent $21,000 (–$1,000, 105% of budget)
   - Year Two: Budgeted $15,000 → Spent $18,000 (–$3,000, 120% of budget)
   - **Combined Variance: –$4,000 overspent (111.4% of approved)**
   - **Concern**: Aircraft rental costs escalated, likely due to increased hourly rates or extended flight hours. Year Two overspend is more significant (20% over). This suggests either scope creep in flight operations or rate increases from the aircraft rental vendor.

   **Direct Labor** — **OVERSPENT**
   - Year One: Budgeted $50,352 → Spent $51,860 (–$1,508, 102.9% of budget)
   - Year Two: Budgeted $37,764 → Spent $38,428 (–$664, 101.8% of budget)
   - **Combined Variance: –$2,172 overspent (102.5% of approved)**
   - **Concern**: Consistent, modest labor overruns across both years. This could reflect additional project management hours, overtime during deployments, or higher-than-budgeted labor rates. Year One overrun is more pronounced.

   **Travel** — **UNDERSPENT**
   - Year One: Budgeted $14,636 → Spent $11,468.64 (+$3,167.36, 78.4% of budget)
   - Year Two: Budgeted $10,977 → Spent $7,954.99 (+$3,022.01, 72.5% of budget)
   - **Combined Variance: +$6,189.37 underspent (75.8% of approved)**
   - **Status**: Favorable. Travel savings in both years (meals, lodging, vehicle rental, and logistics overhead all came in under budget). Total travel savings of $6,189 more than offset equipment and labor overruns.

5. **Burn Rate vs. Period of Performance**
   - **Total Duration**: 27 months (August 1, 2021 – November 29, 2023)
   - **Average Monthly Burn**: $148,711.63 ÷ 27 months = **$5,507/month**
   - **Spending Pattern**:
     - Year One: Invoiced $80,545 over ~12 months = ~$6,712/month (higher intensity due to 4 deployments)
     - Year Two: Invoiced $64,382.99 over ~12 months = ~$5,365/month (lower intensity due to 3 deployments + project winding down)
     - **Close-out (Final 36 days)**: $652.50 (minimal activity)
   - **Assessment**: Burn rate tracked predictably with deployment schedule. No unexpected acceleration or deceleration.

6. **Cash Flow & Payment Status**
   - **Data Available**: Invoice amounts and submission dates from Budget Documents
   - **Data Not Available**: Actual payment receipt dates and QuickBooks account reconciliation
   - **Risk**: Without QB data, cannot confirm whether University of Colorado has paid invoices 1521, 1530, 1535, 1566, 1580, 1586 on time. Invoice 1514 was "Signed 07/01/2022," suggesting approval, but payment timing is unknown.
   - **Note**: Invoice submission dates suggest invoices may lag 4–6 weeks behind period-covered dates (e.g., Invoice 1530 submitted 09/15 for 08/01–09/15 period).

7. **Key Concerns & Observations**

   ✅ **Positive**:
   - Project completed within overall contracted budget (variance <0.02%)
   - Travel cost management was excellent (24% under budget across both years)
   - No documented cost overruns requiring contract modifications or scope reductions
   - Final invoice submitted on schedule (11/28/2023 for 11/29/2023 PoP end)

   ⚠️ **Areas of Attention**:
   - **Equipment rental cost escalation**: Year Two overrun of 20% suggests either higher rates, additional flight hours, or procurement changes. Recommend review of flight operations logs vs. aircraft rental invoices.
