#### ✓ Strengths
1. **Overall Budget Discipline**: Project completed at 100.01% of contracted amount (essentially fully expended with minimal variance).
2. **Travel Cost Management**: Significant underspend on travel ($6,189 under) demonstrates strong logistical efficiency.
3. **Final Invoice Timing**: Invoice 1595 (Final, $652.50) submitted on schedule (11/29/2023), indicating prompt project closeout.
4. **No Cost Overruns Requiring CU Boulder Change Orders**: Project remained within total contract value throughout 27-month period.

#### ⚠️ Areas of Concern
1. **Materials/Equipment Rental Overspend**: Category exceeded budget by $4,000 (11.4%) over two years.
   - **Year One**: $1,000 over (5% variance) – manageable
   - **Year Two**: $3,000 over (20% variance) – significant; likely due to higher per-day costs despite reduced deployment count
   - **Root Cause (unconfirmed from available data)**: Possible explanations include aircraft maintenance costs, standby/mobilization fees, fuel surcharges, or longer-than-planned flight operations per deployment

2. **Labor Overages**: Combined labor overspend of $2,172 (2.5%) across both years suggests:
   - Slightly longer deployment operations than initially budgeted (additional flight hours or data processing time)
   - This is within typical project variance and acceptable for field operations

3. **Deployment Scope Reduction**: Year Two budgeted for 3 deployments vs. Year One's 4, suggesting potential scope change or revised client priorities mid-project. However, this was captured in the Year Two budget and did not require contract modification.
