## Financial Health Assessment

### **Overall Budget Performance: EXCELLENT**

✅ **Project Status**: Fully expended and **on budget**. Total invoiced ($148,711.63) vs. approved budget ($148,729.00) = **$17.37 remaining (0.01% variance)**. Project closed 11/29/2023.

---

### **By-Category Performance**

#### **Materials/Equipment Rental: OVERSPENT**
- **Approved**: $35,000.00
- **Actual**: $39,000.00
- **Variance**: –$4,000.00 (11.4% over)
- **Analysis**: Equipment rental exceeded budget by $4,000. This primarily reflects S2 aircraft rental costs. Possible causes: additional flight days required, increased daily rates, or mission scope expansion. Year One overran by $1,000; Year Two by $3,000.

#### **Labor: SLIGHTLY OVERSPENT**
- **Approved**: $88,116.00
- **Actual**: $90,288.00
- **Variance**: –$2,172.00 (2.5% over)
- **Analysis**: Labor costs exceeded budget by $2,172, representing a minimal overage. This includes project management, flight crew operations, and data processing. Year One overran by $1,508; Year Two by $664. Likely due to additional hours for mission planning, crew time, or data processing.

#### **Travel: UNDERSPENT**
- **Approved**: $25,613.00
- **Actual**: $19,423.63
- **Variance**: +$6,189.37 (24.2% under)
- **Analysis**: Travel costs came in significantly under budget, saving $6,189.37. This reflects lower-than-expected meal per diems, lodging, and vehicle rental costs. Year One saved $3,167.36; Year Two saved $3,022.01. Possible reasons: fewer deployment days than planned, lower lodging rates negotiated, or reduced crew size per deployment.

---

### **Key Financial Concerns**

⚠️ **Minor Concerns Identified**:

1. **Equipment Rental Overages**: Materials/equipment rental exceeded budget by $4,000 (11.4% over). Year Two saw a $3,000 overage, suggesting either higher aircraft daily rates, additional flight days, or mission scope creep in the second year.

2. **Labor Overages Across Both Years**: While modest (2.5% total), labor costs were consistently over budget in both Year One and Year Two, suggesting hourly labor estimates may have been conservative or mission complexity required additional crew/processing time.

3. **Invoice Documentation Gaps**: Four of eight invoices (1535, 1566, 1580, 1586) lack full supporting documentation—only amounts are tracked in the Budget Tracking spreadsheet. This creates a minor record-keeping/audit trail concern, though amounts are confirmed.

---

### **Overall Assessment**

✅ **Project is financially healthy**:
- Approved budget fully utilized with negligible overage ($17.37 remaining).
- Equipment and labor overages were offset by substantial travel savings ($6,189.37).
- Project closed on schedule (11/29/2023, end of contract period).
- No evidence of cost overruns, payment delays, or budget ceiling violations.
- The $4,000 equipment overages and $2,172 labor overages are modest within the context of a $148,729 project and reflect operational adjustments rather than systemic budget problems.

**Recommendation**: No corrective action needed. Project successfully delivered on budget. For future projects, recommend:
- Tighter estimation of equipment rental daily rates and required flight days.
- More detailed labor hour tracking to identify whether overages reflect scope changes or estimation errors.
- Complete invoice documentation for all line items to support audit compliance.
