### Category-Level Analysis

**Materials/Equipment Rental**
- **Status**: 🔴 **OVERSPENT** across both years
- **Details**:
  - Year One: $20,000 budgeted → $21,000 actual (–$1,000 over)
  - Year Two: $15,000 budgeted → $18,000 actual (–$3,000 over)
  - **Combined**: $35,000 budgeted → $39,000 actual (–$4,000 over, 111.4% of budget)
- **Likely Cause**: S2 Aircraft rental costs exceeded per-deployment estimates; possible additional flight days or higher daily rental rates.
- **Assessment**: Significant overrun in equipment costs, offset by travel savings.

**Direct Labor**
- **Status**: 🔴 **OVERSPENT** across both years
- **Details**:
  - Year One: $50,352 budgeted → $51,860 actual (–$1,508 over)
  - Year Two: $37,764 budgeted → $38,428 actual (–$664 over)
  - **Combined**: $88,116 budgeted → $90,288 actual (–$2,172 over, 102.5% of budget)
- **Likely Cause**: Additional flight crew operations or higher billable hours for project management and data processing than estimated.
- **Assessment**: Modest overrun; within typical contingency margins for field operations.

**Travel**
- **Status**: 🟢 **UNDERSPENT** across both years
- **Details**:
  - Year One: $14,636 budgeted → $11,468.64 actual ($3,167.36 under)
  - Year Two: $10,977 budgeted → $7,954.99 actual ($3,022.01 under)
  - **Combined**: $25,613 budgeted → $19,423.63 actual ($6,189.37 under, 75.8% of budget)
- **Likely Cause**: Reduced per diem, lodging, meal, or vehicle rental expenses; possibly efficiency gains or fewer personnel deployed per trip.
- **Assessment**: Significant travel cost savings; budget conservative on per diem and lodging assumptions.
