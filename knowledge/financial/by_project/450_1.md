## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Performance
✅ **Project is on budget overall** with minimal variance ($17.37 remaining out of $148,729.00 budgeted = 0.01% variance).

### Category-by-Category Analysis

**1. Materials/Equipment Rental (S2 Aircraft Rental)**
- ⚠️ **OVERSPENT**: $4,000.00 over budget (11.4% overrun)
- Budgeted: $35,000.00 | Actual: $39,000.00
- **Issue**: Higher costs or additional flight days than budgeted. The original budget assumed $1,000/day × 5 days per deployment, but actual rental costs exceeded this rate or additional days were required.
- **Impact**: Significant category overspend, partially offset by travel underspend.

**2. Direct Labor**
- ⚠️ **OVERSPENT**: $2,172.00 over budget (2.5% overrun)
- Budgeted: $88,116.00 | Actual: $90,288.00
- **Issue**: Likely due to additional hours for project management, flight crew operations, or data processing. The $1,500/day flight crew rate or additional deployment days may have driven overages.
- **Impact**: Moderate overspend; relatively contained.

**3. Travel**
- ✅ **UNDERSPENT**: $6,189.37 under budget (24.2% savings)
- Budgeted: $25,613.00 | Actual: $19,423.63
- **Issue**: Meals, lodging, and vehicle rental were lower than anticipated. Possible cost controls or efficiency in logistics.
- **Impact**: Positive variance offsetting equipment and labor overages.
