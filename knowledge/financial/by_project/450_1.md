**Project 450-1 successfully closed on November 29, 2023, with total spending of $148,729.00 matching the contracted award value exactly.** While individual cost categories showed variances (labor +$2,172, equipment +$4,000, travel –$6,189), the project remained on budget overall due to travel savings offsetting other overruns.
