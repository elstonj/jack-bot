### Category-by-Category Performance

#### ✅ **Direct Labor: +2.5% Overrun (Controlled)**
- **Budgeted**: $88,116.00
- **Actual**: $90,288.00
- **Variance**: –$2,172.00 (overrun)

**Analysis**: Labor costs exceeded budget by ~$2,172, primarily in flight crew operations ($1,500/day) and data processing. This modest 2.5% overrun is typical for field operations where weather delays or additional survey requirements can extend crew days. The overrun was offset by travel savings (see below).

---

#### ⚠️ **Materials & Equipment Rental: +11.4% Overrun (Significant)**
- **Budgeted**: $35,000.00
- **Actual**: $39,000.00
- **Variance**: –$4,000.00 (overrun)

**Analysis**: Equipment rental costs (primarily S2 aircraft at $1,000/day) exceeded budget by $4,000, an 11.4% overrun. Possible causes:
- Additional unplanned flight days (weather make-ups, extended survey areas)
- Rate increases during Year Two operations
- Equipment repositioning or standby fees

This is the largest single cost driver variance. However, it was fully offset by travel savings, so total project remained on budget.

---

#### ✅ **Travel: –24.2% Underspend (Favorable)**
- **Budgeted**: $25,613.00
- **Actual**: $19,423.63
- **Variance**: +$6,189.37 (savings)

**Analysis**: Travel costs (meals, lodging, vehicle rental, overhead) came in 24.2% under budget, generating $6,189.37 in savings. Probable causes:
- Actual per diem and lodging rates lower than budgeted ($76/day for meals and $175/night for lodging may have been conservative)
- Fewer than planned travel instances or shorter deployment stays
- Team efficiency reducing logistics overhead

This favorable variance fully compensated for labor and equipment overruns, resulting in near-perfect overall budget adherence.
