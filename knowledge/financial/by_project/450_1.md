  - Year One: Overran by $1,508 (labor rates or hours higher than planned for project management, flight crew, or data processing)

  - **Root cause**: Labor rate escalation, additional technical labor for data processing, or extended crew days may have exceeded hourly/daily estimates.

#### ✅ **Travel – UNDERSPENT**

- **Variance**: +$6,189.37 (24.2% under)

  - Year Two: Underspent by $3,022.01 (continued cost management; fewer crew days or better negotiated rates)
