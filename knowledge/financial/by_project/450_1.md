## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Performance
✅ **Project is essentially on budget overall**, with a remaining balance of only **$17.37** against a $148,729.00 contract value.

- **Year One**: Slightly underspent by $659.36 (0.77% under budget)
- **Year Two**: Slightly overspent by $641.99 (1.01% over budget)
- **Net variance**: $17.37 underspent (0.01% of total budget)

### Category-Level Analysis

| Category | Spend % | Status | Assessment |
|----------|---------|--------|------------|
| **Equipment Rental (S2 Aircraft)** | 111.4% | ⚠️ OVERSPENT | $4,000 over budget ($35K approved → $39K actual). Represents +5.9% increase; likely due to additional flight hours or higher daily rates than anticipated. |
| **Direct Labor** | 102.5% | ⚠️ OVERSPENT | $2,172 over budget ($88.1K approved → $90.3K actual). Represents +2.5% increase; likely due to additional crew hours or higher billing rates. |
| **Travel** | 75.8% | ✅ UNDERSPENT | $6,189 under budget ($25.6K approved → $19.4K actual). Represents −24.2% savings; actual meal, lodging, and vehicle costs were lower than budgeted per-diem rates and quantities. |

### Burn Rate & Timeline

- **Project Duration**: 27 months (Aug 1, 2021 – Nov 29, 2023)
- **Total Budget**: $148,729.00
- **Average Monthly Burn**: ~$5,512/month
- **Year One Monthly Average** (~11 months): ~$7,666/month
- **Year Two + Closeout Monthly Average** (~15 months): ~$4,292/month

The higher Year One burn rate reflects four (4) deployments; Year Two had lower burn rate with three (3) deployments plus extended post-project data processing.

### Key Findings

**Concerns:**
1. **Equipment Rental Overrun** — Aircraft rental exceeded budget by $4,000 (11.4%). This is the largest variance category and may indicate scope creep (additional flights/hours) or rate changes not captured in the original budget.
2. **Labor Overrun** — Labor exceeded budget by $2,172 (2.5%). Within acceptable variance but worth monitoring for future similar projects.
3. **Invoice Documentation Gap** — Four invoices (1535, 1566, 1580, 1586) lack detailed invoice documents; amounts inferred from budget tracking. Future projects should maintain full documentation trail.

**Positive Indicators:**
1. **Travel Savings** — $6,189 (24.2%) under budget suggests efficient logistics and better-than-expected per diem management.
2. **Overall Compliance** — Project spent exactly to contract value with minimal overage ($17.37 remaining), demonstrating strong cost control.
3. **On-Time Close-out** — Final invoice submitted on 11/29/2023, aligned with contract end date (11/29/2023). No delay charges or extension costs.
4. **No Payment Delays** — All invoices processed; no indication of late payment or cash flow issues.

### Recommendations for Future Similar Projects
- Clarify aircraft rental rate escalation clauses (budget assumed flat $1,000/day; actual may have varied by season or fuel costs).
- Document actual crew hours separately from budgeted assumptions to isolate labor variance sources.
- Continue leveraging travel efficiency; actual lodging/meal costs came in significantly below per-diem budgets.
