## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Performance
✅ **Project On Budget**: Total invoiced ($148,711.63) is within approved contract value ($148,729.00) by $17.37. Project closed successfully with minimal variance.

### Category Performance Analysis

| Category | Status | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|
| **Direct Labor** | ⚠️ Over Budget (+2.5%) | $90,288.00 actual vs. $88,116.00 budgeted. Approximately $2,172 overspent. Likely due to extended data processing hours or additional project management labor. |
| **Equipment Rental (Aircraft)** | ⚠️ Over Budget (+11.4%) | $39,000.00 actual vs. $35,000.00 budgeted. $4,000 overspent across both years (Year One: –$1,000; Year Two: –$3,000). Possible causes: extended rental periods, additional flight days, or rate increases. |
| **Travel** | ✅ Under Budget (–24.2%) | $19,423.63 actual vs. $25,613.00 budgeted. $6,189.37 underspent. Travel costs came in significantly under target, possibly due to optimized logistics or consolidation of trips. |

### Year-by-Year Comparison

**Year One Performance**:
- Budgeted: $84,988.00
- Actual: $84,328.64
- **Variance**: +$659.36 (under budget by 0.77%)
- **Status**: ✅ Well controlled; all categories close to plan except travel (under by $3,167.36) offset by modest overages in rental and labor.

**Year Two Performance**:
- Budgeted: $63,741.00
- Actual: $64,382.99
- **Variance**: –$641.99 (over budget by 1.01%)
- **Status**: ⚠️ Slight overrun, primarily driven by equipment rental (–$3,000) and labor (–$664), offset partially by travel savings (–$3,022.01).

### Burn Rate & Spending Rhythm

**Year One Burn Rate** (Invoices 1514–1535, ~11 months):
- Average monthly spending: ~$7,665/month
- Pattern: Front-loaded setup (June invoice $15,558), then steady deployment invoicing (July–October 2022)
- No concerning acceleration or delay in spending rate.

**Year Two Burn Rate** (Invoices 1566–1595, ~13 months):
- Average monthly spending: ~$4,952/month (lower than Year One, reflects fewer deployments: 3 vs. 4)
- Pattern: Evenly distributed invoicing; final closeout invoice ($652.50) minimal, indicating clean wrap-up.

### Key Financial Observations

1. **No Cost Overruns at Project Level**: Despite category-level overages (Labor +$2,172, Equipment +$4,000), Year One savings in travel ($3,167.36) and cross-year reconciliation kept total project within budget.

2. **Equipment Rental Pressures**: Aircraft rental consistently exceeded budget (–$4,000 total), suggesting either:
   - Higher-than-planned rental daily rates
   - Extended flight hours or additional deployment days
   - Inflation or market adjustments mid-contract
   - *Recommendation: Review actual rental invoices and flight logs to understand drivers.*

3. **Labor Efficiency Concern**: Direct labor exceeded budget by $2,172 (2.5% overrun), distributed across both years. Given the deployment-based structure, this may indicate:
   - Additional data processing work post-deployment
   - Extended project management overhead
   - *Recommendation: Verify labor allocation against scope changes or discoveries.*

4. **Travel Discipline**: Substantial travel savings ($6,189.37, or 24.2% under budget) suggest strong cost control in logistics, meals, and lodging. This offset pressures in other categories.

5. **No Late Payments or Cash Flow Issues Identified**: All invoices submitted on schedule; no evidence of payment delays in provided documents. Project closure invoice (1595) submitted same day as final PoP date (11/29/2023).

6. **Project Fully Expended**: With only $17.37 remaining from $148,729.00 approved, project executed to near-exact budget. Final invoice (1595, $652.50) represents minimal administrative closeout.
