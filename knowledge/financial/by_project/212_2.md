1. **Budget Overage – Nose Cone Kit ($1,947)**

   - This represents a scope creep or change order that was not reflected in the initial Asana project dollar value

2. **Timeline Slippage**
   - Official project due date: September 30, 2026
   - Actual completion: October 27, 2026 (**27-day delay**)

   - Despite timeline slip, all deliverables were ultimately completed

3. **Cost by Phase**
   - **Invoice #1789 ($89,844):** Main S2 UAS system — aligns with approved contract value
   - **Invoice #1790 ($1,947):** Additional nose cone kit — **unapproved scope/cost overrun**

4. **Burn Rate & Remaining Period**
   - Project is 100% invoiced as of October 12, 2026 (13 days past official due date)
   - No remaining budget available; project exceeds approved amount
   - Outstanding balance of $1,947 over contract ceiling requires resolution (change order, internal absorption, or customer approval)

5. **Payment Status**
   - Both invoices marked as "Outstanding" in QuickBooks (Invoice #1789 & #1790, dated 2026-10-12)
   - **Data not available:** Whether NASA has paid these invoices or if they remain receivables

6. **Category-Level Risks**
   - Unable to assess labor vs. materials vs. indirect cost variances due to lack of detailed cost accounting data
   - All costs appear embedded in hardware/system line items

### **Recommended Actions:**

1. **Clarify Nose Cone Kit Authorization:** Determine if Invoice #1790 ($1,947) was a change order or unapproved scope addition. If unapproved, negotiate resolution with NASA (bill modification, credit, or formal change order).

2. **Verify Payment:** Confirm payment status of Invoices #1789 & #1790 in NASA accounting system or receivables aging report.

3. **Document Timeline Slip:** Investigate root cause of 4-month launcher refurbishment delay and 27-day overall project delay for lessons learned and future scheduling.
