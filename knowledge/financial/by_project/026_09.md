# Project 026-09 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [550-1] Navy SBIR Magnetometer
- **Client/Agency:** Department of the Navy (Small Business Innovation Research Program)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I (with Option Period); Phase II negotiation underway
- **Period of Performance:** Phase I Option Period began April 14, 2026; current open work extends through March 2027
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Magnetic anomaly detection (MAD) and passive acoustic sensing integration into a small unmanned aerial system (SL UAS). Work includes design of magnetometer sensor onboarding and logging, build and ground test of S0-MAD hand-launched platform, S0-AD launcher system design and test, field validation at Camp Pendleton (completed Sept 2026), and collaboration with subcontractors (Cetacean Research Technology, QuSpin). Phase II work planned to include integration with UK Merlin Helicopter Launch System (Lockheed Martin UK / Royal Navy "Project RAVEN").
