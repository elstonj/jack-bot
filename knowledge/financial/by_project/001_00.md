# Project 001-00 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Coordinated UAS-Based Plume Tracking (DTRA PLUMES)
- **Client/Agency:** Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I Proposal
- **Program/Solicitation:** DTRA252-001 PLUMES; Proposal Number T252-001-0052
- **Period of Performance:** 7 months (proposal date: May 21, 2025)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development of AI-integrated, autonomous UAS system combining Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) and dynamic scene reconstruction algorithms with BST's fixed-wing UAS platforms and SwiftCore™ Flight Management System to enable real-time detection, tracking, and 3D volumetric reconstruction of airborne plumes during explosive tests. Phase I will establish technical feasibility through controlled laboratory experiments and field demonstrations.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**From Proposal Document (DTRA PLUMES Technical Volume & Budget Justification):**

The 7-month Phase I effort includes the following planned deliverables and milestones:

1. **Laboratory Experiments & Feasibility Studies** — Controlled validation of Neural Radiance Field reconstruction algorithms on plume imagery
2. **Field Demonstrations** — Real-world testing of coordinated drone-based plume tracking system on airborne test scenarios
3. **Performance Requirements Documentation** — Detailed technical specifications for Phase II prototyping
4. **Software Infrastructure & Flight Planning Implementation** — Integration of neural volumetric reconstruction with BST's SwiftCore™ avionics and autonomous flight control
5. **Sensor Integration on UAS Platforms** — Implementation of EO/IR cameras, GPS/IMU telemetry, and environmental sensors on S2 UAS and other BST platforms
6. **Final Phase I Report** — Technical summary demonstrating feasibility and recommending Phase II approach
