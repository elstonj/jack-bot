## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Technical Volume & Budget Justification (DTRA PLUMES Proposal)**

The proposal outlines a 7-month Phase I effort with the following expected outcomes:

1. **Technical Feasibility Demonstration**
   - Controlled laboratory experiments validating NeRF-based plume reconstruction
   - Field demonstrations in operationally relevant conditions

2. **Software Infrastructure**
   - Neural volumetric reconstruction algorithms (NeRF, K-Planes, Gaussian Splatting)
   - Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) for enhanced accuracy
   - Integration with BST SwiftCore™ Flight Management System

3. **Flight Planning & Autonomous Control**
