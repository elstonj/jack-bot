## Financial Health Assessment

### Status: **PROPOSAL STAGE – NO CONTRACT YET AWARDED**

**Key Findings:**

1. **Proposed Budget Feasibility:**
   - Total Phase I cost: **$124,267.75** (Personnel + Fringe + BNL Subcontract)
   - Budget structure is detailed and justified with specific roles and effort allocations
   - Subcontract with BNL ($66,000) represents 53% of total budget—key technical risk

2. **Current Financial Position:**
   - **No invoicing or expenses yet** — proposal submitted May 21, 2025; contract status unknown
   - All $124,267.75 remains available (pending award)
   - Zero burn rate to date

3. **Budget by Category Assessment:**
   - **Direct Labor (36% of budget):** Two key personnel (PI + System Engineer) driving technical work; rates ($98.56/hr, $84.13/hr) appear reasonable for senior aerospace engineering roles
   - **Fringe Benefits (11% of budget):** 29.28% rate is standard for tech firm benefits
   - **Subcontract (53% of budget):** Critical path item; BNL provides NeRF algorithm development (core innovation). 1.51 person-months of effort at $66K implies ~$43.7K/person-month—moderate for National Lab collaboration
   - **Indirect Costs:** Not separately itemized; may be burdened into labor rates or subcontract

4. **Risks & Concerns:**
   - **Contract Award Timing:** Proposal submitted May 2025; no award letter, PWS, or contract in provided data. DTRA typically awards Phase I within 4–6 months of submission.
   - **Subcontractor Dependency:** BNL responsible for core NeRF algorithm; delays or cost overruns here directly impact project feasibility.
   - **Unspecified Costs:** Equipment procurement (UAS platforms, sensors, computing infrastructure for NeRF processing) not itemized in budget detail. May be absorbed by BNL or assumes BST has equipment on hand.
   - **Travel Budget:** Field demonstrations mentioned but travel costs not separately budgeted (may be embedded in labor or assumed minimal).

5. **Financial Health Summary:**
   - ✅ **Well-justified Phase I budget with clear role definitions**
   - ✅ **Realistic labor rates and effort allocations**
   - ⚠️ **Heavy reliance on subcontractor for core technical innovation**
   - ⚠️ **No contract award confirmation or invoicing timeline**
   - ⚠️ **Potential hidden costs in equipment or travel not itemized**

### Recommendations:
- **Confirm DTRA contract award status** before planning execution
- **Establish detailed spend plan and monthly burn targets** once awarded
- **Monitor BNL subcontract execution** closely; implement milestone-based invoicing
- **Track actual vs. proposed labor hours** against the 250 hrs (Elston) and 243.85 hrs (Stachura) allocated
- **Reserve contingency** for Phase II prototyping scope creep if Phase I results are promising
