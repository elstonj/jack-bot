# Project 001-00 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Project Name:** Coordinated UAS-Based Plume Tracking
- **Client/Agency:** Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I Proposal
- **Solicitation:** DTRA252-001 PLUMES; Proposal Number T252-001-0052
- **Proposed Period of Performance:** 7 months (Phase I)
- **Submission Date:** May 21, 2025
- **Brief Scope:** Development of AI-integrated, autonomous UAS system combining Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) and dynamic scene reconstruction algorithms with BST's fixed-wing UAS platforms and SwiftCore™ Flight Management System to enable real-time detection, tracking, and 3D volumetric reconstruction of airborne plumes during explosive tests. Phase I establishes technical feasibility through controlled laboratory experiments and field demonstrations, culminating in performance requirements and software infrastructure for Phase II prototyping.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones (Proposed Phase I)

**Source:** Technical Volume and Budget Justification, DTRA PLUMES Proposal

### Phase I Deliverables
1. **Technical Feasibility Demonstration**
   - Controlled laboratory experiments validating Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) and dynamic scene reconstruction algorithms on simulated plume data
   - Field demonstration of autonomous plume tracking with real-world environmental conditions

2. **Performance Requirements Document**
   - Specification of system accuracy, latency, and autonomous flight control requirements for plume tracking
   - Sensor and software performance baselines

3. **Phase II Software Infrastructure**
   - Development of modular software architecture integrating:
     - NeRF-based volumetric reconstruction (K-Planes, HexPlane, Dex-NeRF extensions)
     - Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs)
     - Gaussian Splatting and NeRFPlayer techniques for near-real-time reconstruction
   - Integration with BST's SwiftCore™ Flight Management System for closed-loop autonomous flight control

4. **Final Report**
   - Summary of Phase I findings, technical feasibility assessment, and recommendations for Phase II

### Systems & Platforms Referenced
- **S2 UAS** (Unmanned Aerial System) — primary platform for plume tracking
- **SwiftCore™ Flight Management System** — autonomous flight control integration
- **SwiftTab, SwiftStation** — ground control and data processing components
- **Multi-sensor payload:** EO/IR cameras, GPS/IMU telemetry, environmental sensors

### Financial Implications
- Phase I budget of $124,267.75 is fixed; successful Phase I completion is a gate for Phase II funding
- Phase II funding amount is not specified in provided documents but will be requested based on Phase I deliverable completion
- Key cost driver: Brookhaven National Laboratory subcontract ($66,000, 53% of Phase I budget) for neural volumetric reconstruction algorithm development
