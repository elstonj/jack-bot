# Project 001-00 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Full Project Name:** Coordinated UAS-Based Plume Tracking (DTRA PLUMES)

**Client/Agency:** Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I Proposal

**Solicitation:** DTRA 2025, DTRA252-001 PLUMES; Proposal Number T252-001-0052

**Proposal Submission Date:** May 21, 2025

**Period of Performance:** 7 months (proposed, from Budget Justification)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Black Swift Technologies proposes to develop AI-integrated, autonomous UAS capabilities for real-time detection, tracking, and 3D volumetric reconstruction of airborne plumes from explosive tests. The effort combines Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) and dynamic scene reconstruction with BST's fixed-wing UAS platforms and SwiftCore™ avionics. Phase I will establish technical feasibility through controlled laboratory experiments and field demonstrations. Key collaboration partner: Brookhaven National Laboratory (BNL).
