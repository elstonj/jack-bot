# Project 001-00 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Coordinated UAS-Based Plume Tracking  
- **Client/Agency:** Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA)  
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I Proposal  
- **Program/Solicitation:** DTRA 2025 (DTRA252-001 PLUMES; Proposal Number T252-001-0052)  
- **Period of Performance:** 7 months (Phase I)  
- **Proposal Submission Date:** May 21, 2025  
- **Brief Description:** AI-integrated autonomous UAS system for real-time detection, tracking, and 3D volumetric reconstruction of airborne plumes using Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) and BST's fixed-wing UAS platforms (S2 UAS with SwiftCore™ Flight Management System). Phase I establishes technical feasibility through controlled laboratory experiments and field demonstrations.

- **Project is not yet active.** Proposal submitted May 21, 2025; contract award status is unknown.

### Budget Assessment (Upon Award):

  - Personnel: $45,070.97 (36% of budget)
  - Fringe: $13,196.78 (11% of budget)
  - Subcontract (BNL): $66,000.00 (53% of budget)

- **Key Budget Observations:**
  - **High subcontract dependency:** 53% of Phase I budget flows to Brookhaven NL for algorithm development and neural volumetric reconstruction software. Success depends on external partner delivery.
  - **Moderate direct labor:** Only 250 hours (Dr. Elston, PI) and 244 hours (Dr. Stachura, SE) planned. Indicates focused, time-limited effort focused on program management and integration, not primary development.
  - **No contingency or indirect costs identified** in the budget justification provided. Unclear if overhead/G&A is bundled or separately quoted.
  - **No equipment or materials budget** noted. Plume tracking requires cameras, sensors, UAS platforms, and field equipment; costs may be absorbed elsewhere or assumed available from BST inventory.

### Risks & Concerns:
1. **Proposal maturity unknown.** No confirmation of DTRA acceptance, funding level, or award status.
2. **BNL performance risk.** Large portion of Phase I ($66K) depends on external lab execution. Delays in BNL deliverables could impact overall schedule and budget.
3. **Scope ambiguity.** Budget justification mentions "field demonstration with controlled plume release" and "field operations," but no field operations costs (travel, rental, permissions, venue) are itemized in the budget; these may be missing or deferred.
4. **Phase II funding dependent.** Proposal describes Phase II as a larger prototyping and integration effort, but budget is Phase I only. Phase II costs unknown.

### Recommendations:
- **Await contract award notification** and official Period of Performance (PoP) and funded budget from DTRA.
- **Formalize subcontract with BNL** with clear milestones, deliverables, and payment schedule.
- **Establish detailed project cost control** in Asana and QuickBooks once active, mapping labor hours and BNL invoicing to Phase I budget line items.
- **Clarify field operations costs.** If not included in current budget, request budget modification or identify internal cost-sharing.
