# Project 001-00 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Name:** Coordinated UAS-Based Plume Tracking  
**Client/Agency:** Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA)  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I  
**Program/Solicitation:** DTRA252-001 PLUMES; Proposal Number T252-001-0052  
**Submission Date:** May 21, 2025  
**Period of Performance:** 7 months (proposed)  

**Scope Description:**  
Development of AI-integrated autonomous UAS system for real-time detection, tracking, and 3D volumetric reconstruction of airborne plumes during explosive tests. System combines Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) and dynamic scene reconstruction algorithms with BST's fixed-wing UAS platforms (S2 UAS) and SwiftCore™ Flight Management System. Phase I establishes technical feasibility through controlled laboratory experiments and field demonstrations, with deliverables including performance requirements and software infrastructure for Phase II prototyping. Collaboration with Brookhaven National Laboratory (BNL) for neural volumetric capture software development.
