## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source:** Technical Volume and Budget Justification (DTRA PLUMES, T252-001-0052)

### Phase I Objectives (7-month effort):
1. **Controlled Laboratory Experiments** – Validation of NeRF-based plume reconstruction algorithms in controlled settings
2. **Field Demonstrations** – Real-world testing of autonomous plume tracking capabilities
3. **Performance Requirements Documentation** – Definition of system specifications for Phase II
4. **Software Infrastructure Development** – Integration of neural volumetric reconstruction with SwiftCore™ avionics and flight control systems
5. **Technical Feasibility Report** – Comprehensive assessment of approach viability

### Key Technologies/Deliverables:
- Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF) implementation for semi-transparent volume reconstruction
- Multi-sensor data fusion platform (EO/IR cameras, GPS/IMU, environmental sensors)
- SwiftCore™ integration for closed-loop autonomous flight control
- Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) implementation
- Gaussian Splatting and NeRFPlayer techniques for near-real-time reconstruction
- Modular, open-architecture flight planning interface
