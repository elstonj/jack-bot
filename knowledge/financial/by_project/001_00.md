# Project 001-00 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** Coordinated UAS-Based Plume Tracking (DTRA PLUMES)  
**Client/Agency:** Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA)  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I Proposal  
**Program/Solicitation:** DTRA252-001 PLUMES; Proposal Number T252-001-0052  
**Period of Performance:** 7 months (proposal submitted May 21, 2025)  
**Brief Description:** AI-integrated autonomous UAS system for real-time detection, tracking, and 3D volumetric reconstruction of airborne plumes using Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) and BST's SwiftCore™ Flight Management System. Phase I establishes technical feasibility through controlled laboratory experiments and field demonstrations in collaboration with Brookhaven National Laboratory.
