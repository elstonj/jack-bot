# Project 001-00 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Project Name:** Coordinated UAS-Based Plume Tracking (DTRA PLUMES)
- **Client/Agency:** Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I Proposal
- **Solicitation:** DTRA 2025-001 PLUMES; Proposal Number T252-001-0052
- **Period of Performance:** 7 months (proposed, as of May 2025 submission date)
- **Brief Description:** Development of an AI-integrated, autonomous UAS system for real-time detection, tracking, and 3D volumetric reconstruction of airborne plumes during explosive tests. Core innovation applies Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) and dynamic scene reconstruction algorithms to BST's fixed-wing UAS platforms and SwiftCore™ avionics. Collaboration with Brookhaven National Laboratory on software development, flight planning, and sensor integration.
