## Key Deliverables & Milestones
*Source: Technical Volume Proposal, DTRA PLUMES*

**Phase I Focus Areas (7-month effort):**
1. **Neural Volumetric Reconstruction Development**
   - Implement NeRF-based algorithms and extensions (K-Planes, HexPlane, Dex-NeRF) for dynamic, semi-transparent scene reconstruction
   - Develop physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) for enhanced accuracy
   - Integrate Gaussian Splatting and NeRFPlayer techniques for near-real-time reconstruction

2. **Multi-Sensor Data Fusion Platform**
   - Integrate EO/IR cameras, GPS/IMU telemetry, and environmental sensors
   - Develop sensor calibration and time-synchronization protocols

3. **SwiftCore™ Integration**
   - Closed-loop autonomous flight control based on plume reconstruction
   - Flight planning implementation on BST's S2 UAS platform

4. **Controlled Laboratory Experiments & Field Demonstrations**
   - Validate plume reconstruction in controlled settings
   - Conduct field tests in operationally relevant conditions

5. **Performance Requirements & Phase II Infrastructure**
   - Document technical feasibility results
   - Establish software infrastructure and requirements for Phase II prototyping

**Financial Implications:** Phase I is estimated to consume the full $124,267.75 proposed budget over 7 months. Phase II funding and scope will depend on Phase I results and DTRA down-select decisions.
