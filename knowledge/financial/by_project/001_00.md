# Project 001-00 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Coordinated UAS-Based Plume Tracking (DTRA PLUMES)
- **Client/Agency:** Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I Proposal
- **Program/Solicitation:** DTRA 2025-001 PLUMES; Proposal Number T252-001-0052
- **Period of Performance:** 7 months (proposal submitted May 21, 2025)
- **Brief Description:** AI-integrated autonomous UAS system for real-time detection, tracking, and 3D volumetric reconstruction of airborne plumes using Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) and BST's fixed-wing UAS platforms (S2 UAS, SwiftCore™ Flight Management System). Phase I establishes technical feasibility through controlled experiments and field demonstrations in collaboration with Brookhaven National Laboratory.

### Overall Status: **PROPOSAL PENDING AWARD**

Since Project 001-00 (DTRA PLUMES) is a submitted SBIR Phase I proposal dated May 21, 2025, **no contract award confirmation or actual spending is visible in the provided data**. The following assessment is based on the proposed budget structure only:

### Budget Composition Analysis:

1. **Personnel Costs (36.3% of total budget = $45,070.97)**
   - **Assessment:** Moderate allocation. Two senior staff (PI + System Engineer) for 7-month effort.
   - **Concern:** Only ~500 hours allocated across two individuals for a Phase I technical development effort. May indicate reliance on BNL subcontractor for heavy lifting.

2. **Subcontractor Costs (53.1% of total budget = $66,000.00 to BNL)**
   - **Assessment:** Majority of budget directed to algorithm/software development via Brookhaven National Lab.
   - **Rationale:** Appropriate for NeRF/neural volumetric reconstruction R&D, where BNL has specialized expertise.
   - **Risk:** High dependency on subcontractor performance and schedule.

3. **Fringe Benefits (10.6% of total budget = $13,196.78)**
   - **Assessment:** Standard 29.28% rate applied; reasonable for Phase I federal contract.

4. **Notable Gaps:**

   - **No travel budget** – Despite "field demonstrations" mentioned as Phase I deliverable, no explicit travel funds are itemized. This may indicate field work will be minimal or absorbed within personnel labor.
   - **No materials/supplies budget** – Consumables, sensors, batteries, etc. not separately budgeted.
