## Project Overview

- **Client/Agency:** Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I Proposal

## Key Deliverables & Milestones (Proposed)

**Source:** Technical Volume and Budget Justification (Proposal T252-001-0052).

### Phase I Deliverables (7-month effort):
1. **Laboratory Experiments & Feasibility Study**
   - Controlled indoor plume generation and neural volumetric reconstruction demonstrations
   - Validation of NeRF-based algorithms on synthetic and semi-synthetic plume data

2. **Field Demonstration Report**
   - Outdoor plume tracking with BST S2 UAS platform
   - Multi-sensor data collection and integration

3. **Technical Requirements & Specifications**
   - Phase II software architecture and integration specifications
   - Performance requirements for autonomous plume tracking

4. **Flight Planning Implementation**
   - Integration of closed-loop autonomous control with plume reconstruction algorithms
   - SwiftCore™ integration for real-time flight adaptation

5. **Sensor Integration Report**
   - EO/IR sensor integration with BST avionics
   - Data fusion pipeline documentation

6. **Final Phase I Report**
   - Technical feasibility summary
   - Recommendations for Phase II prototyping

**Financial Implications:** All deliverables are labor-intensive (personnel + BNL subcontract). No major equipment or material purchases are proposed in the budget.
