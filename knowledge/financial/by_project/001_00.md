# Project 001-00 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** Coordinated UAS-Based Plume Tracking  
**Client/Agency:** Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA)  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I  
**Program/Solicitation:** DTRA252-001 PLUMES  
**Proposal Number:** T252-001-0052  
**Period of Performance:** 7 months (submitted May 21, 2025)  
**Key Personnel:** Dr. Jack Elston (PI), Dr. Maciej Stachura (System Engineer)  

**Scope:** Development of an AI-integrated, autonomous UAS system for real-time detection, tracking, and 3D volumetric reconstruction of airborne plumes produced during explosive tests. The effort combines Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) and dynamic scene reconstruction algorithms with BST's S2 UAS platform, SwiftCore™ Flight Management System, and vertically-integrated avionics. Phase I establishes technical feasibility through controlled laboratory experiments and field demonstrations, with deliverables including performance requirements and software infrastructure for Phase II prototyping. Subcontract with Brookhaven National Laboratory (BNL) for software development led by Dr. Gijs de Boer.
