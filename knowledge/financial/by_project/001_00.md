# Project 001-00 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Coordinated UAS-Based Plume Tracking (DTRA PLUMES)
- **Client/Agency:** Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I Proposal
- **Program/Solicitation:** DTRA252-001 PLUMES; Proposal Number T252-001-0052
- **Period of Performance:** 7 months (proposal date: May 2025)
- **Brief Description:** AI-integrated autonomous UAS system to enable real-time detection, tracking, and 3D volumetric reconstruction of airborne plumes during explosive tests. Combines Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) and dynamic scene reconstruction algorithms with BST's S2 UAS platforms and SwiftCore™ Flight Management System. Phase I will establish technical feasibility through controlled laboratory experiments and field demonstrations.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones (Proposed, Phase I)

**Source: Technical Volume and Budget Justification (DTRA proposal)**

The proposal indicates a 7-month Phase I effort with the following strategic deliverables:

1. **Controlled Laboratory Experiments**
   - Proof-of-concept demonstrations of Neural Radiance Field (NeRF) algorithms on synthetic and semi-controlled plume imagery
   - Performance benchmarking of volumetric reconstruction algorithms

2. **Field Demonstrations**
   - Flight tests integrating NeRF algorithms with BST's S2 UAS and SwiftCore™ avionics

4. **Software Infrastructure**
