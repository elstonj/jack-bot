# Project 001-00 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Name:** Coordinated UAS-Based Plume Tracking (DTRA PLUMES)  
**Client/Agency:** Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA)  
**Program/Solicitation:** DTRA 2025-001 PLUMES; Proposal Number T252-001-0052  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I Proposal  
**Period of Performance:** 7 months (proposed); submitted May 21, 2025  
**Brief Description:** Development of AI-integrated autonomous UAS system for real-time detection, tracking, and 3D volumetric reconstruction of airborne plumes using Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) and BST's SwiftCore™ Flight Management System. Phase I focuses on technical feasibility through controlled laboratory experiments and field demonstrations.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones (Proposed)

Per the Technical Volume and Budget Justification, Phase I deliverables include:

1. **Laboratory Feasibility Experiments**
   - Demonstration of NeRF-based plume reconstruction in controlled environment
   - Validation of neural volumetric reconstruction algorithms
   
2. **Field Demonstrations**
   - Integration of multi-sensor payload (EO/IR cameras, GPS/IMU, environmental sensors) on BST UAS platforms
   - Autonomous flight control based on plume reconstruction via SwiftCore™ FMS
   
3. **Performance Requirements Document**
   - Technical specifications for Phase II prototyping
   
4. **Software Infrastructure & Algorithm Development**
   - NeRF extensions (K-Planes, HexPlane, Dex-NeRF) for time-varying plume tracking
   - Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) integration
   - Gaussian Splatting and NeRFPlayer near-real-time reconstruction techniques
   - Integration with Brookhaven National Laboratory algorithms

5. **Final Report**
   - Phase I technical accomplishments and Phase II recommendations

**Duration:** 7 months (timeline to be finalized upon award)
