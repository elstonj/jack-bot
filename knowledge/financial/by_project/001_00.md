# Project 001-00 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Name:** Coordinated UAS-Based Plume Tracking (DTRA PLUMES)  
**Client/Agency:** Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA)  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I Proposal  
**Solicitation:** DTRA 2025 Topic DTRA252-001 PLUMES; Proposal Number T252-001-0052  
**Period of Performance:** 7 months (proposed)  
**Submission Date:** May 21, 2025  

**Scope Summary** (from Proposal):  
Develop AI-integrated, autonomous UAS system for real-time detection, tracking, and 3D volumetric reconstruction of airborne plumes during explosive tests. Core innovation leverages Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) and dynamic scene reconstruction integrated with BST's S2 UAS platform and SwiftCore™ Flight Management System. Phase I focuses on technical feasibility through controlled laboratory experiments and field demonstrations, with deliverables including performance requirements and software infrastructure for Phase II prototyping.

## Financial Health Assessment

### Current Status: **AWAITING AWARD**

1. **Budget Readiness**
   - Proposed budget is complete, detailed, and compliant with SBIR proposal format
   - Personnel rates are clear (Dr. Elston $98.56/hr, Dr. Stachura $84.13/hr)
   - Subcontractor scope and cost ($66K BNL) is defined
   - Fringe benefits calculated at 29.28% of labor costs

2. **Cost Risk Factors**
   - **Subcontractor dependency:** BNL provides critical AI/ML algorithm work (neural volumetric reconstruction). Any delay or escalation in BNL scope would impact total cost and schedule.
   - **Hardware/equipment costs not visible:** Proposal does not itemize equipment, sensors, or flight platform procurement. If S2 UAS or sensors require acquisition, those costs are not captured in the current $124K budget and could represent scope creep.
   - **Travel/field operations unbudgeted:** Proposal mentions "field demonstrations" but no travel, vehicle rental, or field logistics are broken out. This is a potential underestimate.

3. **Schedule Risk**
   - 7-month Phase I is aggressive for algorithm development + integration + testing + demonstrations
   - BNL allocation (1.51 months total effort across 3 personnel) appears lean for neural network development
   - Risk of schedule slip → cost overrun if technical milestones slip

4. **Post-Award Concerns (if awarded)**
   - No CLINs or option periods mentioned; single fixed-price Phase I
   - Phase II funding and scope contingent on Phase I success (typical SBIR structure)
   - Restricted data assertion (Volume 5) suggests proprietary SwiftCore™ integration; IP ownership and Government rights must be negotiated post-award

5. **Budget Sufficiency Assessment**
   - **On paper:** Budget appears complete and reasonable for a 7-month Phase I ($124K for 2 FTE personnel + 1.51 FTE subcontractor effort + overhead)
   - **In practice:** No contingency or indirect costs visible; proposal appears to be at-cost. Any scope growth or schedule extension will exceed budget.

### Recommendations (Pre-Award)
- Clarify equipment and sensor procurement path (is S2 UAS provided by BST, or Government-furnished?)
- Break out travel and field operations as a separate ODC budget line (~$5–10K for 2–3 site visits)
- Define Phase II scope and funding expectation to set cost expectations with DTRA
- Confirm BNL commitment and ability to deliver algorithm within 1.51-month allocated effort
- Request data rights negotiation pre-award to avoid disputes on SwiftCore™ restrictions
