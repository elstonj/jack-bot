# Project 001-00 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Coordinated UAS-Based Plume Tracking (DTRA PLUMES)
- **Client/Agency:** Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I
- **Program/Solicitation:** DTRA 2025-001 PLUMES; Proposal Number T252-001-0052
- **Period of Performance:** 7 months (proposal submitted May 21, 2025)
- **Brief Description:** Development of AI-integrated, autonomous UAS system to enable real-time detection, tracking, and 3D volumetric reconstruction of airborne plumes produced during explosive tests, combining Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) and dynamic scene reconstruction with BST's S2 UAS platforms and SwiftCore™ Flight Management System. Phase I establishes technical feasibility through controlled laboratory experiments and field demonstrations.
