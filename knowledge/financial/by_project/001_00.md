# Project 001-00 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Project Name:** Coordinated UAS-Based Plume Tracking (DTRA PLUMES)
- **Client/Agency:** Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I Proposal
- **Solicitation:** DTRA 2025-2-001 PLUMES; Proposal Number T252-001-0052
- **Period of Performance:** 7 months (proposal submitted May 21, 2025)
- **Description of Scope:** Development of AI-integrated autonomous UAS system for real-time detection, tracking, and 3D volumetric reconstruction of airborne plumes produced during explosive tests. Integrates Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs), dynamic scene reconstruction algorithms, and BST's fixed-wing UAS platforms with SwiftCore™ Flight Management System. Phase I establishes technical feasibility through controlled laboratory experiments and field demonstrations, culminating in performance requirements and software infrastructure for Phase II prototyping.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones
(From Technical Volume and Budget Justification)

**Phase I (7-month effort):**
1. **Controlled Laboratory Experiments** — Validation of NeRF-based reconstruction on synthetic and laboratory plume data
2. **Field Demonstrations** — Real-world plume tracking trials using BST UAS platforms (S2 UAS)
3. **Software Infrastructure** — Development of modular integration framework for plume detection, tracking, and 3D volumetric reconstruction on SwiftCore™ Flight Management System
4. **Performance Requirements Documentation** — Specification of Phase II technical and operational requirements
5. **Algorithm Optimization** — Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) for enhanced accuracy; Gaussian Splatting and NeRFPlayer techniques for near-real-time reconstruction
6. **System Integration** — Multi-sensor data fusion (EO/IR cameras, GPS/IMU, environmental sensors); closed-loop autonomous flight control based on plume reconstruction
7. **Final Reports** — Technical reports on feasibility, performance metrics, and Phase II recommendations

**Financial Implications:** No milestone-based payment terms are specified in the provided documents. Budget Justification shows a fixed 7-month effort with defined labor hours and subcontract amount, suggesting a lump-sum or fixed-price delivery contract structure (typical for SBIR Phase I).
