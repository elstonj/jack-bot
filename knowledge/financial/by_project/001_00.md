# Project 001-00 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** Coordinated UAS-Based Plume Tracking (DTRA PLUMES)

**Client/Agency:** Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA)

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I Proposal

**Solicitation:** DTRA 2025 (DTRA252-001 PLUMES; Proposal Number T252-001-0052)

**Period of Performance:** 7 months (proposed, submitted May 21, 2025)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Black Swift Technologies proposes development of an AI-integrated autonomous UAS system for real-time detection, tracking, and 3D volumetric reconstruction of airborne plumes during explosive tests. The effort combines Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) and dynamic scene reconstruction algorithms with BST's fixed-wing UAS platforms and SwiftCore™ Flight Management System avionics. Phase I will establish technical feasibility through controlled laboratory experiments and field demonstrations, with Phase II prototyping to follow. Collaboration with Brookhaven National Laboratory (BNL) for software development and sensor integration.
