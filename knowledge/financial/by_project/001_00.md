# Project 001-00 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Name:** Coordinated UAS-Based Plume Tracking (DTRA PLUMES)  
**Client/Agency:** Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA)  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I  
**Solicitation:** DTRA 2025 — DTRA252-001 PLUMES (Proposal T252-001-0052)  
**Period of Performance:** 7 months (proposal submitted May 21, 2025)  
**Key Personnel:** Dr. Jack Elston (PI), Dr. Maciej Stachura (System Engineer)

**Brief Scope:** Development of AI-integrated autonomous UAS system for real-time detection, tracking, and 3D volumetric reconstruction of airborne plumes using Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) and dynamic scene reconstruction. Integration with BST's S2 UAS platform, SwiftCore™ Flight Management System, and multi-sensor payload (EO/IR cameras, GPS/IMU, environmental sensors). Collaboration with Brookhaven National Laboratory on software development and algorithm generation.
