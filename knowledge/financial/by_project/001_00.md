## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: SBIR Phase I Proposal Technical Volume (2025)**

**Proposed Phase I Deliverables & Key Activities:**

1. **Laboratory Feasibility Studies (Months 1–3)**
   - Establish NeRF-based plume reconstruction pipeline
   - Generate synthetic plume datasets for algorithm training
   - Benchmark neural volumetric reconstruction techniques (NeRF, K-Planes, HexPlane, Dex-NeRF, Gaussian Splatting)

2. **Algorithm Integration & Flight Planning (Months 2–5)**
   - Integrate neural volumetric reconstruction with SwiftCore™ Flight Management System
   - Develop automated flight planning algorithms for multi-view image collection
   - Implement closed-loop autonomous flight control based on plume reconstruction

3. **Field Demonstration (Month 6)**
   - Conduct controlled field tests with representative plume scenarios
   - Validate autonomous tracking performance in operationally relevant conditions
   - Collect real-world multi-view imagery for algorithm refinement

4. **Phase II Readiness (Month 7)**
   - Document technical feasibility findings
   - Establish Phase II performance requirements and software infrastructure specifications
   - Final report and transition planning

**Financial Implications:**
- Subcontractor (BNL) engagement is critical path for software development
- Personnel effort concentrated in first 3 months (algorithm development), with ongoing system integration through Month 7
- Field demonstration in Month 6 may require travel and equipment costs (not explicitly itemized in proposal budget)
