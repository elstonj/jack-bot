## Key Deliverables & Milestones (from Proposal)

**From Proposal Documentation:**
- **Phase I Deliverables:** Technical feasibility demonstration through controlled laboratory experiments and field demonstrations
- **Software Infrastructure:** Development of modular, open-architecture software stack integrating:
  - Neural volumetric reconstruction algorithms (NeRF-based, K-Planes, HexPlane, Dex-NeRF, Gaussian Splatting)
  - Multi-sensor data fusion platform (EO/IR cameras, GPS/IMU telemetry, environmental sensors)
  - Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs)
  - Integration with BST's SwiftCore™ Flight Management System for closed-loop autonomous flight control
  
- **Collaboration:** Brookhaven National Laboratory (BNL) leading software development under $66,000 subcontract
  - BNL Point of Contact: Dr. Gijs de Boer
  - Key Contributors: Arthur Sedlacek, Andrew McMahon
  - Effort allocation: ~1.51 person-months across three personnel
