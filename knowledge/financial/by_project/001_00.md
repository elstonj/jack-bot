# Project 001-00 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Name:** Coordinated UAS-Based Plume Tracking  
**Client/Agency:** Defense Threat Reduction Agency (DTRA)  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I  
**Program/Solicitation:** DTRA 2025, Topic DTRA252-001 PLUMES  
**Proposal Number:** T252-001-0052  
**Period of Performance:** 7 months (proposed)  
**Submission Date:** May 21, 2025  

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Black Swift Technologies proposes an AI-integrated, autonomous UAS system to enable real-time detection, tracking, and 3D volumetric reconstruction of airborne plumes produced during explosive tests. The system combines Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) and dynamic scene reconstruction algorithms with BST's fixed-wing UAS platforms and SwiftCore™ Flight Management System. Phase I will establish technical feasibility through controlled laboratory experiments and field demonstrations, with Brookhaven National Laboratory as a key subcontractor for software development.
