- **Full Project Name:** Coordinated UAS-Based Plume Tracking (DTRA PLUMES)

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**From Proposal Technical Volume:**
- **Phase I Objectives:**
  - Establish technical feasibility of NeRF-based plume reconstruction through controlled laboratory experiments
  - Conduct field demonstrations of autonomous plume tracking under operationally relevant conditions
  - Develop performance requirements and technical specifications
  - Generate software infrastructure foundation for Phase II prototyping

- **Key Technologies/Deliverables:**
  - Integration of Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) with BST's SwiftCore™ Flight Management System
  - Multi-sensor data fusion platform (EO/IR cameras, GPS/IMU, environmental sensors)
  - Closed-loop autonomous flight control algorithms
  - Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) for enhanced plume reconstruction accuracy
  - Real-time reconstruction capabilities (Gaussian Splatting, NeRFPlayer techniques)

- **Proposed Phase II:** Prototype development, field validation, and transition to operationally deployable system

**Data Source:** Coordinated UAS-Based Plume Tracking Technical Volume (DTRA PLUMES Proposal T252-001-0052)
