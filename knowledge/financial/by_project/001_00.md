## Key Deliverables & Milestones

*Source: Coordinated UAS-Based Plume Tracking Technical Volume (SBIR Phase I Proposal)*

**Phase I Focus (7-month effort):**
- Feasibility demonstration using Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) for dynamic plume reconstruction
- Controlled laboratory experiments validating 3D volumetric reconstruction from multi-view imagery
- Field demonstrations under operationally relevant conditions
- Integration of plume reconstruction algorithms with BST's SwiftCore™ Flight Management System
- Closed-loop autonomous flight control based on plume tracking
- Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) for enhanced reconstruction accuracy
- Performance requirements and software infrastructure specifications for Phase II prototyping

**Technical Deliverables (inferred from proposal):**
- Real-time plume detection and tracking algorithms
- Neural volumetric reconstruction software (NeRF-based)
- Multi-sensor data fusion module (EO/IR cameras, GPS/IMU, environmental sensors)
- SwiftCore™ integration specifications
- Phase II prototype design and performance baseline
