## Technical Scope & Systems

**Core Technology:**
- Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) and volumetric reconstruction techniques (K-Planes, HexPlane, Dex-NeRF)
- Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs)
- Gaussian Splatting and NeRFPlayer for near-real-time reconstruction

**BST Systems Integrated:**
- S2 UAS (Unmanned Aerial System)
- SwiftCore™ Flight Management System
- SwiftTab (ground station software)
- SwiftStation (command & control)
- Multi-sensor payload (EO/IR cameras, GPS/IMU, environmental sensors)

**Deliverables (Phase I):**
- Technical feasibility demonstration (laboratory experiments)
- Field demonstration of autonomous plume tracking
- Performance requirements document
- Software infrastructure prototype for Phase II
