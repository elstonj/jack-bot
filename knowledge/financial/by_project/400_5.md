# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (AFWERX SBIR Phase II – X22.4D)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** – Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity to assess runway suitability for Air Force operations. The system measures soil moisture as a proxy for runway structural integrity (cone index vs. volumetric water content). Integrated work by BST, Orbital Micro Systems, and CET adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case, with field testing culminating at Tyndall AFB in June 2025.
