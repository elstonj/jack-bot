# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DAF - Department of the Air Force), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED (as of 2026-11-02; no open tasks in Asana)
- **Project Due Date:** 2025-07-11

**Brief Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for high-resolution soil moisture mapping. Originally developed in conjunction with NASA, the technology is being modified for DAF end-user application to map landing area soil integrity. Soil moisture measurement is critical for assessing soil slipperiness, stickiness, and structural strength to support C-130 aircraft runway landing decisions.
