### Brief Description of Scope
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a key variable for understanding soil integrity—as soil moisture increases, soil becomes more slippery and sticky while decreasing in strength. BST, OMS, and CET adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case: mapping landing area suitability for C-130 Hercules aircraft operations. Soil integrity is quantified as cone index and compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. Testing conducted at local field sites and Tyndall Air Force Base.

**Key Technical Outputs:**
- UAS-based L-band radiometer hardware integration
- Soil integrity algorithms (cone index vs. soil moisture relationship with soil type coefficients)
- RevD data processing web interface
- Sensor protective cover design
- E2 and Soil Moisture Sensor added to DIU Blue List (2025-07-24)
