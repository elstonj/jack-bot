## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX; DoD SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

### Brief Description of Scope
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity and runway suitability. The system measures soil moisture as a key variable to understanding soil integrity. The team (BST, OMS, and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case: mapping landing area suitability. Soil integrity is quantified as cone index and compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. Key deliverables included soil integrity algorithm development, local field testing, model refinement, and testing at Tyndall AFB. All milestones completed and accepted by Air Force TPOC as of July 2025; final closeout completed November 2025.
