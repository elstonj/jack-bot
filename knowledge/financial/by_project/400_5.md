# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to assess runway structural integrity and suitability for C-130 aircraft operations. The project involved hardware integration (PCB fabrication, antenna modules), algorithm development (cone index vs. volumetric water content), field testing (local and at Tyndall AFB), and final reporting. Prime contractor: Black Swift Technologies LLC; Subcontractor: Orbital Micro Systems, Inc.
