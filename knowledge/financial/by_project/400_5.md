# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SBIR Phase II - SMM DoD 22.4D)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Program, AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** – Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

### Project Scope
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity and runway suitability. The system measures soil moisture as a key variable for understanding soil integrity and compares cone index against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements to assess landing area conditions for Air Force aircraft (C-130 Hercules). Technical outputs include UAS-based L-band radiometer hardware integration, soil integrity algorithms, and deployment validation at Tyndall AFB.
