# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SBIR Phase II - SMM DoD 22.4D)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Contract Due Date:** 2025-07-11
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** – Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity through soil moisture measurements. The system measures volumetric water content (VWC) to assess runway suitability for Air Force operations, specifically supporting C-130 Hercules landing requirements. The team adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use cases, with testing conducted at Tyndall AFB. All deliverables were completed and accepted by Air Force TPOC as of July 2025.
