# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping)
- **Project Code:** 400-5 | **Contract ID:** X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Defense), AFWERX Program
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS) — $605,000 subcontract
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Due Date:** July 11, 2025
- **Status:** **ARCHIVED in Asana** — No active task tracking; work completed or in administrative/approval phase

**Brief Scope Description:**  
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer system for measuring soil moisture to assess runway structural integrity and landing area conditions. Technology adapted for Department of the Air Force requirements in conjunction with NASA and partner Orbital Micro Systems. Project focuses on validation testing and soil integrity algorithm refinement.
