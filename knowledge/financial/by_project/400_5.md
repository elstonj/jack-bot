# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (AFWERX SBIR Phase II - X22.4 Soil Moisture Mapping)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Program)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

### Brief Description of Scope
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a key variable to understand soil integrity and assess runway structural suitability for Air Force operations (specifically C-130 Hercules landing requirements). The team (BST, OMS, and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case. Key deliverables included soil integrity algorithms, local field testing, model refinement, and testing at Tyndall AFB with final reporting completed in July 2025.
