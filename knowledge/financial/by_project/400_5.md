# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II via AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months; contract due date 2025-07-11)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Scope Description (from Asana & Proposals):**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a key variable for understanding soil integrity, with application to Air Force runway landing area suitability assessment. The team (BST, OMS, and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case, comparing soil integrity (quantified as cone index) against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. All five major milestones (Milestones 4–8) were completed and accepted by Air Force TPOC as of July 2025, with final report delivery completed 2025-07-21.
