# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED – Successfully completed with all deliverables accepted

**Brief Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to assess runway structural integrity for C-130 aircraft operations. The project adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case, with team integration of hardware (OMS), algorithm development (BST), and field testing at Tyndall AFB. All deliverables completed and accepted by Air Force TPOC.
