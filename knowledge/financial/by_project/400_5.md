# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II, AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Scope Description:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity to assess runway suitability for C-130 operations. The system measures soil moisture as a proxy for soil integrity (cone index vs. volumetric water content). BST adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case, with OMS providing RF engineering and sensor development. All five milestones (4–8) completed and accepted by Air Force TPOC as of July 2025; final closeout and OMS payments completed November 2025.
