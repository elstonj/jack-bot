# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping, Topic X22.4)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II), AFWERX program
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** ✓ ARCHIVED – Successfully completed with all deliverables accepted

### Brief Scope Description
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a proxy for runway structural integrity and landing area suitability for Air Force operations. The team (BST, OMS, and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case, validating the relationship between cone index (soil strength) and volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. Testing conducted at local field sites and Tyndall Air Force Base.
