# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 to July 2025 (21 months)
- **Brief Description:** Development and validation of a small unmanned aircraft system (UAS) capable of measuring soil moisture to assess runway structural integrity and support C-130 aircraft landing operations. Project includes sensor modification, UAS adaptation, algorithm development, and field testing at Tyndall AFB.

- **Overrun:** $58,498.43 (**158.5% of budget**)
- **Status:** Marked "In Progress" as of July 2025, suggesting additional spend possible
- **Impact:** If CLIN 0005 is now closed, this overrun requires accommodation through:
  - Cost reductions in remaining CLINs (0006, 0007, 0008)
  - Contract modification to increase total ceiling

- **Risk:** If local field testing revealed unanticipated equipment or labor costs (e.g., additional iterations, extended flight hours, sensor repairs), this pattern may repeat in Tyndall AFB testing, which is higher-risk and higher-cost activity.
