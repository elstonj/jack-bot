# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping)
- **Project Code:** 400-5 / X22.4
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Program)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

### Brief Description of Scope
BST developed and validated a UAS-based L-band radiometer system for mapping soil integrity through soil moisture measurements. The system measures volumetric water content (VWC) as a proxy for soil strength and integrity, with application to runway suitability assessment for Air Force C-130 Hercules aircraft operations. The project involved hardware integration, algorithm development, field testing at local sites, and validation testing at Tyndall Air Force Base. All deliverables were completed and accepted by the Air Force TPOC (Jason Foley) by July 2025, with final closeout by November 2025.

### Detailed Variance Analysis & Data Reconciliation

**Critical Discrepancy:**
- **Proposed/Contracted Total:** $1,249,725.00 (Drive Budget Docs)
- **BST Invoiced (QBO Government.md):** $1,699,725.00
- **BST Paid (QBO Unclassified.md):** $949,725.10
- **OMS Subcontractor (Asana):** $605,000+ (with final $24,200 payment in Nov 2025)
- **Estimated Total Actual:** ~$1,554,725+ (exceeds contract by ~$305,000)

**Explanation:**
1. **Invoiced vs. Paid Gap:** BST invoiced the Air Force $1,699,725.00 but received only $949,725.10 in QBO Unclassified.md as of the data snapshot. This suggests **either:**
   - Later payments not captured in the QBO unclassified.md file, **OR**
   - The $1,699,725.00 in government.md reflects invoices sent but not all payments received by the document cutoff, **OR**
   - Adjustments/credits that account for the $750,000 difference.

2. **Contract Value Overrun:** Drive Budget Docs show contract value of $1,249,725.00. If actual invoicing is $1,699,725.00, this represents a **$450,000 overrun (36% above contract).** However, government.md is labeled "CLIN work (004-008)" which may indicate only a subset of CLINs, or the Air Force may have approved contract modifications.

3. **OMS Subcontractor:** Per Asana, cumulative OMS payments total $605,000+. Drive Budget Docs proposed OMS subcontract at $569,433.16; the increase to $605,000+ (~$35,567 overrun) suggests scope changes or rate adjustments.

4. **Labor vs. Materials Split:** QBO does not separately categorize labor, materials, overhead, or fee. All categories are rolled into project-level revenue (BST) and vendor payments (OMS). Detailed cost analysis by category is therefore **not available from QBO data provided.**

**Conclusion:** Actual spend cannot be precisely reconciled against proposed budget by category due to:
- QBO reporting by project only, not by cost element
- Invoicing/payment timing gaps in provided data
- Lack of final closeout invoice documentation post-July 2025
