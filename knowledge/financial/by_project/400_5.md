# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of the Air Force), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 to July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** (completed as of July 2025)
- **Brief Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping high-resolution soil moisture to assess runway soil integrity for landing area assessment. Includes hardware fabrication (PCB, antenna modules), algorithm development, field testing at Tyndall AFB, and final reporting.
