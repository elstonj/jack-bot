# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** SBIR Phase II - SMM DoD 22.4D (Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer system for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a proxy for runway structural integrity, enabling Air Force assessments of landing area suitability for C-130 aircraft operations. The project adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case, quantifying soil integrity as cone index compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. All deliverables completed and accepted by Air Force TPOC as of July 2025.
