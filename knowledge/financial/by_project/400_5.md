# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping, Topic X22.4)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Program, AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED — Successfully completed with all deliverables accepted

**Project Description:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer system for mapping soil moisture to assess runway structural integrity. The system measures volumetric water content (VWC) and correlates it to cone index (a measure of soil strength) to determine landing area suitability for C-130 Hercules aircraft. The project involved technology adaptation of NASA soil moisture mapping research to Air Force operational use cases. Key testing conducted at local sites and Tyndall Air Force Base. Team included Black Swift Technologies (prime), Orbital Micro Systems (OMS, subcontractor), and CET.
