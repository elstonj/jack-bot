# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II – Soil Moisture Mapping (Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Status:** ARCHIVED – Successfully completed with all deliverables accepted
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity in support of Air Force runway and landing area suitability assessment. The system measures soil moisture (volumetric water content) as a proxy for soil integrity, quantified as cone index. The team adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case, with testing at Tyndall Air Force Base. Key outputs include UAS-integrated L-band radiometer hardware, soil integrity algorithms, RevD data processing web interface, and final validation reports.
