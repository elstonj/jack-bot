# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (AFWERX SBIR Phase II - X22.4)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) Phase II
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity to assess runway suitability for Air Force operations. The system measures soil moisture as a key variable in understanding soil integrity—as soil moisture increases, soil becomes more slippery and sticky while decreasing in strength. The team adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for the Air Force use case of mapping landing area suitability. All deliverables completed and accepted by Air Force TPOC as of July 2025; final payments and reporting closed out by November 2025.
