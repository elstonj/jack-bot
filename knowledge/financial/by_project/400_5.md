# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025
- **Key Contacts:**
  - Jason Foley (Primary TPOC, jason.foley.1@us.af.mil)
  - Thomas Shea (Contracting Officer, thomas.shea.3@us.af.mil)

**Scope Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity via volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. The system measures soil moisture as a key variable to understanding soil integrity—as soil moisture increases, soil becomes more slippery and sticky while decreasing in strength. Adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case: mapping landing area suitability and quantifying soil integrity (cone index vs. VWC). Team includes BST (prime), OMS (subcontractor), and CET (collaborator). Project completed successfully with all deliverables accepted by Air Force TPOC as of July 2025.
