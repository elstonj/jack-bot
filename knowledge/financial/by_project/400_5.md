# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a key variable for understanding soil integrity and runway suitability for Air Force aircraft operations (C-130 landing requirements). The team (BST, OMS, and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for the Air Force use case, with soil integrity quantified as cone index and compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. All deliverables completed and accepted by Air Force TPOC as of July 2025; final payments and reporting closed out by November 2025.
