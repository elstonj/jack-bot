# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED – Successfully completed with all deliverables accepted
- **Brief Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer system for mapping soil moisture as a proxy for runway integrity assessment. The system measures soil moisture to quantify cone index (soil strength), enabling evaluation of landing area suitability for C-130 Hercules aircraft. Technology adapted NASA soil moisture mapping capability to Air Force operational use case. Testing conducted at local sites and Tyndall Air Force Base.
