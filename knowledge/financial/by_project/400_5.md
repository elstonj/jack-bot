# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED – Successfully Completed** (all deliverables accepted)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)

**Project Description:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to assess landing area suitability for the U.S. Air Force, quantifying soil integrity as cone index compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. The team (BST, OMS, and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case. All 85 project tasks completed with 100% completion rate. Technology successfully transitioned to DIU Blue List (E2 and Soil Moisture Sensor listed 2025-07-24).
