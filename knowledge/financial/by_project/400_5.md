# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED – Successfully completed with all deliverables accepted
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)

**Project Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to assess runway suitability for aircraft operations (specifically C-130 Hercules). The team adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use, with soil integrity quantified as cone index compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. Project included hardware integration, algorithm development, field testing (local and at Tyndall Air Force Base), and final reporting. All 85 tasks completed at 100% completion rate.
