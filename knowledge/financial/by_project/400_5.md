# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** SBIR Phase II - SMM DoD 22.4D | Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of the Air Force), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** (project complete or closed out as of October 23, 2026)
- **Brief Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer (in partnership with NASA, OMS, and CET) for mapping high-resolution soil moisture. Technology adapted for U.S. Air Force to map soil integrity for landing area assessment. Soil moisture measurement is critical for understanding soil strength, slipperiness, and stickiness for C-130 aircraft runway operations.
