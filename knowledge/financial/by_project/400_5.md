# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer system for mapping soil moisture to assess runway structural integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a proxy for soil cone index, enabling Air Force assessment of landing area suitability (particularly for C-130 aircraft operations). Project team included Black Swift Technologies (prime), Orbital Micro Systems (subcontractor), and CET. All deliverables accepted by Air Force TPOC (Jason Foley) as of July 2025; final report delivered July 21, 2025.
