# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a proxy for runway structural integrity to support Air Force operational planning. The team (BST, OMS, and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for the Air Force use case of mapping landing area suitability. Soil integrity is quantified as cone index and compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. All five milestone deliverables (Milestones 4–8) were completed and accepted by Air Force TPOC by July 2025.
