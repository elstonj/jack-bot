# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SBIR Phase II - SMM DoD 22.4D)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Air Force), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 to July 2025 (21 months)
- **Primary TPOC:** Jason Foley (jason.foley.1@us.af.mil)
- **Billing Contact:** Thomas Shea, CO (thomas.shea.3@us.af.mil)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** (as of Asana snapshot 2026-10-23)

**Brief Description:**  
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer (in partnership with NASA, Orbital Micro Systems, and CET) for mapping high-resolution soil moisture to assess runway integrity. The technology has been adapted for DAF end-users to map soil integrity, specifically for landing area assessment. Soil moisture measurement is critical for understanding soil strength, slipperiness, and stickiness for C-130 Hercules landing operations.
