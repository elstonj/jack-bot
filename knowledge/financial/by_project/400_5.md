# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping)
- **Project Code:** 400-5 / X22.4
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Status:** Successfully completed and archived as of July 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures volumetric water content (VWC) and soil moisture to assess runway/landing area suitability for Air Force operations (specifically C-130 Hercules aircraft). The team adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for DoD use case, quantifying soil integrity as cone index compared against VWC measurements. Testing locations included local field sites and Tyndall Air Force Base.
