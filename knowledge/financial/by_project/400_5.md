# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping)
- **Project Code:** 400-5 / X22.4
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF) / AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer system for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a proxy for runway structural integrity assessment, enabling the Air Force to determine landing area suitability (specifically for C-130 Hercules operations). The team (BST, OMS, and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use, with soil integrity quantified as cone index compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. All eight milestones completed successfully with deliverables accepted by Air Force TPOC.
