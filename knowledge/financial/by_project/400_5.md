# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (AFWERX SBIR Phase II – Topic X22.4, "Soil Moisture Mapping")
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a key variable for understanding soil integrity, enabling assessment of runway suitability for C-130 aircraft operations. The team (BST, OMS, and subcontractors) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case, quantifying soil integrity as cone index and comparing against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. All milestones and deliverables completed and accepted by Air Force TPOC as of July 2025.
