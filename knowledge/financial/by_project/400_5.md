# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** – Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Scope Description (from Asana & Proposals):** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity to support Air Force runway suitability assessments. The system measures soil moisture as a proxy for understanding soil strength and structural integrity. BST, OMS, and CET adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case, comparing cone index against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. All deliverables completed and accepted by Air Force TPOC; final hardware integration, algorithms, and testing at Tyndall AFB completed by July 2025.
