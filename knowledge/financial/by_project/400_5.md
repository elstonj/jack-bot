# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED – Successfully completed with all deliverables accepted
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer system for mapping soil moisture to assess runway/landing area integrity. The system measures volumetric water content (VWC) and correlates it with cone index (soil strength) to determine landing area suitability for Air Force operations (C-130 Hercules aircraft). The project adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for DoD use. Key deliverables included algorithm development, local field testing, model refinement, and testing at Tyndall Air Force Base, culminating in a final technical report accepted by the Air Force.
