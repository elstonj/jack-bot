# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS)
- **Project Code:** 400-5 / X22.4
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of the Air Force / AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 to July 2025 (21 months)
- **Primary TPOC:** Jason Foley (jason.foley.1@us.af.mil)
- **Billing Contact:** Thomas Shea, CO (thomas.shea.3@us.af.mil)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED (as of Asana snapshot 2026-10-23)

**Scope:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer in partnership with NASA, Orbital Micro Systems, and CET for mapping high-resolution soil moisture. Technology adapted for DAF to map soil integrity and assess landing area integrity by measuring soil strength, slipperiness, and stickiness—critical for C-130 Hercules runway operations.
