- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025**

### Brief Description of Scope
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity via soil moisture measurements. The system measures volumetric water content (VWC) as a key variable for understanding soil integrity and landing area suitability. The team (BST, OMS, and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case: assessing C-130 Hercules aircraft landing requirements. Soil integrity is quantified as cone index and compared against VWC measurements.
