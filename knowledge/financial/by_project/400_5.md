# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 to July 2025 (21 months)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development and demonstration of small UAS-based soil moisture sensing capability to validate runway structural integrity for C-130 aircraft operations. Work includes modification to sensing systems, modification to UAS platform, algorithm development, local and field testing at Tyndall AFB, and final reporting.
