# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II: "Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS"
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II, AFWERX program)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003 (per Asana)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to assess runway structural integrity and suitability for C-130 landing operations. Technical work involved system design, algorithm development, local field testing, validation testing at Tyndall AFB, and final reporting. Key partners: Black Swift Technologies (prime), Orbital Micro Systems (subcontractor $605,000+), and CET (collaboration).
