# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** SBIR Phase II - SMM DoD 22.4D | Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Defense), AFWERX Program
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS) – $605,000 budgeted
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** in Asana as of 2026-11-08; contract execution complete
- **Primary TPOC:** Jason Foley (jason.foley.1@us.af.mil)
- **Billing Contact:** Thomas Shea, CO (thomas.shea.3@us.af.mil)

**Scope Summary:**  
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer (in conjunction with NASA) for mapping high-resolution soil moisture. Technology adapted for Department of Air Force end-user application to assess landing area soil integrity. Soil moisture measurement is critical for evaluating soil slipperiness, stickiness, and structural strength to support C-130 aircraft landing operations.
