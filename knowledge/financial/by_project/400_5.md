# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX; DoD SBIR Program
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity via soil moisture measurement. The system measures volumetric water content (VWC) as a proxy for soil strength to determine landing area suitability for Air Force operations (C-130 landing requirements). Technical work included hardware integration, soil integrity algorithm development, local field testing, and validation testing at Tyndall AFB. All deliverables completed and accepted by TPOC (Jason Foley) as of July 2025.
