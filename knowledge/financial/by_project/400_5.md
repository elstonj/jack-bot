# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (AFWERX SBIR Phase II X22.4D)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Program)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS) – $605,000 subcontract
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** – Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Scope:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity by measuring soil moisture as a proxy for runway structural suitability. The system adapts NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use cases, enabling rapid assessment of landing area conditions. Key deliverables include algorithm development, field testing in Colorado, and operational validation at Tyndall AFB.
