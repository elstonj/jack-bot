# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED – Successfully completed and closed out

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity and runway condition assessment. The system measures soil moisture as a proxy for runway structural integrity to support Air Force operational planning. BST, in collaboration with subcontractor OMS and consultant CET, adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for DoD use. Key technical outputs include: UAS-based L-band radiometer hardware integration, soil integrity algorithms (cone index vs. volumetric water content), RevD data processing web interface, and sensor protective cover design. Project included field testing at local sites and Tyndall AFB with Air Force TPOC Jason Foley.
