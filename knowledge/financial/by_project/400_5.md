# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED — Successfully completed with all deliverables accepted by Air Force TPOC (September 2025)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer system for mapping soil integrity via soil moisture measurements. The system measures volumetric water content (VWC) to assess runway suitability for C-130 aircraft operations. The project involved hardware integration (led by subcontractor Orbital Micro Systems), soil integrity algorithm development, field testing at local sites and Tyndall AFB, and creation of a RevD data processing web interface. BST served as prime contractor; OMS as primary subcontractor ($605,000).
