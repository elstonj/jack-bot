# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** – Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
BST, in partnership with subcontractor Orbital Micro Systems (OMS), developed and validated a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a key variable to assess runway suitability for C-130 Hercules aircraft operations. The team adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case, quantifying soil integrity as cone index and comparing it against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. All eight milestones were completed successfully, with final deliverables accepted by Air Force TPOC by July 2025.
