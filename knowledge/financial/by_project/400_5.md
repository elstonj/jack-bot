# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

### Brief Description of Scope
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a key variable to understanding soil integrity and landing area suitability for Air Force operations. The team (BST, OMS, and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for the Air Force use case: mapping landing area suitability for C-130 Hercules aircraft. Soil integrity is quantified as cone index and compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. All deliverables completed and accepted by Air Force TPOC as of July 2025; final payments and reporting closed out by November 2025.
