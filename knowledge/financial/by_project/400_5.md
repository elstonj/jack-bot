# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SBIR Phase II - SMM DoD 22.4D)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Description of Scope:**
Black Swift Technologies (BST), in partnership with subcontractor Orbital Micro Systems (OMS), developed and validated a UAS-based L-band radiometer system for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a proxy for runway structural integrity assessment, enabling Air Force personnel to determine landing area suitability. The technology adapted NASA soil moisture mapping methodology for Air Force use cases, particularly for C-130 Hercules aircraft operations. Key deliverables included soil integrity algorithms, local field testing, model refinement, and validation testing at Tyndall AFB.
