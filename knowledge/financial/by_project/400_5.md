# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (AFWERX SBIR Phase II X22.4D)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II, AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025
- **Brief Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil moisture as a proxy for runway structural integrity. The system measures volumetric water content (VWC) and relates it to soil cone index to assess landing area suitability for C-130 aircraft operations. Team included BST (prime), Orbital Micro Systems (OMS, subcontractor), and CET.
