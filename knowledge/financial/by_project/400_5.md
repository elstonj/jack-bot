# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (AFWERX SBIR Phase II - X22.4)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Program)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025
- **Brief Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer system for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to assess runway structural suitability for Air Force operations (specifically C-130 Hercules landings). BST partnered with Orbital Micro Systems (OMS) as primary subcontractor for radiometer hardware development. All 8 milestones completed and accepted by Air Force TPOC.
