# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED – Successfully completed with all deliverables accepted

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a key variable for understanding soil integrity—as soil moisture increases, soil becomes more slippery and sticky while decreasing in strength. The integrated team (BST as prime, OMS as subcontractor, and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case: mapping landing area suitability for C-130 aircraft operations. Soil integrity is quantified as cone index and compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. Key technical outputs include UAS-based L-band radiometer hardware integration, soil integrity algorithms, RevD data processing web interface, and sensor protective cover design.
