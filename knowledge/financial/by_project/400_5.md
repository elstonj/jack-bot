# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of the Air Force), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** (Asana shows 0 open tasks as of Nov 14, 2026; project completed July 2025)

**Scope:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer in partnership with Orbital Micro Systems and CET (with NASA collaboration) to measure soil moisture for runway integrity assessment. The technology assesses soil strength, slipperiness, and stickiness for Department of the Air Force use cases (C-130 landing area validation).
