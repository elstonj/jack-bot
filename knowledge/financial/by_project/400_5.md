# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025**

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer system for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a proxy for runway structural integrity, supporting Air Force operational planning for C-130 Hercules landing zone assessments. The team (BST, OMS, and subcontractor CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case. Soil integrity is quantified as cone index and compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. All deliverables completed and accepted by Air Force TPOC as of July 2025, with final payments closed out by November 2025.
