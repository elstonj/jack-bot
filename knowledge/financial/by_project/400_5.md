# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Full Technical Title:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Program, AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Contract Due Date:** 2025-07-11
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

### Project Description
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer system for mapping soil integrity and assessing runway suitability. The system measures soil moisture as a key variable for understanding soil strength and landing area compatibility. The team (BST, OMS, and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use, quantifying soil integrity as cone index and comparing against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. All deliverables were completed and accepted by the Air Force Technical Point of Contact (TPOC) Jason Foley.
