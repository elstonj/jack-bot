# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (AFWERX SBIR Phase II - SMM DoD 22.4D)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (AFWERX), DoD SBIR Program
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED – Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a proxy for runway structural integrity assessment, enabling the Air Force to rapidly evaluate whether runways can safely support C-130 Hercules aircraft operations. The technical approach involved hardware integration (PCB fabrication, antenna modules), soil integrity algorithm development, field testing (local and at Tyndall AFB), and final reporting. All 8 milestones (M4–M8) were successfully delivered on time or ahead of schedule with Air Force TPOC acceptance.
