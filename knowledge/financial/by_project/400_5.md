# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED – Successfully completed (all deliverables accepted as of 2025-07-21)

**Project Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a key variable for assessing runway structural integrity and landing area suitability. The team (BST, OMS, and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case. Soil integrity is quantified as cone index and compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. Technology successfully transitioned to DIU Blue List as of 2025-07-24.
