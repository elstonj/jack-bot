# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (AFWERX SBIR Phase II - X22.4D)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II, AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Scope Summary (from Asana & Proposals):**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a proxy for runway structural integrity assessment, specifically for C-130 Hercules landing suitability. The team (BST, OMS, and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case, quantifying soil integrity as cone index and comparing against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. All 85 tasks completed with 100% delivery rate; final report delivered and accepted July 21, 2025.
