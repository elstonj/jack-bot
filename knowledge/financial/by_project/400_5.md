# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of Defense), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 to July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED in Asana; Active with customer (awaiting approval for Deliverable #5 payment)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer system for measuring soil moisture to map soil integrity and landing area conditions. Technology adapted for Department of the Air Force end-user needs in partnership with NASA and Orbital Micro Systems.
