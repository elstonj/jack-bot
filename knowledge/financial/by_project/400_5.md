# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping)
- **Project Code:** 400-5 / X22.4
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Major Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS) – $605,000 subcontract
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** – Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a proxy for runway structural integrity, supporting Air Force operational planning for C-130 Hercules aircraft operations. Technical outputs include UAS-integrated hardware, soil integrity algorithms, and field-validated mapping capability tested at Tyndall AFB.
