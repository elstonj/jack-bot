# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED – Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to assess runway structural integrity for landing area suitability. The team adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case: determining if runways can safely support C-130 Hercules aircraft operations. Key deliverables include UAS-based L-band radiometer hardware integration, soil integrity algorithms (cone index vs. soil moisture relationship), RevD data processing web interface, and sensor protective cover design.
