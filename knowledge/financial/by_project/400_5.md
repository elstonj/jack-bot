# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping (Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (Department of the Air Force), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** (Project complete as of July 2025)
- **TPOC:** Jason Foley (jason.foley.1@us.af.mil)
- **Billing Contact:** Thomas Shea, CO (thomas.shea.3@us.af.mil)

**Project Description (from Asana & Proposals):**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer (in partnership with NASA, Orbital Micro Systems, and CET) for mapping high-resolution soil moisture. The technology has been adapted for DAF end-user to map soil integrity, specifically for landing area assessment. Soil moisture measurement is critical for understanding soil strength, slipperiness, and stickiness—enabling rapid validation of runway integrity for C-130 Hercules aircraft operations.
