# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping)
- **Project Code:** 400-5 / X22.4
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED – Successfully completed with all deliverables accepted

### Scope
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to assess runway/landing area suitability for military aircraft operations (specifically C-130 Hercules). The team adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case. Key technical outputs include UAS-based L-band radiometer hardware integration, soil integrity algorithms (cone index vs. volumetric water content), RevD data processing web interface, and sensor protective cover. Testing conducted locally and at Tyndall Air Force Base.
