# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (AFWERX SBIR Phase II - X22.4 Soil Moisture Mapping)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2023 – July 2025 (contract due date: 2025-07-11)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025**

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer system for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a key variable to understand soil integrity—enabling assessment of landing area suitability for Air Force operations. BST collaborated with subcontractors Orbital Micro Systems (OMS) and CET to adapt NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case, quantifying soil integrity as cone index and comparing against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. All deliverables completed and accepted by Air Force TPOC; final payments closed out November 2025.
