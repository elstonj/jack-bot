# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (AFWERX SBIR Phase II X22.4D)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a key variable for assessing runway structural integrity and landing area suitability for C-130 aircraft. The team (BST prime, with Orbital Micro Systems as subcontractor, and CET as collaborator) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case, with soil integrity quantified as cone index and compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements.
