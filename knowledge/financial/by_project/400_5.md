# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping)
- **Project Code:** 400-5 / X22.4
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
BST developed and validated a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a proxy for runway structural integrity assessment, enabling the Air Force to evaluate landing area suitability. The team adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case, quantifying soil integrity through cone index vs. volumetric water content (VWC) comparisons. All 8 milestones completed and accepted by Air Force TPOC; final report delivered July 2025; final OMS payment completed November 2025.
