# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (SBIR Phase II - SMM DoD 22.4D)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (AFWERX), DoD SBIR Program
- **Contract Type:** Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Black Swift Technologies, in partnership with subcontractor Orbital Micro Systems (OMS), developed and validated a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity on military runways. The system measures soil moisture as a proxy for runway structural integrity by comparing cone index measurements against volumetric water content (VWC). The technology was adapted from NASA soil moisture mapping research for Air Force landing area suitability assessment. All deliverables were completed and accepted by the Air Force TPOC (Jason Foley) as of July 2025, with final project closeout by November 2025.
