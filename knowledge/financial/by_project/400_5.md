# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025**

**Brief Description of Scope:**
BST developed and validated a UAS-based L-band radiometer system for mapping soil moisture to assess runway integrity. The system measures volumetric water content (VWC) and derives soil integrity metrics (cone index) to determine suitability for C-130 aircraft operations. The team (BST prime, with Orbital Micro Systems as subcontractor) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use. All deliverables were completed and accepted; project is now fully closed.
