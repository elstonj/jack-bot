# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping)
- **Project Code:** 400-5 / X22.4
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (USAF), AFWERX
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED – Successfully completed with all deliverables accepted

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a key variable to understanding soil integrity—as soil moisture increases, soil becomes more slippery and sticky while decreasing in strength. The team (BST, OMS, and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case: mapping landing area suitability for C-130 aircraft operations. Soil integrity is quantified as cone index and compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. Key technical outputs include UAS-based L-band radiometer hardware integration, soil integrity algorithms, RevD data processing web interface, and sensor protective cover. Testing locations included local field testing and Tyndall Air Force Base.
