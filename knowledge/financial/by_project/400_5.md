# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (X22.4 AFWERX SBIR Phase II - Soil Moisture Mapping)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II via AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS) – $605,000 subcontract
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED – Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025**

**Scope:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity via soil moisture measurements. System measures volumetric water content (VWC) as proxy for soil structural integrity (cone index) to assess runway suitability for C-130 aircraft operations. Technical team adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case. All 8 milestones completed; final deliverables accepted by TPOC (Jason Foley) as of July 2025; final OMS payment processed November 19, 2025.
