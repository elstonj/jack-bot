# Project 400-5 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (AFWERX SBIR Phase II X22.4)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (AFWERX)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025**

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Black Swift Technologies (prime contractor) and Orbital Micro Systems (subcontractor) developed and validated a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a proxy for runway structural integrity, enabling Air Force operational planning for C-130 aircraft landings. Key technical outputs include hardware integration, soil integrity algorithms (cone index vs. volumetric water content), data processing web interfaces, and protective sensor covers. All deliverables accepted by Air Force TPOC; final payments and reporting closed out November 2025.
