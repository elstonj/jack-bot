## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Runway Integrity Validation through Soil Moisture Measurements from a Small UAS (AFWERX SBIR Phase II X22.4D)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Prime Contractor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems, Inc. (OMS)
- **Period of Performance:** November 2023 – July 2025 (21 months)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025
- **Key Contacts:**
  - Jason Foley, Primary TPOC (jason.foley.1@us.af.mil)
  - Thomas Shea, Contracting Officer (thomas.shea.3@us.af.mil)

### Scope Summary
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a key variable for understanding soil structural suitability, adapted for Air Force use case: mapping landing area (runway) suitability. Soil integrity is quantified as cone index and compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements.
