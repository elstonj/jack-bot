### Scope Summary
Black Swift Technologies executed a comprehensive long-range fixed-wing aircraft evaluation and DoD autopilot enablement engagement for By Light. Work included SwiftCore integration, truck-mounted launch platform development, flight data collection and analysis, G2 design finalization, COTS procurement (wings, tails, test fuselages, propellers, 3D-printed components), prototype assembly and ground integration testing, flight test campaign with multiple propeller configurations and payload testing, and final design documentation transfer. All major milestones and deliverables were completed on or ahead of schedule between October and December 2025.
