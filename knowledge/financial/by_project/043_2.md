**Brief Description of Scope:**
Design, prototype, and conduct flight testing of a long-range fixed-wing aircraft platform (Mustang variant) with SwiftCore integration and DoD autopilot enablement. Core deliverables include SwiftCore integration, G2 design finalization, COTS procurement, prototype assembly, ground integration, flight testing, and final design package. Post-delivery: two additional flight opportunities (one NOAA clear-air test flight confirmed for late July/early August 2026; one flight TBD).
