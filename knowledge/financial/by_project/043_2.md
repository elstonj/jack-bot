  1. Most COTS procurement and materials costs were either absorbed into the fixed-price labor milestones (no separate line-item invoicing) or sourced through vendor relationships not captured in the [043-2] project coding within QuickBooks.

- Reimbursable invoices were not coded to [043-2] in QuickBooks, or
