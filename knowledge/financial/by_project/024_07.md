3. **Significant Cash Outflow**  
   Total documented spend: **$3.66 million** over 16 months (Jul 2024 – Oct 2025).

4. **Highly Volatile Monthly Spending**  
   - Range: $222 (Nov 2024) to $528K (Mar 2025)
   - Suggests multiple concurrent projects with uneven milestone/invoice timing
   - Does not appear to follow a predictable linear burn rate

5. **No Payment Data**  
   QuickBooks actuals show expenses incurred/invoiced but provide **no information on cash received, outstanding receivables, or collection status.** This is a critical gap for assessing financial health.

6. **Categories Missing**  
   - No cost breakdown by labor, materials, equipment, travel, or overhead
   - Spending appears to be coded by **recipient/project** rather than **expense category**
   - Limits ability to identify cost-control issues within traditional budget categories

### Concerns

- **Lack of visibility into which projects are profitable or at risk**
- **No budget variance analysis possible** — cannot determine if any sub-project is overspent
- **Cash flow timing concerns** — cannot assess whether revenue is keeping pace with expenses
- **Data incompleteness** — missing contract details, invoice-level transaction data, and payment status

### Recommendations

1. **Clarify project structure:** Confirm whether "024-07" is a single project, a fiscal period, or an organizational code
2. **Provide contract and budget documentation:** Supply approved budgets, CLINs, contract values, and periods of performance for each sub-project
3. **Obtain invoice-level detail from QuickBooks:** Export transaction journal with invoice numbers, dates, customers, and payment status
