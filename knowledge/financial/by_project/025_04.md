## Project Overview

**Project Name:** Next-Generation Methane Monitoring for Orphaned Wells: Leveraging Drone Technology and Continuous Ground-Based Solution

**Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Fossil Energy and Carbon Management (FECM)

**Contract Type:** DOE FECM Program (specific topic/solicitation number not provided in available documents)

**Period of Performance:** Data available April 2025 – October 2025 (partial year shown in actuals)

**Scope Description:** Integrated methane detection and monitoring system for orphaned well sites, combining Black Swift S2 UAS equipped with Quanta3 methane sensor, flux towers with eddy covariance monitoring, FluxMapper™ software for data analysis, and HOO-nah remote connectivity devices. Project aims to advance FluxMapper™ technology from TRL-4/5 to TRL-6, achieving 0.2 kg/h methane detection sensitivity. Three-phase development with Go/No-Go decision points.
