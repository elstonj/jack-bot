# Project 025-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 025-04  
**Project Name:** Next-Generation Methane Monitoring for Orphaned Wells: Leveraging Drone Technology and Continuous Ground-Based Solution  
**Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Fossil Energy and Carbon Management (FECM)  
**Contract Type:** DOE FECM Program (specific solicitation number not provided in available documents)  
**Period of Performance:** Data available from April 2025 – October 2025 (ongoing)  
**BST Products:** S2 UAS with Quanta3 methane sensor, FluxMapper™ software, HOO-nah device (HPWT)

**Scope Summary (from Proposal, dated 2025-04-09):**  
Integrated methane detection and monitoring system for orphaned well sites combining Black Swift's drone platforms with flux towers, continuous ground monitoring, and environmental analysis software. Project aims to advance FluxMapper™ technology from TRL-4/5 to TRL-6, achieving 0.2 kg/h methane detection sensitivity with a cost-effective, scalable monitoring approach.
