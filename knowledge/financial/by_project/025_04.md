## Key Deliverables & Milestones

The DOE FECM methane monitoring proposal outlines a three-phase development structure with Go/No-Go decision points:

### Proposed Phase Structure:
1. **Phase 1:** System integration and initial performance validation
   - Integrate S2 UAS with Quanta3 methane sensor
   - Deploy flux towers and continuous ground monitoring
   - Validate FluxMapper™ data processing
   - **Target:** Demonstrate 0.2 kg/h methane detection sensitivity

2. **Phase 2:** Field testing and algorithm refinement
   - Multi-site orphaned well monitoring campaigns
   - Advance FluxMapper™ technology from TRL-4/5 to TRL-6
   - Cost-effectiveness demonstration

3. **Phase 3:** Scalability and commercialization planning
