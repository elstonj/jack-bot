# Project 025-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** Next-Generation Methane Monitoring for Orphaned Wells: Leveraging Drone Technology and Continuous Ground-Based Solution

**Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Fossil Energy and Carbon Management (FECM)

**Contract Type:** DOE FECM Program (specific solicitation number not provided in available documents)

**Period of Performance:** Not specified in available documents

**Brief Description of Scope:**
An integrated methane detection and monitoring system for orphaned well sites, combining Black Swift Technologies' S2 UAS platform equipped with Quanta3 methane sensor, flux towers with eddy covariance systems, and FluxMapper™ software for data analysis. The project aims to advance FluxMapper™ technology from TRL-4/5 to TRL-6, achieving 0.2 kg/h methane detection sensitivity and demonstrating a cost-effective, scalable alternative to traditional orphaned well monitoring. The proposal includes three-phase development with Go/No-Go decision points.
