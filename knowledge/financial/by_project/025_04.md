**Project Name:** Next-Generation Methane Monitoring for Orphaned Wells: Leveraging Drone Technology and Continuous Ground-Based Solution

**Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Fossil Energy and Carbon Management (FECM)

**Contract Type:** DOE FECM Program (specific solicitation number not provided in available documents)

**Period of Performance:** Data available April 2025 – October 2025 (incomplete project; full PoP not specified)

**Scope:** Integrated methane detection and monitoring system for orphaned well sites, combining Black Swift S2 UAS with Quanta3 methane sensor, eddy covariance flux towers, FluxMapper™ software analysis, and HOO-nah remote connectivity devices.
