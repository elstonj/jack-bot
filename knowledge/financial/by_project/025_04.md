# Project 025-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Code:** 025-04

**Project Name:** Next-Generation Methane Monitoring for Orphaned Wells: Leveraging Drone Technology and Continuous Ground-Based Solution

**Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Fossil Energy and Carbon Management (FECM)

**Contract Type:** DOE FECM Program (specific solicitation number not provided in available documents)

**Proposal Submission Date:** 2025-04-09

**Period of Performance:** Not specified in available documents

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Integrated methane detection and monitoring system for orphaned well sites combining Black Swift Technologies' S2 UAS with flux towers, continuous ground monitoring, and FluxMapper™ software. Project aims to advance FluxMapper™ technology from TRL-4/5 to TRL-6, achieving 0.2 kg/h methane detection sensitivity. Three-phase development structure with Go/No-Go decision points. (Source: Proposal document)
