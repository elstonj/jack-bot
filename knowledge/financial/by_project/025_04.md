### DOE Methane Monitoring for Orphaned Wells (S0 Hurricane)
**Source: Proposals & Reports – "Next-Generation Methane Monitoring…" proposal, 2025-04-09**

- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Fossil Energy and Carbon Management
- **Objective:** Advance FluxMapper™ methane detection technology from TRL-4/5 to TRL-6
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Integration of S2 UAS with Quanta3 methane sensor for aerial detection
  - Eddy covariance tower deployment with LI-COR methane evaluation
  - FluxMapper™ data analysis software for flux calculation
  - Three-phase development with Go/No-Go decision points
  - Demonstration of 0.2 kg/h methane detection sensitivity
- **Financial Implication:** Largest single allocation in 025-04 portfolio ($131.6K in Apr, $36K in May, $140.4K in Jun, $105.6K in Aug, $70K in Jul = **$483.6K cumulative through Oct**, with additional $242.3K deferred in Oct)

- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DAF AFWERX)
- **Program:** SBIR Phase II – Soil Moisture Mapping (2022.4)
- **Contract Number:** 400-5
- **Current Status:** Milestone #7 (progress phase)
