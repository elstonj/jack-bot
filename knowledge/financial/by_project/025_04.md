# Project 025-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 025-04  
**Project Name:** Next-Generation Methane Monitoring for Orphaned Wells: Leveraging Drone Technology and Continuous Ground-Based Solution  
**Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Fossil Energy and Carbon Management (FECM)  
**Contract Type:** DOE FECM program (specific solicitation number not provided in proposal)  
**Proposal Submission Date:** 2025-04-09  
**Period of Performance:** Not specified in provided documents  
**Brief Description:** Integrated methane detection and monitoring system for orphaned well sites, combining Black Swift S2 UAS equipped with Quanta3 methane sensor, eddy covariance towers with LI-COR methane evaluation systems, FluxMapper™ software analysis, and HOO-nah remote connectivity device. Project aims to advance FluxMapper™ technology from TRL-4/5 to TRL-6 with 0.2 kg/h methane detection sensitivity.
