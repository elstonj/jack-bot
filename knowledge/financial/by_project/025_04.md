   Project Code 025-04 invoicing appears to reflect a **company-wide portfolio** of 8–10+ active projects, not a single project. Without a consolidated P&L or project-specific profit/loss statement, determining if 025-04 as a whole is on budget is impossible.

   Monthly invoicing ranges from $70K (July) to $530K+ (Nov), a 7.5× swing. This suggests:
   - Milestone-based or phase-gated contract structures (common for SBIR/STTR)
   - Project start/ramp timing variation

3. **S0 Hurricane Dominance**  
   S0 Hurricane accounts for ~33% of total invoicing ($669K of $2.02M) across all 8 months, with the highest single concentration in Nov ($185.8K). Unknown if:
   - This is a large production contract nearing completion
   - Multiple sub-projects bundled under one name
   - Invoicing is on schedule relative to contract ceiling

   Navy SBIR + Navy STTR combined: ~$238K (Nov–Oct months show ~$49K each month, suggesting regular milestone invoicing)  
   NASA Persistence + NASA SBIR: ~$109K across multiple months

   By Light Mustang, By Light Prototype, and other vendor invoicing ($305K combined) represents potential subcontractor costs or equipment purchases. Without GL detail, cannot determine if these are cost-plus pass-throughs or firm fixed-price items.

6. **Missing Payment & Receivables Data**
