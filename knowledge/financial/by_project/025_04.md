# Project 025-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** Next-Generation Methane Monitoring for Orphaned Wells: Leveraging Drone Technology and Continuous Ground-Based Solution

**Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Fossil Energy and Carbon Management (FECM)

**Contract Type:** DOE FECM program (specific solicitation number not provided in available documents)

**Period of Performance:** Data available April 2025 – September 2025 (incomplete; full PoP not specified in provided documents)

**Scope Description:** Integrated methane detection and monitoring system for orphaned well sites, combining Black Swift S2 UAS equipped with Quanta3 methane sensor, flux tower ground-based continuous monitoring, FluxMapper™ software analytics, and remote data transmission via HOO-nah device. Project aims to advance FluxMapper™ technology from TRL-4/5 to TRL-6 with 0.2 kg/h methane detection sensitivity.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**From Proposals (not from Asana project management data):**

The DOE FECM methane monitoring proposal describes a **three-phase development structure with Go/No-Go decision points** to de-risk advancement toward TRL-6. Specific milestones and dates are not detailed in the proposal excerpt provided, but the project includes:

1. **Drone-based methane detection:** S2 UAS with Quanta3 sensor integration
2. **Ground-based continuous monitoring:** Eddy covariance flux towers with LI-COR systems
3. **Software analytics:** FluxMapper™ data integration and processing
4. **Remote data transmission:** HOO-nah device (HPWT) deployment at orphaned well sites
5. **Field validation:** Demonstration of 0.2 kg/h methane detection sensitivity
