## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 025-04  
**Project Name:** Next-Generation Methane Monitoring for Orphaned Wells: Leveraging Drone Technology and Continuous Ground-Based Solution  
**Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Fossil Energy and Carbon Management (FECM)  
**Contract Type:** DOE FECM program (specific solicitation number not provided in available documents)  
**Period of Performance:** Data available from 2025-04 through 2025-11 (incomplete final month)  
**Brief Description of Scope:** Integrated methane detection and monitoring system for orphaned well sites, combining Black Swift S2 UAS equipped with Quanta3 methane sensor, eddy covariance flux towers with LI-COR systems, FluxMapper™ software for data analysis, and HOO-nah remote connectivity device.
