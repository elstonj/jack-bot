## Project Overview

**Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Fossil Energy and Carbon Management (FECM)  
**Contract Type:** DOE FECM Program (specific solicitation number not provided in available documents)  
**Period of Performance:** Data available from 2025-04 through 2025-10; full contract period not specified in source documents  
**Brief Description:** Integrated methane detection and monitoring system for orphaned well sites, combining Black Swift Technologies' S2 UAS platform with flux towers, continuous ground monitoring, and FluxMapper™ software analysis.
