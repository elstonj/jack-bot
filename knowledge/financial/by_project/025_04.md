## Project Overview
**Project Name:** Next-Generation Methane Monitoring for Orphaned Wells: Leveraging Drone Technology and Continuous Ground-Based Solution

**Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Fossil Energy and Carbon Management (FECM)

**Contract Type:** DOE FECM Program (Phase-based development with Go/No-Go decision points)

**Submission Date:** 2025-04-09 (Proposal)

**Period of Performance:** Not explicitly provided in source documents. *Data not available from Proposals.*

**Scope Description:** Integrated methane detection and monitoring system for orphaned well sites combining Black Swift S2 UAS with Quanta3 methane sensor, eddy covariance flux towers, continuous ground monitoring, FluxMapper™ software for data analysis, and HOO-nah remote connectivity device. Project aims to advance FluxMapper™ technology from TRL-4/5 to TRL-6, achieving 0.2 kg/h methane detection sensitivity.
