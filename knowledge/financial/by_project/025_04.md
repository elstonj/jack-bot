# Project 025-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 025-04  
**Project Name:** Next-Generation Methane Monitoring for Orphaned Wells: Leveraging Drone Technology and Continuous Ground-Based Solution  
**Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Fossil Energy and Carbon Management (FECM)  
**Contract Type:** DOE FECM Program (specific solicitation number not provided in available documents)  
**Period of Performance:** Data available from Source: Proposals & Reports indicate submission date 2025-04-09; QuickBooks actuals span 2025-04 through 2025-10  
**Brief Description of Scope:** Integrated methane detection and monitoring system for orphaned well sites, combining Black Swift S2 UAS with Quanta3 methane sensor, eddy covariance flux towers, FluxMapper™ software for data integration and analysis, and HOO-nah remote connectivity device. Project aims to advance FluxMapper™ technology from TRL-4/5 to TRL-6 with 0.2 kg/h methane detection sensitivity.
