# Project 025-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Name:** Next-Generation Methane Monitoring for Orphaned Wells: Leveraging Drone Technology and Continuous Ground-Based Solution

**Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Fossil Energy and Carbon Management (FECM)

**Contract Type:** DOE FECM Program (specific solicitation number not provided in available documents)

**Period of Performance:** Data available April 2025 – November 2025 (incomplete; full PoP not specified in proposals/reports)

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Integrated methane detection and monitoring system for orphaned well sites, combining Black Swift Technologies' S2 UAS with flux towers, continuous ground monitoring, and FluxMapper™ software. Project aims to advance FluxMapper™ technology from TRL-4/5 to TRL-6, achieving 0.2 kg/h methane detection sensitivity. Structured three-phase development with Go/No-Go decision points.
