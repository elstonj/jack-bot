# Project 025-04 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Name:** Next-Generation Methane Monitoring for Orphaned Wells: Leveraging Drone Technology and Continuous Ground-Based Solution

**Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Fossil Energy and Carbon Management (FECM)

**Contract Type:** DOE FECM Program (specific solicitation number not provided in available documents)

**Submission Date:** 2025-04-09 (Proposal date)

**Scope:** Integrated methane detection and monitoring system for orphaned well sites, combining Black Swift S2 UAS equipped with Quanta3 methane sensor, flux towers, continuous ground monitoring, FluxMapper™ software analysis, and HOO-nah remote connectivity. Project aims to advance FluxMapper™ technology from TRL-4/5 to TRL-6, achieving 0.2 kg/h methane detection sensitivity with three-phase development including Go/No-Go decision points.
