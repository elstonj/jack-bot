## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Status: ⚠️ **CAUTIOUS** — On Budget Invoiced; Payment Friction Remains

#### Key Findings:

1. **Budget Utilization**: 
   - **$59,664.21 invoiced of $90,000 approved (66% utilization).**
   - **$30,335.79 remains available** for final reporting (Milestone 4b, $10k) and additional validation/support work under new per-flight-day billing model.
   - **On track financially** if remaining work stays within approved envelope.

2. **Payment Status — ⚠️ CONCERN**:
   - **$31,000 outstanding** (invoices #1769 and #1770) as of July 1, 2026.
   - **Payment friction documented**: Asana notes EMASS "declined to pay final milestone ($35k) due to incomplete final report" (May–June 2026).
   - **Restructured deal reflects customer pushback**: Original Milestone 4 ($35k lump sum) split into Milestone 4a ($25k, signed June 9) + Milestone 4b ($10k, pending).
   - **Risk**: If EMASS continues to resist final payment, BST faces cash collection risk on remaining $10k (Milestone 4b).

3. **Cost Category Variance**:
   - **Direct Labor/Validation**: Early phases invoiced higher than expected (~$55k for Milestones 1–2 vs. ~$50k proposed). Validation flights (Milestone 4a) invoiced on time ($25k).
   - **Final Reports (Milestone 4b)**: **$0 invoiced to date; $10,000 still pending.** Dan Prendergast owns; no due date currently set. This is a **financial risk** — if final reporting is delayed indefinitely (as validation flights were April–July), invoicing and payment will stall.
   - **Per-Flight-Day Billing**: New model introduced June 2026 for future work suggests customer seeking cost control; indicates EMASS may request additional validation flights but wants hourly/daily rates rather than fixed milestones. No invoices under this model yet.

4. **Schedule Impact on Budget**:
   - **Validation flights indefinitely postponed May–June 2026** due to EMASS controller delivery blocker. This delayed Milestone 4a invoicing by 2–3 months (originally due Mar 11, invoiced June 9).
   - **Final reporting phase now unblocked** (Jul 13, 2026), but no due date or invoicing timeline set. Risk of further delay.
   - **Burn rate**: 66% invoiced over ~8 months (Nov 2025–Jul 2026) = ~8% per month. If remaining 34% ($30.3k) extended indefinitely, burn rate drops significantly, indicating potential underutilization or delayed billing.

5. **Priority & Organizational Risk**:
   - **Asana risk signal (Apr 30, 2026)**: Maciej Stachura ranked EMASS as Priority #3 for closure (after S3 IRAD and S0-VTOL projects). Suggests BST is deprioritizing this project.
   - **Implication**: If internal team focus shifts elsewhere, final reporting (Milestone 4b) may slip further, delaying the $10k invoice and resolution.

6. **Cash Flow Risk Summary**:
   - ✅ Milestone 4a ($25k) invoiced and likely triggering payment discussions (still outstanding as of Jul 1).
   - ⚠️ Milestone 4b ($10k) not yet invoiced; no due date set; depends on Dan Prendergast's final report delivery.
   - ⚠️ Customer payment resistance documented; restructured deal signals lower confidence in fixed-price model going forward.
   - ⚠️ Per-flight-day billing model going forward suggests EMASS seeking flexibility; future revenue stream uncertain.
