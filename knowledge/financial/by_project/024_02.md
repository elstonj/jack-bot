## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposals & Related Documents**

Project 024-02 appears to be part of BST's broader methane leak detection (MLD) and flux mapping initiative. Related context from draft final reports indicates:

- **Core Deliverable:** Flux Maps for UAS Phase 1 — a capability to map methane flux across multi-km areas using eddy covariance analysis
- **Technical Innovation:** "Wind memory" technique enabling survey of 4km × 4km areas with only 5-10 downwind passes, producing visual flux maps showing leak distribution and magnitude
- **Integration:** Q3 methane sensor integrated to S2 UAS platform
