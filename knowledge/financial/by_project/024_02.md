## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**From Proposals/Reports (NASA SBIR Phase II Context):**

The Phase II Proposal Checklist indicates this Phase 1 flux maps work is foundational to a larger Phase II SBIR proposal to NASA focused on wildland firefighting. Expected Phase II objectives include:

1. **Improved Fire Weather Forecasting** — Collect atmospheric data around active fires; assimilate into wind maps and evolution models for incident commander decision support.

2. **Automated UAS Deployment Site Selection** — Develop multi-modal tool optimizing UAS flight locations based on forecast impact, airspace, communications, and reachability.

3. **End-to-End Data Infrastructure & Integration** — Develop data pipeline and integrate meteorological products into Team Awareness Kit (TAK) platform for responders.

4. **Multi-UAS Demonstrations** — Conduct field demonstrations in prescribed burns and active wildfire conditions.
