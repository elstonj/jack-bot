### Phase II (Proposal in Development)
**Source:** Proposals & Reports (NASA SBIR Phase II Wildfire proposal draft, created 2024-01-24, modified 2024-02-02)

The Phase II proposal (not yet approved) outlines four core technical objectives:

1. **Improved Fire Weather Forecasting**
   - Collect atmospheric data in critical locations around fires
   - Assimilate data to generate wind maps and evolution predictions
   - Provide decision-support for incident commanders

2. **Automated UAS Deployment Site Selection**
   - Develop multi-modal tool for optimal UAS flight location selection
   - Build on Phase I Ensemble Sensitivity Analysis (ESA) and Observing System Experiment (OSE) methods
   - Optimize for: forecast impact, airspace coordination, communications, reachability

3. **End-to-End Data Infrastructure & Integration**
   - Integration with Team Awareness Kit (TAK) platform for incident commanders
   - Real-time meteorological product delivery

4. **Multi-UAS Demonstrations**
   - Demonstrations in prescribed burns and active wildfire conditions
