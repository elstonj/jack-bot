## Project Overview
- **Project Name:** Flux Maps for Unmanned Aircraft System: Phase 1
- **Client/Funding Source:** Black Swift Technologies LLC (internal project); also associated with Colorado Advanced Industries Accelerator Grant Program (Grant Award No. CTGG1-2023-3372)
- **Contract Type:** Fixed-firm price deliverable / Grant-funded development
- **Period of Performance:** 2/22/23 – 2/29/24 (per Colorado Advanced Industries grant period)
