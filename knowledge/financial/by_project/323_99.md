## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S4 UAS Range Safety Review / DIU/NAVAIR LC-ISR Gauntlet Evaluation
- **Client/Agency:** DIU (Defense Innovation Unit) / NAVAIR, via KrateoSky
- **Program Type:** Government evaluation / technical qualification (not a traditional SBIR, IDIQ, or commercial contract)
- **Period of Performance:** May–June 2027 (planned evaluation window)
- **Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is preparing the S4 unmanned aircraft system (UAS) for a Range Safety Review and participation in the DIU/NAVAIR Low-Cost ISR Gauntlet evaluation.
