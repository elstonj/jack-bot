   - **Aircraft Update & Testing:** $19,555 unbudgeted addition; critical for high-altitude volcanic operations (5,700 m+ elevation, 15,000+ ft AGL performance).

3. **Data Availability Limitation:**
   - QuickBooks export shows project 350-4 as a single consolidated entry ($124,230) classified under "government.md" as "Field operations, travel, rental, permissions" with no line-item expense breakdown by cost category.
   - Proposed budget structure from Drive documents (broken into Travel, Lodging, Meals, Labor, Aircraft, Shipping, NRE) cannot be directly reconciled line-for-line against QBO actuals without access to detailed GL account coding or project cost allocations in QuickBooks.
