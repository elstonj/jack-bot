### Analysis of Variance

**Source:** Drive Budget Docs (proposed 1-Week and 2-Week scenarios) vs. QuickBooks (Invoice 1655, actual charges 03/13/2025)

**Key Findings:**

1. **Aircraft Update & Testing Overrun:** +$19,555 unbudgeted
   - Not anticipated in original proposals
   - Necessary for high-altitude operations certification (>15,000 ft) at Nevado del Ruiz
   - Represents 16% cost increase over proposed 1-week deployment total

2. **Flight Operations Support Expansion:** $47,600 (13 days @ $3,400/day)
   - Exceeds initial proposal estimates for "Aircraft Rental & Insurance"
   - Reflects increased operational complexity and duration

3. **Labor (Flight Planning, Permissions, QA):** +$10,500 over 1-week proposal
   - Flight Planning: 45 hrs invoiced vs. 20 hrs proposed (+$4,375, or 225% of proposal)
   - Permissions Support: 30 hrs invoiced vs. 10 hrs proposed (+$3,500, or 300% of proposal)
   - QA/Archiving: 25 hrs invoiced vs. 10 hrs proposed (+$2,625, or 250% of proposal)
   - Indicates scope expansion due to permitting complexity and data processing requirements

4. **Overall Cost Growth:** Invoice total of $124,230 represents:
   - +$65,090 above 1-week proposal ($59,140) = **+110% cost growth**
   - +$14,230 above 2-week proposal ($109,230) = **+13% cost growth, but within approved budget**

**Conclusion:** Project was fully obligated and invoiced within the approved contract ceiling of $124,230.00. The cost increase from original 1-week proposal reflects expanded scope (aircraft certification testing, enhanced permitting support, increased flight planning complexity) and was absorbed within the higher 2-week deployment proposal and approved contract amount.
