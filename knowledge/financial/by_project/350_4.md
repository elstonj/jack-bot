## Budget by Cost Category

Comparison of Drive Budget Docs proposed amounts vs. QuickBooks actual invoice:

| Cost Category | Proposed Budget (1-Week Base) | Actual Invoiced (Invoice #1655) | Variance | % of Invoiced |
|---------------|-------------------------------|--------------------------------|----------|---------------|
| **Equipment Rental & Services** | $28,000.00 | $89,155.00 | +$61,155.00 | 71.8% |
| **Travel & Subsistence** | $12,740.00 | $16,175.00 | +$3,435.00 | 13.0% |
| **Data Processing & Support (Labor)** | $8,400.00 | $18,900.00 | +$10,500.00 | 15.2% |
| **TOTAL** | **$49,140.00** | **$124,230.00** | **+$75,090.00** | **100.0%** |

**Analysis:**

- **Equipment & Services** increased significantly ($61,155 above 1-week base):
  - 1-week base budget included only S2 rental ($14,000) and basic shipping ($8,000) = $22,000
  - Invoice added Flight Operations Support ($47,600 for 14 days vs. not explicitly budgeted) and Aircraft Update & Testing ($19,555 for high-altitude certification)
  - This suggests scope expansion for enhanced pre-deployment aircraft preparation and extended field operations team engagement

- **Travel & Subsistence** variance (+$3,435):
  - Slight overage vs. 1-week plan, likely due to extended field team presence (14 days of operations support vs. 7-day core mission window)

- **Data Processing & Support** increased (+$10,500):
  - Flight planning, permissions support, and data QA hours substantially increased (123 total hours invoiced vs. 48 hours in base 1-week plan)
  - Reflects complexity of Colombian regulatory approvals (AFAC, CENAPED, customs) and high-altitude mission planning

**Key Finding:** The contract amount of $124,230.00 reflects a scope expansion beyond the original 1-week deployment proposal ($49,140), incorporating enhanced aircraft testing/certification ($19,555) and extended flight operations support ($47,600) to ensure safe operations above 15,000 feet at Nevado del Ruiz.
