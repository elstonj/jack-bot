### Analysis & Observations

1. **Overall Budget Variance:** Actual spend ($124,230) is **110% over the 1-week proposed baseline** ($59,140), representing a $65,090 increase. However, this aligns with the **approved/contracted amount of $124,230**, indicating the contract was scoped to cover enhanced operations.

2. **Direct Labor Overspend:** Proposed NRE labor of $8,400 was exceeded by $10,500 (actual $18,900). Additional 60 hours (108 actual vs. 48 proposed) likely reflects expanded flight planning complexity for high-altitude operations and extended permitting support.

3. **Flight Operations Support:** The $47,600 line item (14 days @ $3,400/day) was **not present in the original 1-week proposed budget**, suggesting this was a scope addition negotiated with USGS, likely driven by the challenging high-altitude terrain and regulatory environment in Colombia.

4. **Aircraft Testing & Update Overspend:** $19,555 expended for aircraft certification for >15,000 ft operations, vs. base rental of $14,000. This $5,555 premium reflects specialized pre-deployment engineering work not explicitly captured in the original proposal.

5. **Shipping Overspend:** Actual shipping of $8,000 vs. proposed $3,000 ($5,000 variance), likely due to international freight and customs handling to Colombia.

6. **Travel & Subsistence Moderate Overspend:** +$3,435 (127% of base) — slightly higher lodging ($4,550 vs. $3,780), higher flight costs ($9,000 vs. $7,000), and car rental adjustments. Variance modest relative to overall project.

7. **No Cost Overrun Against Contract:** Despite exceeding the original 1-week proposal by $65,090, **the contract value of $124,230 was fully obligated and invoiced**, indicating USGS approved the expanded scope (Aircraft Update & Testing, enhanced Flight Operations Support) during project execution.
