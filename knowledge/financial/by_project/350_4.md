### Budget Analysis by Category

**Critical Variances (All Overspent):**

1. **Direct Labor (+225%, +$10.5K):** 
   - Proposed: $8,400 (48 hrs @ $175/hr for 1-week deployment)
   - Actual: $18,900 (108 hrs @ $175/hr)
   - Driver: Expanded scope including 45 hrs flight planning (vs. 20 proposed), 30 hrs permissions support (vs. 10 proposed), 25 hrs QA/archiving (vs. 10 proposed), 8 hrs meetings
   - **Interpretation:** High-altitude operations and complex permitting required significantly more engineering and coordination effort than baseline 1-week deployment plan.

2. **Aircraft Operations & Rental (+340%, +$33.6K):**
   - Proposed: $14,000 (7 days @ $2,000/day for aircraft rental only)
   - Actual: $47,600 (14 days @ $3,400/day for Flight Operations Support)
   - Driver: Invoice line item "Flight Operations Support" at expanded daily rate ($3,400/day vs. $2,000/day) and doubled duration (14 days vs. 7 days)
   - **Interpretation:** Extended field operations and higher-intensity aircraft support requirements drove significant cost increase.

3. **Equipment (+$33.6K, entirely unbudgeted in baseline):**
   - Proposed: $0 (not in 1-week deployment scenario)
   - Actual: $33,555 (S2 Rental $14,000 + Aircraft Update & Testing $19,555)
   - Driver: High-altitude capability upgrade and aircraft testing for >15,000 ft operations
   - **Interpretation:** Pre-deployment aircraft modifications and specialized payload rental were necessary for volcanic monitoring at altitude.

4. **Shipping (+267%, +$5K):**
   - Proposed: $3,000
   - Actual: $8,000
   - Driver: Additional equipment (S2, launch box, rails, support box) required increased shipping volume.

5. **Other Categories (Travel, Lodging, Meals, Car Rental):** Moderate overages (111–175%) consistent with extended field duration and higher-than-budgeted daily rates.

**Summary:** The project consumed 325% of the 1-week deployment baseline budget, driven by aircraft testing/update work ($19.6K), expanded flight operations support (14 days vs. 7 days at higher daily rate), and increased engineering labor. The approved contract amount of $124,230 accommodated these overages, suggesting scope expansion was authorized. No budget variance is noted against the approved contract.
