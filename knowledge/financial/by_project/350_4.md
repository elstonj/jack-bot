1. **Full Contract Expended:** Invoice 1655 (03/13/2025) represents 100% of the $124,230.00 contract value. Contract is fully obligated with no remaining balance. This is consistent and healthy for a government contract executed as planned.

Deployment was originally planned for April 20, 2026, but rescheduled to Fall 2026 (per team communications April 17-20, 2026). **This rescheduling occurred after full invoice and payment, and does not trigger budget or contract modifications per available documents.**

   - **Contract appears administratively extended or re-tasked, but no modification documents or new invoices are evident in provided data.**
