  - As of November 22, 2026: **CRITICAL DELAYS** — 9 open tasks, multiple overdue milestones, 4 completed tasks
- **Brief Description of Scope:**
  Comprehensive UAS (uncrewed aerial system) volcanic data collection mission to monitor Nevado del Ruiz/Mexican volcano crater and lahar features. Mission includes photogrammetry of crater region and lahar-affected terrain (Barranca Huiloac, Barranca Nexpayantla), atmospheric gas measurements, flight planning, permissions coordination (AFAC—Federal Aviation Administration of Mexico), and post-mission data QA/archiving. High-altitude operations (5,700m–11,800m) with aircraft update/testing for >15,000 ft capability.
