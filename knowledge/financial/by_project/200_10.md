- **Full Project Name:** 2024-25 Aeropod (AREN) — AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate (Science Activation); Point of Contact: Geoffrey Bland (geoffrey.l.bland@nasa.gov)
- **Contract Type:** Cooperative Agreement / Science Activation Project (Award: NNX16AB95A)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024–March 2025 (project marked complete March 10, 2025)
- **Brief Description:** Commercialization and design refinement effort for BST's Aeropod atmospheric sensing platform. Multi-year AREN project continuation with significant subcontracting component.
