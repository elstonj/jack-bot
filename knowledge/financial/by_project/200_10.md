# Project 200-10 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2024-25 Aeropod (AREN) — AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate (Science Activation)
- **Contract Type:** Cooperative Agreement (NNX16AB95A)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024 (Calendar Year); Funding cycle concluded March 2025
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED — Marked complete as of March 10, 2025**
- **Point of Contact:** Geoffrey Bland (NASA), geoffrey.l.bland@nasa.gov

**Scope Description:**  
This was a commercialization and product improvement effort for the Aeropod product line, focusing on fin design optimization (thickness reduction, aspect ratio modification), particle sensor integration (Atmotube Pro), material evaluation, and delivery of training materials and educational supplies. Work was executed through a subcontractor-heavy model with Ted Miles (Senior Engineer Technician, Onancock VA) and Kay Rufty (Aerospace Engineer, Denver CO) as primary contributors. The project supported the AREN education network with hardware development, assembly, and distribution of AeroPod, TerraROVER, and PocketLab Weather systems.
