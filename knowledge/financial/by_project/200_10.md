# Project 200-10 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2024-25 Aeropod (AREN) — AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate (Science Activation)
- **Contract Type:** Cooperative Agreement / Science Activation Project (Award: NNX16AB95A)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024–March 2025 (funded through March 2025; project marked complete March 10, 2025)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED/COMPLETED

**Brief Scope Description:**  
Commercialization and design refinement of Black Swift Technologies' Aeropod atmospheric monitoring platform. Deliverables included updated fin design (drag/stability optimization), particle sensor transition (Atmotube Pro → Sensirion SEN55 with Arduino/Raspberry Pi logging), weather instrument refinement (Kestrel 5500), kit design updates, licensing/marketing strategy development, and production/assembly of educational systems (MiniCam 3 Aeropods, TerraROVER platforms, PocketLab weather systems).
