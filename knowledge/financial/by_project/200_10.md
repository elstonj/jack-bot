# Project 200-10 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2024–25 Aeropod (AREN) — AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate (Science Activation)
- **Contract Type:** Cooperative Agreement (NNX16AB95A)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024 through March 2025 (project marked complete March 10, 2025)
- **Brief Description:** Commercialization and design refinement effort for BST's Aeropod technology. Focus areas included fin design optimization, atmospheric particulate sensor transition (Atmotube Pro → Sensirion SEN55), weather instrument refinement (Kestrel 5500 alternatives), and educational kit development (TerraROVER platforms, PocketLab Weather Systems, MiniCam 3 Aeropods). Significant emphasis on licensing/commercialization strategy and multi-year educational outreach through partner institutions.
