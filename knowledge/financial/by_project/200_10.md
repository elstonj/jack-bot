# Project 200-10 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2024-25 Aeropod (AREN) — AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate (Science Activation), Cooperative Agreement NNX16AB95A
- **Contract Type:** Cooperative Agreement / Science Activation Project
- **Point of Contact:** Geoffrey Bland (geoffrey.l.bland@nasa.gov)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024–March 2025 (project completed March 10, 2025)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED / COMPLETED**

**Scope Summary:**  
Commercialization and design refinement of BST's Aeropod technology platform. Work included fin design optimization (drag/stability reduction), weather instrument integration (Kestrel 5500), atmospheric particulate sensor transition (Atmotube Pro → Sensirion SEN55 with Arduino/Raspberry Pi logging), TerraROVER platform assembly and distribution, PocketLab Weather Systems production, and business development for licensing/commercialization strategy. Multi-year activity spanning 2021–present; 2024 award focused on hardware production, assembly, and delivery to educational institutions and partners.
