# Project 200-10 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2024–25 Aeropod (AREN) — AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate (Science Activation)
- **Contract Type:** Cooperative Agreement (NNX16AB95A)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024 through March 2025 (project archived/completed March 10, 2025)
- **Brief Description:** Commercialization and design refinement of Black Swift Technologies' Aeropod weather/atmospheric sensing platform. Work included fin design optimization, particle sensor transitioning, weather instrument evaluation, licensing/marketing strategy, and educational kit assembly and distribution. Total consortium award $92,500; BST's allocated portion $16,461.12 (consulting services). Significant subcontracting to Ted Miles (Senior Engineer Technician, Onancock VA) and Kay Rufty (Aerospace Engineer, Denver CO).
