## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2024-25 Aeropod (AREN) — AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate (Science Activation)
- **NASA Point of Contact:** Geoffrey Bland (geoffrey.l.bland@nasa.gov)
- **Contract Type:** Cooperative Agreement / Science Activation Project (NNX16AB95A)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024–March 2025 (Project marked complete March 10, 2025)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED/COMPLETED
- **Brief Description:** Commercialization and design refinement effort for BST's Aeropod technology platform, including aerodynamic fin optimization, atmospheric particulate sensor upgrades (Atmotube Pro → Sensirion SEN55), weather instrument integration (Kestrel 5500), and business development activities (licensing strategy, subcontractor transition planning). Deliverables included production and distribution of 41+ MiniCam Aeropods, 32+ TerraROVER platforms, and 34+ PocketLab Weather Systems for educational workshops.
