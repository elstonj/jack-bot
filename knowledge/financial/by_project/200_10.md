# Project 200-10 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2024–25 Aeropod (AREN) — AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate (Science Activation)
- **Contract Type:** Cooperative Agreement (NNX16AB95A)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024–March 2025 (project marked complete March 10, 2025)
- **Primary Contact:** Geoffrey Bland (geoffrey.l.bland@nasa.gov)
- **Brief Description:** Commercialization and design refinement effort for Black Swift Technologies' Aeropod technology, including aerodynamic optimization (fin design, weight reduction), sensor upgrades (Sensirion SEN55 particulate sensor, Kestrel 5500 weather instrumentation), and business development activities (licensing submission, marketing strategy). Project also included production/assembly of MiniCam 3 Aeropods, TerraROVER platforms, and PocketLab weather systems for educational distribution.
