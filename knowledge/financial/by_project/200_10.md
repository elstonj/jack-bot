# Project 200-10 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** 2024–25 Aeropod (AREN) — AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network  
**Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate (Science Activation); Point of Contact: Geoffrey Bland  
**Contract Type:** Cooperative Agreement (NNX16AB95A)  
**Period of Performance:** 2024 through March 2025 (project marked complete March 10, 2025)  
**Project Status:** ARCHIVED/COMPLETED

**Scope:** Commercialization and design refinement of BST's Aeropod atmospheric sensing platform, including:
- Weather instrument design updates (Kestrel 5500 optimization and lighter-weight alternatives)
- Atmospheric particulate sensor transition (Atmotube Pro → Sensirion SEN55 with Arduino/Raspberry Pi logging)
- Fin design optimization (drag reduction, aspect ratio adjustment from 3:1 to 2:1)
- Materials evaluation (foam/foamboard alternatives)
- Licensing submission and marketing strategy
- Production and assembly of MiniCam 3 Aeropods, TerraROVER platforms, and PocketLab weather systems for educational distribution
