# Project 200-10 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2024-25 Aeropod (AREN) — AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate (Science Activation)
- **Contract Type:** Cooperative Agreement / Science Activation Project (NNX16AB95A)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024–March 2025 (project archived/completed March 10, 2025)
- **BST Allocation:** $16,461.12 of $92,500.00 total budget (Black Swift Technologies' consulting portion)
- **Brief Description:** Commercialization and design refinement effort for BST's Aeropod atmospheric sensing platform. Scope included fin design optimization, particle sensor transition (Atmotube Pro → Sensirion SEN55), weather instrument alternatives (Kestrel 5500 evaluation), and licensing/business development for product commercialization. Project also supported TerraROVER platform assembly and educational workshop distribution.
