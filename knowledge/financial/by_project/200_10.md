- **Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate (Science Activation)
- **Contract Type:** Cooperative Agreement / Science Activation Project
- **Contract Number:** NNX16AB95A
- **Period of Performance:** 2024–March 2025 (current award cycle; multi-year AREN project active 2021–present)
- **Status:** **ARCHIVED — Project marked complete as of March 10, 2025** *(Source: Asana)*

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Commercialization and product line improvement effort for the Aeropod platform, with emphasis on hardware optimization (fin design with reduced drag, aspect ratio reduction, alternate materials), particle sensor integration (Atmotube Pro), and delivery of training materials and educational supplies to NASA partners. Work executed primarily through subcontractors (Kay Rufty, Ted Miles) with BST technical consulting oversight. Project focused on MiniCam Aeropod production, TerraROVER platform refinement, and AeroPod commercialization feasibility exploration.
