# Project 200-10 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2024-25 Aeropod (AREN) — AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate (Science Activation)
- **Contract Type:** Cooperative Agreement (NNX16AB95A)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024 through March 2025 (project archived/completed March 10, 2025)

Project included significant subcontracted work with Ted Miles (Senior Technician) and Kay Rufty (Aerospace Engineer) for assembly, procurement, and technical support.
