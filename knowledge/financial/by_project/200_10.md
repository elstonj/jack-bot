# Project 200-10 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2024-25 Aeropod (AREN) – AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate (Science Activation)
- **Contract Type:** Cooperative Agreement / Science Activation Project (NNX16AB95A)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024 through March 2025 (project completion date: March 10, 2025)
- **Status:** **ARCHIVED/COMPLETED**
- **Brief Description:** Commercialization and design refinement effort for Black Swift Technologies' Aeropod weather observation platform. Work included fin design optimization, atmospheric particulate sensor integration (transition from Atmotube Pro to Sensirion SEN55), weather instrument evaluation (Kestrel 5500 alternatives), educational kit assembly and distribution (TerraROVER platforms, PocketLab weather systems), and licensing/commercialization strategy exploration.
