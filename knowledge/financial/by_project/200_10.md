# Project 200-10 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2024-25 Aeropod (AREN) — AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate (Science Activation)
- **Contract Type:** Cooperative Agreement / Science Activation Project
- **Contract Number:** NNX16AB95A
- **Period of Performance:** 2024–March 2025 (current award cycle; part of multi-year AREN project active 2021–present)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED — Marked complete as of March 10, 2025**
- **Point of Contact:** Geoffrey Bland (NASA), geoffrey.l.bland@nasa.gov

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Commercialization and advancement of the Aeropod product line for the AREN (AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network) initiative. Work included fin design optimization (reduced thickness and aspect ratio; material evaluation), particle sensor integration (Atmotube Pro), hardware procurement and assembly for educational workshops (MiniCam 3 Aeropods, TerraROVER platforms, PocketLab Weather Systems), and training materials development. Execution model was subcontractor-heavy, with work led by Ted Miles (Senior Technician) and Kay Rufty (Aerospace Engineer).
