# Project 200-10 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2024–25 Aeropod (AREN) — AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate (Science Activation)
- **NASA Point of Contact:** Geoffrey Bland (geoffrey.l.bland@nasa.gov)
- **Contract Type:** Cooperative Agreement / Science Activation Project (Award: NNX16AB95A)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024 through March 2025 (project marked complete March 10, 2025)
- **Brief Description:** Commercialization and design refinement of BST's Aeropod technology, including fin design optimization, atmospheric particulate sensor upgrades (Sensirion SEN55), weather instrument improvements (Kestrel 5500 alternatives), and educational kit development. Project also encompassed TerraROVER platform assembly, PocketLab Weather Systems distribution, and powered winch prototyping.
