# Project 200-10 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Project Name:** 2024-25 Aeropod (AREN) — AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate (Science Activation)
- **Contract Type:** Cooperative Agreement / Science Activation Project (NNX16AB95A)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024–March 2025 (project marked complete March 10, 2025)
- **Point of Contact:** Geoffrey Bland, geoffrey.l.bland@nasa.gov

**Scope:** Commercialization and design refinement of BST's Aeropod atmospheric sensing platform. Work included fin design optimization, particle sensor upgrades (Atmotube Pro → Sensirion SEN55 with Arduino/Raspberry Pi logging), weather instrument integration (Kestrel 5500), and delivery of educational workshop kits (MiniCam 3 Aeropods, TerraROVER platforms, PocketLab weather systems, and powered winch prototypes). Significant effort on licensing strategy and commercialization feasibility with subcontractor Ted Miles.
