# Project 200-10 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2024-25 Aeropod (AREN) — AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate (Science Activation)
- **Contract Type:** Cooperative Agreement / Science Activation Project (NNX16AB95A)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024–March 2025 (project completed March 10, 2025)
- **Project Code:** 200-10
- **Status:** ARCHIVED/COMPLETED

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Commercialization and design refinement of BST's Aeropod technology platform, including weather instrument integration (Kestrel 5500 alternatives), atmospheric particulate sensor upgrades (Sensirion SEN55 with Arduino/Raspberry Pi logging), fin design optimization (aspect ratio reduction, material evaluation), TerraROVER platform assembly and distribution, PocketLab Weather System assembly, and licensing/commercialization strategy development. Project included fabrication of 41+ MiniCam Aeropod systems, 32+ TerraROVER chassis, and 34+ PocketLab Weather units for educational institution distribution.
