## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2024-25 Aeropod (AREN) — AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate (Science Activation)
- **Contract Type:** Cooperative Agreement (Award # NNX16AB95A)
- **Period of Performance:** 2024–March 2025 (funding cycle concluded March 10, 2025)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED — Marked complete as of March 10, 2025**
- **Point of Contact:** Geoffrey Bland (NASA), geoffrey.l.bland@nasa.gov

**Brief Scope:**  
Commercialization effort for the Aeropod product line focusing on aerodynamic optimization (fin design, aspect ratio reduction, material evaluation), particle sensor integration (Atmotube Pro), and educational deliverables. Work included hardware design, assembly, procurement, and distribution of educational kits (MiniCam Aeropods, TerraROVER platforms, PocketLab Weather Systems).
