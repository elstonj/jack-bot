**Key Findings:**

   - Effective rate: ~$78.64/hour
   - Reasonable for NASA work; no overrun flagged

4. **Subcontractor Concentration:**
   - 65.4% of total spend ($193,573.63 of $295,847) goes to subcontractors
   - Largest: MicroFirm Engineering ($84,744.10), Embry Riddle ($56,290), Phase1 Aviation ($24,105), Northwind Composites ($20,696.53)
   - **Risk:** Heavy reliance on external vendors; monitor scope/schedule alignment

5. **Hardware & Equipment Spend:**
   - $58,545.53 (19.8% of budget) on components and assembly (S0 Wings, fuselage, PCB)

6. **Business Consulting (TABA):**

   - **Flag:** If this $13,600 is the full TABA cost, it exceeds the advertised $6,500 maximum. Confirm whether this is cost-share or part of a different funding source.

   - **Burn rate:** ~$59,000/month (if 5 months) suggests on-track spend but over budget ceiling

8. **Critical Gaps:**

   - **No detailed budget breakdown** from proposals; cannot analyze category-level variances
