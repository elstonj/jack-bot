# Project 024-05 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-05

**Project Name:** Improved Fire Weather Forecasting with Continuous and Efficient Monitoring

**Client/Agency:** USDA Forest Service (with NASA Ames and NOAA as partners)

**Contract Type:** NASA ROSES 2024 (A.47) — Federal research grant/cooperative agreement

**Period of Performance:** Proposed multi-year project (Year 1–3 phased operational approach outlined in Letter of Support, dated 2024-05-24)

**Scope Summary:** Deploy small uncrewed aircraft systems (UAS) for continuous observation of fire hotspots and fire perimeters to improve fire weather forecasting. Phased approach: Years 1–2 focus on controlled burns; Year 3 targets larger burns or active fire operations at night. USDA Forest Service to provide operational expertise and airspace integration guidance.
