# Project 024-05 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-05

**Project Name:** Improved Fire Weather Forecasting with Continuous and Efficient Monitoring

**Client/Agency:** USDA Forest Service (United States Forest Service) in partnership with NASA Ames and NOAA

**Program:** NASA ROSES 2024 (A.47)

**Contract Type:** Research/Development (NASA grant-funded project with USDA Forest Service support)

**Period of Performance:** Proposed multi-year effort (Year 1–3 phased operational approach per Letter of Support dated 2024-05-24)

**Brief Description of Scope:** 
Black Swift Technologies proposes to deploy small uncrewed aircraft systems (UAS) for continuous observation of fire hotspots and fire perimeters to improve fire weather forecasting. The phased approach includes Year 1–2 flights over controlled burns and Year 3 operations during active fire conditions. USDA Forest Service will provide operational expertise and guidance on integrating UAS into firefighting missions.
