# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II (Soil Moisture Mapping / Soil Integrity)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED – Successfully completed with all deliverables accepted
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to assess landing area suitability for the Air Force. Team adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case, measuring cone index against volumetric water content (VWC). Testing conducted locally and at Tyndall Air Force Base. Key outputs include UAS-integrated L-band radiometer hardware, soil integrity algorithms, web interface for data processing, and protective sensor cover.
