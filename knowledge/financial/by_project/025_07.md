## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED – Successfully Completed** (as of 2025-07-21)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to quantify soil integrity (cone index) for Air Force use case of mapping landing area suitability. BST led integration, testing, and algorithm development in partnership with subcontractor Orbital Micro Systems (OMS) and CET. All deliverables accepted by Air Force TPOC; final report delivered 2025-07-21.
