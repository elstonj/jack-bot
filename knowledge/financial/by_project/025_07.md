# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date); project archived November 2025
- **Brief Description of Scope:**  
  Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to understand soil integrity (cone index vs. volumetric water content) for Air Force use case of mapping landing area suitability. Team comprised BST, Orbital Micro Systems (OMS) subcontractor, and CET.

**Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED & COMPLETED** — All deliverables accepted by Air Force TPOC as of July 2025; final closeout completed November 2025.
