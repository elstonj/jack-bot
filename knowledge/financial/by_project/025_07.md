# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full project name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date); project archived as of November 2025
- **Brief description of scope:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to quantify soil integrity (cone index) as a key variable for Air Force use case of mapping landing area suitability. Team included BST, Orbital Micro Systems (OMS), and CET. Technology adapted from NASA soil moisture mapping systems.
