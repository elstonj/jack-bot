# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date); project archived November 2025
- **Project Status:** ✓ ARCHIVED – Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to understand soil integrity—a key variable for Air Force landing area suitability assessment. The project adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for DoD use, comparing soil integrity (cone index) against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. BST led the effort with subcontractor Orbital Micro Systems (OMS).
