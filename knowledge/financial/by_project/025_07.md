# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date); project closed out November 2025
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** – Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to assess landing area suitability for Air Force use cases. The team adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology to quantify soil integrity as cone index and compare against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. Key deliverables included soil integrity algorithm development, local and Tyndall AFB field testing, model refinement, and final reporting.

**Subcontractor:** Orbital Micro Systems (OMS) – $605,000 subcontract  
**Key Personnel:** Dan Prendergast (technical lead), Maciej Stachura, Meredith O'hara Needham, Nate Straus, Beck Cotter, Jack Elston
