## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date); project closed November 2025
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to determine landing area suitability for Air Force operations. The project adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case, with soil integrity quantified as cone index compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. Key team members: BST (prime), Orbital Micro Systems (OMS, subcontractor), and CET. Technical leadership by Dan Prendergast.
