- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date); project archived November 2025
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** – Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Description of Scope (from Asana):**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a key variable to understanding soil integrity. The team (BST, Orbital Micro Systems [OMS], and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case: mapping landing area suitability. Soil integrity is quantified as cone index and compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. All 85 tasks completed with 100% completion rate.
