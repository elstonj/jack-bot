# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date); project closed November 2025
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to understand soil integrity and support Air Force use case of mapping landing area suitability. The team (BST, Orbital Micro Systems, and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology to quantify soil integrity as cone index and compare against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements.
