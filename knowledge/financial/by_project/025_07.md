## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED – Successfully completed with all deliverables accepted

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a key variable to understanding soil integrity and adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case: mapping landing area suitability. The team (BST, Orbital Micro Systems, and CET) developed soil integrity algorithms quantifying cone index against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements, conducted local field testing, and performed validation testing at Tyndall Air Force Base.
