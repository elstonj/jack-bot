## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date); closed out November 2025
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to determine soil strength and landing area suitability for the U.S. Air Force. Key technical outputs include UAS-based L-band radiometer hardware integration, soil integrity algorithms (cone index vs. soil moisture relationship), field testing at Tyndall AFB, and final reporting.
