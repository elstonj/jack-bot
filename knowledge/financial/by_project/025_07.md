# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003 (from Asana)
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED – Successfully Completed** (from Asana)
- **Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to assess landing area suitability for the Air Force. The project adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for DoD use, quantifying soil integrity through cone index comparisons against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. Conducted local field testing and validation at Tyndall Air Force Base.
