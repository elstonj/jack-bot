## Project Overview

- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope (from Asana):**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to determine soil integrity (quantified as cone index) for Air Force use case of mapping landing area suitability. Team included Black Swift Technologies (BST), Orbital Micro Systems (OMS), and CET. Project involved system design, field testing at local sites and Tyndall AFB, algorithm development, and final reporting.
