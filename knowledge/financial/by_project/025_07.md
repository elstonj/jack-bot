# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED – Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture as a key variable to understanding soil integrity for Air Force use case: mapping landing area suitability. The team (BST, Orbital Micro Systems, and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology, with soil integrity quantified as cone index and compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements.
