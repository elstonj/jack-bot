## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED – Successfully completed (2025-07-21)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to assess landing area suitability for Air Force operations. The team adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for DoD use, developing algorithms that compare cone index against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. Testing occurred at local sites and Tyndall Air Force Base.
