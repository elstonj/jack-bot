# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** – Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to assess soil moisture content as a key variable for understanding soil strength and landing area suitability for the U.S. Air Force. The team (Black Swift Technologies, Orbital Micro Systems, and Colorado Engineering Testlab) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case by quantifying soil integrity as cone index and comparing against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements.
