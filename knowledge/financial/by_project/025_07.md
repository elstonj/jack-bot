- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to understand soil integrity—as soil moisture increases, soil becomes more slippery and sticky while decreasing in strength. The team (BST, Orbital Micro Systems [OMS], and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case: mapping landing area suitability. Soil integrity is quantified as cone index and compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements.
