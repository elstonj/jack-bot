# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** – Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to assess soil suitability for landing area evaluation. The project adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case, with soil integrity quantified as cone index and compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. Team included Black Swift Technologies (BST), Orbital Micro Systems (OMS) as subcontractor, and CET.
