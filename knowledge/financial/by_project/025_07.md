# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date); project archived November 2025
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to understand soil integrity (cone index vs. volumetric water content relationship) for Air Force landing area suitability assessment. Adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case.
