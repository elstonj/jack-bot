# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date); project archived as of November 2025
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** – Successfully completed and closed out
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to determine soil integrity (quantified as cone index vs. volumetric water content) for Air Force landing area suitability assessment. Team included Black Swift Technologies (BST), subcontractor Orbital Micro Systems (OMS), and CET, adapting NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case.
