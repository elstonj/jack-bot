# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (Contract due date)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED – Successfully completed

**Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to assess landing area suitability for Air Force operations. The team (BST, Orbital Micro Systems, and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case. Soil integrity is quantified as cone index and compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements.

**Key Personnel:** Dan Prendergast (technical lead), Maciej Stachura, Meredith O'Hara Needham, Nate Straus, Beck Cotter, Jack Elston

**Primary TPOC:** Jason Foley (jason.foley.1@us.af.mil); CO: Thomas Shea (thomas.shea.3@us.af.mil)
