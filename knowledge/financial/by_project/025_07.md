# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date); project closed out November 2025
- **Brief Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to determine landing area suitability for Air Force operations. Team collaboration included Black Swift Technologies, Orbital Micro Systems (OMS), and CET. Key technical outputs include UAS-based L-band radiometer hardware integration and soil integrity algorithms correlating cone index to soil moisture.
