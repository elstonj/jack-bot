# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date); project archived November 2025
- **Brief Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to determine landing area suitability by quantifying cone index and comparing against volumetric water content (VWC). Team included BST, subcontractor Orbital Micro Systems (OMS), and CET.
- **Project Status:** ✓ **COMPLETED AND ARCHIVED** — All deliverables accepted by Air Force TPOC as of July 2025; final closeout November 2025.
