# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date); project archived November 2025
- **Brief Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to assess landing area suitability for Air Force operations. Team includes Black Swift Technologies, Orbital Micro Systems (OMS) subcontractor, and CET. Adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case: mapping landing area suitability through soil integrity quantification (cone index vs. volumetric water content measurements).
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED** – Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025. All deliverables accepted by Air Force TPOC. No further work anticipated.
