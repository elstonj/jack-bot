# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date); project archived as of November 2025
- **Brief Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to determine landing area suitability by quantifying cone index and comparing against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. Project involved BST, Orbital Micro Systems (OMS), and CET in adapting NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case.
