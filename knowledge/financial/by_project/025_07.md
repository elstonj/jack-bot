# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (due date); project archived November 2025
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to quantify soil integrity (cone index vs. volumetric water content) for Air Force use case of mapping landing area suitability. Primary team: Black Swift Technologies (BST), Orbital Micro Systems (OMS), and CET.
