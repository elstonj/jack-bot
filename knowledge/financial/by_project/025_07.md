## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date); project archived November 2025
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED – Successfully completed and closed out**

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to quantify soil integrity (cone index) and compare against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. The team adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case: mapping landing area suitability. This is a collaborative effort between Black Swift Technologies, Orbital Micro Systems (OMS), and CET.
