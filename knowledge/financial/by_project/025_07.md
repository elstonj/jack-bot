# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date); project archived as of November 2025
- **Brief Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to assess soil integrity (cone index vs. volumetric water content) for Air Force landing area suitability assessment. Scope includes hardware integration, algorithm development, field testing at local sites and Tyndall AFB, and final reporting.
