**Brief Description of Scope (from Asana):**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to assess soil integrity—as soil moisture increases, soil becomes more slippery and sticky while decreasing in strength. BST, in partnership with Orbital Micro Systems (OMS) and CET, adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case: mapping landing area suitability. Soil integrity is quantified as cone index and compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements.
