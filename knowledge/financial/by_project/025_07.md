# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of the Air Force (DoD)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Period of Performance:** Award through 2025-07-11
- **Brief Description:** Phase II Small Business Innovation Research focused on soil moisture measurement using UAS-based L-band radiometry. Technology developed and validated with NASA for high-resolution soil moisture mapping. Air Force adaptation focuses on mapping landing area soil integrity (soil slipperiness, stickiness, and strength correlate with moisture levels). Includes custom payload development and systems manuals/documentation.
