# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED – Successfully completed

**Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to quantify soil integrity as cone index and support Air Force decision-making for runway/landing area suitability assessment. The project adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for DoD applications. Key deliverables included soil integrity algorithms, local and operational field testing (culminating at Tyndall AFB), algorithm refinement, and final reporting. All 85 tasks completed; all milestones delivered on or slightly late; all deliverables accepted by TPOC.
