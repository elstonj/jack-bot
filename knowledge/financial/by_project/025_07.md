## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 2025 (contract due date 2025-07-11)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** – Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to quantify soil integrity (cone index) compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. Adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case: mapping landing area suitability. Team included Black Swift Technologies, Orbital Micro Systems (OMS), and CET.
