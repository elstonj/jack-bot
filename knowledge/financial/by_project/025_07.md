## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED – Successfully completed as of July 21, 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to quantify soil integrity (cone index) for Air Force use case of mapping landing area suitability. The team (BST, Orbital Micro Systems, and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force applications. Key outputs include hardware integration, soil integrity algorithms, RevD data processing web interface, and sensor protective cover design.
