# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (completed and archived as of November 2025)
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED – Successfully completed and closed out**

**Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to determine landing area suitability for the Air Force. The project adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for military use case, quantifying soil integrity as cone index compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. Team included Black Swift Technologies (prime), Orbital Micro Systems (OMS) subcontractor, and Composite Engineering Technology (CET).
