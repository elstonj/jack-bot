- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date); project archived November 2025
- **Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED & COMPLETED** — All deliverables accepted; project closed as of November 2025

**Project Description (from Asana):**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to determine landing area suitability for the U.S. Air Force. The team (BST, Orbital Micro Systems, and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology to quantify soil integrity as cone index and compare against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. Key outputs: UAS-based L-band radiometer hardware integration, soil integrity algorithms, RevD data processing web interface, and sensor protective cover design.
