# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date); project archived as of November 2025
- **Brief Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to determine landing area suitability for aircraft. The team adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case, quantifying soil integrity as cone index compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. Primary subcontractor: Orbital Micro Systems (OMS).

**Project Status:** ARCHIVED – Successfully completed with all deliverables accepted by Air Force TPOC as of July 2025. Final closeout completed November 2025. No further work anticipated.
