# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II (Soil Moisture Mapping for Soil Integrity Assessment)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003 (per Asana)
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date; final report submitted July 21, 2025)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED – Successfully completed with all deliverables accepted
- **Brief Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to quantify cone index and assess landing area suitability for Air Force operations. Team included Black Swift Technologies (BST), Orbital Micro Systems (OMS), and Composite Engineering Technologies (CET). Adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for DoD soil integrity applications. Testing conducted at local sites and Tyndall Air Force Base.
