- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (Contract due date); Project archived November 2025
- **Project Status:** ✓ **ARCHIVED** – Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to quantify cone index and compare against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. The system adapts NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case: mapping landing area suitability. Team includes Black Swift Technologies (prime), Orbital Micro Systems (OMS, subcontractor), and CET. All deliverables accepted by Air Force TPOC as of July 2025.
