# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date)
- **Project Status:** Archived – Successfully completed with all deliverables accepted
- **Brief Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to assess soil integrity (cone index) for Air Force runway and landing area suitability assessment. Team adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for DoD use case. Key partners: Orbital Micro Systems (OMS, subcontractor), CET.
