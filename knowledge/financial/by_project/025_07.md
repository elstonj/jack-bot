# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED – Successfully completed with all deliverables accepted

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to quantify soil integrity (cone index) for Air Force use case: mapping landing area and runway suitability. The team (BST, Orbital Micro Systems, and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for DoD applications. Key technical outputs include UAS-based L-band radiometer hardware integration, soil integrity algorithms, RevD data processing web interface, and sensor protective cover. Testing was conducted locally and at Tyndall Air Force Base.
