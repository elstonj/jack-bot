# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date); project archived as of November 2025
- **Brief Description:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to quantify soil integrity (cone index) and volumetric water content (VWC) for Air Force landing area suitability assessment. Collaboration with Orbital Micro Systems (OMS) and CET on hardware integration and algorithm development.
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed with all deliverables accepted by Air Force TPOC as of July 2025; final closeout completed November 2025.
