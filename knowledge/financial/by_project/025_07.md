**Brief Description of Scope (from Asana):**  
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to quantify soil integrity (cone index) against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements for Air Force landing area suitability assessment. Team: Black Swift Technologies (BST), Orbital Micro Systems (OMS), and CET.
