# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date); project closed out November 2025
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Description of Scope:**
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to assess landing area suitability for the U.S. Air Force. The team (Black Swift Technologies, Orbital Micro Systems subcontractor, and CET) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case, quantifying soil integrity as cone index compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements.
