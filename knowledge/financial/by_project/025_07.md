## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date); project archived November 2025
- **Status:** **ARCHIVED** – Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to assess soil strength and suitability for landing areas. Team includes Black Swift Technologies, Orbital Micro Systems (OMS), and CET. Key deliverables: soil integrity algorithms, local and field testing, algorithm refinement, testing at Tyndall AFB, and final reporting.
