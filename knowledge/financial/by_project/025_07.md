# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date); project archived November 2025
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to quantify soil integrity (cone index vs. volumetric water content) for Air Force landing area suitability assessment. Technical work included hardware integration, algorithm development, local field testing, and validation testing at Tyndall AFB.
- **Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025. All deliverables accepted by Air Force TPOC.
