# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [400-5] SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date); project closed out November 2025
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Successfully completed and closed out as of November 2025

**Scope Description:**  
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to assess landing area suitability by quantifying cone index and comparing against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. Adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case. Team included Black Swift Technologies (BST), Orbital Micro Systems (OMS) as subcontractor, and other partners.
