# Project 025-07 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** SBIR - SMM DoD 22.4D Phase II (Soil Moisture Mapping for DoD)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Air Force (DoD SBIR Phase II)
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase II
- **Contract Number:** FA864924P0003
- **Period of Performance:** Late 2024 – July 11, 2025 (contract due date)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED – Successfully completed with all deliverables accepted
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for mapping soil integrity. The system measures soil moisture to assess soil suitability for aircraft landing areas. The team (BST, Orbital Micro Systems, and Celerity Engineering Technologies) adapted NASA soil moisture mapping technology for Air Force use case. Soil integrity is quantified as cone index and compared against volumetric water content (VWC) measurements. Testing conducted at local sites and Tyndall Air Force Base. (Source: Asana)
