## Project Overview

**Project Code:** 024-11

**Client/Agency & Program(s):** Multi-program project spanning:
- U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Fossil Energy and Carbon Management (FECM) — Methane Leak Detection Program
- National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) National Hurricane Center — Testbed Program (via Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University)

**Contract Types:** Mix of SBIR/government programs and commercial contracts

**Period of Performance:** November 2024 – September 2025+ (data through 2025-09; one entry dated 2026-08 with $0.00)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
- DOE FECM: Demonstrate S2 fixed-wing UAS equipped with Quanta3 methane spectrometer for methane emission detection from orphaned oil/gas wells at <$100 per well (vs. current $750/well baseline)
- NOAA Testbed: Three-year integration of S0 uncrewed aircraft wind data into National Hurricane Center forecasting operations, including reduction factor development, validation, and operational implementation
- Supporting commercial and academic partnerships (Embry-Riddle, INSTAAR, universities, private entities)

- **Performance Target:** <$100 per well inspection (vs. $750 baseline)
- **System Capability:** Survey up to 50 well sites in one day
- **Sensor Performance:** 3 ppb detection sensitivity; real-time autonomous path modification; simultaneous 3D wind field measurement
- **Timeline:** Not explicitly provided in document excerpts
- **Financial Implication:** Cost reduction technology proposal; likely milestone-based payments tied to validation results

### NOAA Testbed – Tropical Cyclone Forecasting Integration
- **Three-Year Phased Approach:**
  - **Year 1:** Reduction factor development (standardize S0 wind measurements to 10m/1-min reference)

- **Lead Institution:** Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University (Dr. Joshua Wadler)
- **Deliverables:** Validated reduction factor, testbed evaluation results, operational data stream integration
- **Performance Baseline:** 19 S0 flights completed as of proposal date (2024-11-04/2024-11-07)
- **Financial Implication:** Multi-year recurring work (likely annual funding cycles); involves subcontractor relationship with Embry-Riddle
