- **Year 1 (In Progress):** Reduction factor development from 2024 S0 flights combined with dropsonde and radar data
- **Year 2 (Planned):** Validation & testbed operations; run reduced data on historical storms; demonstrate forecast improvement to NHC
- **Year 3 (Planned):** Operational implementation; integrate into HDOBS or real-time data stream; deliver near-real-time data to NHC forecasters
- **Key Contact:** Wallace (NHC), Dr. Joshua Wadler (Embry-Riddle primary investigator)

### DOE FECM Methane Leak Detection Program
- **Technical Objective:** Validate S2 UAS with Quanta3 methane spectrometer for detecting and quantifying methane emissions from orphaned wells
- **Cost Target:** Below $100 per well (vs. current $750/well ground-based approach)
- **System Status:** Technology at TRL 5 (demonstrated in controlled Colorado test site)
- **Validation Approach:** Flux tower comparisons and controlled release testing
- **Operational Capability:** Up to 50 well sites per single flight day
- **Financial Implication:** Invoicing activity noted under "Methane Emission" ($3,087 in Jan 2025); limited data suggests early-stage or pilot work
