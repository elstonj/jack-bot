## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source:** Proposals & Reports

### DOE FECM Methane Leak Detection Program
- **Client:** U.S. Department of Energy (Fossil Energy and Carbon Management)
- **Scope:** S2 UAS equipped with Quanta3 methane spectrometer for detecting emissions from orphaned oil and gas wells
- **Key Capabilities:**
  - Detection at <$100 per well (vs. $750 baseline)
  - 3-meter wingspan fixed-wing platform, 100 km range
  - Quanta3 sensor sensitivity: 3 ppb
  - Autonomous BVLOS operations; can survey 50 wells per day
  - Real-time flight path optimization based on methane gradients
  - Simultaneous 3D wind field measurement
- **Current TRL:** 5 (demonstrated in controlled testing)
- **Validation Plan:** Field validation using flux towers; controlled comparison testing
- **Financial Impact:** Low spending visible in 2025-01 ($3,087); proposal stage suggests this is early in project execution

### NOAA Testbed — Enhancing Tropical Cyclone Forecasts
- **Client:** National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NHC) via Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University
- **Prime Investigator:** Dr. Joshua Wadler (Embry-Riddle)
- **BST Role:** Service provider for UAS observations
- **Scope:** Three-year effort to integrate S0 uncrewed aircraft wind data into NHC operational forecasting
- **Year 1 (In Progress):** Develop reduction factor to standardize S0 wind measurements to 10-minute/10-meter reference standards
- **Year 2:** Validation & testbed operations; demonstrate forecast improvements using historical storm data
- **Year 3:** Operational implementation; integrate into HDOBS real-time data stream
- **Key Metrics:** 19+ S0 flights completed as of proposal date
- **Financial Status:** Significant ongoing spend (S0 Hurricane projects $669,653 cumulative through 2025-11); Embry-Riddle sub-awards ($21,182)
