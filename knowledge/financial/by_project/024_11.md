## Key Deliverables & Milestones

### NOAA Hurricane Testbed (via Embry-Riddle)
**Project:** Enhancing Tropical Cyclone Forecasts through UAS Observations  
**Client:** NOAA / National Hurricane Center  
**Primary Investigator:** Dr. Joshua Wadler (Embry-Riddle)  
**BST Role:** Service provider (S0 UAS operations)

**Planned Milestones (3-Year Phased):**
- **Year 1 (2024–2025):** Reduction factor development from 2024 S0 flights; data standardization methodology
- **Year 2 (2025–2026):** Testbed validation; historical storm simulation with/without reduction factor; NHC forecast improvement demonstration
- **Year 3 (2026–2027):** Operational implementation; real-time HDOBS data delivery to NHC forecasters

**Financial Implication:**  
- Invoices to Embry-Riddle (NOAA pass-through): $221,182.00 (QBO, 2024-11 through 2025-02)
- Ongoing S0 Hurricane invoices: $579,803.00 (largest single project by spend)

### DOE FECM Methane Leak Detection Program
**Project:** Methane Emission Detection using S2 UAS + Quanta3 Spectrometer  
**Client:** U.S. Department of Energy (Fossil Energy and Carbon Management)  
**Scope:** Detect and quantify methane emissions from orphaned oil and gas wells at <$100/well (vs. current $750/well ground-based approach)

**Technical Approach:**
- S2 fixed-wing UAS (3m wingspan, 100 km range, BVLOS capable)
- Quanta3 methane spectrometer (3 ppb sensitivity)
- Real-time autonomous flight path modification
- 3D wind field measurement
- Survey capacity: Up to 50 wells/day

**Current Status:** TRL 5 (demonstrated in controlled releases); field validation planned using flux towers  
**Financial Implication:**  
- Methane Emission invoices: $3,087.00 (QBO, 2025-01)
- Limited spend to date; suggests early-stage or pre-full-execution phase
