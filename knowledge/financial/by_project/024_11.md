# Project 024-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-11  
**Client/Agency:** Multiple (primary: NOAA/National Hurricane Center via Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University; secondary: U.S. Department of Energy FECM)  
**Contract Type:** NOAA Testbed (primary); DOE Research Program (secondary)  
**Period of Performance:** November 2024 – September 2025+ (data available through 2025-09)  
**Brief Description:** Multi-faceted environmental monitoring and hurricane forecasting enhancement program using Black Swift Technologies' S0 and S2 uncrewed aircraft systems. Primary effort: integrate S0 wind data into NOAA/NHC forecasting operations via reduction factor development and testbed validation. Secondary effort: develop methane leak detection capability using S2 UAS with Quanta3 spectrometer for DOE FECM orphaned well monitoring.
