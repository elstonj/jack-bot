# Project 024-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-11  
**Client/Agency:** Multiple (NOAA/National Hurricane Center via Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University; U.S. Department of Energy FECM)  
**Contract Type:** Mixed — Government research (NOAA Testbed SOW); Federal proposal response (DOE FECM RFP)  
**Period of Performance:** November 2024 – ongoing (data extends through October 2025)  
**Brief Description:** Multi-faceted research and development project integrating Black Swift Technologies' S0 and S2 uncrewed aircraft systems into operational forecasting and environmental monitoring. Primary focus: (1) NOAA Testbed — developing wind data reduction factors for hurricane forecasting integration via S0 UAS; (2) DOE FECM — methane leak detection from orphaned oil/gas wells using S2 UAS with Quanta3 spectrometer (proposed cost <$100/well vs. $750/well baseline).
