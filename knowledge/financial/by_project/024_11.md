## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**From Proposals/Reports:**

### NOAA Testbed — S0 Hurricane Forecasting Integration
- **Client:** NOAA / National Hurricane Center (via Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University)
- **Period:** 3-year program (Year 1 starting ~2024-11)
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - **Year 1:** Reduction factor methodology for standardizing S0 wind measurements to 10m/10-minute standard
  - **Year 2:** Validation using historical storm data; testbed evaluation demonstrating forecast improvement
  - **Year 3:** Operational implementation; real-time data integration into HDOBS data stream for NHC forecasters
- **Status:** Year 1 underway (reduction factor development led by Jun Zhang and Josh)
- **Spend to Date (S0 Hurricane line):** $715,803 (sum of all S0 Hurricane entries from Nov 2024–Nov 2025)
- **Source:** Proposals & Reports (NOAA Testbed Proposal Plan, SOW via Embry-Riddle)

### DOE FECM — Methane Leak Detection Program
- **Client:** U.S. Department of Energy (Fossil Energy and Carbon Management)
- **Platform:** S2 fixed-wing UAS with Quanta3 methane spectrometer
- **Technical Goal:** Detect/quantify methane emissions from orphaned wells at <$100/well (vs. $750/well ground-based)
- **Key Specs:** 3m wingspan, 100km range, BVLOS autonomy, 3 ppb sensitivity, up to 50 wells per day
- **Current TRL:** 5 (demonstrated at BST Colorado test site)
- **Validation:** Planned field comparison with flux towers
- **Spend to Date (Methane Emission line):** $3,087 (Jan 2025 entry)
- **Source:** Proposals & Reports (DOE FECM RFP Response, 2024-11-06)
