# Project 024-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Code:** 024-11  
**Client/Agency:** Multiple (NOAA/National Hurricane Center primary, DOE FECM secondary)  
**Contract Type:** Testbed / Research & Development  
**Primary Scope:** Integration of Black Swift S0 uncrewed aircraft system into NOAA National Hurricane Center forecasting operations; development of reduction factor for wind speed standardization; DOE methane leak detection program development  
**Period of Performance:** Data available November 2024 – November 2025 (12 months)  
**Description:** Three-year NOAA testbed project to validate S0 UAS wind data for hurricane forecasting with focus on reduction factor development and operational integration; parallel DOE FECM proposal for methane emissions detection using S2 UAS with Quanta3 spectrometer.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source:** Proposals & Reports (NOAA Testbed SOW and DOE FECM Proposal)

### NOAA Testbed Project (S0 Hurricane Integration)

**Year 1 (2025):** Reduction Factor Development
- Derive reduction factor from 2024 S0 flights combined with dropsonde and radar data
- Establish methodology for standardizing S0 raw measurements to 10-minute/10-meter winds
- **Lead:** Jun Zhang, Josh (co-lead on reduction factor research)
- **Financial Implication:** Development labor, data processing, validation flights (reflected in S0 Hurricane charges: $126K–$185K monthly 2025-03 through 2025-11)

**Year 2 (2026):** Validation & Testbed Operations
- Run reduced data on historical storms
- Demonstrate forecast improvement to NHC
- Requires NHC Letter of Support (confirmed by Wallace, NHC contact)
- **Financial Implication:** Computational resources, testbed evaluation activities, personnel time

**Year 3 (2027):** Operational Implementation
- Implement reduction factor in S0 or ground station processing
- Integrate into HDOBS or real-time data stream
- Deliver near-real-time data to NHC forecasters
- **Financial Implication:** System integration, operational support

### DOE FECM Methane Leak Detection Program

**Proposed Timeline (from Q&A proposal, not formally contracted yet)**
- Technology Status: TRL 5 (demonstrated in controlled releases at BST Colorado test site)
- **Phase 1:** Field validation using flux towers for comparison/accuracy confirmation
- **Phase 2:** Deploy S2 UAS with Quanta3 methane spectrometer for orphaned well surveys
- **Cost Target:** <$100 per well (vs. $750 per well ground-based baseline)
- **Operational Capacity:** Up to 50 well sites in one day
- **Financial Implication:** Minimal charges visible in current QBO data ($3,087 in 2025-01 for "Methane Emission"); likely proposal in development phase
