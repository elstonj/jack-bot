## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposals & Reports (DOE FECM, NOAA Testbed Plan, NOAA NHC SOW)**

### 1. DOE FECM Methane Leak Detection Program
- **Client:** U.S. Department of Energy – Fossil Energy and Carbon Management
- **Scope:** Methane emissions detection at orphaned oil & gas wells using S2 UAS + Quanta3 spectrometer
- **Proposed Cost Reduction:** $100 per well vs. $750/well (ground-based baseline)
- **System Capability:** Survey 50 wells per day with 3 ppb sensitivity
- **Current TRL:** Level 5 (demonstrated at BST Colorado test site)
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Field validation using flux towers
  - Controlled comparison testing
  - System operational readiness
- **Financial Implication (QBO):** $3,087 recorded (2025-01); likely early-stage program or pilot phase

### 2. NOAA National Hurricane Center Testbed (S0 Platform)
- **Client:** National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration / National Hurricane Center
- **Prime Contractor:** Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University (Dr. Joshua Wadler, PI)
- **BST Role:** Service provider / technology developer
- **Scope:** Integration of S0 UAS wind observations into operational hurricane forecasting
- **Three-Year Phased Approach:**
  - **Year 1:** Reduction factor development (standardize S0 measurements to 10m/10-min/1-min wind speeds)
  - **Year 2:** Validation & testbed operations (historical storm reanalysis, forecast improvement demonstration)
  - **Year 3:** Operational implementation (real-time HDOBS data stream integration)
- **Current Status:** 19 S0 flights completed as of proposal date (Nov 2024)
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Wind data standardization methodology
  - Testbed evaluation results
  - Real-time data delivery integration
- **Financial Implication (QBO):** S0 Hurricane Phase II largest allocation – $503,803 cumulative spend (Nov 2024 – Sep 2026)
