- **Client:** U.S. Department of Energy (Fossil Energy and Carbon Management)
- **Proposal Date:** 2024-11-06

- **Technology Readiness:** Currently at TRL 5 (demonstrated in controlled releases)
- **Target Deliverable:** Cost reduction from $750/well to <$100/well for orphaned well surveys
