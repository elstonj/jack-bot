## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposals & Reports**

### DOE FECM Methane Leak Detection Program
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of Energy (Fossil Energy and Carbon Management)
- **Technical Deliverables:**
  - S2 UAS equipped with Quanta3 methane spectrometer (TRL 5 → field-validated)
  - Detection sensitivity: 3 ppb (parts per billion)
  - Capability: Survey up to 50 well sites per day at <$100/well (vs. current $750/well ground method)
  - Field validation with flux towers for accuracy confirmation
- **Current Status:** Proposal-stage (Q&A document dated 2024-11-06); minimal QBO spend observed ($3,087 in Jan 2025)
- **Risk:** Low observed spend relative to proposal scope suggests project may not have been awarded or is in early phase

### NOAA National Hurricane Center Testbed Program
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA / National Hurricane Center (via Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University)
- **Period of Performance:** 3-year program (Year 1: Reduction Factor Development; Year 2: Validation & Testbed; Year 3: Operational Implementation)
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - **Year 1 (FY2025):** Reduction factor to standardize S0 wind measurements to 10m/10-minute standard (led by Jun Zhang, Josh)
    - Derive from 2024 S0 flights combined with dropsonde and radar data
  - **Year 2 (FY2026):** Testbed validation
    - Historical storm simulations with/without reduction factor
    - Demonstrate forecast improvement to NHC
  - **Year 3 (FY2027):** Operational implementation
    - Reduction factor integrated into S0/ground station processing
    - Real-time HDOBS data delivery to NHC forecasters
- **Current Status:** Active operations; S0 Hurricane allocations total **~$815,000+** (dominant spending driver in 024-11)
- **Products:** High-frequency in-situ wind observations from S0 UAS; standardized data for NHC forecasting
