## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposals & Reports — Project Scope Description**

### DOE FECM Methane Leak Detection Program
- **Client:** U.S. Department of Energy (Fossil Energy and Carbon Management)
- **Technology:** S2 UAS with Quanta3 methane spectrometer
- **Target Outcome:** Detect methane emissions from orphaned oil/gas wells at <$100/well (vs. current $750/well via ground technicians)
- **Key Capability:** Autonomous BVLOS flight to survey up to 50 well sites per day with 3 ppb sensitivity
- **Status per Proposal:** Technology at TRL 5; field validation planned using flux towers
- **Deliverables Implied:** Validation report, operational methane detection capability, 3D wind field measurements
- **Financial Impact on 024-11:** "Methane Emission" project shows $3,087 spent in 2025-01; full project budget unknown

### NOAA National Hurricane Center Testbed (3-Year Program)
- **Client:** NOAA / National Hurricane Center (via Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University)
- **Technology:** S0 UAS for hurricane wind measurements
- **Year 1 (In Progress):** Reduce S0 wind measurements to standard 10m/1-min reference; validate against dropsonde/radar
- **Year 2:** Run reduction factor on historical storms; demonstrate forecast improvement; testbed validation
- **Year 3:** Operational implementation; integrate into HDOBS real-time data stream
- **Deliverables:** Reduction factor methodology, historical storm validation report, operational HDOBS integration
- **Financial Impact on 024-11:** "S0 Hurricane" is largest project ($654,850 cumulative 2025-02 to 2025-10); Embry-Riddle invoicing reflects subcontract relationship
