## Key Deliverables & Milestones
(Source: Proposals & Reports)

**NOAA Testbed – "Enhancing Tropical Cyclone Forecasts" (3-Year Project):**
- **Year 1 (2024–2025):** Derive wind speed reduction factor from S0 2024 flights; establish methodology for standardizing to 10m/1-minute wind averages (Led by Jun Zhang, Josh)
- **Year 2 (2025–2026):** Validate reduced data against historical storms; demonstrate forecast improvement to NHC via testbed evaluation
- **Year 3 (2026–2027):** Operationalize reduction factor in S0/ground station processing; integrate into HDOBS or real-time data stream for NHC delivery

**DOE FECM Methane Leak Detection Program:**
- Technology status: TRL 5 (demonstrated through controlled releases at BST Colorado test site)
- Deliverable: Validation of S2 UAS with Quanta3 methane spectrometer against flux towers
- Goal: Enable sub-$100/well methane detection (vs. current $750/well ground-based method)
- Proposed capability: 50 well sites per flight day
