### NOAA Testbed (Primary 024-11 effort)
**Three-Year Phased Approach** (from NOAA Testbed Proposal Plan, created 2024-11-04):

**Year 1 (In Progress/Complete):** Reduction Factor Development
- Derive wind speed reduction factor from 2024 S0 flight data combined with dropsonde and radar comparisons
- Establish methodology for standardizing raw S0 measurements to 10-minute/10-meter reference standard
- **Status:** Appears underway; 19 S0 flights completed as of proposal date
- **Financial Implication:** Core R&D activity; likely drives labor and equipment costs in 2024–2025

**Year 2 (Planned):** Validation & Testbed Operations
- Run reduced data on historical storms (with/without reduction factor applied)
- Demonstrate forecast improvement to NHC
- Testbed evaluation and stakeholder familiarization
- **Planned Deliverable:** Validation report, forecast skill metrics
- **Financial Implication:** Continued platform operations, data analysis, testing infrastructure

**Year 3 (Planned):** Operational Implementation
- Implement reduction factor in S0 or ground station processing
- Integrate into HDOBS or other real-time data stream
- Deliver near-real-time data to NHC forecasters
- **Planned Deliverable:** Operational data pipeline, integration with NHC systems
- **Financial Implication:** Software development, ops support, ongoing platform flights
