## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposals/Reports — Technical Scope & Milestones**

### DOE FECM Methane Leak Detection Program
- **Deliverable:** S2 UAS with Quanta3 methane spectrometer system capable of detecting methane at <3 ppb sensitivity
- **Target Cost:** <$100 per well (vs. current $750/well baseline)
- **Capacity:** Survey up to 50 well sites in one flight day
- **Current TRL:** 5 (demonstrated at BST Colorado test site)
- **Validation:** Field trials with flux tower comparison testing planned
- **Status/Timeline:** Data not available from Asana; proposal appears to be in Q&A/response phase (dated 2024-11-06)

### NOAA Testbed / National Hurricane Center Integration
- **Client:** National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NHC), via Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University (Dr. Joshua Wadler, PI)
- **Three-Year Phased Approach:**

  **Year 1 (Current):** Reduction Factor Development
  - Derive standardized reduction factor to convert S0 measurements to 10m/1-minute wind speeds
  - Use 2024 S0 flight data + dropsonde/radar cross-validation
  - Led by Jun Zhang and Josh (BST)
  - **Status:** In progress; 19 S0 flights completed

  **Year 2:** Validation & Testbed Operations
  - Run historical storm reanalysis with/without reduction factor
  - Demonstrate forecast improvement to NHC forecasters
  - Testbed evaluation activities with NHC participation
  - **Status:** Planned; awaiting Year 1 reduction factor completion

  **Year 3:** Operational Implementation
  - Integrate reduction factor into S0/ground station processing
  - Deploy near-real-time HDOBS data stream to NHC
  - Operational readiness for hurricane season forecasting
  - **Status:** Planned

- **Letter of Support:** Wallace (NHC) has provided letter of support
- **Financial Implications:** Embry-Riddle invoice stream ($243.5K cumulative through 2025-11) reflects service payments for BST participation
