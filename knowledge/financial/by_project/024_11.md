# Project 024-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Code:** 024-11

**Client/Agency:** Multiple (primary: National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration / National Hurricane Center via Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University; secondary: U.S. Department of Energy - Fossil Energy and Carbon Management)

**Contract Type:** NOAA Testbed (primary SOW); DOE FECM proposal (supporting/related work)

**Period of Performance:** November 2024 – October 2025 (based on QBO transaction dates)

**Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is executing multiple environmental monitoring projects centered on uncrewed aircraft systems (UAS):
- **Primary (S0 Hurricane / NOAA Testbed):** Integrating S0 uncrewed aircraft wind data into National Hurricane Center forecasting operations over three years, with Year 1 focused on developing a reduction factor to standardize S0 measurements to 10m/10-minute wind speeds.
- **Secondary (DOE Methane):** Proposing S2 fixed-wing UAS equipped with Quanta3 methane spectrometer for rapid detection and quantification of methane emissions from orphaned oil and gas wells at <$100/well (vs. $750/well current cost).

Additional projects invoiced include NASA Ames, Navy SBIR/STTR, and various other programs (WPO Hurricane Phase II, SMM Phase II, USGS Mexico, UMES, etc.).
