## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposals & Reports**

### NOAA Testbed (S0 Hurricane Project) — Three-Year Program
**Primary Deliverables:**
1. **Year 1 (2024–2025):** Reduction Factor Development
   - Derive wind speed reduction factor from 2024 S0 flights combined with dropsonde and radar data
   - Establish methodology for standardizing S0 raw measurements to 10-minute/10-meter wind speeds
   - Status: In progress (significant invoicing to S0 Hurricane project in 2025-03 through 2025-11)

2. **Year 2 (2025–2026):** Validation & Testbed Operations
   - Run reduced data on historical storms (with/without reduction factor)
   - Demonstrate forecast improvement to National Hurricane Center
   - Testbed evaluation component
   - Status: Expected 2025–2026

3. **Year 3 (2026–2027):** Operational Implementation
   - Implement reduction factor in S0 or ground station processing
   - Integrate into HDOBS or real-time data stream
   - Deliver near-real-time data to NHC forecasters
   - Status: Expected 2026–2027

**Financial Implication:** S0 Hurricane is the largest single project by spend in the data, with allocations in 10 of 13 months (total: $805,653 across months 2025-03 through 2025-11 shown in unclassified.md). This is a multi-year commitment with sustained invoicing pattern.
