- **Status:** TRL 5 (technology demonstrated through controlled releases at BST Colorado test site)
- **Deliverables:**
  - Quanta3 methane spectrometer integration with S2 UAS
  - Field validation using flux towers for comparison
  - Cost target: <$100 per well (vs. current $750/well baseline)
  - Capability: Survey up to 50 well sites per flight day
- **QBO Invoicing:** Methane Emission project ($3,087 invoiced in 2025-01)
- **Financial Implication:** Early-stage validation work; no major budget milestones documented

### NOAA/NHC Testbed — S0 Hurricane Wind Data Integration
- **Three-Year Program:**
  - **Year 1 (FY2025):** Develop reduction factor for S0 wind measurements to standard 10-m/10-minute wind speeds using 2024 S0 flights + dropsonde/radar data
  - **Year 2 (FY2026):** Validation and testbed operations; run historical storm scenarios with/without reduction factor; demonstrate forecast improvements
  - **Year 3 (FY2027):** Operational implementation; integrate into HDOBS real-time data stream; deliver to NHC forecasters
- **QBO Invoicing:**
  - Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University (prime through NOAA Testbed): $243,615.00 cumulative (2024-11, 2025-06)
  - S0 Hurricane / S0 Hurricane Phase II: $509,803.01 total (2025-03 to 2025-07)
  - Combined NOAA testbed project spend: **~$753,418.01 YTD**
- **Financial Implication:** Year 1 of 3-year effort; strong invoicing pattern indicates active operations and platform deployment
