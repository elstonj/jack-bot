# Project 024-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** NOAA Testbed – Enhancing Tropical Cyclone Forecasts through High-Frequency UAS Observations  
**Client/Agency:** National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) / National Hurricane Center (NHC)  
**Contract Type:** Research Services / Testbed Collaboration (via Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University)  
**Period of Performance:** Data available from 2024-11 through 2025-10; three-year project structure referenced in proposal (Year 1: Reduction Factor Development; Year 2: Validation & Testbed Operations; Year 3: Operational Implementation)  
**Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is providing UAS (S0 platform) wind observation services to support NOAA's National Hurricane Center in developing and validating a reduction factor to standardize UAS wind measurements to operationally-standard 10-minute/10-meter wind speeds. The work includes field data collection, reduction factor derivation, testbed validation, and eventual real-time data integration into NHC forecasting operations.
