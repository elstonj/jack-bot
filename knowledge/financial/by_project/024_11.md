# Project 024-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-11  
**Client/Agency:** Multiple (NOAA/National Hurricane Center primary; DOE FECM secondary)  
**Contract Type:** NOAA Testbed (via Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University); DOE FECM proposal  
**Period of Performance:** Data spans 2024-11 through 2025-09 (11 months of actuals available)  
**Description of Scope:** Integration of Black Swift S0 uncrewed aircraft system (UAS) for hurricane wind observations and data standardization to support National Hurricane Center forecasting operations. Secondary technical work on S2 UAS with methane spectrometer for DOE FECM orphaned well detection program. Project focuses on developing reduction factors to convert S0 measurements to standard 10-minute/10-meter wind speeds, testbed validation, and operational implementation roadmap.
