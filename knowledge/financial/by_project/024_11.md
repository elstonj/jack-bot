# Project 024-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-11  
**Client/Agency:** Multiple (NOAA/National Hurricane Center via Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University; U.S. Department of Energy FECM)  
**Contract Type:** Mixed — NOAA Testbed (SOW-based collaborative); DOE FECM proposal/RFP response  
**Period of Performance:** November 2024 – October 2025 (based on QuickBooks transaction range)  
**Brief Description:** Multi-faceted environmental monitoring project integrating Black Swift Technologies' S0 and S2 uncrewed aircraft systems into NOAA hurricane forecasting operations and DOE methane leak detection from orphaned wells. Primary focus: developing wind speed reduction factors for S0 data standardization (Year 1 of 3-year NOAA testbed) and validating S2 UAS with Quanta3 methane spectrometer for low-cost orphaned well emissions detection.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposals & Reports**

### NOAA Testbed (Embry-Riddle/NHC) – 3-Year Program

**Year 1 (Current/In-Progress):** Reduction Factor Development
- **Milestone:** Derive reduction factor from 2024 S0 flights combined with dropsonde and radar data
- **Lead:** Jun Zhang and Josh (co-leading reduction factor research)
- **Status:** 19 S0 flights completed as of proposal date (2024-11)
- **Financial Implication:** Development phase; likely labor-intensive, spanning 2024-2025

**Year 2 (Planned):** Validation & Testbed Operations
- **Milestone:** Run reduced data on historical storms; demonstrate forecast improvement to NHC
- **Status:** Contingent on Year 1 completion and NHC validation
- **Financial Implication:** Operational testbed activities; evaluation and validation costs

**Year 3 (Planned):** Operational Implementation
- **Milestone:** Implement reduction factor in S0/ground station processing; integrate into HDOBS real-time data stream
- **Status:** Long-term deliverable
- **Financial Implication:** Integration and operational support costs

### DOE FECM Methane Leak Detection

**Proposed Deliverables (from proposal Q&A, 2024-11-06):**
- S2 UAS equipped with Quanta3 methane spectrometer operational for field validation
- Technology maturation from TRL 5 (demonstrated in controlled release) to field-proven
- Validation via flux tower comparisons for accuracy confirmation
- Demonstration of <$100 per-well cost vs. $750 baseline
- **Status:** Proposal submitted; approval/contract status not confirmed
- **Financial Implication:** Equipment development, field testing, validation (2025)
