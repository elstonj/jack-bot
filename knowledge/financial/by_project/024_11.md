## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposals & Reports**

### DOE FECM Methane Leak Detection Program
- **Objective:** Demonstrate S2 UAS with Quanta3 methane spectrometer for orphaned well detection at <$100/well (vs. current $750/well ground-based cost)
- **Technology Status:** TRL 5 (demonstrated at BST Colorado test site)
- **Key Milestones:**
  - Controlled release validation testing
  - Field validation using flux towers
  - Atmospheric variation mitigation through comparison testing
- **Financial Implication:** Proposal-stage (no contract value or timeline provided in source documents)

### NOAA Testbed / National Hurricane Center Integration
- **Objective:** Integrate S0 UAS wind data into NHC forecasting operations over three years
- **Platform:** S0 fixed-wing UAS for in-situ hurricane wind measurements
- **Key Phased Deliverables:**
  - **Year 1:** Develop and validate reduction factor to standardize S0 measurements to 10-minute/10-meter wind speeds
  - **Year 2:** Run validation on historical storms; demonstrate forecast improvements; testbed evaluation
  - **Year 3:** Operational implementation; integrate into HDOBS real-time data stream; deliver near-real-time data to NHC forecasters
- **Letters of Support:** NOAA/NHC (Wallace) offered support
- **Lead PI:** Dr. Joshua Wadler (Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University)
- **BST Role:** Service provider / UAS operator
- **Financial Implication:** Appears to be active (invoices to Embry-Riddle and NOAA-related projects visible in 2025 actuals; "S0 Hurricane" and "WPO Hurricane Phase II" line items)
