# Project 024-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** S0 Hurricane Testbed / NOAA Integration Project

**Client/Agency:** National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) / National Hurricane Center (NHC), via Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University

**Contract Type:** Government Testbed / Research Services Agreement

**Period of Performance:** 2024–2027 (three-year phased project, per proposal)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Black Swift Technologies is providing uncrewed aircraft system (S0) observations to support National Hurricane Center forecasting operations. The work focuses on: (1) developing and validating a reduction factor to standardize S0 wind measurements to operational 10-minute/10-meter standards, (2) demonstrating forecast improvements through testbed evaluation, and (3) operationalizing real-time data delivery to NHC forecasters. Associated work includes support for Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University as the prime contractor.
