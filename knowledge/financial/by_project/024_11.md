## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposals & Reports (NOAA Testbed SOW)**

Based on the NOAA Testbed proposal documentation, the project has a three-year phased structure:

### Year 1: Reduction Factor Development (Active)
- **Objective:** Derive wind speed reduction factor from 2024 S0 flights combined with dropsonde and radar data
- **Status:** In progress (19 S0 flights completed as of proposal date)
- **Deliverable:** Standardized methodology for converting S0 measurements to 10m/1-minute wind speed reference

### Year 2: Validation & Testbed Operations
- **Objective:** Run reduced data on historical hurricane cases; demonstrate forecast improvement to NHC
- **Deliverable:** Testbed evaluation report with forecast skill assessment
- **Requirement:** NOAA NHC support/Letter of Support (confirmed by Wallace, NHC contact)

### Year 3: Operational Implementation
- **Objective:** Implement reduction factor in processing pipeline; integrate into HDOBS or real-time data stream
- **Deliverable:** Operational data delivery to NHC forecasters; near-real-time wind product
