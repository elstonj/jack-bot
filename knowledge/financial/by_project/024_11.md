**Brief Description of Scope:**

This is a portfolio project encompassing multiple concurrent BST initiatives:
1. **NOAA/NHC Hurricane Forecasting** — Integration of S0 UAS wind data into National Hurricane Center operations; reduction factor development for standardizing wind measurements to operational standards (10m/10-min); three-year testbed and operational implementation
2. **DOE FECM Methane Leak Detection** — Development and validation of S2 UAS equipped with Quanta3 methane spectrometer for detection of emissions from orphaned oil/gas wells at <$100/well cost
3. **Multiple concurrent commercial and federal projects** — Navy SBIR/STTR, NASA, USGS, and other funded activities
