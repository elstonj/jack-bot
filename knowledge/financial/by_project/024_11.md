**Source: NOAA Testbed Proposal Plan (2024-11-04/07) & SOW (2024-11-22)**
- **Client/Agency:** National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) / National Hurricane Center (NHC)

- **BST Role:** Service provider (UAS operations, data collection, wind data standardization)

- **Systems:** S0 (wind measurement UAS), HDOBS data stream
- **Status:** Active (19 flights completed as of proposal date)
