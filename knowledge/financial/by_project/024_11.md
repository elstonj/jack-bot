# Project 024-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 024-11  
**Client/Agency:** Multiple (Primary: NOAA/National Hurricane Center via Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University)  
**Contract Type:** NOAA Testbed (cooperative research/service agreement)  
**Period of Performance:** 2024-11 to 2025-11+ (3-year project, currently in Year 1)  
**Brief Description:** Integration of Black Swift Technologies S0 uncrewed aircraft system (UAS) wind measurement data into National Hurricane Center operational forecasting. Project focuses on developing and validating a reduction factor to standardize S0 wind measurements to 10-minute/10-meter reference standards, demonstrating forecast improvements through testbed evaluation, and operationalizing real-time data delivery to NHC forecasters.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones
*(From Proposals & Reports)*

**Year 1 (Current/In Progress):**
- Derive reduction factor from 2024 S0 flights combined with dropsonde and radar data
- Establish methodology for standardizing raw S0 measurements to 10-minute/10-meter wind speeds
- **Status:** S0 has completed 19 flights as of proposal date (Nov 2024)

**Year 2:**
- Run reduced data on historical storms (with/without reduction factor applied)
- Demonstrate forecast improvement to NHC
- Testbed evaluation component
- **Dependency:** NHC support (Letter of Support confirmed from Wallace, NHC contact)

**Year 3:**
- Implement reduction factor in S0 or ground station processing
- Integrate into HDOBS or other real-time data stream
