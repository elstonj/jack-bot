## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposals & Reports**

### DOE FECM Methane Leak Detection Program
- **Status:** Proposal stage (submitted 2024-11-06)
- **Scope:** Methane emissions detection from orphaned oil and gas wells using S2 UAS with Quanta3 spectrometer
- **Technical Target:** Sub-$100 per-well detection cost (vs. $750 ground-based)
- **TRL:** Currently at TRL 5 (demonstrated); field validation planned
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Validated methane detection system (S2 + Quanta3 integration)
  - Controlled release and field test results
  - Flux tower comparison data

### NOAA Testbed (Hurricane Forecasting via Embry-Riddle)
- **Status:** Active (SOW established; 19 S0 flights completed as of proposal date)
- **Duration:** 3-year phased project
- **Scope:** Integrate S0 UAS wind data into National Hurricane Center operational forecasting
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - **Year 1:** Reduction factor to standardize S0 measurements to 10m/10-minute wind speeds
  - **Year 2:** Validation using historical storm scenarios; testbed demonstration of forecast improvements
  - **Year 3:** Operational implementation in real-time data stream (HDOBS); delivery to NHC forecasters
- **Partners:** Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University (Dr. Joshua Wadler, PI); NOAA/NHC (Letter of Support from Wallace)
