## Key Deliverables & Milestones
**Source: Proposals & Reports**

### DOE FECM Methane Leak Detection Program
- **Goal:** Detect and quantify methane emissions from orphaned oil and gas wells
- **Technology:** S2 fixed-wing UAS with Quanta3 methane spectrometer
- **Key Capability:** <$100 per well vs. current $750/well (ground-based) — 50 well sites per day survey capacity
- **Technical Maturity:** TRL 5 (controlled release demonstrations at BST Colorado test site)
- **Planned Validation:** Field validation with flux towers for accuracy comparison
- **Status:** Proposal phase (document dated 2024-11-06)

### NOAA Testbed / National Hurricane Center Integration
- **Duration:** Three-year project
- **Goal:** Integrate S0 UAS wind data into operational NHC forecasting
- **Year 1 (Reduction Factor Development):** Derive reduction factor from 2024 S0 flights; standardize raw measurements to 10m/1-minute wind speeds
- **Year 2 (Validation & Testbed):** Run reduced data on historical storms; demonstrate forecast improvement; obtain NHC testbed evaluation
- **Year 3 (Operational Implementation):** Integrate reduction factor into S0/ground station; deliver near-real-time data via HDOBS stream
- **Lead:** Jun Zhang (internal); Dr. Joshua Wadler (Embry-Riddle, PI); Wallace (NHC contact, Letter of Support)
- **Status:** Statement of Work established with Embry-Riddle (2024-11-22); 19 flights completed as of proposal date
