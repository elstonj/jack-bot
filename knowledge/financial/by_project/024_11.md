## Key Deliverables & Milestones

### From Proposals & Reports:

**NOAA/NHC Testbed (S0 Hurricane Forecast Integration)**
- **Year 1 (2024–2025):** Reduction factor development from 2024 S0 flights combined with dropsonde and radar data
  - *Status:* 19 S0 flights completed as of proposal date (early Nov 2024)
  - *Milestone:* Standardization methodology established
  - *Deliverable:* Reduction factor for converting S0 measurements to 10m/10-minute wind standards
  
- **Year 2 (2025–2026):** Testbed validation with historical storm data
  - *Deliverable:* Forecast improvement demonstration to NHC
  - *Financial implication:* Continued operational support; testbed evaluation activities
  
- **Year 3 (2026–2027):** Operational implementation
  - *Deliverable:* Real-time data stream integration (HDOBS or equivalent)
  - *Deliverable:* Operational processing of reduction factor in S0/ground station systems
  - *Financial implication:* Sustained operations and NHC integration costs

**DOE FECM Methane Leak Detection Program**
- **Objective:** Detect/quantify methane emissions from orphaned oil & gas wells using S2 UAS + Quanta3 spectrometer
- **Technical milestones:**
  - S2 equipped with Quanta3 methane spectrometer (TRL 5 demonstrated, field validation pending)
  - Controlled comparison testing vs. flux towers
  - Capability: ~50 well sites surveyable per flight day; target cost <$100/well
- **Financial implication:** Equipment procurement (S2, Quanta3), field testing, validation studies
- **Proposal date:** 2024-11-06; decision/funding status not specified in provided documents

**Embry-Riddle / NOAA Statement of Work (SOW)**
- **Lead PI:** Dr. Joshua Wadler (Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University)
- **BST role:** Service provider for UAS observations and data standardization
- **Deliverables:** UAS data evaluation, wind standardization research, testbed participation
- **Financial implication:** Invoiced through Embry-Riddle (partner institution); ~$21K allocated in this data set
