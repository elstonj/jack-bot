## Key Sub-Projects & Deliverables

### 1. **S0 Hurricane / NOAA Testbed**
- **Client/Agency:** National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) / National Hurricane Center (NHC)
- **Partner/PI:** Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University (Dr. Joshua Wadler)
- **Scope (from SOW):** Integrate UAS wind observations into operational hurricane forecasting; develop reduction factor to standardize S0 wind measurements to 10m/10-minute reference; testbed evaluation and operational data delivery
- **Duration:** Three-year project (Year 1: reduction factor development; Year 2: validation & testbed; Year 3: operational implementation)
- **QBO Spend to Date:** ~$675K (Nov 2024 – Oct 2025)
  - 2024-11: $222.33 | 2025-03: $126,203 | 2025-04: $131,590 | 2025-05: $36K | 2025-06: $140,400 | 2025-07: $70K | 2025-08: $105,610 | 2025-10: Deferred
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Year 1: Reduction factor methodology (due end of Year 1)
  - Year 2: Testbed evaluation results, forecast improvement data
  - Year 3: Operational reduction factor implementation; real-time data stream integration (HDOBS)
- **Status:** In-progress (currently in Year 1 activities)

### 2. **DOE FECM Methane Leak Detection Program**
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) - Fossil Energy and Carbon Management (FECM)
- **Scope (from Proposal):** Deploy S2 fixed-wing UAS with Quanta3 methane spectrometer to detect and quantify methane emissions from orphaned oil and gas wells; target cost <$100 per well (vs. current $750 ground-based rate)
- **Technical Approach:** 3m wingspan S2 UAS, 100 km range, BVLOS autonomous operations, 3 ppb sensitivity, capability to survey 50 well sites per day
- **Technology Readiness:** TRL 5 (demonstrated at BST Colorado test site); field validation planned using flux towers
- **QBO Spend to Date:** ~$3,087 (2025-01)
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Field validation test results (with flux tower comparison)
  - Technology maturation to TRL 6+ (field-tested)
  - Cost-per-well demonstration
