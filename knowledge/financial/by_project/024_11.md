# Project 024-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Code:** 024-11  
**Client/Agency:** Multiple (see breakdown below)  
**Contract Type:** Mixed (SBIR Phase I/II, STTR, Commercial, Government R&D)  
**Period of Performance:** November 2024 – October 2025 (12 months)

**Description of Scope:**  
Project 024-11 is a **multi-contract portfolio** encompassing several Black Swift Technologies initiatives:

1. **DOE FECM Methane Leak Detection Program** — Development and validation of S2 UAS equipped with Quanta3 methane spectrometer for orphaned well emissions monitoring (TRL 5 → field validation phase)
2. **NOAA Testbed / National Hurricane Center Integration** — Multi-year effort (Years 1–3) to integrate S0 uncrewed aircraft wind data into NHC forecasting operations, including reduction factor development and operational implementation (Led by Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University, BST as service provider)
3. **Navy SBIR/STTR Programs** — Multiple small business innovation awards
4. **Commercial & Academic Partnerships** — Includes university collaborations (Embry-Riddle, University of Maryland, INSTAAR CU Boulder, etc.) and equipment/system deployments
