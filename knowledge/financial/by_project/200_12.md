1. **Labor Costs On Track (Partial Data):** Ted Miles invoiced $15,923.20 for 256 hours through June 2025, representing 35.6% of his annual allocation ($44,706.25). Burn rate (~64 hours/month in Jun 2025) is sustainable within the $44,706.25 budget.

4. **All Invoices Paid On Time:** Drive Budget Docs show all Ted Miles invoices (Mar–Jun 2025) and USPS/equipment orders marked "Paid."

#### ⚠️ **Risk Factors & Unknowns:**
1. **Incomplete Actual Spend Data:** QuickBooks provides only Ted Miles labor invoices ($15,923.20) and summary-level project allocation ($117,701.50). **Invoices for Kay Rufty, Consulting Services (Jack Elston/Maciej Stachura), and Equipment/Materials categories are not itemized in QuickBooks data provided.** This obscures true spend across 60%+ of the budget (consulting + equipment).
2. **Project Archived Ahead of Schedule:** Project shut down August 2025, five months before the original March 2026 due date. The majority of the $96,153.87 "remaining budget" was likely **not spent** due to early termination.
3. **No Post-June 2025 Spend Data:** Invoices and orders documented in Drive Budget Docs end in July 2025. Actual July–August 2025 labor, shipping, and closeout costs are not captured in QuickBooks data.
4. **Consulting Services Allocation Unclear:** $20,576 budgeted for Jack Elston & Maciej Stachura consulting services; no invoices or POs documented in Drive Budget Docs or QuickBooks. This line item may have been delivered as internal effort or remain unutilized.
