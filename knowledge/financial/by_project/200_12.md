## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** NASA AREN '25 (AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network)
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25PA610
- **Contract Type:** Science Activation project; cooperative agreement (NNX16AB95A)
- **Period of Performance:** 2025 (part of multi-year AREN project running 2021–present; original due date 2026-03-02)
- **Project Status:** ARCHIVED — Project shut down August 2025 ahead of schedule
- **Project Owner:** Meredith O'hara Needham
- **Key Personnel:** 
  - Kay Rufty (Aerospace Engineer, named Co-Investigator)
  - Ted Miles (Senior Engineering Technician, Onancock, VA)
  - Jack Elston & Maciej Stachura (Technical Consultants)

**Description of Scope:**
BST participated in the AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network, a NASA Science Activation project promoting learning and outreach in aerospace education. BST provided engineering support via named Co-Investigator Kay Rufty and technical consultant team members. Primary deliverable was the Aeropod platform, which completed testing phase in October 2025. Project included hardware integration, assembly, testing, and national distribution to RESA (Regional Education Service Agency) partners and workshop participants.
