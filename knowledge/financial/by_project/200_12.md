3. **Project Shutdown August 2025 — Early Termination**

   - Original Period of Performance: through March 2, 2026
   - **Implication:** Remaining ~$82,000–$95,000 of approved budget will go unspent or be returned to NASA. No Q3–Q4 2025 labor or equipment charges visible post-June.
