# Project 200-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** NASA AREN '25 (AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network)
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25PA610
- **Contract Type:** Science Activation cooperative agreement (part of multi-year AREN program running 2021–present)
- **Period of Performance:** 2025 (original contract due 2026-03-02; project archived August 2025)
- **Project Owner:** Meredith O'hara Needham
- **Brief Description:** BST participated in NASA's AREN Science Activation project promoting aerospace education and outreach. BST provided engineering support via Aerospace Engineer Kay Rufty (named Co-Investigator). Project focused on TerraROVER system development, MiniCam assembly, and hardware integration for educational distribution to RESA partners and workshop participants nationally. **Project was shut down in August 2025 ahead of schedule following retirement of primary NASA POC Geoffrey Bland (NASA Ames).**
