# Project 200-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** NASA AREN '25 (AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network 2025)
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25PA610
- **Contract Type:** Science Activation cooperative agreement (part of multi-year AREN project)
- **Period of Performance:** 2025 (project archived August 2025; original due date 2026-03-02)
- **Project Owner:** Meredith O'hara Needham
- **Key Personnel:** Kay Rufty (Aerospace Engineer, Co-Investigator); Ted Miles (Senior Engineering Technician); Jack Elston & Maciej Stachura (Technical Consultants)

**Scope:** BST participated in the AREN initiative, a NASA Science Activation project promoting STEM learning and outreach. BST's primary contribution was technical support via named Co-Investigator Kay Rufty and field engineering support. The project centered on the Aeropod platform, which completed its testing phase in October 2025. **Project was shut down ahead of schedule in August 2025**, three days ahead of the target 2025-08-29 closeout deadline.
