## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** NASA AREN '25 (AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network)
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25PA610
- **Contract Type:** Science Activation cooperative agreement (part of multi-year AREN project, 2021–present)
- **Period of Performance:** 2025 (original contract due date 2026-03-02; **project archived August 2025 ahead of schedule**)
- **BST's Portion of Total Project Budget:** $20,576 of $117,701.50 total
- **Project Owner:** Meredith O'hara Needham
- **Key Personnel:** 
  - Kay Rufty (Aerospace Engineer, named Co-Investigator)
  - Ted Miles (Senior Engineering Technician)
  - Jack Elston & Maciej Stachura (Technical Consultants)

**Description of Scope:**  
BST participated in a NASA Science Mission Directorate "Science Activation" outreach project promoting learning via the Aeropod platform. Kay Rufty served as a named Co-Investigator providing aerospace engineering support. The project involved hardware integration, assembly, testing, and distribution of TerraROVER systems and related equipment to education and research partners nationally. Primary deliverable (Aeropods platform) completed testing phase in October 2025. **Project is now archived and concluded.**
