# Project 200-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** NASA AREN '25 (AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network)
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate
- **Contract Type:** Science Activation cooperative agreement
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25PA610
- **Period of Performance:** 2025 (part of multi-year AREN project running 2021–present)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED — Project shut down August 2025 ahead of schedule**
- **Brief Description:** BST participated in NASA's AREN initiative, a Science Activation project promoting STEM learning and outreach. BST provided aerospace engineering support via Co-Investigator Kay Rufty, Senior Engineering Technician Ted Miles, and consulting services from Technical Consultants Jack Elston and Maciej Stachura. Primary deliverables included TerraROVER systems, MiniCam assemblies, and Aeropods platform testing. Project concluded ahead of original 2026-03-02 performance period due to key NASA POC (Geoffrey Bland) retirement from NASA in August 2025.
