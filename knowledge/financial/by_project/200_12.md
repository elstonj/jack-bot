## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** NASA AREN '25 (AEROKATS and ROVER Education Network)
- **Client/Agency:** NASA Science Mission Directorate
- **Contract Type:** Science Activation cooperative agreement (NNX16AB95A)
- **Contract Number:** 80NSSC25PA610
- **BST's Contract Value:** $20,576 (BST's portion of $117,701.50 total project budget)
- **Period of Performance:** 2025 (part of multi-year AREN project running 2021–present)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED — Project shut down August 2025 ahead of original 2026-03-02 due date**
- **Project Owner:** Meredith O'hara Needham
- **Key Personnel:** Kay Rufty (Aerospace Engineer, named Co-Investigator); Ted Miles (Senior Engineering Technician); Jack Elston & Maciej Stachura (Technical Consultants)

### Scope
BST participated in the NASA AREN education and outreach initiative, providing aerospace engineering support via named Co-Investigator Kay Rufty. Primary deliverable was the Aeropod platform, which completed its testing phase in October 2025. The project involved hardware integration, assembly, testing, and distribution of TerraROVER and MiniCam systems to education partners and regional contacts.
