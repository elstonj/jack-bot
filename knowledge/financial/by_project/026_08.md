**Period of Performance:** 2026-03 through 2026-10 (data available)  
**Scope Description:**  
Project 026-08 encompasses multiple concurrent government and collaborative initiatives:
- **NOAA UxSOC Master IDIQ:** Contractor-provided Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (UAS) Data-as-a-Service (DaaS) for climate, weather, ocean, and coastal monitoring missions over a 5-year contract period.

- **EMASS Flight Control Evaluation:** Technical assessment of ECS-DOT flight controller versus BST's internal autopilot system.
