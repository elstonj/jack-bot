### 1. NOAA UxSOC Master IDIQ Contract (SOW, dated 2026-08-30)
- **Scope:** Contractor-provided UAS Data-as-a-Service (DaaS) for NOAA climate, weather, ocean, and coastal monitoring.
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Fully supported UAS platforms (variable endurance: <2 hrs, 2–10 hrs, >10 hrs)
  - Sensor integration and Non-Recurring Engineering (NRE) per Task Orders
  - Real-time data delivery with storage and transmission capability
  - Platform adaptation and enhancement as required
  - Program management for multiple simultaneous Task Orders
  - Compliance with FAA and NOAA regulations

- **Contract Type:** Master IDIQ with individual Task Orders
- **Authorized:** Commercial Engagement under Ocean Technology Act (Public Law 115-394) and Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2023
- **Financial Implication:** Task Orders specify missions and costs; no fixed budget provided in SOW. Individual Task Orders will drive invoicing schedule.

### 2. S0-AD METAR Proposal (ROM, dated 2026-08-24) — SOCOM S0-AD METAR Option 2A
- **Scope:** Equip S0-AD tactical UAS with EO/IR cameras and enhanced software for METAR-equivalent weather observations in special operations contested battlespace.
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Forward-looking EO & IR still image cameras (400–700 nm, NVG-compatible)
  - Vertical field-of-view design for cloud layer assessment
  - Modified S0-AD with integrated atmospheric sensors (PTH sonde, wind/humidity)
  - SwiftControl UI updates (Samsung tablet-based)
  - SwiftCore flight management updates
  - Real-time atmospheric telemetry (every 30 sec at 50 nm) and still images (every 5 min)
  - Datalink via Microhard P400 (or upgrade if needed)

- **Proposal Status:** ROM submitted 2026-08-24; cost estimate and award status not provided in available documents.
- **Financial Implication:** If awarded, costs would cover EO/IR camera integration, firmware updates, and fielding support.

### 3. Talisman Sabre '11 Water Purification Demo (White Paper, dated 2026-08-07)
- **Scope:** Demonstration of Seldon Technologies' Waterstick™ and Waterbox™ water purification for AFRL/USSOCOM in DoD experimentation.
