## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposals & Reports**

### Task B.2: S0 Measurement Evaluation
- **Document Date:** Created 2025-07-22; Modified 2026-01-02
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - **Data Validation Plan:** Comprehensive plan with target uncertainties for atmospheric parameters (wind, turbulence, height)
  - **Error Analysis:** Detailed analysis of S0 measurement uncertainty sources across multiple data frequencies (100 Hz, 50 Hz, 25 Hz, 10 Hz)
  - **Platform Compatibility Assessment:** Mechanical tests and simulations verifying S0 integration with Navy launch platforms (P-8A Poseidon, MH-60 helicopter, sonobuoy systems)
  - **De-icing Strategy:** Development and test plan for heat-traced Multi-Hole Pitot Probe (PTC heating), wing/propeller de-icing compound evaluation, and potential NASA wind tunnel testing
  - **Comparison Studies:** Validation of S0 measurements against tower data, dropsondes, radiosondes, and tail Doppler radar
- **Financial Implications:** De-icing work and platform compatibility testing represent significant technical scope; propeller de-icing identified as potential "show stopper" issue requiring resolution.

### Task B.5: S0 Weather Collection Concept of Operations
- **Document Date:** Created 2025-12-16; Modified 2026-01-05
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - **CONOPS Document:** Detailed operational concept for U.S. Navy use of S0 Air-Deployed (S0-AD) platform for weather data collection
  - **System Architecture Definition:** S0-AD aircraft, deployment sleeve, mission planning computer, SwiftTab control tablet, datalink/data recorder specifications
  - **Deployment Procedures:** Integration with P-8, C-130, and MH-60 manned aircraft launch platforms
- **Financial Implications:** Phase II will develop mission planning computer and SwiftTab control interface; development costs not quantified in current documents.
