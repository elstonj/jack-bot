# Project 026-01 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Name:** S0 Measurement Evaluation & Weather Collection (Navy STTR Phase I – Hazardous Weather)

**Client/Agency:** U.S. Navy

**Contract Type:** Navy STTR Phase I (Small Business Technology Transfer)

**Period of Performance:** Not explicitly stated in provided documents. Based on proposal modification dates (2025-07-22 to 2026-01-02 in B2 deliverable; Phase II DCAA checklist dated 2026-01-26), Phase I is in progress with Phase II proposal preparation underway.

**Brief Scope:** Development and validation of S0 air-deployable uncrewed aircraft system (S0-AD) for high-resolution atmospheric and ocean-surface weather data collection in extreme/hazardous environments. Phase I focuses on S0 meteorological measurement evaluation, error analysis, de-icing strategy development, Navy platform integration compatibility testing, and Concept of Operations (CONOPS) documentation. Phase II preparation includes accounting system compliance (DCAA preaward survey).
