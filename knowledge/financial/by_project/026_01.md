# Project 026-01 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Project Name:** S0 Measurement Evaluation & Weather Collection (Navy STTR Phase I)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Navy
- **Contract Type:** Navy STTR (Small Business Technology Transfer) Phase I; Hazardous Weather Topic
- **Period of Performance:** Data not available from budget docs or contracts
- **Scope:** Validation of S0 meteorological measurements, error analysis, de-icing strategy development, and concept of operations for S0 Air-Deployed (S0-AD) weather collection platform. Tasks include integration with Navy launch platforms (P-8A Poseidon, MH-60 helicopter, sonobuoy systems), detailed error analysis of wind/turbulence/height measurements, and development of heat-based de-icing for Multi-Hole Pitot Probe (MHPP).
