## Key Deliverables & Milestones

*Source: Proposals & Reports (B2: S0 Measurement Evaluation; B5: S0 Weather Collection CONOPS)*

The following technical deliverables and milestones are referenced in Navy STTR Phase I documentation:

### Phase I Deliverables (Completed/In Progress):
- **B.2: S0 Measurement Evaluation** (Document dated 2025-07-22, modified 2026-01-02)
  - Data validation plan with target uncertainties for wind, turbulence, height measurements
  - Error analysis across multiple data frequencies (100 Hz, 50 Hz, 25 Hz, 10 Hz)
  - Comparison studies validating S0 data against tower data, dropsondes, radiosondes, tail Doppler radar
  - Platform compatibility verification: P-8A Poseidon, MH-60 helicopter, sonobuoy launch systems
  - De-icing strategy development (PTC heating for Multi-Hole Pitot Probe; Ice-X compounds for wings/propellers)

- **B.5: S0 Weather Collection Concept of Operations** (Document dated 2025-12-16, modified 2026-01-05)
  - Operational concept for S0 Air-Deployed (S0-AD) platform deployment from Navy manned aircraft
  - SwiftTab control tablet interface specification
  - SwiftCore mission planning/data download system concept
  - Deployment procedures and Navy personnel training requirements

### Phase II Preparation:
- DCAA Preaward Survey Compliance (form dated 2026-01-26, modified 2026-02-02) — BST is preparing accounting system documentation for Phase II cost-type federal contract proposals

### Critical Issues Noted in Technical Documents:
- **Propeller de-icing identified as "show stopper" issue** requiring resolution (referenced in B2 document)
- Platform integration testing with Navy launch systems ongoing; some mechanical compatibility testing scheduled via simulation/NASA wind tunnel testing

**Financial implications:** Phase I deliverables appear on track; Phase II preparation underway with DCAA compliance pre-award activities suggesting imminent Phase II contract award anticipated. Current invoice activity (invoices through January 2026) aligns with Phase I execution timeline.
