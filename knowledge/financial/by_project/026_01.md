## Key Deliverables & Milestones
**Source: Proposals & Reports (B2, B5, DCAA documentation)**

### From B2: S0 Measurement Evaluation (Phase I Deliverable)
- **Error Analysis:** Detailed characterization of S0 data uncertainties in wind, turbulence, and height calculations across multiple data frequencies (100 Hz, 50 Hz, 25 Hz, 10 Hz)
- **De-icing Strategy Development:** 
  - Heat Multi-Hole Pitot Probe (MHP) tips using PTC heating
  - Evaluate Ice-X de-icing compounds on wings/propellers
  - Propeller de-icing identified as critical ("show stopper") issue requiring resolution
  - Potential NASA wind tunnel testing
- **Navy Platform Integration Studies:**
  - Sonobuoy launch system compatibility
  - P-8A Poseidon external pod mount compatibility (noted as de-scoped but verification ongoing)
  - MH-60 helicopter mount compatibility
  - Mechanical tests and simulations for launch tube validation

### From B5: S0 Weather Collection CONOPS (Phase I Deliverable)
- **Concept of Operations Document:** Operational procedures for Navy personnel to deploy S0-AD from manned naval aircraft (P-8, C-130, MH-60)
- **System Components Definition:**
  - S0-AD aircraft specifications and autonomous operation
  - Deployment sleeve design
  - SwiftTab control tablet interface
  - Mission planning computer (to be developed in Phase II)
  - VHF datalink and data recorder integration
- **Operational Readiness:** Plans for weather data collection at multiple altitudes in extreme/hazardous conditions
