# Project 026-01 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 026-01

**Project Name:** Arctic Edge 2027 (AE27) / Navy STTR Phase II – Hazardous Weather  
**Client/Agency:** NORAD & U.S. Northern Command (AE27); U.S. Navy / Office of Naval Research (STTR Phase II)  
**Contract Type:** STTR Phase II with optional cost-share; Arctic Edge 2027 demonstration submission  
**Period of Performance:** 
- AE27 Exercise: 19 April – 7 May 2027 (proposal stage as of submission date 2026-01-27)
- ONR STTR Phase II: 24 months base + 24-month option (proposed award $1M base + $1M option with 1:1 cost share)

**Brief Scope Description:**  
Black Swift Technologies is proposing the S0 VTOL UAS for demonstration in the Arctic Edge 2027 joint exercise environment (NORAD/USNORTHCOM) and simultaneously pursuing a Navy STTR Phase II effort to transition the S0 UAS from NOAA-operational research platform into a fully Navy-integrated atmospheric profiling capability for hazardous maritime weather (tropical cyclones, Arctic environments). Phase I successfully validated S0 core sensing capabilities and developed prototype turbulence/wave-height algorithms; Phase II will operationalize these capabilities and demonstrate forecast impact.
