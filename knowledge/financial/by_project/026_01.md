# Project 026-01 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 026-01

**Project Name:** Arctic Edge 2027 (AE27) / Navy STTR Phase II: Hazardous Weather (dual submissions)

**Client/Agency:** 
- NORAD & U.S. Northern Command (NORAD & USNORTHCOM) — Arctic Edge 2027 experimentation
- U.S. Navy / Office of Naval Research (ONR) — STTR Phase II Hazardous Weather Topic N25A-T025

**Contract Type:** 
- AE27: Technology demonstration / experimentation submission
- ONR STTR Phase II: Small Technology Transfer Research (government-funded R&D with 1:1 cost share option)

**Period of Performance:**
- AE27 Exercise: 19 April – 7 May 2027
- ONR STTR Phase II: 24 months (base), plus 24-month option; deadline for proposal submission: 25 February 2026

**Brief Description of Scope:**
BST is proposing the S0 VTOL UAS and related atmospheric profiling capabilities for two concurrent opportunities: (1) Arctic Edge 2027 joint exercise demonstration emphasizing autonomous flight in harsh environments, cruise missile defense, and maritime domain awareness, and (2) Phase II STTR effort to transition S0 from NOAA research platform to Navy-integrated hazardous weather (tropical cyclone and Arctic) atmospheric profiling system with real-time turbulence and wave-state algorithms.
