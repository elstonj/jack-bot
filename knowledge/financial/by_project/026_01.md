# Project 026-01 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Project Code:** 026-01
- **Client/Agency:** Multiple (NORAD & U.S. Northern Command; U.S. Navy Office of Naval Research)
- **Contract Type(s):** STTR Phase II (Navy Hazardous Weather); Experimentation submission (Arctic Edge 2027)
- **Primary System:** S0/S0-VTOL Unmanned Aircraft System (UAS)
- **Period of Performance:** Data available through January 2026; Arctic Edge 2027 exercise: 19 April – 7 May 2027; Navy STTR Phase II: 24-month base period (proposal deadline 25 February 2026)
- **Brief Description:** Black Swift Technologies is proposing/executing development of autonomous atmospheric profiling and hazardous weather observation capabilities for the S0 VTOL UAS platform, including sensor validation, turbulence and wave-state algorithms, cold-weather hardening, and Navy integration. The platform is also positioned for Arctic Edge 2027 NORAD/USNORTHCOM joint exercise demonstration.
