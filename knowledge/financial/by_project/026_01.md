# Project 026-01 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S0 Measurement Evaluation & Weather Collection (Navy STTR Phase I – Hazardous Weather)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Navy
- **Contract Type:** Navy STTR Phase I (Small Business Technology Transfer)
- **Period of Performance:** Data available 2024-04-19 through 2026-01-23 (invoices span ~22 months)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Comprehensive validation of S0 meteorological measurement systems, error analysis, de-icing strategy development, and concept of operations for S0 Air-Deployed (S0-AD) weather collection platform for Navy deployment from P-8, C-130, and MH-60 aircraft.
