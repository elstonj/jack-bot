## Key Deliverables & Milestones
**Source: Proposals & Reports (B2 & B5 documents)**

The following deliverables are referenced in the Navy STTR Phase I proposal documents but **Asana task/milestone data is not provided**. Project completion dates and financial milestones cannot be determined without Asana access.

### B2: S0 Measurement Evaluation
- **Error Analysis Task:** Detailed wind, turbulence, and height calculation error analysis at multiple data frequencies (100 Hz, 50 Hz, 25 Hz, 10 Hz)
- **De-icing Strategy Development:** Heat MHP (Multi-Hole Pitot Probe) tips using PTC heating; evaluate Ice-X de-icing compounds; propeller de-icing identified as critical "show stopper" issue
- **Platform Compatibility Verification:** Integration testing for sonobuoy launch systems, P-8A Poseidon external pod mounts, MH-60 helicopter mounts; mechanical tests and simulations
- **Data Validation Plan:** Validation of S0 measurements against tower data, dropsondes, radiosondes, and tail Doppler radar with target uncertainties defined
- **Document Date:** Created 2025-07-22; Last Modified 2026-01-02

### B5: S0 Weather Collection Concept of Operations
- **S0-AD System Architecture:** Compact Group 1 UAS (~3 lbs, <2 ft wingspan) with autonomous operation and integrated atmospheric/sea-surface sensors
- **Mission Planning & Control Systems:** Development of laptop-based mission planning computer (Phase II deliverable) and SwiftTab real-time control tablet
- **Deployment Sleeve & Datalink:** Drag parachute stabilization system; VHF-based datalink to host aircraft
- **Navy Integration:** Operational procedures for P-8 Poseidon, C-130, MH-60 deployment
- **Document Date:** Created 2025-12-16; Last Modified 2026-01-05

**Financial Implications:** No cost allocations, milestone payment schedules, or deliverable-based payment terms are provided in the proposal documents. Phase I scope is underway; Phase II mission planning/control system development is noted as future work.
