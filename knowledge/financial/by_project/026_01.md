## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S0 Measurement Evaluation & Navy STTR Phase I/II (Hazardous Weather)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Navy
- **Contract Type:** Navy STTR Phase I/II (Small Business Technology Transfer)
- **Program Focus:** S0 Air-Deployed (S0-AD) weather collection platform; measurement validation, error analysis, de-icing strategy development, and concept of operations for Navy deployment from P-8A Poseidon, C-130, and MH-60 aircraft
