- **Status:** In progress (document modified 2026-01-02)
- **Key Workstreams:**
  - Data validation plan against tower, dropsonde, radiosonde, and radar
  - Error analysis of S0 wind, turbulence, and height measurements at multiple frequencies (100/50/25/10 Hz)
  - De-icing strategy development:
    - PTC heating for Multi-Hole Pitot Probe (MHP) tips

  - Operational procedures for Navy personnel
