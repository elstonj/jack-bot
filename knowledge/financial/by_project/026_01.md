# Project 026-01 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 026-01  
**Client/Agency:** Multiple (NORAD & USNORTHCOM; U.S. Navy Office of Naval Research)  
**Contract Types:** 
- Arctic Edge 2027 (AE27) experimentation submission (proposed)
- Navy STTR Phase II — Hazardous Weather (proposed, deadline 2026-02-25)

**Period of Performance:** 
- AE27 Exercise: 19 April – 7 May 2027
- Navy STTR Phase II: 24 months base + 24-month option (if awarded)

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Project 026-01 encompasses two proposed initiatives:
1. **Arctic Edge 2027 (AE27):** Submission of Black Swift's S0 VTOL UAS for demonstration in a NORAD/USNORTHCOM Arctic defense exercise, showcasing autonomous capabilities, modular payload integration, and harsh-weather operations.
2. **Navy STTR Phase II — Hazardous Weather:** Transition of the S0 UAS from NOAA-operational research platform to Navy-integrated atmospheric profiling capability for hazardous maritime weather (tropical cyclones, Arctic environments), including sensor calibration, real-time algorithms, forecast validation, and cold-weather hardening. Phase I (completed) established baseline sensor validation and prototype algorithms.
