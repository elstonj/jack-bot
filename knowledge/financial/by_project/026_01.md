## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposals & Reports (B2 & B5 Navy STTR Phase I Deliverables)**

The Navy STTR Phase I program centers on the **S0 Measurement Evaluation task (B.2)** with the following scope:

### Major Deliverables (Phase I)

1. **Task B.2: S0 Data Validation & Error Analysis**
   - Validation plan with target uncertainties for wind, turbulence, and height measurements
   - Error analysis across multiple data frequencies (100 Hz, 50 Hz, 25 Hz, 10 Hz)
   - Comparison studies: S0 data vs. tower data, dropsondes, radiosondes, tail Doppler radar
   - **Status:** In progress; documentation dated through 2026-01-02

2. **De-icing Strategy Development**
   - Heat Multi-Hole Pitot Probe (MHP) tips using PTC heating with thermostat control
   - Evaluate Ice-X or similar de-icing compounds on wings and propellers
   - Test plan development, potentially through NASA wind tunnel testing
   - **Note:** Propeller de-icing identified as "show stopper" critical issue
   - **Estimated Completion:** Phase I deliverable

3. **Platform Integration & Compatibility Testing**
   - Evaluate integration with Navy launch platforms: sonobuoy systems, P-8A Poseidon external pod mounts, MH-60 helicopter mounts
   - Verify compatibility with P-8 and MH-60 launch tubes
   - Mechanical tests and simulations for platform validation
   - **Status:** Ongoing; some aspects noted as de-scoped

4. **Concept of Operations (CONOPS) B.5**
   - S0 Air-Deployed (S0-AD) operational procedures for Navy personnel
   - System architecture documentation (S0-AD aircraft, deployment sleeve, mission planning computer, SwiftTab tablet, datalink)
   - Deployment from P-8, C-130, MH-60 platforms
   - **Completed:** Dated 2026-01-05

5. **Phase II Proposal Preparation**
   - DCAA preaward survey compliance documentation (checklist completed 2026-02-02)
   - Indicates BST is preparing for Phase II award and cost-type contracting
