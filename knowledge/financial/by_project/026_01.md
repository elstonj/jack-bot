# Project 026-01 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S0 Measurement Evaluation & Weather Collection Concept of Operations (Navy STTR Phase I – Hazardous Weather)
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Navy
- **Contract Type:** STTR Phase I (with Phase II preparation underway)
- **Period of Performance:** Data available from April 2024 – January 2026; Phase I scope indicates active development through 2025
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Task B.2 focuses on validating S0 meteorological measurements, conducting error analysis, and developing de-icing strategies for air-deployed S0 systems. Includes integration testing with Navy launch platforms (P-8A Poseidon, MH-60 helicopter, C-130), error analysis of wind/turbulence/height measurements, de-icing strategy development (PTC heating for Multi-Hole Pitot Probe, Ice-X evaluation), and delivery of Concept of Operations (CONOPS) for S0 Air-Deployed (S0-AD) weather collection platform.
