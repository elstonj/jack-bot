**Scope Summary:**
- **Arctic Edge 2027:** Demonstration of SØ (S-Zero) VTOL UAS capabilities in Arctic defense exercise environment; emphasis on autonomous operations, modular payload integration, and harsh-weather performance (TRL 6+)
- **Navy STTR Phase II – Hazardous Weather:** Transition S0 UAS from NOAA research platform to Navy-integrated atmospheric profiling capability for maritime hazardous weather (tropical cyclones, Arctic environments). Deliverables include sensor calibration/validation, real-time onboard turbulence/wave-state algorithms, forecast impact demonstration, and cold-weather/icing hardening.
