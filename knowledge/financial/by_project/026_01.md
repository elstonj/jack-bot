# Project 026-01 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 026-01  
**Project Name:** Arctic Edge 2027 (AE27) Submission & Navy STTR Phase II – Hazardous Weather  
**Client/Agency:** 
- NORAD & U.S. Northern Command (NORAD & USNORTHCOM) — Arctic Edge 2027 experimentation
- U.S. Navy / Office of Naval Research (ONR) — STTR Phase II, Hazardous Weather Topic

**Contract Type:** 
- Government R&D (STTR Phase II base + option)
- Military exercise participation proposal (AE27)

**Period of Performance:**
- STTR Phase II: 24 months (base) + 24 months (option)
- Arctic Edge 2027 exercise: 19 April – 7 May 2027
- Proposal submission windows: 25 February 2026 (ONR deadline); 22 January 2026 (AE27 submission date)

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Transition the S0 VTOL UAS platform from NOAA-operational research into Navy-integrated atmospheric profiling capability for hazardous maritime weather (tropical cyclones, Arctic environments). Phase I validated core sensing capabilities; Phase II will operationalize sensor calibration, develop real-time onboard algorithms for turbulence and wave-state measurement, demonstrate forecast improvements, and harden for cold-weather/icing conditions. Concurrent AE27 submission positions S0 VTOL for demonstration in Arctic defense exercise emphasizing cruise missile defense, expeditionary operations, and counter-UAS scenarios.
