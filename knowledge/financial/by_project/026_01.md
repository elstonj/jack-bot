## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposals & Reports (B2: S0 Measurement Evaluation & B5: S0 Weather Collection CONOPS)**

### Task B.2: S0 Measurement Evaluation
- **Last Modified:** 2026-01-02
- **Key Deliverables:**
  - Error analysis of S0 data (wind measurements, turbulence estimation, height calculations)
  - Data validation plan with target uncertainties for atmospheric parameters
  - Comparison studies: S0 vs. tower data, dropsondes, radiosondes, tail Doppler radar
  - De-icing strategy development and testing plan
  - Heat exchanger testing for Multi-Hole Pitot Probe (MHP) de-icing
  - Propeller de-icing evaluation (identified as critical "show stopper" issue)
  - Mechanical testing for Navy platform integration (P-8A Poseidon, MH-60 helicopter, sonobuoy launch systems)
- **Frequency Analysis:** Multi-frequency testing at 100 Hz, 50 Hz, 25 Hz, 10 Hz to determine turbulent field leveling
- **Financial Implications:** Subcontractor support from By Light Mustang ($450K invoiced), university testing partnerships (Embry-Riddle, University of Maryland), equipment fabrication, and NASA wind tunnel testing (deferred to Phase II per notes)

### Task B.5: S0 Weather Collection Concept of Operations
- **Last Modified:** 2026-01-05
- **Status:** Technical CONOPS completed for Navy approval
- **Key Components:**
  - S0 Air-Deployed (S0-AD) system architecture documentation
  - Deployment sleeve & launch tube compatibility specifications
  - Mission planning system (SwiftCore) and control tablet (SwiftTab) integration
  - VHF datalink and autonomous flight operations procedures
  - Post-mission data download protocols
  - Navy personnel training & operational procedures
- **Phase II Implications:** Mission planning computer development deferred to Phase II
- **Financial Implications:** Design documentation complete; software/hardware development pending Phase II funding
