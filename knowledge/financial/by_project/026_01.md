**Client/Agency:** U.S. Navy

**Contract Type:** STTR (Small Business Technology Transfer) Phase I; Hazardous Weather Topic

**Period of Performance:** Not explicitly stated in provided documents. Based on invoice dates, activity spans from April 2024 through January 2026, with recent Phase I deliverables dated July 2025 – January 2026.

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Navy STTR Phase I program focused on validating S0 uncrewed aircraft system meteorological measurements, error analysis, de-icing strategies, and platform integration testing. Deliverables include Task B.2 (S0 Measurement Evaluation) and Task B.5 (S0 Weather Collection Concept of Operations).
