# Project 026-01 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 026-01

**Project Name/Context:** Arctic Edge 2027 (AE27) Submission & Navy STTR Phase II (Hazardous Weather)

**Client/Agency:** 
- NORAD & U.S. Northern Command (NORAD & USNORTHCOM) — Arctic Edge 2027
- U.S. Navy / Office of Naval Research (ONR) — STTR Phase II

**Contract Type:** 
- Government STTR Phase II (Small Technology Transfer)
- Proposed: Base $1M (24 months) + Option $1M (24 months, 1:1 cost share required)

**Period of Performance:**
- Arctic Edge 2027 Exercise: 19 April – 7 May 2027
- Navy STTR Phase II: 24 months base + 24-month option (proposal deadline 25 Feb 2026; exact start date not specified in documents)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Black Swift Technologies is proposing to demonstrate and operationalize the S0 VTOL UAS (autonomous unmanned aircraft) for:
1. **Arctic Edge 2027:** Participation in a NORAD/USNORTHCOM joint exercise emphasizing maritime domain awareness, expeditionary operations, C-UAS, and multi-domain awareness in Arctic conditions.
2. **Navy STTR Phase II:** Transition the S0 UAS from NOAA research platform to Navy-integrated atmospheric profiling capability for hazardous maritime weather (tropical cyclones, Arctic conditions). Phase I completed; Phase II focuses on sensor calibration, real-time algorithm maturation, forecast validation, and cold-weather/icing hardening.
