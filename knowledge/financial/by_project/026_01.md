## Key Deliverables & Milestones
**Source: Proposals & Reports (Documents B2 & B5)**

### From B2: S0 Measurement Evaluation (Navy STTR Phase I Deliverable)
- **Task B.2 — S0 Data Validation & Error Analysis**
  - Comprehensive error analysis of S0 measurements (wind, turbulence, height)
  - Data validation against tower, dropsonde, radiosonde, and tail Doppler radar
  - Multi-frequency testing (100 Hz, 50 Hz, 25 Hz, 10 Hz) to characterize turbulent field roll-off
  - Platform compatibility evaluation: sonobuoy launch, P-8A Poseidon pods, MH-60 helicopter mounts
  - **Financial Implication:** Mechanical testing, NASA wind tunnel testing for de-icing evaluation (PTC heater on MHP, Ice-X compounds on wings/propellers)
  - **Status/Timeline:** Document last modified 2026-01-02; propeller de-icing noted as critical ("show stopper") requiring prioritized testing

  - Integration with Navy launch tube systems & datalink (VHF)
  - **Financial Implication:** Phase II development of mission planning/data download software; hardware integration testing
