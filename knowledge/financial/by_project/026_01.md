## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source: Proposals & Reports (B2: S0 Measurement Evaluation & B5: S0 Weather Collection CONOPS)**

### Task B.2: S0 Measurement Evaluation (Navy STTR Phase I)
- **Error Analysis:** Detailed error analysis of S0 wind measurements, turbulence estimation, and height calculations; testing at multiple data frequencies (100 Hz, 50 Hz, 25 Hz, 10 Hz).
- **De-icing Strategy:** Development and testing of PTC heating for Multi-Hole Pitot Probe (MHPP) tips; evaluation of Ice-X de-icing compounds; propeller de-icing identified as critical ("show stopper") issue.
- **Platform Compatibility:** Mechanical testing and simulations to validate integration with Navy platforms (P-8A Poseidon, MH-60 helicopter, C-130); verification of compatibility with sonobuoy launch systems and external pod mounts.
- **Data Validation:** Comprehensive validation plan with target uncertainties for atmospheric parameters; comparison studies against tower data, dropsondes, radiosondes, and tail Doppler radar.
- **Deliverable Date:** Document last modified 2026-01-02; detailed dates for sub-tasks not explicitly provided in excerpts.

### Task B.5: S0 Weather Collection Concept of Operations (Navy STTR Phase I)
- **Operational Framework:** CONOPS for U.S. Navy personnel operating S0 Air-Deployed (S0-AD) platform from manned naval aircraft (P-8, C-130, MH-60).
- **System Development (Phase II):** Mission planning computer (laptop-based system for pre-mission planning and post-mission data download) to be developed.
- **Key Components:** S0-AD Aircraft, Deployment Sleeve, Mission Planning Computer, SwiftTab Control Tablet, Datalink/Data Recorder integration.
- **Operational Capability:** High-resolution atmospheric and ocean-surface weather data collection in extreme environments; hurricane tracking; fleet operational planning.
- **Deliverable Date:** Document created 2025-12-16, modified 2026-01-05; Phase II development ongoing.
