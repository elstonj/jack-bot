**2. Invoice vs. PO Timing Discrepancy**
- Invoice 1666 issued: 05/02/2025
- PO 63769697 issued: 06/12/2025 (10 days after invoice due date)
- **Assessment:** Unusual timing (invoice before formal PO); suggests pre-authorization agreement or verbal commitment. No payment delay noted, but this sequence warrants verification of actual payment receipt via QuickBooks data (not provided).

- **Assessment:** Final contracted amount is $5,000.00 per official PO 63769697. Recommend archiving only the authoritative invoice version to prevent billing disputes.
