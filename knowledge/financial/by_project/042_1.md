3. **Delivery Status Unclear:**

   - PO delivery date: 06/17/2025
   - As of Drive Budget Doc date, no confirmation of shipment/receipt noted
   - **Action Item:** Confirm delivery completion and receipt by Stanford to trigger final payment processing

4. **Payment Status Unknown:**
   - Invoice due: 06/01/2025 (Net 30)
   - QuickBooks payment data not available
   - No payment delays noted in Drive Budget Doc
   - **Assumption:** Standard Stanford university procurement; likely on-time payment anticipated per Net 30 terms

5. **Component Source & Cost Realization:**
   - Drive Budget Doc indicates components sourced from "BST operational spare/inventory" (used parts)
   - Rationale: "Operational fleet replacement urgency" (BST needs to replace components in active use)
   - **Unknown:** BST's actual cost to replace these components in operational fleet. If BST's replacement cost is <$5,000, project may be cash-positive; if >$5,000, project represents a loss or break-even arrangement.
   - QuickBooks data would clarify actual cost basis and profitability.

6. **Strategic Value vs. Transactional Profit:**
   - Discount justified by three non-financial factors:
     - **Partnership:** BST positioned as collaborator on future Stanford funding proposals
     - **Visibility:** S2 platform showcased at IWSHM (Sept 2025) to ~600 industry/DoD attendees
     - **Technology:** Joint "fly-by-feel" SHM development enhances BST product capabilities for future commercial/government sales
   - **Assessment:** Project appears designed to build long-term revenue pipeline rather than capture near-term margin.
