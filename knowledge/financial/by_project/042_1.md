✓ **ON BUDGET** — The project is fully invoiced at the contracted $5,000.00 amount with no remaining balance.

### Budget Analysis by Category

**Equipment / Materials:**
- **Status:** Significantly under original proposal value
- **Details:** Original component pricing totaled $18,283.26; delivered at $5,000.00 (27.3% of original cost)
- **Reason for Variance:** Negotiated 73% discount ($13,283.26) reflecting:
  - Use of spare/inventory components rather than new production units
