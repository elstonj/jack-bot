## Key Observations & Strategic Notes

1. **Partnership Model:** This is not a straightforward commercial transaction. BST provided significant discount (~73%) in exchange for:
   - Strategic visibility at IWSHM September 2025 (600+ participants, including DoD and aviation industry decision-makers)
   - Positioned as technology partner with Stanford for future joint funding proposals (likely SBIR, NSF, DoD research grants)
   - Technology integration opportunity (fly-by-feel SHM on S2 platform) serves as R&D proof-of-concept

2. **Inventory Clearing:** Components were spare/used parts from BST operational fleet; discount reflects opportunity to clear inventory while maintaining strategic relationship.

3. **Email Chain Context (04/21–05/05/2025):**
   - Stanford originally requested loan of complete S2 aircraft; BST unable to provide (aircraft in active use)
   - BST offered component kit as alternative; Prof. Chang budget-constrained to ~$5,000
   - Jack Elston (BST CEO) estimated brand-new components at ~$10K
   - Final negotiation: $5,000 for used/spare inventory + strategic partnership value

4. **Future Revenue Potential:** Document notes "anticipated future joint funding proposals" — this $5,000 transaction may seed larger SBIR Phase I/II or agency-funded collaborations. Track IWSHM conference outcomes and subsequent Stanford/BST proposal submissions.
