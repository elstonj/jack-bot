## Project Overview

## Negotiation & Discount Context

**Negotiated Discount Rationale:**

| Element | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Original Component Pricing** | ~$10,000–$10,493.26 (new components per Jack Elston, BST CEO, 04/30/2025) |
| **Stanford Budget Constraint** | ~$5,000 maximum (Prof. Fu-Kuo Chang, 05/02/2025) |
| **Final Negotiated Price** | $5,000.00 |
| **Discount Provided** | $13,283.26 (73% off component list) |
| **Discount Justification** | Used/spare inventory from BST operational fleet; expedited fulfillment; strategic partnership opportunity |

**Strategic Context (from Drive Budget Document):**
- Stanford initially requested full S2 aircraft loan for "fly-by-feel" technology testing
- Black Swift unable to provide complete aircraft (in active operational use)
- Offered wing, fuselage, and nose cone components from spare operational inventory
- BST estimated $6,000 cost to replace inventory in fleet; Stanford negotiated to $5,000
- Agreement finalized 05/05/2025; formalized via PO 06/12/2025
- **Partnership Value:** Discount structured to enable:
  - Joint participation in future funding proposals
  - Public demonstration at International Workshop on Structural Health Monitoring (IWSHM, Sept 9–11, 2025 at Stanford; ~600 participants including DoD and aviation industry)
  - Collaborative "fly-by-feel" technology development with Acellent Technologies Inc. (Dr. Amrita Kumar)
