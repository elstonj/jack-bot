# Project 042-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S2 UAS Platform Component Supply for Structural Health Monitoring Research
- **Client/Agency:** Stanford University, Department of Aeronautics and Astronautics (Prof. Fu-Kuo Chang)
- **Contract Type:** Commercial equipment purchase / collaborative research arrangement
- **Vendor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC, Boulder, CO
- **Period of Performance:** May 2, 2025 – June 2025 (invoice period); delivery requested by June 17, 2025
- **Brief Description:** Supply of S2 Unmanned Aircraft System (UAS) platform components (right wing, fuselage with avionics, nose cone, payload development kit) for integration of structural health monitoring ("fly-by-feel") technology at Stanford University's Structures & Composites Lab. Components sourced from Black Swift operational spare inventory as part of collaborative research partnership with anticipated future joint funding proposals and public demonstration at International Workshop on Structural Health Monitoring (IWSHM) in September 2025.
