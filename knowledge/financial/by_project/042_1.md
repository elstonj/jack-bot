### Key Financial Observations

#### 1. **Aggressive Discount Structure**
- **Negotiated Price:** $5,000.00
- **Component List Price (retail):** $18,283.26
- **Discount Provided:** $13,283.26 (73% off)
- **Assessment:** Significant discount reflects:
  - Use of spare/operational inventory components (not new manufacturing)
  - Strategic partnership value for BST (Stanford branding, IWSHM demonstration opportunity, future proposal co-development)
  - Operational necessity (BST needed to recover $5K+ cost to replace components in active fleet; $5K negotiated settlement was compromise)
  
  *Financial Risk:* Discount structure indicates this is a below-cost or at-cost transaction for BST. Actual profitability depends on QBO cost data (not provided) — specifically what BST's internal cost-of-goods for these spare components is. If COGS > $5,000, this deal operates at a loss.

#### 2. **Invoice Discrepancies (Resolved)**

- **Resolution:** Stanford PO 63769697 (authoritative source) confirms avionics-inclusive specification; final billed amount $5,000 reconciles across all versions after discount application
- **Financial Impact:** None — all versions finalize to $5,000 total. However, document control/version management should be tightened to avoid future confusion on future projects.

#### 3. **Payment Timeline Status**

- Stanford PO issued (formalized): 06/12/2025 (10 days after invoice)
