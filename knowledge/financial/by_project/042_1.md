### Key Findings & Risk Assessment

#### ✅ **Strengths**
1. **Full Budget Commitment:** Invoice equals contract value; no cost overruns or scope creep.
2. **Clear Negotiation Documentation:** Email chain and budget doc clearly document discount rationale and strategic value.
3. **Delivery Timeline:** Delivery date (06/17/2025) achievable and specified in PO.
4. **Tax-Exempt Status:** Stanford's resale exemption (California Sellers Permit SR-GH-26-001337) properly applied, reducing BST's compliance burden.

#### ⚠️ **Moderate Concerns**
1. **Invoice-PO Timing Mismatch:** Invoice issued 05/02/2025 but PO not formalized until 06/12/2025. This 40-day gap is common in university procurement but creates cash flow uncertainty:
   - **Action:** Confirm payment received by 07/01/2025 (30 days from PO date).
   - **Risk:** If payment delayed beyond 07/01/2025, BST may need to pursue Stanford's Accounts Payable contact (not specified in documents).

2. **Invoice Document Discrepancies:** Multiple versions of Invoice 1666 exist (with/without avionics). Ensure Stanford received and is processing the correct final version (WITH avionics, $10,490.26 fuselage line item, $5,000.00 total):
   - **Action:** Confirm Stanford Invoice 1666 (05.05.25).pdf is official; archive other versions to prevent duplicate payment attempts.

3. **Significant Discount Provided:** 73% discount ($13,283.26 reduction) represents substantial profit margin concession by BST:
   - **Strategic Justification:** Partnership for IWSHM demo (Sept 2025), future proposal collaboration, proof of concept for S2 platform.
   - **Financial Risk:** If IWSHM demo or future proposals do not materialize, BST will not recoup margin.
   - **Mitigation:** Ensure profit/strategic value documented; monitor Stanford's execution of integration and demonstration milestones.

4. **Spare Inventory Components:** Components sourced from BST operational inventory rather than new stock:
   - **Risk:** Spares may have limited warranty/lifespan. Recommend confirming component condition, calibration, and any warranty terms with Prof. Chang.
   - **Delivery Risk:** If Stanford requires replacement of defective components, BST may face unbudgeted rework costs.

#### 🟢 **No Critical Issues**
- Contractual obligations met within approved scope and dollar limit.
- No indication of scope creep, change orders, or budget overruns.
- No cost categories underspent (project is fully committed).
