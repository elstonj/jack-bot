   - BST CEO (Jack Elston) indicated new components would cost ~$10K; negotiated down to $5K per Stanford's budget constraint

     - BST to be included as partner in future Stanford funding proposals
     - S2 platform to be showcased at IWSHM September 2025 (600+ participants, DoD/aviation industry visibility)
     - Joint "fly-by-feel" technology development opportunity
   - **Assessment:** Discount reflects strategic value of partnership and market visibility rather than loss/cost overrun; appropriate for collaborative research arrangement

3. **Invoice Discrepancy Clarified:**
   - Multiple invoice versions exist in documentation with conflicting component descriptions (fuselage with vs. without avionics)
   - Budget document confirms final official invoice per Stanford PO 63769697 includes fuselage WITH avionics
   - All versions total $5,000.00 final amount
   - **Assessment:** Document version control issue resolved; no financial impact; contract amount consistent
