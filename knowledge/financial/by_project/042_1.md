## Negotiation & Discount Context

Per Drive Budget Document email chain and funding modifications section:

**Original Pricing vs. Negotiated Price:**
- **Original component list value:** $18,283.26 (new components)
- **Negotiated contract price:** $5,000.00
- **Discount provided:** $13,283.26 (73% off)

**Rationale for Discount (per Drive Budget Document):**
1. **Inventory Status:** Components sourced from BST operational spare/fleet inventory (used/reconditioned, not new)
2. **Expedited Fulfillment:** Rapid delivery required; FST prioritized order
3. **Strategic Partnership:** Discount provided in exchange for:
   - BST partnership in future Stanford funding proposals
   - Showcase of S2 platform at IWSHM 2025 (estimated 600+ attendees including DoD, aviation industry)
   - Joint "fly-by-feel" technology development collaboration with Prof. Fu-Kuo Chang and Acellent Technologies Inc.
4. **Fleet Replacement Urgency:** BST required ~$6,000 compensation to replace components in operational fleet; Stanford's budget ceiling was $5,000; negotiated to $5,000 after discussion

**Context:** Initial request from Stanford was for a loan of a complete S2 aircraft; BST offered components instead as a compromise, with cost-sharing arrangement.
