# Project 042-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S2 UAS Platform Components for Stanford Structural Health Monitoring Integration
- **Client/Agency:** Stanford University, Department of Aeronautics and Astronautics (Prof. Fu-Kuo Chang)
- **Contract Type:** Commercial equipment purchase / collaborative research arrangement
- **Vendor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC, Boulder, CO
- **Period of Performance:** May 2, 2025 – June 2025 (invoice period); delivery requested by June 17, 2025
- **Brief Description:** Supply of S2 UAS platform components (right wing, fuselage with avionics, nose cone, payload development kit) to support Stanford's integration of structural health monitoring ("fly-by-feel") technology. Components are spare/used inventory from BST operational fleet. Strategic partnership arrangement with anticipated future joint funding proposals and public demonstration at International Workshop on Structural Health Monitoring (IWSHM) in September 2025.
