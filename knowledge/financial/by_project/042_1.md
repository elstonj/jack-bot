# Project 042-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Project Name:** S2 UAS Platform Components for Stanford University Structural Health Monitoring Integration
- **Client/Agency:** Stanford University, Department of Aeronautics and Astronautics (Prof. Fu-Kuo Chang)
- **Contract Type:** Commercial equipment purchase / collaborative research arrangement
- **Vendor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC, Boulder, CO
- **Period of Performance:** May 2, 2025 – June 2025 (invoice period); delivery requested by June 17, 2025
- **Brief Scope:** Supply of S2 UAS platform components (right wing, fuselage with avionics, nose cone, payload development kit) for integration of structural health monitoring ("fly-by-feel") technology. Components are spare parts from BST operational inventory provided at negotiated discount in exchange for partnership in future funding proposals and public demonstration at International Workshop on Structural Health Monitoring (IWSHM) in September 2025.
