# Project 042-1 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** S2 UAS Platform Component Supply & Integration – Stanford University Structural Health Monitoring (SHM) Collaboration
- **Client/Agency:** Stanford University, Department of Aeronautics and Astronautics (Prof. Fu-Kuo Chang)
- **Contract Type:** Commercial equipment purchase / collaborative research arrangement
- **Vendor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC, Boulder, CO
- **Period of Performance:** May 2, 2025 – June 2025 (invoice/delivery period); delivery requested by June 17, 2025
- **Brief Description:** Supply of S2 UAS platform components (right wing, fuselage with avionics, nose cone, payload development kit) to Stanford for integration of "fly-by-feel" structural health monitoring technology. Components sourced from BST operational spare inventory and provided at significant negotiated discount in exchange for partnership opportunities and demonstration at international conference (IWSHM, September 2025).
