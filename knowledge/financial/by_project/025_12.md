**Description:**  
Long-Range Fixed-Wing Evaluation & DoD Autopilot Enablement (BYL-LRFE). Black Swift Technologies conducted a two-effort program to develop and flight-test a long-range, fully electric fixed-wing UAS (Mustang G2) meeting DoD requirements (≤55 lbs GTOW, 400–450 km range, truck/government launcher compatibility). Effort 1 involved SwiftCore integration into existing Mustang Unit 3 for baseline data collection; Effort 2 encompassed full G2 design, COTS procurement, assembly, ground integration, and comprehensive flight testing with SwiftCore as primary flight computer.
