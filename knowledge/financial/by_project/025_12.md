- **Client/Agency:** By Light Professional IT Services
- **Contract Type:** Commercial (fixed-price engagement)
- **Period of Performance:** October 6, 2025 – December 5, 2025 (9 weeks)
- **Project Owner:** Maciej Stachura (Black Swift Technologies)
- **Status:** **ARCHIVED — Project marked complete as of December 31, 2025**

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Black Swift Technologies conducted a two-effort program to develop and flight-test a long-range, fully electric fixed-wing UAS (Mustang G2) meeting DoD requirements (≤55 lbs GTOW, 400–450 km range, truck/government launcher compatibility). Effort 1 integrated SwiftCore into an existing Mustang Unit 3 for baseline data collection; Effort 2 encompassed full G2 design finalization, COTS procurement, prototype assembly, ground integration, and comprehensive flight testing with SwiftCore as the primary flight computer.
