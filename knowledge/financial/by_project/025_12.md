# Project 025-12 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** [043-2] By Light Mustang – Long-Range Fixed-Wing Evaluation & DoD Autopilot Enablement (BYL-LRFE)
- **Client/Agency:** By Light Professional IT Services
- **Contract Type:** Commercial (fixed-price development contract)
- **Period of Performance:** October 6, 2025 – December 5, 2025 (9 weeks)
- **Project Owner:** Maciej Stachura (Black Swift Technologies)
- **Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Project marked complete as of December 31, 2025

**Brief Description:**  
Black Swift Technologies conducted a 9-week program to develop and flight-test a long-range, fully electric fixed-wing UAS (Mustang G2) for ByLight. The effort centered on integrating SwiftCore (BST's flight computer and autopilot system) into an existing Mustang airframe for baseline data collection (Effort 1) and full G2 design, COTS procurement, assembly, ground integration, and comprehensive flight testing (Effort 2). The work culminated in handoff to ByLight for integration of government-provided autopilot systems. Key deliverables included design finalization, prototype assembly, and flight test data.
