- **Brief Scope:** Integration of Black Swift's SwiftCore flight management system into an existing Mustang UAS airframe (Unit 3) for rapid baseline data collection, followed by full G2 design, COTS procurement, prototype assembly, ground/flight integration, and comprehensive flight testing.
