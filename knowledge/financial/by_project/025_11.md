## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Data not available from Asana.** The proposals/reports provided reference the following technical milestones for the Navy SBIR magnetometer work:

1. **Electromagnetic Compatibility (EMC) Testing (Ongoing as of 2025-11-12)**
   - Isolation testing of motor and battery EMI using dedicated test rig
   - Shielding strategy with MuMetal material optimization
   - Magnetometer placement goal: nose or ≤1 foot forward of aircraft
   - Filter testing (10 Hz and unfiltered) to characterize EMI signatures
   - **Deliverable:** Clean characterization data and EMI mitigation report

2. **Hydrophone / Acoustic Sensor Development (Concurrent)**
   - Parallel development of hydrophone integration
