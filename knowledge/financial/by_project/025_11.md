# Project 025-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 025-11  
**Client/Agency:** Multiple (U.S. Navy SBIR, Department of Commerce/NOAA, ByLight)  
**Contract Types:** SBIR Phase I, IDIQ (2026), Statement of Work (SOW)  
**Primary Products/Systems:** S0 UAS, SwiftCore (autopilot/flight computer)  
**Period of Performance:** 2024–2025 (invoices span April 2024 – November 2025)  

**Brief Description:** Project 025-11 encompasses multiple concurrent development and operational efforts:
- **Navy SBIR Magnetometer Integration**: Phase 1 development of sonobuoy-launched UAS for anti-submarine warfare (ASW), focusing on magnetometer integration testing and electromagnetic compatibility on the S0 platform (as of November 2025).
- **NOAA S0 Platform IDIQ (2026):** Proposed indefinite delivery/indefinite quantity contract structure for Black Swift S0 platform services to NOAA's Aircraft Operations Center, covering platform procurement, training, operational support, testing, and integration.
- **ByLight Mustang Program (BYL-LRFE):** 9-week SOW for SwiftCore integration into Mustang G2 fixed-wing UAS development, with flight testing and handoff to ByLight for government autopilot integration.
