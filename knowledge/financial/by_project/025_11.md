## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**From Proposals/Reports (`Navy SBIR Magnetometer Phase 1`),** the following technical work packages are described:

1. **Magnetometer Integration Testing** (ongoing as of 2025-11-12)
   - Isolation testing of motor and battery EMI
   - MuMetal shielding sourcing and optimization
   - Filter testing (10 Hz and unfiltered frequency domain analysis)
   - Data validation at test site ("Sod Farm")

2. **Electromagnetic Compatibility (EMC) Characterization**
   - Motor EMI cutoff range mapping (0–5 feet without shielding)
   - Frequency domain analysis to optimize shielding placement
   - Concurrent hydrophone/acoustic sensor development

3. **S0 UAS Platform Integration**
   - Magnetometer nose mounting or ≤1 foot forward placement
   - SwiftCore autopilot integration and telemetry support
