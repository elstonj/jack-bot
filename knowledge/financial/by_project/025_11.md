# Project 025-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Code:** 025-11

**Project Name(s):** Multiple concurrent efforts identified across data sources:
- Navy SBIR Magnetometer Phase 1 (Sonobuoy-Launched UAS for ASW)

**Client/Agency:** 
- U.S. Navy (SBIR N251-016)

**Contract Types:**
- Navy SBIR Phase 1
- NOAA IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity)
- Fixed-price development agreement (ByLight)

**Period of Performance:**
- Navy SBIR: Ongoing as of November 2025
- NOAA IDIQ: 2026 framework (pricing structure defined)
- ByLight Mustang: 9-week program (created November 25, 2025; modified December 5, 2025)

**Brief Description of Scope:**

Project 025-11 encompasses multiple parallel development initiatives leveraging Black Swift's S0 UAS platform and SwiftCore autopilot system:

1. **Navy SBIR Magnetometer Integration** — Developing a sonobuoy-launched unmanned aerial vehicle for anti-submarine warfare with integrated magnetometer sensor; testing electromagnetic compatibility, shielding strategies, and concurrent hydrophone/acoustic sensor development.

2. **NOAA S0 Platform IDIQ** — Establishing pricing and service delivery framework for the S0 platform under a 2026 indefinite delivery contract with NOAA's Aircraft Operations Center, covering platform procurement, training, operational support, documentation, and testing.

3. **ByLight Mustang G2 Development** — 9-week effort integrating SwiftCore flight computer into a long-range, fully electric fixed-wing UAS (≤55 lbs, 400–450 km range) meeting DoD requirements, with rapid baseline testing on Mustang Unit 3 and full G2 design/assembly/flight test.

### ByLight Mustang G2 Development (BYL-LRFE)
**Program Timeline:** 9 weeks (as of November 25, 2025 SOW creation)

**Effort 1 – Baseline Data Collection (Mustang Unit 3):**
- Integrate SwiftCore autopilot into existing Mustang Unit 3
- Conduct rapid baseline flight testing
- Deliver system identification data (thrust-to-power, trim states, control effectiveness)

**Effort 2 – Full G2 Design, Build, and Flight Test:**
1. Freeze mission constraints (payload, range 400–450 km, ≤55 lbs GTOW, launch compatibility)
2. Power-to-weight optimization and propulsion trade studies
3. COTS procurement (motors, ESCs, batteries, structures)
4. Assembly and integration with SwiftCore
5. Comprehensive flight testing campaign
6. Handoff to ByLight for government-provided autopilot (EchoMAV/Cube) integration
