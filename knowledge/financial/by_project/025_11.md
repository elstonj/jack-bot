# Project 025-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR Magnetometer Phase 1 – Sonobuoy-Launched UAS for ASW Operations
- **Client/Agency:** U.S. Navy
- **Contract Type:** SBIR Phase 1
- **Program/Solicitation:** Navy SBIR N251-016 "Expendable Sonobuoy-Launched Unmanned Aerial Vehicle for ASW Cued Search, Detection, Tracking, and Classification"
- **Period of Performance:** Data not available from proposals/reports
- **Brief Description:** Development of a sonobuoy-launched unmanned aerial vehicle (S0 UAS platform) for anti-submarine warfare (ASW) operations, with focus on magnetometer integration testing, electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) assessment, shielding optimization, and concurrent hydrophone/acoustic sensor development. As of November 2025, the project was in active testing phase with motor/battery EMI characterization and MuMetal shielding evaluation underway.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones
**Source: Proposals & Reports (Navy SBIR Meeting, November 12, 2025)**

### Completed/In-Progress:
- **Isolation Testing** – Motor and battery EMI tested separately using dedicated test rig; frequency domain analysis underway
- **Shielding Strategy** – MuMetal material sourced; modular shielding being tested for magnetometer placement
- **Data Collection** – Clean 30-minute baseline test conducted at "Sod Farm" location; 60 Hz spike identified and analyzed
- **Filter Testing** – 10 Hz filter applied to frequency analysis; extended testing without filtering to characterize motor/battery EMI signatures

### Upcoming/Planned:
- **Full Aircraft Integration Testing** – Integration of shielded magnetometer into S0 UAS airframe
- **Hydrophone/Acoustic Sensor Development** – Concurrent development of acoustic detection capability for ASW mission
- **Magnetometer Placement Optimization** – Goal to position magnetometer in nose or within 1 foot forward of aircraft
- **EMC Validation** – Verification that EMI isolation meets Navy performance specifications

**Financial Implications:** Specific milestone billing or acceptance criteria not detailed in proposals. Budget structure and payment schedule relative to these deliverables is unknown without contract documentation.
