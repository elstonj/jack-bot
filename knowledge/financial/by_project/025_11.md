# Project 025-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 025-11  
**Project Name:** Navy SBIR Magnetometer Phase 1 (Sonobuoy-Launched UAS for ASW)  
**Client/Agency:** U.S. Navy  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase 1  
**Program/Solicitation:** Navy SBIR N251-016 "Expendable Sonobuoy-Launched Unmanned Aerial Vehicle for ASW Cued Search, Detection, Tracking, and Classification"  
**Period of Performance:** Data available from 2024-04 through 2025-11  
**Primary BST Products:** S0 UAS platform, SwiftCore autopilot/flight computer  
**Key Personnel (from docs):** Maciej Stachura, Jack Elston  

**Scope Description:**  
Development and integration of a magnetometer payload onto the Black Swift S0 unmanned aerial system for anti-submarine warfare (ASW) applications. Technical work includes electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) testing, motor and battery EMI isolation testing, MuMetal shielding optimization, and concurrent hydrophone/acoustic sensor development. Phase 1 testing focused on characterizing electromagnetic interference signatures and validating sensor placement in the nose of the aircraft.
