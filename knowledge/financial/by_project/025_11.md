# Project 025-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 025-11

**Full Project Name:** Navy SBIR Magnetometer Phase 1 — Sonobuoy-Launched UAV for ASW

**Client/Agency:** U.S. Navy

**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I

**Solicitation:** Navy SBIR N251-016 "Expendable Sonobuoy-Launched Unmanned Aerial Vehicle for ASW Cued Search, Detection, Tracking, and Classification"

**Period of Performance:** Data not available from Proposals/Reports or Budget Docs

**Description of Scope:** Development of magnetometer integration testing on the S0 UAS platform for anti-submarine warfare (ASW) operations. Technical work includes electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) testing, electromagnetic interference isolation, MuMetal shielding optimization, and concurrent hydrophone/acoustic sensor development. Primary focus is positioning the magnetometer in the nose or forward section of the aircraft with minimal EMI from motors and batteries.

**Status as of 2025-11-12:** Ongoing magnetometer testing; phase development with phased EMC testing approach.
