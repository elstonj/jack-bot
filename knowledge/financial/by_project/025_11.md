## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 025-11  
**Project Name:** Navy SBIR Magnetometer Phase 1 – Sonobuoy-Launched UAS for ASW  
**Client/Agency:** U.S. Navy  
**Contract Type:** SBIR Phase I  
**Program/Solicitation:** Navy SBIR N251-016 "Expendable Sonobuoy-Launched Unmanned Aerial Vehicle for ASW Cued Search, Detection, Tracking, and Classification"  
**Period of Performance:** Data not available from proposals/reports; inferred active through November 2025 based on meeting date (2025-11-12)  
**Brief Description:** Development of a sonobuoy-launched unmanned aerial vehicle (UAS) equipped with magnetometer and hydrophone/acoustic sensors for anti-submarine warfare (ASW) detection, tracking, and classification. Work includes electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) testing of magnetometer integration on the S0 UAS platform, shielding optimization, and concurrent hydrophone/acoustic sensor development.

### Active Work (as of November 2025):
1. **Magnetometer Integration Testing (Ongoing)**
   - Electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) testing of magnetometer on S0 UAS platform
   - Isolation testing of motor and battery using dedicated test rig
   - MuMetal shielding material sourced and in testing phase
   - Goal: Position magnetometer in nose or ≤1 foot out front of aircraft
   - Frequency filtering and validation testing underway
   - Milestone: Clean 30-minute test conducted at "Sod Farm" location (completed as of Nov 2025)

2. **Shielding Optimization (In Progress)**
   - Characterization of motor and battery EMI signatures (frequency domain analysis ongoing)
   - Further testing planned without 10 Hz filtering to validate shielding placement strategies

   - Concurrent development mentioned but detailed timeline not provided in available documents
