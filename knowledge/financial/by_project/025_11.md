## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source:** Proposals & Reports (Navy SBIR meeting 2025-11-12; ByLight SOW 2025-11-25)

### Navy SBIR Phase 1 (Magnetometer Integration)
- **Status (as of Nov 12, 2025):** Ongoing magnetometer integration testing on S0 UAS
- **Key Activities:**
  - Electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) testing: motor and battery isolation testing
  - MuMetal shielding design and placement optimization
  - Frequency domain analysis to characterize EMI signatures
  - Goal: Position magnetometer in nose or ≤1 foot forward on aircraft
- **Next Steps:** Finalize shielding design; conduct full-aircraft integrated testing (implied but not explicitly scheduled)
- **Financial Implication:** Phase 1 scope and milestones documented; no budget ceiling provided.

### ByLight Mustang G2 Program (SwiftCore Integration)
- **Program Duration:** 9 weeks (start date not specified; document created/modified 2025-11-25 to 2025-12-05)
- **Effort 1:** SwiftCore integration into Mustang Unit 3 for baseline data collection (rapid, ~4 weeks implied)
- **Effort 2:** G2 design, COTS procurement, assembly, comprehensive flight testing, handoff to ByLight (5 weeks)
- **Deliverables:**
  - Integrated SwiftCore in Unit 3 with flight-test data
  - G2 design documentation and aerodynamic/propulsion analysis
  - Fully assembled and tested G2 with SwiftCore as primary flight computer
  - High-rate telemetry and logging data in NetCDF format
  - Handoff package for government autopilot (EchoMAV/Cube) integration
- **Financial Implication:** Recent invoices (1714, 1715, 1716; Oct–Nov 2025) show $300k to ByLight for Mustang work; likely tied to this 9-week effort.
