# Project 025-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 025-11  
**Project Name:** Navy SBIR Magnetometer Phase 1 / S0 UAS Platform Development  
**Client/Agency:** U.S. Navy; Department of Commerce (NOAA Aircraft Operations Center)  
**Contract Type:** Navy SBIR N251-016 (Phase 1); 2026 IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity) proposed  
**Period of Performance:** Ongoing; Phase 1 active as of November 2025  
**Brief Description:** Development and integration of a magnetometer sensor system on the Black Swift S0 unmanned aerial system (UAS) for anti-submarine warfare (ASW) applications. Work includes electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) testing, shielding optimization, and sensor integration. Concurrent activities include S0 platform development for NOAA's Aircraft Operations Center and SwiftCore autopilot integration for ByLight's Mustang G2 fixed-wing UAS program.
