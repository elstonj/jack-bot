## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**From Proposals & Reports — Navy SBIR Magnetometer Phase 1 Project (2025-11-12 Meeting):**

1. **Magnetometer Integration Testing**
   - Ongoing electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) testing on S0 UAS platform
   - Motor and battery isolation testing using dedicated test rig
   - Shielding optimization with MuMetal material
   - Target placement: magnetometer in nose or within 1 foot of front of aircraft

2. **Filter and Frequency Analysis**
   - 10 Hz filter testing completed; further unfiltered characterization underway
   - Motor/battery EMI signature analysis in frequency domain
   - Clean 30-minute validation test conducted at "Sod Farm" location

3. **Concurrent Hydrophone/Acoustic Sensor Development**
   - Mentioned as concurrent work stream but details not elaborated in provided excerpt

4. **Shielding Validation**
   - Motor EMI cutoff range identified at 0–5 feet without shielding
   - Ongoing optimization to determine primary source (motor vs. battery) and optimal shielding placement
