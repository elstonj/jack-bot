### Navy SBIR Magnetometer Phase 1 (N251-016)
**Status:** Ongoing as of November 12, 2025 (per meeting presentation)

- **Magnetometer Integration Testing:** Phased electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) approach underway
  - Isolation testing (motor and batteries using dedicated test rig)
  - Shielding optimization with MuMetal material
  - Placement goal: nose or ≤1 foot forward of fuselage
  - 10 Hz and unfiltered filter testing in progress
  - Validation testing completed at "Sod Farm" location
  
- **Expected Deliverables:** Clean integration data, EMI characterization, shielding design specifications (specific dates not provided in documents)
