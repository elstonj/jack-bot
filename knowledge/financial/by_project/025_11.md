## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source:** Proposals/Reports (Navy SBIR meeting notes and ByLight SOW)

### Navy SBIR Phase 1: Magnetometer Integration Testing
- **Focus:** Electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) testing of magnetometer on S0 UAS platform
- **Current Status (as of Nov 12, 2025):**
  - Isolation testing underway—motor and battery EMI characterization
  - MuMetal shielding sourced and being tested
  - 10 Hz filtering applied; further characterization without filtering ongoing
  - Clean 30-minute test completed at "Sod Farm" location
- **Key Milestone:** Motor EMI cutoff range identified at 0–5 feet without shielding
- **Pending:** Final placement strategy (goal: magnetometer in nose or ≤1 foot out front); filter optimization; full EMC clearance
- **Financial Implication:** Phase 1 budget ceiling and contract value **not documented in provided materials**
