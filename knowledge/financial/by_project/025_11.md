# Project 025-11 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
**Project Code:** 025-11  
**Primary Client/Agency:** U.S. Navy (Navy SBIR Program N251-016)  
**Secondary Clients:** Department of Commerce (NOAA/AOC), ByLight (Bryan Sparling)  
**Contract Types:** SBIR Phase I, IDIQ (proposed 2026), Statement of Work (SOW)  
**Primary Program:** Navy SBIR — "Expendable Sonobuoy-Launched Unmanned Aerial Vehicle for ASW Cued Search, Detection, Tracking, and Classification"  
**Period of Performance:** Ongoing (meeting dated November 12, 2025; SOW dated November 25–December 5, 2025)  

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
BST is executing multiple concurrent efforts under Project 025-11:
1. **Navy SBIR Magnetometer Integration** — Electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) testing of magnetometer sensors integrated onto the S0 UAS platform for anti-submarine warfare (ASW) applications, including isolation testing, MuMetal shielding optimization, and hydrophone/acoustic sensor development.
2. **NOAA S0 Platform IDIQ Framework** — Proposed indefinite delivery/indefinite quantity contract structure for the Black Swift S0 platform under Department of Commerce/NOAA's Aircraft Operations Center, covering platform procurement, training, operational support, course development, modeling, integration, and testing.
3. **ByLight Mustang G2 Development (BYL-LRFE)** — 9-week SOW with ByLight to develop and flight-test a long-range, fully electric fixed-wing UAS (Mustang G2) integrating SwiftCore autopilot for DoD requirements (≤55 lbs GTOW, 400–450 km range).
