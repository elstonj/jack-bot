**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Development and deployment of a UAS-based observational system for nighttime fire observations to support NOAA's FIREX mission. Primary platform: Black Swift Technologies S2 UAS. Includes development of multiple sensor payloads (near-IR camera, mid-IR and SWIR telescopes, CO sensors, aerosol instruments) for atmospheric and fire-related data collection.
