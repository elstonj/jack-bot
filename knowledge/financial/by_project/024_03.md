# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, Opportunity A.59)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 proposal submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Brief Description of Scope**: 
  - Multi-partner research project to develop fuel moisture and vegetative index mapping capabilities for wildfire susceptibility assessment
  - Partners: Pepperwood (field validation campaigns), Weather Stream (aerial data collection and processing), Black Swift Technologies (UAS platform and systems integration)
  - Leverages small UAS platform for 2m resolution soil moisture mapping (192 flights/year), NISAR satellite data (200m resolution, 6-day intervals), and UAVSAR data (6m resolution, 9 flights Year 1)
  - 9 field validation campaigns at Pepperwood Preserve with integrated data synthesis
