# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Brief Description of Scope**: 
  - Multi-partner research project integrating small UAS, NISAR satellite, and UAVSAR data for soil moisture and live fuel moisture mapping
  - 192 small UAS flights per year for 2m-resolution soil moisture maps
  - Field validation campaigns (9 annually) at Pepperwood Preserve
  - Data processing and archival with open science protocols
  - Partners: Black Swift Technologies (lead), Pepperwood Preserve, Weather Stream
