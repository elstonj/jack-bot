### Key Observations

1. **Budget Completeness (Proposed Estimate)**
   - Total proposed budget of **$518,293.20** is distributed across two primary partners (Pepperwood $198,426 + Weather Stream $239,900.40).
   - Budget appears reasonably detailed with personnel roles, hours, rates, and supporting operational costs justified by technical scope.
   - Indirect cost rate capped at NASA maximum (20%).
   - Note: A third "Sentry/Crowheart" budget document was included in the Drive folder but is explicitly a separate project and has been excluded from this summary.

2. **Personnel Effort Realism**
   - **Pepperwood**: Ryan Ferrell and Makayla Freed comprise the core field effort (~1,850 hrs/year), supporting 9 field validation campaigns. This aligns with scope (soil moisture and LFM sampling).
   - **Weather Stream**: ~415 hrs/year for satellite/UAS data integration and 3 flights/year, reasonable for remote sensing processing.
   - Management and administrative overhead (Margaret, Zach, Hana, Stephanie) is modest relative to direct labor.

3. **Cost Breakdown**
   - **Direct Labor dominates** (~$222,565 or 43% of total budget) — appropriate for research-intensive project.
   - **Indirect Costs ($46,398.80 or 9%)** — within NASA allowance.
   - **Operating Expenses ($12,429 or 2%)** — field lodging ($3,500), vehicle fuel ($1,000), and supplies ($5,060, $1,395 equipment) are modest and project-specific.
   - **Travel ($2,974 or <1%)** — Weather Stream annual visit to Pepperwood.

4. **Budget Realism vs. Scope**
   - Field campaigns at Pepperwood Preserve (9/year for 3 years = 27 total) are supported by personnel and lodging allocations.
   - Small UAS operations (192 flights/year implied in OSDMP) are budgeted under personnel data processing labor, not as explicit aircraft operating costs — **potential concern** if aircraft fuel, maintenance, or depreciation is unaccounted.
   - Satellite data (NISAR, UAVSAR) appears to be cost-share or no-cost contribution from NASA; no budget line for satellite data access fees.

5. **Risks & Concerns**
   - **Award Status Unknown**: Proposal submitted as Step 2; no indication whether it has been selected for funding or is still under review. Budget may change in final negotiation.
   - **No Actuals Yet**: Cannot assess whether project will stay on budget without QuickBooks data post-award.
   - **Small UAS Operating Costs Unclear**: Field campaign budgets focus on personnel and field supplies; missing explicit line for aircraft fuel, maintenance, sensor wear, etc. Could indicate either (a) costs rolled into personnel labor, (b) cost-shared/donated by BST, or (c) underestimated.
   - **Partner Cost Share**: No indication whether Pepperwood Preserve or Weather Stream are contributing in-kind labor or resources; budget appears to be all-to-BST.
   - **No Contingency**: Budget appears tight with no explicit contingency line (common in NASA grants but worth noting).

6. **Current & Pending Support Conflict Check** (from Proposals document)
   - Jack Elston (PI/PD) has 45% combined effort commitment across active projects (AREN 5%, NOAA Hurricane 15%, NASA UAS 10%, DOI Volcanic 15%) plus 15% on pending DoD-AFWERX project.

     - Proposal was still in preparation when that document was finalized (2024-03-07 to 2024-03-08).
