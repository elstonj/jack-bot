### Budget Analysis (Conditional on Award)

**If awarded at full proposed budget ($518,293.20 over 3 years = ~$172,764/year):**

#### Strengths:
1. **Detailed cost accounting**: Both Pepperwood and Weather Stream budgets show line-item granularity (personnel hours × rates, equipment specificity, travel itemization).
2. **Reasonable fringe/indirect rates**: 40% staff benefits and 20% indirect costs align with typical NASA rates and partner institutional practices.
3. **Personnel effort realistic**: Staffing allocations (e.g., 8.25 weeks/year for Research Scientist, 7 weeks/year for Field Technician) appear proportionate to scope (9 field campaigns/year, 192 UAS flights/year).
4. **Multi-year extended budget**: Weather Stream budget explicitly extends to 3-year period with consistent annual escalation model.

#### Concerns & Risks:

1. **Missing Black Swift Prime Budget Detail**:
   - Aggregate budget is $518,293.20, but provided docs only detail Pepperwood ($198,426) + Weather Stream ($239,900.40) = $438,326.40.
   - **Gap of ~$79,967** (15% of total) is unaccounted for — presumed to be BST prime contractor costs (UAS development, integration, project management), but no budget documentation provided.
   - **Recommendation**: Obtain Black Swift Technologies' detailed budget breakdown to close this gap and assess prime contractor cost allocation.

2. **Field Operations Scalability Risk**:
   - Pepperwood budget assumes **9 field campaigns/year** (yielding 1,850 personnel hours across Ryan Ferrell + Makayla Freed).
   - Field campaign success depends on weather, wildfire activity, and facility access (Pepperwood Preserve).
   - Lodging budget ($3,500/year for 70 nights) is fixed; if campaigns are compressed or extended, cost overruns likely.

3. **External Dependencies with No Cost Contingency**:
   - **NISAR satellite operational readiness** (planned October 2025) — no contingency if launch/commissioning delayed.
   - **UAVSAR campaign availability** — limited number of flights available NASA-wide; no cost buffer if weather/coordination issues arise.
   - **Small UAS flight hours**: 192 flights/year is substantial; no documented reserve for weather delays or maintenance.

4. **Data Processing & Software Development Risk**:
   - Personnel costs assume IDL and Python code will be developed within allocated effort (Ryan Ferrell: 1,000 hrs/yr, Weather Stream support: ~185 hrs/yr data processing).
   - No line-item budget for software tools, cloud computing resources, or data storage/transfer.
   - If processing demands exceed estimates, labor cost overrun likely.

5. **Award Uncertainty**:
   - Proposal archived in "Attic" folder suggests it may not have been submitted or was abandoned.
   - No award notification, contract, or fund disbursement authority documented.
   - **Current financial exposure**: $0 (proposal stage); **risk if awarded**: Full $518,293.20 commitment over 3 years.
