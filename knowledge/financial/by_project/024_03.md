**Strengths:**
1. **Conservative Budget Framing**: Personnel costs represent 43% of total budget ($222,565 / $518,293), with remainder allocated to indirect costs (9%), operating expenses (1.2%), and travel (0.6%). This is typical for NASA ROSES grants.
2. **40% Fringe Benefit Rate**: Assumes standard institutional overhead, providing cushion for benefits/payroll taxes.
3. **Modest Operating Footprint**: Field supplies ($1,955) and equipment ($6,455 total) are tightly scoped to specific deliverables (9 field campaigns, remote sensing data processing).
4. **Multi-Partner Distribution**: Risk is distributed across Pepperwood (field/administrative) and Weather Stream (technical/flight operations), reducing concentration.

**Areas of Attention (Pre-Performance):**

1. **Personnel Effort Concentration — Ryan Ferrell (Research Scientist)**
   - **Proposed**: 1,000 hrs/year = 52 weeks × 19.2 hrs/week (51% FTE)
   - **Concern**: High allocation for a single researcher; risk if Ferrell becomes unavailable or needs to scale back effort.
   - **Mitigation**: Makayla Freed (Field Technician) is co-allocated at 850 hrs/year; roles are interdependent.

2. **Travel Budget Constraints — Weather Stream**

3. **No Equipment Depreciation or Replacement Budget**
   - **Observation**: Bench scale ($1,000) and pruners ($200) are one-time purchases in operating budget with no replacement line items for wear/tear over 3-year period.
   - **Risk**: If equipment fails, budget flexibility is limited.
