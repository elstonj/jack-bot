# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Title:** "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"

**Client/Funding Agency:** NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)

**Contract Type:** NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)

**Period of Performance:** April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)

**Key Personnel:**
- PI/PD #1: Jack Elston
- Co-I: Maciej Stachura
- Pepperwood Lead: Margaret Boeger (President & CEO, Black Swift Technologies)

**Project Scope:** Development and deployment of small unmanned aerial systems (UAS) to generate high-resolution soil moisture and live fuel moisture maps for wildfire susceptibility assessment. Integration of UAS data with NISAR satellite and UAVSAR platforms. Field validation campaigns at Pepperwood Preserve. Data processing codes in IDL and Python. Public data archival and open science delivery.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Data Source:** Open Science and Data Management Plan (OSDMP) — NASA Proposal Section (Created 2024-03-07, Modified 2024-03-29)

### Project Scope & Technical Deliverables

#### Small UAS Campaign Operations
- **Planned Flights:** 192 flights per year over 3-year period
- **Data Output:** 2-meter resolution soil moisture maps
- **Annual Data Volume:** ~10 GB of image data per year (HDF format)
- **Field Validation:** 9 field campaigns per year at Pepperwood Preserve (led by Ryan Ferrell & Makayla Freed)

#### Satellite Data Integration
- **NISAR:** 200m resolution soil moisture maps at 6-day intervals beginning October 2025
  - Annual data volume: ~6 GB (HDF format)
- **UAVSAR:** 6m resolution soil moisture maps
  - Planned missions: 9 flights in Year 1

#### Software & Code Deliverables
- **Data Processing Codes:** Developed in IDL and Python
- **Live Fuel Moisture (LFM) Products:** Generated from UAS and satellite data
- **Open Science Release:** All operational codes to be released publicly upon project completion (April 1, 2028)

#### Data Archival & Public Release
- **Timeline:** All datasets to be archived and shared publicly upon validation
