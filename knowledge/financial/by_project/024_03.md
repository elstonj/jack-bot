# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA ROSES 2024 (A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Brief Description**: Research project integrating small UAS platform data with NISAR satellite and UAVSAR to generate soil moisture and live fuel moisture maps for wildfire susceptibility assessment. Project involves field validation campaigns at Pepperwood Reserve and Weather Stream partnership for airborne data collection.
