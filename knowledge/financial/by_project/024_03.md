# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Project Scope**: Multi-partner research project integrating small UAS for soil moisture mapping, live fuel moisture field validation campaigns, and satellite data (NISAR, UAVSAR) to predict wildfire susceptibility. Key partners: Pepperwood Reserve (field validation), Weather Stream (UAS flight campaign support).
