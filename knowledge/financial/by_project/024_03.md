# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, Topic A.59)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Brief Description**: Multi-partner research project integrating small UAS, NISAR satellite, and UAVSAR data to generate soil moisture and live fuel moisture products for wildfire susceptibility mapping. ### Key Observations

#### Budget Approval Status
- **Proposed Total**: $518,293.20 (aggregate, 3-year)

- **Proposal Status**: Archived in "Attic" folder, suggesting it was not submitted or was abandoned after development (last edit 2024-03-25)

#### Budget Allocation Concerns
1. **Indirect Cost Rate**: Both Pepperwood (20%) and Weather Stream (20%) use the maximum allowed rate under NASA ROSES. This is standard but represents $46,398.80 in overhead (8.95% of total project budget).

2. **Personnel Cost Dominance**: Direct labor (including 40% fringe benefits) represents **$222,565 / $518,293 = 42.9%** of total 3-year budget. This is reasonable for a research project but leaves limited flexibility for unexpected field or equipment costs.

3. **Fringe Benefit Rate**: Both partners apply 40% fringe benefits, which is a standard full-burden rate but should be verified against actual BST cost accounting standards.

4. **Field Campaign Contingency**: Pepperwood budgets only $4,500 for lodging/fuel and $1,955 for field supplies across 3 years and 9 planned field campaigns ($718 per campaign). This appears tight for 9 multi-day campaigns and may present cost overrun risk if:
   - Nightly lodging rates exceed $50/night
   - Fuel prices exceed $5/gallon
   - Equipment requires replacement or repair

5. **Travel Costs**: Weather Stream budgets $2,974/year ($8,922 total) for annual programmatic visits. This covers 2 personnel at GSA rates and appears reasonable.
