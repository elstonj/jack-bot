# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Title:** "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"

**Client/Agency:** NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)

**Contract Type:** NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)

**Period of Performance:** April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3-year extended period)

**Brief Description:** Multi-partner research initiative to develop on-demand wildfire susceptibility mapping using fuel moisture and vegetative index data. Project integrates small UAS platforms for high-resolution soil moisture mapping (2m resolution, 192 flights/year), NISAR satellite data (200m resolution), and UAVSAR for live fuel moisture assessment. Primary field validation campaigns conducted at Pepperwood Preserve in California with Weather Stream providing aerial data collection support and analysis.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source:** Asana project data, OSDMP, and Proposals/Reports

### Project Scope & Technical Deliverables

**Objective:** On-demand wildfire susceptibility mapping using fuel moisture and vegetative index data

**Data Collection & Processing:**

| Activity | Frequency | Output | Timing |
|----------|-----------|--------|--------|
| Small UAS flights (soil moisture mapping) | 192 flights/year | 2m resolution soil moisture maps; ~10GB HDF data annually | Ongoing, years 1–3 |
| UAVSAR flights (live fuel moisture) | 9 flights planned (Year 1) | 6m resolution LFM maps; HDF format data | Year 1 priority |
| NISAR satellite data ingestion | 6-day intervals | 200m resolution soil moisture maps; ~6GB HDF data annually | Starting October 2025 onward |
| Field validation campaigns (Pepperwood) | 9 campaigns per 3-year period (~3/year) | Live fuel moisture samples, species-level data, calibration datasets | Distributed across years 1–3 |

**Key Milestones (Inferred from Budget & Scope):**

1. **Year 1 (April 2025 – March 2026)**
   - Pepperwood field campaign infrastructure established
   - Small UAS operational baseline (192 flights planned)
   - UAVSAR data acquisition complete (9 flights)
   - NISAR data integration begins (October 2025)
   - Data processing codes (IDL/Python) development

2. **Year 2–3 (April 2026 – March 2028)**
   - Continued field validation campaigns
   - Refined soil moisture & LFM products
   - Data archival and public release (upon validation)
   - Operational code release at project completion
   - Technical reports and peer-reviewed publications
