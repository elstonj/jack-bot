# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA ROSES 2024 (A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Brief Description**: Multi-institutional research project integrating small UAS, satellite (NISAR), and airborne (UAVSAR) platforms to generate high-resolution soil moisture and live fuel moisture maps for wildfire susceptibility assessment. Primary partners: Pepperwood (field validation, 9 campaigns/year) and Weather Stream (UAS platform operations, 3 flights/year).
