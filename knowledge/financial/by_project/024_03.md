# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

- **Project Title**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024 A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Key Personnel**: Jack Elston (PI/PD #1), Maciej Stachura, Margaret Boeger (Pepperwood), Ryan Ferrell (Pepperwood CO-I)
- **Brief Scope**: Multi-institutional research project to develop on-demand wildfire susceptibility maps using fuel moisture and vegetative index data from small UAS platforms, NISAR satellite, and UAVSAR. Includes 9 field validation campaigns at Pepperwood preserve and 3 annual flight campaigns through Weather Stream partnership.
