# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Brief Description**: Multi-institutional research project combining small UAS platforms, satellite data (NISAR, UAVSAR), and ground-based field campaigns to map soil moisture and live fuel moisture for wildfire risk assessment and management. Partners include Pepperwood Preserve (field validation) and Weather Stream (UAS operations and data processing).
