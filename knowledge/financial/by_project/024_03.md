- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**From Proposals/Reports** (Drive documents):

### Data & Product Deliverables
1. **Soil Moisture Maps**: 2m resolution maps generated from small UAS platform (192 flights/year producing 10GB HDF data annually)
2. **NISAR Integration**: Large-scale 200m resolution soil moisture maps from satellite (6-day repeat, operational from October 2025)
3. **UAVSAR Data Products**: 6m resolution soil moisture maps (9 flights planned in Year 1)
4. **Live Fuel Moisture (LFM) Index Maps**: Derived from satellite and UAS soil moisture data
5. **Field Validation Campaigns**: 9 campaigns conducted by Pepperwood team (Ryan Ferrell and Makayla Freed)
6. **Operational Software**: IDL and Python codes for soil moisture and LFM product generation; to be released at project completion (publicly archived)
7. **Public Data Archive**: All validated data products to be archived and shared publicly (open science requirement per OSDMP)

- **October 2025**: NISAR satellite operational; integration of NISAR data into processing pipeline
- **Year 1 (Apr 2025 – Apr 2026)**: 9 UAVSAR flights; 192 small UAS flights; 9 field validation campaigns at Pepperwood
- **Year 2 & 3**: Sustained flight campaigns and field validation; data product refinement
- **End of Project (April 1, 2028)**: Software codes released; full data archive finalized

### Budget Sustainability Analysis (Assuming Award)

#### Strengths
1. **Conservative Personnel Allocation**: Key personnel (CEO, COO, Accountant, PI) are allocated only 1 week/year each, keeping overhead modest.
2. **Reasonable Fringe Rate**: 40% fringe benefit rate is standard for research institutions and appropriately applied.

4. **Realistic Travel Budget**: Weather Stream's annual $2,974 travel budget for 2 personnel visiting Pepperwood once per year is within GSA guidelines.
5. **Standard Indirect Rate**: 20% indirect cost rate is at or below typical NASA-negotiated rates for research institutions.

#### Risks & Concerns
1. **No QuickBooks Baseline Yet**: Cannot assess cost control or spending discipline until actual invoicing begins.
2. **UAS Operations Cost Not Detailed**: The budget allocates 192 flights/year for small UAS but does not separately line-item aircraft operating costs (fuel, maintenance, pilot labor beyond Ryan Ferrell's 1,000 hrs). If aircraft operations exceed Ferrell's budgeted hours, cost overrun risk is high.
3. **NISAR Dependency**: Project success and budget efficiency depend on NISAR satellite becoming operational by October 2025. Any delay could increase operational UAS flight burden and costs.
4. **Extended 3-Year Timeline**: Weather Stream's budget is spread over 3 years with static annual allocation ($79,966.80/yr). If inflation, travel costs, or personnel rates increase, budget may become constrained in Years 2–3.
5. **Subcontractor Risk (Implicit)**: No subcontract budget is separately identified for UAVSAR operations or external flight campaign support. If UAVSAR flights require paid airtime or contracted aircraft, this could overrun the $518K budget.
6. **No Contingency Reserve**: The budget does not show an explicit contingency or reserve for cost overruns.
