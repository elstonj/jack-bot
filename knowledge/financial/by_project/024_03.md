# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Brief Description**: Multi-institutional research project to develop wildfire susceptibility mapping using fuel moisture and vegetative indices. Combines small UAS soil moisture mapping with satellite data (NISAR, UAVSAR) and ground validation campaigns at Pepperwood Preserve. Partners include Black Swift Technologies, Pepperwood Preserve, and Weather Stream.
