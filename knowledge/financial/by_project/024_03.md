# PROJECT 024-03 — FINANCIAL SUMMARY

## Project Overview

**Project Title:** "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"

**Client/Agency:** NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)

**Contract Type:** NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)

**Period of Performance:** April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3-year extended period)

**Brief Description:** Multi-institutional research project integrating soil moisture and live fuel moisture mapping using small UAS platforms, NISAR satellite data, and UAVSAR to support wildfire susceptibility assessments. Involves field validation campaigns at Pepperwood Preserve and data processing through Weather Stream partners.
