# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Funding Agency**: NASA ROSES 2024 (A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Key Personnel**: Jack Elston (PI/PD #1), Maciej Stachura (Key Team Member)
- **Brief Scope**: Multi-institutional research project integrating small UAS, NISAR satellite, and UAVSAR to generate on-demand wildfire susceptibility maps from soil moisture and live fuel moisture data. Project includes 9 field validation campaigns annually at Pepperwood Preserve and 3 aircraft flights per year through Weather Stream partner.

### Key Budget Observations

#### Strengths
1. **Well-documented budget structure**: Both partners provide detailed line-item budgets with clear assumptions (hourly rates, effort levels, travel GSA compliance).
2. **Reasonable fringe rates**: 40% fringe on personnel is standard for academic/research institutions.
3. **Indirect cost transparency**: Both partners apply the same 20% indirect rate, consistent with NASA ROSES guidance.
4. **Field operations clearly resourced**: Significant allocation to direct labor (Research Scientist $55k/year, Field Technician $25.5k/year) supports 9 annual field campaigns.

#### Risks & Concerns
1. **No contingency budget documented**: The budget appears to be fixed-price with no explicit contingency or management reserve. If field operations exceed 70 nights lodging or 200 gallons fuel, no buffer is budgeted.
