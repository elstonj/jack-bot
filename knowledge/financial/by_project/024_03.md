# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Full Project Name:** "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"

**Client/Agency:** NASA (ROSES 2024, Opportunity A.59)

**Contract Type:** NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)

**Period of Performance:** April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Multi-partner research project to develop wildfire susceptibility mapping using soil moisture and live fuel moisture data from small UAS platforms, NISAR satellite, and UAVSAR. Includes 9 field validation campaigns annually at Pepperwood Preserve, data processing and synthesis, and dissemination of open-access soil moisture and fuel moisture products. Key partners include Pepperwood (field operations) and Weather Stream (UAS flight campaign support).
