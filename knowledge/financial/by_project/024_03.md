# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Funding Agency**: NASA ROSES 2024 (Announcement A.59)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Project Scope**: Development and deployment of small UAS-based soil moisture and live fuel moisture mapping systems for wildfire susceptibility assessment. Integration with NISAR and UAVSAR satellite data. Field validation campaigns at Pepperwood Preserve and other sites.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source**: *Open Science and Data Management Plan (OSDMP), NASA ROSES 2024 A.59*

### Primary Data Products

| Deliverable | Frequency | Technical Specifications | Financial Implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Small UAS Soil Moisture Maps** | 192 flights/year | 2m spatial resolution, HDF format, ~10GB/year | Primary output; labor-intensive (Ryan Ferrell: 1,000 hrs/yr) |
| **NISAR Soil Moisture Maps** | 6-day intervals (starting Oct 2025) | 200m resolution, 6GB/year HDF format | Satellite data integration (no direct cost to project) |
| **UAVSAR Soil Moisture Maps** | 9 flights in Year 1 | 6m resolution | Partnership-derived data; Weather Stream flight support |
| **Live Fuel Moisture Index Products** | Annual | Derived from soil moisture + vegetation data | Data synthesis labor (Ryan Ferrell effort) |
| **Processing Code Release** | End of project (April 2028) | IDL and Python software | Deferred; open science requirement |
| **Public Data Archive** | Upon validation | NASA DAAC repository | Data management overhead (minimal direct cost) |
