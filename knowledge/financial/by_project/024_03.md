# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)

Primary partners: Pepperwood Reserve (field validation), Weather Stream (aerial platform operations), Black Swift Technologies (lead/coordination).

   - **UAVSAR Coordination**: 9 flights in Year 1 require external coordination; budget allocation for logistics/scheduling is embedded in travel and personnel lines
