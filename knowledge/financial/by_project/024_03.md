# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Project Title**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, Opportunity A.59)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Key Personnel**: Jack Elston (PI), Maciej Stachura (Co-I), Margaret Boeger (Pepperwood partner), Ryan Ferrell (Research Scientist)

**Project Scope** (from Proposals/Reports):
Research project to develop on-demand wildfire susceptibility maps integrating live fuel moisture (LFM) data from field sampling and satellite imagery (NISAR, UAVSAR). Deliverables include 192 UAS flights/year generating 2m-resolution soil moisture maps, data processing codes (IDL/Python), and validation through 9 multi-week field campaigns at Pepperwood Preserve. All data and operational codes to be released as open science upon validation.
