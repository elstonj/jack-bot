# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Funding Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Project Code**: 024-03
- **Key Personnel**: Jack Elston (PI/PD #1), Maciej Stachura (Co-I), Ryan Ferrell (Pepperwood Research Scientist), Margaret Boeger (BST President & CEO)

**Project Scope**: The project integrates data from a small unmanned aerial system (UAS), NISAR satellite, and UAVSAR platforms to generate soil moisture and live fuel moisture (LFM) products for wildfire susceptibility mapping. Work is distributed across two primary partner organizations: Pepperwood Reserve (field validation, data synthesis) and Weather Stream (UAS platform operations and data processing).
