Pepperwood's $198,426 appears to be a fixed amount across the entire 3-year period. **Burn rate analysis requires clarity on whether Pepperwood budget is annual or aggregate.**

4. **Multi-Partner Coordination**: Pepperwood and Weather Stream budgets are separate. If additional partners are involved (per the $518k discrepancy), coordination and invoice tracking across multiple entities will be critical for financial management.

5. **Personnel Effort Commitments**: The "Current and Pending Support" document indicates that key PI Jack Elston and Maciej Stachura have concurrent commitments totaling $1,732,564 in active projects (45% combined effort) plus $1,249,724 in pending proposals. Project 024-03 effort requirements must be carefully monitored against these competing obligations to ensure adequate resource allocation.

6. **Data Management & Operations Costs**: The project involves 192 UAS flights per year and satellite data integration (NISAR, UAVSAR). Annual operational costs for the UAS platform are not broken out; weather-dependent scheduling and potential flight delays could impact timeline and cost.
