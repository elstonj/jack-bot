# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Title:** "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"

**Client/Agency:** NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)

**Contract Type:** NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)

**Period of Performance:** April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3-year extended period)

**Brief Description:** Multi-partner research initiative to develop wildfire susceptibility mapping capabilities using soil moisture and live fuel moisture data. Integration of small UAS (unmanned aerial systems) platforms with satellite data (NISAR, UAVSAR) to generate high-resolution soil moisture and vegetative index maps for fire management agencies. Work performed by Pepperwood (research partner) and Weather Stream (UAS data collection partner), with Black Swift Technologies providing technical and management oversight.
