# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Brief Description**: Multi-institution research project integrating small UAS, satellite (NISAR), and airborne (UAVSAR) data to generate soil moisture and live fuel moisture maps for wildfire susceptibility assessment. Primary partners are Pepperwood (field validation) and Weather Stream (airborne data collection).
