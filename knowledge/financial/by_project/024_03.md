# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA (National Aeronautics and Space Administration)
- **Funding Opportunity**: NASA ROSES 2024 (A.59)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Brief Description**: Research project integrating small UAS platforms, satellite data (NISAR, UAVSAR), and field validation campaigns to develop high-resolution soil moisture and live fuel moisture mapping capabilities for wildfire susceptibility assessment. Project involves two primary partner organizations: Pepperwood (field validation, data synthesis) and Weather Stream (UAS flight operations, data processing).

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Data Source**: Proposals & Reports documents (OSDMP, Facilities & Equipment sections)

### Project-Level Deliverables (3-Year Period)

| Deliverable | Timeline | Description | Financial Implications |
|-------------|----------|-------------|----------------------|
| **Small UAS Flight Campaign Data** | Ongoing (192 flights/year) | High-resolution (2m) soil moisture maps; 10GB annual data output in HDF format | Weather Stream flight operations & processing (primary cost driver) |
| **NISAR Satellite Data Integration** | October 2025 onward | 200m resolution soil moisture maps; 6-day repeat interval; 6GB annual data output | Data processing costs (included in Weather Stream personnel) |
| **UAVSAR Data Acquisition & Processing** | Year 1 (9 flights planned) | 6m resolution soil moisture maps; 9 missions in Year 1 | Ancillary data processing |
| **Field Validation Campaigns** | 9 campaigns over 3 years | Live fuel moisture (LFM) ground truth collection; validation of UAS and satellite data | Pepperwood field operations (primary cost driver: Ryan Ferrell, Makayla Freed effort) |
| **Soil Moisture & Live Fuel Moisture Products** | Ongoing throughout project | IDL and Python processing codes generating operational soil moisture and LFM products | Personnel effort (processing scientists) |
| **Public Data Archive & Release** | Year 3 completion | All validated data archived and released publicly; operational codes released | Data management & archival (included in personnel/indirect costs) |
| **NASA Reporting & Compliance** | Quarterly/Annual | Progress reports, milestone submissions, compliance with NASA ROSES requirements | Administrative overhead (included in CEO/COO/Accountant effort budgets) |

### Risk Factors Affecting Budget & Schedule
1. **NISAR Launch Delay**: NISAR operational availability delayed to October 2025; project must proceed with alternative satellite data sources until then (absorbed in current budget via UAVSAR flights)
2. **Field Campaign Weather Dependency**: 9 field validation campaigns dependent on field conditions; potential for rescheduling; labor costs remain fixed regardless of campaign success
3. **Data Processing Bottlenecks**: UAS flights generate 10GB data annually; processing timelines critical to product delivery
