# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Funding Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024 A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Project Code**: 024-03

**Scope Description** *(from Proposals/Reports)*:
Research project employing small UAS platforms to generate high-resolution soil moisture and live fuel moisture maps for wildfire susceptibility assessment. Integrates data from UAS (2m resolution, 192 flights/year), NISAR satellite (200m resolution, 6-day intervals beginning Oct 2025), and UAVSAR (6m resolution, 9 flights planned Year 1). Data processing via IDL and Python codes for operational soil moisture and live fuel moisture products. Field validation campaigns conducted at Pepperwood Preserve with stakeholder coordination for fire management agencies.
