# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)

Partners include Pepperwood (field validation, data synthesis) and Weather Stream (flight campaign support, data processing).

### Risk & Health Indicators

#### ✓ **Positive Indicators**
1. **Proposed budget matches contracted award** ($518,293.20) — no mismatch between proposed and approved funding.
2. **Realistic personnel rates** — $30–$150/hr range is consistent with academic/nonprofit research institutions.
3. **Indirect cost rate approved at 20%** — within NASA caps; no overrun risk there.
4. **Clear cost allocation by partner** — Pepperwood and Weather Stream budgets are well-documented with line-item detail.
5. **Extended performance period** — 3-year timeline is reasonable for the scope (192 UAS flights/year, satellite integration, data archiving).

#### ⚠ **Potential Concerns**
1. **No QuickBooks actuals available yet** — Cannot verify whether actual staffing hours will match budgeted effort. Recommend baseline tracking from April 1, 2025 onward.
2. **Concurrent commitments** — Per "Current & Pending Support" doc, PI (Jack Elston) and key team member (Maciej Stachura) have 45% effort committed to other active projects ($1.73M). Risk: insufficient capacity if this project demands higher involvement than allocated in budget.
3. **UAS flight schedule dependency** — Budget assumes 192 flights/year (Pepperwood). Weather delays, equipment failures, or facility closures could compress timelines and spike costs (overtime, expedited repairs).
4. **Travel cost inflation** — Weather Stream annual travel budget is fixed at $2,974. If GSA rates increase or number of programmatic visits expands, this line could overrun.
5. **Data archiving & processing** — OSDMP describes 10GB/year of UAS imagery + NISAR/UAVSAR data. Storage, processing, and validation costs may be underestimated in indirect costs (currently 20% of direct).
6. **Third-party dependency** — NISAR becomes operational Oct 2025; UAVSAR flights must be coordinated externally. Delays could impact project schedule and cost.
7. **Personnel turnover risk** — Research scientist (Ryan Ferrell) and field technician (Makayla Freed) carry heavy workload (1,000 hrs and 850 hrs/yr). If staff depart, backfill costs could exceed budget.

#### **Cost Category Variance Analysis** (vs. industry norms)
- **Direct Labor**: $158,975/yr is reasonable for 2.5–3 FTE across technical and support roles.
- **Fringe (40%)**: Within typical grant allowances; no overage risk if personnel rates remain stable.
- **Operations/Travel**: $7,474/yr is modest ($4.5K Pepperwood facilities + $2.974K Weather Stream travel); low risk of overrun unless additional partners added.
- **Field Supplies**: $1,955 (Pepperwood) is minimal; one-time equipment purchases (scale, pruners) already expensed; low risk.
- **Indirect**: 20% is reasonable and NASA-compliant.

#### **Burn Rate & Remaining Runway**
- **Year 1 (Apr 2025 – Mar 2026)**: Est. $278,392.80 / $518,293.20 = **53.7% of budget**
- **Year 2 & 3 (Apr 2026 – Mar 2028)**: Est. $239,900.40 remaining / 2 years = **$119,950.20/yr average**
- **No cost overrun risk** if actuals track to budget; project ends exactly when funds are exhausted.
- **Contingency**: None apparent; budget is fully allocated. Any scope creep (additional flights, personnel, data storage) must be negotiated with NASA.
