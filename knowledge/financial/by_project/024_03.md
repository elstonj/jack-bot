## Project Overview
- **Project Title**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Funding Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)

   - **Assessment**: Budget structure is reasonable for a research project with 9 field campaigns/year and multi-partner coordination.

#### 2. **Partner Staffing & Effort Distribution**
   - **Pepperwood Preserve** ($198,426): 
     - Primary effort: Research Scientist (Ryan Ferrell, 1,000 hrs/yr @ $55/hr = $55k) + Field Technician (Makayla Freed, 850 hrs/yr @ $30/hr = $25.5k)
     - Total field/research labor = $80.5k/year; with overhead = $158.9k/year
     - Effort allocation: ~8.25 weeks/year (Ferrell), ~7 weeks/year (Freed)

     - Primary effort: Flight campaign support (185 hrs/yr @ $95/hr = $17.6k) + Project oversight + firmware support
     - Effort allocation: ~3–4.5 weeks/year
   - **Assessment**: Staffing levels align with proposed 3 flights/year and 9 field validation campaigns/year.

#### 3. **Cost Categories at Risk or Concerns**
   - **Field Operations (Pepperwood)**: 70 nights lodging + 200 gal fuel budgeted; vulnerable to:

     - Remote site access complications

   - **Personnel Rate Sustainability**: 40% fringe benefit rate is standard for non-profit/research organizations; should be monitored for cost escalation in Year 2–3.
