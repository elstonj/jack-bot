# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Title:** "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"

**Client/Agency:** NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)

**Contract Type:** NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)

**Period of Performance:** April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)

**Brief Description:** Research project integrating small UAS soil moisture mapping, NISAR satellite data, and UAVSAR to generate high-resolution fuel moisture and vegetative index products for wildfire susceptibility assessment. Multi-partner effort involving Black Swift Technologies (BST), Pepperwood Reserve (field validation), and Weather Stream (aerial platform operations).
