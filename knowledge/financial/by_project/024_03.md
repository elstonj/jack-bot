# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Funding Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Key Personnel**: Jack Elston (PI/PD #1), Maciej Stachura (Co-I), Margaret Boeger (Pepperwood Partner – President & CEO), Ryan Ferrell (Pepperwood Co-I – Research Scientist)

**Scope Summary**: Research project to develop on-demand wildfire susceptibility mapping by integrating fuel moisture and vegetative index data from a small UAS platform, NISAR satellite, and UAVSAR. Field validation campaigns conducted at Pepperwood Preserve; data processing and product development led by Weather Stream partner. All data to be archived and released publicly; operational codes released at project completion.
