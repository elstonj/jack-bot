# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Funding Agency**: NASA ROSES 2024 (A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Scope**: Research project integrating small UAS platforms with satellite data (NISAR, UAVSAR) to generate soil moisture and live fuel moisture maps for wildfire susceptibility assessment. Includes field validation campaigns at Pepperwood Preserve and weather station support from Weather Stream.

- **Small UAS Flights**: 192 flights per year generating 2m resolution soil moisture maps (10GB HDF data annually)
