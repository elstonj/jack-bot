## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Source**: Proposals & Reports (Open Science and Data Management Plan, OSDMP)

### Technical Deliverables (Project Scope):
1. **Small UAS Soil Moisture Mapping**: 192 flights per year generating 2m-resolution soil moisture maps (HDF format, ~10GB annual data)
2. **NISAR Satellite Integration**: Integration of 200m-resolution soil moisture data beginning October 2025 (6-day repeat interval)
3. **UAVSAR Data Processing**: Integration of 6m-resolution UAS SAR data (9 flights planned Year 1)
4. **Data Processing Codes**: IDL and Python software for generating soil moisture and live fuel moisture products
5. **Field Validation Campaigns**: 9 field campaigns per year led by Pepperwood (Research Scientist Ryan Ferrell, 1,000 hrs/year; Field Technician Makayla Freed, 850 hrs/year)
6. **Live Fuel Moisture (LFM) Products**: Derived from soil moisture and vegetative index maps

### Data & Open Science Deliverables:
- Public archive of all validated soil moisture and LFM products (HDF format)
- Release of operational software code upon project completion
- Coordination with NASA Earth data repositories

### Outreach & Engagement:
- Communications Coordinator (Stephanie Antinoro, 120 hrs total) tasked with outreach to fire management agencies

**Financial Implications**: All deliverables are embedded in the approved budget. No additional costs anticipated beyond those budgeted for personnel, field operations, travel, and equipment.
