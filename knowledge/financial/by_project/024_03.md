# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Title:** "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"

**Client/Agency:** NASA ROSES 2024 (A.59 funding opportunity)

**Contract Type:** NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)

**Period of Performance:** April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3-year extended period)

**Brief Description:** Multi-partner NASA-funded research project combining field validation campaigns at Pepperwood Preserve with Weather Stream small UAS data collection to develop soil moisture and live fuel moisture mapping capabilities for wildfire susceptibility assessment. The project integrates data from small UAS platforms (192 flights/year), NISAR satellite (200m resolution), and UAVSAR (6m resolution).

**Key Personnel:** 
- PI/PD #1: Jack Elston
- Co-I: Maciej Stachura
- Pepperwood Partner Lead: Margaret Boeger (President & CEO, Black Swift Technologies)
