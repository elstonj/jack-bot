# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Project Title**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, Topic A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Brief Description**: Multi-partner research project to develop soil moisture and live fuel moisture mapping capabilities using small UAS platforms, NISAR satellite data, and UAVSAR. Project integrates high-resolution remote sensing with field validation campaigns at Pepperwood Reserve to support wildfire susceptibility assessment and fuel management decision-making.
