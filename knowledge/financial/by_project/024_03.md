# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

- **Full Project Name:** "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency:** NASA (National Aeronautics and Space Administration)
- **Funding Opportunity:** ROSES 2024, Topic A.59
- **Contract Type:** NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance:** April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Status:** Proposal completed; archived in "Attic" folder — appears not submitted or abandoned after initial development
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Multi-partner research project integrating small UAS (unmanned aerial systems), NISAR satellite, and UAVSAR data to generate soil moisture and live fuel moisture maps for wildfire susceptibility assessment. Project includes 9 field validation campaigns at Pepperwood Reserve, 192 UAS flights per year, and production of open-science data products and processing codes (IDL and Python).

- **Black Swift Technologies:** Jack Elston (Founder & Owner), Maciej Stachura (project lead for OSDMP)
- **Pepperwood Reserve:** Ryan Ferrell (CO-I, Research Scientist), Makayla Freed (Field Technician)
- **Weather Stream:** Project Lead, Project & Firmware Support, Flight Campaign & Data Processing Support
- **Partners:** Orbital Micro Systems (Eryan Dai), JPL/Caltech (Seungbum Kim), USGS (Michelle Stern)
