# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Scope Description**: Research project integrating small UAS soil moisture mapping, satellite data (NISAR, UAVSAR), and live fuel moisture products for wildfire susceptibility assessment. Multi-partner effort with Pepperwood (field research, fuel moisture validation) and Weather Stream (UAS flight campaign support). Project includes 9 field validation campaigns annually and 192 small UAS flights per year generating high-resolution soil moisture and vegetative index maps.
