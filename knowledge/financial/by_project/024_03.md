# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Title:** "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"

**Client/Agency:** NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)

**Contract Type:** NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)

**Period of Performance:** April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)

**Project Description:** A multi-partner NASA-funded research initiative to develop soil moisture and live fuel moisture mapping capabilities using small UAS platforms, satellite data (NISAR, UAVSAR), and field validation campaigns. The project aims to produce on-demand wildfire susceptibility assessments for fire management agencies. Primary partners include Pepperwood Reserve (field validation, personnel) and Weather Stream (aerial data collection, 3 flights/year).

- Jack Elston (PI/PD #1)
- Maciej Stachura (Co-Investigator)
- Margaret Boeger (President & CEO, Pepperwood partner oversight)
- Ryan Ferrell (Senior Scientist, field validation lead)
