# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, Topic A.59)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Brief Description**: Multi-partner research project integrating small UAS, NISAR satellite, and UAVSAR data to generate high-resolution soil moisture and live fuel moisture maps for wildfire susceptibility assessment. Combines field validation campaigns at Pepperwood Reserve with remote sensing data processing and algorithm development.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

### Technical Deliverables (from OSDMP & Proposal)
1. **Small UAS Data Products**: 192 flights per year generating 2m resolution soil moisture maps (10GB annual HDF data)
2. **NISAR Integration**: Data ingest from NASA-ISRO satellite (operational October 2025 onward) at 200m resolution, 6-day repeat cycle
3. **UAVSAR Data**: 9 airborne SAR flights planned in Year 1, producing 6m resolution soil moisture maps
4. **Algorithm Development**: IDL and Python processing codes for soil moisture and live fuel moisture retrieval
5. **Field Validation**: 9 field campaigns per year at Pepperwood Reserve (coordinate with 70 nights lodging, ~8-9 weeks direct labor for Ryan Ferrell and Makayla Freed)
6. **Public Data Archive & Open Science**: All validated data and operational codes to be released upon project completion
7. **Agency Outreach**: Coordination with fire management agencies (Stephanie Antinoro, Communications Coordinator)

### Financial Implications
- Field campaign scheduling directly tied to Pepperwood operating budget (lodging, fuel, field supplies)
