# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Funding Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Project Partners**: Black Swift Technologies (BST), Pepperwood Reserve, Weather Stream
- **Brief Description**: Research project integrating small UAS platforms, satellite data (NISAR, UAVSAR), and ground validation campaigns to generate high-resolution soil moisture and live fuel moisture maps for wildfire susceptibility assessment. Includes 9 field validation campaigns annually at Pepperwood Reserve and 3 annual airborne survey flights.

- **Field Campaign Coordination**: Success depends on sustained access to Pepperwood Reserve and logistics (lodging, vehicle fuel, equipment). Budget allows 70 nights lodging and 200 gallons fuel annually.
