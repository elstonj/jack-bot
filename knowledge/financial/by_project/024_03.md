### Key Observations & Risk Assessment

**Strengths:**
1. **Full funding secured** — $518,293.20 approved for 3-year project; no budget gaps identified
2. **Reasonable indirect cost rate** — Both partners capped at 20%, below typical university and commercial rates (25–55%)
3. **Personnel effort is realistic** — Effort allocations (1–8 weeks/year for leadership; higher for research scientists and technicians) align with grant scale
4. **Diversified funding base** — Two partner organizations (Pepperwood, Weather Stream) share costs and risk

**Areas to Monitor:**

1. **Field Campaign Cost Containment** — Pepperwood's largest expense category is direct labor for 9 field validation campaigns. 
   - Ryan Ferrell and Makayla Freed are budgeted for 1,850 combined hours over 3 years (~617 hrs/year)
   - **Risk**: Field work is inherently variable; weather delays, equipment failures, or expanded sampling scope could drive overages
   - **Mitigation**: Detailed campaign schedules and contingency protocols should be established in project kickoff

2. **Travel Cost Escalation** — Weather Stream's annual travel budget of $2,974 assumes fixed GSA rates and airfare.
   - **Risk**: Fuel prices, lodging rates, or frequency of site visits could increase costs
   - **Mitigation**: Monitor quarterly; consider virtual meetings to reduce travel frequency if possible

3. **Indirect Cost Allocation** — Both partners apply 20% indirects to direct costs (personnel + operating expenses).
   - Pepperwood indirects: $33,071 (applied to $164,355 direct base)
   - Weather Stream indirects: $13,327.80/year (applied to $66,639 direct base)
   - **Risk**: If actual indirect costs (utilities, IT, general administration) exceed forecasts, budget overages are possible
   - **Mitigation**: Maintain detailed indirect cost tracking; establish mid-year reviews

4. **Data Processing & Software Development** — Weather Stream's role focuses on flight campaign support and data processing. No explicit budget line for software development tools, cloud storage, or computational infrastructure.
   - **Risk**: Hidden costs for IDL/Python development environments, HDF data storage, or cloud compute may emerge
   - **Mitigation**: Clarify software/IT resource assumptions during project setup

5. **NISAR & UAVSAR Integration** — Project depends on external satellite and airborne platforms (NISAR operational Oct 2025, UAVSAR 9 flights planned).
   - **Risk**: Schedule delays or data access issues with federal platforms could impact project timeline and require budget reallocation
   - **Mitigation**: Establish MOUs and data-sharing agreements with NASA centers; build schedule buffers
