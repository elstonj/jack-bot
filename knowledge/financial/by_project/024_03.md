# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Scope**: Research project integrating small UAS-based soil moisture mapping, satellite data (NISAR, UAVSAR), and field validation campaigns at Pepperwood Preserve to develop wildfire susceptibility tools for fire management agencies. Project involves two primary partner organizations: Pepperwood (field campaigns, data synthesis) and Weather Stream (aerial data processing, flight operations).

### Pre-Award Observations (Budget Documents):

#### Strengths:
1. **Conservative indirect cost rate (20%)** — Well within NASA guidelines; leaves room if actual indirect rate is lower.
2. **Well-documented personnel effort** — Clear allocation of FTE across 6 Pepperwood staff + 3 Weather Stream staff; reasonable annual commitments.
3. **Field operations cost structure is realistic** — Lodging, fuel, and equipment costs are itemized and justified (Pepperwood field campaigns, remote preserve access).
4. **Multi-year stability** — Weather Stream budget is scaled uniformly across 3 years ($79,966.80/year), reducing burn-rate surprises.
5. **Fringe benefits clearly stated (40%)** — Appropriate for institutional research environment.

#### Potential Risks / Areas to Monitor:
1. **Personnel effort concentration** — Ryan Ferrell (Research Scientist) carries 1,000 hrs/year (8.25 weeks), the highest single allocation. If he becomes unavailable, critical path impact.
2. **Travel budget modest** — Weather Stream allocated only $2,974/year for annual Pepperwood visit. Additional travel for conferences or stakeholder meetings not budgeted.
3. **Equipment & supplies relatively low ($6,455 total)** — $1,000 bench scale is largest single item. If field equipment requires replacement mid-project, no contingency available.
4. **No contingency line** — Standard practice is 10% project contingency; this budget appears to have none.
5. **No budget for data archival/repository fees** — Open Science Data Management Plan promises public data release (HDF format, 10GB/year). No budget for data repository hosting/curation costs is visible.
6. **Personnel rate locks** — Hourly rates are fixed ($55/hr for Ryan Ferrell, $30/hr for Makayla Freed). If project extends beyond 3 years or rates increase, overrun risk.

#### Budget Execution Readiness (Expected April 1, 2025):
- **Personnel invoicing**: Likely monthly or bi-weekly timesheets. Monitor Pepperwood field technician hours against 850 hrs/year plan (164 hrs/quarter).
- **Operating expenses**: Lodging and fuel on quarterly/annual basis. Track actual nightly rates vs. $50/night assumption.
- **Indirect cost allocation**: Applied as % of personnel + operating base. Verify 20% calculation monthly.
- **Multi-partner coordination**: Ensure invoicing from both Pepperwood and Weather Stream is synchronized to NASA payment schedule.
