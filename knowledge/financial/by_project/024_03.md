# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Brief Description**: Multi-partner research project integrating small UAS soil moisture mapping, NISAR satellite data, and UAVSAR to generate wildfire susceptibility products. Led by Black Swift Technologies in partnership with Pepperwood and Weather Stream.

## Financial Health Assessment

### Overall Budget Status
- **Status**: Pre-award/pre-launch phase
- **Current Financial Position**: Project has not yet begun. Approved budget of $518,293.20 is fully available. No invoicing, payments, or expense accumulation has occurred.
- **Budget Adequacy**: Budget documents indicate detailed cost estimation by partner. All personnel, operating expenses, and indirect costs have been explicitly budgeted and approved by NASA.

### Budget Allocation Confidence
- **Personnel**: Well-defined with hour allocations and fully-loaded rates (including 40% fringe benefits)
- **Operating Expenses**: Detailed line-item estimates for field supplies, equipment, lodging, and fuel
- **Indirect Costs**: Fixed at 20% (NASA maximum allowable), applied consistently across both partners
- **Data Source Quality**: Budget documents are complete, recent (March 2024), and tied to the NASA ROSES proposal submission

### Risk Factors & Considerations

1. **Project Launch Delay Risk**: If the April 1, 2025 start date slips, year-end budget utilization targets may be affected. Recommend establishing a project kickoff contingency.

2. **Personnel Availability**: Budget assumes specific effort commitments:
   - Pepperwood Research Scientist (Ryan Ferrell): 8.25 weeks/year
   - Field Technician (Makayla Freed): 7 weeks/year
   - Weather Stream Project Lead & support: 3–4.5 weeks/year each
   - **Recommendation**: Confirm personnel are available and not over-allocated given high concurrent effort loads (Jack Elston and Maciej Stachura have 45% combined effort on active projects; see Current and Pending Support document)

3. **Field Operations Variability**: Field campaign costs are tied to Pepperwood preserve access (lodging, fuel, equipment). Weather, access restrictions, or seasonal factors could impact actual vs. budgeted spending on the 9 planned campaigns.

4. **Indirect Cost Rate**: Both partners are budgeted at 20% indirect cost rate. If actual indirect costs exceed or fall short of this rate, adjustments may be needed. Monitor quarterly actuals against this allocation.

5. **Multi-Partner Coordination**: Project spans Pepperwood, Weather Stream, and Black Swift Technologies. Recommend establishing clear invoicing and cost-tracking procedures to ensure each partner's budget is monitored independently.

6. **Data Management Infrastructure**: The OSDMP commits to archiving and releasing substantial data volumes (10GB/year from UAS alone, plus NISAR and UAVSAR products). Ensure IT infrastructure and cloud storage costs are captured under indirect costs or as part of BST management budget allocation.
