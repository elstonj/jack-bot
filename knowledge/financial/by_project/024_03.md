# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Key Personnel**: Jack Elston (PI), Maciej Stachura (Co-I)
- **Scope**: Development and deployment of small UAS platforms to generate high-resolution soil moisture and live fuel moisture maps for wildfire susceptibility assessment, integrating data from UAS, NISAR satellite, and UAVSAR. Field validation campaigns at Pepperwood Preserve (9 campaigns over 3 years). Data processing and open science protocols for public data release.
