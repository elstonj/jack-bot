# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Title:** "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"

**Client/Agency:** NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)

**Contract Type:** NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)

**Period of Performance:** April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3-year award period)

**Brief Scope:** Multi-institutional research project combining small UAS remote sensing, satellite data (NISAR, UAVSAR), and ground-based field validation to develop high-resolution soil moisture and live fuel moisture mapping products for wildfire susceptibility assessment. Project involves field campaigns at Pepperwood Preserve, UAS flight operations, and data processing/validation across three partner organizations.
