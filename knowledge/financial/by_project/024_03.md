# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Brief Description of Scope**: Multi-partner research project integrating small UAS, satellite (NISAR), and airborne (UAVSAR) remote sensing to map soil moisture and live fuel moisture for wildfire susceptibility assessment. Includes field validation campaigns at Pepperwood Preserve and data synthesis across three partner organizations.
