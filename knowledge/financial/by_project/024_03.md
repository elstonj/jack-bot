## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Sourced from NASA ROSES 2024 A.59 Proposal and OSDMP:**

### Research Products & Outputs
1. **Soil Moisture Maps (Small UAS)**: 2m spatial resolution, generated from 192 flights/year
   - Format: HDF, ~10GB annual data volume
   - Operational throughout 3-year period

2. **Live Fuel Moisture (LFM) Products**: Derived from UAS, NISAR, and UAVSAR data
   - Field validation campaigns: 9 campaigns over project life (Pepperwood Reserve)
   - Data synthesis and algorithm refinement

3. **NISAR-Based Soil Moisture Maps**: 200m resolution, 6-day repeat
   - Operational from October 2025 onward
   - ~6GB annual data volume, HDF format

4. **UAVSAR Products**: 6m resolution soil moisture maps
   - 9 flights planned in Year 1 (estimated continuation in Years 2–3)

5. **Open Science Data Archive**: All validated data to be released publicly via NASA archive
   - Timeline: Data released upon validation (ongoing throughout project)

6. **Software Release**: IDL and Python codes for soil moisture and LFM product generation
   - Timeline: Operational codes released at project completion (April 2028)

7. **Scientific Publications**: Peer-reviewed research outputs on methodology and findings
   - Timeline: Distributed throughout 3-year period and post-completion

### Field Campaigns (Pepperwood Reserve)
- **9 field validation campaigns** over 3 years (3 per year)

- **Cost**: ~$2,974 per year (airfare, lodging, meals)
