## Project Overview
- **Project Title**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024 A.59 funding opportunity)

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**From Proposal/Budget Documents (Proposed Scope):**

### **Technical Deliverables**
Based on the Open Science and Data Management Plan (OSDMP):

1. **Small UAS Operations**
   - 192 flights per year generating 2m resolution soil moisture maps
   - 10GB of annual image data in HDF format
   - Full 3-year program: ~576 planned flights

2. **NISAR Satellite Data Integration**
   - 200m resolution soil moisture maps at 6-day intervals
   - Operational from October 2025 onward
   - 6GB of annual image data in HDF format

3. **UAVSAR Campaign Support**
   - 9 planned flights in Year 1
   - 6m resolution soil moisture maps

4. **Data Processing & Code Development**
   - Soil moisture retrieval algorithms (IDL and Python)
   - Live fuel moisture mapping codes
   - Open science archival and release upon validation

5. **Field Validation Campaigns**
   - 9 field campaigns per year at Pepperwood Reserve
   - Field technician and research scientist teams
   - Live fuel moisture (LFM) sampling and ground-truth data collection

### **Project Management Milestones**
- **Start Date**: April 1, 2025
- **End Date**: April 1, 2028
- **Annual programmatic reviews**: Weather Stream site visits to Pepperwood (budgeted travel)
- **Data release**: Upon completion and public archive (NASA ROSES requirement)

**Financial Implications**:
- Field campaign costs (Pepperwood): ~$2,150/campaign ($4,500 lodging + fuel ÷ 3 years ÷ field season activities)
- Personnel scaling: Core effort concentrated in Research Scientist (Ryan Ferrell) and Field Technician (Makayla Freed) roles
- Equipment front-loaded (Year 1): Bench scale ($1,000), pole pruners ($200)

**Source**: Open Science and Data Management Plan, Pepperwood Budget Doc, Technical Approach section.
