# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Title**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"

**Client/Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, Opportunity A.59)

**Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)

**Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)

**Brief Description**: Multi-institutional research project combining small UAS-based soil moisture mapping, satellite data (NISAR, UAVSAR), and field validation campaigns to develop wildfire susceptibility products. Led by Black Swift Technologies in partnership with Pepperwood Preserve and Weather Stream, with oversight from Principal Investigator Jack Elston.

## Key Deliverables & Milestones

**Data not available from Asana.** The provided documents do not include Asana project management data, task lists, or milestone schedules with associated financial implications.

**Technical deliverables mentioned in proposal narrative**:
- 192 small UAS flights per year (generating 2m-resolution soil moisture maps, ~10GB data/year)
- Integration with NISAR satellite data (200m resolution, 6-day intervals starting October 2025)
- UAVSAR flights (6m resolution, 9 flights planned Year 1)
- Live fuel moisture (LFM) indices derived from soil moisture and vegetation data

- Open Science and Data Management Plan (OSDMP) compliance; all data to be published in HDF format upon validation
- Operational codes (IDL, Python) to be released at project completion
