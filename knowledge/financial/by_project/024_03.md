# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA ROSES 2024 (A.59 funding opportunity)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3-year extended period)
- **Brief Description**: Multi-partner research project integrating small UAS platforms with satellite data (NISAR, UAVSAR) to generate high-resolution soil moisture and live fuel moisture maps for wildfire susceptibility assessment. Key partners include Pepperwood Preserve (field validation) and Weather Stream (UAS operations and data processing).
