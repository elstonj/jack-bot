# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, Funding Opportunity A.59)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Key Personnel**: Jack Elston (PI/PD #1), Maciej Stachura (Co-I), Margaret Boeger (Pepperwood Partner – President & CEO), Ryan Ferrell (Pepperwood Partner – Research Scientist)

**Scope Summary** *(from Proposals & Reports)*:
Research project developing on-demand wildfire susceptibility mapping using fuel moisture and vegetative index data. Employs small UAS platforms (~192 flights/year) to generate 2m resolution soil moisture maps, complemented by NISAR satellite (200m, 6-day intervals starting Oct 2025) and UAVSAR (6m, 9 flights Year 1). Data products and operational codes to be released publicly upon validation and project completion.

#### 3. **Budget Risks & Concerns**

**a) Aggregate Budget Discrepancy**
- Pepperwood + Weather Stream = $198,426 + $239,900.40 = **$438,326.40**
- Stated aggregate contract value = **$518,293.20**
- **Unaccounted difference: $79,966.80** (exactly one year of Weather Stream budget)
  - *Interpretation*: Either a third partner's budget is missing from the provided documents, OR the aggregate figure includes additional overhead/reserve not broken down by partner.
  - **Action Required**: Clarify whether additional budget allocations or partners exist.

**b) Field Operations Intensity**
- Pepperwood budget assumes **9 field campaigns** across 3 years with onsite lodging (70 nights @ $50/night = $3,500). This equates to ~2–3 campaigns/year with extended field presence.
- Small UAS operations (per Data Management Plan) require ~192 flights/year. Current budget may underestimate operational overhead if flights extend beyond the 9 planned validation campaigns.
- **Risk**: Cost overruns if additional field campaigns or extended UAS operations are required for validation.

**c) Satellite Data Integration (NISAR)**
- NISAR operational date: October 2025 (within Year 1). No additional capital or licensing costs are budgeted for satellite data access.
- **Assumption**: NISAR data is provided free via NASA partnership; if data licensing or processing fees arise, they would require budget amendment.

**d) Staffing Continuity**
- Ryan Ferrell allocated 1,000 hrs/year (critical path for research). No contingency or backup identified.
- Makayla Freed allocated 850 hrs/year (field operations). Similarly, no backup identified.
- **Risk**: Key person dependencies; turnover or unavailability could impact field campaigns.

**e) Indirect Cost Rate Cap**
- Both Pepperwood and Weather Stream use the maximum 20% indirect rate. If actual indirect costs (utilities, facilities, administrative overhead) exceed 20% of direct costs, BST would absorb overages.
- **Mitigation**: BST's 3,000 sq. ft. facility in Boulder should support indirect cost base; actual rate usage should be monitored against actuals.
