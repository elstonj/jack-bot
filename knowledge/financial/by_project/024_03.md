# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Full Project Name:** "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"

**Client/Agency:** NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)

**Contract Type:** NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)

**Period of Performance:** April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)

**Brief Description:** Multi-institutional research project integrating small UAS platforms, satellite data (NISAR, UAVSAR), and field validation campaigns to develop soil moisture and live fuel moisture mapping capabilities for wildfire susceptibility assessment. Pepperwood Preserve serves as primary field validation site with 9 annual campaigns. Project leverages Black Swift Technologies' UAS capabilities and weather monitoring infrastructure.
