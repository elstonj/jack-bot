# Project 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name**: "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"
- **Client/Agency**: NASA (ROSES 2024, Funding Opportunity A.59)
- **Contract Type**: NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 submission)
- **Period of Performance**: April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)
- **Brief Description**: Multi-partner research project to develop soil moisture and live fuel moisture mapping capabilities using small UAS platforms, NISAR satellite data, and UAVSAR for wildfire susceptibility assessment. Primary partners include Pepperwood Preserve and Weather Stream, with Black Swift Technologies as prime or co-investigator.

4. **Indirect Cost Rate Conservative**: 20% IDC is at the maximum allowed for the funding opportunity and reasonable for research institutions.
