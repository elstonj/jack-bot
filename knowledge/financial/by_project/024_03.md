# PROJECT 024-03 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Title:** "On-demand wildfire susceptibility from fuel moisture and vegetative index maps"

**Client/Agency:** NASA (ROSES 2024, A.59 funding opportunity)

**Contract Type:** NASA ROSES research grant (Step 2 proposal submission)

**Period of Performance:** April 1, 2025 – April 1, 2028 (3 years)

**Brief Description:** This is a collaborative NASA-funded research project led by Black Swift Technologies in partnership with Pepperwood and Weather Stream. The project focuses on developing soil moisture and live fuel moisture mapping capabilities using small unmanned aerial systems (UAS), satellite data (NISAR, UAVSAR), and ground validation campaigns. Deliverables include high-resolution fuel moisture and vegetative index maps for wildfire susceptibility assessment, with data and operational codes to be released publicly upon validation.

- Maciej Stachura (Key Team Member)

- Ryan Ferrell, Research Scientist (primary field effort)
