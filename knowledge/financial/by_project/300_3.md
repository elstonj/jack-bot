# Project 300-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2026 IDIQ (Hurricane) — Black Swift S0 sUAS Platforms & Operational Support
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) — Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC)
- **Contract Type:** IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity); Firm Fixed Price with T&M extensions
- **Contract Number:** 1305M226D0012 (base IDIQ); 1305M226F0084 (delivery order)
- **Period of Performance:** 2026–2030 (five-year IDIQ)
- **Current Focus Period:** March 2026 – July 2026 (Year 1 execution phase)

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Supply and deliver 25 Black Swift S0 small UAS platforms to NOAA for the 2026 hurricane season operations, plus 2 rack-mount ground stations. Project includes mission days operational support, field deployments, firmware finalization, hardware validation, and QC. Critical hardware delivery milestone completed early (May 19, 2026); final platform delivery and ground station delivery due July 31, 2026.
