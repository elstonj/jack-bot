# Project 300-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** 2026 IDIQ (Hurricane) — NOAA Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC)

**Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration), EAD-OMAO, Norfolk, VA

**Contract Type:** IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity), Firm Fixed Price with T&M extensions

**Contract Numbers:** 
- Base IDIQ: 1305M226D0012
- Delivery Order: 1305M226F0084 (Invoice 1736 and related invoices)

**Period of Performance:** 2026–2030 (five-year contract)

**Project Scope:** Delivery of 20 UAS (S0 sUAS platforms) to NOAA for atmospheric measurement capabilities, plus 2 ground stations, operational support, mission days, training, and travel for field deployment during hurricane season.

**Background:** Continuation of SBIR Phase I (2018) and Phase II (2019–2020) collaboration; ongoing partnership since 2018.
