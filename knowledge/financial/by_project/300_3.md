# Project 300-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

- **Full Project Name:** 2026 IDIQ (Hurricane) — NOAA Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC)
- **Client/Agency:** National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) — Environmental and Atmospheric Data Operations, Norfolk, VA
- **Contract Type:** IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity), Firm Fixed Price with T&M extensions for select activities
- **Contract Numbers:** 
  - Base IDIQ: **1305M226D0012**
  - Delivery Order: **1305M226F0084** (for Invoice 1736 and ongoing platform deliveries)
- **Period of Performance:** 2026–2030 (five-year contract); **Current Delivery Window: July 31, 2026**
- **Brief Description of Scope:**
  - Procure and deliver 20 BST S0 sUAS platforms for NOAA hurricane operations
  - Provide 2 ground stations for operational support
  - Include mission days operational support, operational/testing support, and travel for WP-3D onboard deployments during hurricane season (June–November)
  - Ongoing engineering integration, testing, and government-directed modifications

**Historical Context:** Continuation of SBIR Phase I (2018, #1305M218CNRMW0059) and Phase II (2019–2020, #1305M219CNRMW0030) partnership with NOAA since 2018.
