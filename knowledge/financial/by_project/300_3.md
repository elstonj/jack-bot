# Project 300-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2026 IDIQ (Hurricane) — NOAA Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC)
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration), EAD-OMAO, Norfolk, VA
- **Contract Type:** IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity); firm fixed price with T&M extensions
- **Contract Number:** 1305M226D0012 (base IDIQ); 1305M226F0084 (delivery order for current phase)
- **Vendor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC, Boulder, CO (CAGE 6PGF9)
- **Period of Performance:** 2026–2030 (5-year IDIQ); current active delivery order: March 2026 – July 2026
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Procurement, integration, and delivery of 20 Black Swift S0 sUAS platforms + 2 ground stations with advanced atmospheric measurement capabilities for NOAA hurricane reconnaissance operations. Includes operational support, mission days, training, and travel for field deployment.
