## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2026 IDIQ (Hurricane) — BST S0 sUAS Platforms for NOAA
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) — Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC), Norfolk, VA
- **Contract Type:** IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity); Firm Fixed Price with T&M extensions
- **Contract Numbers:** 
  - Base IDIQ: 1305M226D0012
  - Delivery Order for Invoice 1736: 1305M226F0084
- **Period of Performance:** 2026–2030 (five-year IDIQ contract)
- **Brief Description:** Delivery of 25 BST S0 uncrewed aerial systems (UAS) platforms to support NOAA hurricane operations (24 for 2026 season, 4 for sasqwatch missions, 1 carryover from 2025), plus 2 rack-mount ground stations. Includes operational support, mission days, testing, travel, and training. Ongoing partnership since 2018 SBIR Phase I.
