# Project 300-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Full Project Name:** 2026 IDIQ (Hurricane) — NOAA Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC)

**Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) — Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC), EAD-OMAO, Norfolk, VA

**Contract Type:** IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity), Firm Fixed Price with T&M extensions for select activities

**Contract Numbers:**
- Base IDIQ: 1305M226D0012
- Delivery Order (Invoice 1736): 1305M226F0084

**Period of Performance:** 2026–2030 (five-year contract); immediate focus on 2026 field season (June 1 – November 30)

**Brief Description of Scope:**
Procurement, integration, testing, and operational deployment of 25 Black Swift Technologies S0 sUAS platforms for NOAA hurricane research operations. Includes 20 platforms under base contract (CLIN 1001), plus 5 additional units (24 for 2026 season + 4 for sasqwatch + 1 leftover from 2025 + 1 refurb). Scope includes mission operations support, operator training, deployment tube board QC, firmware finalization, web-based controller development, and field deployment during 2026 hurricane season.

**Historical Context:** Ongoing partnership since 2018 (SBIR Phase I 2018, Phase II 2019–2020).
