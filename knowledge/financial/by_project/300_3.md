# Project 300-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Project Name:** 2026 IDIQ (Hurricane)
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) — Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC)
- **Contract Type:** IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity) — Firm Fixed Price with T&M extensions for select activities
- **Contract Number:** 1305M226D0012 (base IDIQ); 1305M226F0084 (delivery order)
- **RFQ Number:** 1305M226Q0003
- **Period of Performance:** 2026–2030 (five-year IDIQ)
- **Primary Deliverable:** 25 UAS S0 platforms for NOAA (24 for 2026 season + 4 for sasquatch + 1 leftover + 1 refurb) + 2 rack-mount ground stations
- **Final Delivery Deadline:** 2026-07-31

**Brief Scope:** Supply and support Black Swift S0 sUAS platforms for NOAA hurricane reconnaissance operations, including mission days operational support, testing, training, travel, and engineering integration. This is an ongoing partnership since 2018 (SBIR Phase I 2018, Phase II 2019–2020).
