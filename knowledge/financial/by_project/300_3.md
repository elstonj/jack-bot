# Project 300-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** 2026 IDIQ (Hurricane) — Black Swift Technologies S0 sUAS Platforms for NOAA  
**Client/Customer:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) — Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC), EAD-OMAO, Norfolk, VA  
**Contract Type:** IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity) — Firm Fixed Price with T&M extensions  
**Contract Number:** 1305M226D0012 (base IDIQ); 1305M226F0084 (delivery order)  
**Vendor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC, Boulder, CO (CAGE 6PGF9)

**Period of Performance:** 2026–2030 (five-year IDIQ)  
**Current Focus Period:** March 2026 – July 2026 (initial delivery order execution)

**Brief Description of Scope:**  
Supply and delivery of 20 BST S0 sUAS (small Uncrewed Aerial System) platforms for atmospheric measurement and hurricane research, plus 2 ground stations for NOAA operations. Platforms feature advanced environmental sensing (Vaisala relative humidity, pressure, temperature) and will be deployed aboard NOAA's WP-3D aircraft for hurricane season operations (June–November). Contract includes firm fixed-price platform procurement, operational support, training, and travel for qualified deployable operators.
