# Project 300-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** 2026 IDIQ (Hurricane) — NOAA S0 sUAS Platform Delivery  
**Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) — Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC), Norfolk, VA  
**Contract Type:** IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity); Firm Fixed Price with T&M extensions  
**Contract Number:** 1305M226D0012 (base IDIQ); 1305M226F0084 (delivery order for Invoice 1736)  
**Vendor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC, Boulder, CO (CAGE 6PGF9, UEI C2J3K9NRE3L3)  
**Period of Performance:** 2026–2030 (five-year IDIQ)  
**Current Reporting Period:** March 2026 – July 2026 (initial delivery phase)

**Scope Summary:**  
Supply and delivery of 20 S0 sUAS (small Unmanned Aerial System) platforms for atmospheric measurement and hurricane reconnaissance operations, plus 2 ground control station (GCS) units. Project builds on NOAA-BST partnership established in SBIR Phase I (2018) and Phase II (2019–2020). Deliverables include firmware updates (Vaisala RH sensor integration), platform validation, hardware assembly, and operational support.

**Critical Dates:**
- Hardware ship date: May 27, 2026
- Final delivery deadline (all 20 units packed): June 30, 2026
- Invoice schedule: March 2026 – July 2026
