# Project 300-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** 2026 IDIQ (Hurricane) — NOAA Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC)

**Client/Customer:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration), EAD-OMAO, Norfolk, VA

**Contract Details:**
- **Contract Type:** IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity), Firm Fixed Price with T&M extensions for select activities
- **Contract Number:** 1305M226D0012 (base IDIQ)
- **Delivery Order:** 1305M226F0084 (CLIN 1001 invoicing)
- **RFQ Number:** 1305M226Q0003
- **BST Vendor Info:** CAGE 6PGF9, UEI C2J3K9NRE3L3

**Period of Performance:** 2026–2030 (5-year IDIQ); Current active phase: March 2026 – July 2026

**Scope Summary:**
Primary deliverable is 20 S0 sUAS (small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems) platforms for NOAA hurricane operations, plus 2 ground stations. This is an extension of prior SBIR Phase I (2018) and Phase II (2019–2020) collaboration. The project funds platform procurement, operational support, training delivery, and travel for hurricane season deployment (June 1 – November 30) aboard WP-3D aircraft.
