# Project 300-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Project Name:** 2026 IDIQ (Hurricane) — BST S0 sUAS Platform Delivery & Operational Support
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration), Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC)
- **Contract Type:** IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity), Firm Fixed Price with Time & Materials extensions
- **Contract Number:** 1305M226D0012 (base IDIQ); 1305M226F0084 (delivery order for initial platforms)
- **Period of Performance:** 2026–2030 (five-year contract)
- **Brief Scope:** Delivery of 25 BST S0 sUAS platforms for NOAA hurricane season operations (24 for 2026 field season, 4 for sasquatch deployment, 1 leftover, 1 refurbished), platform operational support, mission day coordination, firmware finalization, and 2 rack-mount ground station systems. Ongoing partnership since 2018 (SBIR Phase I 2018, Phase II 2019–2020).
