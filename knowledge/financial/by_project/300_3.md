  - Base IDIQ: **1305M226D0012**
  - Delivery Order: **1305M226F0084** (Invoice 1736 and subsequent platform deliveries)
- **Period of Performance:** 2026–2030 (five-year IDIQ base contract)
- **Current Delivery Window:** March 2026 – July 2026 (CLIN 1001 initial phase: 20 S0 platforms + 2 ground stations)
