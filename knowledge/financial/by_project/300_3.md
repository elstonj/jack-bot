# Project 300-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2026 IDIQ (Hurricane) – BST S0 sUAS Platforms for NOAA
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) – Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC), Norfolk, VA
- **Contract Type:** IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity), Firm Fixed Price with T&M extensions for select activities
- **Contract Numbers:** 
  - Base IDIQ: 1305M226D0012
  - Delivery Order: 1305M226F0084
- **Period of Performance:** 2026–2030 (five-year IDIQ); active invoicing phase March 2026 – July 2026
- **Brief Description:** Supply and delivery of 20 BST S0 sUAS platforms (unmanned aircraft systems) to NOAA for hurricane research operations, plus operational support, mission days, testing support, travel, and ground station delivery. Platforms are configured for deployment on NOAA's WP-3D research aircraft during hurricane season.
