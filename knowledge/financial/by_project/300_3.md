# Project 300-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2026 IDIQ (Hurricane) — Black Swift Technologies S0 sUAS Platforms & Operational Support for NOAA
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration), Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC), EAD-OMAO, Norfolk, VA
- **Contract Type:** IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity), Firm Fixed Price with T&M extensions for select activities
- **Contract Numbers:** 
  - Base IDIQ: 1305M226D0012
  - Delivery Order (original): 1305M226F0084
  - Modification P26002 (Option 2): POF0344
- **Period of Performance:** 2026–2030 (five-year contract)
- **Brief Description:** Supply and delivery of 20 Black Swift S0 sUAS platforms with 2 rack-mount ground stations, plus operational support, training, and hurricane-season deployment coordination. Option 2 (enacted July 2026) adds 34 additional S0 units with compressed delivery timelines.
