- **Full Project Name:** 2026 IDIQ (Hurricane) — 20 UAS Units for NOAA
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) — Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC), Norfolk, VA
- **Contract Type:** IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity); Firm Fixed Price per unit with T&M extensions
