# Project 300-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Project Name:** 2026 IDIQ (Hurricane) — NOAA UxSOC S0 sUAS Platforms & Ground Stations
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) — Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC), EAD-OMAO, Norfolk, VA
- **Contract Type:** IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity), Firm Fixed Price with T&M extensions
- **Contract Number:** 1305M226D0012 (base IDIQ); 1305M226F0084 (delivery order for primary invoicing)
- **Period of Performance:** 2026–2030 (five-year IDIQ)
- **Deliverable:** 20 Black Swift S0 sUAS platforms + 2 ground stations for atmospheric measurement operations
- **Brief Scope:** Supply and delivery of 20 uncrewed aerial systems with Vaisala RH/atmospheric sensors and full operational/training support; includes mission days operational support, testing, travel for WP-3D onboard deployment during hurricane season (June–November)
