- **Full Project Name:** 2026 IDIQ (Hurricane) — Black Swift Technologies sUAS Platforms for NOAA
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) — Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC), EAD-OMAO, Norfolk, VA
- **Contract Type:** IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity), firm fixed price with T&M extensions for select activities
- **Contract Numbers:** 
  - Base IDIQ: 1305M226D0012
  - Original Delivery Order: 1305M226F0084 (Modification P26002, CLIN 1001)
  - Option 2: POF0344 (additional CLINs for 34 S0 platforms)

Option 2 adds 34 additional S0 platforms with Oct 30 and Nov 30, 2026 delivery windows. Potential emerging scope of 30 additional S0s for June 1, 2027.
