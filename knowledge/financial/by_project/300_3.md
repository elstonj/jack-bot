# Project 300-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Project Name:** 2026 IDIQ (Hurricane) — Black Swift Technologies S0 sUAS Platform Delivery to NOAA
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) — Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC), EAD-OMAO, Norfolk, VA
- **Contract Type:** IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity), Firm Fixed Price with T&M extensions for engineering
- **Contract Number:** 1305M226D0012 (base IDIQ); 1305M226F0084 (delivery order)
- **Period of Performance:** 2026–2030 (5-year IDIQ); current execution phase: March 2026 – July 31, 2026
- **Brief Description:** Supply and delivery of 25 Black Swift S0 sUAS platforms (24 for 2026 hurricane season + 4 for sasqwatch project + 1 leftover from 2025) plus 2 ground stations to NOAA UxSOC for maritime and hurricane operations. Includes firmware development, hardware validation, operator training, and operational support.

**Background:** Continuation of long-standing partnership with NOAA dating to SBIR Phase I (2018) and Phase II (2019–2020).
