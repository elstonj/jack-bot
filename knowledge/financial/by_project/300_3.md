# Project 300-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2026 IDIQ (Hurricane) — BST S0 sUAS Platforms & Operational Support for NOAA
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) — Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC)
- **Contract Type:** IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity); Firm Fixed Price with T&M extensions
- **Contract Numbers:** 
  - Base IDIQ: 1305M226D0012
  - Delivery Order: 1305M226F0084
- **Period of Performance:** 2026–2030 (5-year IDIQ term); **Current Phase Execution Window: March 2026 – July 31, 2026**
- **Vendor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC, Boulder, CO (CAGE 6PGF9, UEI C2J3K9NRE3L3)

**Brief Scope Description:**  
Supply and deliver 25 BST S0 sUAS platforms (24 for 2026 hurricane season, 4 for sasqwatch, 1 carryover from 2025) plus 2 rack-mount ground stations. Provide operational support, mission days, testing, training, and FTR-compliant travel for deployment with NOAA's WP-3D aircraft during hurricane season (June 1 – November 30). Five-year IDIQ framework for ongoing sUAS procurement and operational services.
