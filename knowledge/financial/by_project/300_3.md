# Project 300-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview

**Project Name:** 2026 IDIQ (Hurricane) – NOAA UxSOC S0 sUAS Platforms  
**Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) – Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC), EAD-OMAO, Norfolk, VA  
**Contract Type:** IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity), Firm Fixed Price with T&M options  
**Contract Number:** 1305M226D0012 (base IDIQ); 1305M226F0084 (delivery order for Invoice 1736)  
**Vendor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC, Boulder, CO (CAGE 6PGF9)  

**Period of Performance:** 2026–2030 (five-year IDIQ); current **active phase: March 2026 – July 2026** (first delivery order)  
**Brief Scope:** Design, manufacture, and deliver 20 Black Swift S0 small Uncrewed Aircraft Systems (sUAS) with advanced atmospheric measurement capabilities (including humidity/Vaisala sensors) for NOAA hurricane reconnaissance operations. Includes operational support, mission days training, and field deployment with qualified operators during hurricane season (June–November).

**Background:** Continuation of SBIR Phase I (2018) and Phase II (2019–2020) partnership with NOAA.
