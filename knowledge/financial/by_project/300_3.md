# Project 300-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2026 IDIQ (Hurricane) — NOAA Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC)
- **Client/Agency:** National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), EAD-OMAO, Norfolk, VA
- **Contract Type:** IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity); Firm Fixed Price with T&M extensions for select activities
- **Contract Numbers:** 
  - Base IDIQ: 1305M226D0012
  - Original Delivery Order: 1305M226F0084 (Modification P26002, CLIN 1001)
  - Option 2 Delivery Order: POF0344 (34 additional S0s)
- **Period of Performance:** 2026–2030 (five-year IDIQ)
- **Brief Description of Scope:** Supply of 20 BST S0 small Unmanned Aircraft Systems (sUAS) platforms with 2 rack-mount ground stations for NOAA hurricane operations. Original Delivery Order completed July 31, 2026 (17 days late on ground stations). Option 2 ($628,000) adds 34 additional S0 platforms with delivery milestones Oct 30, 2026 (13 units) and Nov 30, 2026 (21 units). Potential emerging scope of 30 additional S0s for June 1, 2027 (not yet formalized). Ongoing partnership with NOAA since 2018.

**Historical Context:** SBIR Phase I (2018), Phase II (2019–2020); previous hurricane-related work (300-1) and Phase II follow-on (301-2).
