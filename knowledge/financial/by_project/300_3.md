# Project 300-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2026 IDIQ (Hurricane) – NOAA Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC)
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) – EAD-OMAO, Norfolk, VA
- **Contract Type:** IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity); Firm Fixed Price with T&M extensions for select activities
- **Contract Number:** 1305M226D0012 (base IDIQ)
  - Delivery Order (DO): 1305M226F0084 (original)
  - Modification P26002 / Option 2 (POF0344): Additional funding enacted 2026-08-05
- **Period of Performance:** 2026–2030 (five-year IDIQ)
- **Brief Description of Scope:**
  - Original DO: Delivery of 20 UAS (S0 platforms) + 2 rack-mount ground stations to NOAA ($483,000)
  - Option 2: 34 additional S0 units across two tranches (13 units due Oct 30, 2026; 21 units due Nov 30, 2026) ($628,000)
  - Supporting operational/testing support, mission days, training, and travel for hurricane season deployments (June 1 – Nov 30)
  - Ongoing partnership since 2018 (SBIR Phase I 2018, Phase II 2019–2020)
