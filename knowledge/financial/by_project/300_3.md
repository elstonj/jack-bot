# Project 300-3 — Financial Summary

## Project Overview
- **Full Project Name:** 2026 IDIQ (Hurricane) — NOAA UxSOC S0 sUAS Platform Delivery & Operational Support
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) — Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC), Norfolk, VA
- **Contract Type:** IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity), Firm Fixed Price with T&M extensions for select activities
- **Contract Number:** 1305M226D0012 (base IDIQ); 1305M226F0084 (delivery order)
- **Period of Performance:** 2026–2030 (five-year IDIQ)
- **Current Deployment Window:** 2026 hurricane season (June 1 – November 30)
- **Brief Scope:** Supply, integrate, validate, and deliver 25 Black Swift S0 sUAS platforms to NOAA for autonomous hurricane reconnaissance missions, plus 2 rack-mount ground control stations (GCS). Includes operational support, field deployment, mission days support, training, and travel for qualified operators.
