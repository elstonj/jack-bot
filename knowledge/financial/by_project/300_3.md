- **Full Project Name:** 2026 IDIQ (Hurricane) – NOAA Uncrewed Systems Operations Center (UxSOC)
- **Client/Agency:** NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) – EAD-OMAO, Norfolk, VA
- **Contract Type:** IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity); Firm Fixed Price with T&M extensions for select activities
- **Contract Numbers:** 
  - Base IDIQ: **1305M226D0012**
  - Delivery Order: **1305M226F0084** (Invoice 1736)
  - RFQ: 1305M226Q0003
- **Vendor:** Black Swift Technologies LLC, Boulder, CO (CAGE 6PGF9, UEI C2J3K9NRE3L3)
- **Period of Performance:** 2026–2030 (5-year IDIQ term)
- **Ongoing Partnership:** Since 2018 (SBIR Phase I 2018, Phase II 2019–2020)
- **Brief Scope:** Supply and operational support for 25 Black Swift S0 sUAS platforms for NOAA hurricane reconnaissance operations, plus 2 rack-mount ground control stations (GCS). Includes firmware validation, QC testing, hardware assembly, deployment support, and web-based controller development.
