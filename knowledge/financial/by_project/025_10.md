- **Client/Agency:** By Light Professional IT Services (BYL)
- **Contract Type:** Commercial consulting/design & prototyping engagement
- **Period of Performance:** 2025-10-06 through 2025-12-05 (9 weeks)
- **Project Status:** **ARCHIVED & COMPLETE** as of 2025-12-31
