- **Client/Agency:** By Light Professional IT Services
- **Contract Type:** Commercial consulting/design & prototyping
- **Period of Performance:** 2025-10-06 through 2025-12-05 (9 weeks)
- **Status:** **ARCHIVED & COMPLETE** (marked complete Dec 31, 2025; archived per team direction October 2026)
