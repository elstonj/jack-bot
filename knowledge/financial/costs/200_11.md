### Budget vs. Actual Labor Discrepancy

**Budget Estimated Labor (Base):** $34,906.00  
**Actual Labor Tracked:** $12,143.50  
**Variance:** ($22,762.50) or **65% underrun on tracked hours**

**Explanation:** The budget assumed 280 hours of labor across four roles (60 PI + 60 Engineer + 80 Technician + 60 Pilot). Actual Toggl tracking shows only **84.5 hours** logged, suggesting either:
1. Significant work was performed "off-clock" or not time-tracked
2. Much of the contract deliverables were completed via fixed-price milestone invoicing (more likely given contract structure)
3. Work occurred in prior phases not captured in this 9-month window

The budget documents themselves note that labor spending was "deliverable-based" rather than hourly—i.e., Elston/Stachura billed full $11,635 (with fringe) even though fewer hours were logged, indicating lump-sum invoicing.
