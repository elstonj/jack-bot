## Executive Summary

**Project 301-2** (2020 WPO Hurricane Phase II) is a NOAA-funded SBIR Phase II+ effort to develop and deploy sUAS platforms for tropical cyclone research. Current actuals demonstrate the project is tracking toward the $986K contract ceiling but with cost concentration in unexpected areas.
