## Executive Summary

**Project 400-5** is a completed DoD SBIR Phase II for soil moisture mapping via UAS, awarded to Black Swift Technologies with Orbital Micro Systems as subcontractor. The project ran November 2023–July 2025 (21 months) with a **$1,249,725 contract ceiling**. Final closeout occurred November 2025.

### Budget vs. Actual Summary
• **Approved Budget:** $1,249,725.00
• **Total Invoiced (BST + OMS):** $1,009,312.82
• **BST Direct Spend (QBO 400-5):** $669,364.49
• **OMS Subcontractor Invoices:** ~$605,000 (final payment $24,200 on 2025-11-19)
• **Remaining:** ~$240,412 unspent (19.2% of contract)
• **Status:** Project archived; successfully closed within budget

### Indirect Costs (Government Contract – 29.28% Fringe + 46.67% Overhead)

Per budget document, this is a **DoD SBIR Phase II (government contract)**. Applying government rates:

• **Direct Labor (from Toggl estimate):** $24,307.00
• **Fringe (29.28%):** $7,116.29
• **Overhead (46.67%):** $11,340.27
• **Subtotal Indirect:** $18,456.56
• **Fully Loaded Labor (Toggl basis):** $42,763.56

**However**, the **budget document itself** specifies different rates:
- **BST Fringe:** 10%, Overhead: 10% → **20% combined**
- **OMS Fringe:** 22%, Overhead: 19% → **41% combined**

Since the budget shows **actual negotiated rates with USAF**, those are more authoritative than generic DoD templates. Using budget rates:
- **BST Toggl Labor + 20% indirects:** $24,307.00 × 1.20 = **$29,168.40**
