However, the budget document shows **only 15% overhead applied** to labor rates in the burdened rate calculation, not the typical 29.28% fringe + 46.67% overhead that BST defaults specify for government contracts.

**Applying Budget-Provided Rates:**
• Ted Miles invoices already include 15% overhead in burdened rate ($71.53/hr)
• Kay Rufty labor rate of $62.20 + 15% overhead = $71.53/hr (burdened)
• Consulting at $171.47 + 20% overhead = $205.76/hr (burdened)

**Note:** The burdened rates in the budget document appear to be negotiated/allowable rates under the NASA agreement and are lower than BST defaults (which apply 29.28% + 46.67% = ~98% total). **No additional indirects applied** beyond what is already reflected in the contracted labor rates.
