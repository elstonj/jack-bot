## BURN RATE & CONTRACT COMPLIANCE ASSESSMENT

### Critical Finding: Cost Overrun

• **Invoiced Contract Amount:** $124,230.00
• **Actual Costs Incurred:** $131,233.38
• **Overage:** $7,003.38 (5.6% above contract value)

### Root Cause Analysis

1. **Labor Hours Not Originally Budgeted:** Toggl shows 28.4 hours of internal team labor (Jack Elston, Stachura, Dan, Karen Young) tracked *after* Invoice 1655 was issued on 03/13/2025. These hours represent post-invoice work (likely Q1 2025 activities: flight testing, debugging, payload setup, regulatory coordination).

2. **Data Processing Labor Rate Discrepancy:** Invoice 1655 applied a $175/hr blended rate for flight planning, permissions support, QA, and meetings (108 hours = $18,900). This rate is significantly higher than standard engineering rates ($75–90/hr), suggesting specialized or senior-level effort was charged.

3. **Period of Performance Mismatch:** Invoice 1655 (dated 03/13/2025) invoiced the complete $124,230, but the contract period extends to 09/30/2025. The no-cost extension (P0001, signed 03/05/2024) added 13 months of performance period with no budget increase, creating a risk of uncompensated work through September 2025.

### Burn Rate Assessment: **OFF TRACK**

• **Current Monthly Burn (based on 28.4 hours tracked):** ~$5,003.38 / month (labor only; materials/equipment are non-recurring)
• **Remaining Contract Period:** ~6.5 months (04/01/2025 – 09/30/2025)
• **Available Budget:** $0.00 (fully invoiced as of 03/13/2025)
• **Risk Level:** **HIGH** — Any additional labor, travel, or equipment costs post-03/13/2025 will exceed the fixed contract value.

### Recommendations

1. **Clarify Invoice 1655 Scope:** Verify whether the $124,230 invoice covers *all* work through 09/30/2025 or only the deployment phase (01/2025–03/2025). If additional work is required through September, a contract modification or supplemental billing is necessary.

2. **Monitor Undescribed Work:** Several Toggl entries lack detailed descriptions (Dan's 0.8h, Stachura's 1.1h, Dan's 2.5h). Ensure these hours are properly classified and billed to avoid scope creep or uncompensated effort.

3. **Regulatory Compliance Note:** Export licensing work for Mexico deployment (0.5h Jack Elston) is documented but should be tracked separately to ensure government compliance requirements are fully captured in invoicing.

4. **No-Cost Extension Implications:** The 09/30/2025 end date with no budget increase means all additional support, data archiving, or operational work must either be: (a) absorbed as sunk cost, (b) invoiced separately under a new task/modification, or (c) deferred post-contract.
