| **Schedule Adherence** | **DELAYED** | Originally scheduled for April 2026 flight ops; now postponed to Fall 2026 (6+ month slip). |
| **Burn Rate (Monthly)** | **$5,930/month actual** | $4,290 labor ÷ ~9 months of tracked activity (~$477/month actual; heavily front-loaded at invoicing). |

### Key Observations

1. **Invoice Front-Loading:** The full contract was invoiced upfront (03/13/2025) for a deployment originally planned for April 2026. This is typical for government contracts with fixed pricing, but creates timing risk if deployment is deferred.

2. **Labor Under-Utilization:** Only 28.4 hours tracked against an ~108-hour engineering labor budget ($18,900 ÷ $175/hr = 108 hrs planned). This suggests:
   - Prior labor may have been invoiced but not captured in current Toggl data, OR
   - Mission delay has reduced active engineering work, OR
   - Labor allocation will ramp up when deployment resumes (Fall 2026).

3. **Unspent Operational Budget:** Travel ($16,175) and aircraft rental/testing ($89,155) are contingent on actual mission execution. With deployment now 6+ months deferred, these funds are reserved but not yet committed.

4. **No Cost Overrun Risk (Currently):** The contract is fixed at $124,230 with no modification. However, if further delays occur or scope changes, renegotiation would be required.
