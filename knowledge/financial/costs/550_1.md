## Burn Rate Assessment

### Are We On Track?

**❌ RED FLAG — Not on Track**

1. **Fixed-Price Contract Variance:** Phase I Option is fixed-price ($99,459 total for 3 CLINs). QBO charges of $612,295 represent a **$512K overrun**, suggesting:
   - Cross-project cost allocation (shared facilities, management overhead)
   - Equipment procurement for future Phase II work being charged to Phase I
   - Labor costs for Phase II preparation included in Phase I actuals
   - Possible accounting classification issue (Phase II work invoiced under Phase I project code)

2. **Minimal Labor Tracking:** Only 25.3 hours tracked in Toggl despite $94K/month burn rate implies:
   - Labor costs are not being tracked in Toggl; being captured elsewhere (timesheet, allocation)
   - Non-labor costs (equipment, subcontract overhead) dominate the $612K actual spend
   - Work descriptions missing from all time entries (no visibility into scope/deliverables)

3. **Phase II Budget Reality Check:**
   - Phase II proposal assumes 2,735 base hours ($207K direct labor) + fringe/overhead → ~$500K fully loaded labor
   - At current burn rate ($94K/mo), Phase II base 30-month cost projection would be ~$2.8M vs. $999K proposed
   - **Risk:** Actual labor utilization may significantly exceed Phase II budget

### Recommendations

1. **Reconcile QBO vs. Invoicing:** Determine if $612K in QBO charges includes Phase II prep work, shared overhead allocation, or equipment purchases. If so, reallocate to proper project codes.

2. **Implement Work Tracking Discipline:** All time entries must include task descriptions, CLIN reference, and phase designation (Phase I vs. II). Current Toggl entries are unusable for compliance documentation.
