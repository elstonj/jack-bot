- Actual tracked labor (119.6 hrs) and confirmed material purchases ($1,282) total only ~$17,582.

  - Equipment purchases (magnetometers, UAS platforms, test equipment) not yet expensed in Phase I but included in proposal budget

**This is normal for government SBIR Phase I:** Deliverables are fixed-price CLINs; actual spend may vary month-to-month with lumpier equipment/travel timing. Cost control is evidenced by timely invoicing and no overages against proposed budget.
