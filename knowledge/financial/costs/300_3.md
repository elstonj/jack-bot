## **INDIRECT COSTS ANALYSIS**

**Contract Type:** Government IDIQ (Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity)

**Decision:** **Indirect costs NOT applied to labor** for the following reasons:

1. **CLIN Structure is Firm Fixed Price (FFP)** — CLINs 1001 (platforms), 1002 (mission days), 1003 (operational support), 1006 (travel), and ground stations are invoiced as **completed deliverables**, not as time & materials.
2. **Labor tracking is internal cost allocation** for project management and reporting; actual revenue is recognized upon delivery of platforms or completion of defined milestones.
3. **Budget documents do not explicitly itemize indirect rates** (fringe + overhead) for this project; these costs are embedded in the firm fixed pricing.

**If a fully loaded rate were required** (hypothetically): Fringe (29.28%) + Overhead (46.67%) = 76% markup → $55,620 × 1.76 = **$97,891.20 fully loaded labor** (not applied).
