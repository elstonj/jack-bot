## Government Contract Cost Adjustments

**This is a Subaward; No Indirect Rate Multipliers Applied**

• The contract is structured as a **Subaward from University of Colorado** (not a direct government contract with BST).
• Labor rates in the budget ($174/hr for management & data processing; $1,500/day for crew operations) **already include any overhead/fringe** negotiated as part of the subaward agreement.
• No additional 29.28% fringe or 46.67% overhead rates applied (as would be required for direct NOAA/government contracts under CLIN-based pricing).
• All costs invoiced are **fully loaded costs** as negotiated in the original subaward.
