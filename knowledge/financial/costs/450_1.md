### Indirect Costs Assessment

**Contract Type**: Subaward (research/services to University of Colorado / NOAA)

**Determination**: This is a **GOVERNMENT research contract** (NOAA funding through University of Colorado subaward).

**Indirect Rate Applicability**: Budget documents **do not specify applied fringe or overhead rates** in invoices. Invoices reference composite labor rates ($174/hr for project management and flight crew operations, derived from budget justification labor detail) but do not separately line out fringe (29.28%) or overhead (46.67%) as additive cost categories.

**Analysis**: 
- If $174/hr is a **bare labor rate**, then fringe + overhead should apply:
  - Fringe (29.28%): $50.91/hr
  - Overhead (46.67%): $81.21/hr
  - Total loaded rate should be: $306.12/hr
  
- If $174/hr is already a **composite/loaded rate** (likely given budget context), no additional markup applies.

**Conclusion from Budget Documents**: The invoices totaling $148,711.63 appear to use the $174/hr rate as-invoiced without separate overhead/fringe adders. This suggests **either**:
  1. The $174/hr includes some indirect loading, or
  2. Indirects were absorbed by Black Swift and not passed through separately on this subaward (possible cost-share arrangement given "Non-billable" designation in Toggl metadata).

**For Actual Cost Reporting**: Using invoiced amounts as the authoritative actual cost = **$148,711.63 total, with no separately identifiable indirect add-ons.**
