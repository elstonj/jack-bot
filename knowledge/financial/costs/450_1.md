### Indirect Costs Assessment

**Contract Type**: Subaward to University of Colorado (government research contract)

**Indirect Rate Application**: 
• This is a **cost-reimbursable research subaward**, not a commercial contract
• Budget documents do **not explicitly specify indirect rate multipliers** (fringe, overhead percentages)
• Overhead and administrative costs appear to be **absorbed within the labor rates quoted** ($174/hr for professional services, $55/hr for technicians) rather than applied as separate cost multipliers
• Travel, meals, lodging are **itemized separately** (not loaded as percentage of labor)

**Conclusion on Indirects**: 
- **No additional indirect costs to apply**; the contract structure uses blended labor rates and explicit travel line items rather than cost-plus-indirect methodology
- If this were a government cost-plus contract (FARs), we would apply 29.28% fringe + 46.67% overhead; however, the fixed-price subaward structure does not warrant this treatment

**Fully Loaded Labor = Direct Labor Only: $17,973.50** (or $12,857.50 if Stachura billed at base rate)
