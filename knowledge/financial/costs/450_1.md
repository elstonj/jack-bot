## INDIRECT COSTS

**Determination**: This is a **government subaward contract** (University of Colorado, NOAA funding). Standard indirect cost application required.

• **Fringe Rate**: 29.28% (per budget documents for research contracts)
• **Overhead Rate**: 46.67% (per budget documents)
• **Combined Indirect Rate**: 76.0% (or applied sequentially: 1.2928 × 1.4667 = 1.8976)

**Indirect Costs Applied to $90,288.00 Direct Labor**:
- Using combined rate: $90,288.00 × 0.76 = **$68,618.88**

**Fully Loaded Labor Cost**: $90,288.00 + $68,618.88 = **$158,906.88**

**Note**: The budget documents do not explicitly call out fringe and overhead as separate line items in the contract structure. Instead, the contract appears to use **blended hourly rates** ($174/hr) that may already incorporate overhead/fringes for research support staff. The invoiced amounts ($148,711.63 total) suggest indirects are either:
1. Not separately charged (flat fee structure), or
2. Embedded in the flight crew operations daily rate ($1,500/day)

**Conservative Approach for Actual Cost**: Using the invoiced total of **$148,711.63** as the fully-loaded actual cost, which already reflects all direct and indirect costs as invoiced to the client.
