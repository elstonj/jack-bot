## Indirect Costs Calculation (Government Contract)

**This is a NOAA subaward, so fringe (29.28%) + overhead (46.67%) applies.**

Using Toggl-tracked labor as reference point:
• **Direct Labor (Toggl):** $10,253.00
• **Fringe Rate:** 29.28%
• **Fringe Cost:** $10,253.00 × 0.2928 = **$3,001.28**
• **Overhead Rate:** 46.67%
• **Overhead Cost:** $10,253.00 × 0.4667 = **$4,783.98**
• **Fully Loaded Labor (Toggl subset):** $10,253.00 + $3,001.28 + $4,783.98 = **$18,038.26**

**Full Budget Labor Indirects (projected from approved budget):**
• **Direct Labor (Budget):** $88,116.00
• **Fringe:** $88,116.00 × 0.2928 = **$25,810.52**
• **Overhead:** $88,116.00 × 0.4667 = **$41,146.50**
• **Fully Loaded Labor (Budget):** $88,116.00 + $25,810.52 + $41,146.50 = **$155,073.02**

⚠️ **Discrepancy Note:** Budget labor + indirects would total $155,073, but contract cap is $148,729. This suggests either:
1. Indirect costs are embedded differently in the contract (possibly cost-sharing or reduced rates).
2. Labor rates in budget documents are blended rates (already including some indirects).
3. Some labor is cost-shared or non-reimbursable.

**For this analysis, we use the invoice actuals ($148,711.63) as the true cost—the budget already reflects how costs were invoiced.**
