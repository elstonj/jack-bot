## INDIRECT COSTS ASSESSMENT

**Contract Classification**: University of Colorado Subaward (Research/Services) — **NOT a direct government contract**.

BST's budget submission treats this as a **cost-reimbursable subaward**, not subject to standard government fringe/overhead multipliers. Per contract budget narrative:
• Labor rates cited include project management and flight crew daily rates as all-inclusive (no separate fringe/overhead applied).
• Equipment, travel, and materials are direct costs.

**Therefore**: No additional indirect cost multipliers (fringe 29.28% + overhead 46.67%) applied to this analysis. The invoiced amounts represent fully loaded costs as budgeted and reimbursed by University of Colorado.

If this were a direct government contract (NOAA/NSF), indirect costs would add ~$42,000–$50,000; however, the subaward structure absorbs these within the all-in labor rates and travel budgets.
