## INDIRECT COSTS

**Status**: Not separately calculated in this analysis.

**Reason**: This is a cost-reimbursable subaward with a fixed contract value ($148,729.00). The labor rates ($174/hr for crew operations, $174/hr for project management) are fully-loaded rates provided by the University of Colorado contract and already embed typical indirect costs (fringe, overhead). The contract structure is lump-sum by invoice period, not itemized labor + separate indirects.

**Assessment**: For government contracts of this type, fringe (29.28%) and overhead (46.67%) would typically apply if calculating from base labor rates. However, since all invoicing was against pre-negotiated labor rates and fixed material costs, indirects are already factored into the contract award. No additional indirect cost markup should be applied.
