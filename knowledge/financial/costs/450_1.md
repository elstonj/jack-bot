  - Fringe (29.28%): $5,442.75
  - Overhead (46.67%): $8,674.44

**However, Contract Budget Structure Already Incorporates Burden:**
The budget shows labor rates of $174/hr and $1,500/day that are presented as fixed, all-in rates. This suggests the University of Colorado subaward agreement may use a **fully burdened rate model** rather than applying fringe/overhead as line-item multipliers. The $88,116 labor budget likely already includes burden.

**Recommendation:** Use contract budget structure ($148,711.63 actual) as the authoritative cost figure, since it reflects the negotiated subaward terms. The Toggl data ($18,587.75) represents only final closeout work, not full project labor.
