## INDIRECT COSTS & BURDEN

**Government Contract Treatment**: This is a **NOAA subaward** (non-billable status per Toggl). Budget documents do not explicitly call out indirect rates (fringe + overhead).

**Standard BST Burden Rates** (typically applied to government contracts):
- Fringe: 29.28%
- Overhead: 46.67%
- **Combined Indirect Rate**: 76.95% (if applied)

**Assessment**: Budget narratives reference hourly rates ($174/hr for PM/data processing, $1,500/day for flight crew) that appear to include labor, possible burden, and profit margin. No separate indirect cost line in the budget breakdowns provided.

**Recommendation**: If this were a cost-plus contract requiring burden allocation, applying 76.95% to the $90,288 direct labor would yield ~$69,473 in indirects, resulting in fully-loaded labor of ~$159,761. However, budget documents show fixed cost-per-day rates ($1,500/day) and fixed labor amounts, indicating this is a **fixed-price subaward** where burden is embedded in the rates. **No separate indirect calculation applied** to actual costs.
