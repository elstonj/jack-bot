# #s3

## Overview
This channel is primarily used for the development of BST's S3 VTOL aircraft - a long-endurance drone with vertical takeoff/landing capability. The S3 is designed as a hybrid fixed-wing/quadcopter aircraft with tilting rotors, targeting 2-3 hour endurance with automatic flight operations.

## Key Decisions

**Battery Configuration (2020-2026)**
- Selected 6S10P 18650 cell configuration using LG HG2/VTC6 cells over LiPo packs
- Established 120-second VTOL time budget (60s takeoff + 60s landing)
- Later upgraded to 12S configuration for improved performance
- Current configuration: 2x 540Wh packs (1080Wh total nominal) for S3
- **Operational capacity limitation (May 22, 2026)**: Only approximately half of the 1080Wh nominal capacity (~540Wh) can be used operationally due to landing power requirements
- **Battery swap consideration (April 28, 2026)**: Joshua Fromm proposing battery change with constraints: swap without removing payload, no less capacity than current, no more weight than current, same mounting location (front)
  - Approved by Maciej and Jack Elston
- **Future battery evolution noted (April 30, 2026)**: Next version of battery likely to use different cells, increasing pack cost; old packs will be entirely incompatible with future aircraft
- **Battery shipping approach (May 8, 2026)**: Batteries will ship in approved cardboard box rather than ATA300 case (case identification and procurement ongoing with manufacturing partner MM). Class 9 hazmat shipment configuration. ATA300 case target: before Canada deliveries but likely after UMES deliveries. Future S3 battery versions will have custom foam inserts. Final airframe delivery may include battery case for final customer delivery.
- **High-performance battery upgrade (May 19, 2026)**: New cell configuration tested showing significant voltage improvements
  - New pack provides 5.6V additional voltage after 1-hour flight on landing, 5.4V on takeoff
  - Estimated 16% more RPM or 35% more thrust on landing vs. standard 35V nominal
  - Capacity change: 5000 mAh (old cells) vs. 6500 mAh (new cells) - represents effective capacity increase despite lower nominal rating
  - Joshua Fromm to prepare new pack cells and conduct additional testing to confirm flight time with 3V/cell landing voltage target
  - Approval given to fly methane payload with high-power pack
- **Battery cell sourcing challenge (May 21-22, 2026)**: Current P50B cells may not be available; UK supplier checking stock (known by May 22). Alternative options evaluated:
  - Option 1: Harvest cells from old packs (preferred by Jack Elston due to transition to new geometry soon-ish)
  - Option 2: EVE 50PL cells (~$400 for 50 units)
  - Option 3: Reliance RS50 cells (~$350 for 50 units)
  - Option 4: Amprius high discharge cells
  - Joshua Fromm confirmed UK supplier availability and decided on backup cell procurement
- **High-power battery pack production (May 29, 2026)**: Two high-power battery packs required for S3
  - First pack deadline: June 8, 2026
  - Second pack deadline: June 19, 2026
- **Murphy's Pond Battery Progress (June 4, 2026)**: New battery variant showing significant weight reduction
  - Approximately 260 grams lighter than current battery pack
  - Running time also increased
  - Joshua Fromm provided detailed performance numbers
  - Maciej confirmed positive reception of weight savings
