# Black Swift Technologies - Cross-Channel Strategic Overview

## Core Business Focus
BST operates across three primary domains: **government R&D contracts** (SBIR/STTR), **platform development** (S0, S3 UAS systems), and **commercial sales** (aircraft delivery and payloads). The organization is heavily invested in federal funding mechanisms while building proprietary drone platforms.
