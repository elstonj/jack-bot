## Project Summary
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer (in conjunction with NASA) for mapping high-resolution soil moisture. Technology is being modified for DAF end-user application to map landing area soil integrity. Soil moisture measurement is critical for assessing soil slipperiness, stickiness, and structural strength.
