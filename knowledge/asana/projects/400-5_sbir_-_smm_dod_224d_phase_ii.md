## Project Description
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer (in partnership with NASA, OMS, and CET) for mapping high-resolution soil moisture. The technology has been adapted for DAF end-user to map soil integrity, specifically for landing area assessment. Soil moisture measurement is critical for understanding soil strength, slipperiness, and stickiness.
