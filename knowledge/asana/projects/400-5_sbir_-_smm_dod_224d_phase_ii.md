## Project Description
Development and validation of a UAS-based L-band radiometer for high-resolution soil moisture mapping. Originally developed in conjunction with NASA, the technology is being modified for DAF end-user application to map landing area soil integrity. Soil moisture measurement is critical for assessing soil slipperiness, stickiness, and structural strength.
