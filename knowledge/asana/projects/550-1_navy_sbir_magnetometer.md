## Notes & Context

### Technical Scope
The Phase I effort demonstrated feasibility of integrating **magnetic anomaly detection (MAD) and passive acoustic sensing** into an SL UAS platform (S0-MAD variant). Work included:
- Dual sensor integration (magnetometer + acoustic)
- S0-AD ground launcher design and testing
- Platform modifications and onboard logging systems
- Laboratory and field validation (Camp Pendleton demo flights Sept 14–25, 2026)

### Collaborators
- **Cetacean Research Technology (CRT)** — acoustic sensor partner
- **QuSpin** — magnetometer partner
- **BST** — platform integration and testing

### Regulatory/Strategic Context
Per team feedback (Daniel Prendergast, Oct 7–8, 2026), **ECCN (Export Classification Number) requests will be needed soon** for magnetometer-equipped platforms to avoid impacting:
- Pending **Canada project**
- **Lockheed Martin UK / Royal Navy project (Project RAVEN)** — S0-AD integration with Merlin Helicopter Launch System

The magnetometer integration is partially under **US Navy Program Office oversight**, which may affect export licensing discussions with UK partners.

### Process Gap Flagged
Daniel Prendergast (Oct 19–20, 2026) noted a **lack of well-defined process for transitioning proposed system builds into the production pipeline.** The LMUK/Royal Navy project (requiring 2× S0-AD with Magnetometer, 2× S0-AD with Camera, 2× S0-*) is an example where formal transition procedures should be formalized.
