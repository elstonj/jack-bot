The Navy SBIR Phase I (option period starting April 2026) focused on **magnetic anomaly detection (MAD) and passive acoustic sensing integration** into a small unmanned aerial system (SL UAS). Work included:
- Design of magnetometer sensor onboarding and logging
- Build and ground test of S0-MAD hand-launched platform
- S0-AD launcher system design and test
- Field validation at Camp Pendleton (Sept 2026)
- Collaboration with subcontractors: Cetacean Research Technology (CRT) and QuSpin
