# 2024-25 Aeropod (AREN)

## Overview
- **Client/Customer:** NASA
- **Dollar Value:** $92,500 total budget; $16,461.12 funding to Black Swift; subcontractors Ted Miles ($44,706.25) and Kay Rufty ($21,383.70)
- **Timeline:** Funding ends March 2025
- **Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Project marked complete as of March 10, 2025
- **Team:** Black Swift (whole team) with subcontractors Ted Miles and Kay Rufty
- **Point of Contact:** Geoffrey Bland (NASA), geoffrey.l.bland@nasa.gov

## Key Deliverables & Milestones
- Fin design optimization (thickness reduction, aspect ratio adjustment from 3:1 to ~2:1)
- Profiler Aeropod with 4"×8"×1/8" specifications for initial testing
- Alternate materials evaluation (replacing foam/foamboard)
- Particle sensor integration (Atmotube Pro)

## Project Context
This is a commercialization effort for the Aeropod product line, not a general improvement initiative. Focus areas include aerodynamic optimization (fin design) and sensor integration for commercial deployment.

Monthly budget tracking was being sent to Geoffrey Bland throughout the project. Project was on track as of the final update.
