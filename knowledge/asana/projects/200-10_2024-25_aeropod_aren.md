## Project Scope
This was a commercialization effort for the Aeropod product line (referred to initially as "improvements" but represents a full commercialization initiative). Work focused on:

**Fin Design**
- Reduced fin thickness to decrease drag and instability
- Aspect ratio reduction from 3:1 to ~2:1
- Profiler Aeropod with 4"x8"x1/8" specifications integrated for initial testing
- Evaluation of alternate materials (replacing foam/foamboard)

**Particle Sensor**
- Atmotube Pro integration

**Deliverables:** Training materials and supplies
