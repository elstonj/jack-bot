## Notes & Context

### Core MVP vs. Future Phases
The project is structured in clear phases:
1. **MVP/Release Candidate (Nov 2026):** Power systems, firmware, basic flight testing (hover → transition → forward flight)
2. **Delivery Phase (Nov–Dec 2026):** Build and deliver UMES and INSTAAR airframes
3. **Future Phases:** Cold-weather qualification (Arctic/Whitehorse), EO/IR payload, GCS expansion, certification

### Cold-Weather Qualification (Major Future Work)
Extensive planned work for arctic/Whitehorse operation:
- PCB substrate and assembly process updates for -55°C operation
- Battery heating and insulation
- Heated pitot-static and case systems
- Mechanical component heating/lubrication for cold-weather tolerance
- Sensor qualification (Atmolab vs. NextVision)
- Environmental chamber testing
All currently unassigned and scheduled for execution post-MVP.

### Payload & Datalink Integration
Multiple vendor/subcontractor dependencies:
- AtmoLab/NextVision EO/IR sensor selection (trade study pending)
- Retract system design and fabrication (Ethan Domagala)
- GCS control extension for payload (Ben Busby web controller)
- Datalink configuration (Alex Lomis)
All tasks unassigned pending requirements finalization by Dan Prendergast.

### Flight Test Anomalies
Recent test flights have revealed tuning/design issues:
- GPS dropouts on takeoff (code fix in progress)
- Landing yaw oscillation (magnetometer check recommended)
- Roll loop tuning needed (high vibes near sea level)
- Motor coupling issue in forward flight (may be control law or mechanical)
Sam Hild and Maciej Stachura are actively debugging.
