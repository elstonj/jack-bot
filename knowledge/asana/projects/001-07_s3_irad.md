## Notes & Context

**Cold-Weather Program (Arctic Qualification):**
The project includes a major cold-weather modification track for operation down to -55°C (design spec, -40°C test floor), driven by Whitehorse demonstration requirement. Key workstreams:
- PCB substrate & assembly process changes (FR4 → low-temp material, solder alloy, conformal coating, mechanical staking)
- Battery heating & thermal characterization
- Mechanical component lubrication & heating (servos, motors, actuators, bearings)
- Sealed enclosures & moisture management (IP54, melt-refreeze cycle risk identified)
- EO/IR payload subcontractor qualification (AtmoLab or NextVision sensor)
- Environmental chamber campaign planned (facility + test plan TBD)

**Payload Integration (Task 8):**
EO/IR camera, datalink, and retract system require integration across hardware, GCS software, and functional testing. Leads: Alex Lomis (sensor/camera config), Ethan Domagala (retract design), Josh Fromm (electro-mechanical integration), Dan Prendergast (requirements & testing).

**Multiple Airframes:**
Project expects to build at least 3 S3 variants: UMES S3, 2 INSTAAR S3s, plus determination of which goes to Whitehorse.

**Known Issues & Tuning Work:**
- Servo behavior on startup & comms loss (safety investigation needed)
- Motor interaction in forward flight (rear motor spins when front two slow)
- Flight control tuning (roll loop, roll hover gains near sea level, yaw oscillations on landing)

**Manufacturing & Quality:**
Extensive DFM/DFA (Design for Manufacturability/Assembly), certification planning, and documentation backlog — suggesting transition toward production-readiness, though still in R&D.
