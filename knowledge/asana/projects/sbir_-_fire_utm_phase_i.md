# SBIR - Fire UTM Phase I

## Overview
- **Client/Customer:** National Center for Atmospheric Research (NCAR/RAL), Colorado's Center of Excellence (CoE) for Advanced Technology Aerial Firefighting
- **Project Type:** Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) pilot study
- **Objective:** Demonstrate value of collecting targeted UAS (unmanned aircraft system) observations to improve low-level wind and turbulence representation in regions affected by active wildland fires
- **Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Phase I complete as of February 7, 2024
- **Team:** Black Swift Technologies (whole team partnership with NCAR/RAL and CoE)

## Project Summary
This was a Phase I SBIR project led by BST to explore the use of UAS data for wildfire meteorology applications. The project involved partnership with two external organizations (NCAR/RAL and Colorado's CoE for Advanced Technology Aerial Firefighting) to obtain permission and conduct UAS observations near wildland fire zones.
