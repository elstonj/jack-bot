# SBIR - Fire UTM Phase I

## Overview
- **Client/Customer:** National Center for Atmospheric Research (NCAR/RAL) and Colorado's Center of Excellence (CoE) for Advanced Technology Aerial Firefighting
- **Program:** Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) Phase I
- **Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Phase I complete as of Feb 7, 2024
- **Team:** Black Swift Technologies (whole team partnership effort)

## Project Description
A pilot study to demonstrate the value of collecting and assimilating targeted UAS (unmanned aircraft systems) observations to improve representation of low-level winds and turbulence in regions impacted by active wildland fires. BST led the partnership with NCAR/RAL and Colorado's CoE to obtain permission and conduct UAS observations near wildland fire zones.
