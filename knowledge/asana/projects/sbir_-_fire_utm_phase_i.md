## Overview
- **Client**: U.S. SBIR Program (Small Business Innovation Research)
- **Partner Organizations**: NCAR/RAL, Colorado Center of Excellence (CoE) for Advanced Technology Aerial Firefighting
- **Objective**: Pilot study to demonstrate value of UAS observations for improving low-level wind and turbulence representation in wildland fire regions
- **Lead**: Black Swift Technologies (BST)
- **Timeline**: Phase I completed February 7, 2024
- **Status**: Project archived; Phase I deliverables complete

## Project Description
The project proposed a pilot study to collect and assimilate targeted UAS (Uncrewed Aerial System) observations near wildland fires to improve atmospheric modeling of low-level winds and turbulence in fire-affected regions. BST led partnership work with NCAR/RAL and Colorado's CoE for Advanced Technology Aerial Firefighting.
