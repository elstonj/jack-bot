# SBIR - Fire UTM Phase I

## Status
**ARCHIVED** — Phase I complete as of February 7, 2024.

## Overview
- **Client:** National Science Foundation (SBIR program); partnership with NCAR/RAL and Colorado's Center of Excellence (CoE) for Advanced Technology Aerial Firefighting
- **Objective:** Pilot study to demonstrate value of collecting UAS observations to improve low-level wind and turbulence representation in wildland fire regions
- **Team:** Black Swift Technologies (BST) — whole team involved
- **Timeline:** Phase I completed February 7, 2024

## Project Summary
This Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) Phase I project proposed a partnership between BST, NCAR/RAL, and Colorado's CoE to collect and assimilate targeted UAS observations for wildland fire meteorology. Phase I research and deliverables have been completed.
