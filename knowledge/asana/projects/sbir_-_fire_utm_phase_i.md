# SBIR - Fire UTM Phase I

## Overview
- **Client/Customer:** NCAR/RAL and Colorado's Center of Excellence (CoE) for Advanced Technology Aerial Firefighting
- **Project Type:** SBIR Phase I pilot study
- **Purpose:** Demonstrate the value of collecting and assimilating targeted UAS observations to improve low-level wind and turbulence representation in regions impacted by active wildland fires
- **Status:** **ARCHIVED** — Phase I is complete as of February 7, 2024
- **Team:** Black Swift Technologies (BST) — whole team involvement

## Project Description
BST led a partnership with NCAR/RAL and Colorado's CoE for Advanced Technology Aerial Firefighting to collect UAS observations near wildland fires and improve atmospheric modeling for fire-affected regions.
